Family Relationship Notes

The Peter Stiner family register records Sarah J. Stiner’s birth as 9 March 1860 and identifies her within Peter and Elizabeth Russell’s family. The 1870 census places Sarah in Peter and Elizabeth’s household. Her 1930 Kentucky death certificate names Peter Stiner as her father; the mother field appears to repeat Sarah’s own name and is not reliable identification of her mother.

Sarah’s marriage to Winfield Scott Green is recorded in the Union County, Tennessee, marriage index on 14 September 1876. Their son James’s 1899 marriage certificate names W. S. Green and Sarah J. Green as his parents. The other children and siblings are retained from the family register and compiled family material; original records have not been reviewed for each individual.

Her memorial reports death in Barbourville and burial at Barbourville Cemetery. No verified portrait or headstone photograph is present in this profile, so one placeholder is shown.

Life Story

Sarah Jane Stiner was born in Claiborne County, Tennessee, on 9 March 1860 . She married Winfield Scott Green, and their son James's 1899 marriage certificate names her as Sarah J. Green, the groom's mother . She raised nine or more children and died in Barbourville on 3 September 1930 .
