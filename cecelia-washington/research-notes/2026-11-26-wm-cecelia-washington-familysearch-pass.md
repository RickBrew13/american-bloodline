**Signed in as:** RickBrewster1 (account "Richard", @Rick12619 / rick.brwstr@gmail.com).
**Purpose:** On Rick's order — find what can be found on Wm and Cecelia, and find their parents so profiles can be built for them.

**Result in one line:** nothing more was found on Wm or Cecelia themselves.
