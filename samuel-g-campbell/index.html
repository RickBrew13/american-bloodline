Life Story

Samuel G. Campbell is known so far from his daughter Rhonda’s marriage record. When Rhonda Sue Campbell, then a minor, applied to marry Ronald Keith Brewster in Hamilton County, Ohio, in October 1980, the license named her father as Samuel Campbell and her mother as Janice E. Woodrum. Samuel and Janice both signed the parental consent on 17 October 1980.

Samuel was born on 2 February 1937 in Casey County, Kentucky, the son of Logan Ira Campbell and Ora Foster Campbell, and married Janice Elaine Woodrum on 1 December 1956 in Clementsville, Casey County. The 1940 census lists the family in Dunnville, Casey County. Two daughters died young: Connie Renee Campbell in 1959 and Marva Charlene Campbell in 1962. Rhonda was born in Kentucky in 1963, and by 1980 the family was living in Cincinnati. Samuel died on 16 November 1998 in Jamestown, Kentucky.
