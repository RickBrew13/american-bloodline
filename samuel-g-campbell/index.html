Life Story

Samuel Eugene Campbell was born on 2 February 1937 in Casey County, Kentucky, to Logan and Ora “Doll” Foster Campbell. His 1956 marriage register names his parents and documents his marriage to Janice Elaine Woodrum. Their daughter Rhonda’s 1980 Hamilton County, Ohio, marriage abstract identifies Samuel as her father and Janice as her mother; both parents signed the consent.

A contemporary obituary published 18 November 1998 adds important details missing from the earlier profile. It identifies Sam as a retired postal employee and retired Navy veteran, says he was of Jamestown and died at Russell County Hospital, and records burial in Salem Cemetery with military rites. It names four surviving children—son Dean and daughters Toni Van Stone, Rhonda McCreary and Marsha McDonald—and gives 12 grandchildren. Two other children are independently recorded as having died young: Connie Renee in 1959 and Marva Charlene in 1962. The records reviewed do not identify the mothers of Connie or Marva, and Janice is directly documented as Rhonda’s mother only.

The obituary also names a surviving brother Ray Campbell of Athens, Georgia, and sisters Geneva Bell of Moreland and Elece Hatter of Indianapolis. Ray is likely the Logan Ray Campbell whose 2017 obituary says he was born in Liberty, Kentucky, to the late Ira and Ora Campbell and died in Athens. The compiled FamilySearch profile lists Logan Ray among Logan and Ora’s children, supporting the identification; Geneva’s maiden identity and Elece’s link to Virginia Elece Campbell still need records. Samuel died on 16 November 1998 at Russell County Hospital.

A compiled FamilySearch profile for Logan and Ora lists an eight-child group: Nelma, James Otis, Logan Ray, Robert Lee, Edward Thomas, Ernest Franklin, Samuel Eugene and Virginia Elece. The 1998 obituary independently names Ray, Geneva Bell and Elece Hatter as Samuel's surviving siblings. Logan Ray is likely the Athens brother, and Elece Hatter may be Virginia Elece Campbell, but neither identification is yet supported by an original parent-naming record. Geneva Bell is likely Geneva Campbell Bell, wife of Elmer Eugene Bell of Moreland, based on the 2010 Lincoln County obituary. The compiled list names Nelma Campbell as the other daughter, while Logan’s Find a Grave memorial reportedly names Naomi Campbell Reeves; whether Nelma, Naomi and Geneva are one woman or multiple people remains unresolved. The remaining names in the eight-child group are compiled-tree leads, not yet independently corroborated in Samuel's file.
