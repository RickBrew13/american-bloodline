Life Story

Samuel G. Campbell is known so far from his daughter Rhonda’s marriage record. When Rhonda Sue Campbell, then a minor, applied to marry Ronald Keith Brewster in Hamilton County, Ohio, in October 1980, the license named her father as Samuel Campbell and her mother as Janice E. Woodrum. Samuel and Janice both signed the parental consent on 17 October 1980.

Rhonda was born in Kentucky in 1963, and by 1980 the family was living in Cincinnati. Samuel’s birth, parents and later life are not yet known. A MyHeritage search result points to a Samuel Campbell, born about 1937, who married Janice Elaine Woodrum in Kentucky in 1956; that lead has not been checked against a record.
