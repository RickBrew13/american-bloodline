## Corrections and new family findings
### Death, burial, occupation and Navy service
- Death: the obituary says Samuel was “of Jamestown” and died at Russell County Hospital. This clarifies that Jamestown was his residence, while the hospital was the place of death.
- Burial: Salem Cemetery, with military rites by the Casey County Funeral Squad. FamilySearch's compiled profile says burial in Liberty; Salem Cemetery is the specific cemetery named by the contemporary obituary. The precise plot and cemetery register have not been inspected.
- Occupation: retired postal employee.
- Military: retired Navy veteran. No Navy service dates, rating/rank, ship, unit, conflict, awards or discharge record have yet been found. Do not infer Korean War or Vietnam service solely from the term “Navy veteran.”

### Children
The obituary names four surviving children in 1998:
1. Dean Campbell, Cincinnati.
2. Toni Van Stone, Cincinnati.
3. Rhonda McCreary, Cincinnati (same daughter documented as Rhonda Sue Campbell, born 23 October 1963, and mother Janice E. Woodrum in the 1980 marriage abstract).

Two additional children already documented by death records died young:
- Connie Renee Campbell, died 13 June 1959, Campbellsville, Kentucky; death record names Samuel as father.

Thus the profile now identifies six children in all: four surviving at the time of the 1998 obituary and two deceased before him. The obituary does not identify the mothers of Dean, Toni, Marsha, Connie or Marva. Janice is directly named as Rhonda's mother on the 1980 marriage abstract only. Do not assign the other children to Janice without birth or parent-naming evidence.

The 12 grandchildren are reported by the obituary; no individual names were supplied in the accessible text.

- **Elece Hatter of Indianapolis, Indiana** is named as a surviving sister. FamilySearch's compiled profile lists Virginia Elece Campbell among Logan and Ora's children, making this a possible match; no marriage record or obituary has yet connected Virginia Elece to the Hatter surname.
- FamilySearch's Logan/Ora compiled profile lists additional children including James Otis Campbell, Robert Lee Campbell, Edward Thomas Campbell and Ernest Franklin Campbell. Their sibling relationship to Samuel is supported by that compiled tree; the obituary's three surviving siblings do not enumerate deceased siblings. Original records for the entire sibling group have not all been checked.
