# Barnes family source review — 26 November 2026

## Contemporary newspaper notices
- George O. Barnes, *The Cincinnati Enquirer* (Cincinnati, Ohio), 19 Apr. 1973, p. 68; photo scan attached to [Find a Grave memorial 209316303](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/209316303/george-ozais-barnes/photo). Transcribed text states George was the husband of Elizabeth “Betty” Barnes, née Vonarb; names Christine Steinmann, Irene Vandenberg, Nina Stretch, Lillian Fisher, Russell, Harold, and Garvice as children; and calls Bessie Miles his sister. We rely on the contemporary notice for the later spouse, seven-child enumeration and Bessie link. The scan is a memorial-site reproduction rather than an archival issue independently obtained.
- Nina Minnie Barnes Stretch, *The Cincinnati Enquirer*, 20 Apr. 2010, p. 16; transcription on [Find a Grave memorial 172046799](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/172046799/nina_minnie-stretch). Says Nina was the eldest of seven, identifies the late siblings Garvis, Harold, Christine, Lillian, Irene and the surviving younger brother Russell.
- Lillian Mae Barnes Fisher, *Anderson Herald-Bulletin*, 25 Apr. 1988, p. A-2; clipping transcript on [Find a Grave memorial 36802557](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/36802557/lillian_mae-fisher). Names surviving siblings Russell, Harold, Nina and Christine.
- Ruby Christine Barnes Steinmann, *Tampa Bay Times*, 9 Jan. 2001, p. 16; clipping transcript on [Find a Grave memorial 228706900](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/228706900/ruby_christine-steinmann). Names brothers Russell and Harold and sister Nina.

## Memorial-derived relationships
- [George Ozais Barnes memorial 209316303](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/209316303/george-ozais-barnes) and [Effie M. Miller Barnes memorial 209316879](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/209316879/effie-miller-barnes) each list the same seven children. These memorials also supply vital dates and marker information, which remain attributed to Find a Grave pending original civil/cemetery records.
- George memorial links Francis Menden Barnes and Alice Young Barnes as parents, lists seven sibling candidates, and separately identifies Beckham Bloyd Barnes as half-sibling. Only Bessie Miles is directly called George’s sister in the 1973 notice.
- Effie memorial links Orla Lee Miller as sibling and labels Lacy Eugene and Roy C. Miller as half-siblings. Orla’s memorial separately links Effie and repeats the same parents. These are contributor-supplied family connections; no original family records for these siblings were reviewed in this pass.
