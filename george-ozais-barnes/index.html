Life Story

George Ozais Barnes was born at Jamestown, Russell County, Kentucky, on 28 December 1886, according to his Find a Grave memorial. The same memorial and Effie’s memorial identify Effie M. Miller as his wife and list seven children, including Garvice. A 2025 clipping about Garvice also names George O. Barnes as Garvice’s deceased father, though that clipping conflicts with the contemporaneous 1976 notice about Garvice’s wife. The father-son link is supported by the convergence of family memorials and later clipping but awaits original census or birth evidence.

Find a Grave reports George died in Cincinnati on 17 April 1973 and was buried at Laurel IOOF Cemetery. The dates and cemetery have not yet been verified against original certificates or burial records.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Verify George’s birth and death from original Kentucky/Ohio records and the Laurel IOOF cemetery register.
  • Locate marriage record for George and Effie and original records for the seven children.
  • The 2025 clipping naming Effie Miller and George O. Barnes as Garvice’s parents has a spouse-name conflict; preserve it as corroboration of parents only, not as a reliable source for Garvice’s spouse.
  • Find a Grave links for George’s parents and siblings are unverified secondary claims; no parentage claims about George’s earlier generation were added to Gen VIII.
  • George’s contemporaneous 1973 death notice names Elizabeth “Betty” (Vonarb) Barnes as his wife at death, after Effie’s 1933 death. It gives no marriage date and no children by Betty. The public memorial does not list this later spouse; retain the discrepancy and do not treat a marriage date or child as established.
  • Seven children are directly named in the death notice, and Nina’s 2010 obituary calls her the eldest of seven. No additional child was identified in the sources reviewed. “Christine Steinmann” corresponds to Ruby Christine Barnes Steinmann; “Irene Vandenberg” likely corresponds to Marilyn Irene Barnes Vandenberg, but the exact first-name identity bridge remains a likely match, not a direct statement in the notice.
  • The 1973 notice directly calls Bessie Miles George’s sister. Find a Grave family links list six additional siblings—Henry Clay, Margaret, Lula E., Thomas Porter, Joseph Menden, and Olla C. “Ollie”—and a half-brother Beckham Bloyd Barnes. These links are user-contributed memorial evidence; treat the list beyond Bessie as candidates until original family records are checked.
