# George Ozais Barnes — spouse, children, and sibling audit

Reviewed 26 November 2026. George’s existing parents, Francis Menden Barnes and Alice Young Barnes, were left unchanged as requested.

## Children
The Cincinnati Enquirer death notice dated 19 April 1973, p. 68 (image reproduced on Find a Grave memorial 209316303) names seven children: Christine Steinmann, Irene Vandenberg, Nina Stretch, Lillian Fisher, Russell, Harold, and Garvice. Nina’s 2010 obituary calls her the eldest of seven and names six late siblings, including Garvis, Christine, and Irene. Lillian’s 1988 and Ruby Christine’s 2001 obituaries provide further sibling corroboration. Working count: seven; no additional child found in the records reviewed.

The obituary’s “Christine Steinmann” is likely Ruby Christine Barnes Steinmann. “Irene Vandenberg” likely refers to Marilyn Irene Barnes Vandenberg; preserve the abbreviated newspaper form and the identity as probable rather than explicit.

## Later spouse
George’s 1973 Enquirer notice calls him the husband of Elizabeth (Betty) Barnes, née Vonarb. This is a later spouse than Effie, who died in 1933. The newspaper text provides no marriage date and no children by Betty; no such date or child is asserted here. The Find a Grave memorial’s spouse list currently includes Effie only; preserve that source discrepancy.

## Siblings
The same 1973 notice calls Bessie Miles his sister. George’s Find a Grave memorial and the parallel memorial family groups for Francis’s children additionally list Henry Clay (1870–1942), Margaret (1872–1883), Lula E. Barnes Trimble (1874–1967), Thomas Porter (1875–1961), Joseph Menden (1882–1962), Olla C. “Ollie” Barnes Dunbar (1885–1919), and Bessie Barnes Miles (1889–1975) as siblings; they list Beckham Bloyd Barnes (1902–1973) separately as a half-sibling. Beyond Bessie, these are contributor-entered memorial relationships, not original civil evidence.

## Images
