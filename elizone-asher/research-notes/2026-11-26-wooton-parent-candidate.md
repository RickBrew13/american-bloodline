# Elizone Asher — candidate birth-family identification

Reviewed 26 November 2026.

## Candidate identity
Elizabeth Ann Wooton (FamilySearch public-tree ID L8PV-J2L; reported 1869–1954) is the leading working match for Elizone Asher, named on Russell W. Asher’s memorial. Confidence in this identity bridge: approximately 75%, not proven. The relevant public-tree source titles are:
- 1870 census: “Lizaney Wooton in household of William Wooton.”
- 1900 census: “Elizana Asher in household of Kilas Asher.”
- Kentucky death-record collection: “Elizane Wooten in entry for William Asher.”
Russell’s Find a Grave memorial (68281383) says “S/O Letcher & Elizone Asher.” The spelling progression is plausible and the household context aligns, but the underlying image records were not independently transcribed and “Elizone” is not a direct primary-record name in the material reviewed.

## Parents
The FamilySearch public-tree profile for Elizabeth Ann Wooton names William M Wooton (1841–1922) and Rachel Baker (1845–1928) as her parents. A separate 1870 census source title places “Lizaney Wooton” in William Wooton’s household; the profile identifies Rachel as William’s wife and Elizabeth as their daughter. Working confidence for the parent couple, conditional on the identity bridge: approximately 70%. These parents are listed on Elizone’s profile as a provisional working assignment; do not describe them as proved.
