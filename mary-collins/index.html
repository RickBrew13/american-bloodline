Life Story

Mary Collins was born Mary Murnahan on 1 December 1861 in Ireland, the daughter of Thomas Murnahan and Mary Reddenbough. She came to America as a small child with her mother, in the family crossing of 1862–1873 — she has no ship of her own, and if her mother's manifest is ever found, Mary is on it. She married William Green, was widowed before 1886, and brought two daughters, Julia and Mary Green, into her second marriage. The name she married under the second time was Collins, and whether Collins was an intervening marriage is not resolved in the records found.

On 29 July 1886, in a Catholic ceremony in Hamilton County, Ohio, she married John W. Williams, a Cincinnati drayman and teamster. Together they had five children: Robert Emmett, Margaret, Lenora, John Thomas — the direct line — and Edward. Mary died on 19 January 1923 at St. Francis Hospital, Cincinnati, aged 61; her late residence was 711 Whittier Street. She was interred on the morning of 22 January 1923 in Spring Grove Cemetery, Section 15, Grave 232; her burial was ordered by Daniel Murnahan, whose relationship to her is not established. Her husband John, who had died between 1921 and January 1923, is not buried with her.

No photograph of Mary herself has been found. The two pictures in the couple folder are contextual views of Lytle Park and Spring Grove Cemetery, not family photographs, so none is shown here. The two photographs on her Find a Grave memorial are the Section 15 I.O.O.F. monument (not her personal marker) and her interment record (a document, cited below, not shown as a picture).
