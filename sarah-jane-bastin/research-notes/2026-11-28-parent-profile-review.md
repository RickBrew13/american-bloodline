# Sarah Jane Bastin Carman — research note

Date: 28 November 2026

## Name resolution

The best-supported identity is Sarah Jane Bastin Carman (high confidence, about 90%). Sarah’s own 1921 Kentucky death certificate image (FamilySearch record N9PL-WRK, image 788, file 7379) records “Sarah Jane Carman” and names her parents William Bastin and Sarah Ann Salyers; the database index spells the mother’s surname Salyer. The original 1910 schedule calls her “Sarah J. Carman,” a widowed head in Walltown, Casey County. The 1900 schedule calls Daniel’s wife “Sarah J. Carmon.”

Roscoe’s 1939 death-certificate reproduction posted to Find a Grave instead reports “Mary Jane Bastin” as his mother, with Roy Carman as informant. This is the only located Mary Jane form for Daniel’s wife. Because Sarah’s own record and both census schedules use Sarah J./Jane, Mary Jane is most likely an informant or recording error, not a second wife or established alias. Retain the contradictory source reading, but use Sarah Jane for the profile and family links.

The FamilySearch 1900 index links Roscoe as Daniel and Sarah’s son, but the scanned sheet 19B retrieved here visibly lists Daniel, Sarah J. and Quintella on lines 98–100; Roscoe is not visible on this portion of the schedule. Do not say the scan itself confirms his presence.

## Date and place conflicts

FamilySearch’s compiled profile gives Sarah’s birth as 5 August 1847 in Casey County. Her own death certificate image gives 5 July 1846 and appears to say Lincoln County. The 1910 census age is 65 (approximately 1845). Keep these source-attributed differences; do not silently select one.
