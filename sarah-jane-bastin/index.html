Quick Facts

Name: Sarah J. Carmon in the 1900 census index and Sarah J. Carman in the 1910 census; her own 1921 Kentucky death certificate records “Sarah Jane Carman.” The Bastin maiden name is supported by the death certificate’s father field (William Bastin) and compiled family records. Working identification: Sarah Jane Bastin Carman. Birth dates conflict: FamilySearch gives 5 August 1847, her death certificate reports 5 July 1846, and the 1910 census age implies about 1845; the certificate also appears to report Lincoln County rather than the compiled Casey County birthplace. Died 21 April 1921, Casey County, Kentucky. Occupation not established.

Family Relationship Notes

FamilySearch profile KGCQ-3BL identifies Sarah Jane Bastin as daughter of William Bastin and Sarah Ann Salyers, wife of Daniel W. Carman, and mother in the compiled seven-child family including Roscoe. Her own 1921 Kentucky death-certificate image (file 7379) records “Sarah Jane Carman” and names her parents William Bastin and Sarah Ann Salyers (FamilySearch index spelling: Salyer). The original 1910 census schedule independently records “Sarah J. Carman,” a widowed head in Walltown, Casey County. The original 1900 census sheet 19B records a “Sarah J. Carmon” as wife of Daniel N. Carmon; the index/household link to this exact Daniel and Roscoe is compiled and the scanned lines do not show Roscoe.

Name conclusion (high confidence, about 90%): the best-supported name for Daniel’s wife and Roscoe’s mother is Sarah Jane Bastin Carman. The sole located “Mary Jane Bastin” form is on the reproduction of Roscoe’s 1939 death certificate, with son Roy Carman as informant. Sarah’s own death certificate and the 1910 census give Sarah Jane/Sarah J.; the 1900 census gives Sarah J. for Daniel N. Carmon’s wife. “Mary Jane” is most likely an informant or recording error, not a second wife or established alias. Preserve that certificate’s literal wording as a conflicting statement.

Date and birthplace conflicts remain: the compiled FamilySearch profile says 5 August 1847 in Casey County; her own death certificate appears to say 5 July 1846 and Lincoln County; the 1910 census age 65 suggests about 1845. The date/place discrepancies do not alter the name conclusion.

FamilySearch’s William/Sarah Ann compiled family profile lists 13 child entries including Sarah. Two entries—Alancin Samuel “Lance” Bastin (1858–1888) and Lance M. Bastin (1858–deceased)—may be duplicate representations; they are not merged without an identity bridge.
