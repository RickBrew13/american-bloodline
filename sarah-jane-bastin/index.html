Family Relationship Notes

FamilySearch public profile KGCQ-3BL identifies Sarah Jane Bastin as daughter of William Bastin and Sarah Ann Salyers and spouse of Daniel W. Carman, and lists their seven children including Roscoe. Her Find a Grave memorial is a separate compiled memorial source.

FamilySearch gives Sarah Jane’s birth as 5 August 1847. The existing Roscoe page retains its prior note that another source reports an October date; the difference is not silently resolved here.

FamilySearch’s William/Sarah Ann family profile lists 13 child entries including Sarah. Two entries—Alancin Samuel “Lance” Bastin (1858–1888) and Lance M. Bastin (1858–deceased)—may be duplicate representations, but are not merged without an identity bridge.
