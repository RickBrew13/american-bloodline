# Sarah Jane Bastin Carman — record image inventory

- `1900-census-sheet-19B-Daniel-N-Carmon-household.jpg` — FamilySearch image linked from Daniel N. Carmon’s 1900 census index (M9WP-NR9), original NARA population schedule, sheet 19B. Visible lines 98–100 identify Daniel N. Carmon, wife Sarah J. Carmon, and daughter Quintella; Roscoe is not visible on this scanned page though the extended-family index lists him.
- `1910-census-sheet-1A-Sarah-J-Carman.jpg` — FamilySearch original 1910 U.S. census image 547 for Sarah J. Carman (M28L-WV7), Walltown, Casey County, Kentucky; head, widowed, age 65.
- `1921-KY-death-certificate-file-7379-Sarah-Jane-Carman.jpg` — FamilySearch original image 788, Kentucky death record N9PL-WRK, certificate file 7379. Full name Sarah Jane Carman; parents William Bastin and Sarah Ann Salyers (index spelling: Salyer). The image reports birth 5 July 1846; the public tree gives 5 August 1847.
- `roscoe-carman-kentucky-death-certificate-1939.jpg` — Find a Grave reproduction attached to Roscoe memorial 17419340, credited to Rochelle Riordan, 24 May 2014. It names “Mary Jane Bastin” as Roscoe’s mother and Roy Carman as informant, conflicting with Sarah’s own record and census name forms.

- `Austins-of-America-Aug-1985-pages-166-167-Ruth-Carman.pdf` and page preview — compiled secondary family-history clipping; p. 167 identifies Ruth as a daughter of Daniel and “Sarah Jane (Bastin) Carman.”
