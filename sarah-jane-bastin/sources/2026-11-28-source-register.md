# Source register — Sarah Jane Bastin Carman

Date reviewed: 28 November 2026

## Name and parent identity records
- [FamilySearch, 1900 U.S. census, Daniel N Carmon household (M9WP-NR9)](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:M9WP-NR9) — Index names Daniel N Carmon, wife Sarah J Carmon, daughter Quintella, and associates Roscoe as son. The downloaded original schedule image (sheet 19B) shows Daniel, Sarah J, and Quintella on visible lines 98–100; Roscoe does not appear on those lines. This image supports the wife’s Sarah J name form, not Roscoe’s presence on this page.
- [FamilySearch, 1910 U.S. census, Sarah J Carman (M28L-WV7)](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:M28L-WV7) — Index and original schedule image 547 reviewed. Identifies Sarah J. Carman, head, widowed, age 65, Walltown, Casey County, Kentucky; parents born Kentucky.
- [FamilySearch, Kentucky Deaths, 1911–1967, Sarah Jane Carman (N9PL-WRK)](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:N9PL-WRK) — Index and original image 788 / certificate file 7379 reviewed. The death certificate records “Sarah Jane Carman,” father William Bastin, mother Sarah Ann Salyers (index spells Salyer), death 21 April 1921. The image reports birth 5 July 1846 and appears to give Lincoln County as birthplace, differing from the compiled 5 August 1847 / Casey County.
- [Roscoe Carman Kentucky death-certificate reproduction, Find a Grave memorial 17419340](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/17419340/roscoe-carman/photo) — Reproduction credited to Rochelle Riordan, 24 May 2014. Names his mother “Mary Jane Bastin” and informant Roy Carman. This conflicts with Sarah’s own 1921 death record and 1900/1910 records. Best-supported identity is Sarah Jane; Mary Jane is most likely an informant/recording error. Preserve the literal reading.

## Compiled family context
- [FamilySearch public Sarah Jane Bastin profile (KGCQ-3BL)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/KGCQ-3BL/sarah-jane-bastin-1847-1921) — Compiled profile identifies parents William Bastin and Sarah Ann Salyers, spouse Daniel W. Carman, and seven children including Roscoe. It gives birth 5 August 1847.
- [FamilySearch public William Bastin profile (LHQL-N9R)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/LHQL-N9R/william-bastin-1823-1870) — Compiled family group lists Sarah Jane among William and Sarah Ann Salyers’s children.
- [FamilySearch public Sarah Ann Salyers profile (KNWK-TSF)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/KNWK-TSF/sarah-ann-salyers-1825-1886) — Compiled family group includes Sarah Jane among William Bastin and Sarah Ann’s children.
- [FamilySearch public Roscoe Carman profile (KJZ3-7VS)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/KJZ3-7VS/roscoe-carman-1882-1939) — Compiled child profile identifies Daniel W. Carman and Sarah Jane Bastin as Roscoe’s parents.
- [Find a Grave memorial 17419350, Sarah Jane Bastin Carman](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/17419350/sarah-jane-carman) — User-contributed memorial calls her Sarah Jane Bastin Carman, 5 August 1847–21 April 1921; no grave photo.
