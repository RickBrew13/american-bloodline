Life Story

Roy W. Jordan was born on 27 January 1912 in Kentucky. As a boy he was listed as Leroy Jordan in the Cincinnati household of William J. Jordan and Mary E. Bogart, age 8 in 1920 and age 18 in 1930. He married Irene A. Everhart before April 1940. In the 1940 census he and Irene were living in Cincinnati, where he worked as a storekeeper for an office furniture company. He enlisted in the Army at Cincinnati on 6 January 1943. Their son Richard L. Jordan was born in Cincinnati on 16 June 1943. In the 1950 census the family was still in Cincinnati, where Roy worked as a deck clerk for an express company. He died in Cincinnati on 18 September 1985.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The 1950 census index reads “Ray W. Jordan”; the handwriting on the image reads “Jordan Roy W”. Every other record gives his name as Roy W. Jordan.
  • No marriage record for Roy Jordan and Irene Everhart has been found on FamilySearch; the 1940 census shows they were already married by 8 April 1940.
  • Listings seen in search results name Mount Moriah Cemetery, Withamsville, Clermont County, Ohio, as the burial place for Roy and Irene; the listings have not been opened in full, so burial is still listed as unknown.
  • Irene M. Jordan, named as a sister on the family tree, was not in William and Mary Jordan’s household in the 1920 or 1930 census.
  • The 1950 census occupation handwriting reads “Deck Clerk” or “Deck Stork”; the index reads “Deck Stork”.
