Life Story

Anthony Kevelder was born on 6 March 1888 in Marion Township, Mercer County, Ohio, the son of Henry Kevelder and Mary Hartings DOCUMENTED. His grandfather Anton had come from Holland SUPPORTED. He grew up in the German Catholic farm country around St. Henry and married Elizabeth Schipper of Shelby County about 1910 SUPPORTED.

In St. Henry he kept a restaurant, a pool room and confectionery store, and the local paper called him a "well known business man" DOCUMENTED. He died there suddenly on 10 November 1918, after a few days' illness, leaving Elizabeth with five small children DOCUMENTED. He is buried in the St. Henry church cemetery DOCUMENTED.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • No marriage record for Anthony and Elizabeth has been found. Both were single in the April 1910 census, so they most likely married in late 1910 or 1911, probably in Shelby or Mercer County. The Shelby County marriage register and St. Lawrence parish records are the next places to look.
  • His death certificate gives his birth year as 1887; his birth record, draft card, and censuses say 1888.
  • The Ohio Death Index gives 10 April 1918, most likely an indexing error. His obituary says he died "last week Monday," which would be 11 November; the certificate says 10 November.
  • An infant, Oliver Frederick Kevelder (7 January – 27 February 1911), buried at St. Lawrence Cemetery, Dinsmore Township, may have been their first child UNPROVED.
