Life Story

Eva L. Luft is named as the mother of Deborah Ann Luft and Alexander Joseph Luft on their Hamilton County, Ohio, marriage licenses, with John B. Davis as their father. Robert Vanhorn’s birth certificate names Eva “Luft” as his mother and William Allen VanHorn as his father. These records establish the named parentage, but do not establish the marriages or their sequence (DOCUMENTED).

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Her identity as Eva Leea Luft (later Vanhorn), born 9 February 1918, Cincinnati, died 9 August 2002, is supported by her Social Security records. Some sources give her birth date as 10 February 1918, and the Ohio Death Index gives 9 September 1918; her Social Security records, the censuses, and the tree give 9 February 1918.
  • Robert Vanhorn’s birth certificate names William Allen VanHorn as Robert’s father and Eva “Luft” as his mother. This documents parentage, but does not prove that William and Eva married. Eva’s marriage sequence remains unresolved.
  • No record yet names her as Alex's or Deborah's mother apart from the licenses.
  • She was still single in April 1950, so any marriage to John B. Davis came after that date and before Deborah's birth in January 1953.
  • Her mother’s surname is spelled three ways in the Social Security filings themselves. Eva’s first Form SS-5 (May 1938, filed as Luft) gives “Bella Tomarin”, and her brother David’s February 1937 filing gives “Berthia Tomarin”. The October 1962 filing gives “Bertha Tumarn”, and the three filings from 1988 and 1990 give “Bertha Tarmin”. The earliest filings agree on Tomarin; Tarmin appears only in the late filings. The tree gives Tomarin.
  • A further Form SS-5 was filed in October 1962, three months after Robert’s birth; its contents have not been seen, so whether it records a name change to Vanhorn is not known.
