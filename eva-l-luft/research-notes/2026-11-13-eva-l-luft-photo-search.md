It distinguishes the working identity Eva Leea Luft (later Vanhorn), born 9 February 1918 in Cincinnati and died 9 August 2002, from the unresolved question of whether that woman is the mother of Deborah Ann and Alexander Joseph Luft. The profile says no photos yet and itself notes that no record ties the 1918 Eva to the children.
