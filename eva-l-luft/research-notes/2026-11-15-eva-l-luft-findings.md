## 3. What this dig found

### F1 — Mother's surname: the conflict now has a dated chronology (NEW, SUPPORTED — transcription, read directly)
Re-read the SortedByName Luft Family History page in full
(https://sortedbyname.com/letter_l/luft/index_1.html, Eva entries lines
1367–1399, David entry lines 1346–1347). The mother's name is spelled **three
ways across the Social Security filings themselves**, and the spelling tracks
the filing date:

- **"BELLA TOMARIN"** — Eva's first Form SS-5, 65332608997, dated **May 1938**
  (filed as Luft, age 20).
- **"BERTHIA TOMARIN"** — brother David Luft's Form SS-5, 66150905894, dated
  **February 1937**.
- **"BERTHA TUMARN"** — Eva's Form SS-5, 65332608996, dated **October 1962**.
- **"BERTHA TARMIN"** — Eva's three late filings: Form SS-5 81311032395 dated
  **11 May 1988**, 90277000119 dated **4 October 1990**, 90319000408 dated
  **15 November 1990**.

So: **the earliest filings (1937, 1938) agree on Tomarin; Tarmin appears only
in the 1988/1990 filings; Tumarn appears once, in 1962.** The FamilySearch tree
(GRBJ-DPJ) gives Tomarin. This does NOT settle the spelling — the mother's own
record (her birth, marriage, or death record) would — but it sharpens the
conflict from "SSA says Tarmin, tree says Tomarin" to a dated sequence, and it
leans Tomarin: the two filings closest to the mother's lifetime use both say
Tomarin, and three late filings say Tarmin. Grade the chronology itself
SUPPORTED (transcription read directly); the "true" spelling remains OPEN.
This goes on the page — see §5.

### F2 — October 1962 SS-5 timing lead (NEW, UNPROVED — reasoning only)
Eva's Form SS-5 of October 1962 was filed **three months after Robert's birth
(15 July 1962)**. An SS-5 filed at that point is consistent with a name-change
filing to Vanhorn following a marriage to William Allen VanHorn — but the
filing's contents have not been seen, so no marriage date may be inferred from
it. This is a lead only: it suggests the Vanhorn marriage (if any) falls in or
before 1962, around Robert's birth, not earlier. It does not prove a marriage
happened at all. Recorded in the patch's Research Notes as an open question,
not as a fact.

### F3 — Correction: the January 1944 Robert R. Vanhorn candidate is NOT Eva's son (CONTRADICTED — by the birth certificate already on the page)
The Nov 11 dig's candidate "Robert R. Vanhorn, 2632 Barthas Pl, Cincinnati,
age ~82, b. Jan 1944" cannot be Eva's son: Robert's birth certificate (found
by Rick, already on the live page) gives his birth as **15 July 1962**. The
1944 man is a different Robert Vanhorn; no tie to Eva exists and none is
claimed. The Nov 11 dig's G6(b) reasoning (a ~1942–43 Vanhorn marriage window
built on a 1944 birth) is therefore retired — the marriage window, if any,
moves to ~1962 (see F2). No page change needed: the page already carries the
1962 birth certificate.

### F4 — "William Allen Vanhorn (1888–1962), Shawnee on Delaware, PA" is NOT our William (excluded)

names Debbie or Alex as her children beyond the marriage licenses already on
her page — so the question of whether the 1918 Eva Leea Luft is really their
mother is still open, and the obituary or her death certificate is still the

name: the Social Security filings spell it three different ways, and the
spelling follows the date — her own first filing in 1938 and her brother
David's in 1937 both say Tomarin, while Tarmin shows up only in her filings
from 1988 and 1990. So the earliest paper says Tomarin. Her page now carries
that dated breakdown. Two old leads are dead: the Robert Vanhorn born in
January 1944 in Cincinnati is not her son — her Robert was born 15 July 1962,
per the birth certificate Rick found — and the William Allen Vanhorn buried in
Pennsylvania is not her husband. A small new lead: she filed a Social Security
form in October 1962, three months after Robert was born, which fits a
name-change to Vanhorn around then — but the form itself hasn't been seen, so
that's a guess, not a fact. No photos or documents were found to file, and her
page still has no pictures.
