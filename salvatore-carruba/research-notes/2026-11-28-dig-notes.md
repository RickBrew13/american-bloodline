# Salvatore Carruba — dig notes
Date: 28 November 2026. FamilySearch signed-in pass (RickBrewster1), images read and re-read letter by letter.

## What was found
- Salvatore Carruba is named as Joseph Carruba's father on the San Cataldo christening for Joseph Carruba, baptised 14 October 1892, born 13 October 1892, godmother Carmela Mistretta (ARK 1:1:X936-8HRH). Read from the image and re-read letter by letter in a second pass.
- Rick ruled 28 November 2026 that Salvatore Carruba and Liboria Mistretta ARE Joseph Carruba's parents. They are listed plain on Joseph's page and here.
- His son Joseph was born 31 August 1891 per the Social Security Death Index and the Kentucky death index, died 10 December 1970 in Harlan County, Kentucky, married Mary Schillaci 17 June 1922 in Thomas, Jefferson County, Alabama, and worked as a coal miner. His son Norman, born about 1894-1895 in Italy, married Cora Romano and also worked as a coal miner; Norman is proved as Joseph's brother by the 1920 census image (ARK 1:1:MXDB-XLB), which lists Joseph, 27, single, as "Brother" in Norman's household at Millport, Alabama.

## Not found / not assigned
- The christening gives Joseph's birth as 13 October 1892, against 31 August 1891 on the Social Security Death Index and the Kentucky death index. The birth-date conflict is carried in Research Notes on Joseph's page and here, not on the family cards.
- The San Cataldo birth index names other children of Salvatore Carruba and Liboria Mistretta: Giuseppe (born 4 February 1880, died 1886), Arcangela (born 1881, died 1882), Salvatore (born 26 September 1883, died 1883), a second Arcangela (born 25 March 1887), and Angela, also called Agnes (born 11 February 1890). The index images were not read; none is listed as a son or daughter here. No child of Salvatore and Liboria named Norman appears in the index results.
- No record of Salvatore himself has been found beyond his naming as Joseph's father on the 1892 christening.
