Life Story

Asberry Helton, known on records as A. B., was born on 11 November 1845 SUPPORTED. He married Eliza Tankersley in Knox County, Kentucky, on 5 October 1868 DOCUMENTED, and they farmed near Wilton and raised their daughters there SUPPORTED. In 1899 he signed as a witness at the marriage of his daughter Ella to James O. Green DOCUMENTED. He died on 26 October 1918 and lies in the small cemetery on Indian Creek that carries his name SUPPORTED.
