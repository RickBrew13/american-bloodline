Family Relationship Notes

The 1918 Kentucky death certificate for Asberry names William Helton and Elizabeth Engle as his parents. The 1850 and 1860 census entries place him in their household. His 5 October 1868 Knox County marriage to Eliza Tankersley appears in the county marriage index. Their daughter Martha Ella’s 1899 marriage certificate names A. B. Helton and Eliza Helton as her parents.

Eliza’s 1914 death certificate names William Tankesley and Annie Beams as her parents. The 1850 census and records for several siblings identify William Tankersley and Annie/Anna Beames as the parental couple. The family name appears as Tankersley and Tankesley in the records.

The six children shown are retained from the current compiled family material. Some children, including Mary Ann, Ellen, and Michel Vesta, have weaker or conflicting parentage links and remain research leads. Life Story

Asberry Helton, known on records as A. B., was born on 11 November 1845 . He married Eliza Tankersley in Knox County, Kentucky, on 5 October 1868 , and they farmed near Wilton and raised their daughters there . In 1899 he signed as a witness at the marriage of his daughter Ella to James O. Green . He died on 26 October 1918 and lies in the small cemetery on Indian Creek that carries his name .
