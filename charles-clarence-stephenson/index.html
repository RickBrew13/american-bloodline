Life Story

Charles Clarence Stephenson was born near Lebanon, Ohio, on 7 July 1881, according to the funeral notice transcribed on his Find a Grave memorial. The notice reports that he married Silvie E. Pottorf on 12 October 1904, worked as a stationary engineer at Peters Cartridge Co. for 31 years, and lived in Mason after retiring. It names four surviving children: Charles, Clarence, Bessie Peterson and Eskalene Barnes. Florence is therefore identified as their daughter by her notice name “Eskalene Barnes”; her own notice and later family sources give the fuller name Florence Eskalene.

The Rose Hill cemetery transcription records Charles and Sylvia in Section D2, Lot 79. His parents and wider Stephenson sibling group remain memorial/tree claims unless independently verified.

Research Notes and Open Questions
