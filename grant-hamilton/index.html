Life Story

Grant Hamilton is known from a single record: the Jackson County, Kentucky, marriage register for 28 November 1928, which names Grant Hamilton and Mattie Hamilton as the parents of Anna May Hamilton, whose marriage license to Clarence C. Farmer was issued that day. No birth, death, census, or marriage record for Grant himself has been found, and no other record names him.

His daughter Anna married Clarence Farmer and raised three daughters — Mabel, Marjorie, and Mona Pearl — in Jackson County, Kentucky.
