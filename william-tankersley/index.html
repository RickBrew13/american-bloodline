Life Story

William Tankersley was born in Tennessee, on 11 June 1813 by his headstone SUPPORTED. He married Anna Beames about 1840, and they raised at least twelve children on their farm in Knox County, Kentucky DOCUMENTED.

Anna died in the spring of 1880, and William was living as a widowed farmer on Indian Creek that summer DOCUMENTED. He died on 25 March 1891 and is buried in Bark Camp Cemetery in Whitley County SUPPORTED.
