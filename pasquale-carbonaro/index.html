Family Relationship Notes

The Ancestry public family profile for Ignazia identifies Pasquale Carbonaro (born 1865; other details unknown) as her father and Rosalie Moule as her mother. A separate SortedByName transcription of a 1972 Ignazia SS-5/NUMIDENT record repeats Pasquale Carbonaro and Rosalie Moule as her parents. This is tree plus derivative SSA-index support; the original birth record and SS-5 were not reviewed.

An Ancestry public Carbonaro search result has an Angelo born 1 June 1887 in Palermo, deceased July 1978 in Brooklyn, with parents Pasquale Carbonaro and Rosalie Moule. A separate 1936 SS-5 transcription gives the same father, mother “Rosalia Mule,” and date of birth, but transcribes the birthplace as “BLERMO, Italy.” The combined match strongly supports Angelo as a child and likely sibling of Ignazia; the original SS-5 and civil records were not reviewed, so the spelling/place variant remains source-attributed.

Pasquale’s parents and siblings remain unidentified. A separate Ancestry same-name result for Pasquale (3 January 1865, Messina; parents Santo and Antonetta) and a FamilySearch 1950 index hit for “Pasquale Carbonar,” born 1865 in Italy and residing Brooklyn, are not bridged to this family. Do not assign Santo or Antonetta as his parents.
