Family Relationship Notes

Pasquale Carbonaro was born 15 February 1855 in Palermo, the son of Angelo Carbonaro and Ignazia Fanara, and worked as a miller. The 1886 marriage act and the 1895 Vallelunga marriage papers both name his parents; the 1895 papers name them as deceased. The Ancestry tree’s birth year of 1865 is wrong.

He married Rosalia Mule on 27 February 1886 in Brancaccio, Palermo. She was born in Zisa, Palermo, the daughter of Vincenzo Mule and Anna Pisciotto, and died 22 October 1893 in Palermo. Pasquale remarried Giuseppa Noto on 19 December 1895 in Vallelunga Pratameno. He was alive in December 1895; no death record for him has been found.

His children with Rosalia were Angelo, born 1 June 1887, Ignazia, born 3 August 1889, and Vincenzo, born 28 January 1892, all in Brancaccio, Palermo. The Messina Pasquale (born 3 January 1865, parents Santo and Antonetta Carbonaro) and the 1950 Brooklyn “Pasquale Carbonar” are different men and are not this Pasquale.

Life Story

Pasquale Carbonaro was born 15 February 1855 in Palermo, the son of Angelo Carbonaro and Ignazia Fanara, and worked as a miller. He married Rosalia Mule on 27 February 1886 in Brancaccio, Palermo; she was born in Zisa, the daughter of Vincenzo Mule and Anna Pisciotto. Their children Angelo, Ignazia, and Vincenzo were born in Brancaccio in 1887, 1889, and 1892. Rosalia died 22 October 1893 in Palermo, and Pasquale remarried Giuseppa Noto on 19 December 1895 in Vallelunga Pratameno, where he had been living about nine months. He was alive in December 1895; no death record for him has been found.
