Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Ancestry public Ignazia profile lists Pasquale Carbonaro (1865–unknown) as her father and Rosalie Moule as her mother. Treat this as a sourced working parent assignment, not an original-record proof.
  • SortedByName transcribes an SS-5 for Angelo Carbonaro, born 1 Jun 1887 in “BLERMO” (probably a transcription/typographic error for Palermo), with father Pasquale Carbonaro and mother Rosalia Mule. This likely identifies a sibling of daughter Ignazia, but the mother-name variant and identity need checking against the original SS-5.
  • Ancestry public Pasquale name search yields 13 same-name people. One 3 Jan 1865 Messina candidate is the correct year but differs from the tree’s absent birthplace; his parents Santo and Antonetta are not assigned here as established grandparents.
  • FamilySearch 1950 index returns a “Pasquale Carbonar” (one final o), born 1865 in Italy, in Brooklyn. This may be the tree’s Pasquale, but the index exposes no parents/household relationships and does not prove the identification.
  • Ancestry “similar people” shown on Ignazia’s profile are not labeled as siblings; none were converted into kin links.
  • Ignazia’s compiled profile lists six child entries: Pasquale Cincimino (1922–1993), Rosolia Cincimino (1919–2015), Josephine Ciancimino (1913–unknown), two separate Frances Cincimino entries (1910–unknown), and Frances Ciancimino (1910–1973). The repeated Frances entries are kept separate and not assumed to identify Frances Guglielmo.
  • No verified portrait or grave marker located; no original birth/marriage/death record reviewed.
