## HEADLINE: the live pages' premises were wrong in two material ways

1. Pasquale Carbonaro was NOT born in 1865. He was born 15 February 1855 in
   Palermo, son of Angelo Carbonaro and Ignazia Fanara (image-read, §2). The
   "born 1865" came from conflating him with other Brooklyn Pasquale Carbonaros
   born in 1865.
2. His wife is Rosalia MULE (not "Moule"), born in Zisa, Palermo, daughter of
   Vincenzo Mule and Anna Pisciotto (image-read marriage act, §3). She died in
   Palermo on 22 October 1893; Pasquale remarried in Sicily in 1895. There is
   therefore NO Pasquale/Rosalie household in any US census — they never
   emigrated. The US households that exist are those of their children Angelo
   and Ignazia.
