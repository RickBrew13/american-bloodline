# Pasquale Carbonaro — research update

Date: 28 November 2026

## Findings

- Ignazia Carbonaro is named as a daughter in the public Ancestry tree; the Ancestry public profile reports 29 July 1889 in Palermo and parents Pasquale Carbonaro and Rosalie Moule.
- A derivative SortedByName index transcribes a 1972 Ignazia SS-5/NUMIDENT record, names parents Pasquale Carbonaro and Rosalie Moule, and gives birth dates 4 August 1889 / 29 July 1889 in “PALERNO, Italy.” The original application/certificate was not inspected.
- An Ancestry public Angelo Carbonaro search result lists a person born 1 June 1887 in Palermo, deceased July 1978 in Brooklyn, parents Pasquale Carbonaro and Rosalie Moule. A separate derivative transcription of Angelo’s December 1936 SS-5 gives the same date and father but transcribes “BLERMO, Italy” and mother Rosalia Mule. This strongly supports Angelo as another child and likely sibling of Ignazia; underlying originals were not reviewed.
- No additional children of Pasquale and Rosalie were established in the sources examined. Other Carbonaro records on SortedByName name different parents and are not attached.
- Pasquale’s parents and siblings remain unidentified. The 3 Jan. 1865 Messina Pasquale with parents Santo and Antonetta is an unlinked namesake lead; the “Pasquale Carbonar” 1950 Brooklyn census-index hit is also not identified.
- Six small uncaptioned images attached to Ignazia’s Ancestry public profile were reviewed. They are not verified portraits of either parent. No appropriate Find a Grave marker was identified in the earlier audit; no photo was assigned.

## Research limits

No original Italian civil/church record, U.S. census image, or original SSA application was reviewed in this update. No paid record was purchased. Parents/siblings are not invented and no placeholders for unidentified ancestors were created.
