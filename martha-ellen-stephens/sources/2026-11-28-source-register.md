# Source register — Martha Ellen Stephens

Date reviewed: 28 November 2026
- [FamilySearch public Martha Ellen Stephens profile (KN3Y-MPQ)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/KN3Y-MPQ/martha-ellen-stephens-1847-1925) — Compiled profile reports about 13 March 1847–12 June 1925, parents George Washington Stephens and Cyrenia or Serina Baldwin, spouse Nathanial Price, 20 March 1867 marriage, eight children including Lucy, and two siblings. It cites a 1920 census and county marriage; original images not reviewed.
- [FamilySearch public Lucy Jane Price profile (KN3Y-MRJ)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/KN3Y-MRJ/lucy-jane-price-1882-1959) — Compiled child profile identifies Nathanial Price and Martha Ellen Stephens as Lucy’s parents.
- [FamilySearch public George Washington Stephens profile (LBJ6-BX7)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/LBJ6-BX7/george-washington-stephens-1817-1866) — Compiled profile identifies George Washington Stephens and Cyrenia or Serina Baldwin as parents of Martha and lists three children. It cites an 1860 census and Kentucky marriage entry; original image not reviewed.
- [FamilySearch public Cyrenia or Serina Baldwin profile (KN3Y-M51)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/KN3Y-M51/cyrenia-or-serina-baldwin-1820-1877) — Compiled profile reports the 17 March 1847 marriage to George Washington Stephens and three children including Martha; name and death dates vary in the profile’s own report.
- [Find a Grave memorial 51886730, Martha Ellen Stephens Price](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/51886730/martha_ellen-price) — User-contributed memorial reports 13 April 1848–15 June 1926 and burial at Price Family Cemetery, Sadieville, Scott County, Kentucky; one photo is listed.
- [Find a Grave grave photo, Price family cemetery marker](https://images.findagrave.com/photos/2010/120/51886730_127277632930.jpg) — Photo credited to Janet Cochran and added 1 May 2010. The same image is shown on Nathaniel Price memorial 51886498; the marker inscription is faint.
