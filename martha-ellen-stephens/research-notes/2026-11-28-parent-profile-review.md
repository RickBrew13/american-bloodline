# Martha Ellen Stephens — research note

Date: 28 November 2026

- FamilySearch reports Martha’s birth about 13 March 1847 and death 12 June 1925; Find a Grave reports 13 April 1848 and 15 June 1926. Preserve both source-attributed variants; the existing Lucy parent card remains unchanged.

- FamilySearch spells her spouse Nathanial Price; Find a Grave and Lucy’s parent card spell him Nathaniel. Preserve the variant.

- The Price Family Cemetery marker is photographed on both spouses’ memorials and credited to Janet Cochran. The image is visibly a grave marker, but the inscription cannot be read clearly enough to identify the individual names from this copy.

- FamilySearch’s public profile lists two siblings, Lucius or Lucas Stephens and Lucy Ann Stephens. They are compiled family-tree entries, not independently verified here.
