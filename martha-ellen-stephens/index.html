Family Relationship Notes

FamilySearch public profile KN3Y-MPQ identifies her as daughter of George Washington Stephens and Cyrenia or Serina Baldwin, spouse of Nathanial Price and mother of Lucy Jane Price. Her FamilySearch dates conflict with the Find a Grave memorial dates; neither variant is silently corrected.

FamilySearch reports the marriage to Nathanial Price on 20 March 1867 in Scott County, Kentucky and lists eight children including Lucy. The original marriage record was not inspected.

The same Price Family Cemetery grave-photo image appears on Martha’s Find a Grave memorial and Nathaniel’s memorial; the stone’s inscription is too faint to verify individual names from this copy.
