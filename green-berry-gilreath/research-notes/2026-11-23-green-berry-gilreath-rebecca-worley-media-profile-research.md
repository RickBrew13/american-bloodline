# 40. Green Berry Gilreath × Rebecca J. Worley — parent-couple profile evidence and media
Research date: 23 November 2026

## Profile-image decision
Both profiles use the labeled couple/family photograph from Find a Grave memorial 24238045 as the main image, because the caption names “Rebecca & Green with Carl, Glenneth & granddaughter Glenna Lee.” It is a shared family photograph, not an individual portrait. Both profiles also include the shared Gilreath marker at Mill Creek Cemetery. The family photo was added by Wanda Worley on 24 Apr 2014; the memorial does not provide a contemporaneous date or identify all children in the picture. Source: https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/24238045/green-berry-gilreath/photo

## Green Berry Gilreath
FamilySearch’s compiled profile reports 7 Aug 1869–6 Mar 1926, names Benjamin Perry Gilreath and Susan Elizabeth Kidd as parents, Rebecca J. Worley as spouse, and eight children. https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/LZL8-JLG/green-berry-r-gilreath-1869-1926
Find a Grave memorial 24238045 reports dates, Mill Creek Cemetery burial, and a family photo caption identifying Green and Rebecca with Carl, Glenneth, and Glenna Lee. https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/24238045/green-berry-gilreath

## Rebecca J. Worley
FamilySearch’s compiled profile reports 6 Nov 1876–23 Dec 1946, parents Samuel Benjamin Worley and Mary Ann Hamlin, spouse Green Berry, and eight children. https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/LZL8-J5C/rebecca-j-worley-1876-1946
Find a Grave memorial 24238074 reports the same dates and Mill Creek Cemetery burial. Its linked parent/spouse/sibling entries are memorial-family links, not a substitute for original records. https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/24238074/rebecca_j-gilreath

## Direct evidence connecting them to Glenneth
The image of Glenneth’s Kentucky death certificate reproduced on Find a Grave memorial 24238018 was inspected. It names his father “Green B. Gilreath,” mother “Rebecca Worley,” wife “Trula Mae Gilreath,” occupation teacher, death at Marshes Siding, and burial at Mill Creek. Death date reads 21 Jan 1948; handwritten fields require careful reading. This is the best direct parentage bridge located in this pass. https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/24238018/glenneth-gilreath/photo
The same memorial page reproduces Glenneth’s 1942 draft registration: birth 31 Dec 1920, Marsh Siding residence, employer McCreary County Board of Education, and next of kin Trula Mae Gilreath. The article and certificate/draft images are saved in Books & Sources.

## Glenneth’s 1948 clipping
The Commonwealth, Wednesday 28 Jan 1948, p. 3, attached to Glenneth’s Find a Grave memorial. It identifies him as son of late Green B. Gilreath and Mrs. Gilreath, wife Trula Mae Foster Gilreath, and names one daughter, one son, sister Mrs. Walter Strunk and brothers Herbert, Rufus and Delmer. It reports preliminary inquest circumstances; do not repeat those as a proven cause of death. https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/24238018/glenneth-gilreath/photo

## Children recorded in compiled sources
The couple’s FamilySearch profile links Flonnie (1898–1924), Rufus Marion (1900–1972), Herbert R. (1902–1970), Oda Mae (1904–1947), Delmer O. (1908–1989), Elsie (1910–1982), Carl G. Relationship links beyond Glenneth are compiled/memorial evidence; separate sibling profiles are not being created.

## Uncertainty
- Dates and family links are supported by a combination of compiled FamilySearch profiles, Find a Grave memorials, and reproduced original certificate/draft images. The original birth/marriage/death register images for Green and Rebecca were not reviewed in this task.
- Do not assert that every sibling/child link has been independently proved from original records.
- The couple photo is not an individual portrait; label it as a shared family photo wherever used.
