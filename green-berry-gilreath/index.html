Life Story

Green Berry Gilreath was born in the Whitley County area of Kentucky in 1869. A labeled family photograph on his Find a Grave memorial identifies Green and Rebecca with Carl, Glenneth, and granddaughter Glenna Lee; the shared marker is at Mill Creek Cemetery.

A reproduced 1948 Kentucky death certificate for son Glenneth names “Green B. Gilreath” as father and Rebecca Worley as mother. This directly supports Green’s father link. Dates, siblings, and other children are also listed in compiled FamilySearch profiles and memorial family links; their underlying records were not all independently reviewed here.
