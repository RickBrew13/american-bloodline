Family Relationship Notes

Andrew Lyon is Minnie M. Lyon Powell’s father. The records spell his surname Lyon, with no s; the family tree spells it Lyons. His daughter Minnie’s 1903 marriage license names Andrew Lyon as her father, her 1952 death record names Andrew Lyon and Henrietta Green as her parents, and the 1880 census lists 9-year-old Minnie in Andrew and Henrietta’s household in Coal District, Harrison County. Minnie’s birth record, for an unnamed daughter born 11 September 1870 at Lamberts Run, names Andrew Lyon and Henrietta Lyon as the parents.

Andrew married Henrietta Green in Harrison County. The family tree gives 10 March 1865, and the 1900 census says he had been married 34 years, which points to about 1866; no marriage record has been found.

Life Story

Andrew Lyon farmed in Harrison County, West Virginia. He married Henrietta Green, and the 1880 census lists them in Coal District with three of their children, including their daughter Minnie, 9. By 1900 they lived in Clarksburg with three grown children. Andrew died on 31 March 1909 in Hepzibah and is buried in Odd Fellows Cemetery in Clarksburg. His daughter Minnie married Dexter Powell in 1903 and was the mother of Rowland Edmund Powell.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • 25 November 2026: His exact birth and death dates come from his tombstone; no West Virginia death certificate for him has been found. The 1900 census gives his birth as October 1843 and his age as 57; the 1880 census age 34 points to about 1846.
  • The marriage date 10 March 1865 comes from the family tree only; no marriage record for Andrew and Henrietta has been found, and the 1900 census, which says he had been married 34 years, points to about 1866.
  • The family tree names his parents as William Ellis Lyon (1806–1876) and Sarah Denham (1807–); no record naming them has been found. The tree also flags a possible duplicate person, “Andrew Lyon” MKTG-76P.
  • A birth record dated 27 August 1875 names a child “Anderson Lyon” where the tree lists Eustace Lyon; Eustace’s full name may have been Anderson Eustace Lyon, or the tree has a name error. The tree also lists a second child born in 1875 with no name given, who may have been a twin.
