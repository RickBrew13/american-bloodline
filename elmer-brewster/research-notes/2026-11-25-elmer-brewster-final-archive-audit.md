## Evidence status and findings after full pass
### Identity, parents and siblings
- **Identity and parents — strongly supported:** Elmer Ellis Brewster, born 17 January 1935 at Brewstertown, Tennessee, to Homer Brewster and Sarah Kelly, from the indexed Social Security NUMIDENT entry. The original application has not been inspected.
- **Parents and sibling group — independently corroborated:** Elmer's own 1993 notice names Homer and Sarah as his parents and siblings Eugene, Eunice, Homer Jr. and Aileene, plus twin Earl who predeceased him. Sarah's and Homer's obituaries, Eunice's notice, Elmer's notice, and Aileene's 2015 obituary also identify the same family group. This is a strong multi-notice family identification.
- **Twin relationship — strongly supported:** Elmer's obituary explicitly calls Earl his twin; the birth dates and family records agree.

### Birth, census and death
- **Birth:** NUMIDENT indexes 17 January 1935, Brewstertown, Tennessee. 1940/1950 census references are already cited on the profile, but the original population schedules have not been re-read in this final pass.
- **Death:** direct 1993 newspaper notice, Florida death index and SSA index agree on 21 August 1993. The GenealogyBank/FamilySearch obituary index's 17 August date is contradicted by the directly inspected contemporary notice and state/SSA indexes; retain it only as a documented index discrepancy, not as an equal competing death date.
- **Place:** notice gives West Palm Beach as his residence; Florida index gives Palm Beach County as event place. Do not assert the exact death facility or street address without the certificate.

### Marriage and children
- **Mary Jane:** directly documented as his wife at death in 1993. Her maiden name, date of birth/death, marriage date, and whether she is the woman named in the 1951 family account remain unknown. The Mary Jane profile in the shared project appropriately avoids publishing identifying details because she may be living.
- **Ruth Lee Wainscott:** a separately attributed family Facebook account says Elmer and Ruth eloped 17 February 1951, when she was 14 and he was 16; Ruth's existing profile preserves a camper photo caption as a family recollection. This remains FAMILY ACCOUNT, because the original post and a civil marriage record have not been inspected. Ruth's later identity as Ruth Lee Wainscott Moore is supported by her death index, memorial, headstone and later-family obituary evidence, but those do not prove an Elmer marriage.
- **Conflict:** another family account identifies Mary Jane as the 1951 bride. Neither version is settled by the 1993 notice. Do not infer two sequential marriages or claim that Ruth was definitively the first wife. The Mary Jane profile currently phrases the Ruth-first sequence as settled; that wording is not supported by the records reviewed here and should be reviewed in her own profile pass. No change to her profile was made in this Elmer pass.
- **Children:** the original 1993 notice names five children: Cathy Bubolz, Diana Keaton, Michael Brewster, Cheryl Schaefer and Wanda Bilby. It does not name their mother. The family's account identifies Ruth as their mother, but no original birth records or other independent parentage record was examined in this pass. Preserve that attribution as family evidence, not as a proven maternity finding. Living descendants' current contact details are not researched or published.

### Military, occupation, residence and property
- **Military:** family newspaper coverage of twin Earl's Korean War service states that both twins volunteered/enlisted 17 January 1952; Elmer served in California and was not sent overseas. This is contemporaneous family-newspaper reporting, but Elmer's own USMC personnel file or muster roll has not been obtained. Do not claim a unit, rank, campaign, medals or exact service dates beyond what the source says.
- **Occupation:** the Palm Beach Post notice documents printing-firm consulting for over 35 years and supervision of the Lantana Toll Plaza for the final three years of his life.
- **Residence:** the obituary gives West Palm Beach for the last four years and the printed prior place as “Perrysville, Missouri.” Although Perryville is a plausible interpretation, the primary newspaper image itself says Perrysville. No independent residence record has resolved the spelling or location; the profile now retains the newspaper wording as uncertain rather than silently normalizing it.
- **Property:** exact-name searches for Elmer Ellis/Elmer E. Brewster with Palm Beach, West Palm Beach, Lake Worth, and Perryville did not produce an attributable deed, parcel or probate record. No property ownership is claimed.
- **Burial:** newspaper reports planned interment at Lake Worth Memory Gardens. No independent interment register, plot number or individual headstone has been located. South Florida National Cemetery searches did not surface an attributable entry. Do not state completed burial as proven until cemetery records confirm it.
