# Elmer Brewster — Palm Beach Post funeral notice located by Palm Beach County Library, 7 November 2026

## Library response and access
Joe Dellosa, Librarian II (Community Research), Palm Beach County Library System, Government Research Service, replied to Rick Brewster's lookup inquiry and said he located a funeral notice in the 22 August 1993 edition of the *Palm Beach Post*. The library supplied the relevant clip inline as an image and attached a PDF of the newspaper page, at no charge as part of its free research service. The reply stated no fee, identification requirement, or restriction; it invited further questions and offered an optional survey. No order or payment was placed or authorized.

## Direct inspection of the supplied original-page copy and clip
The attached PDF is titled *Palm Beach Post (published as The Palm Beach Post) — August 22, 1993 — page 28* in its NewsBank citation/metadata. The newspaper page itself shows the printed page designation 7B. The inline clip and the page image both show a classified funeral notice headed “ELMER BREWSTER”; this is a contemporary newspaper notice, not a long-form editorial obituary.

The notice states that Elmer Brewster, age 58, of West Palm Beach, Florida, “passed away Saturday, August 21, 1993.” It says he had lived in the area for four years after coming from Perryville, Missouri, had worked as a consultant for various printing firms, and for the last three years had been supervisor for the Lantana Toll Plaza.

The notice names his survivors as wife Mary Jane of West Palm Beach; children Cathy Bubolz of Wellington, Diana Keaton of Cincinnati, Ohio, Michael Brewster of Hawaii, Cheryl Schaefer of Williamsport, Pennsylvania, and Wanda Billy of Williamsport, Pennsylvania; brother Homer Brewster Jr. of Canton, Ohio; sister Aileene Gilpin of West Palm Beach; ten grandchildren; brother Eugene; sister Eunice Burton; and parents Homer and Sarah Brewster. It says twin brother Earl predeceased him. (Names and relationships transcribed from the supplied image; preserve the newspaper's spelling and wording.)

Funeral services were scheduled for Tuesday, August 24, at 11 a.m. at E. Earl Smith & Son Funeral Home Memory Gardens Chapel, 3041 Kirk Road, Lake Worth, Florida, with Rev. Reener R. Richter officiating; interment was to follow at Lake Worth Memory Gardens. This reports planned interment, not independent confirmation of completed burial.
