Life Story

Elmer Ellis Brewster and his twin brother Earl were born on January 17, 1935, in the Tennessee mountains, at Brewstertown, sons of Homer William Brewster Sr. and Sarah Hazel Kelly. DOCUMENTED The family was still in Morgan County, Tennessee, in 1940, and by 1950 had moved north to Cincinnati, Ohio, where Elmer grew up on 12th Street. SUPPORTED The family account says that on February 17, 1951, at sixteen, he eloped with Ruth, who was fourteen. FAMILY ACCOUNT

On their seventeenth birthday in January 1952, Elmer and Earl volunteered for the Marine Corps together; Elmer served in California and was not sent overseas. DOCUMENTED He went on to spend more than 35 years as a consultant to printing firms, setting up new businesses, and lived for a time in Missouri before moving to West Palm Beach, Florida, about 1989. In his last three years he was supervisor of the Lantana Toll Plaza. DOCUMENTED

He died on August 21, 1993, aged 58, survived by his wife Mary Jane, five children, ten grandchildren, his brother Homer Jr. and his sister Aileene. DOCUMENTED His funeral was held at Lake Worth, with burial to follow at Lake Worth Memory Gardens. SUPPORTED

  • Ruth's maiden name, Wainscott, is SUPPORTED by her Find a Grave memorial and two online trees, but no record yet names her as Elmer's wife. A 1951 marriage record would settle it: try Campbell or Kenton County, Kentucky (teenage couples from Cincinnati often married across the river), and Hamilton County, Ohio.
  • Mary Jane's maiden name and their marriage date are not known.
  • Death date conflict: a GenealogyBank obituary index entry gives 17 August 1993 UNPROVED. His obituary, the Florida death index and the Social Security indexes all give 21 August, which is used here.
  • Place of death: the records give his residence (West Palm Beach) and the county of death (Palm Beach); the exact place is not stated. Florida death certificate 95968 would settle it.
