## Target 2 — Col. William Farr's will (Union Co. SC, d. ~1794)
STATUS: partial win — full will text still paywalled, but a FREE official substitute found

### FOUND (free, official): SC House concurrent resolution, 9 Feb 1994 (sess110_1993-1994)
URL: https://www.scstatehouse.gov/sess110_1993-1994/hj94/153.htm
Commemorating the 200th anniversary of Col. William Farr's death; states verbatim:
- "Colonel William Farr departed this life on March 2, 1794"
- "the devoted father of eleven children: Richard, William Black, James, and Hannah Green, who were born to his first wife Eleanor Black Jeter Farr; other children were John Pulaski, Titus Green, Thomas John Smith, Eleanor Thomas, Ann Kincheloe, Elizabeth Frances, and Robert Goodloe Harper, who were born to Colonel Farr's second wife, Elizabeth Taliavo Stribling Farr"
This CONFIRMS Richard Farr as Col. William's son and names Richard's mother: **Eleanor Black Jeter Farr** (first wife). Note: Pharr 1955's transmitted child-list OMITS Richard, but the 1821 equity bills independently prove Richard was Col. William's son and executor — the resolution corroborates.
