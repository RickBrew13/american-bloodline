## Housing Timeline Corrections

Susan was reading the chapter about her dad and found errors:

1. **They didn't move to Indian Hill** — the house was in the Indian Hill school district in Madeira (not Indian Hill itself).

2. **They didn't move there until right before Ruth and Tom got married** — when Susan was 15.

3. **When Susan was born**, Richard and Ruth owned an apartment building in Bond Hill on Catalina Ave. They lived there for a couple years.

4. **Then they sold the apartment building** and bought a house in Roselawn on Blackstone Place. They lived there until moving to Madeira in July 1976.

5. **Ruth and Tom got married** at Kenwood Baptist in the garden on July 10, 1976.

## Richard as Policeman — The Full Cow Story

"When Richard was a policeman there were stockyards downtown. One night they got a call that a cow had escaped from the stockyards and they were dispatched to help find and return it to the stockyards. They found it and when they tried to catch it, it stepped on my father's foot and wouldn't move. His foot hurt for weeks afterward."

## Why Richard Left the Police Force

"He left the police force after his partner was shot and killed. Ironically he wanted a safer job. He then became a truck driver and worked for Roadway."

## How Richard Died — Susan's Account

"The night he died, he was asleep in the sleeper but the other driver fell asleep at the wheel on the Pennsylvania turnpike and drove off a cliff. My father was crushed to death and the other driver jumped out at the last second and lived."

"I was in second grade at the time. I was called out of my classroom to go to the office. I don't remember anything else until we were at his funeral and my uncle (can't remember which one) picked me up to see his body. I remember thinking that it did not look like him. The body was so skinny and my dad was a big man."

"I went to the school psychologist for a long time afterward and the other kids at school thought it was quite amusing to have 'the crazy girl' in their class."

"Mom always told me I built a wall around myself back then that no one could break through. That I would just shut down when there was something I couldn't handle and crawl into my own little shell and stay there."

"I was 7 when he died and it destroyed me. I was a complete and utter daddy's girl. It was always me and daddy, and mom and Tricia. I was his Susie Q."

## Indoor Campouts

"Every weekend that he was home we had an indoor camp out and slept either in the bunk beds in the basement or in sleeping bags in the living room."

## The '57 Chevy — "The Redhead"

"He had a '57 Chevy. Black body with a red roof. He called it the redhead, and we would go for rides in it."
