## 1. Spring Grove Cemetery & Arboretum, Cincinnati, Ohio — PLACE VERIFIED; burials SUPPORTED
- **The place — VERIFIED:** Spring Grove Cemetery and Arboretum, 4521 Spring Grove Avenue, Cincinnati, Ohio. 733 acres; chartered 1845 (first burial Sept 1, 1845); Adolph Strauch's 1855 landscape-lawn design; **U.S. National Historic Landmark District** (designated March 29, 2007); NRHP #76001440 (listed May 13, 1976); Spring Grove Cemetery Chapel separately NRHP-listed (#80003086).
  Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring_Grove_Cemetery ; https://homepages.uc.edu/~ehrlicgn/FINAL/landmark2.html ; https://www.mausoleums.com/spring-grove-cemetery-and-arboretum-mausoleums/
- **Richard's burial — SUPPORTED (book-level):** the book states Richard is "buried at Spring Grove beside her own father" (Ruth's father Dallas). WikiTree confirms death 3 Oct 1968, Cincinnati. **Marker UNVERIFIED:** three separate web searches (2026-10-02) found no Find a Grave memorial for Richard Lawrence Shamblen (1932–1968); the obituaries surfaced were different men (Richard D. Shamblen 1949–2022 of Omaha; Richard G. Shamblen 1937–2015 of Bolivar, OH). No grave-marker photo on file.
- **Dallas Cecil McKinney's burial — SUPPORTED (book-level):** the book states Dallas (d. Dec 1, 1958, at home in Cincinnati) "was laid to rest in Spring Grove Cemetery." Note: several cemeteries share the "Spring Grove" name; the Cincinnati identification rests on the book's Cincinnati context, not an independent record.
- **Photo filed:** `pictures/spring-grove-cemetery-lake-mausoleum.jpg` — neoclassical mausoleum reflected in a cemetery lake with swan, autumn trees. This is an **exact-place photograph** (it depicts Spring Grove Cemetery itself), not a generic substitute; it does not depict any individual grave. Photographer: Carol M. Highsmith; Carol M. Highsmith Archive, Library of Congress (public domain — "No known restrictions on publication" per PICRYL). Caption file: `spring-grove-cemetery-lake-mausoleum.jpg.caption.txt`.

## 2. Fairplain, Jackson County, West Virginia — COMMUNITY VERIFIED; birthplace SUPPORTED
- **The place — VERIFIED:** Fairplain is an unincorporated community in Jackson County, WV — GNIS feature ID **1554431**, 38°45′09″N 81°41′09″W, elevation 755 ft, on I-77 five miles south-southeast of Ripley. Named for the "fair plains" near the original town site (Kenny, *West Virginia Place Names*, 1945, p. 237).
  Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairplain,_West_Virginia ; https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q5430679
- Jackson County place-names (WVJCHS): "Fairplain — name of a village, former post office and elementary school located on a rolling plain 'fair plain' on the headwaters of the north branch of Grasslick Creek, about five miles south of Ripley."
  Source: https://wvjchs.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/PLACE_NAMES_OF_JACKSON_COUNTY.pdf
- **Richard's birthplace — SUPPORTED:** book gives b. Feb 4, 1932, Fairplain, Jackson County, WV; WikiTree agrees (Shamblen-14). No birth record viewed in free sources.
- **Historic buildings in Fairplain — honest negative:** no NRHP-listed or otherwise documented historic building found in Fairplain in free sources (2026-10-02). No photograph filed — no verifiable Fairplain image available from free sources; a stock landscape would add nothing and is omitted deliberately.
