## Certificate image — NOT free
No free image source: FamilySearch's "Ohio Deaths, 1908–1953" ends 1953; "Ohio, County Death Records, 1840–2001" is county probate-court records (wrong state anyway). **Order route:** Pennsylvania Department of Health, Division of Vital Records.
- Fee: **$20 per death certificate** (+ $10 online service fee at mycertificates.health.pa.gov), non-refundable (pa.gov official page, verified 2026-10-18)
- Routes: online at mycertificates.health.pa.gov, by mail (check/money order to "VITAL RECORDS"), or in person at a Vital Records branch office
- Eligibility: PA restricts requesters to qualifying relationships/interests — Rick as a grandson should qualify, but confirm against the eligibility list on the official page (pa.gov/agencies/health/programs/vital-records/death-certificates) before applying. Rick's call on ordering.
