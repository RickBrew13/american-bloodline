Life Story

The 1950 census index places Leon Niles, age 40, born in Alabama, in Birmingham, Jefferson County, Alabama. He is indexed as a married household head working as a baker in the retail bakery industry. Artherine Niles is listed as his wife; Jessie L. Niles, Ronald Niles, and Altheria Niles are listed as his children. The same household roster names Jessie and Altheria as Ronald’s siblings. These details come from an indexed census record; the original schedule has not yet been transcribed from image.

In a 16 February 1959 Alabama marriage index, Ronald C. Niles, age 26, and Leslie M. Collins married in Fort Payne. The index names the groom’s parents as Leon Niles and Artherine E. Crochen. That supports Ronald’s parentage and supplies Artherine’s maiden/birth surname in this record, but it does not establish that Leon and Artherine married each other.

Do not confuse this Alabama Leon (indexed born about 1910 in Alabama; household in Birmingham) with Leon G. Niles of Wellsboro, Pennsylvania (born 16 January 1917 in Pennsylvania). The latter man’s 2010 obituary names parents Mae Henry and Fred Niles and brothers Hobart, Homer, Lloyd, and Jerome; no evidence links that Pennsylvania family to Ronald’s father.
