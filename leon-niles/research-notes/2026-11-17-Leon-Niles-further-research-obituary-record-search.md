Ancestry’s public Leon Niles profile candidate gives Alabama birth 1909, death 19 May 2004, and Ronald C. Niles as a child, while parents are unavailable. This is a promising identity link but has not been proved because the obituary doesn't name Ronald or parents and Ancestry's linked records are behind a membership gate.
