# Leon Niles — identity, parents, children and siblings research
Date: 17 November 2026

## Research target and identity
The public profile Leon Niles is the father identified for Ronald Coleman Niles in an Alabama 1959 marriage index (FamilySearch Q21F-3YS4). The corresponding FamilySearch index details identify Ronald as age 26, giving estimated birth about 1933, and name Leon Niles and Artherine E. Crochen as his parents.

A separate FamilySearch 1950 census index record identifies Leon Niles, age 40 (estimated birth 1910), born Alabama, in Birmingham, Jefferson County, Alabama. His indexed household includes wife Artherine Niles and children Jessie L. Niles, Ronald Niles, and Altheria Niles. Leon is listed as household head, married, occupation baker, industry retail bakery. Ronald is age 17, born Alabama. This is strong evidence that the Leon in the 1959 marriage record is the same Birmingham household father, while the 1950 schedule provides the best current direct identifier.

## Supported family details
- Father of Ronald C. Niles: Leon Niles — DOCUMENTED in the 1959 marriage index and 1950 census index.
- Mother of Ronald: Artherine E. Crochen in the marriage index; Artherine Niles, wife of Leon, in the 1950 census index — DOCUMENTED in those indexes.
- Leon's wife in the 1950 census: Artherine Niles. Their legal marriage itself has not yet been located; do not overstate the marriage index's parental pair as proof the parents were married to each other.
- Children in 1950 household: Ronald (son, age 17), Jessie L. (daughter, age 15), Altheria (indexed as son, age 6). Indexed census household member relations provide the evidence; no independent adult identities located for Jessie or Altheria.
- Leon's own parents: unknown. Do not attach Fred Niles and Mae Henry: those belong to a distinct Pennsylvania Leon G. Niles born 16 January 1917 in Wellsboro, whereas this Alabama Leon is indexed age 40 in April 1950 and born in Alabama.

## Same-name distinction
Leon G. Niles of Wellsboro, Pennsylvania, died 20 July 2010, aged 93. His obituary says born 16 January 1917 in Wellsboro to Fred Niles and Mae Henry; he was married first to Harriet (Klock) and later to Betty (Benson), and was survived by son William and daughters Linda/Janet, plus stepchildren. It says brothers Hobart, Homer, Lloyd and Jerome. Find a Grave's memorial for Fred Harrison Niles reproduces a 1951 obituary naming son Leon G. in Wellsboro. No bridge connects this Pennsylvania Leon to Ronald's Alabama father. Keep their families distinct; do not add the Pennsylvania parents or siblings to the Alabama Leon profile.
