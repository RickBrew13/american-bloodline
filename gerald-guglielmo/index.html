Life Story

Gerald Guglielmo, recorded in the family tree as Gioglio P. Guglielmo, was born in New York on 24 July 1909. He married Frances "Fannie" Cincinnino in the Bronx on 16 February 1935, and they raised their daughters there. In 1950 he worked as a butter printer at a butter market. He died in January 1969. Several of his daughters later settled in the Las Vegas and Pahrump area of Nevada.
