Frances Hoh Mader’s memorial gives her birth in New Richmond, Ohio, and death in Dayton, Kentucky. The memorial narrative says she was daughter of George Hoh and wife of Robert J. Mader; family links provide eight Hoh siblings and three children. A shared marker names Frances (née Hoh) and Robert Joseph.
