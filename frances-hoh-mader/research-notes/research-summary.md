# Frances Hoh Mader — research notes

Frances Hoh (12 Feb 1864–1 Apr 1943), born New Richmond, Clermont County, Ohio; died Dayton, Campbell County, Kentucky; buried Saint Stephen Cemetery, Fort Thomas (Find a Grave memorial 22796455). The memorial narrative says spouse Robert J. Mader and daughter of George Hoh. Memorial family links give parents George Hoh Sr. (1820–1882) and Victoria Werner Hoh (1834–1918); these are contributor-maintained derivative links.

Linked siblings: Burkhart Hoh (1852–1914), Matilda Hoh Prigge (1857–1944), Mary Hoh Deters (1859–1931), Sophia Hoh Prudent (1862–1948), John N. Hoh (1866–1932), Josephine Hoh Weymann (1868–1950), Anna Hoh (1871–1940), and George Ferdinand “Pete” Hoh (1873–1951).

Children in memorial/parish transcription evidence include Marie/Maria Katharine (1894), Michael Franz (1897), Cyrus Johan (1900), Anne/Anna Elisabeth (1903), and Lawrence Joseph (1906). The baptism transcription is derivative; original registers should be checked. Shared stone image labels Robert Joseph (1861–1944) and Frances (née Hoh) (1864–1943), a family/burial image, not a portrait.
