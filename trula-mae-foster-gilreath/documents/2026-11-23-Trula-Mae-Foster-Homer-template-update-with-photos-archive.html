Life Story

A five-generation newspaper photograph reproduced on Manuel Foster’s Find a Grave memorial includes a caption naming “Mrs. Trula Mae Foster Green” of Mason, Ohio. The caption places her in the generation between Manuel Foster and Emma Lee Gilbreath Henry. It is a labeled family image, not an individual portrait.

The 1950 Covington census schedule directly identifies Trula Mae Gilreath as a widowed daughter of Emma B. Foster and lists Jackie and Bobbie as her sons. The census does not establish that Emma B. Foster is the same woman as Emma Lou Gregory or identify Trula’s father. A Beulah Anderson obituary calls Trula a sister and names Manuel L. and Emma Lou Gregory Foster as Beulah’s parents.

The 1948 death certificate image names Trula Mae Gilreath as Glenneth’s wife; his 1948 obituary calls her Mrs. Trula Mae Foster Gilreath. Separate later materials call her Foster Green and Foster Whalen. Keep each source-specific form; do not resolve chronology from names alone.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Name forms in sources: Foster, Gilreath, Green (five-generation photo caption), Whalen (Manuel obituary). Preserve all forms and investigate marriages/name changes; do not treat them as resolved by surname alone.
  • FamilySearch’s reported 29 May 1939 marriage would make Trula 12. No original county marriage record was reviewed; date remains unverified.
  • The 1950 schedule records Trula as a 23-year-old widow and daughter of Emma B. Foster; reconcile Emma B. with Emma Lou Gregory through original records.
  • The 1950 boys Jackie (8) and Bobbie (6) are likely but not yet proved to be Jack Lynden and Robert S. Gilreath; Kathy is compiled-only in the reviewed material.
  • No individually identified portrait of Trula was found. Her profile image is the captioned five-generation group photo with caption and name variants shown openly; do not describe it as a solo portrait.
