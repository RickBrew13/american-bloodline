Life Story

The strongest direct record reviewed for Trula is the 1950 Covington census. It records her as a 23-year-old widow and daughter of Emma B. Foster; it also places Jackie (8) and Bobbie (6), both Gilreath boys, in the household as Emma’s grandsons. The census supports Emma B. Foster as Trula’s mother, but does not establish that she is the same person as Emma Lou Gregory or identify Trula’s father.

FamilySearch’s compiled profile names Manuel Levi Foster and Emma Lou Gregory as Trula’s parents and lists siblings Leamon, Herbert, Clyde, Beulah, and Manuel Jr. These are compiled links pending original-record review. The same compiled profile links Glenneth as spouse and names Emma Lee, Jack Lynden, Robert S., and Kathy as children. The identities of census boys Jackie and Bobbie with Jack Lynden and Robert S. are likely but not yet proved.

The reported 29 May 1939 marriage date conflicts with Trula’s reported age: it would make her 12. No original marriage record has been examined, so the date remains unresolved. Emma Lee’s parentage is supported by the combined compiled-family and memorial evidence, but not documented by an original parent-naming birth record.
