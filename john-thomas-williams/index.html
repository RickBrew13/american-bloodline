Life Story

John Thomas Williams was born 28 March 1892 at Cincinnati, Ohio, the son of John W. Williams and Mary Collins. No birth record has been found for him.

He worked as a truck driver in Cincinnati from 1935 to 1951, living in Mt. Adams at 944 Pavilion and later at 6 Riverview Place. The city directories name his employer as "C J Robson" in 1945.

He and Helen E. Rich had four children: Mary Jane, Kathleen Rita, Margaret "Margy May," and John Thomas Jr. Mary Jane married William P. Palmer in 1938. John died in 1961. No death record, obituary, or burial place has been found for him, and no photograph of him has been found.
