Family Relationship Notes

The 1910 census places Rose in the Columbus household of William and Katie Ritzer with eight siblings. The 1945 Kentucky marriage record for William F. Young and Rose Dague names her parents as William Ritzer and Catherine. Woodrow’s 1936 Ohio marriage record names Miles Dague and Rose Ritzer as his parents. The Find a Grave memorial and Social Security/Ohio death indexes provide her reported death and burial details.

Rose’s birth date is reported as 18 March 1895. Her birthplace is inconsistent: the 1920 census says Germany, while other records point to Ohio. The profile retains the conflict rather than choosing a birthplace without sufficient evidence.

Rose married Miles about 1913–14; no marriage record has been found. After Miles’s death in 1935, she married William F. Young on 23 July 1945 in Newport, Kentucky. The headstone image is for William F. and Rose Dague Young at Eastlawn Cemetery and remains in the hero and gallery.

Life Story

Rose Ritzer was born on 18 March 1895, one of at least nine children of William Ritzer, a native of Ulm in Württemberg, and his wife Katie . She married Miles Dague about 1913, and their son Woodrow was born in Columbus in 1915 .

Widowed in 1935, Rose married William F. Young in 1945 . In 1950 she was working as a press operator at a roller-bearing plant . She died in Columbus in 1986 at 91 and is buried with William at Eastlawn Cemetery .
