Life Story

Rose Ritzer was born on 18 March 1895, one of at least nine children of William Ritzer, a native of Ulm in Württemberg, and his wife Katie DOCUMENTED. She married Miles Dague about 1913, and their son Woodrow was born in Columbus in 1915 SUPPORTED.

Widowed in 1935, Rose married William F. Young in 1945 DOCUMENTED. In 1950 she was working as a press operator at a roller-bearing plant DOCUMENTED. She died in Columbus in 1986 at 91 and is buried with William at Eastlawn Cemetery SUPPORTED.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • No marriage record for Miles and Rose has been found. Rose was 18 at marriage per the 1930 census, so about 1913–14; the unindexed Franklin County marriage books for 1913–15 would settle it.
  • The 1920 census gives her birthplace as Germany; other records point to Ohio.
  • An online tree gives her dates as 1895–1960, which is wrong.
  • Her obituary (Columbus Dispatch, 22 April 1986) has not yet been read.
