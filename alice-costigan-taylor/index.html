Life Story

Alice Costigan was born in Kentucky on 15 October 1904 SUPPORTED. As Alice Costigan she is named in the Kentucky birth index as the mother of Shirley A. Taylor, born in Boone County on 11 August 1933 DOCUMENTED. She and her husband Wilbur K. Taylor lived in Butler, Kentucky, when they announced Shirley's engagement in 1956 DOCUMENTED. She outlived Wilbur by nineteen years and died in Kenton County on 15 September 1995 SUPPORTED.
