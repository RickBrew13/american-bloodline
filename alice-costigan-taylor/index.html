Family Relationship Notes

The Kentucky birth index names Alice Costigan as the mother of Shirley A. Taylor, born 11 August 1933. Contemporary 1956 newspaper engagement notices identify Mr. and Mrs. Wilbur K. Taylor of Butler, Kentucky, as Shirley’s parents. The relationship between Alice and Wilbur is also listed in the Find a Grave memorials, although their marriage record has not been located.

Alice’s birth date is unresolved: the Social Security Death Index gives 1 October 1904, while her Find a Grave memorial gives 15 October 1904. Life Story

Alice Costigan was born in Kentucky on 15 October 1904, a daughter of John Samuel Costigan and Amelia Friend . Her brother Swope named a son Wilbur Taylor Costigan in 1933, a month after Shirley was born . As Alice Costigan she is named in the Kentucky birth index as the mother of Shirley A. Taylor, born in Boone County on 11 August 1933 . She and her husband Wilbur K. Taylor lived in Butler, Kentucky, when they announced Shirley's engagement in 1956 . She outlived Wilbur by nineteen years and died in Kenton County on 15 September 1995 .
