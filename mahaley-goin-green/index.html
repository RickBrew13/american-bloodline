Life Story

Mahaley Goin was born in Tennessee on 31 January 1822 SUPPORTED. She married John Green in Claiborne County on 4 April 1845 DOCUMENTED, and she raised their four children in the hills of Claiborne and Union Counties before the family moved to Knox County, Kentucky DOCUMENTED.

Mahaley died on 24 October 1889 and is buried in Dowis-Lee Cemetery in Knox County. Her son Hewlet named her as "Mahala Goins" on his death certificate DOCUMENTED.
