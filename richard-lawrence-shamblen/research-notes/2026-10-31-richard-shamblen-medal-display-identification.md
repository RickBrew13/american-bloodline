## Identification, top to bottom

- Wings (not a medal): Enlisted Aircrew Badge — qualification badge for enlisted aircrew,
  wings with the U.S. coat of arms in the center. Fits a B-26 tail gunner.
- Top pair, gold frames — unit citations, earned by his unit:
  - Solid blue, left: U.S. Presidential Unit Citation (called Distinguished Unit Citation in
    the Korean War era), same blue ribbon in a gold frame.
  - White with red-and-blue taegeuk, right: Republic of Korea Presidential Unit Citation,
    awarded by South Korea to U.N. Command units for outstanding performance defending Korea.
- Middle bar:
  - Red with white stripes, left: Army Good Conduct Medal, for exemplary conduct, efficiency,
    and fidelity. Air Force personnel received the Army version 1947 – June 1963, which fits
    1951–53 service; the Air Force's own version came in 1963.
  - Blue with gold edges and 2 oak leaf clusters, right: Air Medal, for meritorious achievement
    in aerial flight. Two clusters = 2 additional awards on top of the first, so 3 Air Medals total.
- Bottom bar:
  - Red with wide yellow center, left: National Defense Service Medal, for service during the
    Korean War emergency period.
  - Thin blue-and-white stripes (17 alternating stripes), middle: United Nations Service
    Medal (Korea), for service under U.N. command in Korea.
  - Light blue with white center stripe and 3 bronze stars, right: Korean Service Medal, the
    main U.S. campaign medal for Korea. The stars mark campaigns, one per campaign, so
    3 campaigns — campaign names NOT identified from the photo alone.
