--- PAGE 5 --- "Grandparents Shamblen Side". Richard Lawrence Shamblen (Feb 4, 1932 – May 15, 1968, split-shift driver; veteran; Cincinnati police officer) × Ruth Laverne McKinney (Aug 8, 1933; daughter of Dallas Cecil McKinney × Laverne Hurt).

--- PAGE 8 --- "Great Grandparents Shamblen Side". Arvel Lawrence Shamblen (Jan 20, 1909 Jackson WV – Jan 11, 2001 Fairfield OH; WWII Army, discharged Camp Atterbury Dec 1945; buried Cross Lanes, Kanawha Co WV) × Georgia Mae Fisher (Dec 24, 1909 Fairplain, Jackson Co WV – Jul 3, 1988 Charleston WV; daughter of Virdie Lee × Rhoda Alice Fisher). Children: Richard Lawrence (1932–1968), Betty Carolyn (m. Shircliff), Lowell Harold, Clifford Earl (1938–2023). Arvel's siblings: Bevin Moody, Frankie Odell (Parsons), Orpha Pearl (Casto), Roma LaFayette, Sarah Louise (Foglesong). Georgia's siblings: Velma Alice, Virdie Burton, Paul Carroll, James Weston, Wilma Faye (Beeghly).

--- PAGE 14 --- "Second Great Grandparents Shamblen Side". David Clayton Shamblen (May 26, 1869 – Mar 16, 1947) × Sarah Louise Staats (1871–1933). Claims: David son of "Union cavalry veteran" George (11th WV Cavalry); Sarah daughter of William F. Staats × Emily Jane Casto; married May 31, 1888 (bride 17, father's consent); Sarah's mother "very likely" daughter of Rev. Nicholas L. Casto (needs one more document); Isaac Staats son of pioneer Enoch Staats (NOT Cornelius Staats × Anna Carney as old book claimed); Margaret "Peggy" Parsons died 1855; Jesse B. Shamblin died 1852. Children: Arvel Lawrence, Bevin Moody, Roma LaFayette, Frankie Odell Parsons, Orpha Pearl Casto, Sarah Louise Foglesong.

--- PAGE 24 --- "Third Great Grandparents Shamblen Side". George Washington Shamblen (photo caption: Aug 16, 1837 – Jan 6, 1905) × Sydna Ann Miller (photo caption: Jun 29, 1841 – Sep 11, 1883). Claims: George son of Jesse B. Shamblin × Margaret "Peggy" CASTO (!!); served 11th West Virginia Cavalry; married Sydna March 15, 1858; Sydna daughter of George Stewart Miller × Emily Lane, born June 29, 1841, Goldtown settlement. Children listed: David Clayton (b. May 26, 1869; m. Sarah Louise Staats 1888), Josephine (m. into Casto family), Artemesia M. (m. into Fisher family), DeMarquis L. (went west), Clora B. (youngest; m. into Parsons family). Sydna d. Sep 11, 1883 age 42; George d. Jan 6, 1905 age 67, buried beside Sydna.
