# Philip A. Spiess × Marie L. (Lavanier) Spiess — family research audit

**Research date:** 25 November 2026 (America/New_York)  
**Generation:** VI. The live Gen VI order lists Philip A. Spiess and Marie L. Lavanier Spiess.

## Philip’s parents and siblings

The public FamilySearch tree profile for Philip Adam Spiess (LJLJ-B4C) reports dates 18 September 1906–24 April 1966 and names parents **Adam Spiess (1866–1932)** and **Henrietta Wittner (1867–1931)**. It lists Ohio birth, census and marriage sources, but I did not inspect the original birth record in this pass. Adam’s public tree independently places Philip in a five-child group with Henrietta.

**Working assignment to Adam and Henrietta: approximately 80% confidence; not independently proven by a reviewed birth certificate.** Reciprocal links are published on the Philip profile and the two Gen VII parent profiles.

Philip’s public tree lists four siblings:

- Charles Spiess (1892–1932)
- Louise Spiess (1894–1900)
- Fred A. Spiess (born 21 April 1896; later life/death not shown on the public page)
- Catherine E. Spiess (1898–1957)

The sibling entries are public tree assertions. Related profiles for Fred and Catherine also reproduce the parent pair and family group, but no full original birth/death-record review for all siblings was completed in this pass. They are listed as sibling findings on Philip’s page, not newly created separate profiles.

## Marie’s parents and siblings

Marie’s public FamilySearch profile (GQQ8-L5H) reports birth 4 June 1908, death 29 December 1993, and names **August Lavanier (1888–1953)** and **Mari Karch (1890–1954)** as her parents. August’s public profile reports a 1906 marriage in Cincinnati and the ten-child Lavanier family; Marie’s profile cites an Ohio county-marriage source and census/death-index entries. A SortedByName page transcribing SS-5/NUMIDENT-style data also reports Marie L. Spies/Spiess, born 4 June 1908, child of August F. Lavanier and Mary/Mari Karch.

**Working assignment to August and Mari: approximately 75% confidence.** The names converge in public tree/family-group sources and one derivative Social Security transcription, but no original birth certificate or SS-5 application was inspected. Gen VII links are published reciprocally.

The public tree says **“Siblings (10)”** including Marie, so nine siblings. Its profile’s family timeline names the following children of August and Mari:

- Helen Lavanier (1909–1988)
- Infant Lavanier (1911–1911)
- Augustine Lavanier (born 13 April 1911; later life not fully reviewed)
- John Lavannier (1915–1915)
- Lorraine Germaine Lavanier (1919–1997)
- Antoinette Lavanier (1921–1997)
- Frank Lavanier (1923–; later life not shown on public profile)
- Lawrence Lavanier (1925–1991)
- Yvonne Lavanier (1927–2017)

These are attributed to the public FamilySearch family group. The profile page lists the first names and conceals five of the ten entries under “+5 more”; names above that are visible in its family timeline. They have not all been independently proven by original records. Sibling links were not created as separate profile pages.

## Spiess couple’s children: tree count and obituary variants

The public FamilySearch profiles for both parents count seven children. The visible “Children (7)” list names five: Ronald P. (tree birth year 1935), Jacqueline Spiess (1936–1969), Mary C. Spiess (born 1939), Phyllis A. Spiess (born 1940), and David M. Spiess (tree birth year 1943). The same public family timeline also shows **Mary Spiess** and **Betty J. O’Neal** as child-name entries. On the live couple profiles, those timeline-only forms are kept separately from the five main child entries rather than silently merged.

Ronald’s B.J. Meyer Sons funeral-home obituary (4 February 2024) identifies his mother as “Marie Lavanier Spiess” and names siblings: late David Spiess, Debbie Duncan, Jackie Wells and Phyllis Stump; younger sisters Mary Montgomery and Betty O’Neal. The surname forms may reflect marriages, but the obituary does not supply birth surnames for all siblings or name Philip as Ronald’s father. Plausible but unproved name links include Jacqueline Spiess / “Jackie Wells,” Phyllis A. Spiess / “Phyllis Stump,” and public-tree timeline “Mary Spiess” / obituary “Mary Montgomery” or “Betty J. O’Neal” / obituary “Betty O’Neal.” Debbie Duncan is an obituary-only sibling lead not yet mapped to a named child in the FamilySearch tree.

No separate child profiles were created for these unresolved or potentially private people. Name variants and unknown relationships remain explicitly attributed.

**David date discrepancy:** the existing project profile for David Michael Spiess documents 1 August 1942–11 January 2020 from its source trail; Marie/Philip’s public FamilySearch child entry gives birth year 1943. The 1950 census transcribed age 7 in April is consistent with 1942. Keep the FamilySearch year conflict visible pending an original birth record.

## Marriage evidence

Marie’s public FamilySearch page lists an Ohio County Marriages source. Its underlying entry/image was unavailable to inspect in this pass, so no exact marriage date or certificate wording is entered. The 1950 census places Philip and Marie in one home as parents with their children; the census itself does not prove a marriage. The Gen VI profile describes the indexed marriage source and the shared household distinctly, and requests the original return for date/party verification.
