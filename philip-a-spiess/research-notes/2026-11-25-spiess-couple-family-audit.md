# Philip A. Spiess × Marie L. Lavanier Spiess — couple research audit

**Research date:** 25 November 2026 (America/New_York)  
**Generation VI couple:** Philip A. Spiess × Marie L. Lavanier Spiess

## Existing evidence and marriage status

The existing profiles cite the 1950 census household in Williamsburg Township, Clermont County, Ohio: Philip A. and Marie L. Spiess with sons Ronald P. and David M. The transcription supports their shared household and parental family group, but the census alone is not a marriage record.

Marie’s public FamilySearch profile (GQQ8-L5H) lists an Ohio County Marriages source. That is a useful lead that a marriage record exists in the tree’s source collection, but I could not inspect the entry/image in this pass, so I am not transcribing a date, county or license particulars from it. The live profiles now state this precisely: public tree citation plus shared census support the couple assignment; the original return still needs verification. No claim that no marriage record exists.

Ronald’s 2024 B.J. Meyer Sons obituary directly identifies Marie Lavanier Spiess as his mother and David Spiess as his brother. It does not name Philip as Ronald’s father, so the paternity link remains based on the 1950 household and public FamilySearch parent profile rather than the obituary.

## Philip’s parents and siblings

Philip Adam Spiess’s public FamilySearch profile (LJLJ-B4C) gives parents **Adam Spiess (1866–1932)** and **Henrietta Wittner (1867–1931)**. The same profile lists an Ohio birth source, census and county marriage sources. Adam’s FamilySearch profile independently places Philip in a five-child family with wife Henrietta.

**Working parent assignment: approximately 80% confidence; not directly proved here by a reviewed birth certificate.** Both parents are in Gen VII with linked profiles and reciprocal child link to Philip.

Philip’s public tree shows four siblings: Charles Spiess (1892–1932), Louise Spiess (1894–1900), Fred A. Spiess (1896–; later life not shown in public tree), and Catherine E. Spiess (1898–1957). These are listed as public-tree siblings; I did not independently open original birth/death images for each.

## Marie’s parents, siblings and birth-family group

Marie Louise Lavanier’s public FamilySearch profile (GQQ8-L5H) names **August Lavanier (1888–1953)** and **Mari Karch (1890–1954)** as her parents, gives a birth date of 4 June 1908, and lists a ten-child family group including Marie. It cites census, Ohio county marriage and death-index sources. August’s profile states marriage to Mari in 1906, Cincinnati. A secondary SortedByName page transcribing Social Security/NUMIDENT data also gives August F. Lavanier and Mary/Mari Karch as Marie’s parents, with Marie L. Spies/Spiess as a name form.

**Working parent assignment: approximately 75% confidence; not directly proved here by Marie’s original birth record or SS-5 application.** August Lavanier and Mari Karch are in Gen VII with reciprocal links to Marie. ## Children and name reconciliation

The public FamilySearch profiles for both parents list seven children. The 1950 census project transcription includes sons Ronald P. and David M. in the household. Public FamilySearch child entries display Ronald P. (tree year 1935), Jacqueline Spiess (1936–1969), Mary C. Spiess (born 1939), Phyllis A. Spiess (born 1940), and David M. Spiess (tree year 1943); its public family timeline also displays “Mary Spiess” and “Betty J. O’Neal.” The profile pages have only the first five as the visible “Children (7)” list and add “+2 More Children.” The extra timeline forms are retained separately as child-name leads, not silently merged.

Ronald’s funeral-home obituary lists deceased siblings David Spiess, Debbie Duncan, Jackie Wells, and Phyllis Stump, and younger sisters Mary Montgomery and Betty O’Neal. These forms may connect to the tree’s David, Jacqueline, Phyllis, Mary and Betty forms, but this obituary does not give maiden names or identify parentage. “Jackie Wells” is a plausible reference to Jacqueline Spiess; “Phyllis Stump,” “Mary Montgomery,” and “Betty O’Neal” are possible married-name forms, not yet individually bridged. “Debbie Duncan” is a sibling lead not matched to a named FamilySearch child. No separate profiles were created for these living/private or identity-unresolved names.
