Family Relationship Notes

The 1950 census lists Philip A. and Marie L. Spiess in the same Williamsburg Township, Clermont County, Ohio, household with sons Ronald P. and David M. Ronald’s 2024 obituary names his mother as Marie Lavanier Spiess and identifies David as a brother. These records support the family relationships shown here.

Ronald’s obituary also names Debbie Duncan, Jackie Wells, and Phyllis Stump as siblings who died before him, and Mary Montgomery and Betty O’Neal as younger sisters. They do not appear in the 1950 household. Their relationship to Philip and Marie has not been independently established, so they are retained as research leads rather than listed as confirmed children.

No marriage record has been located for Philip and Marie. The census records them in one household as parents of Ronald and David, but does not itself prove a marriage. Philip’s parents, siblings, life dates, occupation, and burial remain unknown.

Life Story

Philip Adam Spiess (18 September 1906–24 April 1966) is identified as the son of Adam Spiess and Henrietta Wittner in his public FamilySearch tree and as a member of their five-child family group. In the 1950 census he appears in Williamsburg Township, Clermont County, Ohio, with Marie L. Spiess and sons Ronald P. and David M. Ronald’s 2024 obituary identifies Marie as his mother and names several siblings. The profile now preserves both the FamilySearch count of seven children and the obituary’s married-name sibling leads without assuming the two unnamed tree children are Debbie Duncan and Betty O’Neal.

No birth date or place, marriage record, death date or place, or grave has been found for Philip. He is known only from the 1950 census household and his sons’ records.
