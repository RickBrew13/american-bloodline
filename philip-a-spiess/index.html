Family Relationship Notes

The 1950 census transcription places Philip A. and Marie L. Spiess in the same Williamsburg Township, Clermont County household with sons Ronald P. and David M. Ronald’s 2024 obituary directly names Marie Lavanier Spiess as his mother and David as his brother, but does not identify Philip as father.

Philip’s public FamilySearch profile (LJLJ-B4C) names Adam Spiess and Henrietta Wittner as his parents and lists seven children with Marie. Adam’s FamilySearch profile also places Philip in their five-child group. This supports a working parent assignment to Adam and Henrietta (~80%), not an independently reviewed birth certificate.

Marie’s public FamilySearch profile cites an Ohio County Marriages source. I could not inspect the underlying record; the public-tree spouse link and shared 1950 household support the couple assignment, but neither gives a verified marriage date in this review.

FamilySearch lists five child names and counts seven; its family timeline additionally displays Mary Spiess and Betty J. O’Neal. Ronald’s obituary uses married-name forms Mary Montgomery, Betty O’Neal, Jackie Wells, Phyllis Stump and Debbie Duncan. These possible matches are retained as attributed leads, not proven aliases or parent-child links.
