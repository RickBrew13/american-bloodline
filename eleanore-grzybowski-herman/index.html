Life Story

Eleanore R. Grzybowski was born 24 June 1915 in Detroit, Wayne County, Michigan, the daughter of Francis Grzybowski and Josephine M. Ryniecki SUPPORTED. She married Lawrence Herman in Detroit on 22 January 1934, and the two appear together with their children in the 1940 census in Detroit SUPPORTED. After Lawrence died in 1943, Eleanore married John Louis Krause; by 1950 the two were living in Detroit with her daughters Rosemary, Elenore, Janet, and Victoria Herman SUPPORTED. Eleanore died 24 February 1990 in Dearborn, Michigan, at age 74; her daughter Victoria later married Patrick L. Moore SUPPORTED.
