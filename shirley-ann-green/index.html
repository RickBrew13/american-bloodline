Life Story

Shirley Ann Taylor was born in Boone County, Kentucky, on 11 August 1933, the daughter of Wilbur Kyle Taylor and Alice (Costigan) Taylor DOCUMENTED. She graduated from Holmes High School and attended Western Kentucky State College SUPPORTED. In August 1956 her parents, then of Butler, announced her engagement to Fairest Richard Green, with the wedding set for 8 September at the Methodist church in Falmouth DOCUMENTED. She was the wife of Fairest Green and the mother of seven: Michael, Mardi, Michelle, Rusty, Mary Jo, Eddie, and Maggie. She died on 11 September 2015 SUPPORTED.
