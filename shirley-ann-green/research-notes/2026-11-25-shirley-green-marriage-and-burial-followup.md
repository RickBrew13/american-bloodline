# Shirley Ann (Taylor) Green — marriage and burial follow-up
**Date:** 25 November 2026  
**Folder-first review:** Shirley's profile, shared Fairest–Shirley archive reference, existing life/photo audit, and the profiles of her parents Wilbur and Alice were reviewed before new searches. The individual and shared archive contain the existing memorial/context image(s), but no independently authenticated portrait beyond the profile's current image. No new image was found or copied.

## Marriage-date evidence clarified
The Cincinnati Enquirer engagement announcement of 7 August 1956 (p. 16) states that Shirley Ann Taylor and Fairest Richard Green planned to marry on **8 September 1956** at Falmouth Methodist Church, Falmouth, Kentucky. The Kentucky Post printed a matching engagement notice on 3 August 1956.

Shirley's 11 September 2015 Dignity Memorial obituary directly identifies her as the beloved wife of the late Fairest Green. This proves they did marry, but the engagement announcement alone does not prove that the wedding occurred on the scheduled date. No civil marriage certificate has been located in the accessible records. The profile now distinguishes the planned date from the confirmed marriage relationship.

## Death and inurnment
The Dignity Memorial obituary gives:
- Born 11 August 1933.
- Died Friday 11 September 2015.
- Beloved wife of the late Fairest Green.
- Children: Michael, Mardi, Michelle, Rusty, Mary Jo, Eddie and Maggie.
- Fifteen grandchildren and three great-grandchildren.
- Graveside service and inurnment at Rest Haven Memorial Park, 10209 Plainfield Road, Blue Ash, Ohio, on 25 September 2015.

Source: https://www.dignitymemorial.com/obituaries/reading-oh/shirley-green-6593687

The existing memorial page also refers to Whispering Winds as the scattering garden for her cremains. That detail is preserved as memorial-page information; the contemporary obituary specifically says “inurnment” at Rest Haven. No cemetery register, niche/plot number or marker has been inspected. Do not state that the cremains were both scattered and inurned without the cemetery record.

## Family and household review
The 1940 census index and Alice Costigan Taylor's 1950 census entry place Shirley with parents Wilbur Kyle Taylor and Alice Costigan. No additional children/siblings appear in the household indexes reviewed. This does not prove she was an only child; no sibling obituary or birth record naming another child was found. Keep siblings unknown rather than declaring none.

The 2015 obituary names all seven children and the number of grandchildren/great-grandchildren. No new parentage or descendant links were established in this pass.
