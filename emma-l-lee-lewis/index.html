Family Relationship Notes

The 1950 census directly records Emma L. Lewis, age 59, as wife of James S. Lewis in Lesterville Township. Her estimated birth year is about 1891 and her birthplace is recorded as Missouri. A derivative transcription of Arnold Floyd Lewis’s 1941 SS-5 gives his mother’s name as Emma L. Lee, which is consistent with Emma L. Lewis but does not itself prove the maiden-name form from an inspected original.

The couple’s 1950 census sheet lists daughter Lora B. (23; surname appears as James), and granddaughters Sharon L. and Karen L. (both 5). Arnold Floyd is linked as their son by the SS-5 transcription; the original application remains to be reviewed.
