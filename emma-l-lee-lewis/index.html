Family Relationship Notes

The 1950 census directly records Emma L. Lewis, 59, as wife of James S. Lewis in Lesterville Township. A derivative transcription of their son Arnold Floyd Lewis’s 1941 SS-5 gives his mother’s name as Emma L. Lee. Arnold’s 2000 obituary also identifies his parents as James S. and Emma née Lee and names their children. The combined evidence supports Emma’s connection to Arnold, while the original SS-5 remains uninspected.

Find a Grave identifies Emma L. (Lee) Lewis as 11 February 1891–4 May 1975 and shows her shared marker with James Samuel Lewis at Rayfield Cemetery. The exact dates and burial are from that memorial/marker; the 1950 ages are consistent.

The 1950 census lists daughter Lora B., 23, surname rendered as James in the index, and two granddaughters. Arnold’s 2000 obituary names sister Bernice Durham. Find a Grave’s Lora Berniece Lewis Durham memorial links her to James and Emma and to Arnold, likely resolving this daughter/sibling identity.

Life Story

Emma L. Lewis, identified as Emma L. Lee by her son Arnold’s SS-5 transcription and as Emma née Lee in his 2000 obituary, lived with husband James S. Lewis in Lesterville Township in 1950. Find a Grave’s shared marker gives her dates as 11 February 1891–4 May 1975 and burial at Rayfield Cemetery. Her parents and siblings remain unidentified in the records reviewed.

  • Emma’s maiden name “Lee” is supported both by Arnold’s SS-5 transcription and his obituary’s “Emma, née Lee.” The original SS-5 application remains uninspected.
  • The census indexes daughter Lora B. as Lora B. James. The 2000 obituary and Lora’s Find a Grave memorial strongly suggest she is Lora Berniece Lewis Durham, but the original birth-name/marriage record has not been reviewed.
  • Arnold’s 2000 obituary names the other children/siblings listed above. No separate sibling profiles were created; Emma’s own parents and siblings remain unknown.
