# Emma L. (Lee) Lewis — Gen VII source and family review

**Reviewed:** 24 November 2026.

## Evidence

The NARA original 1950 census image, Reynolds County, Missouri, ED 90-5, sheet 26 (enumerated 26 April 1950), lists Emma L. Lewis, age 59, as wife of James S. Lewis, age 58, in Lesterville Township; daughter Lora B., age 23 (surname appears as James), and granddaughters Sharon L. and Karen L., both age 5, are also present. Missouri birthplaces and approximate birth years come from the same census.

A secondary transcription of son Arnold Floyd Lewis's 1941 SS-5 (66353334323) gives his mother as Emma L. Lee. This supports the maiden-name form for this Emma but the original SS-5 is not yet inspected.

## Children / sibling question

- Arnold Floyd Lewis (working identity), linked to Gen VI Arnold Lewis.
- Lora B. (recorded/indexed as James), listed as daughter on the 1950 census; likely Arnold's sister based on shared parents, with name history unproved.
- Two granddaughters also listed; their parents are not determined here.

Emma's parents, siblings, birth/death dates beyond census estimates, and burial remain unknown. No Gen VIII profiles or sibling profiles were created.
