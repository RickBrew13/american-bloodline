Family Relationship Notes

  • The 1880 census places Mike as a child in the household of Andrew M. Stephens and Eliza in Pine Knot, Whitley County, Kentucky. His death certificate instead names James A. and Liza Stephens; the conflicting parent names remain unresolved.
  • Mike married Sylvania McCartt on 4 February 1910 in Scott County, Tennessee. The 1910 and 1920 censuses show him raising Sylvania’s two older children, Beatrice and Willie, as stepchildren.
  • Mike and Sylvania’s children included Homer, Christine, and Mary Jane. The reported later marriage to Mary Jane Duncan, about 1941, remains supported by compiled research but needs a direct marriage record.
  • His birth state conflicts between the Tennessee death certificate and the 1910 census, which gives Kentucky. The exact birthplace is not established.

Life Story

Mike Stephens was born on 15 May 1879 , most likely the son of Andrew M. Stephens and Eliza Childress of Pine Knot, Kentucky, just over the Tennessee line . In 1910 he married Sylvania McCartt in Scott County, Tennessee, and raised her children Beatrice and Willie with their own children, Homer, Christine and Mary Jane .

Mike spent the rest of his life around Huntsville, Tennessee. He died there on 19 February 1954 and is buried in Harness Memorial Cemetery .
