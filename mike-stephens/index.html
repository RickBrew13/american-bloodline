Life Story

Mike Stephens was born on 15 May 1879 DOCUMENTED, most likely the son of Andrew M. Stephens and Eliza Childress of Pine Knot, Kentucky, just over the Tennessee line SUPPORTED. In 1910 he married Sylvania McCartt in Scott County, Tennessee, and raised her children Beatrice and Willie with their own children, Homer, Christine and Mary Jane DOCUMENTED.

Mike spent the rest of his life around Huntsville, Tennessee. He died there on 19 February 1954 and is buried in Harness Memorial Cemetery DOCUMENTED.
