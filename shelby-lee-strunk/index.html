Family Relationship Notes

Shelby’s delayed-birth record is transcribed as born 13 July 1890 in Huntsville, son of Gideon Strunk and Martha Jenkins. His 1965 obituary names five daughters and four sons; Flora McCartt is one of the daughters. It identifies his widow as Ada Pearl. Find a Grave links Nellie Phillips as his earlier wife and lists Flora, Seigal, Rev. Leslie Earl, Pearl and Harold among children, but the 1965 obituary uses “Earl Strunk” separately; that identity match remains unproved. Shelby’s obituary also names a brother “Zeke” and sisters Josie, Occie and Bell; Find a Grave’s linked sibling set offers possible matches, but I have kept the forms distinct. A Find a Grave photo labeled “Death Certificate for Shelby Strunk” actually displays a certificate for Walter Lee Fink, so it is not used as evidence for Shelby. The separate delayed-birth image was not legible in the web-extracted text; rely on the typed record transcription with this caveat. A Find a Grave photo-tab image associated with Shelby’s memorial is described by web image-text extraction as a shared marker for Ada Pearl and Shelby Lee, but the extracted dates conflict with the delayed-birth record and obituary; I do not use its transcribed dates as facts.

Life Story

Shelby Lee Strunk was born 13 July 1890 in Huntsville, Scott County, Tennessee, according to a delayed-birth record transcription that names Gideon Strunk and Martha Jenkins as parents. His 1965 obituary reports that he was survived by his wife Ada Pearl, four sons and five daughters, including Flora McCartt of Detroit. Shelby’s Find a Grave memorial links Nellie Phillips as his earlier wife and links additional children; those family-group relationships are derivative and are distinguished from the obituary’s exact name forms. Flora’s connection to Nellie is a probable working assignment, not a proven parent-child record.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The 1965 obituary lists five daughters and four sons; preserve its forms. “Earl Strunk” may correspond to Rev. Leslie Earl Strunk (1912–2005) in Find a Grave links, but this identity is not proved.
  • The obituary’s “Zeke,” “Josie,” “Occie,” and “Bell” may be General/Ezekiel, Josephine, Osey Violet, and Verde Belle in Find a Grave, but do not merge these name forms without a record bridge.
  • The 1940 census places Shelby with wife Pearl, Ruby age 11 and Harold age 10. The household supports an association with Ada Pearl but does not establish the maternity of every child.
  • Flora is directly named as Shelby’s daughter by his obituary. Nellie is a probable mother based on the linked family group, timing, and Seigal’s records; original birth evidence has not been reviewed.
  • One Find a Grave image captioned as Shelby’s death certificate is actually an image for Walter Lee Fink. It has been excluded. The attached delayed birth image did not yield readable text here; the typed transcription remains a derivative source.
  • Find a Grave lists eight siblings and Shelby’s obituary names one brother and three sisters. Potential name matches remain unresolved; see profile family list and source notes.
  • The Find a Grave image associated with Shelby’s memorial appears to be a shared grave marker; image-text extraction gives dates conflicting with the delayed-birth record and obituary. Do not normalize the discrepancy until the photo can be visually inspected.
