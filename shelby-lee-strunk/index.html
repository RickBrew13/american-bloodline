Life Story

Shelby Lee Strunk was born in Huntsville, Scott County, Tennessee, on 13 July 1890, the son of Gideon Strunk and Martha Jenkins . With his first wife, Nellie Phillips, he raised a large family that included Flora, Seigal, and Leslie Earl . He later married Ada Pearl, who survived him when he died on 5 November 1965 .
