# Shelby Lee Strunk — Flora and family review (25 November 2026)

Shelby’s 1965 obituary identifies Flora McCartt as his daughter, and names five daughters/four sons. Flora’s mother is probably Nellie Phillips, not proven by a direct child record. Shelby’s Find a Grave memorial links Nellie and Ada Pearl as wives and lists child/sibling relationships; these remain derivative where not directly corroborated.

The obituary’s “Earl Strunk” may be Rev. Leslie Earl Strunk in Find a Grave, but that is not established. The obituary’s sibling names “Zeke,” “Josie,” “Occie,” and “Bell” may correspond to linked memorials for Ezekiel, Josephine, Osey Violet, and Verde Belle, but remain separate name forms pending a bridge.

A photo labeled on Find a Grave as Shelby’s death certificate is actually for Walter Lee Fink and was excluded. The page’s separate delayed-birth image was not legible in the image extraction, so the typed transcription is retained with qualification.
