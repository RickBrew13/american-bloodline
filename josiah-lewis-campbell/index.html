Life Story

FamilySearch’s published profile for Josiah identifies him as Josiah Lewis Campbell, born in March 1825 in Casey County and deceased there on 1 February 1907. The Find a Grave memorial uses the shorter name Josiah Campbell, gives years 1825–1907, and places him at Pine Grove Church Cemetery.

James Logan Campbell’s FamilySearch profile names Josiah Lewis Campbell and Lenora Harrison as his parents. FamilySearch’s own Josiah and Lenora profiles repeat that couple and list James among their children. A WikiTree descendant page also places Josiah S. Campbell and Lenora Harrison as grandparents in the same Casey County family network. These are compiled or contributor-maintained trees, not a direct parentage record; the relationship is retained as the existing working family link with that limitation explicit.

Research Notes and Open Questions

The parent-child links to James and spouse link to Lenora are represented in the project because they are already present in the published FamilySearch family profiles and are repeated in related compiled family trees. No original record directly naming James’s parents was inspected in this pass.

The identity of Josiah S. Campbell in WikiTree Campbell-15201 is not assumed to be the same household as the FamilySearch Josiah Lewis Campbell: WikiTree lists a different spouse (Leah Harrison) and different children. Preserve this conflict pending an identity bridge.

Find a Grave’s memorial photo is a grave marker, not a portrait. No individual portrait was found.
