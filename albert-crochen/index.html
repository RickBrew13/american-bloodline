Life Story

Albert Crochen was the father of Artherine E. Crochen. The 1920 census lists him in Hayneville, Lowndes County, Alabama, age 35, with his wife Callie, 30, and four children: Fannie, 12; Albert Jr., 7; “Artha Rene,” 9; and Anna May, 4. The enumerator wrote the family name “Crackeson” and the daughter’s name “Arta Rene” — the rendering of Artherine. Artherine’s 1973 Alabama death record names her father as “Albert Crocker” and her mother as “Callie Martin,” with her spouse Leon Niles. His FamilySearch profile gives his name as “Albert L. Crocker” and his wife as “Collie” Crochen. The family surname is spelled Crochen, Crochan, Crocker, and Crackeson across records — same family, spellings carried as recorded.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The family surname varies by record — Crochen, Crochan, Crocker, Crackeson. Spellings are carried as recorded, not smoothed over; the death record’s “Crocker” is from the index, and the certificate image is restricted, so it should be checked at a FamilySearch center or affiliate library.
  • The tree profile’s dates “1889–1889” for Albert are not used. They are internally impossible — FamilySearch itself flags “Birth after Father’s Death” three times — and the records say he was 35 in the 1920 census (born about 1885) and died in 1962.
  • The 1920 census shows one son, Albert Jr., age 7. The tree lists a son “Albert Lewis (1909–1973)” and separately a son “Albert (1911–).” Whether those are the same son is not settled; no fifth child is invented here.
  • No Alabama birth record for Artherine (about 1909) has been found. Albert’s own parents are not yet recorded.
