Life Story

Albert Crochen was the father of Artherine E. Crochen. The 1920 census lists him in Hayneville, Lowndes County, Alabama, age 35, with his wife Callie, 30, and four children: Fannie, 12; Albert Jr., 7; “Artha Rene,” 9; and Anna May, 4. The enumerator wrote the family name “Crackeson” and the daughter’s name “Arta Rene” — the rendering of Artherine. Artherine’s 1973 Alabama death record names her father as “Albert Crocker” and her mother as “Callie Martin,” with her spouse Leon Niles. His FamilySearch profile gives his name as “Albert L. Crocker” and his wife as “Collie” Crochen. The family surname is spelled Crochen, Crochan, Crocker, and Crackeson across records — same family, spellings carried as recorded. By 1930 the family had moved to Birmingham, Jefferson County, where Albert worked in a steel plant; the 1930, 1940 and 1950 censuses all place them there. The 1900 and 1910 censuses place Albert as a son in George and Hannah Crocheron’s Hayneville household, and his 1962 death index names George as his father.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • 21 November 2026 — parents now supported. The 1870, 1880, 1900 and 1910 censuses all place Albert in George and Hannah Crocheron/Crochen’s Hayneville household, and Albert’s 1962 death index names his father as George Crochen. George and Hannah are now listed as his parents at SUPPORTED. One reading of the 1910 image instead labels Albert son-in-law (which would make George Callie’s father) — the index labels him “Son,” the 1900 census labels him “son,” and the death index names George as his father, so the weight is on George as Albert’s father; the conflicting reading is carried here, not hidden. The Ancestry extract naming Henry Crochen as father of an Albert born Hayneville 1880, died 1962, was checked on FamilySearch and not found; Henry remains UNPROVED and is not the same man as George. Eddie Mae (born Bowie) was Albert’s sister-in-law; the children were his nephews and niece. Joe Bell was not found in any search. SUPPORTED

The only 1906 Lowndes marriage for an Albert is Albert Crocheron to Mattie Martin, 28 November 1906 — a different woman; do not treat Mattie as Callie.
