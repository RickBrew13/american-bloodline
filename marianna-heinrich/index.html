Life Story

Marianna Heinrich was born on 3 November 1836 in Kleinjetscha, in the Banat — a date carried from Dietmar Giel’s Familienbuch, entry P53, and not yet checked against a church register. She was the daughter of Peter Heinrich and Anna Maria Moling of Kleinjetscha; her parents are documented from the original parish registers — Peter baptized 21 October 1814, Anna Maria baptized 7 January 1818, married 15 February 1835. No photograph of Marianna has been found.

Marianna married three times. Her first husband was Georgius Benz, married before 1860; she was his widow when she married Philipp Palmer on 31 January 1860 at the Catholic parish of Kleinjetscha — the marriage register entry, entry #1, page 14, is the couple’s founding record. They had six children: two whose names have not yet been recovered, born about 1860–1863; Philippus, born 29 September 1864; Anna, born 9 December 1866, who died on 19 April 1868; Josef, born 28 April 1869, who died on 21 November 1872; and Gertrud, whose birth date is cut off in every recovered text window of the Familienbuch entry. Philippus married Barbara Wagner; he is the direct line.

Philipp died between 1860 and May 1883, and Marianna married a third time, to Nicolaus Konrad, in 1883 — the record names her as the widow of Philipp Palmer, aged 47. She lived on until 21 February 1916, by the date carried in Giel’s Familienbuch; her burial entry has not been pulled.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Grades on this page: DOCUMENTED means an original record was read (the marriage register entry; the Kleinjetscha registers on FamilySearch film 858420); SUPPORTED means a claim attested by filed research — Giel’s Familienbuch, the signed-in tree checks, the bracketed death window — but not by a register read here; UNPROVED means a claim carried from the research folder with no corroboration found.
  • Parentage: Marianna’s link to Peter Heinrich × Anna Maria Moling is documented from the original registers, read from the images on FamilySearch film 858420 (see Sources). The register spells Anna Maria’s surname Molling; the family form is Moling. Philipp’s parents, Peter Palmer × Elisabeth Reisz, are documented in his own marriage register entry — but whether Peter’s wife was Elisabeth Reisz or Magdalena Mayer is open: the Luxembourg-Banat extraction (luxracines family F8357, as given in the research folder) shows Peter Palmer (born about 1797, died 1 May 1865, married 7 January 1821) × Magdalena Mayer. A full read of Giel’s Palmer entries (P49–P57) would settle it. The Each Generation page carries Peter × Elisabeth Reisz at the next generation back.
  • Three marriages: Georgius Benz before 1860 (she is named his widow in the 1860 register; the marriage record itself has not been found); Philipp Palmer, 31 January 1860, documented; Nicolaus Konrad, 1883 (listed as “vidua Philippi Palmer,” 47; the record itself has not been pulled and may name Philipp’s death date). Her first husband’s name is Benz — the marriage register spells Benz, and Familienbuch snippets reading “Pencz” or “Pentz” misread the register.
  • Birth and death dates: Marianna’s dates (3 November 1836 – 21 February 1916) come from Giel’s Familienbuch, entry P53, and have not been checked against the church registers; her burial entry has not been pulled. Philipp’s birth date (22 August 1837) is likewise from Giel P53, unchecked; his death is bracketed only, married 1860 and his widow remarried in 1883.
  • Children: six per Giel P53, recovered by snippet-mining on 23 October 2026 — the Familienbuch is search-only online and no full page of P53 has been displayed. The two oldest children’s names have not been recovered, and Gertrud’s birth date is cut off in every recovered text window. Their baptisms sit in the Iecea Mică baptism register 1852–1876 (Romanian National Archives, cota TM-F-00004-01194-182-6), held staff-only online; a request for it is on hold. Anna and Josef both died young, in 1868 and 1872. Geni and FamilySearch list only Philippus as a child.
  • Siblings: Marianna’s siblings are not recorded in this folder. Philipp was the seventh child of Peter × Elisabeth, but his brothers’ and sisters’ names have not been recovered either. No Siblings card is shown.
