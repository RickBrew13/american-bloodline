Life Story

Lucy Brown was born about 1830 in the Sequatchie Valley of Tennessee, the daughter of David W. Brown and Mary "Polly" Brown — a link carried as supported, not proved: no record explicitly states she was their daughter and Mary Catherine's mother. The link rests on her daughter Mary Catherine's sworn 1907 statement, which names Dave and Polly Brown as her maternal grandparents and lists their children, and on the family register in the Lovett Reed pension file, which gives a daughter Lucy born 15 May 1830. About 1846 she married William Patton. Their daughter Mary Catherine was born on 12 January 1847.

Lucy died on 26 January 1847, two weeks after her daughter's birth, aged about sixteen. No death record survives — Bledsoe County's early records were lost in the 1909 courthouse fire — and no stone or memorial for her has been located. Mary Catherine was raised by her mother's family. No photograph of Lucy has been found.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Birth: about 1830, from the family register in the Lovett Reed pension file (Lucy born 15 May 1830) — transcription-grade only; no birth record exists. The page uses about 1830.
  • Death: 26 January 1847, two weeks after Mary Catherine's birth, on her daughter's sworn statement; no death record exists. An earlier proposal dating her death to 1852 was withdrawn.
  • The 1850 census question: the 1850 census lists a Lucy, aged 19, in David W. Brown's household. A woman who died in January 1847 cannot be enumerated in October 1850. Two readings circulate: Mary's page reads the 1850 Lucy as a younger sister, also named Lucy; the October 2026 correction reads the 1850 Lucy as the 1830-born Lucy. The pension-file register lists only one Lucy (born 15 May 1830), and Mary's application names Lucy once among the seven children. The same household also lists an infant Lucy, five months old, whose parentage is unresolved. The question is unresolved — younger sister, census error, or a wrong death date — and the page carries the 1830 birth and 1847 death on the sworn statement and the pension register, with the census Lucy flagged as a conflict.
  • Parentage: David W. × Polly Brown is supported, not proved — no record explicitly states "Lucy, daughter of David and Polly, mother of Mary Catherine." The link rests on Mary's sworn statement naming Dave and Polly as her maternal grandparents and listing their children, all seven of whom appear in the 1850 household.
  • Polly's maiden name: Reed. Geni displays "(Thomson)" but its About text says no surname should be asserted; "formerly Polly Reed" was read on the original pension file (W5671 image 24, Jan 1841 testimony). The page carries her as Polly Reed.
  • David's parents: unproved. Thomas "Pompey" Brown × Lucy Gentry is a working hypothesis only; Mary's oath does not say whose parent Pompey was. Pompey appears in no other record found.
  • Siblings: the six named in Mary's application besides Lucy — Tom, Lark, Peggy, Barbara, Betsy, Becky — match the 1850 household (Thomas, Larkin K., Margaret, Barbery, Rebecca, Elizabeth).
