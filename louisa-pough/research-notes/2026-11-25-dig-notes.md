# Louisa Pough — dig notes, 25 November 2026

Louisa Pough, born about 1852 in Georgia, is the mother of Winnie Pough (Pew), named on Winnie's 1930 Alabama death record (FamilySearch ARK 1:1:JDGC-3RQ), which spells her "Louiser Pough."

- 1900 census, Precinct 2 Enon, Bullock County, ED 27, sheet 13B, household 259 (FamilySearch ARK 1:1:M9ZR-3RG): Louisa J Pough, 48, wife of Ben Pough, 52, head, farmer; daughter Minnie Pough, 36, single, living with them.
- Her maiden name is not established: her son Edmond's death record gives her as Louisa James, and her daughter's death record gives her as Louiser Pough.
- Ben and Louisa are recorded as married 20 years in 1900 (about 1880), yet Winnie was born in 1864, so Louisa may be a stepmother or the marriage year is wrong.

Next records: Louisa in the 1880 census; her death record; a record naming Winnie's mother before 1880, which would decide mother vs stepmother.
