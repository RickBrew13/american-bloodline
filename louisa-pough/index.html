Family Relationship Notes

Louisa Pough is named only as the mother of Winnie Pough on Winnie’s 1930 Alabama death record, which spells her “Louiser Pough,” and as Ben Pough’s wife in the 1900 census, which spells her “Louisa J Pough.” No birth, marriage, or death record for Louisa herself has been found.

She was born in Georgia about 1852, according to the 1900 census (age 48). Her maiden name is not established: her son Edmond’s death record gives her as Louisa James, and her daughter’s death record gives her as Louiser Pough.

Ben and Louisa are recorded as married 20 years in 1900, which would put the marriage about 1880 — yet Winnie was born in 1864, so Louisa may be a stepmother, or the marriage year is wrong.

A Willie Pough, 17, is listed as Ben’s son in the 1900 census; Winnie’s son Willie has birth years drifting across records (1882–1887), so it is not settled whether the 1900 Willie is Ben’s son or Winnie’s son.

Life Story

Louisa Pough was born in Georgia about 1852 and married Ben Pough, a farmer in Bullock County, Alabama. Their daughter Winnie was born in Bullock County in July 1864. By 1900 Louisa was living with Ben at Enon, with Winnie, then single with young children, under their roof. Whether Louisa was Winnie’s mother or stepmother is not settled — the census says Ben and Louisa had been married twenty years, but Winnie was born sixteen years before that. Her death record has not been found.
