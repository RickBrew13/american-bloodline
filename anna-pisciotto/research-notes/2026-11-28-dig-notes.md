# Anna Pisciotto — dig notes
Date: 28 November 2026. FamilySearch signed-in pass (RickBrewster1), images read and re-read letter by letter.

## What was found
- Anna Pisciotto is named as Rosalia's mother on her marriage act of 27 February 1886 (Palermo, Brancaccio, Act No. 15; ARK 3:1:3QS7-997B-VWVB): Pisciotto Anna, resident in Brancaccio, wife of Vincenzo Mule.
- Her daughter Rosalia was born in Zisa, Palermo, married Pasquale Carbonaro 27 February 1886 in Brancaccio, and died 22 October 1893 in Palermo (Vallelunga church letter, 26 November 1895; ARK 3:1:3Q9M-CS86-SQQ4-M, which states the date twice).
- Her grandchildren Angelo, Ignazia, and Vincenzo Carbonaro were born in Brancaccio, Palermo, in 1887, 1889, and 1892 (Palermo birth index 1886-1895, image 125; ARK 3:1:3Q9M-CS9L-Q99L-8).

## Not found
- No record of Anna herself beyond the one naming. No birth, marriage, or death record for her has been found. Next: the Palermo birth records of 1862-1863 in Zisa for Rosalia Mule; the Palermo marriage records for Vincenzo Mule and Anna Pisciotto.
