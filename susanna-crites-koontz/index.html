Life Story

No photo of Susanna Crites Koontz has been found yet. This story is built from the research notes in the research folder.

Susanna Crites was born about 1817–18 in Virginia. Her maiden name is Crites — verified on the record of her first marriage — and her parents are unknown in the records. She is carried on this page with John Crites × Gloria Pfost as her parents at UNPROVED, per the position on the Each Generation page. The research folder’s position is UNKNOWN, and the will of John Crites weighs against the reconstruction: it names seven children plus Mrs. Henry Bouse, and no Susanna.

She married Henry Koontz on 21 April 1835, by minister Jesse Prior, the record naming her “Susanna Crites”. The 1850 census image reads the household in full: Henry 35, Susanna 33, Cloa 14, John 12, Margaret 10, Magdalina 6, Elvin 2, and Washington 3/12 — with two people in the household who are not yet identified, Frances Redman 28 and Magdalina Redman 7. No Ellon, no Nancy Rebecca, and no E G or G W appear in that household.

Henry died on 9 January 1852. On 4 December 1853 Susanna married William Fisher Jr. of Pocatalico. The marriage index is found; the record image is restricted to a FamilySearch center or affiliate library. The 1860 census at Grasslick puts William 61 and Susan 43 with the Koontz stepchildren and their own young children Isaac M., James M., and Elizabeth C. The 1870 census in Mill Creek Township reads William 71 and Susan 53 with Isaac M. 15 and James M. 12, and two people not yet identified: “Elvin” 22 and “Margaret” 21.

William died between September 1879 and March 1880 — his will was proved 15 March 1880 — and the will never names his wife. Susanna was enumerated a widow in her son James M.’s household on 12 June 1880. In her eightieth year she lost her daughter Chloe Greene, murdered with her husband James Greene and Matilda Pfost on 3 November 1897. The killer, John F. Morgan, was captured by G. W. Shamblen and hanged on 16 December 1897 — the last public execution in West Virginia.

Susanna died two months later, on 7 January 1898 at Fairplain, of “Old Age”, aged 81 — the death register read directly, spelling her name “Susana”. Her burial place is unknown. A Goldtown burial is contradicted: the Goldtown transcription checked does not list her.

Her son James M. Fisher married Nancy Jane Casto in 1878, and through James the line runs down to the Fisher families of Jackson County.
