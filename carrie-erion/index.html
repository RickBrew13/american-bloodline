Life Story

Carrie Erion married Otto F. Graff on 29 July 1903 in Hamilton County, Ohio — the marriage index spells her name Carrie Irion. The couple settled in Cincinnati, where their son Frank G. Graff was born on 21 October 1906. The 1910 and 1920 censuses show the three of them together in Cincinnati, Frank aged 3 and then 13.

Her death certificate, read in full, gives her birth as 3 January 1873 in Cincinnati and names her parents as Peter Irion and Sophia Hagar, both born in Cincinnati; her son Frank was the informant. The censuses still give her age four years younger — 33 in 1910 and 43 in 1920 — which would put her birth about 1877. Whether she had brothers or sisters is not yet known.

Otto died in Cincinnati on 21 December 1932. Carrie outlived him by eleven years and died in Cincinnati on 31 January 1943, at 1:45 in the afternoon. She is buried at St. Mary Cemetery, St. Bernard, in Section 23, Grave 931, buried 4 February 1943.

Otto Graff Sr., born about 1902, is listed as a son on this profile.

Research Notes and Open Questions

23 November 2026 — a free-records search looked for Carrie’s birth and death records, her parents and siblings, the 1930 and 1940 censuses, an obituary, and a burial. Her birth date is not settled: a burial index gives 3 January 1873, but both censuses give her age four years younger — 33 in 1910 and 43 in 1920 — which would put her birth about 1877. Her death certificate, from 1943, would give her birth date and place and name her parents; it has not yet been read.

Where she was born has not been found at all, and her parents and any brothers or sisters are not yet known. The Irion families traced in free records cluster in Gallia County, Ohio, Tennessee, Michigan, and Indiana, and none ties to Cincinnati or to a Carrie who married a Graff.

She is buried in Section 23, Grave 931, in Saint Bernard, Hamilton County, Ohio. The cemetery name has not been established. Vine Street Hill Cemetery uses Section 23 numbering and sits at the St. Bernard border, but that is only a lead and is not asserted here.

23 November 2026 — Carrie’s Ohio death certificate has now been read in full. It gives her birth as 3 January 1873 in Cincinnati and names her parents as Peter Irion, born in Cincinnati, and Sophia Hagar, born in Cincinnati — the certificate spells her mother’s surname Hagar, where the tree spells it Hager. Her son Frank was the informant. She died 31 January 1943 at 1:45 in the afternoon in Cincinnati and was buried at St. Mary Cemetery on 4 February 1943; St. Bernard is the burial place, not the death place. The cause of death is only partly legible on the certificate and reads tentatively as cardiac vascular disease with coronary embolism. The censuses still imply a birth about 1877, so the year is not settled by every record. The marriage record, also read, was 29 July 1903; it names her father Peter Irion and leaves her mother’s name blank. The 1900 census entry for a “Carrie Metzal,” daughter of George and Sophie Metzal, is a different person and is not her.
