Life Story

Wilbur Mark Forste was born in Ohio and died in Cincinnati, according to compiled memorial/profile sources and the obituary clipping supplied from his Find a Grave page. His original Hamilton County marriage-banns record documents his 8 January 1936 marriage to Marie Elise Barnhorst at St. Agnes. Marie’s obituary says she died 29 December 1951 and names Wilbur as her husband.

Wilbur’s 1974 obituary names Virginia Forste, née Robinson, as his wife at his death, and names daughters Arlene and Gay and son Michael. This shows a later wife, but the clipping alone does not establish when or how Wilbur and Virginia married. The 1950 Census index lists Arlene and Gabrielle as children in Wilbur and Marie’s household; no reviewed source settles whether “Gay” was Gabrielle’s nickname or a different daughter.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Wilbur’s 1974 obituary names Virginia Forste (née Robinson) as his wife at death. No marriage record or marriage date for them has been located.
  • Both obituaries name Arlene, Gay, and Michael. The 1950 Census index lists Arlene and Gabrielle; whether Gabrielle and Gay are one person remains unresolved.
  • Marie’s obituary names Mrs. Elsie Barnhorst as her mother and George Barnhorst as her brother. The clipping supplies no maiden surname for Elsie and names no father.
  • The newspaper names and issue dates are not visible in the cropped obituary images. Preserve them as clippings from the user-provided Find a Grave photo collection until the newspaper citation is recovered.
  • Wilbur’s birth, parents, siblings, and several vital details still rely on compiled sources; original civil records have not yet been reviewed.
