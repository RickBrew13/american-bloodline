Life Story

Wilbur Mark Forste (18 March 1908–27 December 1974) was born in Ohio and died in Cincinnati, according to matching Ancestry and Find a Grave compiled profiles. His memorial places him at Saint Joseph New Cemetery in Cincinnati and identifies his parents as John H. Forste and Emma Mohs.

The original Hamilton County marriage-banns page states that Wilbur Forste and Marie Elise Barnhorst were married on 8 January 1936 at St. Agnes (volume 16, page 333). This documents the marriage itself. Wilbur’s Find a Grave memorial gives his wife as Marie Elise Barnhorst; Ancestry calls her Marie (Peggy) Elise Barnhost. The 1950 Census index supplies a later household bridge: Wilbur M. Forste and Marie P. Forste are indexed as spouses in Cincinnati, with children Gabrielle and Arlene. The census image has not been inspected.

Arlene Marie Forste’s Kentucky birth-index entry names Marie Barnhorst as her mother but does not name her father; the 1950 household index lists Arlene as Wilbur and Marie’s child. The same census index lists Gabrielle Forste as a daughter and Arlene’s sibling. Compiled Ancestry and Find a Grave profiles also list Michael Anthony Forste (reported 24 July 1950–30 March 2015) as their child. His reported birth is after the 18 April 1950 census date, so his absence from that census household is expected. Earlier compiled summaries listing only Michael or only Arlene appear incomplete in light of the broader evidence.

Marie’s birth year is unresolved by one year: Find a Grave and Ancestry give 1916, while the 1950 Census index estimates 1917 and the marriage-tree snippet describes her as 19 in 1936. Keep the source variants and uncertainty visible. No direct death record or obituary for Marie, original birth/death record for Wilbur, or primary record naming Gabrielle’s later identity has been inspected here.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Marriage index entry is “Marie Elsie Barnhorst” and “Wilbur Forste,” 8 Jan 1936, St. Agnes, vol. 16, p. 333. Other sources use Marie Elise / Marie (Peggy) Elise and Barnhorst / Barnhost / Marie P. Forste. The original register is the next direct record to verify identity, ages, and parents.
  • The 1950 census index lists Arlene and Gabrielle as daughters of Wilbur M. and Marie P. Forste. Its Arlene birth estimate (1937) differs from her Kentucky birth-index date (11 Nov 1936); original images remain uninspected.
  • Michael Anthony Forste (reported July 1950–2015) appears as Wilbur and Marie’s child in compiled profiles. His reported birth followed the 18 Apr 1950 census enumeration. Locate original vital records.
  • Marie is reported born 1916 by compiled profiles; 1950 census index suggests 1917. Her parents, siblings, exact birth/death details, and any death notice are unresolved.
  • Wilbur’s parents and seven siblings are listed in Find a Grave/Ancestry compiled profiles; original records not yet reviewed for those relationships.
  • Find 1940 Census household evidence, including the family-tree snippet’s reported 1031 Benz address, but treat the snippet only as a lead until the original schedule is checked.
  • Several MyHeritage snippets report a 1935 Covington residence and say only one child Arlene. The site is blocked and those derivative tree snippets conflict with the 1950 census index and other compiled profiles; do not treat a snippet as complete family enumeration.
