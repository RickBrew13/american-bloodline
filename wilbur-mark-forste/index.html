Life Story

Wilbur Mark Forste was born in Ohio on 18 March 1908 and died in Cincinnati on 27 December 1974. Ancestry lists John Henry Forste and Emma Mohs as parents and Marie (Peggy) Elise Barnhost as wife. His Find a Grave memorial likewise names John H. Forste and Emma Mohs as parents, Marie Elise Barnhorst as wife, and Michael Anthony Forste as a child. A MyHeritage search-result summary supplied by a family member says Marie and Wilbur had daughter Arlene Forste. Arlene’s Kentucky birth index names Marie Barnhorst as her mother, but no original record reviewed here names Wilbur as her father; that link remains plausible and supported, not confirmed.
