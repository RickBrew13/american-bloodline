## Later marriage to Gobelle Moore — independent support
Newcomer Dayton's 2017 obituary for Michael D. Moore states he was the son of Gobelle and Alma Moore and was preceded in death by his stepmother, **Ruth Moore**. This independently supports Ruth as a wife/stepmother in Gobelle's family. Gobelle's own 2011 obituary, already reviewed in the prior note, identifies Ruth Moore as his second wife. Ruth's Ohio death index records her as married at death in 2000, while her Find a Grave memorial names Gobelle as spouse. Together these are strong evidence for the Ruth–Gobelle relationship, but they do not give an exact marriage date or independently connect the Ruth Moore in the obituaries to the maiden name Wainscott. The date range “about 1998–2000” should remain a compiled-tree estimate, not a documented marriage date.
