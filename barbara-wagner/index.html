Life Story

Barbara Wagner was born on 26 June 1860 in Jimbolia, then Hatzfeld, in the Banat, and baptized there the next day. Her baptism register entry names her parents, Mathias Wagner and Margaretha Schuldt of Hatzfeld house 168, and her godparents, Mathias Tavle and Barbara Halm. Her father, baptized at Hatzfeld in 1832, died at Gilad in 1871, when Barbara was ten; her parents had married at Hatzfeld in 1856.

On 9 January 1888, per Giel's Kleinjetscha family book, Barbara married Philippus Palmer of Kleinjetscha. The wedding record itself has never been found, though three parish registers were read entry by entry. Four children followed: Anna in 1889, Heinrich, who lived twelve days in November 1890, Nikolaus, who lived two days in April 1892, and Peter, born and baptized on 20 September 1893 at Iecea Mică, the only one known to have survived childhood. Peter would emigrate to Cincinnati and marry Margaret Lorch.

Barbara died on 3 February 1941, per Giel's family book; the death register entry has not been pulled, and no burial record or Find a Grave memorial has been found. No photograph of Barbara has been found — the folder holds her baptism register page, cited here as a document, not shown as a gallery picture.
