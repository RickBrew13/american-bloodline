Family Relationship Notes

Shirley is listed as child of Dudley Wagers and Ellen Baker Wagers in Shirley’s and Dudley’s Find a Grave family panels. This is a working assignment (~75% confidence), not an inspected original birth record. Shirley’s memorial lists three siblings: Mary Belle, Conrad, and Anderson.

Merida’s 2006, David’s 2017, and Kathleen’s 2025 obituaries identify Shirley and Amanda/Mandy Baker as parents of this ten-child family. Names vary across sources: Pearl/Pearlie, Linia/Linea, Aline/Ailene, and Freddy/Freddie; variants are retained rather than silently normalized.

The couple’s shared Drive folder has no photos. Only son Merida’s reunion photo and VA marker were located; these are family-context images, not parent portraits or parent burial evidence.

Shirley’s own date range is uncertain: Find a Grave reports birth in 1900 and death in 1973; census indexes imply about 1901–1903. Parents above this generation are memorial-reported only and not added.

Find a Grave family panels link him as son of Dudley and Ellen; this is a working assignment (~75%), not an original birth-record proof. The ten-child family group is supported across three obituaries. Shirley’s own parents’ parents remain unverified.

The Find a Grave family listing gives Shirley A. Wagers as 1900–1973. The census indexes give Shirley ages 27 in 1930 and 38 in 1940, implying a birth around 1902–1903, so the year 1900 remains a reported secondary date rather than a reconciled fact.
