# Shirley A. Wagers × Amanda “Mandy” (Baker) Wagers — family audit

**Research date:** 26 November 2026  
**Generation VI couple:** Shirley A. Wagers × Amanda “Mandy” Baker Wagers

## Parents and siblings

### Shirley A. Wagers
Find a Grave memorials for Shirley (145450642) and his older father Dudley Wagers (52726021) have reciprocal family panels listing **Dudley Wagers (1866–1942)** and **Ellen Baker Wagers (1868–1916)** as Shirley’s parents. The same panels list Shirley’s siblings Mary Belle Wagers Bowling (1898–1989), Conrad Wagers (1899–1967), and Anderson Wagers (1908–1972).

The Dudley memorial narrative says Dudley was the son of **Dutton Wagers and Lucinda Holland Wagers**, and that his first wife Ellen Baker was the daughter of **Jefferson and China Smith Baker**. The memorial also summarizes Dudley’s 1942 Kentucky death certificate as naming parents “Dut Wagers” and “Lucy Wagers,” and wife Jane at death; it does not reproduce the certificate image. These are derivative memorial assertions and the spouse/date details conflict. Shirley’s parent assignment to Dudley and Ellen is supported at about **75%** by reciprocal memorial family groups, but it is not directly proved here by birth records. The further-grandparent pairs (Dutton × Lucinda and Jefferson × China) remain **unverified leads**; no Gen VII profiles were created for them pending primary or independent corroboration.

### Amanda “Mandy” Baker Wagers
The 1930 and 1940 census indexes, Merida’s NUMIDENT, and family obituaries identify Amanda/Mandy Baker as the mother in this couple. **No record reviewed names Amanda’s parents or siblings.** The Baker surname alone is insufficient to attach a same-name Baker family. Her parents and sibling group remain unknown.

## Children of Shirley and Amanda

The 2006 Bowling Funeral Home obituary for son Merida names Shirley and Amanda Baker Wagers as his parents, lists five sons and daughter Yvonne, and identifies living/deceased siblings. David Wagers’s 2017 Reppert Funeral Home obituary independently names both parents and describes seven deceased siblings and two surviving siblings. Kathleen Wagers Tyree’s 2025 Lakes Funeral Home obituary identifies Shirley and Mandy Baker Wagers as her parents and says she was preceded by her nine siblings. Together these sources support a ten-child family group:

1. **Ida Wagers** (later Carnahan), c.1920–1984 — named as Ida Carnahan in the Merida obituary and listed in memorial family panel.
2. **Pearlie/Pearl Wagers** (later Carmack), reportedly born 13 March 1922 — the public Ancestry profile gives 15 September 2002 as death date; obituary and memorial use variant name forms.
3. **Dudley Wilson Wagers**, 17 November 1924–13 November 2004.
4. **Linea/Linia Wagers** (later Hammons) — 2006 obituary uses Linea; David’s 2017 obituary uses Linia. Exact dates not reconciled.
5. **Merida Henry Wagers**, 18 January 1930–29 January 2006.
6. **David Wagers**, 24 September 1932–8 January 2017.
7. **Aline/Ailene Wagers** (later Helton), died 24 April 2004; spellings vary across records.
8. **Price Wagers**, 4 April 1938–30 May 1993.
9. **Kathleen Wagers Tyree**, 24 November 1940–5 January 2025.
10. **Freddie/Freddy Wagers**, reported c.1943–2021.

The obituary evidence supports the ten-child total; variants remain source-attributed. Shirley and Amanda’s profiles now list these ten children, and each child is listed as a sibling on the other parent’s profile.

## Other descendants

Merida’s obituary names sons Billy, Paul, Bruce, Merdy, and Jeffrey, and deceased daughter Yvonne Wuerdmann. Only Yvonne’s parentage with Merida and Aileene Willie Brewster is directly supported by her NUMIDENT; the other sons’ mothers are not established in the reviewed notices. They are therefore not automatically added as children of Aileene or siblings of her other known children.

## Photos and archive

- The shared Wagers couple Drive Pictures folder contained no files.
- No verified portrait or grave photo of Shirley or Amanda was located in their existing site folders or the searched sources.
- Two photos already archived under son Merida are surfaced on both parents’ pages as **child/family context only**: a 2000 Wagers family reunion photo credited in the existing archive to Find a Grave contributor Beverly Dunn and said to have been shared by Paul Wagers; and Merida’s VA marker photo credited to Find a Grave contributor Earth Angel. Neither image depicts the parents or proves their burial.
- No new media was found in the couple’s shared folder; no image has been relabeled as a portrait of Shirley or Amanda.

## Parent-hunt conclusion and Gen VII

- **Shirley → Dudley Wagers + Ellen Baker Wagers:** supported working assignment, ~75% confidence, based on reciprocal Find a Grave family panels; not a reviewed birth record.
- **Dudley → Dutton Wagers + Lucinda Holland:** memorial narrative / derivative claim only; unverified.
- **Ellen Baker → Jefferson + China Smith Baker:** memorial narrative / derivative claim only; unverified.
- **Amanda’s parents:** unknown; no supported pair identified.

No Gen VII profiles were created in this pass because the only evidence naming ancestors of Shirley’s parents is memorial text not yet corroborated, and Amanda’s parents are not named. The profiles retain explicit unknowns instead of promoting same-name claims to facts.
