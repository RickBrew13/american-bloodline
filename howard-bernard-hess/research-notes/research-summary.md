# Howard Bernard Hess — research notes

## Working identity
Howard Bernard Hess (10 Apr 1899–23 Oct 1953), Cincinnati-born and associated with 535 Elberon Ave., Cincinnati; Find a Grave memorial says died Cleveland, burial Saint Joseph New Cemetery. His 1953 newspaper notice describes his death aboard the Ohio State Limited while en route from Cincinnati to New York.

## Parents
Charles H. Hess (1869–1923) is strongly supported as father: Charles’s obituary names son Howard; Howard’s own memorial lists Charles as father. Howard’s mother appears as Katherine A. Hess in his obituary. Charles’s notices call wife Katherine (née Winterholter), while Katherine’s 1967 obituary calls her Katherine A. (née Wiedmer); family memorial data also names John B. Winterhalter and Catherine Schlitz and gives another conflicting set John Wiedmer/Mary Stanger. Treat the woman as a likely spouse/mother but leave her maiden name and parentage unresolved.

## Spouse and children
Anne Elizabeth Mader (21 Jul 1903–7 Jul 1990), marriage listed 1928. Howard’s 1953 obituary enumerates children Howard Jr., Richard, Charles, Robert, Catherine, Nancy and Mary Helen. Memorial-linked identities give John Richard Hess (1935–1986), Katherine Hess Brady (1929–2002), Howard B. Hess (1930–1967), Nancy Jane Hess Herbert (1932–2017), Robert Charles/Herbert Robert Charles Hess (1933–2018; given-name order varies), Mary Helen Hess Pratt (1937–2012), and Charles H. Hess (1941–2002). Keep the name/date and obituary enumeration discrepancies explicit.

## Siblings
Contributor-maintained Charles/Katherine family memorials and the 1967 Katherine notice support three siblings: Marguerite A. Hess (later Dorger; 1898–1951), Charles H. Hess Jr. (1902–1938), and Catherine/Catherine Hess (later Ryan; 1908–1967). The first 1923 obituary calls one daughter “Mrs Margaret Borger”; the 1967 notice identifies the late Mrs Clarence A. Dorger. Same-name and surname variations are preserved; no separate profiles created.

## Other facts / uncertainty
A 1923 death notice lists businessman Charles’s home, companies, bank role, and Catholic funeral; the 1953 obituary calls Howard president of Montclair Realty Co., gives the 535 Elberon address, and funeral at Holy Family Church. Find a Grave’s Ohio WWI roster image says Howard R. Hess at 535 Filberon/Elberon Ave., but initial/date conflict (R, 7 Apr 1899 vs B, 10 Apr 1899): research lead only, no military service claim.
