# Hess–Mader records, source notes and transcription extracts

Accessed 24 November 2026. Extracts below preserve the wording relevant to this family; original archival record images were not all independently inspected. Find a Grave memorial entries are contributor-maintained derivative sources and are not treated as primary proof.

## Hess household and relationships

### Charles H. Hess, 1923 newspaper notices
The Find a Grave memorial photo gallery for Charles H. Hess (22 Dec 1869–23 Apr 1923), memorial 188780579, links two images transcribed as newspaper notices. The fuller notice reports he died at home, age 54, and was president/treasurer of Charles H. Hess Company, president of Tri-State Butter Company and Merchants Ice and Cold Storage Company; he was survived by widow Katherine (née Winterholter), sons Charles H. Jr. and Howard, and daughters Catherine and Mrs. Margaret Borger. Another notice names Katherine (née Winterholter) and St Joseph’s Catholic Mausoleum. These images support the family group and work, but the maiden-name spelling conflicts with Katherine’s 1967 obituary.

### Howard B. Hess, 1953 obituary and notice
Howard Bernard Hess memorial 188866554 has a newspaper clipping transcribed as: Howard B. Hess, “president of the Montclair Realty Co.,” age 54, died aboard the Ohio State Limited while travelling Cincinnati to New York; home 535 Elberon Ave., Price Hill; burial St Joseph’s New Cemetery; widow Mrs Ann Mader Hess; surviving children Howard Jr., Richard, Charles, Robert, Catherine, Nancy and Mary Helen; mother Mrs Catherine A. Hess, sister Mrs Edward T. Ryan. A separate notice says funeral at Holy Family Church and enumerates four sons and three daughters living at the home address. The dates/initials in these derivative transcripts conflict in places with memorial child records; retain exact source wording and do not silently harmonize.

The memorial gallery also includes an Ohio roster image labelled Howard R. Hess at 535 “Filberon/Elberon” Avenue, with service dates/transcription and a birth date in April 1899. Because the roster gives middle initial R and date 7 April, whereas the researched subject is Howard B. Hess, born 10 April, this is only an identity lead, not proof of military service.

### Katherine A. Hess, 1967 obituary and family memorials
A funeral notice image in Katherine’s Find a Grave memorial 129455596 is transcribed: Katherine A. (née Wiedmer), wife of the late Charles H. Hess; mother of the late Mrs Clarence A. Dorger, Mrs Edward T. Ryan, Howard B. and Charles H. Hess; died in Phoenix 14 Nov 1967. Her main memorial narrative elsewhere calls her a daughter of John B. Winterhalter and Catherine Schlitz, while another embedded text gives John Wiedmer and Mary Stanger; the Find a Grave family links list Charles H. as spouse. Charles’s 1923 notices call his wife Katherine Hess (née Winterholter). This is a material maiden-name/parentage conflict; parents and middle surname remain unresolved pending original records.

## Mader parish baptism transcription extracts
The Campbell County USGenWeb transcription of St Francis of Assisi Catholic Church baptisms, Dayton, Kentucky, labels entries as birth and baptism dates and is a derivative transcription, not the original register image.

- 1893–1896 list: Maria Katharine Mader, born 4 July 1894, baptized 15 July, daughter of Robert and Frances (Hoh); sponsors Wilhelm Bruegger and Katharine Mader. This is consistent with the memorial-linked daughter Marie Mader, but name forms differ.
- 1897–1901 list: Michael Franz Mader, born 28 Sep 1897, baptized 3 Oct, son of Robert and Frances (Hoh).
- Same 1897–1901 list: Cyrus Johan Mader, born 10 June 1900, baptized 16 June, son of Robert and Frances (Hoh).
- 1902–1906 list: Anna Elisabeth Mader, born 22 July 1903, baptized 29 July, daughter of Robert and Frances (Hoh). This agrees in date/year and parent names with Anne Elizabeth Mader, though spelling differs.
- Same 1902–1906 list: Lawrence Joseph Mader, born 18 Sep 1906, baptized 23 Sep, son of Robert and Frances (Hoh).
- A separate Frederic Leo Mader entry (20 May 1905) names parents Frederic and Maria (Hauser), not Robert and Frances. Do not include him as their child absent further evidence.
