# Research note — William Runyon

Reviewed 26 November 2026.

The public FamilySearch shared-tree profile GXX7-CXM reports William Runyon was born about 1855 in Kentucky, lived in Poplar Plains, Fleming County in 1880, and was husband of Ella Brafford and father of Frances Armstrong Runion and Robert Runyon. It cites an 1880 census source titled “Wm. Runyon.” The same household is described in the profiles of Ella and Robert. The household image attached to the shared tree is archived as a low-resolution thumbnail and labeled only as a source-document preview; it has not been fully transcribed from the original. The parentage of Frances is therefore a working tree assignment, not an independently proven relationship.
