Life Story

Raymond Dean Dukes was born on August 24, 1929, at Mt. Carmel, on the Wabash River in southeastern Illinois, the son of Raymond I. Dukes and Birdie D. Kellogg. DOCUMENTED He applied for a Social Security card at thirteen, in the middle of the Second World War. DOCUMENTED

He made his home on the Mississippi Gulf Coast at Ocean Springs, and he became the last husband of Yvonne Suzanne Brewster, Eugene Brewster's former wife; a family tree dates the marriage to 1976. SUPPORTED Yvonne died at Ocean Springs in 1994. DOCUMENTED Raymond outlived her by ten years and died on June 19, 2004, aged 74. DOCUMENTED
