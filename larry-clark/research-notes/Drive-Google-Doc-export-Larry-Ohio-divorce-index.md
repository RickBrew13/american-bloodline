# **Larry Clark – Ohio divorce index entries (read 17 November 2026\)**

Source: Ohio Marriage/Divorce Search (VitStats), Henschen & Associates, http\://hacourts.com/VitStats/index.shtml (results via http\://hacourts.com/cgi-bin/vssearch.cgi). Index of Ohio Department of Health marriage and divorce abstracts; divorces 1962–2006. The divorce index gives the wife’s first name and middle initial only. Searches run: groom first name Larry, last name Clark, bride first name Judith (marriages and divorces).

## **1\. Divorce, 6 September 1984, Hamilton County – most likely Judy (Palmer) and her husband Larry**

Transcribed exactly: \- Husband: Larry Clark · Birth year: (19)40 · Residence: Hamilton · Times married: 2 \- Wife: Judith M. · Birth year: (19)39 · Residence: Hamilton · Times married: 2 \- Certificate \#: 035410 · Volume \#: 05370 · County: Hamilton · Date: 09/06/1984 \- Granted: Divorce · Grounds: Gross Neglect Of Duty · To whom: Husband \- Marriage duration: 08 years · Minor children: 1

Why it fits: wife Judith M., born 1939, Hamilton County, matches Judith Mae Palmer (b. 9 Feb 1939 Cincinnati). The one minor child fits Barry T. Clark (born 25 Aug 1969, age 15 in 1984). “Times married 2” for Judith fits Homer (1956) and Larry. Not proof: the index does not give the wife’s surname. Grade: SUPPORTED as a match, not DOCUMENTED.

Problem to resolve: an 8-year marriage ending in Sept 1984 began about 1976, seven years after Barry’s 1969 birth. Either they married (or remarried) about 1976, or the index’s duration counts from a later ceremony. No Larry Clark marriage appears in the Hamilton County probate marriage index 1963–1982, so the marriage was probably licensed outside Hamilton County (Kentucky across the river is a common choice).

## **3\. Divorce, 9 May 1968, Hamilton County – possibly the same Larry (lead only)**

* Husband: Larry C. Clark · b. (19)40 · Hamilton · times married 1

* Wife: Judith · b. (19)42 · Hamilton · times married 2

* Cert 010730 · Vol 01873 · Hamilton · 05/09/1968 · Divorce · Gross Neglect of Duty · to Wife · duration 05 years · minor children 2 A Larry C. Clark, born 1940, of Hamilton County, ended a first marriage one week after Judy’s divorce from Homer. Same birth year and county as the 1984 Larry, who was on his second marriage by 1984\. This MAY be Judy’s Larry ending his first marriage, which would make his middle initial C. UNPROVED: Larry Clark is a common name.

## **Ruled out by these records**

* Larry Thomas Clark (22 Sep 1946 – 20 Aug 1999, Spring Grove): Judy’s Larry was born about 1940, so the 1946 man does not fit the 1984 divorce.
