# Research note: Larry Clark and Barry T. Clark (2026-11-06)

Question: Who was Larry Clark, Judith Mae (Palmer) Clark's later husband, and who was their son Barry? Is Larry living? Was Della Clark his wife?
Free sources only (Find a Grave, BillionGraves, Spring Grove Locate-a-Loved-One map and burial card, SortedByName Numident/BIRLS/DMF/TN marriage indexes, SSDI site, people-search, obituary sites). No logins, no payments.

- Barry T. Clark, 25 Aug 1969 – 24 Jul 2008 IS Judy's son: shared headstone photo (FaG 228001842, added by Jil 31 May 2021) reads "IN LOVING MEMORY / MOTHER JUDITH M. PALMER FEB. 9, 1939 JUN. 7, 2006 / SON BARRY T. CLARK AUG. 25, 1969 JULY 24, 2008". DOCUMENTED (headstone) + SSDI/DMF (b. 25 Aug 1969, SSN issued Ohio, d. 24 Jul 2008, last res. Cincinnati 45216). - Judith's FaG photo 1 (Searchin4u2, 2024) is a different flat marker "PALMER / RONALD H. 1939 — TOGETHER FOREVER — JUDITH M. 1939 ✝". Possibly a different Judith M. Palmer (wife of a Ronald H. Palmer) misattached, or unknown. UNPROVED; do not use.
- Barry T Clark × Terri L Meade, Sevier Co TN, 8 May 1993 (sortedbyname index_363, verified page): very probably NOT our Barry — Terri Lynn (Meade) Clark is linked in people-search to a living Barry Todd Clark (Newport/Kodak TN, Lima OH). UNPROVED, leaning different man.
- Larry Clark identity: STILL UNKNOWN. Best local candidate = Larry Thomas Clark, b. 22 Sep 1946 Princeton (Mercer Co.) WV, son of Thomas C. Clark & Mary H. Lambert, US Army 1973–76 and Jan–Mar 1991, d. 20 Aug 1999, buried Spring Grove LN-61A-13-12 (Numident + BIRLS via sortedbyname index_2713; FaG 78908299; Spring Grove card: spouse/parents blank). Nothing ties him to Judy or Barry; not in the same grave as them (Judy/Barry not in Spring Grove index). Barry's middle initial T. is only a coincidence-level hint. UNPROVED.
- Is Larry living? UNKNOWN. No record names Judy's Larry. If he is Larry Thomas Clark, he died 1999. WikiTree marks "Living" (unverified).
- DELLA CLARK: was she Larry's wife? ANSWER: UNKNOWN, almost certainly NO. Grade UNPROVED (no evidence for). The Geni/Ancestry match is a Phoenix, AZ "Lawrence Clark", carpenter/laborer, with wife Della, in directories 1947–1956: an adult householder in 1947 was born by c.1929, while Judy was b.1939 and their son 1969; nothing ties that man to Cincinnati; the popup lumped Judy in only because it merged directory entries by name. If our Larry is the 1946 Larry Thomas Clark, he was a baby in 1947 and cannot be that man.
