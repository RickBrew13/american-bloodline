# **Larry Clark – Hamilton County Probate Court marriage license index, searched 17 November 2026**

Source: Hamilton County Probate Court, Marriage License Index (1817–1983), https\://www\.probatect.org/court-records/archive-categories/marriages/marriage-license-index-(1817-1983). Index-book PDFs were read page by page.

Result: NO Larry or Lawrence Clark marriage to Judith/Judy (Palmer, Brewster or Clark) was found in Hamilton County, 1963–1982.

Books checked: \- 1963–1967, C–D book, Clark pages (PDF p. 9): Clark entries run from Elaine to Terry R. No Larry. One “Lawrence J.” (vol. 703, p. 142); one “Judith Anne” (685/161) and one “Judith L.” (734/318), neither ours. \- 1968–1972, C book, Clark pages (PDF p. 13): no Larry. “Lawrence J.” (779/391). “Judy” (755/206) and “Judy A” (794/101) are namesake leads only; the index gives no spouse. \- 1968–1972, B book, Brewster pages (PDF p. 30): no Judith or Judy Brewster. \- 1968–1972, N–O–P–Q book, Palmer pages (PDF p. 12): no Judith M. Palmer. One “Judy A.” Palmer (797/50), a different middle initial. Penelope Palmer (764/49), Judy’s sister, is there, which shows the book covers the family. \- 1974–1982, Ci–Coda book (typed index with spouse names), Clark pages (PDF pp. 13–14): Clark entries go Kevin P, Kimberly Ann, Lawrence (spouse Sharon Brooks, 1978), Lawrence Joseph (two marriages, 1976 and 1980), Leonard Edgar… No Larry Clark.

What this means: Judy and Larry probably got their marriage license outside Hamilton County. Northern Kentucky (Kenton or Campbell County) is the most likely place. The Kentucky Marriage Index 1973–1999 would list a 1973-or-later Kentucky marriage with the groom’s age and county. An Ohio divorce in 1984 after “8 years” points to a marriage about 1976\.

Also checked: 1974–1982 Bras–Brl book, Brewster pages (PDF p. 24): the only Brewster entries are Homer Wm Brewster Jr × Flora L (1977). No Judith Brewster marriage to a Clark.

Not checked: the 1973 index books; the 1983 book (outside the range); any Kentucky index.
