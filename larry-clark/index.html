Life Story

Larry Clark married Judith Mae "Judy" Palmer after her marriages to Homer William Brewster Jr. FAMILY ACCOUNT The marriage is supported by more than the family's word: a BillionGraves record on Judy's Geni profile names her spouse as Larry Clark, and a U.S. city directory lists "Larry Clark & Judith Palmer," with Judy herself as "Judy M Clark." SUPPORTED Judy carried the surname Clark from then on and still bore it when she died in 2006; Social Security lists her as Judith M. Clark. DOCUMENTED

Larry and Judy had a son, Barry T. Clark, born August 25, 1969. FAMILY ACCOUNT Barry's mother is set in stone: the shared headstone reads "MOTHER, JUDITH M. PALMER" and "SON, BARRY T. CLARK." DOCUMENTED Barry died July 24, 2008, two years after his mother. DOCUMENTED The family remembers Barry dying before Judy, and holds that he had HIV/AIDS; no record confirms the illness, and the dates show he outlived her. FAMILY ACCOUNT

Who Larry was beyond that is still an open question. No birth date, parents, or death date has been tied to him. One Cincinnati candidate is a Larry Thomas Clark, born September 22, 1946, in Princeton, West Virginia, son of Thomas C. Clark and Mary H. Lambert, an Army veteran who died August 20, 1999, and is buried at Spring Grove Cemetery. UNPROVED Nothing yet links that man to Judy or Barry, and his Spring Grove burial card names no spouse.

Geni lists a Della Clark as another wife of Larry's. That link comes from a city-directory match for a different man: a Lawrence Clark, carpenter and laborer, living in Phoenix, Arizona, from 1947 to 1956 with a wife named Della. A man keeping house in Phoenix in 1947 was born by about 1929, and nothing ties him to Cincinnati. The Della marriage is not accepted here. UNPROVED

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Was Larry the stepfather who helped raise Garry after Homer left? The family story says a stepfather did; whether it was Larry is not known.
  • When did the marriage end, and how? Judy's companion of her later years, Ralph, was with her for ten to fifteen years, which suggests the Clark marriage ended well before 2006.
  • Is Larry living? Unknown. WikiTree holds him as a private "living" profile; no record settles it.
  • Order of marriages: one family memory calls Larry Judy's second husband; another account places him after several marriages to Homer. The order is not settled.
  • Larry Thomas Clark (1946–1999) of Spring Grove is a name match only. His Cincinnati Enquirer obituary (August 1999) could show whether he left a son Barry.
  • Records that would settle Larry's identity: Barry's Social Security application (SS-5, by request to SSA) and Barry's 1969 Ohio birth record, both of which name his father; the Hamilton County marriage license index (groom Clark, 1963–1972); the Spring Grove interment cards for Judy and Barry.
  • Searched, not found: Spring Grove's online burial map has no entry for Judith Clark/Palmer or Barry Clark, though their headstone is photographed there; no obituary for Barry (2008) found; no Larry Clark record in Taney County, Missouri.
  • A 1993 Sevier County, Tennessee marriage of a Barry T. Clark and Terri L. Meade appears to belong to a different Barry T. Clark (Barry Todd Clark, living).
  • No photo of Larry is known. None will be used until a real photo of him turns up.
