Life Story

The Roane County birth register records Harry Earl Hoff on 25 February 1892 and names Daniel Homer Hoff as father and Elmanza C. Hoff in the mother field. The direct register image is archived.

In 1910, a Roane County household transcription lists Daniel H. Hoff, wife Elmanza, and a son “Earl,” age 18, alongside Grover, Laura, Atlee, Grace, and Darrell. The age and name fit Harry, but “Earl” is not expanded in that transcription.

Harry married Edith E. Samples in 1920. The 1940 census indexes their four children. One child appears as Lorena A. Hoff in the census and as Alma Young in a brother’s obituary; that appears to be a married-name variation, not a separate child.

Harry’s younger son Harry Earl Hoff Jr. is listed as born 22 May 1926 and died June 1980 on his Find a Grave memorial, conflicting with the existing profile’s 1926–2014 dates. The 1980 memorial has the same parents and must be distinguished from Harry Sr.; the younger son’s death date is not being rewritten here without further confirmation.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The 1910 transcription lists six children in the Daniel H. Hoff household and a separate grandson Bruce. Do not count Bruce as their child without a record.
  • The public FamilySearch profile for Roy Darrell Hoff has a sibling graph with twelve named Hoff siblings and shows “13” in its presentation count; that count/name-set discrepancy is unresolved. Names above are labeled as shared-tree candidates, not all independently established.
  • Flora B. Hoff and Grover Cleveland Hoff are both assigned 1887 births in the shared tree; preserve the same-year inconsistency. Jasper B. Hoff and Harry Earl Hoff are both assigned 25 February 1892; no twin/duplicate identity is assumed.
  • The exact 6 April 1987 death and burial place are only supported here by derivative/tree/index references. No original death certificate or marker image has been checked.
  • Find a Grave’s Harry Earl Hoff Jr. memorial conflicts with the current project’s reported 2014 death year; the younger Harry is a separate person and the discrepancy remains open.
