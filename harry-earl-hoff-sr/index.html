Life Story

Harry Earl Hoff Sr. was born about 1892. On 24 December 1920 he married Edith Elizabeth Samples in Clendenin, Kanawha County, West Virginia. Their children were Lorena Alma, Harry Earl Jr., Robert Lee, and Charles Edward Hoff.

In the 1940 census the family was living in Big Sandy Magisterial District, Kanawha County, West Virginia: Harry, 48, head of household, with his wife Edith, 36, and their children Lorena, 18, Harry Jr., 13, Robert Lee, 10, and Charles Edward, 2. His son Robert’s Social Security record names Harry and Edith as Robert’s parents, and his son Charles’s obituary names them as Charles’s parents.

Harry died in 1987. That year comes from the family tree alone; no death record or obituary has been located yet.
