Family Relationship Notes

  • Ora’s Kentucky death certificate gives her birth as 5 June 1869, her father as James W. Mullins, and her mother’s name in a difficult hand as “Lina J. Beales.” Eliza J. Scales is the likely reading based on the family and census evidence.
  • The 1880 census places Ora in the household of James W. Mullins in Fleming County, Kentucky. Her birthplace is recorded as Kentucky; the Kenton County birthplace on Find a Grave has not been substantiated.
  • Ora married James Hope Taylor on 4 February 1897 according to the Pendleton County marriage index. The original marriage record has not yet been inspected.
  • The seven children listed follow the family research and census records. The 1910 census reports six children born and six living, which does not reconcile with the known death of Myra in 1898; that census discrepancy remains open.

Life Story

Ora Mullins was born on 5 June 1869, the daughter of James W. Mullins, a Civil War veteran, and his wife Eliza . She grew up in Fleming County, Kentucky, and married Hope Taylor in Pendleton County in 1897 .

Ora kept house on the family farm near Butler and raised six daughters and a son, Wilbur . She died at Butler on 11 February 1940, and her stone in Butler Cemetery reads "Mother, Ora Taylor, nee Mullins" .
