Life Story

Ora Mullins was born on 5 June 1869, the daughter of James W. Mullins, a Civil War veteran, and his wife Eliza DOCUMENTED. She grew up in Fleming County, Kentucky, and married Hope Taylor in Pendleton County in 1897 SUPPORTED.

Ora kept house on the family farm near Butler and raised six daughters and a son, Wilbur SUPPORTED. She died at Butler on 11 February 1940, and her stone in Butler Cemetery reads "Mother, Ora Taylor, nee Mullins" DOCUMENTED.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Her mother's name on the death certificate is hard to read; "Eliza J. Scales" is the most likely reading.
  • Find a Grave gives her birthplace as Kenton County; no record supports it UNPROVED.
  • The 1910 census says 6 children born, 6 living; counting Myra (died 1898) it should be 7.
