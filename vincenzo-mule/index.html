Family Relationship Notes

Vincenzo Mule is named as Rosalia’s father on her marriage act of 27 February 1886 (Palermo, Brancaccio, Act No. 15): Vincenzo Mule, resident in Brancaccio, husband of Pisciotto Anna.

His daughter Rosalia was born in Zisa, Palermo, married Pasquale Carbonaro on 27 February 1886 in Brancaccio, and died 22 October 1893 in Palermo. Her birth year is not settled: she is 23 on the February 1886 marriage act and 23 again on her daughter Ignazia’s August 1889 birth act.

His grandchildren Angelo, Ignazia, and Vincenzo Carbonaro were born in Brancaccio, Palermo, in 1887, 1889, and 1892.

Life Story

Vincenzo Mule lived in Brancaccio, Palermo, Sicily. He is named as the father of Rosalia Mule on her marriage act of 27 February 1886, when she married Pasquale Carbonaro. His daughter was born in Zisa, Palermo, married in Brancaccio in 1886, and died in Palermo on 22 October 1893. His grandchildren Angelo, Ignazia, and Vincenzo Carbonaro were born in Brancaccio in 1887, 1889, and 1892. No record of Vincenzo’s own birth, marriage, or death has been found.
