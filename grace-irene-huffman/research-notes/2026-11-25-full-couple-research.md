# Grace Irene Huffman — parent, sibling, child, and photo research update

**Date:** 25 November 2026

## Identity and 1950 marriage clue

The 1950 U.S. census original schedule for Linville Township, Burke County, ED 12-13, sheet 26, was reviewed. It lists Melvin T. Moore, 21, as head, and Irene Moore, 17, North Carolina-born, as his wife. Geni identifies Melvin's spouse as Grace Irene Huffman, born in 1933 in Morganton. Their age, state, and place context align. A derivative compiled-name index also lists Grace Irene Huffman among the aliases of a woman called Irene Ben Moore / Grace Irene King. This supports—but does not itself document—that the 1950 “Irene Moore” is Grace. Keep this identification **probable (~75%)**, not established; no marriage certificate or original birth record has been found.

The birth date conflicts: Geni says 17 May 1933; a derivative SortedByName compilation says 17 March 1933. The 1950 age only supports about 1932–1933, not a day or month. Retain “about 1933” pending an original record.

The census itself does not establish Grace's birth surname or her parents. A thumbnail of this original page is archived in Grace's GitHub documents folder.

**Working assessment:** Nannie M. Huffman/Coleman is a **probable mother lead (~65%)**, not proven. Richard Benfield is a **possible/probable father lead (~60%)**, derived from the name compilation only. Coleman vs. Huffman vs. Benfield name sequence, marriage(s), and identity are unverified. No parent should be shown as documentary fact or used to create a new profile without new evidence.
