# William M Wooton — parentage and family audit

Reviewed 26 November 2026. FamilySearch public-tree profile LJ5M-XH8 reports William M Wooton (1841–1922), husband of Rachel Baker (married 28 March 1860), and nine children. Its daughter Elizabeth Ann Wooton (1869–1954) is the leading candidate for the woman whose later names appear as Lizaney Wooton (1870), Elizana Asher (1900), Elizane Wooten (in a Kentucky death-record entry), and Elizone Asher on Russell’s memorial. The original image is not independently transcribed here.

This profile intentionally does not infer William’s own parents, notwithstanding a conflicting shared-tree claim with implausible ages. It is limited to his marriage and child group relevant to the current task.
