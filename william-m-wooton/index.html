Life Story

The available source trail places William M Wooton in Leslie County, Kentucky, and the FamilySearch public-tree profile names Rachel Baker as his wife and lists nine children, including Elizabeth Ann Wooton, born in 1869. A census source title attached to Elizabeth’s profile reads “Lizaney Wooton in household of William Wooton” in 1870, which is consistent with this parent-family placement. The underlying record images and the family tree remain subject to direct source review; this profile distinguishes the compiled family group from independently proven relationships.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Candidate identification: Elizabeth Ann Wooton is a strong working match for the “Elizone” named on Russell’s memorial, based on the 1900 index name “Elizana Asher” in Kilas Asher’s household, a 1870 census source titled “Lizaney Wooton in household of William Wooton,” a Kentucky death-record source titled “Elizane Wooten in entry for William Asher,” and the shared-tree assignment of Elizabeth as Russel W. Asher’s mother. Confidence is about 75% for this identity bridge; original schedules and the death-record image have not been independently transcribed.
  • There is a substantial conflict with the project’s existing Letcher Asher (1868–1910) profile: the FamilySearch public-tree family group identifies Russell’s father as a different Letcher Kilas Asher (1870–1954), husband of Elizabeth Ann Wooton. The cemetery note has only the first name “Letcher.” This new record trail does not prove which Letcher is represented in the existing profile; do not merge or silently change the existing spouse/parent links.
  • The family link of Elizabeth Ann Wooton to William M Wooton and Rachel Baker is stated in the public tree and supported by the source-title trail above, but no original birth or direct parent-child record was reviewed. Treat the couple as a provisional working parent assignment, about 70% confidence pending direct image review.
  • Dates differ: Elizabeth’s tree birth is November 1869; 1870 census title spells her “Lizaney”; Russell’s memorial says 1899 while FamilySearch gives 9 July 1898. Preserve differences.
