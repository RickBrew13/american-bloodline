Family Relationship Notes

John Henry Forste’s Find a Grave memorial reports Emma Mohs as his spouse, gives her lifespan as 1864–1950, and lists their marriage in 1886. The memorial also lists Wilbur Mark Forste among their children. Wilbur’s Ancestry and Find a Grave profiles name Emma Mohs as his mother and John Henry as his father. These are compiled sources; original records have not been examined here.

John’s memorial lists seven additional children: Herbert William, Frank, Ida, Flora, Clarence, Robert Louis, and Clifford Raymond. They are retained in the family list, but their dates and individual parentage have not been independently reviewed. Emma’s own parents, siblings, exact vital dates, birthplace, residence, occupation, and burial are not established in the material reviewed.
