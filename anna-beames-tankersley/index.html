Life Story

Anna Beames was born in Kentucky about 1820 SUPPORTED. She married William Tankersley about 1840, and over the next twenty-five years she bore at least twelve children, among them Eliza, who married Asberry Helton DOCUMENTED.

Anna died in Knox County in April 1880 DOCUMENTED. She is most likely buried at Lynn Camp Cemetery in Corbin, under a stone now too worn to read SUPPORTED.
