# Angelo Carbonaro — dig notes
Date: 28 November 2026. FamilySearch signed-in pass (RickBrewster1), images read and re-read letter by letter.

## What was found
- Angelo Carbonaro is named as Pasquale's father on Pasquale's marriage act of 27 February 1886 (Palermo, Brancaccio, Act No. 15; ARK 3:1:3QS7-997B-VWVB): Angelo, 55, miller, resident in Palermo. The marriage application (Publication No. 11, 7 February 1886; ARK 3:1:3QSQ-G97B-VRCG) gives the same: Pasquale, 31, miller, son of Angelo, 55, miller. So Angelo was born about 1830-1831.
- He is named again in the Vallelunga marriage papers of 26 November 1895 (letter No. 32; ARK 3:1:3Q9M-CS86-SQQ4-M), described as deceased ("del fu Angelo"), as is his wife Ignazia Fanara. Both were dead by that date.
- His son Pasquale was born 15 February 1855 in Palermo (poverty certificate, Vallelunga, 17 November 1895; ARK 3:1:3QS7-997Y-LPVY), baptized in the parish of Albergheria, worked as a miller, married Rosalia Mule 27 February 1886, was widowed 22 October 1893, and remarried Giuseppa Noto 19 December 1895 in Vallelunga Pratameno.

## Not found / not assigned
- No record of Angelo himself beyond the two namings above. No birth, marriage, or death record for him has been found.
- A Palermo marriage index entry of 27 September 1851 (v. 317, n. 262) names a Carbonaro Angelo marrying an Isabella; if this is the same Angelo, Ignazia Fanara would be a second wife. The act has not been read; not assigned.
- Two index leads may be other sons of Angelo and Ignazia Fanara: Carollo Angelo, married 7 August 1884 in Zisa (v. 111, n. 55), and Gaetano Paolo Maniscalco, married 7 June 1879 (v. 74). The index images were not read; neither is listed as a son.
