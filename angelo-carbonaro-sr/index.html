Family Relationship Notes

Angelo Carbonaro is named as Pasquale’s father on Pasquale’s marriage act of 27 February 1886 (Palermo, Brancaccio, Act No. 15): Angelo, 55, miller, resident in Palermo. The marriage application gives the same: Pasquale, 31, miller, son of Angelo, 55, miller.

He is named again in the Vallelunga marriage papers of 26 November 1895, where he is described as deceased (“del fu Angelo”), as is his wife Ignazia Fanara (“della fu Ignazia Fanara”). Both were already dead by that date.

His son Pasquale was born 15 February 1855 in Palermo, worked as a miller, married Rosalia Mule on 27 February 1886 in Brancaccio, was widowed 22 October 1893, and remarried Giuseppa Noto on 19 December 1895 in Vallelunga Pratameno.

Life Story

Angelo Carbonaro was a miller living in Palermo, Sicily. He was 55 in February 1886, when his son Pasquale married Rosalia Mule in Brancaccio, Palermo, and the marriage act names him as Pasquale’s father. By November 1895 he was dead, named as deceased in Pasquale’s Vallelunga marriage papers, as was his wife Ignazia Fanara. His son Pasquale was born 15 February 1855 in Palermo, worked as a miller, was widowed in 1893, and remarried in Vallelunga Pratameno in 1895. No record of Angelo’s own birth, marriage, or death has been found.

Pasquale Carbonaro, 31, miller, born Palermo, son of Angelo Carbonaro and Fanara Ignazia, married Mulè Rosalia, 23, born Zisa, daughter of Vincenzo Mule and Pisciotto Anna, 27 February 1886, Casa comunale di Brancaccio, before Michele Patricolo.   3. Vallelunga Pratameno, Marriage Supplements 1894–1911, letter No. 32, 26 November 1895. Diocese of Caltanissetta: Pasquale Carbonaro, son of the late Angelo and the late Ignazia Fanara, widower since 22 October 1893 of the late Rosalia Mule, born in Palermo in 1855, baptized in the parish of Albergheria, had lived in Vallelunga about nine months, asking to marry Giuseppa Noto, daughter of Francesco Noto and Maria Luzi. The letter states Rosalia’s death date twice. The letter was read from the image and re-read letter by letter in a second pass.
  4. Palermo, Marriage Banns Supplements 1895, poverty certificate, Vallelunga, 17 November 1895. Carbonaro Pasquale, son of Angelo and of Ignazia Fanara, miller, born in Palermo 15 February 1855; Noto Giuseppa, daughter of Francesco and of Maria Lappi, born in Vallelunga 31 March 1877. Both declared truly poor.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • No record of Angelo himself has been found beyond his naming as Pasquale’s father on the 1886 marriage act and application and the 1895 Vallelunga papers.
  • A Palermo marriage index entry of 27 September 1851 (v. 317, n. 262) names a Carbonaro Angelo marrying an Isabella; if this is the same Angelo (born about 1830–1831), Ignazia Fanara would be a second wife. The act has not been read and the entry is not assigned.
  • Two index leads may be other sons of Angelo and Ignazia Fanara: Carollo Angelo, married 7 August 1884 in Zisa (v. 111, n. 55), and Gaetano Paolo Maniscalco, married 7 June 1879 (v. 74). The index images were not read; neither is listed as a son here.
  • No death record for Angelo has been found. Next useful records: the Palermo marriage records of the 1850s for Angelo Carbonaro and Ignazia Fanara, and the Palermo death records before November 1895.
