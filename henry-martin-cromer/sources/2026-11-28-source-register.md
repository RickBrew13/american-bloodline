# Henry Martin “Hen” Cromer — source register

- FamilySearch Henry Martin Cromer profile (KG8R-96H): https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/KG8R-96H
  - Compiled profile gives parents David Crockett Cromer and Elizabeth Southard, spouse(s), and child list.
- Kentucky death record, Henry Martin Cromer (NSPD-SRX): https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:NSPD-SRX
  - Index and original image reviewed: death 15 December 1955; father “Dave Cromer”; mother is recorded as unknown/blank.
- 1890 Kentucky marriage, Henry Cromer and Sarah E. Bradley (FW14-68F): https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:FW14-68F
  - Index gives marriage 2 August 1890. Henry’s profile and the Rockcastle family transcription expand the names as Henry Martin Cromer and Sarah Louisa Bradley.
- 1910 U.S. census, Henry and Sarah Cromer household (M26R-NCG): https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:M26R-NCG
  - Index and original image reviewed. Household includes Henry and Sarah with children listed as Maggy, Pearly, Roy, Dewey, Dora A., Hugh, Ida, and Roberta.
- 1930 U.S. census, Henry C. Cromer household (XMFJ-D9D): https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XMFJ-D9D
  - Original schedule and index show Henry with later wife Martha, son Austin, step-sons Arley and Earl Doan, and infant Eugene; not evidence that those stepchildren were Sarah’s.
- Rockcastle County Cromer family transcription: https://kygenweb.net/rockcastle/family/cromer/
  - Compiled family page names David Cromer and Elizabeth Southard as Henry’s parents and lists the children; derivative source, not treated as a primary parent record.

- 1860 U.S. census, David Croomer household (MZB8-J83): https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MZB8-J83
  - Original page image reviewed; David Croomer (21), Elizabeth (18), William (3), and Mary C. (1), Rockcastle County. Henry was born in 1869 and is not listed; candidate-household context only.
