Family Relationship Notes

FamilySearch compiled profile 29SH-F11 and his son Roscoe’s profile identify Daniel W. Carman and Sarah Jane Bastin as Roscoe’s parents. Isham Carman’s profile 972J-2BN lists Daniel among the children of Isham Carman and Frances Stephens. These are public compiled family profiles, not original birth records.

The public Daniel profile is internally inconsistent: its life-history paragraph says Daniel married Mary Ashley on 27 March 1866, while its spouse-and-children section identifies Sarah Jane Bastin as his spouse and reports seven children including Roscoe. A separate FamilySearch profile reports the Daniel–Sarah marriage about 1867. The Mary Ashley relationship and sequence are unresolved; no marriage record image was reviewed.

FamilySearch and Find a Grave differ on Daniel’s birth date: 5 February versus 2 May 1836. The existing Roscoe profile keeps its pre-existing date note unchanged. Do not choose one date without reviewing a primary record.
