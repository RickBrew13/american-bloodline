Family Relationship Notes

FamilySearch compiled profile 29SH-F11 and his son Roscoe’s profile identify Daniel W. Carman and Sarah Jane Bastin Carman (listed as Sarah Jane Bastin in those compiled profiles) as Roscoe’s parents. Isham Carman’s profile 972J-2BN lists Daniel among the children of Isham Carman and Frances Stephens. These are public compiled family profiles, not original birth records. The public Daniel profile is internally inconsistent: its life-history paragraph says Daniel married Mary Ashley on 27 March 1866, while its spouse-and-children section identifies Sarah Jane Bastin Carman (listed there as Sarah Jane Bastin) as his spouse and reports seven children including Roscoe. A separate FamilySearch profile reports the Daniel–Sarah marriage about 1867. The Mary Ashley relationship and sequence are unresolved; no marriage record image was reviewed. FamilySearch and Find a Grave differ on Daniel’s birth date: 5 February versus 2 May 1836. The existing Roscoe profile keeps its pre-existing date note unchanged. Do not choose one date without reviewing a primary record.

Name resolution: Daniel’s wife and Roscoe’s mother is best identified as Sarah Jane Bastin Carman (high confidence, about 90%). The original 1910 schedule identifies widowed “Sarah J. Carman”; Sarah’s own 1921 Kentucky death certificate records “Sarah Jane Carman” and names parents William Bastin and Sarah Ann Salyers. The 1900 schedule records “Sarah J. Carmon” as wife of Daniel N. Carmon, but identity of that enumerated Daniel to this profile is not completely independent because the schedule age and initial differ from the compiled Daniel W profile. Roscoe’s later 1939 death-certificate reproduction instead reports mother “Mary Jane Bastin” (informant Roy Carman). In light of Sarah’s own certificate and the 1910 schedule, Mary Jane is most likely a reporting/recording error; preserve the conflicting wording without creating a second spouse or a proven alias.
