# Source register — Daniel W. Carman

Date reviewed: 28 November 2026
- [FamilySearch public Daniel W. Carman profile (29SH-F11)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/29SH-F11/daniel-w-carman-1836-1905) — Compiled profile gives birth 5 February 1836, death 24 March 1905, parents Isham Carman and Frances Stephens, Sarah Jane Bastin spouse-and-children section, and seven children; narrative separately reports 27 March 1866 marriage to Mary Ashley. Underlying marriage images were not reviewed.
- [FamilySearch, 1900 U.S. census, Daniel N Carmon household (M9WP-NR9)](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:M9WP-NR9) — Original schedule sheet 19B reviewed. Shows Daniel N. Carmon with wife Sarah J. Carmon and daughter Quintella on lines 98–100. FamilySearch’s extended-family index associates Roscoe as son, but Roscoe is not visible in this scanned household segment.
- [FamilySearch, 1910 U.S. census, Sarah J Carman (M28L-WV7)](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:M28L-WV7) — Index and original schedule image 547 reviewed; records Sarah J. Carman, widowed head, in Walltown, Casey County.
- [FamilySearch, Kentucky Deaths, 1911–1967, Sarah Jane Carman (N9PL-WRK)](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:N9PL-WRK) — Original image 788 / certificate file 7379 reviewed; records Daniel’s spouse as Sarah Jane Carman and names parents William Bastin and Sarah Ann Salyers (index: Salyer).
- [FamilySearch public Isham Carman profile (972J-2BN)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/972J-2BN/isham-carman-1812-1880) — Compiled profile identifies Isham and Frances Stephens as Daniel’s parents, reports their 24 December 1835 marriage and lists 14 children including Daniel.
- [FamilySearch public Roscoe Carman profile (KJZ3-7VS)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/KJZ3-7VS/roscoe-carman-1882-1939) — Compiled child profile identifies Daniel W. Carman and Sarah Jane Bastin as Roscoe’s parents.
- [Find a Grave memorial 17419225, Daniel W. Carman](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/17419225/daniel_w-carman) — User-contributed memorial reports 2 May 1836–24 March 1905 and Salyers Cemetery; photo page lists zero photos.
- [Roscoe Carman Kentucky death-certificate reproduction, Find a Grave memorial 17419340](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/17419340/roscoe-carman/photo) — Reproduction credited to Rochelle Riordan (24 May 2014); names father Daniel Carman and mother “Mary Jane Bastin,” informant Roy Carman. The latter conflicts with Sarah’s own 1921 death certificate and census records; Sarah Jane is the better-supported identity.
