# Daniel W. Carman — research note

Date: 28 November 2026

- The FamilySearch 1900 census index and original sheet 19B identify Daniel N. Carmon and wife Sarah J. Carmon; the scan visibly shows Quintella but not Roscoe among lines 98–100, although the index’s extended-family grouping links Roscoe as a son.
- The original 1910 schedule records widowed Sarah J. Carman. Her original 1921 Kentucky death certificate records Sarah Jane Carman and names parents William Bastin and Sarah Ann Salyers (index: Salyer).
- On the evidence, Daniel’s wife is best identified as Sarah Jane Bastin Carman (high confidence, about 90%). Roscoe’s later 1939 death-certificate reproduction says “Mary Jane Bastin,” informant Roy Carman; treat this as a likely reporting/recording error, retain the literal reading, and do not create a separate Mary Jane spouse without new evidence.
- Daniel’s public FamilySearch page separately shows a Mary Ashley marriage in its narrative and Sarah Jane Bastin in the spouse-and-children section. Keep that separate spouse entry unresolved; it is not evidence that Roscoe’s mother was Mary Jane.


- Austins of America, August 1985, p. 167, entry J3E6 on Ruth Ellen Carman Horton Austin, identifies her parents as Daniel and “Sarah Jane (Bastin) Carman.” Compiled secondary genealogy; not an original record and not a direct record for Roscoe.
