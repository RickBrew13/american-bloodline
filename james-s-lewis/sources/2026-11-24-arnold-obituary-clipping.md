# Arnold F. Lewis obituary clipping

Image clipping is stored in `documents/Arnold-F-Lewis-2000-obituary-clipping.jpg`. It was contributed to Arnold Floyd Lewis’s Find a Grave photo tab by Ron Roberts on 3 January 2023. The visible clipping identifies Arnold F. Lewis, age 77, of Collinsville; born 23 August 1923 in Lesterville; died 24 November 2000 at Maryville Manor. It names wife Martha née Sullivan; children Edward, Patricia Dominick, Pamela Embick, Jeffrey and Thomas; parents James S. and Emma née Lee; and siblings Elmo, Winford, Audrey Pinkley, Wilma Meyers, Osa Mazo, Mildred Sherrill, and Bernice Durham. The clipping states burial at St. John Cemetery, Collinsville.
