# James S. Lewis — Gen VII source and family review

**Reviewed:** 24 November 2026.

## Evidence

The NARA original image for the 1950 U.S. census, Reynolds County, Missouri, enumeration district 90-5, sheet 26 (enumerated 26 April 1950), records James S. Lewis, age 58, as head of household; Emma L., age 59, as wife; daughter Lora B., age 23 (surname appears as James); and granddaughters Sharon L. and Karen L., both age 5. The image and its line relationships were inspected directly. Birthplace entries for James and Emma are Missouri; their ages suggest approximate births 1891/1892.

A secondary transcription of Arnold Floyd Lewis's 1941 SS-5 reports the same parents, James S. Lewis and Emma L. Lee, and Arnold's birth in Lesterville on 23 August 1923. This connects Arnold to the James/Emma couple strongly, but the original SS-5 remains to be inspected.

## Children / sibling question

- Arnold Floyd Lewis (working identity), linked to the Gen VI Arnold Lewis profile.
- Lora B. (recorded/indexed as James), daughter in the 1950 census. Given Arnold's SS-5 names James and Emma, likely Arnold's sister; exact identity/name change unresolved.
- The two granddaughters on the sheet are not assigned to Arnold or as additional children of James/Emma.

## Boundaries

Parents, siblings, death, burial and exact marriage details for James are not established. No Generation VIII profiles were created and no other Lewis relatives were promoted from same-name leads.
