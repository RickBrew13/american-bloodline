Family Relationship Notes

Ralph Morrow Powell is Dexter Galen Powell’s father. His death record names his parents Ruben Powell and Ellen Powell and his wife Rosalina Powell, and gives his age as 49 when he died on 31 March 1866 in Doddridge County. His son Dexter’s 1931 death record names Ralph Powell as Dexter’s father, and Dexter’s 1903 marriage license names Ralph M. Powell and Roxaline Powell as Dexter’s parents. The records spell his wife’s first name many ways — Rosalina on the marriage record and his death record, Rocksalina in the 1850 census, Roxaline on the 1903 license — and her tombstone reads Rocksalina.

Ralph married Rosalina Williams in Harrison County, Virginia, in September 1838. The two copies of the marriage record give 17 and 20 September.

Life Story

Ralph Morrow Powell was born in Harrison County, West Virginia, and farmed in Doddridge County. He married Rosalina Williams in Harrison County in September 1838. The 1850 census lists him in Doddridge County with his wife and five of their children. He died on 31 March 1866 in Doddridge County at 49 and is buried in Victory Cemetery. His son Dexter Galen Powell, born in 1850, married Minnie M. Lyon in 1903 and was the father of Rowland Edmund Powell.
