# Harry O. Patton × Lola G. Patton — research update (25 November 2026)

## 1950 household, original schedule checked

The 5 April 1950 census, Pike Township, Brown County, Ohio, Enumeration District 8-26, spans pages 6–7 (image 7, digital folder 108876829). On page 6 the schedule lists Harry O. Patton, 37, head; wife Lola G. Patton, 36; Ronald C. Sparks, 14, explicitly “step son” to the head; David N. Patton, 8, son; and Carolyn J. Patton, 5, daughter. The household is marked as continued on the next sheet. Page 7 starts with Daniel G. Patton, age 3, relationship “son”; it is the continuation. Harry’s census occupation is indexed as “Building and Archway,” industry “Carpenter”; handwriting is difficult to read. Harry’s and Lola’s birthplace is Ohio. The census does not name either person’s parents; Lola’s record gives both parents’ birthplaces as USA.

## Children and related household members

- David N. Patton, age 8 (estimated birth 1942), listed as son.
- Carolyn J. Patton, age 5 (estimated birth 1945), listed as daughter.
- Daniel G. Patton, age 3 (estimated birth 1947), listed as son on page 7.
- Ronald C. Sparks, age 14, listed as Harry’s stepson, not a joint child of Harry and Lola. His parentage is not stated. Do not assume Lola was his mother.

Daniel G. Patton is a strong likely match to Daniel “Gus” Patten (died 15 June 2006 at age 59). The Cincinnati Enquirer obituary says he had siblings “the late Dave Patten,” Ronnie Sparks, and Janey Stormer. Dave and Ronnie correspond by name to David N. Patton and Ronald C. Sparks in the census household. The age window and sibling concordance support the match; no direct birth/death record has been located to bridge Daniel G. Patton to Daniel Gus Patten or explain Patton/Patten. Janey Stormer is not in the 1950 household; her relation to Harry and Lola remains unknown. Do not add her as their child without a record.

## Parentage and earlier-life searches

Harry: 1950 census age 37 gives estimated birth year 1913, Ohio. It lists no parent names; indexed father/mother birthplaces are USA. Targeted FamilySearch 1930/1940 census searches using Harry Patton/Patten and the Ohio birth-year window produced zero results. These are bounded searches, not proof of absence. No parents or siblings are identified.

Lola: 1950 census age 36 gives estimated birth year 1914, Ohio. It gives parents’ birthplaces (USA), but not names. FamilySearch’s 1950 record page lists “Lola G Beach” in NUMIDENT and “Lola Genieve Beach” in Ohio County Marriages / Ohio Stillbirths under “Similar Records.” These similarity suggestions are unverified leads only; no underlying record has been inspected, and Beach is not established as Lola’s maiden name. Targeted 1930/1940 FamilySearch searches under Patton in the expected age range returned no results. No parents or siblings are identified.


## Same-name Harry O. Patton exclusion

A separate FamilySearch 1950 census record for Pamela B. Patton places her in a Hempstead, Nassau County, New York household with a Harry O. Patton, age 42, born in New York, and wife Katherine. Our Harry O. Patton is age 37, Ohio-born, in Pike Township, Brown County, Ohio, with wife Lola. The census records distinguish them by age, birthplace, household, and wife. The New York Pamela is not assigned as a child of Harry and Lola. A derivative SortedByName lead also associated “Katherine L Baisley” with a Harry O. Patton and Pamela; the stronger census comparison confirms the named father belongs to the separate New York household, not the Ohio couple.
