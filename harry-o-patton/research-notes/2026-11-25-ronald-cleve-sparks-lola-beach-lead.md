# Harry O. Patton and Lola G. (likely Beach) Patton — follow-up research (25 November 2026)

## New Ronald C. Sparks / Lola Beach lead

The 1950 U.S. census schedule for Pike Township, Brown County, Ohio, ED 8-26, page 6 lists Ronald C. Sparks, age 14, explicitly as Harry O. Patton’s “step son.” The household lists Lola G. Patton, age 36, as Harry’s wife. (The schedule continues on page 7, where Daniel G. Patton, age 3, is listed as a son.)

A secondary compilation, “Sparks Family History” on SortedByName.com, has an entry for “Ronald C,” also “Ronald Cleve Sparks.” The displayed search-result text reports a birth on 6 March 1936 in “Wmsburg Cler” (interpreted as Williamsburg, Clermont County, Ohio), identifies Cleves Sparks as father and Lola G. Beach as mother, and says the record is based on a Social Security application (SS-5). This full name, matching approximate birth year, and the census’s explicit step-son relationship make it a strong working identification of the census Ronald as Lola’s son from an earlier relationship. It is not a transcription of the original SS-5, and no original application, birth certificate, or marriage record was reviewed in this pass. Do not state that Lola and Cleves were married; the record supports only parentage as reported by this derivative source.

**Assessment:** Lola G. Beach as the same woman as the 1950 Lola G. Patton is strongly plausible, with the census stepchild relationship and SS-5-derived parental statement converging; it is still not proven by an inspected primary record. Cleves Sparks is a likely biological father of Ronald based on that derivative report. No parents or siblings of Lola are identified by this evidence.
