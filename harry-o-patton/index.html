Family Relationship Notes

The 5 April 1950 census schedule for Pike Township, Brown County, Ohio (ED 8-26, pages 6–7) lists Harry O. Patton, age 37, Ohio-born, as head, with wife Lola G. Patton, age 36, Ohio-born. Page 6 lists David N. Patton, 8, and Carolyn J. Patton, 5, as their children, and Ronald C. Sparks, 14, as Harry’s stepson. The household is marked “continued on next sheet”; page 7 lists Daniel G. Patton, 3, as a son. The original sheets were checked directly.

Daniel G. Patton is a strong, but not conclusive, match to Daniel “Gus” Patten (died 15 June 2006, age 59). His obituary identifies a late brother Dave Patten and brother Ronnie Sparks, corresponding to David N. Patton and Ronald C. Sparks in the 1950 household. It also names sister Janey Stormer, who is not accounted for in that household; her relationship to Harry and Lola is unresolved. The Patton/Patten surname difference lacks a direct identity document.

Harry’s birth year 1913 and Ohio birthplace are reported by the 1950 census. The record gives the parents’ birthplaces as USA but does not name his parents. No reliable evidence naming Harry’s parents or siblings has been found in this pass; exact birth/death dates and burial remain unknown.

A secondary compiled entry for Ronald C. Sparks, also called Ronald Cleve Sparks, attributes information to his Social Security application and names Cleves Sparks as father and Lola G. Beach as mother. It reports his birth as 6 March 1936 in Williamsburg, Clermont County, Ohio. The name, approximate birth year, and 1950 schedule’s explicit “stepson” relationship make this a strong working match to Harry’s stepson Ronald. It suggests Ronald was Lola’s child from an earlier relationship, but the original SS-5 has not been inspected, and this entry does not establish a marriage between Lola and Cleves.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Harry O. Patton is documented in the 1950 census as age 37 and born in Ohio. That record does not name his parents; the 1930 and 1940 census searches for Harry Patton/Patten with the matching Ohio birth-year range returned no results in the targeted FamilySearch collections. This is bounded search evidence, not proof that no earlier record exists.
  • No reliable record has yet established Harry’s parents or siblings. The three Patton children in the 1950 household are David N., Carolyn J., and Daniel G. The Daniel/Gus identity remains likely from age and obituary sibling concordance, not proven by a direct bridge.
  • Ronald C. Sparks is identified as Harry’s stepson on the original census schedule and should not be counted among Harry and Lola’s children together. Whether Lola was Ronald’s mother remains unstated on the schedule.
  • Daniel’s obituary names sister Janey Stormer. She is not identified in the 1950 household; do not place her as a child of Harry and Lola without a documentary bridge.
  • No verified portrait, family photograph, or gravestone has been located in the current profile folder. The 1950 census sheets are records, not portraits.
  • A separate 1950 FamilySearch census record shows another Harry O. Patton, age 42, born in New York, living with Katherine and daughter Pamela in Hempstead. This is not the subject here: the Ohio Harry is age 37 and Ohio-born. Do not attach Pamela or that household to this couple.
