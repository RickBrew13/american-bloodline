Family Relationship Notes

The original 1950 census schedule, Pike Township, Brown County, Ohio, lists Harry O. Patton, wife Lola G., and children in a household continued across pages 6–7. Page 7 lists Daniel G. Patton, age three, as a son. Page 6 also lists David N. Patton and Carolyn J. Patton as children and Ronald C. Sparks as a stepson.

Daniel G. Patton is strongly matched to Daniel “Gus” Patten, who died in 2006 at age 59 and whose obituary names brothers “the late Dave Patten” and Ronnie Sparks. The age window and sibling names fit, but no original vital record directly bridges the Patton/Patten spellings. This remains a likely identity match, not a proven one.

Harry’s birth year and Ohio birthplace are estimated from the 1950 census. His parents, full dates, death, burial, and earlier residence have not been identified in the records reviewed.
