## Children

Her 1939 Alabama marriage index names Cornelius as father and Rosabelle Grant as mother ([VZVK-B1Q](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1/VZVK-B1Q)); Ruby’s 1945 marriage index is reported to name Cornelius again and gives Rosa Bell White as the mother ([29NV-B74](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1/29NV-B74)). No additional child was identified in reviewed, identity-linked records. Ethelene Moore and George Howard Moore Jr. are reported as Ruby and George Howard Moore’s children; they are grandchildren in this line, not listed under Cornelius.
