## 8. Rosa Belle White — parents FOUND

Rosa Belle Pugh, Alabama Deaths, 1908-1974: died 1951, Birmingham, Jefferson Co., age 46 (b. est. 1905), certificate no. cn 16719; parents **Pete White** (father) and **Sallie Parker** (mother). ARK 1:1:J6QM-K56. No spouse named in the index and no exact death date or birthplace. This proves Rosa's maiden name was White and her parents were Pete White and Sallie Parker. The 1950 census (Rosa B in Cornelius's household) plus a 1951 death fits. The "Rosabelle Grant" name in Ruby's 1939 marriage record is therefore unexplained: it may be an indexing error, or Rosa may have used Grant (e.g., a stepfather's name), or — importantly — Ruby (b. ~Dec 1922, before Cornelius married Rosa on 7 Sep 1924) may be Cornelius's daughter by an earlier wife named Rosabelle Grant, with Rosa Belle White as her stepmother. The 1945 marriage names Rosa Bell White as Ruby's mother, so this is unresolved. "Rosabelle Grant" as a child in the 1910/1920 censuses was not searched to completion; Pete White and Sallie Parker as a household in 1910/1920 was not found in the indexed search (only the marriage of Peter White to Sallie Smith, 2 Dec 1903, Autauga, and their children Rosa Belle, Ell White, Buster White and Gladys Bell White, named on death/marriage records).
