# Cornelius Pugh — African American parent and family-structure search update

**Research date:** 25 November 2026 (America/New_York)  
**Profile:** [Cornelius Pugh](https://rickbrew13.github.io/american-bloodline/cornelius-pugh/)  
**Family context:** Rick identifies the Pugh family in this research as African American. This is the family-history context for searches; it does not, by itself, identify a particular record or establish an individual relationship.

## Ruby’s father — current evidence

Ruby Mae Pugh’s 1939 marriage index names Cornelius Pugh as her father and Rosabelle Grant as her mother ([FamilySearch VZVK-B1Q](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1/VZVK-B1Q)); the 1945 index is reported to repeat Cornelius and gives Rosa Bell White as the mother ([29NV-B74](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1/29NV-B74)). These two indexes support the father–daughter claim, but the original marriage pages were not inspected in this pass. The 1930 census index places Ruby, age 7, with Rosabelle Pugh, age 22, in a Birmingham household. It does not put Cornelius in that household.

## Parent candidates checked

Ancestry’s public Cornelius Pugh search results returned 35 profiles and several incompatible Alabama claims. One compiled profile reports a Cornelius born about 1900 in Alabama with parents David H. Pugh and Sarah Elizabeth Bush. Another result proposes a different parent, Dora Moore, for a Cornelius born about 1899 in Alabama. The results also include many men born in other places and centuries. The conflicting names and dates show the danger of same-name conflation; no reviewed original or near-contemporary record connects David, Sarah, or Dora to Ruby’s father.

**Decision:** David H. Pugh and Sarah Elizabeth Bush, and Dora Moore, remain unlinked candidate names only. Do not add them as Cornelius’s Gen VII parents or create linked parent profiles unless a record bridge identifies this particular father of Ruby.

## Additional-child / partner lead

A [SortedByName Mattie Patterson aggregation page](https://sortedbyname.com/letter_p/patterson/mattie_patterson_1.html), echoed in web-search snippets, reports a son known as N. Patterson / Norman Patterson, born 14 December 1930 in Birmingham, with mother Mattie Patterson and father Cornelius Pugh. This is a significant lead for the requested other-child/spouse investigation, but the page aggregates many unrelated Mattie Pattersons and does not reveal the underlying source in the accessible text. It does not distinguish which Cornelius Pugh is named or connect him to Ruby’s father. It is **not** sufficient evidence to list Norman as a confirmed child or Mattie as a spouse; the birth record needs to be found and compared with Ruby’s father’s identifying facts.

A FamilyTreeNow search snippet for Rosabelle Pugh (approx. 1908, Birmingham) lists a mixed “Family” group containing Cornelius Pugh, Ruby M. Pugh, Adam Sheppard, Daniel Townsley, Savannah Wells, and Hannah Roan. The page is blocked by human verification; labels and source records cannot be inspected. Do not treat these search-result names as siblings, spouses, or additional children.

## African American research routes searched / queued

Broad web searches combining Cornelius Pugh, Ruby, Alabama, Birmingham, and Black/African American did not surface an identity-linked parent record. The Alabama county-by-county African American genealogy guide recommends checking the 1866 Alabama State Census, 1870 census, and Freedmen’s Bureau materials. These are research routes—not discoveries about this family. The 1866 and 1870 entries may help locate older Pugh households and distinguish branches, but a shared surname or nearby household alone will not prove parentage.

## What is needed to add Cornelius’s parents to Gen VII

2. Inspect Ruby’s 1939/1945 marriage images and the original 1930 schedule, then search 1940 census and Birmingham city directories/newspapers for Cornelius.
3. Search the 1866 Alabama State Census, 1870–1920 census, Freedmen’s Bureau, church, cemetery, probate, and local records using spelling variants. The correct county is not yet established for Cornelius; do not assume Lowndes from unrelated Pugh research.
4. Add a Gen VII parent only after a record or sufficiently connected evidence identifies that candidate as Ruby’s father’s parent. Until then, no candidate parent profile or reciprocal link is warranted.
