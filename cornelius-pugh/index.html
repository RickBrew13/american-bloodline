Family Relationship Notes

Rick identifies this Pugh family as African American. I used that as the historical research context and did not treat unrelated same-name Pughs as relatives merely because their names match.

Ruby’s 1939 and 1945 marriage indexes name Cornelius Pugh as her father. They provide repeated support for Ruby as his daughter, but the original images were not reviewed here.

Ruby’s 1930 census index places Ruby, age 7, in the same Birmingham household as Rosabelle Pugh, age 22 (prior transcription: household 208, sheet 13B, line 55). It does not place Cornelius in that home or prove he married Rosabelle.

A secondary search result on SortedByName reports a child named Norman Patterson, born 14 December 1930 in Birmingham, with mother Mattie Patterson and father Cornelius Pugh. It is a lead extracted from secondary/indexed data, not an inspected birth record. The source does not identify which Cornelius Pugh this is or connect him to Ruby. Keep Mattie Patterson and Norman as an unverified other-child/partner lead, not a spouse or proven child of this Cornelius.

Ancestry search results separately show a Cornelius Pugh born about 1900 in Alabama with proposed parents David H. Pugh and Sarah Elizabeth Bush, as well as other Alabama Cornelius profiles with conflicting parent claims. None is linked to Ruby by a reviewed record. Do not assign David or Sarah as Cornelius’s parents without a bridge.

A prior Drive log reports Roger Pugh as Ruby’s uncle in a 1940 census context; the entry and relationship field were not rechecked, so Roger is not listed as Cornelius’s sibling.
