# Aaron Mahle — Generation VII parent investigation

**Reviewed:** 24 November 2026

## What is documented

The 1 July 1912 Hamilton County, Ohio, marriage application identifies the bride as Bella Mahle, 25, born in Russia, daughter of Aaron Mahle and Kati (Kate) Tahmali. This is the only located source naming Aaron as Bella's father. The original application image is linked at FamilySearch but redirects to sign-in in this session. The application itself does not state Aaron's age, birthplace, parents, siblings, or whether he was alive then.

## Name and identity boundary

Bella Mahle on the 1912 application is the working identification for the later Bertha/Bella Luft in the children's records. The later maternal surnames are transcribed as forms such as Tarmin, Tomarin or Tamarin, unlike “Mahle” on the application. The daughter Minnie Luft's Ohio death-index transcription gives father “Luft” and mother “Tomarin”; it does not name Aaron. The application supports Aaron as Bella Mahle's reported father, but the Bella-to-Bertha identity and surname spellings remain unresolved.
