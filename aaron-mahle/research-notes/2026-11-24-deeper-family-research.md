# Aaron Mahle — deeper parent/family review

**Reviewed:** 24 November 2026

## Parent-child evidence and Bella/Bertha identity

The 1 July 1912 Hamilton County, Ohio, marriage application names Bella Mahle, age 25, born in Russia, as daughter of Aaron Mahle and Kati (Kate) Tahmali. It is the only located direct source naming Aaron as Bella's father; the application's original image redirected to sign-in during this review.

A derivative SortedByName compilation transcribes Social Security applications filed by Bella's children. Eva's records identify her mother as Bella and also as Bertha Tarmin/Tumarn; Minnie’s card gives mother Belle; David’s gives mother Berthia. The listed forms include Eva's SS-5 65332608996 (Oct. 1962), 65332608997 (May 1938) and later SS-5 numbers; Minnie SS-5 65337767611 (May 1938); David SS-5 66150905894 (Feb. 1937). This is useful, converging derivative evidence for the working identity Bella Mahle = later Bertha Tarmin Luft. The actual applications were not inspected and do not establish Aaron's ancestry.

## William/Bill Tomarin: important possible sibling lead, not proved

FamilySearch's 1950 census index search returns William Tamarin, born in Russia in 1880, in Cincinnati on 1 April 1950; it shows no relationships. The JCGC Jewish cemetery register lists Bill Tomarin, born 15 April 1880, died 6 July 1957 and buried 9 July at Love Brothers Cemetery, Section 003, Row 02, Grave 81. The Bill entry names Violet Tomarin as his wife. SortedByName's derivative transcription of William's Form SS-5 65327807598 (April 1938) gives the same birth date, Russia, and parents Adron Tomarin and Kessia T. Zeltzman. The matching name variant, exact birth date, Russian origin, Cincinnati residence and later Ohio death make William Tamarin and Bill Tomarin very likely the same man.

That does **not** prove he was Bella's brother. His transcribed SS-5 parents “Adron Tomarin” and “Kessia T. Zeltzman” do not match the application names “Aaron Mahle” and “Kati Tahmali” without further explanation or a documentary bridge. The different surnames may point to variants, prior names, or a separate family; no option is established.

The cemetery register also contains Marcus Tomarin (10 Apr. 1882–30 Jul. 1974) with wife Sima, and Harry Tomarin (15 Jan. 1907–24 Jun. 1968) with Toby W. Tomarin recorded as his wife. The shared surname and Cincinnati cemetery do not establish that these people were Bill's siblings/children or Bella's relatives.
