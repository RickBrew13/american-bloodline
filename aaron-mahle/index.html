Family Relationship Notes

  • Aaron is named as Bella Mahle’s father on Bella’s 1 July 1912 marriage application; Kati (Kate) Tahmali is named as her mother. The application is the only located direct record naming Aaron as father.
  • Descendants’ SS-5 applications are transcribed in a derivative online compilation as naming their mother Bella/Belle/Berthia and using forms Bertha Tarmin/Tumarn; this supports the working identification Bella Mahle = later Bertha Tarmin Luft, but does not identify Aaron’s own parents or siblings.
  • A William Tamarin indexed in Cincinnati in 1950 (born Russia, 1880) is a strong identity match to Bill Tomarin in the Cincinnati Jewish cemetery register (15 Apr. 1880–6 Jul. 1957; spouse Violet). A transcribed 1938 SS-5 names Bill’s parents as Adron Tomarin and Kessia T. Zeltzman. These names differ from Aaron Mahle and Kati Tahmali; William/Bill is a plausible sibling lead only, not a proven child of Aaron and Kati.
  • The same cemetery register contains separate Tomarin entries for Marcus and Sima (buried together) and Harry/Toby, but it provides no connection from these people to Bill or to Bella. Shared surname and cemetery are not proof of kinship.
  • No birth/death record, parents, siblings, exact origin, or burial has been established for Aaron. Russia as a residence remains an inference from Bella’s reported birthplace.

Life Story

The 1912 application reports Aaron Mahle as father of Bella Mahle. Later descendants’ Social Security applications, as transcribed in a secondary compilation, refer to their mother as Bella/Belle/Berthia and use Tarmin/Tumarn forms, supporting the working Bella-to-Bertha identity but not identifying Aaron’s ancestry. A likely William/Bill Tomarin of the same age appears in Cincinnati records; his transcribed SS-5 gives parents Adron Tomarin and Kessia T. Zeltzman, not a proven match to Aaron and Kati. The possible sibling connection remains open.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • William Tamarin (1950 census index) and Bill Tomarin (cemetery register; exact birth date 15 Apr. 1880, Russia-origin SS-5 transcription) are very likely the same individual, with surname variant Tamarin/Tomarin and given-name form William/Bill. His cemetery record names wife Violet. The source describing his parents gives Adron Tomarin and Kessia T. Zeltzman; no record equates these people with Aaron Mahle and Kati Tahmali.
  • Bill is not listed in Aaron’s Siblings/Children card. No evidence yet establishes that he is Bella’s brother, or that Marcus Tomarin, Sima Tomarin, Harry Tomarin, or Toby Tomarin are siblings/children of Bill or Bella.
  • The compilation’s child SS-5 transcriptions strengthen the identification of Bella/Bertha as the mother of Minnie, David and Eva, while the application’s parent fields remain the sole located evidence for Aaron and Kati. The application’s 1887 birth date conflicts with the 1885 death/SSDI index date; the 1976 death certificate remains an important unresolved record.
  • No Generation VIII parent/sibling relationships were added. Priority: inspect original daughter SS-5 cards, Bella’s 1976 death certificate and William’s 1938 SS-5 before considering any parent or sibling assignment.
