Life Story

Lawrence Herman was born 5 September 1900 in Holdingford, Stearns County, Minnesota, the son of Peter Herman Sr. and Victoria Langner . He married Phyllis Budny in Detroit on 12 June 1922, and their son Chester William Herman was born in 1924 . After Phyllis, Lawrence married Eleanore R. Grzybowski in Detroit on 22 January 1934, and the family appears together in the 1940 census in Detroit . Lawrence died 11 November 1943 in Detroit of pneumonia, while living at 4554 Military Avenue, and was buried in Woodmere Cemetery, Detroit . His daughter Victoria later married Patrick L. Moore, and his daughter Janet's 2021 obituary names Lawrence and Eleanore as her parents .
