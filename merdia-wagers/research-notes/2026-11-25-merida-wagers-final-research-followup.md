## Family and identity review
- **Identity, parents, birth/death:** The profile's Social Security Numident, death index, VA grave marker and obituary transcribed on Find a Grave converge on Merida Henry Wagers, born 18 January 1930 in Perry County, Kentucky, son of Shirley Wagers and Amanda/Mandy Baker, died 29 January 2006 in London, Laurel County, Kentucky.
- **Childhood and siblings:** 1930 and 1940 census indexes place him with Shirley and Amanda in Clay County, Kentucky. His sister Aline Helton's 2004 obituary lists “Merdia Wagers” of London as living, independently disproving the erroneous 2004 death year found on some trees. His 2006 obituary names his parents, five sons (Billy, Paul, Bruce, Merdy and Jeffrey), late daughter Yvonne, and surviving brothers/sisters. The exact sibling list in the profile is drawn from his Find a Grave obituary transcription and sister's obituary; no additional siblings were verified in this pass.
- **Aileene and children:** Yvonne's Numident and Paul J. Wagers's 1971 Ohio marriage index name Merida Wagers and Aileene Brewster as parents. This supports their parentage but does not prove the date or legal existence of an Aileene–Merida marriage. Aileene's obituary lists Paul and William Wagers and late daughter Yvonne. Do not add Bruce, Merdy or Jeffrey as Aileene's children: the available notices do not establish their mother.
- **Later spouse leads:** The 1961 Helen Louise Henson marriage is currently only a compiled-tree lead. The 19 November 1979 Volusia County Florida marriage index does not identify the bride; the 9 May 1987 Laurel County Kentucky index names Pearl Hicks. Neither index alone proves the groom is this Merida Wagers. His 2006 obituary transcription does not name a wife. The profile wording has been tightened to make these evidence limits explicit.
- **Other descendants:** No new parentage claims for the five sons were made beyond what the existing 2006 obituary and family materials support.
