Family Relationship Notes

The original 1920 Lincoln County marriage record names Roscoe Carmen and Lucy Carmen as Lillie’s parents and records her marriage to William Pollard Henry on 1 June 1920.

The 1934 Kentucky birth index names Lillie Carman as Elmer’s mother, and the 1940 census index lists Elmer, age five, as the son of William and Lillie. The original census schedule has not been reviewed for this update.

Lillie’s 2026 obituary index names three children: David Elmer Henry, James A. Life Story

Lillie Mae Carman was born on 4 April 1904 in Casey County, Kentucky, the daughter of Roscoe Carmen and Lucy Carmen. She married William Pollard Henry on 1 June 1920 in Lincoln County, Kentucky — she was 16, from Waynesburg, and he was 18, a farmer from Arabia. Her son Elmer David Henry was born on 6 August 1934 in Lincoln County; the birth index names her as his mother. By 1940 the family had moved to Cincinnati, Ohio, where the census lists Lillie with William and their children. She died on 12 February 1995 in Florence, Boone County, Kentucky, and was buried in Erlanger, Kenton County, according to the FamilySearch family profile.

  • The FamilySearch profile gives William and Lillie’s marriage as 1 January 1920; the marriage bond image and index both say license 31 May 1920 and marriage 1 June 1920 — the record is authoritative.
