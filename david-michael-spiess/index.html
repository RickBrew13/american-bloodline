Life Story

David Michael Spiess was born on 1 August 1942, a son of Philip A. and Marie L. Spiess. In 1950 the family was living in Williamsburg Township, Clermont County, Ohio, with David and his older brother Ronald. A matching Frederick Funeral Home obituary for David M. Spiess Sr. reports his death on 11 January 2020 in Colerain Township, age 77; it names wife Penny (née Palmer), children, and brother Ronald. Those name/family details strengthen the identity match, though the obituary does not state David’s full middle name.

David married Penelope Jane "Penny" Palmer, a daughter of William Peter Palmer and Mary Jane Williams, sometime between 1964 and 1982. They had a son, David. David died before his brother Ronald, who died in February 2024; the date and place of his death have not yet been found.
