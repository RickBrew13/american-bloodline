      Life Story

      Very little about Otto Graff Sr. has been established from directly reviewed records. Tricia’s Shamblen/McKinney Ancestry tree identifies him as the husband of Bertha M. Kissel and the father of Otto Graff, reportedly born 5 May 1925 in Cincinnati. It gives Otto Sr. approximate birth year 1902 and death year 1963. The tree lists census, draft, military, death, and burial sources for the son, but those cited records have not yet been inspected, and the tree does not establish Otto Sr.’s parents, siblings, exact dates, or whether he and Bertha had other children.

      The MyHeritage Silverton 1930 snippet remains unresolved and does not identify which Otto or show a household. The 3933 Kirkup lead is contradicted by the original Cincinnati directories checked: George D. Bercher is listed there in 1935; Alex Stesch (Grace E.) in 1940. Do not assign this address to Otto or Bertha. No portrait or verified couple photograph has been found.

      Research Notes and Open Questions

      23 November 2026 — a four-leg search of free records looked for Otto Sr.’s parents and siblings in the 1910–1950 censuses, Ohio births and deaths, Cincinnati city directories, obituaries, and marriage indexes. No parents and no siblings were found, and no 1930 or 1940 census household for Otto Sr., Bertha, and their son turned up. Several leads survive, but none is proven. Frank G. Graff, born 21 October 1906 in Cincinnati and died 3 May 1988, was the son of an Otto Graff and Carrie Erion; that older Otto cannot be our Otto Sr., who was born about 1902 and would have been about four in 1906, but whether the older Otto was our Otto’s father is not established, and the question of whether our Otto was a junior stays open — the only place calling him “Otto Graff Jr.” is his son Raymond’s profile, and no record checked confirms it. The 1930–31 Williams’ Cincinnati Directory lists one Otto Graff, “Otto F. Graff, machinist, h 549 W McMicken Av,” at the same address as Frank Graff, pressman, with no Bertha listed; which Otto that is has not been established. An Ohio marriage record names an Otto Graff and Mary Krezek, and which Otto that is has not been established either. The son Otto’s dates are in conflict: the tree says he was born 5 May 1925 and died 25 August 2004 in Cincinnati, while the Social Security Death Index, NUMIDENT, and Find a Grave Index agree on 11 November 1925 and 5 January 2004, with NUMIDENT adding a birthplace of North Bergen, New Jersey and burial at Wildwood Cemetery, Beckley, West Virginia; the conflict is unresolved and no date on this page has been changed. A baptism cluster for Charles and Sophie Graff names sons Carl Martin, LeRoy, and Arthur Vincent, with no Otto among the baptisms seen, so that family is only a possible parent candidate, not proven. Still open: a signed-in search of the 1910 and 1920 censuses, the son’s 1925 birth record, and the Ohio death index for 1963.
