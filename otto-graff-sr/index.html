      Life Story

      Very little about Otto Graff Sr. has been established from directly reviewed records. Tricia’s Shamblen/McKinney Ancestry tree identifies him as the husband of Bertha M. Kissel and the father of Otto Graff, reportedly born 5 May 1925 in Cincinnati. It gives Otto Sr. approximate birth year 1902 and death year 1963. The tree lists census, draft, military, death, and burial sources for the son, but those cited records have not yet been inspected, and the tree does not establish Otto Sr.’s parents, siblings, exact dates, or whether he and Bertha had other children.

      The reported 3933 Kirkup address is not corroborated by Cincinnati directories: the 1935 directory names George D. Bercher at the address; the 1940 directory names Alex Stesch (Grace E.). The MyHeritage snippets are unresolved and must not be used to place Otto or Bertha in either household. No portrait or verified couple photograph has been found.
