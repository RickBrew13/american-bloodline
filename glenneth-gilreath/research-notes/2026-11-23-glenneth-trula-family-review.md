## Trula Mae Foster — parents and siblings
### Parents
- The FamilySearch compiled profile names Manuel Levi Foster and Emma Lou Gregory as Trula's parents and gives her birth as 3 Aug. 1926, Whitley City, McCreary County, Kentucky. https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/LK76-931/trula-mae-foster-1926-1997
- The original 1950 census schedule (Covington, Kenton County, Kentucky, ED 121-31, sheet/page 73, enumerated 10 Apr. 1950; FamilySearch record 6F76-JLX7) directly lists Emma B. Foster, age 49, divorced, as head, and Trula Mae Gilreath, age 23, widowed, as her daughter. Trula's recorded occupation is machine operator at a paper-box manufacturing company. This supports Emma B. Foster as Trula's mother. It does not by itself establish that Emma B. is the same person as Emma Lou Gregory, or identify Trula's father.
  - Record: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:6F76-JLX7?lang=en
  - Original image: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QHJ-5QHW-B16Z?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3A6F76-JLX7&action=view&cc=4464515&lang=en
- Assessment: Trula's mother Emma B. Foster is documented by the census relationship as indexed and legible on the schedule. Identifying her as Emma Lou Gregory is plausible and supported by the compiled tree but the initial/middle-name discrepancy needs a record bridge. Manuel Levi Foster as Trula's father remains a compiled-tree claim in the records reviewed here.

## Trula's 1950 household and children
The 1950 original schedule cited above shows Trula as a widowed daughter in Emma B. Foster's household, and two Gilreath boys as grandsons:
- Jackie Gilreath, age 8 (estimated birth about 1941/42)
- Bobbie Gilreath, age 6 (estimated birth about 1943/44)
The record directly supports their presence as grandsons of Emma B. Foster and the FamilySearch index links Trula as their mother. The compiled FamilySearch family lists Jack Lynden Gilreath (23 Aug. 1941–11 Aug. 2004) and Robert S. Gilreath (25 Nov. 1943–20 Mar. 2007), so Jackie may be Jack Lynden and Bobbie may be Robert S. Ages are close but names differ; these identities are likely, not yet proved by a direct identity bridge. FamilySearch also lists Kathy Gilreath as a child of Glenneth and Trula, but no direct record for Kathy was found in this review; retain that as compiled-only.

## Marriage-date conflict
The shared FamilySearch compiled profile reports a 29 May 1939 marriage in Whitley City. Given Trula's reported 3 Aug. 1926 birth, that would make her 12. MyHeritage search snippets also surfaced 1939 but provide no verifiable source image or age-resolution. The 1950 census records her as age 23 and widowed. No original marriage license/register entry has been inspected. Keep 1939 as an unresolved compiled date, not a verified marriage fact; do not infer an alternative date from the children's ages.
