# Glenneth Gilreath × Trula Mae Foster — Emma Lee Gilreath’s parentage and Generation VI
**Research date:** 17 November 2026

## Conclusion
Glenneth Gilreath and Trula Mae Foster are the best-supported working parents of Emma Lee (Gilreath) Henry. Grade: **SUPPORTED**. The current record set does not include an original Emma birth registration naming both parents, so do not call the parent-child relationship DOCUMENTED. Their couple profile, both individual profiles, Emma’s profile, and one shared Gen VI card were published with this grade explicit.

## Identity bridge
- Emma’s documented Henry-line identity is established through her son James David “Jimmy” Henry’s records. His NUMIDENT names Emma L Gilreath and Elmer D Henry as parents and gives his birth as 30 Dec 1956 in Covington, Kentucky: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:6K3P-X8RX

Original schedule image is linked on the record page: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:6F7W-DH9N ; image: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QHN-GQHW-B979-8?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3A6F7W-DH9N&action=view&lang=en. ## Conflicts and limits retained on profiles
- Emma’s 1950 census index reports age 10 and Ohio birthplace (approx. 1940). FamilySearch and Find a Grave compiled profiles instead give exact birth 15 Dec 1939 and McCreary County, Kentucky. No original birth certificate was inspected; retain both accounts.
- The FamilySearch compiled Glenneth profile reports marriage to Trula on 29 May 1939. On that date Trula, born 3 August 1926 per the compiled profile, would have been 12. That marriage date is unresolved and was not stated as established. A separate search snippet suggested 1946, but no original record or trustworthy indexed result was retrieved to verify it. The original county marriage record must be inspected to settle this.
- The 1950 census identifies Emma as a granddaughter in a Keith household. It does not establish which daughter of the Keiths was her mother, nor does it directly name Glenneth or Trula.
- The FamilySearch profiles are compiled tree pages, and the Find a Grave relationship entries are secondary memorial links. They support but do not substitute for an original child-parent record.
