Family Relationship Notes

FamilySearch’s compiled profiles and the Find a Grave memorial connect Glenneth with Trula Mae Foster and identify Emma Lee Gilreath as their daughter. Emma’s 1950 census entry places her as a granddaughter in a McCreary County household, which fits the proposed maternal-grandparent network but does not name Glenneth as her father. No original birth or parentage record for Emma has been located.

The compiled FamilySearch profile also lists Jack Lynden Gilreath, Robert S. Gilreath, and Kathy Gilreath as additional children. Those entries have not been independently verified and are retained as research leads, not placed in the Children card.

The compiled profile gives 29 May 1939 as Glenneth and Trula’s marriage date. That would make Trula 12 years old; the date is unresolved and should not be treated as an established marriage date without the original county record.
