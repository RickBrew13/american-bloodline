Life Story

Glenneth Gilreath was born in McCreary County, Kentucky, in 1920, according to a Kentucky birth-record transcription. That transcription names his mother as Rebecca Warley, a spelling consistent with Rebecca J. Worley. FamilySearch and Find a Grave identify Green Berry R. Gilreath as his father; that paternal link remains compiled/secondary rather than established by an inspected original birth record.

The compiled family sources list seven siblings and link Glenneth to Trula Mae Foster. Their reported 1939 marriage date is unresolved: it conflicts with Trula’s reported age, and the original county record has not been reviewed.

By 1950, Trula was recorded as widowed in her mother’s Covington household, with two young Gilreath boys. The schedule records Jackie and Bobbie; compiled profiles give possible full-name matches as Jack Lynden and Robert S. Emma Lee’s relationship to Glenneth is supported by compiled family profiles and memorials, but no original birth record naming him as her father has been reviewed.
