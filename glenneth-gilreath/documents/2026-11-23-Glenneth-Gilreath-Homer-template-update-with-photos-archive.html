Life Story

Glenneth’s family photo and Navy portrait are reproduced on his Find a Grave memorial. His 1942 draft card, 1948 Kentucky death certificate, and 28 Jan. 1948 newspaper article are also attached there and archived in the family Drive folder.

The death certificate directly names his parents as Green B. Gilreath and Rebecca Worley and wife Trula Mae Gilreath. The Commonwealth obituary identifies him as their son, reports that he taught at Pigeon School and lists one daughter and one son; it also names brothers Herbert, Rufus and Delmer and a sister Mrs. Walter Strunk. The compiled FamilySearch tree lists additional children and siblings; retain the differences rather than silently assuming completeness.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The death certificate directly names Green and Rebecca and Trula. Save its handwriting and field readings as image evidence; it is a Find a Grave reproduction rather than a newly issued certificate.
  • The Commonwealth’s article records an ongoing inquiry and pending autopsy report; do not state the allegations or alcohol as proven cause of death.
  • The article names one daughter and one son; FamilySearch compiles four children. The exact additional children and names require original records; retain the conflict.
  • Emma Lee’s parentage remains supported but not documented by a parent-naming original birth record. Her 1950 census says granddaughter but does not identify parents.
  • Siblings on this profile are compiled family entries, corroborated in part by Glenneth’s obituary; no sibling profiles are created.
