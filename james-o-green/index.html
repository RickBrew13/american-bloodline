Life Story

James O. Green was born in Tennessee on 23 January 1881, the son of Winfield Scott Green and Sarah Jane Stiner DOCUMENTED. At 18 he married Ella Helton at his father's house in Knox County, Kentucky, on 19 August 1899, with his father, her father, and his brother Haze as witnesses DOCUMENTED. They farmed in Knox County and raised ten or more children, the youngest a boy listed in 1930 as Pharis, the Fairest of later records DOCUMENTED. James died in Kenton County on 8 February 1954 and is buried at Forest Lawn Memorial Park in Erlanger DOCUMENTED.
