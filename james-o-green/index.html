Life Story

James O. Green was born in Tennessee about 1881 SUPPORTED. On 18 August 1899 he married Ella Helton in Knox County, Kentucky DOCUMENTED. By 1930 he was farming in Knox County with Ella and six children still at home, the youngest a little boy listed as Pharis, the Fairest of later records DOCUMENTED.
