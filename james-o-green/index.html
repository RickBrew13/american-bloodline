Keep both readings visible until the original death certificate and a clearer marker/source review resolve them.

Life Story

James O. Green was born in Tennessee on 23 January 1881, the son of Winfield Scott Green and Sarah Jane Stiner . At 18 he married Ella Helton at his father's house in Knox County, Kentucky, on 19 August 1899, with his father, her father, and his brother Haze as witnesses . They farmed in Knox County and raised ten or more children, the youngest a boy listed in 1930 as Pharis, the Fairest of later records . The Kentucky Death Index reports that James died in Kenton County on 8 February 1954 at age 73 ; the original certificate has not been checked. His likely family marker at Forest Lawn is inscribed 6 February 1956, so the death date remains unresolved.
