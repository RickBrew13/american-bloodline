Family Relationship Notes

FamilySearch’s compiled profile identifies Sarah Louisa Bradley as daughter of James Bradley and Sarah J. Bullock, and spouse of Henry Martin “Hen” Cromer. The 1890 marriage index instead names the bride Sarah E. Bradley. Her 1925 death certificate, indexed as Louisa Croomer, names father James Bradley and mother “Sallie Bradley.” Keep Sarah E. / Sarah Louisa and Sallie Bradley / Sarah J. Bullock as source-attributed variants; the certificate does not establish that “Sallie” is Bullock or spell out Sarah Louisa’s full name.

The 1910 census indexes Sarah L. “Crumes” as wife of Henry Crumes, with children including Dora A. and Roberta. The compiled Henry–Sarah family group lists ten children; the 1910 household directly corroborates eight of them. Rosa Lee is not indexed in that household, and Austin was born later. Census spellings and omissions are preserved rather than flattened into the compiled list.
