Family Relationship Notes

Angelo Carbonaro was born 1 June 1887 in Acqua dei Cossari, Palermo, to Pasquale Carbonaro and Rosalia Mule. His WWI draft card, registered 5 June 1917 in New York City, gives that birth date and place, and names him an alien, Italian citizen, a baker living at 427 E. 12th St., married with wife and child dependent, with three years of prior service as a private in the Italian infantry.

By 1930 he was living in Brooklyn with his wife Anna and five children — Lena, 15, Michael, 12, Minnie, 10, Vito, 5, and Frank, 3 — and owned a home valued at $12,000 while running an ice business. The census says he and Anna both immigrated in 1904 and were naturalized, but passenger lists show Angelo Carbonaro arrivals in 1913, so the immigration year is not settled.

His birth act has not yet been read: the Palermo birth index writes the 1 June 1887 child as “Angela,” in Brancaccio volume 239, act 218. He died 17 July 1978 and is buried at Saint Charles Cemetery, East Farmingdale, New York. His mother’s name is Rosalia Mule on the records; the family tree spells it “Rosalie Moule.”

Life Story

Angelo Carbonaro was born 1 June 1887 in Acqua dei Cossari, Palermo, to Pasquale Carbonaro and Rosalia Mule. He registered for the draft on 5 June 1917 in New York City as a baker living at 427 E. 12th St., married with wife and child dependent, with three years of prior service in the Italian infantry. By 1930 he was living in Brooklyn with his wife Anna and five children — Lena, Michael, Minnie, Vito, and Frank — owned a home valued at $12,000, and ran an ice business. He died 17 July 1978 and is buried at Saint Charles Cemetery, East Farmingdale, New York. His birth act (Brancaccio, v. 239, n. 218) has not yet been read; the birth index writes the 1887 child as “Angela.”
