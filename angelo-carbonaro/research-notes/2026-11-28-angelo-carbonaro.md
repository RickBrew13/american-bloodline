# Angelo Carbonaro — research note

Date: 28 November 2026

An Ancestry public search result lists Angelo Carbonaro born 1 June 1887 in Palermo, deceased July 1978 in Brooklyn, with parents Pasquale Carbonaro and Rosalie Moule. A derivative transcription of a December 1936 SSA SS-5 reports the same birth date and father, mother Rosalia Mule, birthplace “BLERMO, Italy,” and marks him deceased without a date. These sources strongly support him as a child of Pasquale and Rosalie. Ignazia Carbonaro’s separate derivative 1972 SS-5/NUMIDENT entry also names Pasquale Carbonaro and Rosalie Moule; the Ancestry public tree names the same pair. Angelo is very likely Ignazia’s sibling, but the underlying records and spelling/place variants have not been reviewed in original form. No spouse, children, or identified portrait/marker found.
