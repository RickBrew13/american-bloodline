# Mina Edel — deeper parent/family review

**Reviewed:** 24 November 2026

## Confirmed record evidence

Alexander Luft's 1 July 1912 Hamilton County marriage application reports Mina Edel as his mother and David Luft as his father. Alexander's 1944 Ohio death record reports his parents as “unknown.” The marriage application remains the only located direct record naming Mina as Alexander's mother. The record's image is linked at FamilySearch but redirected to sign-in during this review.

## Candidate identity: Minna Mirdel Edel — unresolved

The public Geneanet contributor tree by David Mark Dror lists Minna Mirdel Edel as spouse of David Michel Luft (1824–1905) and mother of five children born 1858–1871. The displayed Minna page gives no birth/death dates, parents, or siblings. The tree's cited sources are contributor genealogy / Geni World Family Tree, not original records. The names “Mina Edel” and “Minna Mirdel Edel,” the spouse named David, and the broad German/Posen setting make this a plausible candidate, especially if David Michel is Alexander's father. But Alexander is not among that tree's five listed children, and no document bridges that Minna to Alexander's mother.
