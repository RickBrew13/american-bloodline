Family Relationship Notes

Mary and Joseph married on 17 June 1922 in Thomas, Jefferson County, Alabama. The 1930 and 1940 census images list children in their household. Later marriage certificates, Social Security files, and Josephine’s 2007 obituary add parent links. The family cards retain the names from those records and the obituary; the obituary’s list includes children not all present in the 1940 household.

Mary is called “Mary” in the censuses and marriage record and “Carmella” on some children’s records. The 1922 marriage record spells her surname Schillaci; other records index it as Schillace or Schillari. The available records support these as name variants for the same wife/mother.

Her birth estimate remains about 1903–1904 from census ages and the marriage affidavit; a family-tree date of 14 May 1905 conflicts and is not adopted. The 1930 census immigration column reads 1907 for her, confirmed by a second look at the image, but that would make her about 4 on arrival, and no 1910 or 1920 census for her was found — her arrival is open. Her death and her own parents remain unidentified. A search on 28 November 2026 found no death record for her in Birmingham in 1978; the family-tree date of 19 October 1978 has no source behind it, and the Alabama deaths collection on FamilySearch ends in 1974. No Social Security record for her as a principal was found. Do not confuse her with other women named Mary Schillace in unrelated public records.

Life Story

Mary Carmella Schillaci married Joseph Carruba on 17 June 1922 at Thomas, in Jefferson County, Alabama. She was called Mary in the censuses and in her marriage record, and Carmella was her middle name — the form used on her daughter Cora's marriage certificate and in some of her children's birth-index entries. That is why the records name Cora's mother both "Mary Schillace" and "Carmella Schillaci": the two names belong to one woman.

Mary and Joseph's first children were born in Alabama — Sam about 1924, Rosa about 1926, Carmella about 1929 — and the family moved to Harlan County, Kentucky about 1930. Josephine was born at Totz in 1931, Susie about 1933, and Cora on 22 September 1934. By the 1940 census the household at Cumberland held Mary and Joseph with eight of their children. Joseph died in Harlan County in 1970; Mary is named as his wife in the records of their children into the 2000s, when Josephine’s 2007 obituary called her "Mary Carmella Schillaci Carruba" and listed Cora Fields Mullins among her children. No record of her birth, arrival, or death has been found; the 1978 Birmingham death date on the family tree has no source behind it.

Several other women named Mary Schillace appear in public sources, and none of them is Cora's mother. A 1950 census-index result records a Mary Schillace born about 1912 in Italy in Forest Park, Illinois, with spouse Anthony; a Chicago obituary names Mary née Guardalabene, wife of Anthony. A separate 2021 Cleveland obituary is for Mary née Patakos, wife of Jerry, and a 2018 Louisiana obituary is for Mary Theresa Ferrante Schillace, born in 1933, wife of Joseph. A MyHeritage search snippet gives an unverified family-tree lead involving Anna Mae Rogas and a daughter Mary Schillace; the page was blocked and no independent evidence connects that person to Cora. These are distinct named people, not proof about Cora's mother.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Mary and Carmella are one woman: "Mary" in the censuses and her 1922 marriage, "Carmella" her middle name on Cora's marriage certificate and some children's birth-index entries. The Kentucky birth index names "Mary Schillace" as mother of Cora and Susie, and "Carmella Schillaci" as mother of Catherine A. and Thomas — the same mother under both given names.
  • Her birth date is not settled. The 1930 census gives her age as 25 and the 1940 census as 36, and her 1922 marriage affidavit says she was over 19 — all pointing to about 1903–1904. The family tree gives 14 May 1905 in Sicily, which conflicts with those records; the tree’s date is not adopted here. The 1930 census immigration column reads 1907 for her, confirmed by a second look at the image, but that would make her about 4 on arrival and no 1910 or 1920 census for her was found, so her arrival is open.
  • Her death is not established. The family tree gives 19 October 1978 in Birmingham, Alabama, with no source attached; a search on 28 November 2026 found no death record for her, and the Alabama deaths collection on FamilySearch ends in 1974, so it cannot hold a 1978 death. That date is not adopted here. No Social Security record for her as a principal was found.
  • Her own parents are not named in any record found. The 1922 Alabama marriage record names no parents for her.
  • The "Mary Schillaci, Alabama Deaths" record attached to her tree profile is not her death — it is her infant daughter Carmie Lou Carruba, age 0, died 1928 in Birmingham.
  • Duplicate tree persons exist for her under the Carmella form ("Carmella Schillaci"), and a second spouse, Mary Tudisco, is listed for Joseph with no marriage events; neither is adopted here. Mary Tudisco’s tree sources were read on 28 November 2026 and never name Joseph — she belongs to Louis Carruba in the tree.
  • Do not conflate Cora's mother with Mary née Guardalabene/Anthony, Mary née Patakos/Jerry, or Mary Theresa Ferrante Schillace/Joseph. Those public records describe distinct people and none is linked to Cora.
  • The MyHeritage Anna Mae Rogas → Mary Schillace family-tree snippet is unverified. No profile link was made for Anna Mae, and it does not identify Cora's mother.
  • No 1950 census household has been found for Mary, Joseph or Cora in Harlan County or in Birmingham, Alabama, under Carruba or Carubba.
  • Joseph’s brother Norman Carruba is proved by the 1920 census image — Joseph, 27, single, is listed as “Brother” in Norman’s household at Millport, Alabama. Joseph’s own parents are still not named in any record found.
