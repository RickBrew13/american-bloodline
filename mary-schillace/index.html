Life Story

A derivative transcription of Kentucky birth-index entries reports Cora Carrubba born 22 September 1934 in Harlan County to “Mary Schillace.” It does not name Cora’s father. The same source lists Cora as mother of Dale M. Fields (9 March 1954, Kenton County) and Joy D. Fields (29 March 1957, Harlan County), supporting a link to Gen VI Cora (Carubba) Fields Mullins but not identifying this Mary among similarly named adults.

Several other women named Mary Schillace appear in public sources. A 1950 census-index result records a Mary Schillace born about 1912 in Italy in Forest Park, Illinois, with spouse Anthony and children Lucille, Mary Ann, Elizabeth, and Josephine; a Chicago obituary names Mary née Guardalabene, wife of Anthony, with a family listing that overlaps those names. No record found so far connects her to Cora. A separate 2021 Cleveland obituary is for Mary née Patakos, wife of Jerry and mother of Marietta and Tony; a 2018 Louisiana obituary is for Mary Theresa Ferrante Schillace, born in 1933, wife of Joseph and mother of Ronnie, Dolores, Linda, and Patricia. These are distinct named people, not proof about Cora’s mother. A MyHeritage search snippet gives an unverified family-tree lead involving Anna Mae Rogas and a daughter Mary Schillace; the page was blocked and no independent evidence connects that person to Cora.

For now, this profile represents only the woman named as Cora’s mother in the birth-index transcription. Her own dates, parents, spouse, and identity among same-name women remain unresolved. An original Kentucky birth certificate is the best next source for Cora’s father and may help identify her mother.
