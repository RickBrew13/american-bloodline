Life Story

Mary Schillace is identified as the mother of Cora Carrubba in a derivative transcription of Kentucky birth-index entries. The same page lists Cora as the mother of Dale M. Fields, born 9 March 1954 in Kenton County, and Joy D. Fields, born 29 March 1957 in Harlan County. Dale’s date matches Dell Michael Fields’s date of birth on his reviewed 1982 marriage license; Joy D. aligns with Joy Dianne in Dell Michael’s obituary. This makes Mary a plausible and evidence-supported maternal ancestor of Cora, with an estimated working confidence of about 75%.

The original 1934 birth certificate/index image has not yet been inspected. The transcription does not identify Cora’s father, and no spouse for Mary is established. Mary’s own birth, death, parents, and other children remain unknown.
