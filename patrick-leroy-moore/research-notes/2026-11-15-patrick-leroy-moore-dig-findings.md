### 2e. NEW CONTEXT — Why the Michigan birth record cannot be found online (not a missed search)
- Michigan birth records are restricted for 100 years under MCL 333.2882: a 1936 certificate is obtainable only by an eligible person (the registrant, a parent, heir, or legal representative) until 2036.
- FamilySearch's free Michigan birth collections end at 1902 ("Michigan, Births, 1867-1902", 1,409,988 records) and 1917 ("Michigan, County Births, 1867-1917"); "Michigan, Births and Christenings, 1775–1995" is a partial legacy index with recent records withheld for privacy. No public online birth record for a 1936 Michigan birth is expected to exist.
- Consequence: the open question "find a Michigan birth record naming Leroy and Mila" can only be closed by an eligible family member requesting the certificate (fee + eligibility proof), or by waiting until 2036. Recorded in the patch's Research Notes as such.
