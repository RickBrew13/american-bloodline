Life Story

Patrick Leroy Moore, remembered in the family as “Gpa Pat,” is strongly matched to the Patrick L. Moore described in the funeral-home obituary. The obituary identifies him only as Patrick L. Moore; the expansion of L to Leroy remains the family/project name and is not confirmed by an original identity record. His family remembers visits with Patrick and Victoria in Massachusetts. Public records place the couple in Uxbridge, Massachusetts, from 1993 to 2007.

Research Notes and Open Questions

Additional Quick-Fact Details

  • Work: Retired supervisor for General Motors
  • Military: Served in the U.S. Air Force; service dates and unit not yet located
  • Find an original birth, marriage, or military record that confirms Patrick's full middle name and birth details.
  • Find a Michigan birth record (28 Feb 1936) naming Leroy and Mila to confirm parentage; find Mila's maiden name. Note: Michigan birth records are restricted for 100 years (MCL 333.2882) — a 1936 certificate is obtainable only by an eligible person (the registrant, a parent, heir or legal representative) until 2036 — and the FamilySearch Michigan birth collections end in 1902/1917, so no public birth record is expected online.
  • Look for service dates, branch records and General Motors employment sources; do not infer dates or rank.
  • Resolve the funeral-home page’s conflicting burial information: its obituary text says a private burial was planned at Indiana Veterans Memorial Cemetery in Madison, while its structured “Final Resting Place” field lists Greater Cincinnati Crematory in Cincinnati. Neither entry confirms interment. Checked 15 November 2026: no Find a Grave memorial found for him, and the VA-sourced Interment.net listing for Indiana Veterans Memorial Cemetery covers deaths only through 2022, so it cannot confirm or exclude a December 2023 interment. A 2023 Indiana death certificate is obtainable only by a person with a direct interest, from the Indiana Department of Health or VitalChek, for a fee.
  • Search for an obituary or original records for Victoria, including her dates and marriage record. Victoria's obituary index now gives her dates (born 28 Dec 1941, died 6 Oct 2018, Richmond, Indiana); her full obituary text sits behind paywalls (GenealogyBank, NewspaperArchive). No marriage record for Patrick × Victoria found in open indexes; later Michigan/Indiana/Ohio marriage records are not freely indexed online — needs a signed-in FamilySearch search and/or a county-clerk check.
  • The couple photo is family-labeled; photographer and date remain unknown. It is being used temporarily as the profile picture at family request.
  • Public records now date the Uxbridge, Massachusetts residence to 1993–2007; a street address is not established.
  • No Air Force service record located in free sources; a Fold3/NPRC pull needs a signed-in check (Fold3 blocked automated access in the earlier sweep).
  • Not the same man as 2nd Lt Patrick L. Moore, P-51 pilot of the 55th Fighter Group, 343rd Fighter Squadron, from Griffin, Indiana, credited with an aerial victory on 30 March 1945 — this Patrick was born in 1936 — nor the Patrick L.
