# 41. Manuel Levi Foster × Emma Lou Gregory — parent-couple profile evidence and media
Research date: 23 November 2026

## Images and clippings
- The five-generation newspaper photograph attached to Find a Grave memorial 63033692 has a caption identifying seated Mrs. Elizabeth Worley Foster, and standing left-to-right her son Manuel Foster; granddaughter Mrs. Trula Mae Foster Green, Mason, Ohio; great-granddaughter Mrs. Emma Lee Gilbreath Henry; and great-great-grandson James Henry. It is a group photograph, not an individual portrait. It is used on Manuel’s and Trula’s profiles with the caption and name caveat preserved. The caption gives no issue/date visible in the clipping. https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/63033692/manuel_levi-foster/photo
- Manuel’s memorial includes a clipped obituary (image added 14 Apr 2021) giving birth 16 Dec 1898 Wayne County, death 28 Feb 1980 Harriman TN, burial McCreary County Memorial Gardens, railroad special agent, Pleasant Rock Baptist Church, wife Arlena Hurt Foster, children Spencer, Trula Mae Whalen and Beulah Anderson, stepson Kenneth Phillips, and six sisters by married names. The clipping’s masthead/date are cropped; use it as an undated Find a Grave-reproduced obituary clipping, not a fully sourced citation. Same source URL above.
- Manuel’s shared gravestone photo with Arlena is from Find a Grave memorial 63033692; it supports an Arlena Foster spouse/marker association, but the stone does not establish the exact marriage chronology.
- Emma Lou’s headstone at Hickman Cemetery reads Emma Gregory Foster, 22 Jan 1900–18 Jul 1961, “Her end was peace.” Find a Grave memorial 54783218, photo page https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/54783218/emma-lou-foster/photo. No verified individual portrait was located, so her headstone photograph is used as her profile image and explicitly labeled as a grave marker, not portrait.
- Emma Lou’s Find a Grave page has a clipped obituary image labeled “Page 4 of McCreary County Record,” without a legible issue date. It identifies Mrs. Emma Gregory Foster, 61, death 18 July in Cincinnati, birthplace McCreary County, Pleasant Rock Baptist Church in Smithtown, surviving children/relatives, funeral at Second Baptist Church, and burial at Hickman Cemetery. Preserve the clipping’s own wording and avoid inventing the publication date.

## Manuel Levi Foster
FamilySearch compiled profile M2RR-ZW7 reports 16 Dec 1898–28 Feb 1980, birth Wayne County KY, death Harriman Roane County TN, burial McCreary County Memorial Gardens, marriage to Emma Lou Gregory on 17 Apr 1916, and six children. It names parents James Shelby Foster and Elizabeth A. Abbott. https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/M2RR-ZW7/manuel-levi-foster-1898-1980
Find a Grave memorial 63033692 and the reproduced obituary clipping support his dates, Wayne County birth, Harriman death, cemetery, railroad work, church, and later spouse Arlena. The same obituary names his mother as Elizabeth Worley and father Shelby Foster. This conflicts with the compiled profile’s Elizabeth A. Abbott. Do not merge those two maternal names as one person without a documentary bridge; keep the parent field qualified and this conflict conspicuous.
Find a Grave’s family links name sisters Carrie M. Foster Keith and Vina Jane Foster Brown. The 1980 clipping says six sisters and names Linda Jones, Zelma Ross, Alpha Ball, Carrie Keith, Kate Clowers and Vina Brown. These married-name entries are source-attributed; full birth names and exact kinship need corroboration.

## Emma Lou Gregory
FamilySearch compiled profile KD92-S2H reports 22 Jan 1900–18 Jul 1961, birth McCreary County KY, death Cincinnati Hamilton County OH, burial Hickman Cemetery, parents Benjamin Gregory and Sarah Burk, and marriage to Manuel on 17 Apr 1916. https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/KD92-S2H/emma-lou-gregory-1900-1961
Find a Grave memorial 54783218 repeats dates/place/burial and supplies the headstone photograph and obituary clipping. Compiled parent assignments to Benjamin and Sarah are not independently proven in this review.

## Trula Mae Foster — direct family context
The caption on the five-generation photo provides a valuable contemporaneous family-group identification: it names Trula Mae as Foster and married name Green, identifies Manuel as her grandfather/relative, and identifies Emma Lee Gilbreath Henry as the next generation. This supports the family relationship chain and explains a surname variation, but exact identity/sequencing remains to be reconciled with Glenneth Gilreath, “Gilreath” and a later “Whalen” form appearing in the 1980 Manuel obituary. Do not silently overwrite one name with another.
The 1950 U.S. census schedule for Covington, Kentucky directly lists Trula Mae Gilreath (23, widowed) as daughter of Emma B. Foster and includes Jackie/Bobbie Gilreath as grandsons. The schedule and indexed record are already archived in the Gen VI couple Books & Sources folder. It corroborates household placement but not the identity Emma B.=Emma Lou Gregory or Trula’s father.

## Profile linkage and sibling policy
Manuel and Emma Lou’s profile child lists will include the six names in compiled FamilySearch, with Trula linked to her Gen VI page and siblings listed by name only. Trula’s siblings stay listed on her Gen VI profile only; no sibling profiles are created. The Gen VII index will receive one shared couple card for this parent pair. Glenneth and Trula’s Gen VI pages will have reciprocal links to their respective parents’ profiles.
