Life Story

Beulah (nee Cornett) Cossman’s obituary identifies her as Stanley P. Cossman’s wife, names four children and four siblings, and reports her age as 82 at death. The Find a Grave memorial gives her birth date as 5 October 1926 and links her to William M. Cornett Sr. and Roberta Cromer Cornett. Roberta’s public FamilySearch profile also lists Beulah among the children of Roberta and William. These compiled-family links support Beulah’s parentage, but an original birth record has not been reviewed.
