# Source abstract: Martha J. (Sullivan) Lewis obituary

Source: Belleville News-Democrat, published 27 March 2014; Legacy copy: https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/belleville/name/martha-lewis-obituary?id=20576088.

Reports Martha J. Lewis, née Sullivan, born 16 July 1928 in St. Louis and died 26 March 2014 in Maryville. Names husband Arnold Lewis and marriage date 16 May 1947; parents Matthew and Lena (Spencer) Sullivan; siblings Mary Jane Schuab, Dorothy Sullivan, Matthew Edward Jr., James S., Robert J., Dan J., Jo Ann Graham, and Elizabeth Wright; children Patricia, Pamela, Edward A., Jeffery and Thomas. Gives Collinsville Unit 10 School District retirement after 20 years and burial at St. John Cemetery, Collinsville. This is one family-submitted obituary source; the Legacy copy is a reproduction, not independent corroboration.
