Life Story

Florence Eskalene Stephenson was born 9 August 1913 in Warren County, Ohio, and died in Cincinnati on 25 July 1980, according to her Find a Grave memorial and the Warren County funeral index. Her 1980 funeral notice calls her the wife of Garvice E. Barnes and names daughters Patsy Lee Ray and Gerry Shamblen. Garvice’s 1976 funeral notice identifies his wife as Florence (née Stephenson), while Patsy’s 2025 obituary names both Garvice and Florence (née Stephenson) as her parents and identifies Lois G. Shamblen as her sister.

A later 2025 clipping attached to Garvice’s memorial calls his wife “Estelle Stevenson,” conflicting with the contemporary 1976 notice and Patsy’s obituary. The profile retains Florence as supported and documents the isolated conflict instead of silently treating the names as equivalent.
