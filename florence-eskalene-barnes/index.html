Life Story

Florence was born in Warren County, Ohio, on 9 August 1913, according to her Find a Grave memorial and the public FamilySearch tree for her mother. The currently accessible evidence supports her identification as a daughter of Charles Clarence Stephenson and Sylvia Etta Pottorf Stephenson, but no original record directly naming those parents has been reviewed. The exact town of birth is therefore left open rather than repeating “Kings Mills” without a record.

Her relationship to Garvice Euel Barnes is established by both spouses’ contemporary funeral notices. Florence’s 1980 notice names daughters Patsy Lee Ray and Gerry Shamblen; Patsy’s 2025 obituary later identifies Garvice and Florence (née Stephenson) as her parents and Lois as her sister. Florence was interred in Rose Hill Cemetery on 28 July 1980. Cemetery sources preserve a name variation: the stone transcription reads “Eskalene S. Barnes,” while the interment register entry is “Florence E. Barnes.”

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Parents Charles Clarence Stephenson and Sylvia Etta Pottorf are supported by Find a Grave family links and the FamilySearch public tree. An original birth certificate or contemporaneous census showing Florence in the household would strengthen the link.
  • Birthplace: Warren County is what the Find a Grave memorial states. The older profile’s “Kings Mills” locality has not been verified from an original source, so it is not carried as fact here.
  • Death date/place are supported by the memorial and notice; Rose Hill’s cemetery transcription lists interment 28 July 1980. Obtain the original Shorten & Ryan funeral record (S&R Vol. 7, p. 9) if possible.
  • The name on the cemetery transcription is “Eskalene S. Barnes,” while the register entry is “Florence E. Barnes.” Keep both forms attributed to their sources.
  • A later 2025 clipping attached to Garvice’s memorial calls his wife “Estelle Stevenson,” conflicting with Garvice’s contemporary 1976 notice, Florence’s 1980 notice, and Patsy’s obituary. Do not treat Estelle as a second established wife or silently replace Florence.
  • The Find a Grave memorial lists three photos; none was suitable for use here without direct visual verification. No verified portrait was located. Do not substitute Garvice’s marker as an individual image for Florence.
  • No original marriage record for Florence and Garvice was located in this pass. Their marriage/relationship is nevertheless corroborated by the contemporary notices and daughter’s obituary.
