Life Timeline

1850 · Blevens Asher, 26, and Rutha Asher, 20, are indexed in Clay County, Kentucky with three young Asher children; the index does not define each relationship.

1860 · Bleve Asher, 38, and Ruth Asher, 28, appear in Perry County with Josiah, Hetta, Nancy, and Dill; Harvey Muncy, Rosanna Aasher, Jackson Asher, and an Andrew Asher also appear in the indexed household, but no relationship is assigned to each.

1870 · Blivius/Blevins Asher and Ruthy (also indexed as Kathy) appear in McIntosh, Perry County with Josiah, Hilley, Nancy, Dilian, Hilsey, and Litcher (age 3).

1880 · Blevins Asher, 59, and wife indexed as Brittny appear in Leslie County with Dillion, Kelts, Andrew, Mary Jane, and Ivanna; this is a later household and does not identify Brittny as Ruth.

Life Story

Census indexes and compiled family sources place Blevins Asher in Clay, Perry, and Leslie Counties, Kentucky, with repeated household groups involving Ruth Muncy and Asher children. The 1870 index records “Litcher Asher,” age 3, in Blevins/Blivius Asher’s household; this matches the Letcher line and assigned parents already recorded in the project. The 1850, 1860, 1870, and 1880 indexes vary in names and ages, and some household members are not marked as children. The Muncy Family Database separately reports a nonmarital relationship with Ruth’s sister Mary (Polly) Muncy and children under that union. Those two family groups must stay distinct. Blevins’s own parents (Dillon Asher and Henrietta Bowling) are only a compiled database claim here, not a primary-record conclusion.
