# Blevins Asher — family group and sibling audit

Reviewed 26 November 2026.

Blevins’s indexed census-name forms: Blevens (1850), Bleve (1860), Blivius/Blevins (1870), Blevins (1880). Birth estimates differ slightly: 1824 (1850 index), 1822 (1860), 1823 (1870), and about 1821 (1880). Muncy Family Database reports 1822–1880 and names Dillon Asher with Henrietta (Hetty) Bowling as parents; this parent pair is a compiled claim and no original birth/parent record was reviewed.

The 1850 and 1860 indexes place Rutha/Ruth in his household. The 1870 index includes Ruthy/Kathy and Litcher age 3, plus Josiah, Hilley, Nancy, Dilian, and Hilsey. These household entries underlie the candidate sibling group on Letcher’s profile; they are not all individually proven parent-child links. The 1880 index shows wife “Brittny,” Dillion, Kelts, Andrew and Mary Jane. Do not equate Brittny with Ruth; the Muncy database reports Ruth died by 1870.

Muncy Family Database also reports a separate nonmarital relationship between Blevins and Mary (Polly) Muncy, Ruth’s sister, and lists children under that relationship. Those children must remain distinct from the Ruth/Asher household.

No verified personal portrait or individual marker was located. The Muncy database thumbnail captioned “Asher, Blevins” is blocked by its host (403) and is not copied or used as a portrait.
