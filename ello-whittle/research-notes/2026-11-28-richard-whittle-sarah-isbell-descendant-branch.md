## Possible identification with the 1850 census Richard Whittle (age 4)
The 1850 Russell County census transcription lists **Richard Whittle, age 4**, in the same family-number household as **William Whittle, 28**, **Darius Whittle, 33**, **Deby Whittle, 6**, and **James C. Whittle, 1** (M432-217, page 260A, household/family 290). The 1843 Russell County marriage register records William Whittle and Dorcas Litrell; a compiled 1868 marriage entry for Deborah “Debby” Whittle identifies her parents as William Whittle and Dorcas Luttrell.

The age and county fit Richard Whittle (1847–1895), making the 1850 Richard age 4 a strong candidate for the later husband of Sarah E. Isbell. But the 1850 census has no relationship-to-head field, and the census household does not name Dorcas. A separate Russell County marriage transcription records William Whittle and Milly Stephens on 18 November 1846. If this was the same William who married Dorcas in 1843, Milly may have been a second wife and could be Richard's mother. **Do not treat William and Dorcas as Richard's proven parents, or assign his mother, until an original marriage, birth, death, probate or census record connects him to them.**

## Sarah E. Isbell — parents and ancestry lead
A WeRelate profile for Sarah E. Isbell Whittle reports:
- Born **28 November 1845**.
- Died **3 September 1912**.
- Parents: **John F. Isbell** and **Mary Herriford**.
- The profile cites a Kentucky death certificate, certificate no. 31165, and Russell County death-record information.

This is a derivative family profile, but the certificate number and parent names provide a specific primary-record route. The original death certificate should be obtained and read before treating the parentage as proven. Sarah's parents and siblings are collateral ancestors for this Richard Whittle branch, not yet connected to Avery's direct line.
