# Avery Ello Whittle — source lead for son Rob Whittle's photograph
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Follow-up on the Rockwall County developer identified in the 2011 local newspaper and the family connection established by the 2016 obituary.

## Photo located — Rob Whittle, not Avery
A 26 January 2018 Spot On Images article, *Heath Golf & Yacht Club*, includes a specifically captioned photograph link **“Rob Whittle Celebrates Groundbreaking.”** The article describes Rob as a long-time Rockwall County developer and identifies his Whittle Development projects. The photograph is a potential image for Avery's son Rob, not a portrait of Avery. The article's identity is consistent with the 2011 *Rockwall County News* report that named Rob Whittle as the developer and Louretta Moukhtar as manager of the same Hidden Creek Estates development; Theresa's 2016 obituary identifies Rob and Louretta as siblings.

The photo source is copyrighted by Spot On Images. It is linked here as a discovery lead only; it has **not** been copied into GitHub or represented as a photo of Avery. Reproduction permission would be needed before rehosting it on the public American Bloodline website.

Source:
- Spot On Images, Jane Sadek, “Heath Golf & Yacht Club,” 26 January 2018. The article's image caption link “Rob Whittle Celebrates Groundbreaking” appears in the article: https://www.spotonimages.com/post/2018/01/26/heath-golf-yacht-club

## Marriage and business identity cross-check
A 4 June 2010 *Rockwall County News* issue explicitly refers to “Rob Whittle and his wife, Sara,” confirming Sara as Rob's wife. A 26 July 2007 *Rockwall County News* feature describes Rob and Sara as a married couple and reports their long-term plan for the waterfront hotel. A 25 July 2008 *Rockwall County News* feature identifies **Mike Whittle** as Director of Construction for Whittle Development and describes Rob and Sara's development work. An August 2012 report identifies Sara, Rob and Mike Whittle together as the partners who acquired the Hilton Dallas/Rockwall Lakefront. The 2016 Theresa obituary independently identifies Rob and Mike as Avery's sons and names Debi Whittle among the in-laws; Debi is likely Mike's spouse, but the obituary does not explicitly pair them.

## Business identity cross-check
- *Rockwall County News*, 25 February 2011, page 3, identifies Rob Whittle as developer of Hidden Creek Estates and Louretta Moukhtar as its manager.
- City of Rockwall development application PZ1999-007-01, dated 20 January 1999, lists **Robert Whittle** as owner/applicant for Meadow Creek Estates, with the company name Robert Whittle and Rockwall, Texas mailing address. This is a contemporaneous municipal record supporting the full-name form **Robert Whittle** for a Rockwall developer, but it does not itself state his family relationship to Avery.
- Theresa Whittle's 2016 obituary names Rob Whittle and Louretta Moukhtar as siblings and identifies Avery as their father.

## Evidence boundary
This supports the working identification of Avery's son Rob with the Rockwall developer Robert Whittle and reveals a potential photograph for Rob. It does not provide a new photograph of Avery, and the image must not be copied or labelled as Avery.
