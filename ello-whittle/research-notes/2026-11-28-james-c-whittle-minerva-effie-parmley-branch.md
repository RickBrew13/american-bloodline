# Avery Ello Whittle — James C. Whittle, Minerva and Effie Parmley branch
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed James C. Whittle (1849–1902) from the 1850 Russell County census and identified his wife and child through cemetery and obituary/birth indexes.

## James C. Whittle (1849–1902) and Minerva E. Whittle (1844–1893)
PeopleLegacy's Coleman Cemetery family page identifies:
- **James C. Whittle**, 1849–1902, buried Coleman Cemetery, Russell County.
- Wife **Minerva E. Whittle**, born 6 November 1844, died 11 October 1893, buried Coleman Cemetery.
- Their child **Effie Whittle Parmley**, 1882–1969, buried Parmleys Grove Cemetery.

This is a secondary cemetery aggregation, not a marriage or birth record. It does not give Minerva's maiden name or James's parents.

Source: PeopleLegacy, Minerva E. Whittle (1844–1893): https://peoplelegacy.net/minerva_e__whittle-3o2c1y

## Possible identification with the 1850 Russell County James C. Whittle (age 1)
The 1850 Russell County census transcription lists **James C. Whittle, age 1**, in household/family 290 on page 260A, with William Whittle (28), Darius Whittle (33), Deby Whittle (6) and Richard Whittle (4). The age and Russell County location strongly fit the later James C. Whittle (1849–1902), but the census has no relationship-to-head field.

The 1843 Russell County marriage register records **William Whittle and Dorcas Litrell**. A compiled 1868 marriage entry for Deborah “Debby” Whittle names William Whittle and Dorcas Luttrell as her parents. This makes William the likely father of the 1850 household's Deby, but it does not prove that James C. had the same mother.

Important second-marriage possibility: a separate Russell County marriage transcription records **William Whittle and Milly Stephens** on **18 November 1846** (Book 30, page 204). If this is the same William, Milly may have been a second wife after Dorcas and could be the mother of Richard (born about 1846) and James C. (born about 1849). The original 1846 record and any death/divorce record for Dorcas or Milly are needed. Do not list Dorcas as James's mother without evidence.

## Effie Whittle Parmley (1882–1969) — children
A Pulaski County obituary index for **Ollie Burgess (1904–2003)** identifies her parents as **Thomas Parmley** and **Effie Whittle** (lookup ID 454). This supports a marriage/partnership between Effie and Thomas Parmley and identifies at least one child, Ollie.

A Kentucky birth-index transcription lists:
- **Alvin Parmlee**, born **10 September 1912**, Pulaski County; mother's maiden name **Effie Whittle**.

The birth index does not name Alvin's father, so do not assign Thomas Parmley as his father until the original birth record or a later census/obituary confirms it. These are at least two children associated with Effie: Ollie (1904) and Alvin (1912).

Sources:
- KYGenWeb, Pulaski County obituary index, Ollie Burgess, 1904–2003, parents Thomas Parmley and Effie Whittle, lookup ID 454: https://mail.kygenweb.net/pulaski/obits/sandy_ab.html
