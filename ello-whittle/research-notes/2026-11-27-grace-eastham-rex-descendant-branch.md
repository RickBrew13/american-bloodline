# Avery Ello Whittle — sister Grace Eastham and Rex Eastham descendant branch
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Expanded the descendant line of Avery's sister Grace Whittle Eastham using Rex Allen Eastham's January 2026 obituary.

## Grace and Kirby Eastham
Grace Whittle Eastham's 2018 obituary identifies her parents as **Stanton B. Whittle and Louvenia McGowan Whittle**, and names her sister **Veldean Beckner**. It lists her children:
- **Gayla Sue Kerr**
- **Treva (Roger) Meirose**
- **Tina Milliner**
- **Rex Allen Eastham**
- **Phyllis Holder** (deceased)

The 2026 obituary for Rex independently confirms his parents were Grace Whittle Eastham and Kirby Eastham, and names the three surviving sisters plus deceased sister Phyllis. This establishes five children for Grace and Kirby.

Sources:
- Grace Eastham obituary, 2018, WVLC/WHVE archive: https://wvlc.com/whve-obits/archives/2018-04/
- Rex Allen Eastham obituary, 31 July 1951–11 January 2026, WVLC/WHVE archive: https://wvlc.com/whve-obits/archives/2026-01/
- Rex Allen Eastham obituary, Tribute Archive: https://www.tributearchive.com/obituaries/46905787/rex-allen-eastham

## Rex Allen Eastham (31 July 1951–11 January 2026)
Rex's obituary names:
- Wife: **Nancy Lee Eastham**, married 46 years.
- Children: **Jason Eastham**, **Amy Eastham**, **Amber (Brian) Grippe**, and deceased son **David Eastham**.
- Grandchildren: **David Eastham Jr.**, **Dakota Eastham**, **Madison Snow**, **Maxton Grippe**, and one grandchild expected at the time of the obituary.
- Great-grandchild: **Jayden Martin**.
- Sisters: Gayla Sue Kerr, Treva (Roger) Meirose, Tina Milliner; deceased sister Phyllis Holder.

Rex worked as a farmer, previously at General Electric and Tecumseh, and for 18 years at Popplewell's Alligator Boat Dock. His funeral was 16 January 2026; burial at Pine Grove Cemetery, Russell County.

## Relationship to Avery
Grace was Avery's sister. Her children are Avery's nieces/nephews; Rex's children are Avery's great-nieces/nephews; later generations are further collateral descendants. This branch is now supported by Grace's obituary and Rex's obituary.
