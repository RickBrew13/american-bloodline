# Avery Ello Whittle — Cordella Whittle's children and descendant branches
**Research date:** 26 November 2026  
**Scope:** Avery's paternal grandmother Cordella Whittle and the children named in her 1951 obituary. This research follows the existing Avery folder and prior parentage notes; no other person's profile was edited.

## Cordella's 1951 obituary and the unresolved six-child count
A transcription of Cordella Whittle's obituary from *The Russell County News*, 22 March 1951, reports that she was born 14 May 1878, died 12 March 1951, and married **R. S. Whittle, known as Bob Whittle**, on 24 December 1895. It says Bob preceded her in death on 1 December 1949. The notice states that six children were born to the union, but the transcription names only five: **Otha Whittle, Rese Whittle, Jim Elmer Whittle, Caleb Whittle and Grady Whittle**. It also reports 17 grandchildren and three great-grandchildren.

The transcript was produced by a genealogy blogger using AI from a newspaper clipping, not by a professional newspaper transcription. The mismatch between “six children” and five names could be a dropped name or an OCR/transcription omission. The original newspaper clipping image and the full issue have not been read directly. The missing sixth child is not assumed to be Stanton.

## Descendant branches supported by later obituaries

### Otha Whittle — likely born 15 September 1898, died 1 October 1978
A derivative Social Security index transcription lists an **Otha Whittle**, born 15 September 1898, died 1 October 1978, last residence Russell Springs, Russell County, Kentucky. A PeopleLegacy cemetery aggregation places **Otha L. Whittle (1898–1978)** and **Robert S. “Bob” Whittle (1875–1948)** among burials at McBeath Cemetery, Russell Springs. This is a possible identity match to Cordella's son Otha, not proof by itself.

Two later obituaries establish an Otha Whittle as father of children in the Russell Springs family:
- **Arlus “Johnny” Whittle** (7 December 1925–16 January 2010) is identified in his Bernard Funeral Home obituary as son of **Otha Whittle** and **Mae Meadow Whittle**. He was survived by wife Sylba Whittle, daughter Becky Jo (Rex) Johnson and three granddaughters.
- **Lana Kay Hale** (20 November 1946–15 July 2022) is identified in her obituary as daughter of the late **Otha Whittle** and **Nancy Mae Meadows Whittle**. It names siblings Alene McKinley, Lavern Elswick, Wilma Mercer, Jean Bringer, Jerry Holt and brother Johnny Whittle; husband David Hale; son John David Hale; two grandchildren and three great-grandchildren.

The matching father name, Russell Springs location, and sibling/child branch make it plausible that the Otha named in Cordella's obituary is the Otha (1898–1978) who married Nancy Mae Meadows and fathered Arlus/Johnny and Lana. The 2010 and 2022 obituaries do not name Otha's parents, so the Cordella parent-child link remains a working identification until a primary record connects them.

### Caleb Whittle — possible 2 January 1911–1 April 1983
A derivative Social Security index page lists a Caleb Whittle born 2 January 1911 and died 1 April 1983, last residence Russell Springs, Russell County. The age and location are compatible with the Caleb named in Cordella's 1951 obituary, but no obituary, death certificate or census record directly connecting this Caleb to Cordella was located in this pass. Treat as a candidate only.

### Grady Whittle — possible 14 February 1914–1 February 1975
The same derivative index lists Grady Whittle born 14 February 1914 and died 1 February 1975, last residence Russell Springs. This is compatible with the Grady named in Cordella's obituary, but no primary record linking this Grady to Cordella was located. Treat as a candidate only.

### Rese Whittle — unresolved
The 1951 transcription names “Rese Whittle.” A derivative index contains an **R. Whittle**, born 26 May 1901 and died 1 May 1973, last residence Russell Springs. This could be the same person, but the index does not expand the initial and no obituary or record explicitly links “Rese” to this R. Whittle. A separate **Ruel Whittle** (16 March 1903–1 November 1976) also appears in the same derivative index and must not be conflated with Rese without evidence.

### Jim Elmer Whittle — unresolved
No identity-secure Russell County record for the “Jim Elmer Whittle” named in the 1951 transcription was found. A search result for **James Elmer Whittle (1887–1986)** in Missouri is a different geographic/family context and is excluded. No marriage, children or descendants are assigned to the Russell County Jim Elmer.

## Stanton Whittle parentage — what this does and does not prove
The 1951 obituary names R. S. “Bob” Whittle and Cordella as a married couple from 24 December 1895. Stanton's reported birth is 10 July 1893 (Social Security index) or 1892 (headstone), before that marriage. Therefore:
- Stanton could be a child of Cordella from a prior relationship, a child of R. S. by a prior relationship, or have an inaccurate birth/marriage date in one of the records.
- The obituary's “six children” but five transcribed names does not establish that Stanton was the sixth child.
- The 1940/1910/1900 census households and original Cordella obituary clipping remain the key records to resolve the relationship.
- The derivative death index lists **Robert S. “Bob” Whittle** as 1875–1948, while Cordella's obituary says Bob died 1 December 1949. This one-year conflict also needs an original death record or cemetery register.

No parentage claim for Stanton is changed by this note.
