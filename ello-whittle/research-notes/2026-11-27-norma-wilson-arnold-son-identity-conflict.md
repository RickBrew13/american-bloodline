# Avery Ello Whittle — Norma Wilson's son Arnold Ray identity conflict
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Reconciled two conflicting obituaries about a son/brother named Arnold in Norma Jean Whittle Wilson's family.

## 2017 source
Norma Jean (Whittle) Wilson's official Bernard Funeral Home obituary (17 January 1933–30 January 2017) states that she was preceded in death by her husband Carson Wilson and **a son, Arnold Ray Wilson**. It then names four surviving children: Carl J., Sheena, Troy and Lyn.

Source: https://www.bernardfuneralhome.com/obituary/Norma-Wilson

## 2025 sources that create a conflict
- Carl J. Wilson's 2025 obituary identifies his parents as **Carson Wilson and Norma Jean Wilson**, and names a **brother Arnold Wilson** alongside siblings Sheena, Troy and Lyn.
- A separate Ever Loved memorial page identifies **Arnold Ray Wilson**, born **26 December 1948**, died **13 January 2025**, in Kenmore, Washington. The memorial does not name his parents.

Sources:
- Carl J. Wilson obituary (15 April 1949–8 February 2025): https://www.echovita.com/us/obituaries/md/preston/carl-j-wilson-19321257
- Arnold Ray Wilson memorial (26 December 1948–13 January 2025): https://everloved.com/life-of/arnold-ray-wilson/

## Possible explanations
1. The 2025 Arnold may be the same son of Carson and Norma, and the 2017 obituary's statement that Arnold had already died may be an error.
2. The 2025 Arnold may be a half-brother/stepbrother of Carl through Carson, while Norma's son Arnold Ray was a different person who died before 2017.
3. The 2025 Arnold may be an unrelated same-name person, and the Carl obituary's brother Arnold is another individual.
4. One or more obituary summaries may have omitted or misreported family relationships.

No primary record yet connects the 2025 Arnold Ray Wilson to Norma and Carson. Do not merge the 2025 Arnold with Norma's deceased son or change the sibling count until a direct record is found.
