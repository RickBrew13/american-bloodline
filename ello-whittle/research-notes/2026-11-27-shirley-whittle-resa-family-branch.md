# Avery Ello Whittle — brother Shirley Whittle, wife Resa and descendants
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed the Whittle Cemetery entry for Resa Foster Whittle and identified her as the wife of Avery's brother Shirley, with their children and one descendant branch.

## Shirley and Resa
- Avery's 1977 obituary (family-held clipping, already in Avery's folder) names **Shirley Whittle** among Avery's surviving brothers.
- The 2021 obituary of **Betty Lou Dalton** identifies her parents as **Shirley and Resa Whittle**.
- A secondary cemetery index lists **Resa Foster Whittle (1926–2011)** buried at Whittle Cemetery, Russell County, Kentucky. This is a likely match to Shirley's wife Resa.
- Avery's existing sibling profile identifies **Shirley William Whittle (1923–1987)**. The 2021 obituary independently supports the Shirley/Resa family connection, though the original marriage record and Shirley's death certificate/obituary have not yet been located.

Sources:
- Betty Lou Dalton, Ellers Mortuary / Dignity Memorial, 11 July 2021: https://www.dignitymemorial.com/obituaries/kokomo-in/betty-dalton-10267264
- Alternate Dignity Memorial page for Betty (same obituary text, conflicting birth date by one day): https://www.dignitymemorial.com/obituaries/kokomo-in/betty-dalton-10265150
- PeopleLegacy, Whittle Cemetery listing (Resa Foster Whittle 1926–2011): https://peoplelegacy.com/david_allen_eastham__sr-6Y5g3F

## Children of Shirley and Resa
Betty's obituary identifies these siblings:
- **Betty Lou Whittle Dalton** (22 or 23 December 1943–11 July 2021), wife of Hugh Dalton for 61 years.
- **Danny Whittle** (deceased by 2021).
- **Linda Whittle** (deceased by 2021; married surname not supplied).
- **Ronnie Whittle** (deceased by 2021).
- **Donnie Whittle** (surviving brother in 2021).
- **Aaron Whittle** (surviving brother in 2021).

This establishes at least six children for Shirley and Resa. The obituary does not provide dates or spouses for Danny, Linda, Ronnie, Donnie or Aaron.

## Betty's descendants
Betty's obituary names:

- Children: **Lisa Dalton**, **Desi Dalton** (wife Kendra), and deceased daughter **Julie** (surname and dates not supplied).

## Source discrepancies and boundaries
- The two Dignity Memorial pages differ on Betty's birth date (22 vs. 23 December 1943); both agree on death date, parents, siblings and descendants. Keep the birth day unresolved until a birth record or family source is located.
- The 2021 obituary does not name Shirley and Resa's marriage date or Resa's maiden name beyond the cemetery index's “Foster.”
- The obituary gives the siblings' first names only; do not invent their full names, spouses or dates.
- No direct parentage record yet connects Shirley to Stanton and Louvenia beyond the family-held 1977 obituary and the established sibling list.
