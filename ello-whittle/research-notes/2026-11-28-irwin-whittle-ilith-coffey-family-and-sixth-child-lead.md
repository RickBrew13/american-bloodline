# Avery Ello Whittle — Irwin/Irvin Whittle and Ilith Coffey family; possible sixth child of Bob and Cordella
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed the Russell Springs Irwin/Irvin Whittle (1911–1979) and wife Ilith Coffey Whittle (1918–2003), and tested whether Irvin may be the unnamed sixth child in Cordella's 1951 obituary.

## Irvin/Irwin Whittle and Ilith Coffey Whittle
A Kentucky death-index aggregation lists:
- **Irwin Whittle**, born **18 September 1911**, died **1 November 1979**, last residence Russell Springs, Kentucky.
- **Ilith Whittle**, born **5 February 1918**, died **31 March 2003**, last residence Russell Springs, Kentucky.

Later family obituaries independently identify the couple:
- **Mary Josephine “Mary Jo” Hopper** (31 December 1946–26 February 2023) was born in Eli, Kentucky, a daughter of **Irvin and Ilith Coffey Whittle**. She married Lowell Hopper on 19 October 1963 and had children Mark and Becky.
- Mary Jo's obituary names her siblings: brothers **Larry, Estes, Frankie**, and deceased brother **Bobby**; sisters **Nellie Gaskin** and **Donna Sue Goodin**.
- **Imogene Coffey Cain's** 2021 obituary identifies her half-sister **Ilith Whittle** and notes Ilith's husband **Irvin**. This independently corroborates the marriage and Ilith's Coffey birth family.
- **Mark Anthony Hopper's** 2026 obituary names maternal grandparents **Irvin and Ilith Whittle**, confirming the grandparent connection through Mary Jo.

## Possible connection to Bob and Cordella Whittle — leading sixth-child candidate, not proven
Cordella Tucker Whittle's 22 March 1951 obituary says that six children were born to her marriage to R. S. “Bob” Whittle, but the available transcription names only five: **Otha, Rese, Jim Elmer, Caleb and Grady**. It does not include Irwin/Irvin.

Irwin/Irvin is now a strong candidate for the unnamed sixth child because:
- He was born in 1911 and died in 1979, with Russell Springs as his last residence.
- The obituary names a six-child family group but only five children in the online transcription.
- His later family is well documented in local obituaries and fits the same Russell County Whittle network.

However, this does **not** prove that Irwin was Bob and Cordella's son. The family could descend from another Russell County Whittle branch. The death-index entry and descendant obituaries do not name Irwin's parents. The original 1951 newspaper issue and Irwin's original 1979 death certificate/obituary are required before assigning him as a child.

The 1951 issue is available through the local digital archive: https://russellcounty.historyarchives.online/viewer?bdd=1950&by=1951&d=03221951-03221951&df=1&dt=10&fn=russell_county_news_usa_kentucky_russell_springs_19510322_english_1&i=f

- **Frankie Whittle**, spouse Rose.
- **Nellie Whittle Gaskin**, spouse David.

The 2023 obituary is the strongest source for this sibling group. It does not provide dates for the siblings or state that Irvin's parents were Bob and Cordella. The family branch should remain separate from Avery's direct tree until the parentage is proved.
