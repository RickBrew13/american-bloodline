# Avery Ello Whittle — Cordella Whittle obituary details and Tucker-name disambiguation
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Rechecked the original newspaper transcription, the Tucker family group and same-name Narcissus records before extending Avery's paternal line.

## 1951 Cordella Whittle obituary — full details
The 22 March 1951 *Russell County News* clipping is transcribed by The Shy Genealogist. It reports:
- Cordella was born **14 May 1878** and died **12 March 1951**.
- She married **R. S. “Bob” Whittle** on **24 December 1895**; Bob died **1 December 1949**.
- It says six children were born “to this union,” but names only five: **Otha, Rese, Jim Elmer, Caleb and Grady Whittle**.
- It says she left 17 grandchildren, three great-grandchildren, two sisters and three brothers.
- Funeral: 14 March 1951, Clear Fork Baptist Church; burial reported as **Old Eli Cemetery**; H. E. Pruitt Funeral Home, Jamestown.

The discrepancy between “six children” and five named children remains open. It is not evidence by itself that Stanton was the unlisted sixth child. The wording “to this union” is also a caution: Stanton's reported 1893 birth predates the 1895 marriage. He could be a pre-marital child of Cordella, but the obituary does not say so.

Source: The Shy Genealogist, “The Passing Of Cordella Whittle,” transcribed from *The Russell County News*, 22 March 1951: https://theshygenealogist.wordpress.com/page/3/

## Burial-place reconciliation and Bob's death-date conflict
The 1951 obituary says Cordella was buried in **“old Eli cemetery.”** PeopleLegacy's compiled Tucker family page lists Cordelia E. Tucker Whittle as buried in **McBeath Cemetery**. These may be compatible descriptions rather than conflicting cemeteries: recent local obituaries explicitly use the wording **“McBeath Cemetery, Old Eli, KY”**, showing that McBeath Cemetery is identified with the Old Eli community. PeopleLegacy also lists Cordelia's husband **Robert S. “Bob” Whittle** as buried in McBeath Cemetery. This makes McBeath Cemetery the leading identification for the “old Eli cemetery” in Cordella's obituary, but the 1951 burial register or a contemporary marker/photo should confirm it.

A separate date conflict remains for Bob: the 1951 obituary states he died **1 December 1949**, while PeopleLegacy lists his death year as **1948**. Prefer the contemporary obituary provisionally, but verify with his death certificate, grave marker and probate file.

## Important same-name distinction: another Narcissus Tucker
A 1884 biographical volume, *History of Kentucky*, Vol. 5, contains a separate Russell County family: **Wesley Monroe Tarter** married **Narcissus Tucker**, reported born in Russell County in **1858**. Their children included Mollie, Dr. Tarter, Joseph, Elmer, Jennie and Chrisman V. Tarter. This is not the same person as **Narcissus Popplewell (born 26 December 1849; died 23 September 1908)**, wife of Eli (Ell) Tucker, as reported in Paula Kite's 1998 Russell County genealogy query and the compiled Popplewell family group. The two Narcissus women differ in reported birth year by nine years and have different spouses. Do not merge the Tarter wife into the Popplewell-Tucker family.

Source: *History of Kentucky*, Vol. 5, biographical entry for Dr. James Ballinger Tarter; excerpt identifies his parents as Wesley Monroe Tarter and Narcissus Tucker: https://www.therainwatercollection.com/reference/ref059.pdf

## Tucker family parentage — evidence status
The strongest currently visible support for Eli Tucker and Narcissus Popplewell as Cordella's parents remains:
1. PeopleLegacy's compiled family group for Lillie Bell Tucker McBeath, which names Eli and Narcissus as Lillie's parents and lists Cordelia among Lillie's siblings.
2. Paula Kite's 1998 Russell County query, which gives Louisa “Babe” Tucker's parents as Narcissus Popplewell and Eli (Ell) Tucker and identifies Narcissus's parents as Mason B. Popplewell and Caroline Chamberlain.
3. FamilySearch's community tree for Caroline Chamberlain, which lists Mason B. Popplewell and Caroline as parents of Narcissus and gives Caroline's parents as William Chamberlain and Rachel Brown.

These sources support a promising family reconstruction but are not a substitute for the 1878 birth-register entry or census household evidence. The exact 1878 Russell County birth-register image is identified as Ancestry image **KYVR_994054-0061_2** on KDLA roll **994054**, but the image was blocked in this session. The 1880 Russell County census schedule is available on NARA T9-441 / FamilySearch collection 1417683, but the Tucker household has not yet been located and read.
