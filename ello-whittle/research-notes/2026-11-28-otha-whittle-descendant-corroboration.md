# Avery Ello Whittle — Otha Whittle descendant corroboration
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Extended the Otha Whittle branch using a direct obituary for a child of Otha and Nancy Mae Meadows.

## Direct parentage statement
A 2010 obituary for **Arlus “Johnny” Whittle (7 December 1925–16 January 2010)** states that he was the son of the late **Mae Meadow Whittle and Otha Whittle**. It identifies his sister as Kay (David) Hale of Russell Springs. This independently corroborates that Otha and Mae/Nancy Mae Meadows Whittle were parents of at least two children, Johnny and Kay.

Source: ColumbiaMagazine, “Obituary: Arlus (Johnny) Whittle, 84, Russell Co., KY,” 19 January 2010: https://www.columbiamagazine.com/index.php?sid=34499

## Additional descendant cross-check
The 2022 obituary for **Lana Kay Hale** gives her parents as Otha Whittle and Nancy Mae Meadows Whittle and names siblings Alene McKinley, Lavern Elswick, Wilma Mercer, Jean Bringer, Jerry Holt and Johnny Whittle. It gives her surviving husband David Hale, son John David Hale, two grandchildren and three great-grandchildren.

Source: WVLC/WHVE obituaries, “Lana Kay Hale, 75, Russell Springs, KY,” July 2022: https://wvlc.com/whve-obits/archives/2022-07

## Relationship to Cordella and Bob
Cordella's 1951 obituary names an Otha Whittle among her children. The Otha in these later records is a strong candidate for the same person: the reported birth date 15 September 1898 in the derivative index fits his later family chronology, and his wife/children lived in Russell County. However, neither the 2010 nor 2022 obituary names Otha's parents, so the direct connection to Cordella and Bob still rests on the 1951 obituary plus identity/date/location correlation. Obtain Otha's original 1978 death certificate or obituary and the 1900/1910 census to close the link.
