# Avery Ello Whittle — R. S. “Bob” Whittle and Cordella Tucker Whittle: children identity check
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Compared the five names in Cordella's 1951 obituary with Russell County Whittle death-index entries and later family obituaries.

## The 1951 obituary — six children, five names transcribed
The contemporary *Russell County News* obituary for Cordella Whittle (14 May 1878–12 March 1951) says she married R. S. “Bob” Whittle on 24 December 1895, that he died 1 December 1949, and that six children were born to the union. The online transcription names only five:
1. Otha Whittle
2. Rese Whittle
3. Jim Elmer Whittle
4. Caleb Whittle
5. Grady Whittle

The obituary also reports 17 grandchildren and three great-grandchildren. The original newspaper clipping image has not yet been inspected, so the missing sixth child's name may be absent from the original text or omitted in the online transcription.

Source: The Shy Genealogist, “75 Years Ago Tomorrow – March 22, 1951,” *The Russell County News*, 22 March 1951: https://theshygenealogist.wordpress.com/page/3/

Original issue access: [The Russell County News, 22 March 1951, digital newspaper viewer](https://russellcounty.historyarchives.online/viewer?bdd=1950&by=1951&d=03221951-03221951&df=1&dt=10&fn=russell_county_news_usa_kentucky_russell_springs_19510322_english_1&i=f). The archive's viewer link covers the issue's pages 1–10; the site did not expose the page image to this research session. Inspect the original image for the exact sixth-child wording and whether the transcription omitted a name.

## Probable matches for four named children
A derivative Kentucky death-index aggregation lists these Russell Springs Whittles:
- **Otha Whittle**, 15 September 1898–1 October 1978.
- **Caleb Whittle**, 2 January 1911–1 April 1983.
- **Grady Whittle**, 14 February 1914–1 February 1975.
- **James Whittle**, 30 November 1903–1 March 1976 (a possible identity for “Jim Elmer”).

The names, dates and Russell Springs residence are consistent with the obituary's children, but the index alone does not establish parentage. The Otha match is strengthened by later family obituaries:
- Arlus “Johnny” Whittle's 2010 obituary identifies his parents as Otha Whittle and Nancy Mae Meadows Whittle.
- Lana Kay Hale's 2022 obituary identifies her parents as Otha Whittle and Nancy Mae Meadows Whittle, and names siblings including Johnny.

The James Elmer match is strengthened by a 2015 obituary for Leslie “Tuger Bill” Whittle, which identifies Leslie's parents as **James Elmer Whittle and Sola Owens Whittle** and names Hollis “I.U.” Whittle and Lucille Braley as siblings. The 1951 Cordella obituary separately names “Jim Elmer Whittle” as one of her children. This is a strong cross-obituary identity match, but a direct birth/death record naming Bob and Cordella as James Elmer's parents is still needed.

## “Rese” — do not yet equate with Ruel
The same derivative index lists **Ruel Whittle**, born 16 March 1903 and died 1 November 1976, last residence Russell Springs. This is a possible phonetic/reading match for “Rese,” but it is not established.

A FamilySearch community-tree profile for **Garnet Whittle (1920–2003)** lists **Ruel B. Whittle** as a sibling and identifies their parents as **Edgar M. Whittle and Zeona C. Flanagan**. If this is the same Ruel Whittle, he belongs to a different Whittle branch and should not be assigned as a child of Bob and Cordella. The original 1951 clipping and Ruel's original death certificate/obituary are needed to resolve the name.

Source: FamilySearch community-tree profile, Garnet Whittle: https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/PNBN-P4D/garnet-whittle-1920-2003

## The missing sixth child — Irwin/Irvin Whittle is now the leading candidate
A Kentucky death-index aggregation lists **Irwin Whittle**, born 18 September 1911 and died 1 November 1979, last residence Russell Springs. Later obituaries identify his wife as **Ilith Coffey Whittle** and name their children, including Mary Jo Whittle Hopper (1946–2023). Mary Jo's 2023 obituary names her parents as Irvin and Ilith; a 2021 obituary for Ilith's half-sister independently names Ilith Whittle and her husband Irvin. This makes Irwin/Irvin a strong candidate for the unnamed sixth child in Cordella's obituary, but his parents are not identified in those records. The 1911 birth year is also close to the indexed 1911 birth of Caleb Whittle; this chronology is possible but needs census and vital-record checking.

Clyde remains an alternative, unproved candidate. The derivative index lists:
- **Clyde Whittle**, 23 December 1919–25 August 2001.
- **Clyde O. Whittle**, 27 November 1921–6 December 2007.

A 14 March 1968 *Lebanon Enterprise* article identifies the owner of **Whittle's Super Market** in Russell Springs as **Clyde Whittle**, but gives no middle initial, age, parents or family relationship. Either indexed Clyde could be the store owner. Neither has been proved to be Bob and Cordella's child.

## James Elmer's likely descendants
The 2015 Leslie obituary identifies James Elmer and Sola Owens Whittle as Leslie's parents and names brother Hollis “I.U.” Whittle. A 2019 obituary for **Remona Whittle Popplewell** states that she was first married to Hollis “I.U.” Whittle and had son **Ronnie Whittle**. A later 2020 obituary for Geraldine “Jerry” Stephens identifies daughter Lisa (Ronnie) Whittle and grandchildren Amber Hale and Connor Hale, consistent with Ronnie's family. These records support a descendant branch for Hollis, but they do not yet prove James Elmer's parentage.

## Current assessment
- **Otha, Caleb, Grady and Jim Elmer** each have a plausible death-index match; James Elmer has the strongest later-obituary corroboration.
- **Rese** remains unresolved; do not equate him with Ruel B. Whittle because a community-tree profile places that Ruel in the Edgar M. Whittle family.
- **Clyde** is a possible sixth child because a local newspaper names a Clyde Whittle as supermarket owner and two Russell Springs Clyde death-index entries exist; neither has proven parentage.
- The original 1951 clipping image, Bob and Cordella's original census households, and death certificates for the named children remain necessary to complete the sibling/child group.

No child was added to the family tree based only on a same-name index entry.
