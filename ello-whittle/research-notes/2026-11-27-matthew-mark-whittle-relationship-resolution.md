# Avery Ello Whittle — Matthew Mark Whittle relationship cross-check
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Reconciled the “Matthew” mentioned in Rebecca Ann Whittle's 2008 obituary with the “Matthew Mark Whittle” named in Theresa Whittle's 2016 obituary and Charlotte Whittle's 2025 obituary.

## Three independent family obituaries
1. **Rebecca Ann Whittle**, obituary published 30 September 2008:
   - Gives her parents as **Jeff and Donna Whittle**.
   - Names her living brothers Daniel, David, Joshua and Mark, and sisters Leah, Jordan and Abbey.
   - Separately says she had **one brother named Matthew** who had died before her.
   - Names Avery Whittle and Gladys Christensen as deceased grandparents.
   - Source: Dallas Morning News obituary: https://obits.dallasnews.com/us/obituaries/dallasmorningnews/name/rebecca-whittle-obituary?id=25026934

2. **Theresa Lynn Whittle**, obituary published 2016:
   - Names her deceased nephew **Matthew Mark Whittle**.
   - Names Jeff Whittle and Donna Whittle among her siblings/in-laws.
   - Also names her niece **Rebecca Ann Whittle**.
   - Source: Charles W. Smith & Sons: https://www.charleswsmithandsonsfuneralhome.com/obituaries/theresa-whittle

3. **Charlotte Ann (Mulvey) Whittle**, obituary published 2025:
   - Names her deceased grandson **Matthew** and granddaughter **Becca**.
   - Source: Charles W. Smith & Sons: https://www.charleswsmithandsonsfuneralhome.com/obituaries/charlotte-whittle

## Best-supported interpretation
The strongest explanation is that **Matthew Mark Whittle is the deceased brother Matthew named in Rebecca Ann's obituary**, and therefore likely another child of **Jeff and Donna Whittle** and a grandson of Avery and Charlotte. Theresa's obituary calls Matthew Mark her nephew; Rebecca's obituary places Matthew in the same immediate sibling group as Rebecca, whose parents are Jeff and Donna; Charlotte's obituary independently confirms a deceased grandson Matthew. The name, generation, relationships and family group align.

This is strong cross-obituary corroboration, but none of the three notices supplies Matthew's birth/death dates or explicitly states his parents. Keep the parentage labelled **probable**, not proven, until an original birth/death record or a direct obituary for Matthew is found.

## Important Rebecca distinction

- **Rebecca Jo Whittle (1967–1968)** — Avery and Charlotte's infant daughter, called Theresa's younger sister in the 2016 obituary.
- **Rebecca Ann Whittle (13 May 1986–27 September 2008)** — daughter of Jeff and Donna, granddaughter of Avery and Charlotte, called Theresa's niece in the 2016 obituary.

The records consistently distinguish them as sister versus niece.
