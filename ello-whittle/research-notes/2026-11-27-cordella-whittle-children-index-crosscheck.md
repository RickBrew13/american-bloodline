# Avery Ello Whittle — Cordella and Bob Whittle's children cross-check
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Cross-checked the five named surviving children in Cordella Whittle's 1951 obituary transcription against Kentucky death-index entries and later family obituaries.

## 1951 obituary statement
The transcription of Cordella Whittle's obituary (Russell County News, 22 March 1951) says she and R. S. “Bob” Whittle had six children, and names five:
- Otha Whittle
- Rese Whittle
- Jim Elmer Whittle
- Caleb Whittle
- Grady Whittle

It says she left 17 grandchildren and three great-grandchildren. The sixth child is not named in the transcription. Because the source is a later transcription of the clipping, the spelling “Rese” and the exact name “Jim Elmer” should be checked against the original newspaper image.

Source: The Shy Genealogist, “The Passing Of Cordella Whittle,” 22 March 1951: https://theshygenealogist.wordpress.com/page/3/

## Candidate death-index matches
A secondary Kentucky index lists several Russell County Whittles whose dates fit four of the names:
- **Otha Whittle:** 15 September 1898–1 October 1978 — strong likely match to the named Otha; later obituaries identify Otha as father of Arlus “Johnny” and Lana Kay.
- **R. Whittle:** 26 May 1901–1 May 1973 — possible match to “Rese Whittle,” but the index provides only an initial and does not prove the name.
- **James Whittle:** 30 November 1903–1 March 1976 — possible match to “Jim Elmer Whittle”; the death index gives only James, not the middle name.
- **Caleb Whittle:** 2 January 1911–1 April 1983 — likely match by full given name and county.
- **Grady Whittle:** 14 February 1914–1 February 1975 — likely match by full given name and county.

Source: LocateAncestors, Kentucky Whittle index: https://www.locateancestors.com/whittle-kentucky/

## Important exclusions / disambiguation
A separate **Ruel Whittle (1903–1976)** appears in a different Russell County family group with parents Edgar M. Whittle and Zeona C. Flanagan. Do not merge him with the “Jim Elmer” named in Cordella's obituary. A different 1903–1976 Russell County entry is **James Whittle**, which is a better possible match for Jim Elmer, but still needs an obituary or original death record.

The 1951 obituary's five named children and the index dates form a plausible family group, but the five identity matches are not all proven. The original obituary clipping, 1900/1910 census schedules, marriage records and death certificates should be checked before adding dates or spouses as established.

## Descendant work
Otha's likely descendant branch is documented separately in the 27 November note on Otha Whittle. No reliable spouse/children information has yet been established for the R. Whittle, James/Jim Elmer, Caleb or Grady candidates. These branches remain open.

## Relevance to Avery
If Cordella is proven to be Stanton's mother, these five men would be Stanton's maternal half-brothers and would form part of Avery's extended collateral family. That parentage remains unproven; do not attach the five men as Avery's relatives yet.
