# Avery Ello Whittle — Benjamin H. Whittle, Louisa Rainwater and Hobart Whittle branch
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed the 1912 B. H. Whittle–Louisa Rainwater marriage lead and Whittle burials in McBeath Cemetery.

## 1912 marriage and 1913 birth — probable couple/child match
The Rainwater Collection's transcription of the Pulaski County marriage register records:
- **B. H. Whittle**, age 22, first marriage, living in Pulaski County; he and his parents were born in Russell County.
- **Louisa Rainwater**, age 18, first marriage, living in Pulaski County; she was born in Pulaski County, her father in Indiana and mother in Tennessee.
- Marriage bond 17 July 1912; marriage 18 July 1912; Pulaski County Marriage Book 42, pages 448–449.
- The transcriber notes the marriage certificate spells the groom's surname **White**, although the indexed name is Whittle. The original image must be checked.

A Kentucky birth-index transcription lists **Hobart G. Whittle**, born **27 April 1913** in Russell County, with mother's maiden name **Rainwaters** (indexed as “Luize” in the Rainwater Collection's transcription). The timing and mother's maiden name strongly suggest Hobart was the child of B. H. Whittle and Louisa Rainwater, but the birth index does not identify the father and the original birth record has not been inspected.

Sources:
- Rainwater Collection, Pulaski County marriage records, B. H. Whittle–Louisa Rainwater, 18 July 1912: https://www.therainwatercollection.com/marriage.shtml
- Rainwater Collection, Kentucky births 1911–1940, Russell County, Hobart G. Whittle, 27 April 1913: https://www.therainwatercollection.com/birth.shtml

## Working identification of B. H. Whittle as Benjamin H. Whittle
WeRelate lists **Benjamin H. Whittle**, born **17 April 1889**, died **21 May 1976** in Russell County, with an association/marriage to **Jermima Ellen Gosser (1897–1973)**. A 1998 family-research correspondence cited on that page also links Benjamin and Jermima. This Benjamin is a plausible match for the 1912 groom B. H. Whittle, age 22, and may have later married Jermima after Louisa. No original marriage or divorce record has yet been located to prove the sequence.

Source: WeRelate, Benjamin H. Whittle (1889–1976): https://www.werelate.org/wiki/Person:Benjamin_Whittle_%282%29

## Hobart and Opal Flanagan Whittle — one child branch
A WeRelate/Ancestry compiled family grouping identifies **Hobart/Hobert G. Whittle (1913–2004)** as the husband of **Opal Flanagan (15 March 1913–11 January 1983)**. Ancestry's public profile gives Opal's parents as Arcillus Flanagan and Queen Victoria Edmonds, and lists one child:
- **Darris L. Whittle (born 1933, died 1989)**.

This is a compiled-tree relationship; the original Hobart birth/death certificates and marriage record to Opal should be located before treating the family group as proven. Opal is listed among burials in McBeath Cemetery, which places this Whittle branch in the same local cemetery community as Robert S. “Bob” Whittle, Cordelia Tucker Whittle and Otha L. Whittle.

Sources:

## Possible relationship to Bob Whittle — unproved
A separate 1936 Russell County marriage transcription identifies **Harria Whittle** as a son of **Robert and Matilda Rexroat Whittle**. If Benjamin H. Whittle and Harria were both children of Robert and Matilda, and if R. S. “Bob” Whittle was also their child, this would establish a wider sibling/cousin group in Russell County. No source yet identifies Benjamin's parents or Bob's parents. Their shared surname and burial at McBeath Cemetery are not proof of kinship.

Do not add Benjamin, Harria or Hobart as siblings/descendants of Bob or Cordella until the original census, marriage and death records establish the connection.
