# Avery Ello Whittle — Henry County photo and document archive follow-up
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Rechecked the Henry County, Indiana local archive route for photographs and records relating to Avery, Charlotte and their New Castle family.

## Official repository confirmation
The Henry County Historical Society & Museum's 2024 *Genealogical Repository and Resource* guide confirms several physical collections that may hold family material:
- Family surname files.
- A separate **Family Surname Photo/Document Files** collection described as containing original photographs and documents.
- Family Bibles and scrapbooks.
- Local yearbooks and county-school yearbooks.
- General city/county files and township files; the latter include cemetery resources and photographs.
- Military conflict files, including World War II.
- Historical society accessions and old courthouse ledgers.

The guide shows the archive's physical cabinets and shelves, but it does not publish a searchable surname-by-surname inventory. Text search of the PDF found no entries for Whittle, Mulvey or McGowan; that is **not evidence that the archive has no such files**, because the guide describes the collections rather than enumerating every folder.

## Other official local repositories
The New Castle Henry County Public Library's Indiana Room confirms:
- A full card index for *The Courier-Times*, 1848–1989.
- New Castle city directories, death index (1882–2000), marriage index (1850–1920), birth index (1882–2000), cemetery records and deed index.
- Subject files containing newspaper articles, family histories and genealogical records.
- A searchable online newspaper index with digital images.
- Digitized New Castle High School yearbooks, 1916–2013.

The public index site loaded as a JavaScript application without searchable records exposed in this research session. No new Avery article or image was retrieved from it.
