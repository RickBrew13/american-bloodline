## Kentucky Historical Society surname file located
The Kentucky Historical Society's 2008 *Surname Files at the KHS Library* inventory lists **Whittle — 1 File**. This is a real archival lookup lead, not a digitized item: the public PDF is an inventory of surname-file counts and does not identify the contents or the family branch represented. It may contain submitted family notes, correspondence, clippings, photographs or other genealogical material, but none of those contents can be assumed until the file is inspected.

## Kentucky Ancestors full digital run — searchable by issue
The Kentucky Historical Society confirms that all available print issues of *Kentucky Ancestors* (1965–2013) have been digitized. The Society states that there is no master index, but individual issues can be searched after opening the PDF. The 2004 Volume 39 index lists “Whittle” on page 72; the actual page/issue reference has not yet been located and must be checked against the earlier issues in that volume. This may reveal an article, query, or historical record relevant to the Russell County Whittle family, but the index entry alone does not establish relevance.
