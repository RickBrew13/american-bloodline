# Avery Ello Whittle — sister Veldean Beckner, husband James and descendants
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Identified Veldean's husband by matching the children listed in her 2020 obituary with James Beckner's 2017 obituary.

## Veldean and James Beckner
- **Veldene Beckner** (13 September 1938–15 October 2020) was born in Russell County to **Stanton and Louvenia McGowan Whittle**.
- Her 2020 obituary names four living children: Kathy (Tommy) Howard, Donna Sibley, Barbara Grider and James Beckner Jr.; it also says her son Timothy Beckner and granddaughter Traci Howard predeceased her.
- **James Beckner (12 May 1933–20 January 2017)** is the likely husband. His obituary names the same four children and says son Timmy Beckner predeceased him. The exact matching child group is strong evidence that he was Veldean's husband, though the obituary does not explicitly state the marriage.
- James's parents were Lloyd Beckner and Cecil Tinney Johns.

Sources:
- Veldene Beckner obituary, WVLC/WHVE, October 2020: https://wvlc.com/whve-obits/archives/2020-10/
- James Beckner obituary, ColumbiaMagazine, 21 January 2017: https://columbiamagazine.com/index.php?sid=88245

## Children of Veldean and James
- **Kathy (Tommy) Howard**, Indiana.
- **Donna Sibley**, Louisiana.
- **Barbara Grider**, Russell Springs, Kentucky.
- **James Beckner Jr.**, Russell Springs, Kentucky.
- **Timothy “Timmy” Beckner**, deceased before 2017/2020; no dates or spouse/children identified yet.

The obituaries give nine grandchildren, 23 great-grandchildren and six great-great-grandchildren in 2020, but do not name them. James's 2017 obituary reported nine grandchildren, 17 great-grandchildren and four great-great-grandchildren, so the later increase is plausible.

## Relationship to Avery
Veldean was Avery's sister. Her children are Avery's nieces/nephews; later descendants are collateral descendants through this sibling branch. The 2020 obituary's statement that she was preceded by four sisters and three brothers is consistent with the adult sibling group: Iza Pearl, Marie, Norma and Grace (sisters) and Avery, Shirley and Calvin (brothers). This supports the existing sibling roster.
