# Avery Ello Whittle — McGowan family-origin leads for Louvenia
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed the McGowan surname of Avery's mother, Louvenia M. McGowan Whittle (c.1900–1940), to identify her parents and grandparents. The current evidence establishes a substantial Russell County McGowan family cluster but does not yet place Louvenia in it.

## Direct evidence for Louvenia
Three independent later obituaries identify **Stanton B. Whittle and Louvenia/Louvienie McGowan Whittle** as the parents of their children. The cemetery aggregation gives Louvenia's approximate lifespan as 1900–1940. No source located in this pass names Louvenia's own parents, exact birth date, or siblings. The McGowan family group below is a lead, not a proven parentage assignment.

## Russell County McGowan family group — strong collateral lead
A 2004 typed/handwritten genealogical research file, *Bailey—Johnson*, reports:
- **John Henry “Briar John” McGowan** (20 November 1853–7 May 1939), son of **David McGowan and Elizabeth Wilson**.
- John Henry married **Mary W. Upchurch** on 13 March 1877 in Russell County; the note cites Russell County Marriage Book 3 and identifies G. H. Wilson as officiant and Moses Wilson as bondsman.
- Their listed children include Vernetta (December 1880), Jeretta Clementine (21 December 1882), Hettie (August 1884), Joseph H. (June 1886), Hattie B. (December 1887), Benjamin H. (February 1890), Nora A. (December 1892), Laura B. (October 1894), Melvina (November 1895) and John (March 1898).
- It also describes Vernetta's marriage to Stewart P. Johnson and lists seven children.
- A separate typed note in the same file says Joseph H. McGowan (17 June 1886–28 February 1970) married Sallie Whittle on 12 February 1905 and lists their children. This is a distinct Whittle–McGowan marriage; it does not identify Louvenia as their child.

The source is a compiled family-research file, not a set of original certificates. Its marriage-book citation is a useful route to the county record. The exact identity of John Henry “Briar John” in this file is consistent with Paula Kite's 1998 query, which calls Briar John a brother of Thomas W. McGowan (1861–1933).

## Earlier generation of the McGowan group — compiled tree
The *Family Tree McGowan-Hall* compilation by Edith Kyles identifies **David McGowan (1821–1898)** and **Elizabeth Wilson (1826–1915)** as parents of:
- John Henry McGowan (1853–1939), also called Briar John in other family notes;
- Pernecia Ellen McGowan (7 January 1858–17 March 1921), who married Joseph Newton Roy on 10 December 1879 and had a large family;
- Sarah Jane McGowan (born 1852; death not supplied);
- Nancy Elizabeth McGowan (1859–1930), shown as wife of James Sampson Foster;
- additional siblings/branches appear in the wider compilation.

This is a secondary, sourced family-tree publication. Its source list refers to 1860 and 1900 census, Kentucky birth and death records, but not every claim is accompanied by an image visible in the public entry. It does not yet identify Louvenia as a descendant.

## Same-surname Whittle/McGowan woman — not yet connected
A PeopleLegacy cemetery aggregation lists **Sarah Elizabeth McGowan Whittle (1866–1926)** at Hugh Frank Cemetery, Russell County. She is a possible member of the wider McGowan family and a Whittle marriage connection, but no husband, children, or parents were established in this pass. She must not be conflated with:
- Louvenia M. McGowan Whittle (c.1900–1940), wife of Stanton Bell Whittle;
- Sallie Whittle, who married Joseph H. McGowan in 1905.

The same cemetery listing includes Lee McGowan (1931–1931), but no evidence connects that infant to Louvenia or Sarah.

Source: PeopleLegacy, Sarah Whittle burial aggregation and Hugh Frank Cemetery listing: https://peoplelegacy.com/burial-records/sarah/whittle/

## Documentary route to Louvenia's parents
The best next records are:
1. Louvenia's original Kentucky birth-register entry, likely around 1900; search variant spellings Louvenia, Louvienie, Luvinia, Lavinia and McGowan/McGowen.
2. 1900 census Russell County households with McGowan children born 1899–1901; compare the original schedules, not only index hits.
3. 1910 census for Louvenia as a child, likely with her birth family; search all Russell County McGowan households and neighboring counties.
4. Marriage record for Stanton Bell Whittle and Louvenia McGowan, expected before Avery's 1927 birth; original license/application may identify her age, residence, and parents or guardian.
5. Louvenia's 1940 Kentucky death certificate, which may name her parents and informant.
6. Death certificates/obituaries for Sarah Elizabeth McGowan Whittle and the children of David McGowan and Elizabeth Wilson to test any sibling or cousin connection.

**Current conclusion:** Louvenia's maiden surname places her in a well-documented Russell County McGowan research cluster, but no parentage has yet been proved. Do not add David McGowan, Elizabeth Wilson, John Henry McGowan, or any other McGowan as Louvenia's parent without the birth/death/census evidence.
