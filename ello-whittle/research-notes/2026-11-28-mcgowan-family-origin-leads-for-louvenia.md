# Avery Ello Whittle — McGowan family-origin leads for Louvenia
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed the McGowan surname of Avery's mother, Louvenia M. McGowan Whittle (c.1900–1940), to identify her parents and grandparents. The current evidence establishes a substantial Russell County McGowan family cluster but does not yet place Louvenia in it.

## Direct evidence for Louvenia
Three independent later obituaries identify **Stanton B. Whittle and Louvenia/Louvienie McGowan Whittle** as the parents of their children. The cemetery aggregation gives Louvenia's approximate lifespan as 1900–1940. No source located in this pass names Louvenia's own parents, exact birth date, or siblings. The McGowan family group below is a lead, not a proven parentage assignment.

## Russell County McGowan family group — strong collateral lead
A 2004 typed/handwritten genealogical research file, *Bailey—Johnson*, reports:
- **John Henry “Briar John” McGowan** (20 November 1853–7 May 1939), son of **David McGowan and Elizabeth Wilson**.
- John Henry married **Mary W. Upchurch** on 13 March 1877 in Russell County; the note cites Russell County Marriage Book 3 and identifies G. H. Wilson as officiant and Moses Wilson as bondsman.
- Their listed children include Vernetta (December 1880), Jeretta Clementine (21 December 1882), Hettie (August 1884), Joseph H. (June 1886), Hattie B. (December 1887), Benjamin H. (February 1890), Nora A. (December 1892), Laura B. (October 1894), Melvina (November 1895) and John (March 1898).
- It also describes Vernetta's marriage to Stewart P. Johnson and lists seven children.
- A separate typed note in the same file says Joseph H. McGowan (17 June 1886–28 February 1970) married Sallie Whittle on 12 February 1905 and lists their children. This is a distinct Whittle–McGowan marriage; it does not identify Louvenia as their child.

The source is a compiled family-research file, not a set of original certificates. Its marriage-book citation is a useful route to the county record. The exact identity of John Henry “Briar John” in this file is consistent with Paula Kite's 1998 query, which calls Briar John a brother of Thomas W. McGowan (1861–1933).

Sources:

- Allied Families, *Oliver Smith* PDF, same McGowan/Upchurch family notes and Joseph H. McGowan's two marriages: https://www.alliedfamilies.com/uploads/1/3/1/8/13181256/13__n_-_p_smith.pdf

## Earlier generation of the McGowan group — compiled tree
The *Family Tree McGowan-Hall* compilation by Edith Kyles identifies **David McGowan (1821–1898)** and **Elizabeth Wilson (1826–1915)** as parents of:
- John Henry McGowan (1853–1939), also called Briar John in other family notes;
- Pernecia Ellen McGowan (7 January 1858–17 March 1921), who married Joseph Newton Roy on 10 December 1879 and had a large family;
- Sarah Jane McGowan (born 1852; death not supplied);
- Nancy Elizabeth McGowan (1859–1930), shown as wife of James Sampson Foster;
- additional siblings/branches appear in the wider compilation.

This is a secondary, sourced family-tree publication. Its source list refers to 1860 and 1900 census, Kentucky birth and death records, but not every claim is accompanied by an image visible in the public entry. It does not yet identify Louvenia as a descendant.

## Same-surname Whittle/McGowan woman — not yet connected
A PeopleLegacy cemetery aggregation lists **Sarah Elizabeth McGowan Whittle (1866–1926)** at Hugh Frank Cemetery, Russell County. She is a possible member of the wider McGowan family and a Whittle marriage connection, but no husband, children, or parents were established in this pass. She must not be conflated with:
- Louvenia M. McGowan Whittle (c.1900–1940), wife of Stanton Bell Whittle;
- Sallie Whittle, who married Joseph H. McGowan in 1905.

The same cemetery listing includes Lee McGowan (1931–1931), but no evidence connects that infant to Louvenia or Sarah.

Source: PeopleLegacy, Sarah Whittle burial aggregation and Hugh Frank Cemetery listing: https://peoplelegacy.com/burial-records/sarah/whittle/

## Documentary route to Louvenia's parents
The best next records are:
1. Louvenia's original Kentucky birth-register entry, likely around 1900; search variant spellings Louvenia, Louvienie, Luvinia, Lavinia and McGowan/McGowen.
2. 1900 census Russell County households with McGowan children born 1899–1901; compare the original schedules, not only index hits.
3. 1910 census for Louvenia as a child, likely with her birth family; search all Russell County McGowan households and neighboring counties.
4. Marriage record for Stanton Bell Whittle and Louvenia McGowan, expected before Avery's 1927 birth; original license/application may identify her age, residence, and parents or guardian.
5. Louvenia's 1940 Kentucky death certificate, which may name her parents and informant.
6. Death certificates/obituaries for Sarah Elizabeth McGowan Whittle and the children of David McGowan and Elizabeth Wilson to test any sibling or cousin connection.

**Current conclusion:** Louvenia's maiden surname places her in a well-documented Russell County McGowan research cluster, but no parentage has yet been proved. Do not add David McGowan, Elizabeth Wilson, John Henry McGowan, or any other McGowan as Louvenia's parent without the birth/death/census evidence.


## Highest-priority candidate household to test — William James McGowan and Nancy Elizabeth McQueary
A separate Russell County family-history report, *Narrative Report for Rice Wilson*, gives a specific McGowan couple who fit the county and generation for Louvenia's possible birth family:
- **William James McGowan**, born April 1864 in Jabez, Russell County; died 26 February 1940 in Pulaski County, Kentucky.
- **Nancy Elizabeth McQueary**, born November 1866 in Russell County; married William James McGowan on 22 March 1888.
- Nancy later married **John William West** about 1907.

Because Louvenia's cemetery-index birth year is 1900, this couple is a plausible household to check in the 1900 Russell County census. If Louvenia appears as their daughter, the McQueary maternal line would also extend Avery's ancestry through **Louisa Jane Wilson and Rice Wilson**. The compiled report does not list children for William and Nancy and does not name Louvenia, so this is only a targeted hypothesis. It is not evidence that they were her parents.

The same report identifies Nancy as a daughter of Louisa Jane Wilson and Harvey McQueary, and records her two marriages. The 1900 census is the decisive next check: look for William, Nancy and any children in the Jabez/Russell County household, recording each child's exact name, age, relationship and birthplace from the original schedule.

Source:
- *Narrative Report for Rice Wilson*, 6 January 2026, page 7, entry 14 (Nancy Elizabeth McQueary and William James McGowan): https://kygenweb.net/russell/families/files/wilson-rice-1795-1878.pdf

**Priority:** Search the 1900 Russell County census household of William James McGowan and Nancy Elizabeth McQueary before attaching any other McGowan family. Also obtain William's 1940 Kentucky death certificate and Nancy's death record to confirm identities and surviving family.


## Additional McQueary family-book and collateral McGowan connection
FamilySearch's catalog confirms a 285-page protected/search-only family book, **The McQueary Clan: Thru Mountains and Sunsets**, by Marjorie McQueary and Freda Strampe (FamilySearch book identifier 1003592). A FamilySearch Memories upload identifies a scan of page 20 as “Children of William and Nancy.” The catalog description says the book traces the descendants of the McQueary family from North Carolina into Kentucky and other states. The book is not fully open online in this session, but it is a strong source to consult for the ancestry and collateral relatives of Nancy Elizabeth McQueary. It may help identify the exact McQueary branch and any children of Nancy's marriage to William James McGowan; the page-20 title alone does not prove anything about Louvenia.

Sources:

## Additional Russell County McGowan birth-index entries — collateral leads
A Kentucky birth-index transcription (delayed-index collection) exposes three Russell County records that may help reconstruct the wider McGowan community:
- **Alverta E. McGowan**, born 5 August 1906, Russell County; mother's name indexed as **Sarah Smith**; index no. 217 48729.
- **Ezra B. McGowan**, born 10 November 1910, Russell County; mother's name indexed as **Sarah Smith**; index no. 198 92665.
- **Theo V. Popplewell**, born 14 April 1906, Russell County; mother's name indexed as **Iola McGowan**; index no. 214 48622.

The first two entries suggest a woman named Sarah Smith had at least two children indexed under the McGowan surname. The third suggests Iola McGowan was the mother of a Popplewell child. These may represent relatives or another McGowan household in Russell County, but the index omits the fathers and does not establish any connection to Louvenia. Do not attach Alverta, Ezra, Iola or Theo to Louvenia's family without census, marriage, death or original birth-register evidence.

## Newly identified collateral McGowan household — Andrew McGown, Pheby and Leuvena/Lavina
The 1860 Russell County census transcription (M653-394, page 734, household/family 628) lists:
- **Andrew McGown**, age 60, born Virginia, farmer;
- **Pheby McGown**, age 47, born Kentucky;
- **Leuvena McGown**, age 21, born Kentucky.

A FamilySearch community-tree profile for **Morgan McQueary (born 1843)** reports that he married **Lavina McGowan** on **25 August 1863** in Russell County. It gives Lavina's birth year as 1839 and lists three children: **James Alford Coldwell McQuary (1865–), Morgan A. McQueary (1868–1906), and Phoebe Jane McQueary (1870–)**. The 1860 census Leuvena (age 21, born about 1839) is a strong age/name match for Lavina McGowan, and the 1863 marriage is consistent with her leaving the Andrew/Pheby household. The original marriage record and birth/death records are still needed to prove that the census Leuvena is the same person as Lavina.

Sources:
- USGenWeb Census Project, Russell County, 1860, M653-394, page 734, household 628, lines 14–16: https://www.us-census.org/pub/usgenweb/census/ky/russell/1860/pg00730.txt
- FamilySearch community-tree profile, Morgan McQueary and Lavina McGowan, marriage 25 August 1863 and children: https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/LHDF-WFW/morgan-mcqueary-1843

A separate Russell County marriage index records **Mary E. McGown** marrying **Thomas Hatfield** on **2 September 1875**. She may be another member of the Andrew/Pheby McGown family, but the index supplies no parents and this is not yet proven. It is a useful collateral lead to compare with 1870/1880 census households and the original marriage register.

Source: The Heritage Lady, Jerry Hatfield's compiled genealogy, Russell County marriage list entry 4577: https://www.theheritagelady.com/jerry-hatfields-compiled-genealogy/hatfxky0.htm

**Relationship to Louvenia remains unknown.** The Andrew/Pheby/Lavina group is a distinct Russell County McGowan branch from the David McGowan–Elizabeth Wilson group discussed above. It may be a collateral branch of Louvenia's birth family, but no source yet links Louvenia to Lavina, Andrew or Pheby. Do not attach them as her ancestors until her 1900 birth record, 1940 death certificate or original census household establishes the connection.
