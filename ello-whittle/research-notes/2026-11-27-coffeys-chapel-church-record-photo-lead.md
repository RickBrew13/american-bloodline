# Avery Ello Whittle — Coffey's Chapel church record and photo lead
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed the church named in Cordella Whittle's 1951 obituary and identified where baptism, membership and possible family photographs may survive.

## Family connection to the church
The 22 March 1951 *Russell County News* obituary transcription for **Cordella Whittle** says she joined the Methodist church at **Coffey's Chapel** early in life and remained a faithful member. This is a direct family-history clue to her religious community, although the transcription does not identify a baptism date or parents.

The present congregation is **Coffey's Chapel United Methodist Church**, at **3991 S. Highway 76, Eli, Russell County, Kentucky**. Contemporary church notices and obituaries confirm the church and its cemetery remain active.

## Church-record preservation guidance
The United Methodist Church's official genealogy guidance states that baptism and local church membership records are normally held by the local congregation. If a church closes and merges, its records should transfer to the successor; if it closes without a successor, records should transfer to the annual conference archives. The Kentucky Conference's records-retention schedule identifies the Area Archives at Asbury University or Kentucky Wesleyan College as recognized repositories for older Kentucky Methodist church records.

Sources:
- UMC.org, *Genealogy Search — Baptism and Local Church Membership*: https://www.umc.org/who-we-are/history/genealogy-search
- Kentucky Annual Conference, *What Do We Do with the Church Records?* (records-retention schedule): https://www.kyumc.org/files/oldfileslibrary/Chapt_10_JOURNAL.pdf

## Records and photographs to seek
If a later contact or in-person search is authorized, ask the church or conference archivist whether it retains:
1. Baptismal register and membership roll for Cordella Tucker/Whittle, especially 1878–1951.
2. Membership, baptism or Sunday-school records for Stanton Whittle and other Whittle/Tucker children.
3. Marriage, funeral and cemetery registers for the Whittle, Tucker and Popplewell families.
4. Sunday-school class photographs, choir photographs, church anniversary photographs, group portraits and cemetery photographs.
5. Any church history or anniversary booklet naming the Tucker, Whittle, Popplewell and McGowan families.

A family photograph in a church group could include Cordella or her children, but no such image has been located or identified yet. No staff member was contacted and no record request was submitted.

## Evidence boundary
This is a targeted archival lead based on Cordella's obituary and the current church's documented location. It does not prove Cordella's baptism, membership dates, Stanton's parentage, or the existence of photographs. Keep the Tucker-to-Stanton relationship as an unproven hypothesis until original church/census/vital records are found.
