# Avery Ello Whittle — Cordelia E. Tucker Whittle parentage lead
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Investigated the possible mother of Avery's paternal grandfather, Stanton Bell Whittle, and traced the Tucker family group that may be Cordelia's birth family.

## Cordelia's identity
A secondary cemetery/family aggregation identifies **Cordelia E. Tucker Whittle (1878–1951)**. Her 22 March 1951 Russell County obituary transcription, already linked in the earlier Cordella research note, identifies her as Cordella Whittle, wife of R. S. “Bob” Whittle, and reports that she had two sisters and three brothers. The Tucker maiden name is therefore a useful new lead, but it needs a primary record.

## Tucker parents and sibling group — working hypothesis
PeopleLegacy's family page for **Lillie Bell Tucker McBeath (1889–1974)** names her parents as:
- **Eli Tucker**, 1849–1919, buried Johnson Cemetery, Russell County.
- **Narcissus “Arsis” Popplewell Tucker**, 1849–1908, buried Johnson Cemetery, Russell County.

The same page lists these siblings for Lillie:
- Christmon Van Winkle Tucker (1873–1940)
- Cornelius Tucker (1874–1953)
- Louisa Jane Tucker Stephens (1877–1933)
- **Cordelia E. Tucker Whittle (1878–1951)**
- Mary Magdalene Tucker Stephens (1880–1966)
- Parrish Elmer Tucker (1883–1967)
- Martha Carolyn Tucker Wilson (1885–1948)

This makes Eli Tucker and Narcissus Popplewell the leading working parents for Cordelia. The listed Tucker sibling group has three brothers (Christmon, Cornelius and Parrish) and four sisters (Louisa, Mary, Martha and Lillie). Cordella's 1951 obituary says she was survived by two sisters and three brothers. Two of the four listed sisters could have died before 1951, which is consistent with the reported two surviving sisters. However, Christmon died in 1940, leaving only two of the three listed brothers alive in 1951, while the obituary reports three surviving brothers. That mismatch suggests an additional brother missing from the PeopleLegacy grouping, a misidentified sibling, or an error in one of the sources. The sibling count is therefore a partial fit, not confirmation. The PeopleLegacy family grouping remains secondary.

Independent corroboration: a 1998 Russell County genealogy query by Paula Kite states that **Louisa (Babe) Tucker (1877–1933)** was a daughter of **Narcissus Popplewell (1849–1908) and Eli (Ell) Tucker (1849–1915/1919)**, and identifies Narcissus's parents as **Mason B. Popplewell and Caroline Chamberlain**. The same MyTrees submitter family (jo003994) lists a daughter of Mason and Caroline under the variant **“Acissa Popplewell”** born in 1849, likely a spelling/transcription variant for Narcissus but not confirmed. That submitter's tree also contains a possible later marriage of Eli Tucker to Angeletta McQueary in 1909; see the separate second-marriage note. Louisa and Cordelia are listed in the same Tucker sibling group by PeopleLegacy. The 1998 query is a researcher's submission, not an original record.

## Why this may matter to Stanton
Stanton's reported birth is 10 July 1893; Cordella was born in 1878 and married R. S. “Bob” Whittle on 24 December 1895. If both dates are accurate, Cordella was about 15 when Stanton was born and married Bob about two years later. Stanton could be a child from before her marriage to Bob, but neither the 1951 obituary nor the secondary Tucker family grouping explicitly calls him her son. The obituary's six children “to this union” names five survivors (Otha, Rese, Jim Elmer, Caleb and Grady) and does not name Stanton. **Do not add Cordella as Stanton's mother without census/vital-record proof.**

The Kentucky 1900 census Soundex uses code **T260** for Tucker. The digitized NARA T1047 volume **T-260 Kate through T-416 Butler** is available as Wikimedia/Internet Archive item **soundex1900ky173**:

The 1900 Soundex card for **Eli Tucker** should identify the enumeration district, sheet and line for his household. The original 1880 and 1900 schedules should then be checked for Cordelia, Louisa, Lillie and the other children. The 1900 schedule should also state the number of years Eli and Narcissus had been married and the number of children born/living. No Soundex card or schedule image was successfully read in this pass.

## Earlier Popplewell ancestry
The 1998 query and FamilySearch's Caroline Chamberlain profile place Narcissus as a daughter of **Mason B. Popplewell (1823–1893) and Caroline Chamberlain (1829–1923)**. The FamilySearch profile further lists Caroline's parents as **William Chamberlain and Rachel Brown**. These are useful compiled-tree leads; original census, marriage, probate and death records still need to be located before upgrading the relationships to proven.

No relationship was changed in the family tree. The Tucker line remains a well-supported working hypothesis, not a proven parentage claim.
