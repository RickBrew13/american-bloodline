# Avery Ello Whittle — sibling spouse and descendant network
**Research date:** 26 November 2026  
**Scope:** Avery's siblings and their immediate family branches. The existing Avery folder, sibling list, original 1977 obituary clipping and prior research notes were reviewed before this cross-reference. This note records only relationships supported by the obituary evidence currently available; unknown spouses/children remain open.

## Avery's 1977 obituary — surviving siblings
The original family-held 1977 clipping names five surviving sisters—**Marie Stacey, Pearl Coffey, Veldean Beckner, Grace Eastham and Norma Jean Wilson**—and two surviving brothers—**Calvin Whittle and Shirley Whittle**. This is consistent with the adult sibling group in Avery's current profile. Four additional siblings in the family roster died in infancy/childhood before the obituary and therefore are not named among the survivors.

## Sibling branches with later obituary evidence

### Norma Jean Whittle Wilson (17 January 1933–30 January 2017)
Bernard Funeral Home's obituary identifies her parents as **Stanton B. Whittle and Louvenia McGowan Whittle**, and husband **Carson Wilson**. It reports that a son, **Arnold Ray Wilson**, predeceased her. Four surviving children are named:
- **Carl J. Wilson** (Leslie)
- **Sheena Wilson** (Walter Grider)
- **Troy Wilson** (Sheila)
- **Lyn Wilson** (Myra)

The obituary reports 10 grandchildren and five great-grandchildren. It says she was preceded in death by two sisters and three brothers and survived by two sisters, **Grace Eastham** and **Dean Beckner**. “Dean Beckner” is consistent with the sister later identified as **Veldean Beckner**; treat Dean as a likely shortened name variant, not a separate sibling.

Sources:

### Grace Whittle Eastham (27 March 1925–20 November 2018)
The 2018 obituary independently identifies her parents as **Stanton B. and Louvenia McGowan Whittle**. It says a daughter, **Phyllis Holder**, predeceased her and names four surviving children:
- **Gayla Sue Kerr**
- **Treva Meirose** (Roger)
- **Tina Milliner**
- **Rex Allen Eastham** (Nancy)

The obituary reports 14 grandchildren and several great-grandchildren, and names one surviving sister: **Veldean Beckner**. A 2026 obituary for Rex Allen Eastham identifies his parents as Grace Whittle Eastham and **Kirby Eastham**. Kirby is therefore the father of Rex; whether he was Grace's legal spouse is not established by the records reviewed here. Grace's obituary does not name a husband.

Sources:

### Veldean / Veldene / “Dean” Whittle Beckner (13 September 1938–15 October 2020)
The 2020 obituary identifies her parents as **Stanton and Louvenia McGowan Whittle** and reports four sisters and three brothers predeceased her. It names a deceased son, **Timothy Beckner**, and a deceased granddaughter, **Traci Howard**. Four surviving children are:
- **Kathy Howard** (Tommy)
- **Donna Sibley**
- **Barbara Grider**
- **James Beckner Jr.**

It reports nine grandchildren, 23 great-grandchildren and six great-great-grandchildren. The obituary uses the spelling **Veldene**; earlier records use Veldean, Velden and Dean. These are treated as spelling/nickname variants for one sister, pending a birth record.

Source:

## Siblings named in 1977 with no complete family branch yet

### Iza Pearl Whittle — known as Pearl Coffey
The 1977 obituary names “Mrs. Pearl Coffey.” A derivative death-index page lists a **Pearl Coffey** born 16 December 1920 and died 1 August 1996, last residence Russell Springs; this is a likely match to Avery's sister Iza Pearl Whittle (1920–1996), but the birth date and identity should be checked against the original death certificate/obituary. The 1944 draft card's contact person is transcribed as **Waldo Coffee/Coffey**. A Waldo L. Coffey (1917–1994) appears in a derivative index; he may be related through Pearl, but no marriage or kinship record links them. Do not identify Waldo as Pearl's husband or Avery's brother-in-law without a record.

### Marie Whittle Stacey
The 1977 obituary names “Mrs. Marie Stacey.” The family profile lists Marie Whittle Stacey (about 1930–2001). Her exact married name spelling (Stacey/Stacy), spouse and children remain unverified from primary records. The 1940 census index names **Imogene** in Stanton's household rather than Marie; this may be a full/middle-name variant or an indexing error, but no separate Imogene sibling is created until the original census image is checked.

### Calvin Whittle
Named as Avery's brother in the 1977 obituary; the family profile reports 1931–1996. No identity-secure spouse or child record was located in this pass.

### Shirley Whittle
Named as Avery's brother in the 1977 obituary; the family profile reports 1923–1987. No identity-secure spouse or child record was located in this pass.

## Four early-deceased siblings in the current family roster
The current profile lists **Anna (1916–1938), Heasby Earl (1922–1923), Ulissus (1928–1929) and Gearlind (1936, infancy)** as children of Stanton and Louvenia. They are not named in the 1977 obituary's surviving-sibling list. Their original birth/death/cemetery records and Gearlind's sex/full name still need verification; do not infer additional descendants.

## 1940 census household discrepancy
The 1940 index lists Avery as “Ello Whittle” and names household members **Gracy, Shirley, Imogene, Calvin, Norma G. and Veldene**. The later 1977 obituary names **Marie Stacey** rather than Imogene. The 2017/2018/2020 obituaries confirm Grace, Norma and Veldean/Dean as siblings, and the 1977 obituary confirms Marie. The identity of 1940 “Imogene” remains unresolved; it may be Marie's full name or an index transcription error. The original 1940 schedule image is needed before changing the roster.

## Family-level cross-check
- The 1977 obituary's five sisters/two brothers correspond to the seven adult siblings known to be alive then.
- Norma's 2017 obituary identifies two surviving sisters, Grace and Dean/Veldean; Grace's 2018 obituary names Veldean as her only surviving sister; Veldean's 2020 obituary reports four sisters and three brothers predeceased her. This sequence is internally consistent for the seven adult siblings.
- The four infant/child deaths in the family roster are not necessarily counted in later obituaries' sibling totals.
- The current profile's total of 11 siblings should remain provisional until the 1900–1940 census images, birth/death records and cemetery inscriptions are reviewed.
