# Avery Ello Whittle — Waldo Coffey draft-card contact follow-up
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed the “Waldo Coffee” contact-person field on Avery's original 3 July 1944 WWII draft registration card. The card remains the primary source for the contact name; later sources are used only to identify the possible contact.

## What the card says
Avery's 1944 draft registration card lists **“Coffee, Waldo”** as the contact person. The card does not state the relationship. The surname is spelled Coffee on the transcription; the Kentucky family surname is commonly Coffey, so both forms were searched.

## New independent identification lead
A 2011 obituary for **Cleatus Cordell Coffey** (2 March 1924–15 May 2011) identifies him as a son of **Pearl Stephens Coffey and “Bug” Coffey** and names two deceased brothers: **Glendo Coffey and Waldo Coffey**. A derivative Social Security-style index lists **Waldo L. Coffey**, born 2 October 1917 and died 5 August 1994, last residence Russell Springs, Kentucky. The dates and location are compatible with the Waldo named on Avery's draft card.

- LocateAncestors, Kentucky Coffey index, entry for Waldo L. Coffey (2 October 1917–5 August 1994): https://www.locateancestors.com/coffey-42642/

## Relationship to Avery remains unproved
Avery's 1977 obituary names a sister **Pearl Coffey**. The family profile identifies her as Iza Pearl Whittle (16 December 1920–1 August 1996), a likely but not fully primary-source-verified match. The similar ages and surname make it reasonable to investigate whether Waldo L. Coffey was Pearl's husband and therefore Avery's brother-in-law. The 2011 obituary does not name Waldo's spouse, and no marriage record or obituary explicitly connects Waldo to Pearl Whittle. **Do not add Waldo as Pearl's husband or Avery's brother-in-law yet.**

The name “Waldo Coffee” on the draft card could instead refer to another relative, family friend, employer or contact. The 2011 obituary confirms a Russell County Waldo Coffey in the right generation but does not prove that he is the same man on the card.
