# Avery Ello Whittle — Becky / Rebecca Jo Whittle identity resolution
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Reconciled the infant daughter called “Becky” in Charlotte Whittle's 2025 obituary with “Rebecca Jo Whittle” in Theresa Whittle's 2016 obituary.

## Three-source match
- **Charlotte Ann (Mulvey) Whittle obituary, 27 May 2025:** says Avery and Charlotte had eight children and that their youngest child, daughter **Becky**, died at six months old. It names Becky again among Charlotte's deceased family members.
- **Theresa Lynn Whittle obituary, 29 November 2016:** names her younger sister **Rebecca Jo Whittle** as deceased.
- **Secondary obituary-index result:** lists **Rebecca Jo Whittle**, New Castle, Indiana, born **8 November 1967**, died **15 May 1968**. The dates make her six months and seven days old, matching Charlotte's statement that Becky died at six months.

## Conclusion
The strongest explanation is that **Rebecca Jo Whittle was the baby called Becky by her mother**. The matching six-month age, same parents/family, and matching generation strongly support one child, not two. This also reconciles the eight-child total:
- Six siblings named in Theresa's obituary (Rob, Debra, Jeff, Mike, Cindy and Louretta),
- Theresa herself,
- Her deceased younger sister Rebecca Jo / Becky.

List her once as **Rebecca Jo “Becky” Whittle (8 November 1967–15 May 1968)**. The exact dates currently rely on a secondary obituary index; no original birth/death certificate or funeral record has been obtained. Do not create a separate ninth child called Becky.
