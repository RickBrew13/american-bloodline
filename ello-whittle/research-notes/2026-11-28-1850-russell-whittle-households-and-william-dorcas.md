# Avery Ello Whittle — 1850 Russell County Whittle households and William–Dorcas family
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed the 1843 William Whittle–Dorcas Litrell marriage and tested the Russell County Whittle family cluster against the 1850 census transcription.

## 1843 marriage — William Whittle and Dorcas Litrell
A partial transcription of the Russell County marriage register records:
- **William Whittle** and **Dorcas Litrell**, married **19 February 1843**.
- Russell County Marriage Book 40, page 40; officiant **J. Bowlin, J.P.**

Source: Genealogy Trails, Russell County marriage certificates, transcription from *Kentucky Ancestors* (July 1965): https://www.genealogytrails.com/ken/russell/russellco_marriages.html

## 1850 Russell County census — William Whittle household
The USGenWeb Census Project's Russell County 1850 transcription (M432-217, Second District, enumerated 3 September 1850) lists household/family number 290 on page 260A:
- **William Whittle**, 28, farmer, born Kentucky.
- **Darius Whittle**, 33, born Kentucky.
- **Deby Whittle**, 6, born Kentucky.
- **Richard Whittle**, 4, born Kentucky.
- **James C. Whittle**, 1, born Kentucky.

The census transcription does not give relationships, and no wife is listed in this household. The household is consistent with the William who married Dorcas in 1843, but that identity is not yet proven. The 1868 compiled marriage entry for Deborah “Debby” Whittle identifies her parents as William Whittle and Dorcas Luttrell; this supports, but does not prove, that the 6-year-old Deby in the 1850 household is their daughter Deborah. The 1850 census also lists Darius as a 33-year-old male in the same household; his relationship to William is unknown.

Source: USGenWeb Census Project, 1850 Russell County, Kentucky, transcription, M432-217, page 260A, lines 24–28: https://www.us-census.org/pub/usgenweb/census/ky/russell/1850/pg0257a.txt

## Other Whittle households in the same 1850 Russell County census
The same 1850 transcription lists additional Whittle people in other households:
- **Orry Whittle**, 20, in household 254 with Andrew Miller.
- **Susana Whittle**, indexed age 24, with Nancy (17), Ann (11), Robert (8), and Elizabeth (5 months) in household 257, whose head is transcribed as Richard Miller. Susana's reported age is inconsistent with the children's ages and may be a transcription/reading error; the original schedule should be checked.
- **Nancy Whittle**, 44, in household 287, with Brummett children and Richard Garrett.
- **William Whittle**, 28, and Darius Whittle, 33, in household 290 with Deby, Richard and James C.

These entries show several Whittle individuals in Russell County in 1850, but the census does not establish whether they were one extended family or several separate lines. In particular, do not merge Susana, Nancy, Orry, Darius or Robert into the William–Dorcas family without further evidence.

Source: USGenWeb Census Project, Russell County 1850 index and transcription:
- Index, Whittle entries: https://www.us-census.org/pub/usgenweb/census/ky/russell/1850/indx-w-z.txt
- Full page transcription, M432-217: https://www.us-census.org/pub/usgenweb/census/ky/russell/1850/pg0257a.txt

## Deborah “Debby” Whittle — possible child of William and Dorcas
A compiled family-data page for the Polston family states:
- **Deborah “Debby” Whittle**, born November 1843 in Kentucky, died before 1920.
- Parents: **William Whittle and Dorcas Luttrell**.
- Marriage to **William S. Polston** on **9 January 1868** in Russell County; the entry says the bond was signed by “Wm Whitl.”
- It reports that the 1900 census gave the couple seven children, five living, and lists children including Asa C. Wells, James, Michael/Mike O., David Hagard, Sopiah “Suffry,” and Dorcas.

This is a compiled online family tree, not an original marriage or birth record. It is consistent with the 1843 William–Dorcas marriage and the 1850 child “Deby,” but the identity link needs the original 1868 marriage record and later census/death evidence.

Source: Genealogy Data, Polston family page (archived GeoCities): https://www.oocities.org/ebastin.geo/ALLIEDPOLSTON/dat0.htm

## Possible connection to John L. (Jack) Whittle — hypothesis only
A separate family-history account says **John L. (Jack) Whittle (1796–1866)** was born in East Tennessee to **Robert and Susannah Whittle**, came to Kentucky with his parents and siblings (Joseph, Levi, William, Nancy and Sarah/Sally) at about age 12, and grew up near **Jabez Ridge**, south of Somerset. He married Elizabeth Tarter in Pulaski County in 1823 and moved with his family to Edmonson County in 1837.

The 1850 Russell County William Whittle (born about 1822) could be a son of John L. and Elizabeth, because the age fits a child born before the 1837 move and a marriage in 1843. However, the family-history account says John L.'s family remained in Edmonson County, and no source names his six children. This is only a lead; do not attach William to John L. without census, probate, deed or family-bible evidence.

Source: Miller County Museum, “John L. (Jack) Whittle” and Whittle–Keeth families, quoting *Edmonson County, Kentucky Families* (1989): https://www.millercountymuseum.org/bios/bio_w.html
