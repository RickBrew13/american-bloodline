# Avery Ello Whittle — obituary portrait and Kentucky birth-index follow-up
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Visually inspected the family-held 1977 obituary clipping and checked an additional Kentucky-wide birth-index resource for the unresolved Cordelia Tucker parentage lead.

## Avery photograph — second image found within the obituary clipping
The original family-held image `documents/Avery-Ello-Whittle-1977-obituary-clipping-Drive-original.png` was opened and visually inspected. In addition to the obituary text, the clipping contains a small black-and-white halftone photograph of a man standing outdoors beside a building. Because it is printed directly under the heading **“Avery Whittle”** on his own obituary, it is attributable to Avery as a contemporary obituary portrait, although the newspaper reproduction is low-resolution and the original photograph/source is not identified.

This is a second image of Avery in the existing archive, in addition to the larger c.1946 Army portrait already stored in `img/Avery-Ello-Whittle-Army-portrait-Drive-original.jpg`. The newspaper clipping remains the original archival item; it was not duplicated or altered. A separate crop was not made because the clipping reproduction is halftone and the source image is already preserved in full.

## New 1874–1878 Kentucky birth-index resource
The Kentucky Genealogical Society Digital Archive has a public catalog entry for its **Birth Index 1874–1878** workbook. The description states that volunteers compiled the index between 2006 and 2009 and that it covers births registered in 114 Kentucky counties. It also says the underlying images are available on microfilm at the Kentucky Department for Libraries and Archives.

This is a new index route for the 14 May 1878 Cordella/Cordelia Tucker entry already identified in the KDLA Russell County register (roll 994054; Ancestry image KYVR_994054-0061_2). The downloadable workbook link was exposed by the archive, but the binary spreadsheet could not be opened in this research session; **no Cordelia entry or parent names were verified from the KGS index**. The original register image remains the decisive record.

Sources:
- Kentucky Genealogical Society, [Birth Index 1874–1878 catalog entry](https://kygs.omeka.net/items/show/635) — metadata and coverage description.
- Kentucky Department for Libraries and Archives, [County Records Inventory](https://kdla.ky.gov/Archives-and-Reference/Documents/Inventory_County_Records.pdf) — lists Russell County 1878 births.
- The Shy Genealogist, [Russell County birth-register guide](https://theshygenealogist.wordpress.com/births/) — explains that the registers can name child, father, mother's maiden name, residence and remarks.
