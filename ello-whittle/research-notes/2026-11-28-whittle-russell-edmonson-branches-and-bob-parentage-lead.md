# Avery Ello Whittle — Russell and Edmonson County Whittle branches; Bob's parentage lead
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Cross-checked the 1850 Edmonson County and Russell County census transcriptions, later Whittle cemetery/descendant records, and the unresolved identity of R. S. “Bob” Whittle.

## 1. Edmonson County Whittle household, 1850
The USGenWeb transcription of the 1850 Edmonson County population schedule (NARA microfilm M432-198, handwritten pages 2–3) records household/family 8:
- John Whittle, 54, farmer, born Kentucky
- Elizabeth Whittle, 45, born Kentucky
- Cecilia, 21
- James, 19
- Alford, 17
- Mary, 15
- Martin, 13
- Ann E., 12
- Elizabeth, 10
- Henry, 7
- John, 5
- David, 3

A separate Edmonson household/family 26 records:
- Robert Whittle, 25, farmer, born Kentucky
- Amanda Whittle, 23
- Nancy Whittle, 3
- Ira Whittle, 5 months

The local history account for John L. “Jack” Whittle and Elizabeth Tarter (married 1823; associated with the move to Edmonson County) makes the John/Elizabeth household a strong candidate for that couple, and Martin age 13 is a candidate for the Martin Whittle later recorded in Edmonson County. The census itself does not give relationships, however, and the Robert age 25 in household 26 is not yet proven to be a son of John and Elizabeth.

Source: USGenWeb Census Project, [1850 Edmonson County, Kentucky, transcription, M432-198](https://www.us-census.org/pub/usgenweb/census/ky/edmonson/1850/pg0001b.txt). The transcription explicitly identifies the original census roll and pages.

## 2. Separate Whittle households in Russell County, 1850
The 1850 Russell County transcription (M432-217) records several separate Whittle entries:
- “Orry” Whittle, 20, in the Andrew Miller household (family 254).
- Susana Whittle, indexed age 24, with Nancy 17, Ann 11, Robert 8 and Elizabeth 5 months, enumerated in the Richard Miller household (family 257). Susana's recorded age is inconsistent with the ages of the children; it may be a transcription/handwriting problem. The census does not state their relationships.
- Nancy Whittle, 44, in the household/family of the Brummett/Garrett group (family 287).
- William Whittle, 28, and Darius Whittle, 33, with Deby 6, Richard 4 and James C. 1 (family 290). The census does not identify which adult was the children's father or either man's relationship to the other.

Source: USGenWeb Census Project, [1850 Russell County, Kentucky, transcription, M432-217](https://www.us-census.org/pub/usgenweb/census/ky/russell/1850/). The entries are separate households/families and must not be combined merely because they share the surname.

## 3. A later Russell County Richard Whittle — possible match to the 1850 child
A secondary cemetery/descendant compilation identifies **Richard Whittle (1847–1895)** of Russell County, buried at Coleman Cemetery, with wife **Sarah E. Isbell Whittle** and children reported as Edgar, Clancy, Minnie, Herbert, Mary and an infant. His reported birth year is close to the 1850 Russell census child Richard, age 4, in family 290.

This is a plausible identity match, but it is not yet proved. If the match is correct, the 1850 census does not establish whether Richard was the son of William Whittle or Darius Whittle. The marriage and death records for Richard and Sarah should be located before assigning parents.

Source: PeopleLegacy's secondary Russell County Whittle cemetery/descendant aggregation, also summarized in the 28 November Richard Whittle–Sarah Isbell branch note.

## 4. R. S. “Bob” Whittle — unresolved identity and parentage
Cordella Whittle's 22 March 1951 obituary transcription says she married **R. S. Whittle, known as Bob Whittle, on 24 December 1895**, and that he died **1 December 1949**. A secondary cemetery aggregation instead lists **Robert S. “Bob” Whittle, 1875–1948**, in McBeath Cemetery. The full name and year of death therefore remain unresolved until the original death record and headstone/interment record are checked.

If the 1875 birth year is correct, Bob would have been about 20 at the 1895 marriage. Richard Whittle (1847–1895) is a possible father based on chronology and the Russell County Whittle network, but **no record yet connects Bob to Richard or Sarah E. Isbell**. Do not add this parentage.

## 5. Stanton Bell Whittle — possible pre-marital child, not established
Stanton Bell Whittle's reported birth in 1893 predates Bob and Cordella's 24 December 1895 marriage. Cordella's 1951 obituary says six children were born “to this union” and names five surviving children. Stanton is not named. One possible explanation is that Stanton was Bob's child from an earlier relationship, but there is no source identifying Stanton's father or mother, and the sixth child in Cordella's obituary remains unresolved.

If Bob is the son of Richard Whittle and Sarah Isbell, the Richard/Sarah branch could be relevant to Avery's paternal ancestry. This is only a research hypothesis. It must not be entered as a relationship until a birth record, death certificate, census household, probate record or direct family obituary establishes it.

## 6. Records and next steps
1. Obtain the original 1948/1949 death certificate for Robert S. “Bob” Whittle. Kentucky death certificates from 1911–1965 are held by KDLA and the 1911–1967 FamilySearch collection includes index and images; FamilySearch access may require a free account. The certificate should name his parents and informant.
2. Locate the original 1895 Russell County marriage license/bond/consent and minister's return for R. S. Whittle and Cordella Tucker. Cordella was about 17 and Bob about 20 if the reported birth years are correct; consent papers may be informative.
3. Search the 1900 Russell County population schedule, NARA microfilm T623-550, for Bob and Cordella. The 1900 census records month/year of birth, relationship, marital status/years married and children's birth/living counts.
4. Inspect the Russell County 1900 printed transcription by Irma Miller Shepherd, *Twelfth Census of the United States: Schedule No. 1, 1900—Population of Russell County, Kentucky* (420 pages; Family History Library holding), if available.
5. Locate original 1860/1870/1880 census images for the Russell County Whittle households, and the original 1850/1860/1870/1880 census images for the Edmonson John/Elizabeth and Martin households.
6. Compare the original headstone/interment entries for Richard Whittle (1847–1895) and Robert S. “Bob” Whittle to resolve cemetery dates and locations.

**Current conclusion:** Several distinct Russell and Edmonson Whittle groups are now documented in census transcriptions. A possible relationship between Bob, Richard Whittle and Stanton is worth testing, but no new parentage or sibling relationships are proven. No family-tree relationship was changed.

## 7. John Whittle appears as a witness in a 1866 Russell County marriage
A separate contemporary marriage-record transcription in Carol L. Sanders, *Russell County, Kentucky Marriage Records, 1860 Loose Paper Marriages, Book III, 1861–1867* (1985), page 28, records **Paul Carroll and Nancy West** marrying on 14 July 1866 at the home of Barney West in Russell County. The witnesses included **George Carroll, John Whittle and Jasper Brummett**; the bond was signed by Paul Carroll and Jasper Brummett.

This supplies another dated Russell County appearance for a man named John Whittle. He could be the John Whittle in the 1860 Polston-family census extract, but the marriage abstract does not give his age, residence or relationship to either party. It does not establish that he belongs to Avery's direct line. The record is a useful lead to compare against the original loose marriage paper and nearby West, Carroll and Brummett households.

Source: Carol L. Sanders, *Russell County, Kentucky Marriage Records, 1860 Loose Paper Marriages, Book III, 1861–1867* (1985), page 28, scan hosted by The Rainwater Collection: [PDF, page 28](https://www.therainwatercollection.com/reference/ref277.pdf).
