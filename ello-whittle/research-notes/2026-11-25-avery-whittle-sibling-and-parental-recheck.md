## Independent obituary corroboration for three siblings
Three later obituaries provide direct family statements about Stanton and Louvenia's children:

- **Grace (Whittle) Eastham (27 March 1925–20 November 2018):** WHVE obituary identifies her as a daughter of Stanton B. and Louvenia McGowan Whittle. It names her children Gayla Sue Kerr, Treva Meirose, Tina Milliner and Rex Eastham, and identifies one surviving sister as “Veldean Beckner.” She was buried in Whittle Cemetery. Source: WHVE, “Grace Eastham, 93, Russell Springs,” 20 November 2018, https://wvlc.com/whve-obits/archives/2018-11 (entry around lines 854–894).
- **Norma Jean (Whittle) Wilson (17 January 1933–30 January 2017):** Bernard Funeral Home obituary identifies her parents as Stanton B. and Louvienie McGowan Whittle. It names surviving sisters Grace Eastham and “Dean Beckner,” and her four children Carl J., Sheena, Troy and Lyn Wilson. The “Dean Beckner” is likely Veldean Beckner, because Grace's obituary independently names Veldean as her sister, but I am retaining the name-variant inference rather than silently treating it as proved. Source: Bernard Funeral Home, https://www.bernardfuneralhome.com/obituary/Norma-Wilson.
- **Veldean / Veldene (Whittle) Beckner (13 September 1938–15 October 2020):** WHVE obituary identifies her parents as Stanton and Louvenia McGowan Whittle. It says she was preceded in death by four sisters and three brothers, and names her children Kathy Howard, Donna Sibley, Barbara Grider and James Beckner Jr. It does not name her siblings individually. Source: WHVE, “Veldene Beckner, 82, Russell Springs,” October 2020, https://ridingthewave.com/obits/archives/2020-10/.

These independent obituaries corroborate the parental couple and the sibling identities Grace, Norma and Veldean/Veldene. They do not name Avery individually as their brother, but Avery's own 1940 census household and existing 1977 obituary supply the bridge to the same parental family.

## Grandchild records — new direct family link
A new search found the full 2008 *Dallas Morning News* obituary for **Rebecca Ann Whittle** (13 May 1986–27 September 2008). It explicitly names her parents as Jeff and Donna Whittle, her paternal grandmother as Charlotte Whittle, and her grandfather Avery Whittle as already deceased. This directly establishes Rebecca as Avery's granddaughter and connects Jeff to Avery's son Jeffery Whittle in the 2016 Theresa obituary (which names Jeff among Theresa's brothers and identifies Donna as his spouse). The obituary also lists Rebecca's siblings, but I have not added living descendants' names to Avery's public profile.

Theresa Whittle's 2016 obituary separately names her deceased nephew **Matthew Mark Whittle**. This establishes him as a grandchild of Avery and Charlotte through one of Theresa's siblings, but the obituary does not identify which sibling was his parent. Rebecca's obituary mentions a deceased brother named Matthew; this may be the same person as Matthew Mark, but the name-only correspondence is not enough to merge them. Charlotte's 2025 obituary refers to a deceased grandson “Matthew” and a granddaughter “Becca”; these may correspond to Matthew Mark and Rebecca Ann, respectively, but only Rebecca Ann is independently linked by name to Avery in her own obituary.

Charlotte's obituary reports 25 grandchildren, 35 great-grandchildren and five great-great-grandchildren. Only Rebecca Ann and Matthew Mark have been individually identified in the records reviewed here. The profile now lists those two with the evidence limits stated; no other descendant names were added.

## Additional Whittle-cemetery cluster and Stanton birth-year conflict
A broader cemetery search surfaced further Whittle entries in Hugh Frank Cemetery, Russell County:
- **William Anderson Whittle**, reported 1858–1932;
- **E. Joanna Whittle**, reported 1897–1918;
- **Ulissus Hue Whittle**, reported 1928–1929;
- **Gearlind Whittle**, reported 1936–1936.

The first two may be from Stanton's extended family, but cemetery co-location is not proof of a relationship. William Anderson is a possible research lead for Stanton's parentage only; E. Joanna is a possible relative of the same generation, but neither has been connected to Stanton by a census, birth/death certificate, obituary or probate record. Do not add them as father/sister.

A second birth-date discrepancy surfaced for Stanton himself. The cemetery/compiled memorial listing used in the current profile gives **1892–1972**. LocateAncestors' derivative SSDI-style listing gives **10 July 1893–1 October 1972** and last residence Russell Springs. No original Social Security application, death certificate, marker photograph or census image was retrieved to settle this. Keep the profile's current 1892 date flagged as provisional; do not change it to 1893 based only on the derivative directory.
