# Avery Ello Whittle — Waldo Coffey and Pearl Stephens family disambiguation
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Tested whether the Waldo Coffey named on Avery's 1944 draft card could be connected to Avery's sister Iza Pearl Whittle Coffey.

## The 2011 obituary's Pearl is not Avery's sister Iza Pearl
The 2011 obituary for Cleatus Cordell Coffey identifies his parents as **Pearl Stephens Coffey** and **“Bug” Coffey**, and names deceased brothers **Glendo Coffey and Waldo Coffey**. A separate Russell County family-history post by The Shy Genealogist (24 April 2010) identifies **Pearl Stephens**, born February 1891, as a daughter of Andrew J. Stephens and Theodosia W. C. Coffey.

Avery's sister **Iza Pearl (Whittle) Coffey** was born 16 December 1920. She is therefore not the Pearl Stephens Coffey identified as Cleatus's mother, who was born in 1891. The two women are distinct people. This removes the apparent link through a shared Pearl name.

Sources:
- ColumbiaMagazine, “Cleatus Cordell Coffey, Russell Springs, KY (1924–2011),” 16 May 2011: https://columbiamagazine.com/index.php?sid=44382
- The Shy Genealogist, “Surname Saturday — Coffey in Russell Co, KY,” 24 April 2010: https://theshygenealogist.wordpress.com/category/russell-county-kentucky/page/5/
- Avery's sister Iza Pearl Whittle's dates and identity are supported by the family obituaries and the existing sibling research note in this archive.

## What remains of the Waldo lead
Avery's 3 July 1944 draft card lists **“Coffee, Waldo”** as contact person, without a relationship or address. The 2011 obituary and a derivative index identify a Russell County **Waldo L. Coffey (2 October 1917–5 August 1994)**, brother of Cleatus. The matching surname, county and generation make him a possible same-name candidate, but there is now no supported route connecting him to Avery's sister Iza Pearl.

The safest conclusion is:
- The Waldo Coffey in the 2011 obituary is a real Russell County man in the right age range.
- The 1944 draft-card contact may or may not be that man.
- No evidence establishes that Waldo was Avery's brother-in-law, relative or friend.
- Do not add Waldo as Iza Pearl's husband or as a Whittle relative.
