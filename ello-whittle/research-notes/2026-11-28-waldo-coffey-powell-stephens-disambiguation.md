# Avery Ello Whittle — Waldo Coffey and Pearl Stephens family disambiguation
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Tested whether the Waldo Coffey named on Avery's 1944 draft card could be connected to Avery's sister Iza Pearl Whittle Coffey.

## The 2011 obituary's Pearl is not Avery's sister Iza Pearl
The 2011 obituary for Cleatus Cordell Coffey identifies his parents as **Pearl Stephens Coffey** and **“Bug” Coffey**, and names deceased brothers **Glendo Coffey and Waldo Coffey**. A separate Russell County family-history post by The Shy Genealogist (24 April 2010) identifies a **Pearl Stephens**, born February 1891, as a daughter of Andrew J. Stephens and Theodosia W. C. Coffey. That may be the same Pearl Stephens Coffey named in Cleatus's obituary, but the post does not explicitly connect the two women.

Avery's sister **Iza Pearl (Whittle) Coffey** was born 16 December 1920. Cleatus was born 2 March 1924, so Iza Pearl would have been only three years old at his birth and cannot be his mother. Therefore, Cleatus's mother Pearl Stephens Coffey is not Avery's sister Iza Pearl. The 1891 Pearl Stephens in the family-history post is a plausible candidate for Cleatus's mother, but that identity also remains to be proved.

Sources:
- ColumbiaMagazine, “Cleatus Cordell Coffey, Russell Springs, KY (1924–2011),” 16 May 2011: https://columbiamagazine.com/index.php?sid=44382
- The Shy Genealogist, “Surname Saturday — Coffey in Russell Co, KY,” 24 April 2010: https://theshygenealogist.wordpress.com/category/russell-county-kentucky/page/5/
- Avery's sister Iza Pearl Whittle's dates and identity are supported by the family obituaries and the existing sibling research note in this archive.

## What remains of the Waldo lead
Avery's 3 July 1944 draft card lists **“Coffee, Waldo”** as contact person, without a relationship or address. The 2011 obituary and a derivative index identify a Russell County **Waldo L. Coffey (2 October 1917–5 August 1994)**, brother of Cleatus. The matching surname, county and generation make him a possible same-name candidate for Avery's draft-card contact, but there is no supported route connecting him to Avery's sister Iza Pearl.

The safest conclusion is:
- The Waldo Coffey in the 2011 obituary is a real Russell County man in the right age range.
- The 1944 draft-card contact may or may not be that man.
- No evidence establishes that Waldo was Avery's brother-in-law, relative or friend.
- Do not add Waldo as Iza Pearl's husband or as a Whittle relative.
