Whittle and Eunice Brewster, their July 23, 1947 marriage, both residences as Covington, Kentucky (Eunice's entry is ditto), each as single, his occupation as hotel work, and their respective parents Stanton/Lavina Whittle and Homer/Sarah Brewster. Charlotte’s 2025 obituary independently describes their marriage/family. No child with Eunice is established in this pass; her 1950 census with Avery lists only the two adults.
