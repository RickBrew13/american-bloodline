# Avery Ello Whittle — Robert and Matilda Rexroat Whittle family lead
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed the Russell County Whittle family named in a 1936 marriage record to identify the parents of R. S. “Bob” Whittle and possible siblings.

## 1936 marriage record — direct parental statement for Harria Whittle
A transcription of the Russell County marriage register gives:
- Groom: **Harria Whittle**, age 47, widowed, born in Russell County, living at Eli, Russell County.
- Parents: **Robert and Matilda Rexroat Whittle**.
- Bride: **Lela Rainwater**, age 23, single, born in Casey County, living at Windsor, Casey County.
- Marriage: **30 December 1936**, Russell County Marriage Book 35, page 71.

This is the clearest source located so far for a Robert Whittle and Matilda Rexroat Whittle family in Russell County. The original marriage certificate/register image has not yet been inspected; the entry is transcribed by the Rainwater Collection researcher from Russell County marriage records.

Source: Rainwater Collection, Russell County marriage records, 30 December 1936 Harria Whittle–Lela Rainwater entry: https://www.therainwatercollection.com/marriage.shtml

## Possible connection to R. S. “Bob” Whittle — hypothesis only
Avery's possible grandmother **Cordella Tucker** married **R. S. “Bob” Whittle** on 24 December 1895. A secondary cemetery aggregation lists Robert S. “Bob” Whittle as born about 1875 and died in 1948, while Cordella's 1951 newspaper obituary gives Bob's death as 1 December 1949. The age and dates make it possible that Bob was another child of Robert and Matilda, alongside Harria, but no record found in this pass identifies Bob's parents.

Do not add Robert and Matilda as Bob's parents or connect Harria as Bob's brother until the 1880/1900 census, Bob's death record, probate, or another direct record confirms the relationship.

## Related Whittle marriage lead — B. H. Whittle
A separate transcription of a 18 July 1912 Pulaski County marriage register records:
- **B. H. Whittle**, age 22, first marriage, living in Pulaski County; he and his parents were born in Russell County.
- Bride **Louisa Rainwater**, age 18, born in Pulaski County; her father was born in Indiana and mother in Tennessee.
- Marriage Book 42, pages 448–449; the transcription notes that the certificate spells the groom's surname **White**, although the index/transcription heading gives Whittle.

B. H. Whittle may belong to the same wider Russell County Whittle family, but no parents' names or connection to Bob/Harria are given. The spelling difference may be a clerk or transcription issue and needs the original image.

Source: Rainwater Collection, Pulaski County marriage records, B. H. Whittle–Louisa Rainwater entry, 18 July 1912: https://www.therainwatercollection.com/marriage.shtml
