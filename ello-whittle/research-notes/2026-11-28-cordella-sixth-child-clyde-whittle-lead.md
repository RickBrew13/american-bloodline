# Avery Ello Whittle — missing sixth child in Cordella Whittle's obituary: Clyde Whittle lead
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Investigated the unexplained statement in Cordella Whittle's 1951 obituary that six children were born to her marriage, while only five are named.

## The obituary discrepancy
The 22 March 1951 *Russell County News* obituary transcription states that Cordella Tucker Whittle and R. S. “Bob” Whittle had **six children**, but lists only:
1. Otha Whittle
2. Rese Whittle
3. Jim Elmer Whittle
4. Caleb Whittle
5. Grady Whittle

The missing sixth name may be absent from the transcription, omitted in the original, or refer to a child who died young or was otherwise not listed. The original newspaper issue is accessible through the Russell County Digital Archives, but the image has not yet been successfully opened in this session:

## Two possible Russell County men named Clyde Whittle
A local newspaper and later veteran/cemetery index produce two distinct possible Clyde identities. Neither has yet been linked to Cordella and Bob.

### Clyde O. Whittle (27 November 1921–6 December 2007)
- A secondary LocateAncestors index gives these dates and a last residence in Russell Springs, ZIP 42642.
- A Kentucky veterans cemetery transcription lists **Clyde O. Whittle**, 27 November 1921–6 December 2007, buried Russell Springs City Cemetery, and identifies him as a U.S. Army sergeant and World War II veteran.
- The 1968 *Lebanon Enterprise* article about the robbery of Whittle's Super Market in Russell Springs identifies the store owner only as **Clyde Whittle**. It does not supply a middle initial or age, so it cannot yet be matched to Clyde O.
- If Clyde O. was the missing sixth child, he would have been born when Cordella was 43. That is biologically possible but currently unsupported.

Sources:

- Interment.net, Kentucky veterans buried in miscellaneous cemeteries, Clyde O. Whittle entry: https://www.interment.net/united-states/kentucky/veterans-burials/transcription/630.php?page=616

### Clyde Whittle (23 December 1919–25 August 2001)
- The same derivative LocateAncestors index separately lists a **Clyde Whittle**, born 23 December 1919 and died 25 August 2001, last residence Russell Springs, ZIP 42642.
- This is a separate indexed identity from Clyde O. Whittle (1921–2007); the different dates must not be merged.
- No obituary, parentage, spouse or child record was located for this Clyde in this pass.

Source: LocateAncestors, Kentucky Whittle index above.

## Why this matters to Avery's line
If one of these Clydes proves to be the unlisted sixth child of Bob and Cordella, he would be part of the same Whittle/Tucker family branch under investigation for Stanton Bell Whittle. This could help clarify whether Cordella had a child before her 1895 marriage (possibly Stanton) and a separate group of six children with Bob. But it does **not** prove that Stanton was Cordella's son, or that either Clyde was her child.

The five named children in the obituary include **Otha Whittle**, whose later family branch is documented in Russell County obituaries. Otha's son Arlus “Johnny” Whittle (1925–2010) is explicitly identified as a son of Otha Whittle and Mae Meadow(s) Whittle. The 2022 obituary for Johnny's sister Lana Kay Hale identifies the same parents and names five sisters and one brother, Johnny; it does not name Clyde. This is a caution against attaching Clyde to Otha's household without direct proof. Clyde could be another child of Bob and Cordella, another Whittle relative, or an unrelated same-surname man.

Sources:

- Lana Kay Hale obituary, WVLC, July 2022: https://wvlc.com/whve-obits/archives/2022-07

## Census geography resource — Eli / Whittle settlement and enumeration maps
A Russell County map guide identifies **Whittle** as a historic populated place in the **Eli census division**, on the **Eli quadrangle**, near Ono Ridge. This is a geographic place-name and should not be treated as proof that any specific Whittle family owned the land. The 1950 map guide places Eli-area households across EDs 104-12 through 104-14; the precise district for Bob and Cordella still needs to be located.
