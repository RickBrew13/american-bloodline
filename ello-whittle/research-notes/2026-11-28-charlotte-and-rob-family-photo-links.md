# Avery Ello Whittle — family photo links: Charlotte and Rob
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Located direct external photo links for Avery's wife Charlotte and son Rob. Both are preserved as links only because the images are hosted by third parties and their reproduction rights have not been cleared.

## Charlotte Ann (Mulvey) Whittle — obituary profile photo
Charlotte's 2025 obituary at Charles W. Smith & Sons Funeral Home contains a profile-photo link. The image URL is:
https://cdn.tukioswebsites.com/a6f0944d-cffa-4ff4-94dd-bf232174ec03/lg

The funeral-home page identifies Charlotte as Avery Ello Whittle's wife and says they raised eight children in New Castle, Indiana. The profile-photo URL returned a cache miss in this research session, but the exact link was exposed by the funeral-home page. The obituary page remains the stable reference:
https://www.charleswsmithandsonsfuneralhome.com/obituaries/charlotte-whittle

Do not copy/rehost the funeral-home image without permission.

## Rob Whittle — 2018 groundbreaking photograph
Spot On Images' 26 January 2018 article, “Heath Golf & Yacht Club,” contains a photograph captioned **“Rob Whittle Celebrates Groundbreaking.”** The article identifies Rob as the developer of Heath Golf & Yacht Club and describes his work in Rockwall County. Theresa Whittle's 2016 obituary identifies Rob as Avery and Charlotte's son, so this is a family photograph of Avery's son, not Avery.
