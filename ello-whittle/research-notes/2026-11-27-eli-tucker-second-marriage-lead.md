# Avery Ello Whittle — possible Eli Tucker second marriage / half-sibling lead
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed a compiled family-tree entry that may concern the Eli Tucker identified as Cordelia E. Tucker Whittle's father.

## New compiled-tree lead
MyTrees' McQueary surname page contains a family preview:
- **Eli Tucker**, born 1849 in Kentucky.
- **Angeletta McQueary**, born 1880 in Kentucky.
- Reported marriage: **1909** (county not displayed in the preview).
- One listed child: **Ada P. Tucker**, born 1915 in Kentucky.
- Submitter/tree identifier: **jo003994**.

This is chronologically possible for the Eli Tucker who was married to Narcissus “Arsis” Popplewell (reported death 1908) and whose death is given inconsistently as 1915 or 1919 in the 1998 family query. If it is the same Eli, Angeletta and Ada would be a second-wife/half-sibling branch for Cordelia E. Tucker Whittle. But the MyTrees preview is a compiled family tree with no source citation visible, and it gives no county for the marriage or Ada's birth.

Source: MyTrees, McQueary surname family preview, entry “Eli Tucker & Angeletta McQueary”: https://www.mytrees.com/names/Event/Year/1/MCQUEARY

## Important possible same-name conflict
A separate Russell County record cluster identifies **Ada West Tucker (1915–1985)**, buried at Hugh Frank Cemetery. A 2025 obituary for her daughter Clora Jane Luttrell says Clora was born in 1946 to **Earl and Ada (West) Tucker** in Russell County. This Ada's maiden name is West, so she is not automatically the same person as the MyTrees child “Ada P. Tucker.” The similar birth year and Tucker surname are not enough to merge them.

## Verification needed before adding a second marriage or half-sibling
1. Locate the original 1909 Kentucky marriage record for Eli Tucker and Angeletta McQueary; identify county, ages, residences and marital status.
2. Search the 1910 Kentucky Miracode index, NARA microfilm **T1266, roll 117** (T-000 through T-400 Green, which includes Tucker code **T260**), for Eli Tucker/Angeletta. The index should supply the 1910 census sheet and line; then inspect Russell County population schedules, NARA roll **T624-501**, if the card identifies Russell County. Compare age, birthplace, county and household.
3. Locate Ada P. Tucker's 1915 Kentucky birth record and determine her mother's maiden name and county.
4. Find Ada's later marriage, death certificate, obituary or cemetery record to determine whether she is Ada West Tucker or a different Ada P. Tucker.
5. Reconcile Eli's death date with the 1915 birth and the reported 1915/1919 death dates.

**Current status:** Possible second marriage only. Do not attach Angeletta or Ada to the Tucker family group, and do not add a half-sibling to Cordelia, until primary or independently corroborating records establish that this is the same Eli.
