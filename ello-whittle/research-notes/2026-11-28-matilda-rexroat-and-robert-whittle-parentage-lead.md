# Avery Ello Whittle — Matilda Rexroat and Robert Whittle parentage lead
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed the parents named for Harria Whittle on his 1936 marriage record and tested whether they can be identified in the 1850 Russell County census.

## 1. Harria Whittle's 1936 marriage record names his parents
A transcribed Russell/Casey County marriage record states:
- **Harria [sic] Whittle**, age 47, widowed, born Russell County, living in Eli, Russell County.
- Parents: **Robert and Matilda Rexroat Whittle**.
- Married **Lela Rainwater** on 30 December 1936 in Casey County, Kentucky.
- Original marriage book reference: Book 35, page 71.

This is the strongest direct evidence currently located for the names of Harria's parents. It is a derivative transcription of the marriage register, not the original certificate image.

Source: The Rainwater Collection, [Russell/Casey County marriage transcription](https://www.therainwatercollection.com/marriage.shtml), entry for Lela Rainwater and Harria Whittle, 30 Dec 1936.

## 2. A possible Matilda F. Rexroat in the 1850 Russell County census
The 1850 Russell County census transcription, NARA M432-217, page 265B, family 272, records:
- **George Rexroat**, 46, born Pennsylvania.
- **Catharine Rexroat**, 40, born Kentucky.
- Children/household members: Mary J. 18, John A. 17, Sarah E. 13, Jeremiah F. 9, Eliza A. 6, **Matilda F. 2**, and Riley W. 1 month.
- **Adam Rexroat**, 79, born Pennsylvania, is also in the same family/household.

The 1850 census does not explicitly state the relationships, but George and Catharine's marriage is independently listed in the Russell County marriage certificate abstract:
- **George Rexroat × Catharine Cumpton**, 28 September 1828, officiant Thornton Shepherd (printed page 88; original marriage book page 5).

The age and surname make Matilda F. Rexroat (born about 1848) a plausible match for the **Matilda Rexroat** named as Harria's mother in 1936. If they are the same woman, George Rexroat and Catharine Cumpton are likely her parents. This identification is not proven because the 1936 record does not give Matilda's age or middle initial and no marriage record for Robert Whittle and Matilda Rexroat has yet been located.

Sources:
- USGenWeb Census Project, [1850 Russell County census transcription](https://www.us-census.org/pub/usgenweb/census/ky/russell/1850/), page 265B, family 272.
- Kentucky Ancestors, *Russell Co., KY Marriage Certificates, Book 1, 1826–1954*, [George Rexroat and Catharine Cumpton, 28 Sep 1828](https://mail.kygenweb.net/russell/records/files/ref321-marriages-ky-ancesetors.pdf), printed page 88.

## 3. Adam Rexroat — possible older generation
The 1850 census places Adam Rexroat, age 79 and born Pennsylvania, in the same household as George and Catharine. This makes him a plausible father or close older relative of George, but the census does not state the relationship.

Independent Rexroat-family context:
- An Adair County marriage index records **Micajah Compton × Margaret Rexroat**, 22 February 1820, and identifies Margaret as a daughter of **Adam**. This confirms an Adam Rexroat family in the region, but does not prove that he is the same Adam in George's 1850 household.
- The 1885 *History of McDonough County, Illinois* says Peter Rexroat was born in Pennsylvania in 1802, that his parents moved to Kentucky in 1809, and that Peter's family later moved to Illinois and Iowa. This supports an early Pennsylvania-to-Kentucky Rexroat migration.
- A Geneanet family tree identifies George Washington Rexroat (1803–1886) as a son of Johann Adam Rexroat (reported 1770–1859) and Sarah “Sally” McCord (reported 1780–1815), with Peter Rexroat (1802–1877) and other siblings. This is a compiled tree, not primary proof.
- FamilySearch's Johann Zacharias Rexroth profile lists two separate sons, **Adam Rexroat (1765–1850)** and **Johann Adam Rexroat (1772–1856)**, with Anna Margaret Weiss. The 1850 Adam age 79 would fit a birth about 1771, closer to the second profile, but the conflicting Adam identities and dates must be reconciled before assigning George's father.

Sources:
- Adair County, Kentucky, [Compton marriage index](https://mail.kygenweb.net/adair/wed38.htm), entry for Micajah Compton and Margaret Rexroat, 22 Feb 1820.
- *History of McDonough County, Illinois* (1885), [Rexroat biographies](https://ldsgenealogy.com/IL/books/History-of-McDonough-County-Illinois-together-with-sketches-of-the-towns-villages-and-townships-portraits-of-prominent-individuals-and-biographies-of-the-representative-citizens-part-81.htm).
- Geneanet, [George Washington Rexroat family tree](https://gw.geneanet.org/tdowling?lang=en&n=rexroat&p=george+washington) — compiled.
- FamilySearch, [Johann Zacharias Rexroth (1744–1791)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/L6L2-QXM/johann-zacharias-rexroth-1744-1791) — community tree with multiple Adam entries.

## 4. A possible Robert Whittle in the same 1850 Russell County census
The 1850 Russell County transcription also records **Robert Whittle, age 8**, in a household with Susana Whittle (transcribed age 24), Nancy 17, Ann 11 and Elizabeth 5 months. Susana's age is inconsistent with the ages of the children and may be a transcription/handwriting error. The census does not state relationships.

If this Robert is the same Robert named as Harria's father in 1936, he would have been born about 1842. That is chronologically possible, but there is no direct record connecting the two. The 1850 Russell census entry must not be treated as proof that this child later married Matilda F. Rexroat.

## 5. Possible relationship to R. S. “Bob” Whittle and Stanton
If Robert and Matilda were the parents of Harria, they may also be candidates for the parents of R. S. “Bob” Whittle (reported as Robert S. Whittle, 1875–1948/1949). Bob and Harria would then be possible brothers. This is not proven by any source currently located.

Stanton Bell Whittle's reported birth in 1893 predates Bob's 1895 marriage to Cordella Tucker. If Bob were Stanton's father, Stanton could have been born from a prior relationship, but no record identifies Stanton's parents. Do not attach Bob, Harria or Stanton to the Robert/Matilda couple until a census, birth/death record, probate record or direct obituary proves the relationship.

## 6. Records needed
1. Original 1936 Casey County marriage license/application for Harria Whittle and Lela Rainwater (Book 35, page 71).
2. Robert Whittle and Matilda Rexroat marriage record, likely in Russell or an adjacent county; search county marriage books and loose papers.
3. Original 1850 census image for Russell County, page 265B, family 272, to verify the Matilda F. entry and Adam's age.
4. Original 1850 census image for Russell County, page 257B, family 257, to verify the Robert age 8 and Susana's age.
5. 1870/1880/1900 census households for Robert and Matilda; the 1900 Russell County transcription by Irma Miller Shepherd is a possible offline route.
6. Adam Rexroat probate/estate and the 1820 Adair County marriage bond for Margaret Rexroat to establish the older Rexroat family.

**Current conclusion:** Matilda F. Rexroat (age 2 in 1850) is a strong candidate for the Matilda Rexroat named as Harria's mother in 1936. George Rexroat and Catharine Cumpton are probable parents if that identity match is correct. Adam Rexroat is a possible older ancestor of George. Robert Whittle (age 8 in 1850) is a possible match for Harria's father. These remain leads, not proven relationships. No family-tree relationship was changed.
