# Avery Ello Whittle — Russell Springs Whittle's Super Market location photographs
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Located two University of Louisville Photographic Archives images of the Big Boiling Springs / Russell Springs resort grounds, then compared them with the 1974 local-history photograph caption identifying the later Whittle's Super Market site.

## 1. University of Louisville photograph: building at Russell Springs
The Warren Kellar Frederick Collection includes **ULPA 1980_029_874b, “Building at Russell Springs.”** Its catalog describes a front view of a long, single-story light-colored building with two cars parked in front. The cataloger says it is *possibly* located on the grounds of the Big Boiling Springs health spa in Russell County; the original date is unknown (“19--?”). The catalog does not identify the building as Whittle's Super Market.

This is a possible photograph of a building at the resort site, but it must not be captioned as Whittle's Super Market without a location match or further confirmation.

Source: University of Louisville Photographic Archives, Warren Kellar Frederick Collection, [ULPA 1980_029_874b — Building at Russell Springs](https://digital.library.louisville.edu/concern/images/ulpa_1980_029_874b?locale=en). ## 2. University of Louisville photograph: people at Russell Springs
The same collection includes **ULPA 1980_029_874a, “Russell Springs, in Russell Co. Ky.”** The catalog describes two women and two men standing beneath a gazebo, with a single-story building nearby. The scene is possibly on the Big Boiling Springs health-spa grounds. The people are **unidentified** and the catalog does not connect them to the Whittle family.

This may be useful as a local-history image, but it is not evidence of a Whittle family photograph.

Source: University of Louisville Photographic Archives, Warren Kellar Frederick Collection, [ULPA 1980_029_874a — Russell Springs](https://digital.library.louisville.edu/concern/images/ulpa_1980_029_874a?locale=en). Rights statement: InC-NC.

## 3. Connection to Whittle's Super Market
- A 14 March 1968 *Lebanon Enterprise* report identifies **Clyde Whittle** as the owner of Whittle's Super Market in Russell Springs during an armed robbery. It does not give his age or middle initial, so it cannot distinguish Clyde Whittle (1919–2001) from Clyde O. Whittle (1921–2007).
- The 4 July 1974 *Times Journal* Russell County Bicentennial edition describes the former Russell Springs Hotel at the corner of Jamestown and Main Streets and says that the site was where Whittle's Super Market then stood. The edition includes historical photographs of the resort grounds and hotel, not a confirmed photograph of Clyde or the store.
- The hotel burned in 1942. The University of Louisville building photograph is undated and may or may not depict the later store building.

Sources:
- *The Lebanon Enterprise*, 14 March 1968, [“Gunman Robs Russell County Supermarket”](https://kdl.kyvl.org/digital/api/collection/marion-news/id/28593/download).
- *The Times Journal*, 4 July 1974, [Russell County Bicentennial edition](https://mail.kygenweb.net/russell/area/book-ky-russell-1974.pdf), pp. 8–9.

## Photo handling decision
The two University of Louisville images are discovery links only. The catalog marks them InC-NC; no files were copied into GitHub. The 1974 newspaper images are linked to the complete issue rather than extracted or rehosted. The family archive continues to hold Avery's original Army portrait and the 1977 obituary clipping, which itself contains a small halftone portrait of Avery.

No photo of Clyde Whittle, the supermarket itself, Stanton Bell Whittle or Louvenia McGowan Whittle was identified in this search.
