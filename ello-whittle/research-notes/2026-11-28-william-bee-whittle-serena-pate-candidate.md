# Avery Ello Whittle — William “Bee” Whittle and Serena Pate candidate
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Tested whether the William Whittle in the 1850 Russell County census could be William “Bee”/William W. Whittle, later associated with Serena Pate and their son Sidney D. Whittle.

## 1. 1850 Russell County census candidate
The 1850 Russell County, Kentucky transcription (NARA M432-217, family 290) records:
- William Whittle, 28, male, farmer, born Kentucky.
- Darius Whittle, 33, male, born Kentucky.
- Deby Whittle, 6, female, born Kentucky.
- Richard Whittle, 4, male, born Kentucky.
- James C. Whittle, 1, male, born Kentucky.

The census does not identify relationships. The two adult men may be relatives, but it is not possible to determine which one was the children's father from this schedule alone. The age of William implies birth around 1821–1822.

Source: USGenWeb Census Project, [1850 Russell County, Kentucky, transcription](https://www.us-census.org/pub/usgenweb/census/ky/russell/1850/). The Polston family transcription independently reproduces the same family group as household #230: [Polston/Whittle census extract](https://www.oocities.org/ebastin.geo/censusrussell.htm).

## 2. Later compiled profile for William “Bee” Whittle and Serena Pate
An Ancestry compiled profile for **USA Grant Sidney (Sidney D.) Whittle (20 February 1875–24 January 1941)** gives his parents as:
- **William Bee Whittle**, 1822–1907.
- **Serena Pate**, 27 November 1837–5 December 1927.

A WikiTree orphan profile for **William W. Whittle** gives 28 October 1821, Grainger County, Tennessee, to 19 September 1902, Russell County, Kentucky, and names Serena Pate as his spouse. The two compiled sources agree on a William Whittle born about 1821–1822 and a wife Serena Pate, but they conflict on his death year (1902 versus 1907). Neither page cites a primary record that establishes this William's identity or parentage.

Sources:
- Ancestry, [USA Grant Sidney or Sidney D. Whittle](https://www.ancestry.ca/genealogy/records/usa-grant-sidney-or-sidney-d-whittle-24-qnctz) — user-tree compilation.
- WikiTree, [William W. Whittle (Whittle-403)](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Whittle-403) — orphan profile without a primary-source citation visible in the retrieved page.
- WikiTree, [orphan-profile suggestion listing](https://plus.wikitree.com/Profiles/Suggestions/Challenge_410_Orphans/676_1800-1899_0.htm).

## 3. Possible relationship to the 1850 family
The birth year for William Bee/William W. Whittle fits the 1850 Russell County William Whittle, age 28. If they are the same man, the 1850 household may be his earlier family group before a later marriage to Serena Pate. The children Deby, Richard and James C. could belong to him or to Darius; the census does not resolve this.

A secondary Russell County descendant/cemetery compilation identifies Richard Whittle (1847–1895), husband of Sarah E. Isbell, with children including Edgar, Clancy, Minnie, Herbert and Mary. His birth year is close to the Richard age 4 in the 1850 family 290. This is a plausible match, but the 1850 census does not identify his parents.

The 1843 Russell County marriage entry for William Whittle and Dorcas Litrell and the 1846 entry for William Whittle and Milly Stephens are additional leads. They may concern two different men, or successive marriages; the original marriage documents have not been examined. No marriage to Serena Pate has yet been located.

## 4. Why this is not yet Avery's proven line
No record currently connects William Bee/William W. Whittle to:
- Richard Whittle (1847–1895);
- R. S. “Bob” Whittle (reported as Robert S. Whittle, 1875–1948/1949);
- Stanton Bell Whittle (1893–1972); or
- Avery Ello Whittle.

The William/Serena family may be part of the broader Russell County Whittle network, but the direct path to Avery is not established. Do not attach William or Serena as Avery's ancestors, or Richard/Bob as siblings/descendants, until a census, marriage, probate or death record proves the relationships.

**Current conclusion:** William Bee/William W. Whittle is a plausible identity for the 1850 Russell County William, but the family links remain unproven. No family-tree relationship was changed.

## Russell County William Whittle marriages — exact abstract entries
The 1967 *Kentucky Ancestors* continuation of the Russell County marriage-certificate abstract lists two separate entries:
- **William Whittle × Dorcus Litrell**, married **19 February 1843**, officiant **J. Bowlin, J.P.**
- **William Whittle × Milly Stephens**, married **18 November 1846**, officiant **J. Bowlin, J.P.**

The two entries may represent successive marriages of one man or two different men named William Whittle. The 1850 Russell census family 290 has a William age 28 and Darius age 33 with three children, but no wife. The dates of the marriages and children's approximate birth years make it tempting to connect these entries to that household; the census does not identify which man was the father, and no original bond/license has yet been examined. Do not assign either woman as the mother of Deby, Richard or James C.

Source: *Kentucky Ancestors*, Volume 2, Number 4 (April 1967), “Russell County, Kentucky Marriage Certificates,” continuation, [digital scan](https://www.kyhistory.com/digital/api/collection/p16602coll3/id/85/download), printed page 153.
