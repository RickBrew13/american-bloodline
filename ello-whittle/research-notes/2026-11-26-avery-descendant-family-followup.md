# Avery Ello Whittle — descendant and photograph follow-up
**Research date:** 26 November 2026  
**Scope:** Rechecked Avery's existing GitHub archive and profile, then followed the 2008 obituary of granddaughter Rebecca Ann Whittle and cross-checked it against Theresa Whittle's 2016 obituary and Charlotte Whittle's 2025 obituary.

## New descendant evidence: Jeff and Donna Whittle's family
The 30 September 2008 *Dallas Morning News* obituary for **Rebecca Ann Whittle** states she was born 13 May 1986 to **Jeff and Donna Whittle**. It names four brothers—Daniel, David, Joshua and Mark—and three sisters—Leah, Jordan and Abbey. It identifies Charlotte Whittle as her paternal grandmother and Avery Whittle as her deceased grandfather. This directly places Rebecca and her seven named siblings in Avery's descendant line through his son Jeff. The obituary says Rebecca had one brother named Matthew; it does not give his full name.

Theresa Lynn Whittle's 2016 obituary independently identifies Matthew Mark Whittle as her nephew and Rebecca Ann Whittle as her niece. These two records make it plausible that Matthew Mark is the Matthew mentioned as Rebecca's brother, but neither source explicitly supplies both names in one record. Retain the identity link as a well-supported working hypothesis, not a proven full-name match.

Charlotte's 2025 obituary reports 25 grandchildren, 35 great-grandchildren and five great-great-grandchildren across the family, but it does not enumerate them. The eight children named in the 1977 clipping and later obituaries remain the children of Avery and Charlotte; no children are assigned to Avery's 1947 marriage to Eunice.

## 26 November 2026 — reconcile the full eight-child family and the Matthew/Rebecca branch

### Theresa's 2016 obituary directly identifies Avery and Charlotte's children
The original Charles W. Smith & Sons obituary for **Theresa Lynn Whittle** (27 June 1965–29 November 2016) names Avery Ello Whittle as her father and Charlotte Ann (Mulvey) Whittle as her mother. It states that Theresa was preceded in death by her father Avery, younger sister **Rebecca Jo Whittle**, nephew **Matthew Mark Whittle**, and niece **Rebecca Ann Whittle**. It names her six surviving siblings as **Rob Whittle, Debra Ray, Jeff Whittle, Mike Whittle, Cindy Matheny and Louretta Moukhtar**. It also names four in-laws: **Buck Ray, Donna Whittle, Debi Whittle and Mohamed Moukhtar**.

This confirms the 1977 obituary's children under later married names:
- Debra Smith → **Debra Ray** (husband/in-law Buck Ray)
- Cynthia Whittle → **Cindy Matheny** (spouse not named in Theresa's notice)
- Loretta Whittle → **Louretta Moukhtar** (husband/in-law Mohamed Moukhtar)
- Teresa Whittle → **Theresa Lynn Whittle** (1965–2016)
- Robert Whittle → **Rob Whittle**
- Jeffery Whittle → **Jeff Whittle** (wife/in-law Donna Whittle)
- Michael Whittle → **Mike Whittle** (wife/in-law Debi Whittle)
- **Rebecca Jo Whittle** (died at six months; called “Becky” in Charlotte's 2025 obituary)

The 2016 obituary confirms that Avery and Charlotte had eight children in total: the six named surviving siblings plus Theresa and the deceased infant Rebecca Jo. The 1977 obituary's seven surviving children plus one unnamed granddaughter is consistent with this family structure.

### Charlotte's 2025 obituary confirms Becky, Matthew and Becca
Charlotte Ann Whittle's funeral-home obituary (26 February 1936–27 May 2025) says Avery and Charlotte built a home in New Castle, Indiana, for their eight children; she lost her youngest child Becky at six months; and she was preceded in death by husband Avery, baby girl Becky, grandson **Matthew**, granddaughter **Becca**, and great-granddaughter Kaylee. It records 25 grandchildren, 35 great-grandchildren and five great-great-grandchildren, without enumerating them.

The 2008 obituary for **Rebecca Ann “Becca” Whittle** (13 May 1986–27 September 2008) identifies her parents as Jeff and Donna Whittle and names Charlotte as paternal grandmother and Avery as deceased paternal grandfather. It names four living brothers (Daniel, David, Joshua and Mark) and three living sisters (Leah, Jordan and Abbey); it separately mentions one brother named Matthew who had died before her. Theresa's 2016 obituary names **Matthew Mark Whittle** as her nephew and Rebecca Ann as her niece, while Charlotte's 2025 obituary names a grandson Matthew and granddaughter Becca. This makes **Matthew Mark Whittle** a strong working match for the deceased brother Matthew in Rebecca's 2008 obituary and the grandson Matthew in Charlotte's 2025 obituary, but the sources do not explicitly connect all three references in a single record.

Thus Jeff and Donna's branch appears to include Rebecca Ann, Matthew Mark and the seven other siblings named in Rebecca's obituary: at least nine children in total. Do not treat Matthew Mark as proven identical to the unnamed Matthew until a birth/obituary/parent record makes the link explicit.

### Theresa's birth location and Charlotte's family story
Theresa was born in **Tucson, Arizona**, on 27 June 1965, although the family was associated with New Castle, Indiana. Her obituary says she grew up in New Castle until age 14, then moved with her family to Texas. Charlotte's 2025 obituary says she later followed her children to North Texas. These are useful residence/migration anchors; no explanation for Theresa's Tucson birth is inferred.

## Avery and Charlotte marriage — still not located
The 2016 and 2025 obituaries establish the couple and their family but do not state their marriage date or county. Avery was still married to Eunice in the 1950 census; by the birth of their eldest known children and the 1977 obituary, he and Charlotte were a family. The original marriage license and the end of the Avery–Eunice marriage remain unlocated. Indiana marriage records before 1958 are held at county level; the New Castle Henry County Clerk and local library's Courier-Times index/microfilm are priority routes. The date should not be inferred from the children's birth years.
