# Avery Ello Whittle — Louretta Moukhtar and Rob Whittle Texas follow-up
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed the in-law names in Theresa Whittle's 2016 obituary and checked public Texas marriage and newspaper records for Avery's children.

## Louretta Whittle Moukhtar — marriage index
A Texas marriage-index transcription for 2004 lists:
- **Mohamed H. Moukhtar**, age 50
- **Louretta J. Whittle**, age 42
- **Marriage date:** 13 April 2004
- **County:** Rockwall County, Texas
- **Index numbers:** 017369 / 017370 (the two duplicate index lines)

This supports the identity of the “Mohamed Moukhtar” named as Louretta's in-law in Theresa's 2016 obituary and supplies the indexed marriage date and county. The source is a derivative transcription of a Texas state marriage index, not the original license/certificate. The original Rockwall County marriage record should still be obtained if the exact legal name spelling, officiant and application details are needed.

Sources:

## Rob Whittle and Louretta in Rockwall County — 2011 newspaper
The 25 February 2011 *Rockwall County News* issue includes a feature on Hidden Creek Estates in Royse City:
- It identifies **Louretta Moukhtar** as the development's manager.
- It identifies **Rob Whittle** as the developer of Hidden Creek Estates.
- Rob is quoted discussing the development, FEMA-related creek preservation, neighborhood design and family use of the outdoor space.
- The article gives the local business context for the two siblings' later Texas lives. It does not explicitly state their kinship; that relationship comes from Theresa's 2016 obituary, which names both as Avery and Charlotte's children.
- The issue includes photographs of homes/development, not an identifiable portrait of Rob or Louretta.

Source:
