# Avery Ello Whittle — Stanton and Louvenia cemetery cross-check
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Checked burial evidence for Avery's parents and the Whittle family cemetery context.

## Secondary cemetery index
A PeopleLegacy aggregation for **Whittle Cemetery, Russell County, Kentucky** lists:
- **Stanton Bell Whittle:** 1892–1972.
- **Louvenia M. McGowan Whittle:** 1900–1940.
- **Avery Ello Whittle:** 1927–1977.
- **Charlotte Ann Whittle:** born 1936.

This places Avery, his parents and his wife in the same family cemetery. It independently corroborates the reported burial place for Stanton and Louvenia, but it is a secondary cemetery aggregation and does not provide a headstone image, plot number or original burial register. It does not establish Stanton's parents.

Source: PeopleLegacy, cemetery aggregation shown on the Whittle Cemetery listing: https://peoplelegacy.com/david_allen_eastham__sr-6Y5g3F

## Official local record repository
Russell County Public Library's History & Genealogy page confirms that its physical collection includes family histories, cemetery records, county histories, birth/death/marriage records and the Creelsboro Digital Media Archives. These holdings may provide the original cemetery transcription/register, the Whittle family-history volumes, and additional photographs or documents. The page does not show a surname-level catalog or an online name search.

Source: Russell County Public Library, History & Genealogy: https://www.russellcountylibrary.com/discover-learn/history-genealogy

## Parentage and photograph limits
The PeopleLegacy listing supports that Stanton and Louvenia are buried in the same Whittle Cemetery as Avery, but it supplies no parentage for Stanton and no portrait/headstone photograph for either parent. The profile's existing birth-date conflict for Stanton remains:
- headstone/cemetery index: 1892–1972;
- derivative Social Security-style index: 10 July 1893–1 October 1972.

The 1951 Cordella Whittle obituary gives Cordella's birth as 14 May 1878 and her marriage to R. S. “Bob” Whittle as 24 December 1895. If Stanton's 1893 birth is accurate, Cordella was approximately 15 at his birth and had married Bob two years later. This makes her a possible mother from a pre-marital relationship, but the obituary's six-child count names five children and does not name Stanton. No relationship is proven. The 1900/1910 census and original birth/death records remain necessary.

No new parentage claim or photograph was added.
