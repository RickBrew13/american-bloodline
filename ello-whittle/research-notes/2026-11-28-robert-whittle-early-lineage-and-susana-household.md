# Avery Ello Whittle — early Robert Whittle lineage query and 1850 Susana household
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed a 1996 Russell County query about early Robert and John Whittle families and compared it with the 1850/1860 Russell County census transcriptions.

## 1996 researcher query — Robert Whittle and John Whittle of Casey County
A 22 December 1996 query by **Barbara Edwards** asks for information about:
- **Robert Whittle**, estimated birth 1807–1818, in Wayne, Russell or Edmonson County.
- **John Whittle**, 1794–1838, in Casey County.
- The submitter believed John might have been Robert's uncle.

This is a contemporary genealogy research query, not evidence of the relationship. It is valuable because it identifies a possible early Whittle family connection across Wayne, Russell, Edmonson and Casey Counties. It must not be conflated with **John L. (Jack) Whittle (1796–1866)** of Edmonson County, who is separately reported as a son of Robert and Susannah Whittle and husband of Elizabeth Tarter.

Source: KYGenWeb, Russell County Queries, 1996, Barbara Edwards entry dated 22 December 1996: https://kygenweb.net/russell/queries/queries-1996.html

## 1850 Russell County — Susana Whittle household
The 1850 Russell County census transcription (M432-217) lists a Whittle group in family number 257 on page 257B:
- **Susana Whittle**, indexed age 24;
- **Nancy Whittle**, 17;
- **Ann Whittle**, 11;
- **Robert Whittle**, 8;
- **Elizabeth Whittle**, 5 months.

The enumerated household head immediately above is **Richard Miller**, age 41, and the Whittle individuals are recorded under the same family number. The census has no relationship-to-head field in 1850, so this could be a mixed household or a widow/relative group living with Miller. The age 24 for Susana is inconsistent with the ages of Nancy and Ann if they were her children; it may be a transcription/reading error (possibly 44), but the original schedule has not been inspected. Do not assume that Susana was their mother or that Robert was the son of a deceased Robert Whittle.

Source: USGenWeb Census Project, 1850 Russell County, M432-217, page 257B, lines 29–34: https://www.us-census.org/pub/usgenweb/census/ky/russell/1850/pg0257a.txt

## Possible bridge to Robert and Matilda Rexroat Whittle — unproved
A 1936 Russell County marriage transcription identifies **Harria Whittle** as a son of **Robert and Matilda Rexroat Whittle**. If the 1850 census's Robert Whittle (age 8, born about 1842) later married Matilda Rexroat, he could be the Robert named as Harria's father. That would make him about 33 when R. S. “Bob” Whittle was born in 1875 and about 47 when Harria was born around 1889—chronologically possible. The 1850 household and the 1936 marriage record do not, by themselves, prove that identity.

The 1996 query's older Robert (estimated 1807–1818) could conceivably be the father of the 1850 Robert age 8, but the query does not name a wife or children. The 1850 Susana household is a lead to test, not proof.

Source for Harria's parents: Rainwater Collection, Russell County marriage records, Harria Whittle–Lela Rainwater, 30 December 1936, Book 35, p. 71: https://www.therainwatercollection.com/marriage.shtml

## 1860 Russell County — John Whittle and Nancy Polston household
A Polston family genealogy transcription of the 1860 Russell County census identifies household no. 585:
- **John Whittle**, 25;
- **Nancy**, 25;
- **Larena**, 3;
- **Ivy**, 1;
- **Sarah Whittle**, 57;
- **Nancy Polston**, 21.

A separate compiled Polston family page identifies **John Whittle (born about 1836)** as the husband of **Nancy Polston (born about 1836)** and lists children including Ivy (born about 1859), Charles, Analiza, William S., Sarah E., Unaty, James B. and Emma. The census's Ivy (age 1) fits the compiled birth year. However, the transcription's extra Larena, Sarah Whittle and Nancy Polston require explanation, and the family group has not been verified against the original 1860 schedule.

This John Whittle may be part of the broader Russell County Whittle family, but no evidence yet connects him to Robert/Matilda, Bob/Cordella, Stanton or John L. Whittle of Edmonson County.

## Separate Edmonson County John L. Whittle line — do not merge
The 1850 Edmonson County census lists **John Whittle, 54**, wife **Elizabeth, 45**, and children Cecilia, James, Alford, Mary, Martin, Ann E., Elizabeth, Henry, John and David. A separate Edmonson household lists **Robert Whittle, 25**, wife Amanda, and children Nancy and Ira. A 1989 county-family history identifies John L. (Jack) Whittle (1796–1866) as a son of Robert and Susannah Whittle, married to Elizabeth Tarter, and says he moved from the Jabez Ridge area to Edmonson County in 1837. The 1850 census and the family-history account support this as a distinct Edmonson family group; a direct link to the Russell County Robert/Matilda family is not yet established.

Sources:
- USGenWeb Census Project, 1850 Edmonson County, household of John Whittle and separate Robert Whittle household: https://www.us-census.org/pub/usgenweb/census/ky/edmonson/1850/pg0001b.txt
- Miller County Museum, “John L. (Jack) Whittle” and Whittle–Keeth families: https://www.millercountymuseum.org/bios/bio_w.html
