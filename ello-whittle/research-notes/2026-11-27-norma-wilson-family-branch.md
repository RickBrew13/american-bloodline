# Avery Ello Whittle — sister Norma Jean Wilson and descendants
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Expanded the family of Avery's sister Norma Jean Whittle Wilson using her official Bernard Funeral Home obituary.

## Norma Jean Wilson
**Norma Jean (Whittle) Wilson** was born **17 January 1933** in Russell County and died **30 January 2017** at Fair Oaks Nursing Home, Jamestown, Kentucky. Her official obituary names her parents as **Stanton B. and Louvenia McGowan Whittle** and her deceased husband as **Carson Wilson**. She was a homemaker and member of Church of God of Prophecy.

Source: Bernard Funeral Home, *Official Obituary of Norma Jean (Whittle) Wilson*: https://www.bernardfuneralhome.com/obituary/Norma-Wilson

## Children
The obituary names four surviving children:
- **Carl J. (Leslie) Wilson**, Harmony, Maryland.
- **Sheena (Walter) Grider**, Jamestown, Kentucky.
- **Troy (Sheila) Wilson**, Russell Springs, Kentucky.
- **Lyn (Myra) Wilson**, Bowling Green, Kentucky.

It also names deceased son **Arnold Ray Wilson**. The obituary reports ten grandchildren and five great-grandchildren, but does not name them.

## Sibling cross-check
Norma's obituary says she was preceded by two sisters and three brothers, and survived by sisters **Grace Eastham** and **Dean Beckner**. This matches the sibling group: the two adult sisters who had died by 2017 were Iza Pearl Whittle Coffey and Marie Whittle Stacy; the three adult brothers were Avery, Shirley and Calvin. Grace and Veldean (“Dean”) were still living. This is a strong independent cross-check of the sibling roster and supports identifying “Dean Beckner” as Veldean Beckner.
