**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Interpreted the “Enlistment for the Panama Canal Department” field on Avery's 5 January 1946 WWII Army enlistment record and identified the relevant National Archives record group.

## What the enlistment record actually says
Avery's WWII Army enlistment record (serial no. **35888669**) records:
- Enlisted **5 January 1946** at Fort Thomas, Kentucky.
- Regular Army; Private; no branch assignment recorded.
- Term of enlistment: **“Enlistment for the Panama Canal Department.”**
- Civilian occupation code: engineers, electrical.
- Education: grammar school.
- Marital status: single, without dependents.
- Army release date in the BIRLS record: **9 July 1947**.

The Panama Canal Department wording is the official code for the *term/department of enlistment* (code 6) in the WWII Army enlistment dataset. It is not, by itself, a unit assignment, posting order or proof of the specific location where Avery served. Do not state that he was stationed in the Canal Zone unless a personnel record, unit roster or other service record confirms it.

## Relevant National Archives holdings
NARA's Guide to Federal Records identifies **Record Group 548, Records of U.S. Army Forces in the Caribbean**, 1939–1964. It includes:
- Records of the Panama Canal Department, 1940–1947.
- Records of U.S. Army, Caribbean, 1946–1964.
- Headquarters correspondence, ca. 1947–1951, command reports, orders and related operational records.

This is the correct archival group to investigate for command-level documentation that might identify Avery's unit or assignment. The guide does not confirm that a named roster or individual personnel file survives for him.

Source: NARA, Guide to Federal Records, RG 548: https://www.archives.gov/research/guide-fed-records/groups/548.html
