# Avery Ello Whittle — Cordella's husband Bob and son Otha cross-check
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Cross-checked the 1951 Cordella Whittle obituary against cemetery-index entries and later descendant obituaries.

## Robert S. “Bob” Whittle
A PeopleLegacy burial aggregation for McBeath Cemetery, Russell Springs, Kentucky, lists **Robert S. “Bob” Whittle (1875–1948)**. Cordella's 1951 obituary transcription says her husband R. S. Whittle, known as Bob, died **1 December 1949**. The matching initials, nickname and Russell County setting make this a plausible identification, but the death year conflicts by one year. Do not silently choose 1948 or 1949 until an original death certificate, cemetery register or contemporary notice resolves it.

## Otha Whittle
The same McBeath Cemetery listing identifies **Otha L. Whittle (15 September 1898–1 October 1978)**. This is chronologically and geographically consistent with the Otha Whittle named as a child of Cordella and Bob in her 1951 obituary. A 2022 obituary for daughter **Lana Kay Hale** independently identifies her parents as the late **Otha Whittle and Nancy Mae Meadows Whittle**, and names siblings including Alene McKinley, Lavern Elswick, Wilma Mercer, Jean Bringer, Jerry Holt and Johnny Whittle. This supplies a descendant branch for Otha but does not independently identify his parents as Bob and Cordella.

## What is and is not established
- The obituary directly names Otha among Cordella's children; this is the strongest evidence connecting Otha to her.
- The cemetery aggregation supplies approximate life dates and a shared burial location for Bob and Otha, but is derivative and not a substitute for the cemetery's register or death certificates.
- The 2022 obituary confirms Otha's wife Nancy Mae Meadows and several children, but does not state his parents.
- The obituary still reports six children but names only five. Rese, Jim Elmer, Caleb and Grady remain unresolved individuals; do not identify same-name people without matching evidence.
- Nothing in these records proves that Stanton Bell Whittle was a child of Cordella and Bob. His reported 1893 birth predates their 1895 marriage; a pre-marital relationship remains only a possibility.

## Additional child-name lead: Grady Whittle
A derivative Social Security-style index lists **Grady Whittle**, born **14 February 1914** and died **1 February 1975**, last residence Russell Springs, Kentucky (42642). Cordella's 1951 obituary names a son Grady Whittle. The matching full name, county and chronology make this a plausible identification, but the index does not give parents or spouse. Do not attach this Grady to Cordella and Bob until his original death certificate, obituary, census or marriage record names his parents.
