# Avery Ello Whittle — 1947 marriage age discrepancy
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Reconciled the age fields on the 23 July 1947 Avery E. Whittle–Eunice Brewster marriage record against the known birth-date evidence already in both family profiles.

## What the marriage index reports
The FamilySearch index for the 23 July 1947 Kenton County, Kentucky marriage reports:
- Groom **Avery E. Whittle**, age **22**, parents Stanton and Lavina Whittle.
- Bride **Eunice Brewster**, age **21**, parents Homer and Sarah Brewster.

Source: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:Q2DX-LBZS

## Conflict with the birth evidence
- Avery's profile has 4 January 1927 on his headstone and Kentucky birth-index/Death Master File entries, while the 1944 draft card and BIRLS supply July 1926 dates. On 23 July 1947, those birth dates would make him about **20 or 21**, not 22.
- Eunice's documented birth date is 9 February 1931. On 23 July 1947 she was **16**, not 21.
- Thus both ages in the marriage index conflict with the other birth evidence; Eunice's age differs by five years.

## Interpretation and next steps
Do not replace the birth dates with the marriage-index ages. The discrepancy may reflect inaccurate ages supplied at the time, an indexing/transcription error, or another record issue; the current evidence does not determine which.

The original marriage certificate image is stored once in Eunice's shared archive folder: [Eunice-Brewster-1947-marriage-certificate-Drive-original.jpg](https://github.com/RickBrew13/american-bloodline/blob/main/eunice-burton/documents/Eunice-Brewster-1947-marriage-certificate-Drive-original.jpg). It is the same couple record and should be linked, not duplicated in Avery's folder. It should be re-read directly for the exact handwriting and any license/application annotations. Then seek the Kenton County marriage application, consent/affidavit papers, bond, and minister's return, if retained. These may show whether either party's age was intentionally misstated or whether the index is wrong.

The original record is shared evidence for Avery and Eunice; keep the same document in the couple archive and link it from both profiles rather than creating duplicate scans. This note is stored in Avery's folder because it addresses his marriage chronology; the corresponding age issue should also be reflected in Eunice's own marriage notes when her profile is next updated.
