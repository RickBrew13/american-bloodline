# Avery Ello Whittle — Henry County local archive, directory and photograph leads
**Research date:** 26 November 2026  
**Scope:** Local New Castle/Henry County repositories relevant to Avery's residence, marriage, property and family photographs. The existing Avery folder was reviewed before this research.

## Henry County Historical Society family-file surname index
Downloaded and searched the Society's current **Surname Index of Family Files** (16-page PDF, posted February 2024). The Society explains that these family files can contain photographs, documents, pedigree charts, family group sheets, obituaries and other family material; if a surname is in the index, the file is held at the Society and researchers are invited to visit.

### Surnames present that may connect to Avery's family
- **BECKNER** — Avery's 1977 obituary names sister Veldean Beckner.
- **COFFEY** — his 1977 obituary names sister Pearl Coffey; this may also help identify Waldo Coffee/Coffey, the contact person on Avery's 1944 draft card.
- **STACY** — the 1977 obituary names sister Marie Stacey; the index uses the spelling Stacy.
- **RAY** — Theresa's 2016 obituary names sister Debra Ray.
- **WILSON** — the 1977 obituary names sister Norma Jean Wilson.

### Surnames not present in this index
No entries were found for **WHITTLE**, **MULVEY**, **MCGOWAN**, **EASTHAM**, **MATHENY**, or **MOUKHTAR**. Absence from this one family-file index does not mean the Society has no records mentioning those people; it means no separate surname file is indexed under those spellings.

This is a promising photo/document lead for Avery and his sisters, especially the Coffey, Beckner, Stacy and Wilson family files. The actual files are not downloadable from the index; the Society says they must be consulted in person. No visit, call or email request was made.

## Henry County land and property records
Henry County's Auditor maintains a public tax-list search by owner name, parcel or address. Its real-property pages include owner/transfer history for current parcels. The Recorder moved from Doxpop to **Tapestry** in January 2025; historical deed documents can be searched by person, parcel or document, with older deed indexes/books still available through the Recorder. The public auditor page returned a gateway error during this pass, so no name search for Avery or Charlotte was completed and no negative result is claimed.

Sources:

- Henry County Auditor, Real Property Information example showing deeded-owner transfer history: https://auditor.henrycounty33.us/cgi.exe?CALL_PROGRAM=C009INFO&FINDINFO=00001043979&ORIGIN=A&TAXYEAR=2024

## New Castle directories and newspapers
New Castle-Henry County Public Library's genealogy holdings include a **Courier-Times index (1848–1989)** and city directories; the 1977 Avery obituary and 1950s–1970s directory entries are likely candidates for checking. The library's general marriage index holdings listed online cover 1850–1920, so they do not include Avery and Charlotte's likely 1950s marriage. The Henry County Clerk is the custodian for later marriage licenses; local newspaper indexes and microfilm are the alternative route for an announcement.
