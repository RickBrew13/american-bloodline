## Stanton Bell Whittle — birth-date conflict
The existing profile uses 1892–1972 from a compiled cemetery reference. A derivative Social Security-style index gives **10 July 1893–1 October 1972**, with last residence Russell Springs, Russell County, Kentucky. That exact birth date conflicts with the 1892 cemetery date. The Social Security-style listing is a useful lead but not the original application or death certificate; the cemetery aggregation is also secondary. Do not silently choose either date until the original Kentucky death certificate, Social Security application, or an original census image resolves it.

## 1940 census — exact enumeration-district route now established
The National Archives' 1940 Census dataset index identifies the Russell County, Kentucky 1940 enumeration districts. The family was previously reported in Magisterial District 3. The applicable 1940 EDs are therefore **104-6, 104-7, 104-8 and 104-9**, whose official descriptions cover the subdivisions of Magisterial District 3. These EDs are a bounded set for browsing the original schedules; the household has not yet been located in an original image in this session.

The official NARA metadata gives these exact directories:
- ED 104-6: `population-schedules/ky/russell-county/ed/104-6/`
- ED 104-7: `population-schedules/ky/russell-county/ed/104-7/`
- ED 104-8: `population-schedules/ky/russell-county/ed/104-8/`
- ED 104-9: `population-schedules/ky/russell-county/ed/104-9/`

NARA's 1940 census microfilm roll list confirms Russell County is on **T627, roll 1354**. The 1900 Russell County census is on **T623, roll 550**; the 1910 Russell County census is on **T624, roll 501**. The 1900/1910 schedule images or indexed household entries have not yet been retrieved. The 1900/1910 records are the highest-value next evidence for identifying Stanton's parents and Louvenia's family of origin.
