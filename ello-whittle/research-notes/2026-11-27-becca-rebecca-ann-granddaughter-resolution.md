# Avery Ello Whittle — Becca / Rebecca Ann Whittle granddaughter identity cross-check
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Reconciled the “Becca” named as a deceased granddaughter in Charlotte Whittle's 2025 obituary with Rebecca Ann Whittle, named in the 2008 and 2016 family obituaries.

## Three-source match
- **Rebecca Ann Whittle**, born 13 May 1986 and died 27 September 2008, is identified in her 2008 obituary as the daughter of **Jeff and Donna Whittle** and granddaughter of **Avery Whittle and Gladys Christensen**. The obituary also names her siblings, including a deceased brother Matthew.
- **Theresa Whittle's 2016 obituary** names Rebecca Ann Whittle as her niece and Matthew Mark Whittle as her nephew.
- **Charlotte Whittle's 2025 obituary** names her deceased granddaughter **Becca** and deceased grandson **Matthew**.

Sources:
- Rebecca Ann Whittle obituary, Dallas Morning News, 30 September 2008: https://obits.dallasnews.com/us/obituaries/dallasmorningnews/name/rebecca-whittle-obituary?id=25026934
- Theresa Lynn Whittle obituary, 29 November 2016: https://www.charleswsmithandsonsfuneralhome.com/obituaries/theresa-whittle
- Charlotte Ann Whittle obituary, 27 May 2025: https://www.charleswsmithandsonsfuneralhome.com/obituaries/charlotte-whittle

## Conclusion
The strongest explanation is that Charlotte's “Becca” is **Rebecca Ann Whittle**, Avery and Charlotte's granddaughter through Jeff and Donna. The nickname, same generation, the 2008 death, and the 2016 obituary's niece relationship all align. The same cross-obituary pattern supports identifying Charlotte's “Matthew” as the deceased nephew Matthew Mark Whittle, likely another child of Jeff and Donna; that relationship is detailed in the separate Matthew note.

List Rebecca Ann only once. Do not add a separate granddaughter named Becca unless another source identifies a different person. The 2008 obituary provides the exact life dates; the 2025 obituary does not provide dates or a surname for Becca.
