## Family and record follow-up
- Parents remain Stanton Bell Whittle (1892–1972) and Louvenia M. McGowan Whittle (1900–1940), supported by the existing profile/census and cemetery evidence. Veldene Beckner’s 2020 obituary independently names “Stanten & Louvenia McGowan Whittle” as her parents and says she was preceded by four sisters and three brothers. This corroborates the parental couple and a large sibling group, although it does not enumerate all siblings.
- Avery’s existing sibling list contains eleven children of Stanton and Louvenia. The 1977 obituary directly confirms seven siblings living at that time: Marie, Pearl, Veldene, Grace, Norma Jean, Calvin and Shirley. A compiled Hugh Frank Cemetery listing records Gearlind Whittle (1936–1936), supporting that the sibling died in infancy; the original burial register and exact dates remain unverified.
- Theresa Lynn Whittle’s 2016 obituary independently names Avery Ello Whittle as her father, Charlotte Ann (Mulvey) Whittle as her mother, and siblings Rob, Debra, Jeff, Mike, Cindy and Louretta. This corroborates several of the children, while showing name variants: Cynthia/Cindy and Loretta/Louretta. Charlotte’s 2025 obituary states Avery and Charlotte raised eight children and specifically confirms Becky died at six months.
- The 1947 marriage certificate records Avery’s occupation as hotel work. The 1950 census records him as a gardener at a distillery. The profile’s previous “occupation not found” wording was therefore too broad and has been corrected to these dated entries.
- The 1944 draft card remains the strongest contemporary source for Avery’s 2 July 1926 birth date; BIRLS gives 26 July 1926; headstone/Kentucky birth index/DMF give 4 January 1927. The 1947 marriage certificate states age 22, inconsistent with each date. No birth date has been selected as definitive.

## Independent 2016 and 2025 family obituary review — 25 November 2026
A fresh web-source review of the funeral-home obituaries adds independent, named-family evidence:
- Theresa Lynn Whittle's 2016 Charles W. Smith & Sons obituary explicitly identifies her father as **Avery Ello Whittle** and her mother as **Charlotte Ann (Mulvey) Whittle**. It names her six surviving siblings: Rob, Debra, Jeff, Mike, Cindy and Louretta; it also identifies deceased younger sister Rebecca Jo. This independently confirms the parentage of Theresa and the six sibling identities, while the 1977 clipping supplies the forms Robert, Jeffery, Cynthia and Loretta. Preserve the differences as name variants, not separate children.
