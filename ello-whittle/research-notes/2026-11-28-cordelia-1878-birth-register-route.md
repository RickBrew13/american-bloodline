# Avery Ello Whittle — Cordelia Tucker's 1878 Russell County birth register
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Located the county birth-register year that corresponds exactly to Cordelia Whittle's reported birth year and identified its archival access route.

## Exact record series located
The Kentucky Department for Libraries and Archives' updated *Vital Statistics Microfilm Inventory* (8 June 2026) lists Russell County on **microfilm roll #994054**, with birth records for **1852–1859, 1874–1875 and 1878**, among other years. This confirms a surviving Russell County birth register for **1878**, the year Cordella Whittle is reported to have been born (14 May 1878). The Ancestry image identifier shown by the county-births guide is **KYVR_994054-0061_2**, matching the KDLA roll number. Kenton County Public Library's microfilm guide also lists Russell County 1852–1904 on local copy **353.59 K372v, Reel 25**.

The Shy Genealogist's Russell County Births page describes the original birth-register fields:
- child's name and sex;
- whether living or stillborn;
- county of birth;
- father's name;
- mother's maiden name;
- parent's county of residence;
- remarks such as illegitimacy or twins.

The same page lists the **1878 Russell County birth register** among the Ancestry digitized registers (five pages). Its Ancestry image link returned HTTP 403 in this research session, so the original entry was not viewed. The record can be requested by the exact roll #994054 and Ancestry image identifier above.

## Why this is important
If Cordella E. Tucker is the same woman as Cordella Whittle, her 1878 birth-register entry may identify her father and her mother's maiden name directly. This is much stronger than the compiled PeopleLegacy Tucker family grouping and could confirm or refute Eli Tucker and Narcissus Popplewell as her parents.

The entry should be searched under:
- Cordelia / Cordella / Cordelia E. / Cordella E.;
- Tucker;
- date 14 May 1878 (but the register may give only a year or may have a different date).

The register may also reveal whether she was recorded as a child of an unmarried mother. Do not assume that the register's parent names will match the compiled family group until the original entry is read.

## Access route
1. Use Ancestry Library Edition at a public library, if available, to view the five-page 1878 Russell County birth register.
2. Alternatively, ask KDLA staff whether the 1878 Russell County birth register can be inspected or copied and request the entry for Cordelia/Cordella Tucker.
3. If an image is obtained, transcribe every field exactly and save the image in the shared Tucker/Whittle source archive, then link it from Avery's profile. Do not duplicate it in multiple people folders.

No institution was contacted and no fee or record request was submitted.
