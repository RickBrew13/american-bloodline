# Avery Ello Whittle — Charlotte Mulvey marriage and Eunice Whittle divorce search
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Searched for the end of Avery's 1947 marriage to Eunice Brewster and the date/place of his later marriage to Charlotte Ann Mulvey.

## Established sequence
- Avery E. Whittle and Eunice Brewster married in Kenton County, Kentucky, on **23 July 1947**. The FamilySearch index reports their ages as 22 and 21, but both ages conflict with the other birth-date evidence; see the 27 November marriage-age note.
- The 1950 census places Avery and Eunice together in Cincinnati, Ohio.
- Charlotte Ann Mulvey was born 26 February 1936 in Covington, Kentucky. Her 2025 obituary says she met Avery, that they built a home and raised eight children in New Castle, Indiana, and identifies him as her husband.
- The 2016 and 2025 obituaries confirm Avery and Charlotte's family, but neither gives a marriage date or county.
- No divorce decree for Avery and Eunice or marriage record for Avery and Charlotte was located in the accessible online sources searched.

## Indiana record repositories and exact coverage
Indiana's official Archives and Records Administration states:
- Before 1958, marriage records were collected at the county level only.
- Marriage records from 1958–2005 are digitized in Ancestry (genealogical-use copies); certified copies come from the county clerk.
- The Indiana State Library's Indiana Legacy database includes an online **Indiana Marriages 1958–2022** index, plus local VINE marriage records.
- The statewide marriage index for 1957–1992 is available on microfilm at the Indiana State Archives reading room.
- Divorce records are maintained by the county clerk where the decree was granted; there is no statewide Indiana divorce index.
