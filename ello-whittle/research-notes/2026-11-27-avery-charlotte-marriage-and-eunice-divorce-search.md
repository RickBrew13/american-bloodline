# Avery Ello Whittle — Charlotte Mulvey marriage and Eunice Whittle divorce search
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Searched for the end of Avery's 1947 marriage to Eunice Brewster and the date/place of his later marriage to Charlotte Ann Mulvey.

## Established sequence
- Avery E. Whittle and Eunice Brewster married in Kenton County, Kentucky, on **23 July 1947**. The FamilySearch index reports their ages as 22 and 21, but both ages conflict with the other birth-date evidence; see the 27 November marriage-age note.
- The 1950 census places Avery and Eunice together in Cincinnati, Ohio.
- Charlotte Ann Mulvey was born 26 February 1936 in Covington, Kentucky. Her 2025 obituary says she met Avery, that they built a home and raised eight children in New Castle, Indiana, and identifies him as her husband.
- The 2016 and 2025 obituaries confirm Avery and Charlotte's family, but neither gives a marriage date or county.
- No divorce decree for Avery and Eunice or marriage record for Avery and Charlotte was located in the accessible online sources searched.

## Indiana record repositories and exact coverage
Indiana's official Archives and Records Administration states:
- Before 1958, marriage records were collected at the county level only.
- Marriage records from 1958–2005 are digitized in Ancestry (genealogical-use copies); certified copies come from the county clerk.
- The Indiana State Library's Indiana Legacy database includes an online **Indiana Marriages 1958–2022** index, plus local VINE marriage records.
- The statewide marriage index for 1957–1992 is available on microfilm at the Indiana State Archives reading room.
- Divorce records are maintained by the county clerk where the decree was granted; there is no statewide Indiana divorce index.

## Hamilton County, Ohio divorce-index route rechecked — 28 November 2026
The 1950 census places Avery and Eunice in Cincinnati, Hamilton County. A current Hamilton County Genealogical Society guide confirms that mid-20th-century divorce cases are indexed through the Hamilton County Clerk of Courts website. It instructs researchers to use the **Comprehensive Name Search**, then inspect Common Pleas case numbers (older cases may begin with “A”; later Domestic Relations cases use “DR”). A divorce entry should show the parties and filing date; request the full petition-through-decree file to establish the legal end of the marriage and any children/custody/property details.

- Hamilton County Clerk of Courts, [Search by Name](https://www.courtclerk.org/records-search/name/) and [Domestic Relations Name Search](https://www.courtclerk.org/records-search/domestic-relations-name-search/).
- Hamilton County Genealogical Society, [Marriages and Divorces guide](https://www.hcgsohio.org/cpage.php?pt=60), especially the divorce section.
- Hamilton County Clerk of Courts, [Domestic Relations Division](https://www.courtclerk.org/our-office/common-pleas-court-clerk/domestic-relations-division/) — divorce decrees/dissolutions from 1885 to current are held at the courthouse, Room 315; phone 513-946-5693.

The clerk's name-search page timed out in this research session, so no case result could be verified. The University of Cincinnati guide notes that older cases may require an in-person paper-room lookup; the Clerk's current page should be tried first. The Ohio History Connection states there is **no statewide divorce index before 1954**; the Ohio Department of Health maintains an index from 1 January 1954 onward. If the divorce occurred before 1954, Hamilton County's local case index is essential; if after 1954, also request a search of the state index.

Sources:
- Ohio History Connection, Divorce Records guide: https://ohiohistory.libguides.com/vital/divorcerecords
- University of Cincinnati, Cincinnati and Ohio Legal Documents guide: https://libguides.hamilton-co.org/c.php?g=773110&p=5546224

No clerk, archive or state agency was contacted; no copy or search fee was paid.


## Ohio statewide index coverage checked — 28 November 2026
The Ohio Vital Statistics Search hosted by Henschen & Associates (data compiled by the Ohio Department of Health) has:
- marriage records beginning **14 December 1926**;
- divorce records beginning **2 January 1962**.

This is a potential searchable index for Avery's later marriage to Charlotte if it occurred in Ohio, and for the divorce from Eunice only if the divorce was finalized in Ohio in 1962 or later. It cannot resolve an Ohio divorce in 1950–1961. The search form is interactive and this research session could not submit names, so no match/no-match result is claimed.

Search portal: https://hacourts.henschen.com/VitStats/  
Help and coverage details: https://hacourts.henschen.com/VitStats/Help/index.shtml

The Ohio History Connection says the Ohio Department of Health maintains a separate divorce index from 1 January 1954 onward. Therefore:
- before 1954: search the county court where the divorce occurred;
- 1954–1961: request the Ohio Department of Health index or local county file;
- 1962 onward: also search the Henschen statewide index.
