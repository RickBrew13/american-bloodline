# Avery Ello Whittle — paternal aunt branches: Norma, Grace and Veldean
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Extended the documented sibling group of Avery's father, Stanton Bell Whittle, using three later obituaries that independently name Stanton and Louvenia McGowan Whittle as parents.

## Norma Jean (Whittle) Wilson — daughter of Stanton and Louvienie
The official Bernard Funeral Home obituary states Norma was born 17 January 1933 in Russell County to **Stanton B. and Louvienie McGowan Whittle**, and died 30 January 2017. She was the widow of Carson Wilson.

Her obituary identifies:
- Four children: Carl J. Wilson, Sheena Grider, Troy Wilson and Lyn Wilson.
- Ten grandchildren and five great-grandchildren.
- Two surviving sisters: Grace Eastham and Dean Beckner.
- Two sisters and three brothers who had died before Norma.

It also reports 19 direct living descendants. The obituary does not name all grandchildren or great-grandchildren.

Sources:
- Bernard Funeral Home, official obituary: https://www.bernardfuneralhome.com/obituary/Norma-Wilson
- ColumbiaMagazine, full obituary transcription: https://columbiamagazine.com/index.php?sid=88430

## Grace (Whittle) Eastham — daughter of Stanton and Louvenia
The WHVE obituary archive states Grace was born 27 March 1925 in Russell County to **Stanton B. and Louvenia McGowan Whittle**, and died 20 November 2018. She was a homemaker and member of Parks Ridge Church of God.

Her obituary identifies:
- Four children: Gayla Sue Kerr, Treva Meirose, Tina Milliner and Rex Eastham.
- A daughter, Phyllis Holder, who predeceased her.
- Fourteen grandchildren and several great-grandchildren.
- One surviving sister: Veldean Beckner.
- Burial in Whittle Cemetery.

Source: WHVE, November 2018 obituaries archive, “Grace Eastham, 93, Russell Springs”: https://wvlc.com/whve-obits/archives/2018-11/

## Veldean (Whittle) Beckner — daughter of Stanton and Louvenia
The WHVE obituary archive identifies her under the spelling **Veldene Beckner** (other family sources use Veldean). It states she was born 13 September 1938 in Russell County to **Stanten and Louvenia McGowan Whittle**, and died 15 October 2020.

Her obituary identifies:
- Four children: Kathy Howard, Donna Sibley, Barbara Grider and James Beckner Jr.
- A son, Timothy Beckner, and a granddaughter, Traci Howard, who predeceased her.
- Nine grandchildren, 23 great-grandchildren and six great-great-grandchildren.
- Four sisters and three brothers who had died before her.
- Burial in Whittle Cemetery.

Source: WHVE, October 2020 obituaries archive, “Veldene Beckner, 82, Russell Springs”: https://wvlc.com/whve-obits/archives/2020-10/

## Sibling-count reconciliation
Avery's 1977 obituary names **seven surviving siblings**. The current working list has eleven siblings: Iza Pearl, Shirley, Grace, Marie, Calvin, Norma and Veldean were alive in 1977; Anna, Heasby Earl, Ulissus Hue and Gearlind had died earlier. This matches the reported seven survivors if the 1940 census index's **“Imogene”** is the same person as the sister later named **Marie Stacey** in Avery's obituary. That identity is not yet proven. The 1940 household index lists Imogene, not Marie; if Imogene was a separate child, the total sibling group may be larger than eleven.

Norma's 2017 obituary lists five siblings who had died and two surviving sisters; it does not name all surviving siblings. Veldean's 2020 obituary lists seven siblings who had died but does not name surviving siblings. These later counts do not disprove the working list; the notices provide incomplete survivor lists and reflect deaths at different dates. The 1940 Imogene/Marie identity must be resolved from the original census image and vital records before the total sibling count is finalized. The original census and vital records remain the proof route for the full group.

## Why this matters to Avery's family archive
These obituaries independently reinforce the Stanton/Louvenia parent couple and document descendant branches from three of Avery's paternal aunts. They do not identify Stanton's own parents, and they do not establish Cordella Tucker as his mother. The full names of every grandchild and later descendant in these collateral branches remain incomplete; do not infer names from family totals.
