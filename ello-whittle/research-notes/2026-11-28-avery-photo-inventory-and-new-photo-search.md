# Avery Ello Whittle — photo inventory and new-photo search
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Rechecked Avery's existing image/archive folders before looking for additional photographs, then followed the Russell County and Henry County photo-collection leads.

## Existing images in Avery's GitHub archive
- **Army portrait, c. 1946:** family-held original and web-sized display copy in `img/`; attributed on Find a Grave to Matthew Dion Whittle.
- **Headstone photograph:** family-held original in `img/headstones/` and web-sized display copy in `img/`; attributed on Find a Grave to Janet Ward.
- **1977 obituary clipping:** family-held original in `documents/`; the clipping includes a small portrait of Avery but also identifies living relatives. It remains preserved as a source document and is not shown in the public gallery or cropped/re-published separately.
- **Family photographs folder:** `img/family-photos/` currently has no family photograph files.
- **Family books folder:** `family-books/` currently has no book files.

The two display-size files are derivatives of the preserved originals; they are intentionally kept for the existing profile gallery. No existing photo or document was deleted or moved.

## New-photo search
A web image search for Avery Ello Whittle and his Find a Grave memorial did not surface an independently verifiable additional portrait. Search results included unrelated Whittle/Tucker material and were not added.

The Henry County Historical Society & Museum's 2024 repository guide confirms a physical **Family Surname Photo/Document Files** collection, along with family Bibles, scrapbooks and local yearbooks. It does not provide a public surname-level inventory. The New Castle Henry County Public Library's Indiana Room holds local yearbooks (including New Castle High School, 1916–2013), directories and newspaper indexes. These are promising in-person/staff lookup routes, but no Whittle photo was retrieved and no archive was contacted.

The Russell County Public Library confirms digital archives with strong local holdings in photographs, maps, oral histories, newspapers and yearbooks, plus family histories and cemetery records. Its online resource descriptions do not identify an individual Avery Whittle photo. No new image was retrieved.
