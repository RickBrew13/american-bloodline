# Avery Ello Whittle — Otha Whittle possible maternal half-sibling branch
**Research date:** 27 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed Otha Whittle, one of the five named surviving children in Cordella Whittle's 1951 obituary. This branch matters to Avery only if Cordella is later proven to be Stanton's mother.

## Otha's identity and parents
- Cordella Whittle's 22 March 1951 obituary names **Otha Whittle** among the children born to her marriage with R. S. “Bob” Whittle.
- A secondary Kentucky death index lists **Otha Whittle**, born **15 September 1898**, died **1 October 1978**, last residence Russell Springs, Russell County.
- The same index lists **Mae Whittle**, born **11 April 1905**, died **1 November 1979**.
- Two later obituaries identify Otha's wife as **Mae Meadows / Nancy Mae Meadows Whittle**:
  - 2010 obituary for son Arlus “Johnny” Whittle calls his parents Otha Whittle and Mae Meadow Whittle.
  - 2022 obituary for daughter Lana Kay Hale calls her parents Otha Whittle and Nancy Mae Meadows Whittle.
- These details make the 1898–1978 Otha in the death index the likely same Otha named in Cordella's 1951 obituary. The original Otha death certificate/obituary has not yet been obtained.

## Otha and Mae's children
Lana Kay Hale's 2022 obituary says her parents were Otha and Nancy Mae Meadows Whittle and names five deceased sisters and one deceased brother:
- **Alene McKinley**
- **Lavern Elswick**
- **Wilma Mercer**
- **Jean Bringer**
- **Jerry Holt**
- **Arlus “Johnny” Whittle**

Kay is the seventh known child in this sibling group. Her obituary gives:
- Husband: David Hale (55 years).
- Son: John David Hale (Loretta).
- Grandchildren: Brittany Kaylene Hale Fox and Bradley David Carl Hale.
- Great-grandchildren: Wyatt Fox, Wren Fox and Wilder Fox.

Johnny's 2010 obituary gives:
- Wife: Sylba Whittle (1928–2019).
- Daughter: Becky Jo (Rex) Johnson.
- Granddaughters: Sara Elizabeth (Ryan) Stephens, Hanna Jo Johnson and Rebecca Hope Johnson.
- One sister: Kay (David) Hale.

Sylba's 2019 obituary independently identifies Johnny as her husband and Becky Jo as their daughter; it also names three grandchildren and two great-grandchildren.

## Relationship to Avery — conditional only
If Cordella is proven to be Stanton's mother, then Otha (her son with Bob Whittle) would be Stanton's maternal half-brother and a half-uncle of Avery. Otha's descendants would then be part of Avery's extended collateral family. At present, **Cordella's parentage of Stanton is unproven**, so this is not added as an established half-sibling relationship.
