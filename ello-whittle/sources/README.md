## Official and contemporary obituary sources

- [Charlotte Ann (Mulvey) Whittle, 2025 obituary](https://www.charleswsmithandsonsfuneralhome.com/obituaries/charlotte-whittle) — Avery's wife; confirms their eight children and family life in New Castle.
- [Theresa Lynn Whittle, 2016 obituary](https://www.charleswsmithandsonsfuneralhome.com/obituaries/theresa-whittle) — names Avery as father and identifies siblings/in-laws and descendants.
- [Norma Jean (Whittle) Wilson, 2017 official funeral-home obituary](https://www.bernardfuneralhome.com/obituary/Norma-Wilson) — independently names Stanton B. and Louvienie McGowan Whittle as her parents.
- [Grace (Whittle) Eastham, 2018 obituary archive](https://wvlc.com/whve-obits/archives/2018-11/) — names Stanton B. and Louvenia McGowan Whittle as her parents and Veldean Beckner as her sister.
- [Veldene Beckner, 2020 obituary archive](https://wvlc.com/whve-obits/archives/2020-10/) — names Stanten/Stanton and Louvenia McGowan Whittle as her parents.
- [Rebecca Ann Whittle, 2008 obituary](https://obits.dallasnews.com/us/obituaries/dallasmorningnews/name/rebecca-whittle-obituary?id=25026934) — names Jeff and Donna Whittle as her parents and Avery as her grandfather.
- [The Passing of Cordella Whittle, 1951 newspaper obituary transcription](https://theshygenealogist.wordpress.com/page/3/) — names R. S. “Bob” Whittle as husband and five of six reported children. The original clipping still needs inspection for the omitted sixth child's name.

## Research note index

See the [research-notes folder](../research-notes/) for full transcriptions, source evaluations, conflicting evidence, descendant branch studies and unresolved record requests.

- [FamilySearch, Kentucky Deaths, 1911–1967](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/1417491) — indexed state death certificates with images; use to seek Stanton Bell Whittle (1972), Louvenia McGowan Whittle (1940) and Eli (Ell) Tucker (reported 1915 or 1919).
- [FamilySearch, Kentucky Vital Record Indexes, 1911–1999](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/2531527) — state indexes that may help identify the filing year/certificate number before obtaining the image.
- [Kentucky Office of Vital Statistics, genealogy guidance](https://www.chfs.ky.gov/agencies/dph/dehp/vsb/Pages/genealogy.aspx) — confirms the state index is derivative and original certificates are held by the Office of Vital Statistics.

- [NARA, 1940 Census FAQs](https://www.archives.gov/research/census/1940/faqs) and [1940 Census research guide](https://www.archives.gov/research/census/1940/start-research) — the 1940 census has no NARA name index; original schedules are free to browse by ED. The Stanton household search is narrowed to Russell County EDs 104-6 through 104-9.

- [KDLA, County Records Inventory (Russell County row)](https://kdla.ky.gov/Archives-and-Reference/Documents/Inventory_County_Records.pdf) — lists Russell County birth registers for 1852–1859, 1874–1875, 1878, 1903–1906 and 1909–1910; **no 1892 or 1893 birth register is listed**. The inventory is explicitly not comprehensive, but it means an 1893 Russell County birth register has not been identified in KDLA's published holdings. The 1893 date for Stanton remains derivative until an original source is found. This does not affect the separate **1878 Cordelia Tucker birth register**, which is confirmed on roll #994054 below.

- [KDLA, County Records Inventory](https://kdla.ky.gov/Archives-and-Reference/Documents/Inventory_County_Records.pdf) — Russell County birth records include 1878; see the [Russell County Births register guide](https://theshygenealogist.wordpress.com/births/) for the five-page Ancestry register and its parent-name fields. This may identify Cordella Tucker's parents directly.

- [KDLA, Vital Statistics Microfilm Inventory](https://kdla.ky.gov/Archives-and-Reference/Documents/Vitals%20Microfilm-Combo%20List.pdf), Russell County, **roll #994054**, birth register includes 1878; [Kenton County Public Library local microfilm copy](https://www.kentonlibrary.org/microfilm-kentucky-vital-statistics-sutton-records-birth-marriage-and-death-records-1852-1907/) lists Russell County 1852–1904 as **353.59 K372v, Reel 25**. The 1878 entry for Cordelia/Cordella Tucker is the direct next parentage record to inspect.

- [1880 Russell County, Kentucky census, NARA microfilm T9-441](https://archive.org/stream/10thcensus0441unit) — Internet Archive item 10thcensus0441unit; Russell County images 147–304, with EDs 98–103 mapped in [The Shy Genealogist's guide](https://theshygenealogist.wordpress.com/2017/11/07/russell-county-resources-1880-census/). Search the index for Eli Tucker/Cordelia Tucker, then inspect the original household image for relationships and parent birthplaces.

- [NARA Record Group 548, U.S. Army Forces in the Caribbean](https://www.archives.gov/research/guide-fed-records/groups/548.html) — includes Panama Canal Department records, 1940–1947, and U.S. Army Caribbean records, 1946–1964. Potential command/unit-record route for Avery's 1946 enlistment category; not proof of an individual assignment.
- [WWII Army Enlistment Records codebook](https://www.ksgenweb.org/archives/statewide/military/wwII/enlist/WWIIarmy.html) — code 6 means “Enlistment for the Panama Canal Department”; this is a term-of-enlistment field, not proof of actual station or unit.

- [NARA, Official Military Personnel File (OMPF) access](https://www.archives.gov/veterans/military-service-records/service-record) — Avery's 9 Jul 1947 Army discharge is more than 62 years ago, so his OMPF is archival and open to the public; any requester may order it for a copying fee. Service number 35888669 is available. No paid request submitted.
