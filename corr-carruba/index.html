Life Story

The 1982 Hamilton County marriage license for Dell Michael Fields names his parents as “Dell Fields” and “Corr. Carruba.” Dell Michael’s 2012 obituary calls his mother Cora Fields, and Clyde Mullins’s 2022 funeral-home obituary names his wife as “Cora Fields Mullins (nee Carubba).” Together, these sources strongly support that “Corr. Carruba” refers to Cora (Carubba) Fields Mullins. The original abbreviated/spelling form is retained rather than silently corrected.

The 1982 license documents the two as co-parents of Dell Michael; it does not establish their marriage. Clyde’s obituary names Gary, Joy, Tony, and the late Michael as his stepchildren. Dell Michael’s obituary names Garry Allen, Dave Anthony, and Joy Dianne as his siblings. These sources support at least three other children in Cora’s family, but the name lists do not fully reconcile and none of the siblings is assigned to Dell Fields Sr. as a biological child without a direct record.

Cora’s own parents, siblings, birth/death details, and a verified portrait remain unidentified. FamilySearch searches did not produce a credible Cora Carubba/Carruba match; a Cora Lee Fields in the 1950 Letcher household is Dell Fields’s sister and is a different person.
