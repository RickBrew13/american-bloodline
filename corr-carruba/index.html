Life Story

The 1982 Hamilton County marriage license for Dell Michael Fields names his parents as “Dell Fields” and “Corr. Carruba.” Dell Michael’s 2012 obituary calls his mother Cora Fields, and Clyde Mullins’s 2022 funeral-home obituary names his wife as “Cora Fields Mullins (nee Carubba).” A Kentucky birth-index transcription adds an important lead: it says Cora Carrubba was born 22 September 1934 in Harlan County to Mary Schillace, and it lists Cora Carrubba as mother of Dale M. Fields (9 March 1954, Kenton County) and Joy D. Fields (29 March 1957, Harlan County). The Dale birth date matches Dell Michael’s date of birth on his marriage license, and Joy D. aligns with the Joy Dianne named as his sister. This evidence strongly supports identifying that birth-index Cora with Dell Michael’s mother and supports Mary Schillace as Cora’s mother, at about 75% working confidence.

The transcription does not name Cora’s father. The original birth record or a separate direct record has not yet been inspected. I have therefore linked Mary Schillace as a supported mother lead, left the father explicitly unidentified, and created no father profile from guesswork. “Corr. Carruba” is preserved exactly as read on the license. The 1982 license documents Dell Fields and Cora as co-parents of Dell Michael; it does not establish that they married.

Clyde’s obituary names stepchildren Gary, Joy, Tony, and the late Michael; Dell Michael’s obituary names siblings Garry Allen, Dave Anthony, and Joy Dianne. These notices support other children in Cora’s family, but do not establish the siblings’ biological fathers or fully reconcile each name form.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Cora Carrubba born 22 September 1934 to Mary Schillace, Dale M. Fields born 9 March 1954, and Joy D. Fields born 29 March 1957 are transcribed on the same Kentucky birth-index source page. Dell Michael’s 1982 license supplies his matching birth date and the original wording “Corr. Carruba”; his 2012 obituary gives mother Cora Fields and sister Joy Dianne; Clyde’s 2022 obituary gives wife Cora Fields Mullins née Carubba. This strongly supports the identity reconciliation.
  • Mary Schillace is the maternal name stated in the derivative birth-index transcription. Multiple unrelated/same-name women appear in census and obituary results, so Cora’s mother has not yet been matched to one of their identities; her parentage is not established beyond the index transcription.
  • Cora’s father remains unknown: the available transcript omits him, and no source reviewed identifies him. Do not infer from Cora’s surname.
  • Clyde Mullins is documented as Cora’s husband by his 2022 obituary. No marriage date/place has been found.
  • Dell Fields is documented as father/co-parent of Dell Michael, but the 1982 license is their son’s marriage license; no Cora–Dell marriage has been established.
  • Clyde’s stepchild list and Dell Michael’s sibling list support other children in Cora’s family but do not prove biological fatherhood or fully reconcile name forms.
  • The 1950 census-index result for Cora Carruba born about 1902, wife of Norman Carruba, is a different older woman. Cora Lee Fields in Arlie and Maudie Fields’s 1950 Letcher household is Dell Fields Sr.’s sister, not Cora Carubba.
