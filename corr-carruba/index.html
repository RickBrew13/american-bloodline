Life Story

The 1982 Hamilton County marriage license for Dell Michael Fields names his parents as “Dell Fields” and “Corr. Carruba.” Dell Michael’s 2012 obituary calls his mother Cora Fields, and Clyde Mullins’s 2022 funeral-home obituary names his wife as “Cora Fields Mullins (nee Carubba).” A Kentucky birth-index transcription adds an important lead: it says Cora Carrubba was born 22 September 1934 in Harlan County to Mary Schillace, and it lists Cora Carrubba as mother of Dale M. Fields (9 March 1954, Kenton County) and Joy D. Fields (29 March 1957, Harlan County). The Dale birth date matches Dell Michael’s date of birth on his marriage license, and Joy D. aligns with the Joy Dianne named as his sister. This evidence strongly supports identifying that birth-index Cora with Dell Michael’s mother and supports Mary Schillace as Cora’s mother, at about 75% working confidence.

Her parents are now identified. The 1940 census lists Cora, 5, as the daughter of Joe Carruba and Mary Carruba at Cumberland, Harlan County, and her own marriage certificate — she married Dell Fields on 28 January 1953 at Jonesville, Virginia — names her father as Joseph Carruba and her mother as Carmella Schillaci. Mary and Carmella are one woman: Mary in the censuses and her 1922 marriage to Joseph, Carmella her middle name. “Corr. Carruba” is preserved exactly as read on the license.

Clyde’s obituary names stepchildren Gary, Joy, Tony, and the late Michael; Dell Michael’s obituary names siblings Garry Allen, Dave Anthony, and Joy Dianne. These notices support other children in Cora’s family, but do not establish the siblings’ biological fathers or fully reconcile each name form.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Cora's parents are Joseph "Joe" Carruba and Mary Carmella Schillaci. The 1940 census lists Cora, 5, as their daughter, and her 1953 Virginia marriage certificate names Joseph Carruba as her father and Carmella Schillaci as her mother. The Kentucky birth-index transcription names only "Mary Schillace" and no father.
  • Mary and Carmella are one woman: "Mary" in the censuses and her 1922 marriage, "Carmella" her middle name. See her profile for the full evidence and the same-name women who are not her.
  • Cora and Dell Fields married 28 January 1953 at Jonesville, Lee County, Virginia. The 1982 license for their son is not their marriage record; the Virginia certificate is.
  • Mary's own birth date and parents are not established; the family tree's 14 May 1905 Sicily birth and 19 October 1978 Birmingham death are unproved. Joseph's own parents are not named in any record found.
  • No 1950 census household has been found for Cora, Joseph or Mary in Harlan County under Carruba or Carubba.
  • No marriage record has been found for Cora and Clyde Mullins; his 2022 obituary is the record of that marriage. No death record or obituary has been found for Cora herself; she was alive in 2007 and 2022, and would be 92 in November 2026.
  • The 1950 census-index result for Cora Carruba born about 1902, wife of Norman Carruba, is a different older woman. Cora Lee Fields in Arlie and Maudie Fields’s 1950 Letcher household is Dell Fields Sr.’s sister, not Cora Carubba.
  • Clyde’s stepchild list and Dell Michael’s sibling list support other children in Cora’s family but do not prove biological fatherhood or fully reconcile name forms.
