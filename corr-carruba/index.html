Life Story

Cora is named as Dell Michael Fields’s mother on his 1982 Hamilton County marriage license, but the license transcribes her name as “Corr. Carruba.” His 2012 obituary names his mother as Cora Fields. A 2022 obituary for Clyde Mullins identifies his wife as “Cora Fields Mullins (nee Carubba)” and names the late Michael among Clyde’s stepchildren.

These sources strongly support that the license’s “Corr. Carruba” is Cora (Carubba) Fields Mullins. The source forms are preserved rather than silently normalized. The 1982 license establishes co-parentage of Dell Michael, not that Cora and Dell Fields were married. Clyde’s obituary names other stepchildren, consistent with the siblings listed in Dell Michael’s obituary; their biological fathers remain unknown.

Cora’s own birth/death details, parents, and siblings have not been identified. Exact-name FamilySearch search results for Cora Carubba/Carrubba in Ohio did not return her; that index-search outcome is not proof she is absent from the records.
