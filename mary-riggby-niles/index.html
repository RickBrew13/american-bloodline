Life Story

Mary Riggby Niles is known only as the mother of Leon Niles. Leon’s Social Security NUMIDENT record names “Major Niles — Parent” and “Mary Riggby — Parent.” Leon was born 27 November 1908 in Stockton, Alabama, and died 19 May 2004 in Birmingham. Her maiden name, Riggby, is as named in that record. Nothing else is yet known about Mary — no birth, death, marriage, or residence record has been found, and no tree profile for her has been verified.
