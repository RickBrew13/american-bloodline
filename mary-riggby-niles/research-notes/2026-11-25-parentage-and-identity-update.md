# Mary Riggby Niles — parentage and identity research update

**Date:** 25 November 2026

## Finding

FamilySearch’s [U.S. Social Security NUMIDENT entry for Leon Niles](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:6K7Q-ZC4F) gives Leon’s birth as 27 November 1908 in Stockton, Alabama, and names Mary Riggby and Major Niles under “Parents and Siblings.” Its linked [Mary Riggby detail](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:6K7Q-ZC45) explicitly describes Leon as Mary’s son and Major Niles as her spouse. The linked [Major Niles detail](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:6K7Q-ZC42) reciprocally lists Mary as spouse and Leon as son.

These are related views of one NUMIDENT correspondence entry, not three independent records. The entry reports a Social Program Application Date of 17 October 1990 and Claim Date of 19 July 1975; it has no image and gives no biographical dates or details for Mary. It is the only located evidence for her parentage of Leon. The source display associates Mary and Major as spouses, but no separate civil marriage record has been found.

The census age implies about 1910, versus the NUMIDENT’s exact 1908 birth; keep that discrepancy explicit.

- Public FamilySearch indexed searches for Mary Riggby/Rigby in census and Alabama marriage/death collections, including targeted Major–Mary searches, returned no matching record in the access path used. The results are bounded index searches, not proof that records do not exist.
- Find a Grave exact-name searches did not identify a plausible Alabama memorial for Mary Riggby. The exact spelling results were unrelated older England memorials; a broad Mary Rigby Alabama search returned many same-name results without a verified identity bridge. None is assigned.
