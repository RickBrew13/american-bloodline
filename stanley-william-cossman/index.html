Life Story

Stanley William Cossman (3 June 1948–26 August 2017) was born in Cincinnati to Stanley Peter and Beulah Mae Cornett Cossman. His Hickman-Strunk obituary describes him as a U.S. Army veteran, Goshen United Methodist Church member, and Montgomery Lodge #94 member; he loved old cars and motorcycles, told jokes, and especially enjoyed his grandchildren. He was preceded in death by his wife Kathryn Coffey Cossman and sister Linda Nelson. The obituary names sons Samuel, Matthew, Daniel and daughter Ashley. It establishes the children as Stanley’s, but it does not identify their mothers. Kathryn’s obituary identifies Samuel and Matthew as her stepsons. Monica’s relationship to Sam remains unverified.
