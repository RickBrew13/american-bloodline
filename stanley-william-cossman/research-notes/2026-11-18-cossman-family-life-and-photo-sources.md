## Life-story evidence from obituaries
Stanley William Cossman (3 Jun 1948–26 Aug 2017): born Cincinnati to Stanley Peter Cossman and Beulah Mae Cornett Cossman; U.S. Army veteran; Goshen United Methodist Church and Montgomery Lodge #94 member; enjoyed old cars, motorcycles, joke-telling, and time with grandchildren. Hickman-Strunk obituary names his children Samuel, Matthew, Daniel, and Ashley and says his wife Kathryn predeceased him. Legacy obituary calls her late wife Kate (Coffey) Cossman. The Hickman-Strunk/FindaGrave text dates his death 26 Aug 2017; Find a Grave vital-data header says 27 Aug, so retain that discrepancy rather than silently changing it.

Kathryn (Kate) Coffey Cossman (5 Sep 1952–10 Aug 2016): born Oneida, Tennessee, daughter of Clyde Estill and Dora (Dobbs) Coffey; member of Cherokee United Baptist Church; enjoyed cooking, caring for grandchildren, family gatherings, and caretaking for family. Her Hickman-Strunk obituary names husband Stanley, children Daniel and Ashley, and stepsons Samuel and Matthew. Keep Sam's birth mother unproved; do not transfer stepmother information to Monica or treat Stanley's obituary as proof of Sam's mother.
