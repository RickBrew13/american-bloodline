# Research note — 25 November 2026

Find a Grave provides Maggie Marie Mooney McFadden’s birth/death dates (19 Jan 1909–27 Jun 1943), Nicholson Cemetery burial and a readable headstone photo. Laura Lee Johnson’s obituary says she was oldest of three children born to Abe and Maggie and names brother Abe Jr. and sister Etta Fay Marcum; Abe’s 1964 obituary independently says his first wife Maggie died in 1943. Find a Grave links her father Lee B. Mooney but no mother. The Finda Grave-linked siblings are listed with source caveat; no separate sibling profiles created.
