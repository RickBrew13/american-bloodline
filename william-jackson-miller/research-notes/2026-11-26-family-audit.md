# William Jackson Miller — source and family audit (26 Nov 2026)

Find a Grave memorial 83338792 reports William born 21 December 1861 in Kentucky and died 29 December 1948 at Bryan, Russell County, Kentucky; burial at Hadley Cemetery. It links him to Sophia J. Kimbler Miller (marriage year given as 1889) and later Daisy Pearl Sullivan Miller (marriage year given as 1921). Family links list Effie M. Miller Barnes and Orla Lee Miller, plus Lacy Eugene and Roy C. Miller as children. Treat these as contributor-entered relationships pending original records. Effie/Orla are linked on Sophia’s memorial; Lacy/Roy appear with William’s later wife.

Find a Grave lists William Jackson Miller (1816–1861) and Elizabeth E. Warriner Shearer as his parents, and Mary Lou Miller Melson as a sibling; those claims have not been independently checked.

Photo: marker image on memorial 83338792, credited there to Judy Lynn, added 30 May 2012. It reads “MILLER / WILLIAM J.” and is used as a headstone image, not a portrait.
