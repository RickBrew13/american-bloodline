Life Story

Elizabeth Russell married Peter Stiner in Union County, Tennessee, on Christmas Eve 1856 DOCUMENTED. The family register gives her birthday as 10 May but leaves the year blank; census ages point to about 1840 SUPPORTED.

She raised eight children in Union County, among them Sarah Jane, who married Winfield Scott Green DOCUMENTED. Her stone in Moyers Cemetery gives her death as 1888 UNPROVED.
