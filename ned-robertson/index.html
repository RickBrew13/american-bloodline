Family Relationship Notes

Pansy Robertson’s 13 February 1930 Kentucky marriage record names Ned Robertson as her father and Viola Robertson as her mother. The 1910 census lists Ned, 22, and his wife Viola, 18, living together in Floyd, Newberry County, South Carolina, so the two were married by 1910, though no marriage record has been found. The 1910 census gives Ned’s birth year as about 1888 and both his parents’ birthplaces as South Carolina, but it does not name his parents.

No birth, death, or other record for Ned has been found beyond the 1910 census and his daughter’s marriage record. His parents are not named on any record, and no other children besides Pansy have been found.

Life Story

Ned Robertson was born in South Carolina about 1888. By 1910 he was married to Viola Robertson and living in Floyd, Newberry County, South Carolina. His daughter Pansy was born in South Carolina about 1910–1913 and married Herman Washington in Newport, Kentucky, on 13 February 1930; the marriage record names Ned as her father. No birth, death, or other record for Ned has been found, and his parents are not named on any record.
