# Joseph Leslie Collins — Ohio namesake exclusion
**Date:** 25 November 2026  
**Subject:** Alabama-born Joseph Leslie Collins, born 22 January 1918 in Mobile, married Ruby Mae Pugh in Birmingham on 15 November 1939.

## New namesake lead examined
An exact-name web search found an obituary for **Joseph Leslie Collins**, husband of **Alberdine M. Heil/Collins**, who died 5 August 2004. Alberdine's obituary states that they married on 3 April 1948 and had 56 years of marriage:
https://www.lindseyfh.com/obituaries/alberdine-collins

At first this looked potentially significant: the name is exact, the reported 1918 birth year is compatible, and the Ohio marriage occurs after Ruby Mae Pugh's documented 1945 marriage to George Howard Moore. I traced the Ohio man's identity before attaching anything.

## Identity bridge proving this is a different Joseph
A compiled Tuscarawas County genealogy, with citations to original census schedules, identifies the Ohio Joseph as:
- born in 1918 in **Ohio**;
- son of **John Z. Collins** and **Martha Tanzie**;
- age 21, single, born Ohio, in his father John Z. Collins's household in Dennison, Tuscarawas County, in 1940;
- age 31, married, born Ohio, and son-in-law in Elizabeth M. Heil's household in Dennison in 1950, with Alberdine M. Collins.

Source: https://www.ra-go.com/g0/p396.htm

This is incompatible with the Alabama Joseph's original 1939 marriage image, which states he was born **22 January 1918 in Mobile, Alabama**, lived at 1st Street in Birmingham, and was already married to Ruby Mae Pugh in November 1939. The Ohio man was still single in the 1940 census and was born in Ohio. The full name and year alone are not enough to merge them.

**Conclusion: positively excluded as a namesake.** Do not attach the Ohio Joseph's 1948 marriage, Alberdine, children, 2004 death, Ohio residence or St. Mary's Cemetery burial to the Alabama-born Joseph.

## Current Alabama Joseph identity and remaining gap
The Alabama man's identity remains supported by:
1. Original 15 November 1939 Birmingham marriage image: Joseph Collins, age 21, born 22 January 1918 in Mobile, occupation porter, residence 1st Street, Birmingham; parents Joseph Collins and Sarah Johnson.
2. 1940 Birmingham census entry: Joseph Collins, age 20, married, nephew in Roger and Rosa Pugh's household; Ruby Collins, age 19, married, niece.
3. Daughter Leslie's 1940 NUMIDENT: parents Joseph Collins and Ruby M. Pugh.
4. Leslie's 1959 marriage index: father Joseph Leslie Collins, mother Ruby Mae Pugh.
5. Ruby's 1945 marriage to George Howard Moore.

No attributable later-life death, obituary, burial, military or property record for the Alabama-born Joseph has been found. The Ohio candidate is now resolved and excluded, but it does not solve the Alabama man's later life. The 1940 Joe Collins with mother Sara Rogers remains excluded because it conflicts with the known marriage and mother name; the age 72 Sara Rogers would also have been about 51 at the 1918 birth, making the mother assignment implausible.
