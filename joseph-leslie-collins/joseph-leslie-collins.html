Life Story

Joseph Leslie Collins is supported as the 21-year-old Joseph Collins who married Ruby Mae Pugh on 15 November 1939: the marriage index names Joseph Collins and Sarah Johnson as the groom’s parents, Leslie’s NUMIDENT names Joseph Collins as her father, and her 1959 marriage index names Joseph Leslie Collins as her father. The FamilySearch public tree gives Joseph Leslie the same parents. The original 1939 marriage image was read on 20 November 2026: it gives his birth as 22 January 1918 in Mobile, Alabama, his residence as 1st Street, Birmingham, and his occupation as porter, and it confirms his parents as Joseph Collins and Sarah Johnson. The 1940 census finds him with Ruby in Birmingham in the household of her uncle Roger Pugh. No later-life record for Joseph Leslie has yet been identified.
