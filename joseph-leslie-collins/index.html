Life Story

Joseph Leslie Collins was born in Alabama about 1918, the son of Joseph Collins and Sarah Johnson SUPPORTED. On 15 November 1939 he married Ruby Mae Pugh DOCUMENTED, and their daughter Leslie Mae was born in Birmingham the next July DOCUMENTED. The marriage did not last: by February 1945 Ruby had married George Howard Moore DOCUMENTED. Joseph’s later life and death have not yet been found.
