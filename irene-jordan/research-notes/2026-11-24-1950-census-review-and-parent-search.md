# Irene Jordan — 1950 census source review and parent-search checkpoint

**Reviewed:** 24 November 2026

## Household evidence

The National Archives 1950 Census original population schedule for Cincinnati, Hamilton County, Ohio, enumeration district **91-829**, sheet **44A**, was reviewed. The household is on lines 14–16: Ray W. Jordan (head, age 38, birthplace Kentucky), Irene Jordan (wife, age 33, birthplace Ohio), and Richard L. Jordan (son, age 6, birthplace Ohio). FamilySearch indexes the enumeration date as **10 April 1950** and cites Ray on line 14, Irene on line 15, and Richard on line 16. This supports the couple and child household, but it does not name Ray’s or Irene’s parents or Irene’s maiden name.

### Handwriting and occupation cautions

The handwritten name entry for Ray is not perfectly clear at this scan size. FamilySearch’s index renders it **Ray W Jordan**; do not change his name to “Ray Jr” or treat a middle initial/suffix as newly proved from this image alone. The industry entry is legible as **Express Co**; the occupation wording is unclear and should not be normalized from the scan.

## Parent-search checkpoint

The 1930 and 1940 FamilySearch collection/residence searches attempted in this pass did not return a matching record in the public results view. That is a search outcome only—not proof that Ray or Irene is absent from those censuses. No verified parent couple, Irene maiden name, marriage record, death record, or attributable portrait has been established. The prior project’s potential link between this child Richard L. and the adult Richard Lee Jordan remains probable, not conclusive.

Next useful evidence: identify Ray in 1930/1940 (especially any household member named Ray senior if the suffix can be verified), identify Irene’s maiden name from a marriage or child-birth record, and then trace each person to a pre-marriage household. Keep namesakes separate; do not add parents to Gen VII until the identity bridge is documented.
