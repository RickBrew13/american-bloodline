# Irene Jordan — Everhart family lead (not yet linked conclusively)

**Reviewed:** 24 November 2026

## Candidate identity

A strong candidate for the Irene Jordan in Ray W. Jordan's 1950 household is **Irene Everhart Jordan**, listed by Find a Grave as born 31 Dec 1916 and died 17 Feb 1992, both in Cincinnati, Hamilton County, Ohio, buried at Mount Moriah Cemetery, Withamsville, Clermont County, Ohio. This fits the 1950 census index for Ray's wife Irene (age 33, estimated birth about 1917, birthplace Ohio) and her later surname Jordan. The birth/death/cemetery memorial is derivative and user-contributed; it does **not** state that Ray W. Jordan was her spouse. Treat as a strong working lead, not an established identity.

## Independent corroborating lead

FamilySearch's public 1950 census search returns a **Hazel Everhart**, age/birth year about 1895, born Ohio, living in Cincinnati, Hamilton County, Ohio, on 10 April 1950. The exact public search used was: https://www.familysearch.org/search/record/results?count=100&q.givenName=Hazel&q.surname=Everhart&q.birthLikeDate.from=1890&q.birthLikeDate.to=1900&q.birthLikePlace=Ohio. This is consistent with, but does not independently prove, the memorial's Hazel Marie Henderson Everhart; the original census image and household relationships have not yet been inspected.

A Campbell County Evergreen Cemetery transcription states that a child, Sue Carol Jordan, born and died in Cincinnati in February 1957, was daughter of “Roy & Irene (Everhart).” Source: https://www.usgenwebsites.org/KYCampbell/evergreenJ.htm (entry indexed under Jordan). This suggests a possible later Roy Jordan marriage/relationship for an Irene Everhart, but it does not identify her as the same Irene from Ray's 1950 household; keep it as a separate lead until records bridge the identities.
