Life Story

Irene A. Everhart was born in Cincinnati on 31 December 1916, the daughter of Charles H. Everhart and Hazel M. Henderson. As a girl she lived with her parents and her brothers and sisters in Akron, where the family was counted in the 1920 census, and in Deer Park Village in Hamilton County, where they were counted in 1930. She married Roy W. Jordan before April 1940. In the 1940 census she and Roy were living in Cincinnati, where she worked as a book binder for a book manufacturing company. Their son Richard L. Jordan was born in Cincinnati on 16 June 1943. In the 1950 census the family was still in Cincinnati, where Irene worked as an office cleaner. She died in Cincinnati on 17 February 1992.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Her middle initial is “A” in the 1920 census and on the family tree, and “T” in the 1940 census; the records do not explain the difference.
  • Her surname is spelled “Everhardt” in the 1920 census.
  • No marriage record for Roy Jordan and Irene Everhart has been found on FamilySearch; the 1940 census shows they were already married by 8 April 1940.
  • Listings seen in search results name Mount Moriah Cemetery, Withamsville, Clermont County, Ohio, as her burial place; the listings have not been opened in full, so burial is still listed as unknown.
