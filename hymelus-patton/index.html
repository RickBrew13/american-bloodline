Life Story

Hymelus Patton was born 1 August 1802 in Orange County, North Carolina, by the Orange County court minutes. The minutes record a boy named Hemelius Patton born that day, bound out as an apprentice in May 1810 to Luke Peacock to learn farming; the indenture was cancelled in November 1812, and he was bound to John Lankston in May 1813 until he came of age, about August 1823. Whether that boy is this Hymelus is supported, not proved: the name is vanishingly rare — no second bearer has surfaced — and the dates fit, free at twenty-one in August 1823 and married in Orange County in October 1824, aged twenty-two. The censuses give about 1804–05 instead, and the compiled 1795 is contradicted by both censuses.

He married Nancy Woods in Orange County on 26 October 1824. Four children were born in North Carolina — Mary about 1826, William about 1828, Alexander about 1829–1832, and Jesse Y. about 1834 — and the family had moved to Tennessee by 1850. Family tradition puts the move between about 1835 and 1840, which the census window, about 1834–1850, allows but does not prove. He farmed: $100 in real estate in 1850, none in 1860, $100 in personal estate. The 1850 census, taken 24 October 1850 in Bledsoe County, finds the household of six — Hymelus, Nancy, and the four children, all born in North Carolina.

Nancy Woods died in the 1850s, before about August 1859. Hymelus married again, to a second wife also named Nancy, about 1858–60; no marriage record survives — Cumberland County’s pre-1905 marriage records were destroyed in the 1905 courthouse fire. The 1860 census, taken 10 July 1860 in Cumberland County, finds him with the second Nancy, 32, born in Tennessee, and their infant daughter Sarah F., two months old. None of the four older children remained in the household.

He enrolled 11 October 1861 as a private in Company G, 2nd Tennessee Volunteer Infantry, Union, and died 10 August 1862 of disease contracted in service, in the line of duty. The death place, Cumberland Gap, is supported, not documented. His widow applied for a pension on 12 November 1867 and was granted one from 25 May 1868. No portrait of Hymelus exists — not in the folder, and not on any tree site checked — and no grave has been located.

  • 1850 enumeration date: the manuscript image reads 24 October 1850; the transcription’s “26 August 1850” is wrong for this sheet.
  • Alexander: the 1850 image reads his age as 26, the transcription as 21; the gravestone gives his birth as 1 October 1831; the memorial says he died 9 October 1891, the stone reads July 9, 1891 — the day is open.
  • Sarah F.’s age in 1860: 2 months by the image and the transcription, 9 months by the Encyclopedia of Scott County — the image controls.
