Life Story

Helen E. Rich was the daughter of James Bernard Rich and Ida Mae Clark, who married 3 February 1897 in Hamilton County. Her maiden name is verified: Social Security applications name "RICH, HELEN" as the mother of Kathleen and Margaret.

A Cincinnati birth-records pull found no Helen Rich birth from 1896 to 1899, and the family was in Newport, Kentucky, by 1900 — Newport and Campbell County records are the lead for her birth. Her birth year is left open: an 1897 header, a 1907 narrative, November 1898 records, and a 30 November 1897 lead have not been reconciled.

She married John Thomas Williams. No marriage record has been found. They had four children: Mary Jane, Kathleen Rita, Margaret "Margy May," and John Thomas Jr. She died about April 1967. No photograph of her has been found, and no Find a Grave memorial was found for her.
