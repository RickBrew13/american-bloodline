Life Story

Salvatore Cincimino was born 30 April 1881 in Corleone, Sicily, to Pasquale Cincimino and Francesca Perone. He entered New York on 21 June 1904 on the vessel North America, from Palermo, and married Ignazia Carbonaro in New York in 1906. Their four children, all born in New York City, were Frances (18 February 1910), Josephine (3 February 1912), Rosalie (29 September 1919), and Pasquale (25 March 1922). He died 18 April 1948 in Brooklyn. The compiled tree had given his birth as 19 March 1879 in Sciacca and his parents as Salvadore Ciancimino and Antonina Marotta; both are refuted by his 1933 Declaration of Intention and his 1906 marriage record. The declaration names Frances, born 18 February 1910, as his daughter; the family tree carries her as Dominick Guglielmo’s wife.
