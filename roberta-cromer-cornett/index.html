Family Relationship Notes

Roberta’s public FamilySearch profile reports her vital dates and places, her 23 December 1925 marriage to William McKinley Cornett, and five children. Her Find a Grave memorial also lists William as spouse and Beulah as a child. William’s memorial likewise lists Roberta and Beulah. These are compiled family sources; original vital records have not been inspected in this review.

A Kentucky birth-index transcription lists Roberta, born 15 August 1908 in Rockcastle County, with mother Sarah Bradley (volume 233, file 49389). A Rockcastle marriage index lists Henry C. Cromer and Sarah E. Bradley as married on 2 August 1890. A compiled Rockcastle family transcription identifies the groom as Henry Martin “Hen” Cromer and the bride as Sarah Louisa Bradley, and names them as Roberta's parents. Together these sources strongly support the parental couple, but the birth index itself does not name Roberta's father and the full middle names remain compiled. The same Kentucky birth index lists Dora A. Cromer, born 27 August 1899 in Rockcastle County, with mother Sarah Bradley; she may be a sister, but no original census or birth record explicitly links Dora and Roberta as siblings. Her Find a Grave gallery includes a couple photograph captioned “William & Roberta Cornett,” a marker image, and several other images whose identities are not independently verified. All existing gallery images are retained.

Roberta’s earlier surname was Cromer; Cornett is used after her marriage. The available notes mention a later spouse on her memorial, but no later spouse is added to the Spouses card without further review of the underlying record.
