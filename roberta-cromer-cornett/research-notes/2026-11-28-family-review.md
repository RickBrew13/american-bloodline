# Roberta Cromer Cornett — family review

**Date:** 28 November 2026

## Parents and siblings

Roberta’s parents are best identified as Henry Martin “Hen” Cromer and Sarah Louisa Bradley (high confidence, about 90%). The 1908 Kentucky birth-index transcription names mother Sarah Bradley but not her father. The 1910 original census independently places Roberta (indexed “Crumes,” age 1) in Henry and Sarah L. Crumes’ household, alongside the older Cromer children; this is the strongest household link to both parents. The 1890 marriage index records Henry C. Cromer and Sarah E. Bradley, while FamilySearch and the Rockcastle compiled family page expand their names.

The compiled Henry–Sarah child group identifies Roberta’s nine siblings as Margaret May “Maggie” (1891–1979), Rosa Lee (1892–1918), Pearl Helen (1894–1974), Roy C. (1896–1969), Dewey (1898–1979), Dora Anne (1899–1986), Hughie (1901–1979), Ida Cleo (1906–1977), and Austin (1912–1971). The 1910 census directly shows eight children—Maggy, Pearly, Roy, Dewey, Dora A., Hugh, Ida, and Roberta—while Rosa is not present in the indexed household and Austin was not yet born. Preserve the census omissions and variants rather than force a complete count from one record.

## Children and household

The 1925 marriage index records Roberta Cromer and Bill Cornett, but indexes her age as 23 (estimated birth about 1902), conflicting with the 1908 birth-index entry and her age 1 in the 1910 census; do not use the marriage index age to shift her birth year. The 1950 household shows three children resident at that date (Ralph, Sharon, Sandra). FamilySearch’s compiled profile lists five children, also including Beulah Mae and William Jr.; Beulah’s 2009 obituary gives her sibling group. Roberta’s FamilySearch page also lists a later spouse, Leslie Emerson Caudell; no new marriage profile or change to her parentage is inferred from that compilation.

The existing memorial galleries contain a shared photo captioned “William & Roberta Cornett,” used as a couple image, plus individual/other images attached to Roberta’s memorial whose subjects are not all independently identified. No new individual portrait is asserted from those captions.
