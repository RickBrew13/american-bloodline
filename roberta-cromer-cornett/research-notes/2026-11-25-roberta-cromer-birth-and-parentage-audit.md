# Roberta Cromer Cornett — birth, parentage and sibling leads
**Date:** 25 November 2026

## Birth-index evidence
A Kentucky birth-index transcription lists **Roberta Cromer**, born **15 August 1908** in Rockcastle County, Kentucky. The index identifies her mother as **Sarah Bradley** and gives volume **233**, file **49389**. This independently supports the date/place and Sarah's maiden surname shown in the public FamilySearch profile. The original certificate image has not been inspected; the index does not name Roberta's father.
- Kentucky Births, 1908: https://kygenweb.net/knox/Births/births-c.html

## Marriage-index evidence for the reported parents
The Rockcastle County marriage index lists **Henry C. Cromer** and **Sarah E. Bradley** as married on **2 August 1890**. The Rockcastle Cromer family transcription identifies this couple as **Henry Martin “Hen” Cromer** (1 January 1869–15 December 1955) and **Sarah Louisa Bradley** (died 10 January 1925), and lists Roberta among their children. The birth-index entry for Roberta names her mother as Sarah Bradley, tying her to the bride's maiden surname. Together these records strongly support the reported parental couple, but the original 1890 marriage register and Roberta's original birth certificate have not been inspected.
- Rockcastle marriages, surname C: https://kygenweb.net/rockcastle/marriages/c.htm
- Rockcastle Cromer family transcription (submitted by Bob Bray): https://kygenweb.net/rockcastle/family/cromer/

## Possible sibling: Dora A. Cromer
The same Kentucky birth-index transcription lists **Dora A. Cromer**, born **27 August 1899** in Rockcastle County, with mother **Sarah Bradley**, volume 167, file 46752. The shared mother maiden surname, county and Cromer surname make Dora a plausible sister of Roberta. The compiled Rockcastle Cromer family page also lists Dora among Henry and Sarah's children. No original census schedule or birth certificate has yet been inspected to prove the sibling relationship. Do not add Dora as a confirmed sibling in the public profile until the household/parentage is independently corroborated.
- Kentucky Births, 1899: https://kygenweb.net/knox/Births/1873-1899.html
- Kentucky Births, 1908: https://kygenweb.net/knox/Births/births-c.html

## Earlier Cromer ancestry lead
The Rockcastle Cromer family transcription identifies Henry Martin “Hen” Cromer as son of **David Cromer** (born July 1834, Laurel County, Kentucky) and **Elizabeth Southard** (born February 1840, North Carolina). A Rockcastle marriage index separately lists **David Cromer and Elizabeth Southard** married 7 July 1856. This is the leading working great-grandparent couple for Roberta (and great-great-grandparents of Stanley William Cossman), but the original marriage register and census images have not been inspected.
- Rockcastle marriages, surname C: https://kygenweb.net/rockcastle/marriages/c.htm
- Rockcastle Cromer family transcription: https://kygenweb.net/rockcastle/family/cromer/

A 1997 family letter transcribed by Mike Borchardt gives a conflicting account of David's birth year as 1838 and repeats his marriage to Elizabeth Southard. This conflict is noted, not resolved; the 1834 year in the Rockcastle family transcription and the 1838 family letter are both derivative.
- Borchardt/Cromer family letters: https://oocities.org/Heartland/Plains/2559/cromer1.html

## Profile changes made
Roberta's public profile now:
