Family Relationship Notes

The 1898 Warren County birth register names George F. and Etta May Doughman as parents of their son Virgil. Virgil’s 1941 Ohio death record gives his mother’s maiden name as Long. The family stone at West Woodville Cemetery gives Etta’s dates as 25 March 1874–26 October 1932.

Stanley and Floyd are listed in existing research material, but no direct record tying them to George and Etta has been reviewed. Myrtle is reported in the family-stone context and family-tree material. Lola appears in a FamilySearch shared-tree group; her linked profile cites a 1920 census household, but the original image and independent parentage evidence have not been reviewed. Mamie Doughman and Leona Marjorie Doughman are additional shared-tree leads and are not added as established children.

Etta’s parents, siblings, last residence, occupation, and religion are not identified in the material reviewed. Her maiden name Long is reported by her son’s death record; that informant-supplied detail has not been corroborated by a birth or marriage record.

Life Story

Etta May Long was born on 25 March 1874 . Her son Virgil's death record gives her maiden name as Long . She is named with her husband George F. Doughman as a parent of their son Virgil in the 1898 Warren County birth register . She lost her daughter Myrtle in December 1930 and died on 26 October 1932; she shares a stone with George and Myrtle at West Woodville Cemetery .
