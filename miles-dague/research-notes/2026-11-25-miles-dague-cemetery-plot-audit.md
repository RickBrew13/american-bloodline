# Miles Dague — cemetery plot and burial-source audit
**Date:** 25 November 2026

## New cemetery plot evidence
The Franklin County cemetery transcription titled **“Ayers Cemetery Warranty of Deed Received on the 30th of April, 1969”** also contains a separate section titled **“Dague Cemetery Warranty of Deed Received on the 9th of February, 1946.”** Within the Dague Cemetery section, Miles Dague is listed at **Row 7, Plot 10**, with birth date **10/3/1893** and death date **8/8/1935**. This independently corroborates the exact dates already transcribed from his Ohio death certificate and identifies his cemetery plot.
- Source: GenealogyBug, Franklin County Cemeteries, Jefferson Township, “Ayers, Dague and Havens Cemeteries,” page 2: https://genealogybug.net/Franklin_Cemeteries/jefferson/Ayers_Dague_Havens.pdf

The record is a cemetery warranty-of-deed/plot transcription, not the original 1935 interment card or burial register. It does not state the name of the lot owner or the person who purchased the plot. The profile now identifies Row 7, Plot 10 with that limitation. The public profile was re-fetched and verified.

## Existing direct records retained
- Ohio death certificate, file 48181: born 3 October 1893 in Gahanna; died 8 August 1935 at home, 1420 Oxford Street, Columbus; buried 12 August 1935 in Dague Cemetery. FamilySearch: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X6WN-SY7
- Columbus Dispatch obituary, 9 August 1935, page 6A: wife Rose, one son Woodrow Wilson, brother Nelson, sisters Mrs. Ollie Strait, Mrs. Eva Meyers and Miss Roxie Dague.
- Find a Grave memorial 17984765, Miles Dague, Dague Cemetery, Gahanna; headstone photograph credited to David K. Gustafson, 7 May 2006.
- The History and Genealogy of the Dague Family (1938), pages 120–121, identifies Miles as son of Daniel B. Dague and Mary Devore and says he had one child.

## Outstanding discrepancy
Miles's death certificate gives his parents as “David Dague” and “Mary Dimore,” while the county copy, 1938 Dague family history and Find a Grave use Daniel B. Dague and Mary Devore. The cemetery plot record does not resolve the parent-name conflict. The profile retains the discrepancy and does not silently replace the death certificate's wording.
