Family Relationship Notes

Miles’s Ohio death certificate gives his birth and death details, burial date, and cause of death. His obituary in the Columbus Dispatch names his wife Rose, one son Woodrow Wilson, brother Nelson, and sisters Ollie Strait, Eva Meyers, and Roxie Dague. The 1938 Dague family history identifies Miles as a son of Daniel B. Dague and Mary Devore and says he had one child. The family history and memorial material provide the broader sibling list; some siblings and half-sibling links need original-record review.

The marriage to Rose is estimated at about 1913–14 from the census ages and household chronology; no marriage record has been located. Woodrow’s 1936 marriage record names Miles Dague and Rose Ritzer as his parents.

Miles’s birth year differs across sources: 1893 on his death certificate, 1892 on his draft card, and October 1891 in the 1900 census. The death certificate itself gives his parents as “David Dague” and “Mary Dimore,” while the county copy, 1938 family history, and Find a Grave use Daniel and Mary Devore. The discrepancy remains noted rather than silently resolved.

Life Story

Miles Dague was born on 3 October 1893 in Gahanna, Ohio, into the large Dague family of Jefferson Township, the son of Daniel B. Dague and Mary Devore . He married Rose Ritzer, a Columbus girl of German parents, about 1913, and their only child, Woodrow Wilson Dague, was born in 1915 .

Miles worked as a shoemaker at the Kinnear Buggy Co. and later as a furnace setter, and by 1930 the family owned a house on Oxford Street in Columbus . He died by suicide at home on 8 August 1935, aged 41, and was buried in the family's own Dague Cemetery at Gahanna .

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • No marriage record for Miles and Rose has been found. Rose was 18 at marriage per the 1930 census, so about 1913–14; the unindexed Franklin County marriage books for 1913–15 would settle it.
  • His birth year varies: 1893 on his death certificate, 1892 on his draft card, October 1891 in the 1900 census.
  • His death certificate writes his parents as "David Dague" and "Mary Dimore"; the county copy, the 1938 family history, and Find a Grave say Daniel.
  • The 1938 family history says both parents died in 1908 and names his grandparents as Edmund Spenser Dague and Sarah "Sallie" Cook .
  • The cemetery plot transcription places Miles at Dague Cemetery, Row 7, Plot 10; the cemetery's original interment card has not been located.
