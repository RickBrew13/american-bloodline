Life Story

Miles Dague was born on 3 October 1893 in Gahanna, Ohio, into the large Dague family of Jefferson Township, the son of Daniel B. Dague and Mary Devore SUPPORTED. He married Rose Ritzer, a Columbus girl of German parents, about 1913, and their only child, Woodrow Wilson Dague, was born in 1915 SUPPORTED.

Miles worked as a shoemaker at the Kinnear Buggy Co. and later as a furnace setter, and by 1930 the family owned a house on Oxford Street in Columbus DOCUMENTED. He died there on 8 August 1935, aged 41, and was buried in the family's own Dague Cemetery at Gahanna DOCUMENTED.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • No marriage record for Miles and Rose has been found. Rose was 18 at marriage per the 1930 census, so about 1913–14; the unindexed Franklin County marriage books for 1913–15 would settle it.
  • His birth year varies: 1893 on his death certificate, 1892 on his draft card, October 1891 in the 1900 census.
  • His death certificate writes his parents as "David Dague" and "Mary Dimore"; the county copy, the 1938 family history, and Find a Grave say Daniel.
  • The 1938 family history says both parents died in 1908 and names his grandparents as Edmund Spenser Dague and Sarah "Sallie" Cook UNPROVED.
