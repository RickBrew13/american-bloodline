The original 1920 Lincoln County marriage record names John N. Henry and Rebecca Susan Henry as William’s parents. It records his marriage to Lillie Mae Carman on 1 June 1920.

Elmer’s 1934 birth index names Lillie Carman as his mother, and the 1940 census index lists Elmer, age five, as the son of William and Lillie. The original census schedule has not been reviewed for this update.

Lillie’s 2026 obituary index names three children: David Elmer Henry, James A. Henry, and Charlotte Janelle Rice. The index identifies William Pollard Henry as her husband. The available sources do not yet provide a complete, reconciled list of all children. The 1940 census index lists seven other children in the household besides Elmer, while the FamilySearch compiled profile lists eight children but omits Elmer and Charlotte. Do not assume the lists are complete or identical.

Life Story

William Pollard Henry was born on 11 March 1902 in Sadieville, Scott County, Kentucky, the son of John N. Henry and Rebecca Susan Henry. He married Lillie Mae Carman on 1 June 1920 in Lincoln County, Kentucky — he was 18, a farmer living in Arabia, and she was 16, from Waynesburg in Casey County. By 1940 the family had moved to Cincinnati, Ohio, where the census lists William and Lillie with their son Elmer, age 5, and seven other children. Their son Elmer David Henry was born on 6 August 1934 in Lincoln County. William died on 15 November 1989 in Cincinnati, according to the FamilySearch family profile.
