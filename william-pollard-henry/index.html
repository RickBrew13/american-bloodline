Life Story

William Pollard Henry was born in Scott County in 1902, according to his FamilySearch profile. In 1920, at age 18, he married Lillie May Carmen in Lincoln County; the marriage record names John N. Henry and Rebecca Susan Henry as his parents and records him as a farmer. The family later lived in Cincinnati. The 1940 census index lists Elmer, age five, as his son, and seven other children in the household. His full child list remains a source-by-source reconstruction, not a settled single tree roster.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Parentage is unusually well supported: the 1920 marriage-record index expressly names John N. Henry and Rebecca Susan Henry as William’s parents. This is a transcript of the original register; image rereview remains desirable.
  • The 1940 census index gives the household’s seven children besides Elmer. The FamilySearch profile’s eight-child set includes Alma and Hellen, but omits Elmer and Charlotte; Alma appears to have left the home by 1940 and Hellen died in 1931. Charlotte/“Charlot” aligns by age and name but should remain a documented working identity until original schedule and obituary text are inspected.
  • Working child roster now includes ten names, reconciled from the family tree, direct census index, obituary index and birth index. Keep the source-specific conflicts explicit; do not delete candidates or create child profiles from this pass.
  • William’s sibling list is not resolved in this profile; his parents’ other children are listed on John and Rebecca’s profile with tree/index provenance.
  • Birth 11 Mar 1902 and death 15 Nov 1989 are FamilySearch compiled-profile details. Marriage index gives age 18 on 1 Jun 1920; the linked FamilySearch page says the couple’s tree marriage 1 Jan 1920, which conflicts with the register. Use 1 Jun 1920 as the documented date.
