Life Story

Marye Elizabeth Hooper was born about 21 February 1910 in Puryear, Tennessee. She married Rev. James William McGregor, and they had three sons: William Clarence, called Bill, born in 1936; James Thomas, called Tom, born in 1937; and Wayne Martin, who later lived in Germany. To the grandchildren she was “Mamaw.” The family settled in Harrison, Ohio; family trees say she and James are buried there, but no burial record has been found. The family tells that for a time they lived in a renovated one-room schoolhouse with a garden of about a quarter acre, and the grandchildren worked that garden, picked sweet berries, and pulled peanuts straight out of the ground.
