Family Relationship Notes

The 1950 U.S. Census records Mary E. McGregor, 40, born in Tennessee, as James W. McGregor’s wife, with sons William C., James T., and Wayne M. Bill’s obituary independently names James W. and Marye (Hooper) McGregor as his parents; Tom’s Ohio death certificate names Marye Hooper as his mother and James McGregor as his father.

The 1962 McGregor genealogy reports Mary Elizabeth Hooper born 21 February 1910 and married to James on 21 February 1935. A contemporary obituary clipping attached to her Find a Grave memorial states she died Sunday, 3 April 1994, age 84, at her Harrison residence and names her husband and sons.

Rick and Tom’s 2021 Geni work records William Luther Hooper and Ethel Minerva Hooper as Marye’s parents. The memorial for Marye independently links her as a child of Will L. Hooper and Ethel Minerva Howard Hooper; the obituary names her Hooper siblings. These are mutually consistent derivative family sources, but no birth record naming her parents was located. Working parent assignment: about 70% confidence, not documented.

The full name of Will L. Hooper is unresolved: the Geni profile expands the initial as “Luther,” while the memorial says “Will L.” The same memorial page’s attached marker image appears to name “Edith,” not Ethel, and its dates do not match Marye’s obituary. The image is not used as proof of Ethel’s identity or the couple’s burial marker.

The school portrait attached to Marye’s memorial carries the caption “Grove High Paris, TN Class of 1929.” It is a promising candidate but not independently identified as Marye; it is shown only as an uncertain photo lead.

Life Story

Marye Elizabeth Hooper McGregor was born in Tennessee in 1910 according to the 1962 McGregor genealogy and later family sources. She married Rev. James William McGregor in 1935, and they raised sons Bill, Tom, and Wayne. Her obituary identifies the family in Harrison, Ohio, and names her sisters Grace and Hinda, sister Merle, and brother J. T. The family remembers her as “Mamaw,” gardening with the grandchildren at their renovated one-room schoolhouse. She died in 1994 at age 84.
