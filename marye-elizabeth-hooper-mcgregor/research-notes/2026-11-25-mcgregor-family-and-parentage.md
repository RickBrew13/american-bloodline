# Marye Elizabeth Hooper McGregor — family, parentage, obituary, and photo review

**Research date:** 25 November 2026

## Working conclusion

**Parents:** Will L. Hooper (expanded as William Luther in the Geni tree) and Ethel Minerva Howard Hooper, **about 70% working confidence, not proved by a direct birth record**. Rick and Tom’s 2021 Geni work names this couple; Marye’s Find a Grave memorial independently links her as their child; the 1994 obituary names a sibling group that matches children linked on the memorial. These are derivative tree/memorial sources. No original birth record or census household directly naming Marye with these parents was located in this pass.

## Family group and records

- 1950 U.S. Census, Boone County, Kentucky, 10 Apr 1950, ED 8-3, p. 72, line 15: Mary E. McGregor, age 40, Tennessee-born, wife of James W.; sons William C., James T., and Wayne M. The census directly supports spouse/child household relationships, not her parents.
- Locker, *McGregor Genealogy* (1962), printed p. 48: Mary Elizabeth Hooper born 21 Feb 1910, married James William McGregor 21 Feb 1935; sons William Clarence, James Thomas, and Wayne Martin.
- Obituary clipping attached to Marye’s Find a Grave memorial says she died Sunday 3 Apr 1994, age 84, residence Harrison, Ohio; calls her the wife of Rev. James W. McGregor and mother of William C., James Thomas, and Wayne M.; names sisters Grace Duke, Hinda Littlejohn, late Merle Young, and brother J. T. Hooper. Funeral at Harrison Avenue Baptist Church. The image’s newspaper issue/date caption appears internally inconsistent, so the death/family text is cited while the issue date stays unverified.
- Find a Grave memorial links Marye to parents Will L. Hooper and Ethel Minerva Howard Hooper, lists burial Glen Haven Cemetery, and carries a school portrait candidate. Geni saved notes from Rick/Tom give William Luther Hooper and Ethel Minerva Hooper as parents.
- Public Geni index surfaces William Luther Hooper with a wife/children profile and Ethel Minerva Hooper (c.1881–1974) as his wife; individual pages are currently blocked. Do not treat the Geni profile alone as proof.

## Siblings and unresolved naming

- Full sibling group named in the obituary and linked on the memorial: Grace Hooper Duke (1912–2001), Hinda Hooper Littlejohn (1914–2008), Merle Hooper Young (1918–1961), J. T. Hooper (1915–1977).
- Half-brother candidate: Virgil Prentice Hooper (1901–1931), linked as William/Will Hooper’s child with first wife Edna A. Paschall; this makes him a half-brother through Will, not a child of Ethel. The saved Geni note says “Prentis Hooper” without enough detail to tell whether it refers to Virgil; do not merge names as certain.

## Identity conflicts and images

- Will’s public-tree full name “William Luther” is an expansion of memorial initial “L.”, not verified by an original record. Profile title remains Will L. Hooper with the full-name expansion qualified.
- A marker photo attached to both Will and Ethel’s memorials appears to read “W. I. Will … Edith” and dates that do not fit Ethel’s recorded lifespan. It is not used as her portrait or as the couple’s verified grave marker.
- The school portrait attached to Marye’s memorial has the visible caption “Grove High Paris, TN Class of 1929.” The memorial attachment is an identification lead, not proof that the subject is Marye; it is shown only with an explicit warning.
- The obituary clipping is included as a source image link, not copied into the GitHub archive because direct image download returned 403.
- The family says James and Marye were “Papaw” and “Mamaw” and recalls their renovated one-room schoolhouse and garden. Preserve those as attributed family memories, not public-record facts.
