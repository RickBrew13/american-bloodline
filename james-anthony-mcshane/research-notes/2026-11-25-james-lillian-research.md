# James Anthony McShane × Lillian Gardner — research update

**Research date:** 25 November 2026

## James Anthony

The 1950 U.S. census index (17 April, Hopkinsville, ED 24-11, p. 5, lines 18–23; FamilySearch digital folder 109019934, image 6) identifies James A. McShane, age 56, estimated birth 1894, as the son of Hugh I. McShane and Emma McShane. It lists him as “separated,” in the same home as his parents and sister Mary Harris. This is a direct census household relationship, though the indexed values should be checked against the image when feasible. His 1942 draft card reports 11 October 1893 in Nashville; the 1950 census age/year differs slightly. Preserve both source values.

James’s 1914 Tennessee marriage record was located: James McShane, age 21, and Lillie Irene Gardner, age 20, married 19 January 1914 in Davidson County (FamilySearch index; Book 30, certificate 7226). This replaces the earlier profile wording that the marriage record had not been found. The index’s estimated years (1893 and 1894) do not match every later reported birth date.

James’s existing sibling list comprises ten siblings, including Mary Veronica McShane Harris (1918–2002). The 1950 census independently lists Mary Harris as a daughter of Hugh and Emma and James’s sister. The same record adds no new sibling identity beyond that known roster. His mother’s 1960 obituary reports eleven children, one son James deceased by 1954.

Find a Grave memorial 154988759 contains two contributor-added grave images. One bears the inscription “James Anthony McShane 1893–1954”; it is suitable only as a labeled headstone image, not a portrait. Direct image download returned 403, so no image file was copied into the archive. No verified personal portrait or family photo of James and Lillian was found in this pass.

## Lillian

The 1914 index calls her Lillie Irene Gardner, age 20. Her 1926 Ohio marriage record calls her Lillian McShane, age 31, born 21 January 1895, and explicitly records her marital status as divorced when she married Harley Gillispie on 14 September 1926. It names J. M. Gardner and Emma Guthrie as her parents. Thus her first marriage had ended by 1926; the record does not provide the divorce date or decree. “T.” in the current profile is not explained by the reviewed records and remains unresolved against Irene.

The 1930 Norwood census index lists Lillian Gillespie with three McShane children: Ruth, Dorothy, and James. These are the three known children. No additional child is identified by the sources reviewed, but later or out-of-state children have not been ruled out. No sibling set for Lillian, post-1930 record, death date, or burial has been established. The prior 1900 Nashville Gardner household lead (James and “Kate,” with a Lillian reportedly born February 1896) conflicts with the 1926 parent names/January 1895 birth and has not been tied to her; it remains an unverified lead, not added as fact.
