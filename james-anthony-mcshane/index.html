Family Relationship Notes

James was one of eleven children of Hugh Ignatius McShane and Emma A. Wright. Emma’s 1960 obituary states that she married Hugh on 15 November 1892, that the family moved to Hopkinsville in 1898, and that son James had died in 1954. The sibling names and dates above come from the existing family research and memorials.

James married Lillian T. Gardner; their marriage record has not yet been located. Their three children are identified through Kentucky birth-index entries, Social Security records, and obituaries. Mable Ruth was born in Nashville in 1915, Dorothy in Hopkinsville in 1917, and James Earl in Hopkinsville in 1919.

James’s 1942 “old man’s draft” registration gives his birth date as 11 October 1893 and birthplace as Nashville, Tennessee. Registration is not evidence of military service. His death and burial details are reported by his Find a Grave memorial and should be checked against the original Kentucky death certificate and cemetery record.

Life Story

James Anthony McShane was born on 11 October 1893, the eldest of eleven children of Hugh Ignatius McShane and Emma A. Wright, and grew up in Hopkinsville, where the family moved in 1898 . His first daughter, Ruth, was born in Nashville in 1915 . He and his wife Lillian Gardner raised their family in Hopkinsville, Kentucky, where their daughter Dorothy was born in 1917 and their son James Earl in 1919 . He died in Christian County on 15 March 1954 and is buried in Riverside Cemetery, Hopkinsville .
