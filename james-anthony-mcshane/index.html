Family Relationship Notes

James was one of eleven children of Hugh Ignatius McShane and Emma A. Wright. Emma’s 1960 obituary states that she married Hugh on 15 November 1892, that the family moved to Hopkinsville in 1898, and that son James had died in 1954. The 1950 census directly places James A. in Hugh and Emma’s Hopkinsville household as their son; it also lists Mary Harris as their daughter and James’s sister. The sibling names and dates on this page are from existing family research and memorials, with Mary’s sibling relationship additionally shown in the 1950 census.

James married Lillie Irene Gardner in Davidson County, Tennessee, on 19 January 1914, according to the indexed county-marriage record. A later Ohio marriage record calls her Lillian McShane, daughter of J. M. Gardner and Emma Guthrie, and records her as divorced when she married Harley Gillispie on 14 September 1926. This establishes that the first marriage had ended by then, but does not supply the divorce date or proceeding. Three children are supported: Ruth, Dorothy, and James Earl. The 1930 Norwood census lists those three McShane children in Lillian’s household; no additional children have been identified in this review.

James’s 1942 “old man’s draft” registration gives his birth date as 11 October 1893 and birthplace as Nashville, Tennessee. Registration is not evidence of military service. His 1950 census entry gives an estimated birth year of 1894, age 56, and marital status “separated,” and places him in his parents’ home in Hopkinsville. His death and burial details are reported by his Find a Grave memorial; the memorial photo shows a headstone inscribed James Anthony McShane, 1893–1954, but is a grave image, not an individual portrait.

Life Story

James Anthony McShane, born in Nashville in 1893 according to his 1942 draft registration, was one of Hugh and Emma McShane’s eleven children. He married Lillie Irene Gardner in Davidson County in 1914. Their three known children were Ruth, Dorothy, and James Earl; the 1930 census places Lillian and all three children in Norwood, Ohio. By 1926 Lillian’s Ohio marriage record described her as divorced, and the 1950 census described James as separated while he lived with his parents and sister in Hopkinsville. The 1950 census and his mother’s obituary support his parentage and family setting. His memorial headstone photo is used as a grave image only; no portrait of James was identified.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • His parents rest on his mother's 1960 obituary card; his 1954 Kentucky death certificate would confirm them.
  • Marriage to Lillie Irene Gardner is supported by the indexed Davidson County record dated 19 Jan 1914. Lillian’s 14 Sep 1926 marriage record explicitly says divorced; the divorce date and record remain unknown.
  • The 1950 census has now been reviewed as an indexed household record: James was listed as the separated son of Hugh and Emma in Hopkinsville, with sister Mary Harris. The 1930 census index lists Lillian in Norwood with Ruth, Dorothy, and James; inspect the original 1930 image and earlier household censuses when accessible.
  • Three children are currently supported: Ruth, Dorothy, and James Earl. The 1930 census household lists those three; no fourth child has been found, but this does not establish that no later or out-of-state child existed.
  • The 1950 census’s “separated” status and Lillian’s 1926 “divorced” remarriage record are consistent with the marriage having ended by 1926; no divorce decree or date was located.
  • A verified individual portrait was not located. The Find a Grave stone image is used as a grave-marker image, with contributor attribution; do not describe it as James’s portrait.
