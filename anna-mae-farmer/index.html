Life Story

The shared marker at Tyner West Cemetery identifies “Mae” with Clarence C. Farmer and records the marriage date 29 November 1928. Find a Grave supplies her full name as Anna Mae Hamilton and dates 12 October 1912–28 September 1999; Mona Jackson’s obituary independently names her mother as Mae Farmer and names Mabel and Marjorie as sisters. This supports the family link, though no birth or marriage certificate has yet been inspected. Anna Mae’s parents and siblings remain unestablished; nearby or same-cemetery Hamiltons are not assumed to be her relatives.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Her parents and siblings have not been identified from records checked. Tyner West Cemetery includes several Hamilton families, but shared surname/cemetery is not a parent or sibling link. Andrew Jackson Hamilton and Jane Bullock Hamilton are a distinct older family; no record reviewed ties Anna Mae to them.
  • The 1950 census search index uses “Annamay” and transcribes one child as “Magoie.” The original image was not inspected, so the child’s identity and exact spelling remain unresolved.
  • No independently captioned portrait was found. Rick Brewster identifies the photo attached to Mabel’s Find a Grave memorial as Mabel. It is shown as a photo of Mabel, not as a portrait of Clarence or Anna Mae. The shared inscribed marker is included separately as their gravestone image.
