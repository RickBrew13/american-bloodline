Life Story

Julia Bell Kelly was the younger daughter of James Patrick Kelly and Laura Bell Underwood. On 20 January 1920 she was enumerated as Julia B., 4, at Aragon, Polk County, Georgia, in her parents' household with her siblings Bessie, Sallie, and William.

On 15 June 1929, at Newport, Kentucky, she married William Alexander Brewster, whose first wife Mary Ellen McKinney had died. The marriage record lists her parents only as "P Kelly and B Kelly." She was William's second wife and the mother of nine children named in the family obituaries: Cleo, William Jr., Mary Lee, James, Sarah Elizabeth, Frankie, Laura Belle, Lucy, and Edward.

She died in 1997. She was the younger sister of Sarah Hazel Kelly, who married William's son Homer William Brewster Sr.
