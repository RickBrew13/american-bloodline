Life Story

Catherine Mae Wainscott was born in Cincinnati on 23 March 1930, the eldest daughter of Ray Wainscott and Elizabeth Lawrence. SUPPORTED On 4 January 1947 she married Earl N. Russell in Newport, Kentucky. DOCUMENTED

She outlived her husband by two years and died on 12 May 1994. DOCUMENTED Catherine and Earl share a stone at Baltimore Pike Cemetery, where her parents and two little sisters also lie: "Russell · Earl 1925–1992 · Catherine 1930–1994 · Together Always." SUPPORTED
