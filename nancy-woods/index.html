Life Story

Nancy Woods married Hymelus Patton in Orange County, North Carolina, on 26 October 1824. The marriage record gives the date; the original bond has not been read. Four children were born in North Carolina — Mary about 1826, William about 1828, Alexander about 1829–1832, and Jesse Y. about 1834 — and the family had moved to Tennessee by 1850.

The 1850 census finds them in Bledsoe County, Tennessee, on 24 October 1850: Hymelus, 45, a farmer; Nancy, 44; and the four children, all born in North Carolina. It is the last record of Nancy. By the 1860 census, taken 10 July 1860 in Cumberland County, Hymelus’s household holds a different, younger Nancy — 32, born in Tennessee — with a two-month-old daughter, Sarah F. Nancy Woods had died before about August 1859. No death record, obituary, or burial record for her has been found, and no record gives her birth date or place beyond the 1850 census age.

The two Nancys are kept strictly separate: the 1850 Nancy, 44 and North Carolina-born, is Nancy Woods; the 1860 Nancy, 32 and Tennessee-born, is Hymelus’s second wife. A compiled memorial giving a “Nancy Wood (1802–1860)” is not attached to either woman — no evidence links it to Nancy Woods, and the second Nancy was alive and applying for Hymelus’s pension in 1867. No portrait of Nancy Woods exists, and no grave has been located.
