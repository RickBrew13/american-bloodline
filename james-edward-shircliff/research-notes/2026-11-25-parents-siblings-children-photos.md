# James Edward Shircliff — parents, siblings, children, cemetery record and photos

**Research date:** 25 November 2026 (America/New_York)  
**Couple:** James Edward Shircliff × Rita Pauline French Shircliff

## Parents and parent links

James Edward’s existing Ohio Death Index citation identifies his father’s surname as Shircliff and his mother’s maiden name as Yates. His project profile already carried James Absalom Shircliff (1881–1954) and Ida Belle Yates (1890–1967). Find a Grave memorials for James Edward, James Absalom, Ida Belle and Marvin Andrew cross-link this same parent pair, and the public FamilySearch tree for James repeats it. The existing parent assignment is retained as **strong working evidence (~80%)**, but no original birth certificate naming both parents’ given names was reviewed here. James Edward’s Immediate Family now links to the two new Gen VII profiles, and those pages link back to him.

## Siblings

The James Edward FamilySearch public profile counts 10 siblings/children in the parental group including James himself; James’s Find a Grave memorial names nine siblings: Ida Marie Whelan (1913–2004), Thelma Cecilia Mills (1915–2007), Rosella Mary “Rose” Widmer (1916–2013), Joseph Russell (1918–1969), Charles Anthony (1920–1997), Theresa Jane Shields (1922–2002), Bernard H. “Bernie” (1924–1978), Raphael Anslom “Ray” (1931–2007), and Marvin Andrew (1935–2001). These are now listed on James’s profile; individual sibling profiles were not created.

Marvin’s 4 July 2001 Courier-Journal obituary text reproduced on his Find a Grave memorial names brother Ray and sisters Marie Whelan, Rose Widmer, Theresa Shields and “Selma Mills.” The linked Shircliff memorial and FamilySearch tree give the Mills sister as **Thelma Cecilia**. I keep the discrepancy open rather than creating an extra Selma or silently treating the forms as identical.

## Children / additional-child findings

The 1940 household lists sons James W., Joseph H., and Raymond (ages 3, 1, 0). The 1950 Hamilton Township census lists James W., Joseph H., Raymond E., Ronald E., Mary R., Jerome C., Howard A., and Charles K. Joyce Ann Shircliffe (1941–1941) is named in the FamilySearch tree and linked Find a Grave memorial.

The Find a Grave memorial for James lists Joseph H. (1938–2022), Joyce A. (1941–1941), and Robert Anthony Shircliff (1949–2020) as children. Rita’s 1997 obituary (below) names nine surviving children: daughter Rita Gallagher; sons Wayne, Raymond, Joseph, Ronald, Jerry, Lawrence, Kenneth and Robert; it also says daughter Joyce Ann had died. This expands the family list but leaves identity conflicts: Wayne is supported as James Wayne; the obituary’s Jerry may correspond to census Jerome but is not independently bridged; Robert Anthony may match the obituary Robert; Rita Gallagher, Lawrence and Kenneth are not reconciled with the 1950 initials. Mary R., Howard A. and Charles K. appear in 1950 but are not named in the obituary. Do not invent full names or merge candidates solely by first name. The obituary plus census may indicate **at least ten children including Joyce**, but the exact identities/count should remain qualified until birth records or further obituaries are checked.

## Cemetery index and conflicting marriage date

The Find a Grave stone photo for the couple reads “Married April 21, 1936.” The James Edward FamilySearch tree gives 18 April 1936 and an index citation for a Kentucky county marriage; the existing project profile’s 18 April date had been drawn from that tree. I have changed the profile to present the marker date as the inscription and explicitly preserve the 18 April tree claim as a conflict pending the original Meade County register.

The [Lebanon Cemetery index](https://warren.ohgenweb.org/Cemetery/Lebanon/s.htm) lists “Shircliff, Edward” interment no. 10666, with note “Edward Shircliff on stone; James Ed Shircliff in Register of Interments,” Section I, lot 67-10, and “Shircliff, Pauline” no. 10532, Section I, lot 67-9. This independently helps connect James Edward’s “Edward” stone name and Pauline’s burial location. Index only; register not inspected.

## Photo/archive work

A Find a Grave shared stone photo for James Edward/Rita (memorials 53662522 and 53662524) was downloaded from the memorial’s image URL through an image-fetch proxy and placed, unchanged, in both person archives. It visibly shows both engraved panels: Pauline, 27 April 1916–23 May 1997; Edward, 25 January 1911–17 February 1999; “Married April 21, 1936.” Credit: Find a Grave contributor **DebOhio**.
