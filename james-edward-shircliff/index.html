Life Story

James Edward Shircliff was born 25 January 1911 in Kentucky. On 18 April 1936 he married Rita Pauline French in Meade County, Kentucky. Their children were James Wayne, Joseph H., Raymond E., Ronald E., Mary R., Jerome C., Howard A., and Charles K. Shircliff, and Joyce Ann Shircliffe, who died in infancy.

In the 1940 census the family was living in Magisterial District 3, Meade County, Kentucky: Edward, 29, head of household, with his wife Pauline, 23, and their sons James W., 3, Joseph H., 1, and Raymond, 0. By 1950 they had moved to Hamilton Township, Franklin County, Ohio, where James E., 39, worked as a farm laborer, with Pauline, 33, and eight children at home.

He died 17 February 1999 in Butler, Ohio, at the age of 88. His family tree profile gives his burial at Lebanon Cemetery, Lebanon, Warren, Ohio; no burial record has been located yet.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • His birth and death dates are documented by his Ohio Death Index entry.
  • His burial at Lebanon Cemetery, Lebanon, Warren, Ohio, comes from the family tree alone; no burial record has been located yet.
  • His marriage date, 18 April 1936, comes from the family tree alone, but the marriage itself is shown by the 1940 and 1950 censuses, which list him and Pauline as married head of household and wife.
  • His parents, James Absalom Shircliff (1881–1954) and Ida Bell Yates (1890–1967), married 23 November 1909, Meade, Kentucky, are from the family tree alone; no record has been seen for them.
  • His wife appears as Pauline Shircliff in the censuses, while his son’s Kentucky birth index names her as Rita French and his own family tree profile gives her full name as Rita Pauline French — one woman, not two.
  • The 1950 census lists his son Raymond E. as 13, the same age as James, although the 1940 census had Raymond 0 and James 3 — that looks like a census or indexing error; every other household identifier aligns.
  • His family tree profile lists only one child, Joyce Ann Shircliffe (1941–1941); James Wayne and the other children are not attached there.
