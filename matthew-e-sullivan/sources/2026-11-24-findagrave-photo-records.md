# Find a Grave photos and burial-marker records: Matthew E. and Lena O. Sullivan

Matthew Edward Sullivan Jr. (Find a Grave Memorial 106402579) is listed as born 14 July 1890 in St. Louis and deceased 18 September 1960 in Bonne Terre; burial at Lesterville Cemetery, Reynolds County, Missouri. The memorial identifies spouse Lena O. Sullivan. Its photo tab contains four submitted photos: (1) Matthew’s U.S. Army World War I marker, (2) a close view of his date/name panel, (3) the full shared Sullivan marker, and (4) an older 2013 view of that marker. The photo tab credits photos 1–3 to Cathy Hill (21 Apr. 2026), and photo 4 to ancestorhunter (9 Mar. 2013). Memorial: https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/106402579/matthew-edward-sullivan Photo tab: https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/106402579/matthew-edward-sullivan/photo

Lena O. Sullivan (Find a Grave Memorial 106404547) is listed as born 14 October 1891 in Illinois and deceased 5 December 1970; burial at Lesterville Cemetery. Its memorial text identifies Matthew Edward Sullivan Jr. as spouse and names children including Martha J. Sullivan. Its photo tab contains three images: Lena’s date/name panel, the shared marker, and an earlier view of the shared marker; contributors are Cathy Hill (21 Apr. 2026) and ancestorhunter (9 Mar. 2013). Memorial: https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/106404547/lena-o-sullivan Photo tab: https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/106404547/lena-o-sullivan/photo

These memorials strongly match the parents named in Martha’s 2014 obituary, but the Find a Grave material is derivative. The tombstone uses Lena O. while Martha’s obituary supplies maiden name Spencer. The obituary and memorial child lists do not fully agree: preserve both source forms; do not infer a definitive reconciliation of Daniel G./Dan J./Darriel G., Joann/Jo Ann, or other sibling names from the memorial alone. The shared cemetery-marker photos are grave photographs, not portraits or family snapshots.

Lena’s Find a Grave photo tab includes three Lena-associated views (two later marker photos credited to Cathy Hill and an earlier 2013 marker photograph by ancestorhunter).
