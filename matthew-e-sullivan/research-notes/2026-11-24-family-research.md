# Matthew E. Sullivan — Gen VII source and family review

**Reviewed:** 24 November 2026.

## Original 1950 census image inspected

The NARA image for the 1950 U.S. census, Reynolds County, Missouri, ED 90-5, sheet 4, enumerated 4 April 1950, records household 26 in Lesterville Township:

- Matthew E. Sullivan, head, age 59, married, born Missouri.
- Lena O. Sullivan, wife, age 58, born Missouri.
- Daniel G. Sullivan, son, age 18, born Missouri.
- Joann Sullivan, daughter, age 16, born Missouri.

The image was inspected directly and saved under this person's `documents/` archive. FamilySearch's derivative index agrees on the household members and reports Matthew's birth about 1891.

Martha J. Lewis's 2014 obituary explicitly identifies her parents as Matthew and Lena (Spencer) Sullivan, supporting the parent link from Matthew to Martha and identifying Lena's maiden name.

## Child/sibling reconciliation

Martha's obituary lists eight siblings: Mary Jane Schuab, Dorothy Sullivan, Matthew Edward Sullivan Jr., James S. Sullivan, Robert J. Sullivan, Dan J. Sullivan, Jo Ann Graham, and Elizabeth Wright. The 1950 census's Daniel G. and Joann are possible matches to Dan J. and Jo Ann, but initials and Jo Ann's married surname differ; the identities are not merged. The obituary indicates the family group is larger than the two children still in the parents' 1950 household.

## Boundaries and remaining work

Matthew's parents, siblings, exact birth/death dates, marriage date to Lena, and burial remain unresolved. Census age and birthplace are reported as such, not treated as exact vital facts. No Gen VIII or separate sibling profiles were created.
