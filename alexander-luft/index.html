Life Story

Alexander Luft was born in Germany about 1872, the son of David Luft and Mina Edel DOCUMENTED. A butcher in Cincinnati, he married Bella Mahle, a Russian-born nurse, on 1 July 1912 DOCUMENTED. He and his wife Bertha raised their family in Cincinnati, where the censuses from 1920 to 1950 find their daughter Eva at home with them SUPPORTED. Their grandson Alexander Joseph Luft carries his name SUPPORTED. Alexander died in Cincinnati on 25 May 1944 and was buried at Covedale, the group of Orthodox Jewish cemeteries on the city's west side DOCUMENTED.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • His age varies: about 1872 from his marriage and death records, about 1864–66 from the 1920–1940 censuses.
  • The marriage application is the only record naming his parents; his death certificate says "unknown."
  • Exact cemetery and plot at Covedale not yet found.
  • He is not the Alexander Luft of Klamath Falls, Oregon (1912–1998), or the older Catholic Luft family of Saint Joseph Cemetery; no tie to either is known.
