Family Relationship Notes

Alexander’s 1 July 1912 Hamilton County marriage application names Alex Luft, age 40, butcher, born in Germany, son of David Luft and Mina Edel. It names his bride as Bella Mahle, age 25, born in Russia, daughter of Aaron Mahle and Kati (Kate) Tahmali. Other family and child records identify Alexander’s wife as Bertha Tarmin. Whether Bella Mahle and Bertha Tarmin are the same woman has not been resolved; do not merge the identities without a direct record.

Records and family memorials identify Minnie Luft (1914–1999), David Luft (1916–1993), and Eva L. Luft (1918–2002) in Alexander’s family. Eva’s Social Security NUMIDENT names Alexander Luft and Bertha Tarmin as her parents. Harry (Henry) Luft, born about 1913, appears in the existing family research but needs an original record review. Anne Luft, later Grinter, is an unconfirmed additional child and is not included in the Children card.

Alexander’s death certificate reports death on 25 May 1944 in Cincinnati, occupation butcher, residence 3130 Bowman Avenue, and burial at Covedale on 26 May. It lists his parents as unknown, so David Luft and Mina Edel remain parent names reported on the 1912 marriage application.

His reported birth year varies: the 1912 marriage application and 1944 death certificate point to about 1872–1873, while census ages suggest about 1864–1866. The census household references in earlier research notes need to be rechecked against the original images.

Life Story

Alexander Luft was born in Germany about 1872, the son of David Luft and Mina Edel . A butcher in Cincinnati, he married Bella Mahle, a Russian-born nurse, on 1 July 1912 . He and his wife Bertha raised their family in Cincinnati, where the 1920–1940 censuses place their daughter Eva at home with them . Their grandson Alexander Joseph Luft carries his name . Alexander died in Cincinnati on 25 May 1944 and was buried at Covedale, the group of Orthodox Jewish cemeteries on the city's west side .

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • His age varies: about 1872 from his marriage and death records, about 1864–66 from the 1920–1940 censuses.
  • The 1912 marriage application names David Luft and Mina Edel as Alexander’s parents; it is the only located record doing so. His 1944 death certificate gives his parents as “unknown,” so the parent names are documented as reported on the marriage application, not independently confirmed by the death certificate.
  • Exact cemetery and plot at Covedale not yet found.
  • He is not the Alexander Luft of Klamath Falls, Oregon (1912–1998), or the older Catholic Luft family of Saint Joseph Cemetery; no tie to either is known.
