Family Relationship Notes

The 1920 Detroit census lists Frank and Josephine Grzybowski with seven children, including Eleanore. The 1930 and 1940 census references also place the couple in Detroit. Frank’s 1942 Michigan death certificate records his birth date and birthplace and describes him as widowed. Eleanore’s Social Security NUMIDENT index names Frank Grzybowski and Josephine Ryniecka as her parents.

Frank’s death record names his father as Frank Grzybowski; his mother is not identified in the reviewed record. Josephine’s maiden name appears as Ryniecka in Eleanore’s NUMIDENT record; Ryniecki is the form used in the family profile. Her exact birth and death dates remain unresolved. A Jozefa Grzybowski buried at Holy Cross Cemetery is only a possible match, not identified as Josephine.

The seven children are listed from the 1920 census household, with Eleanore separately named in the NUMIDENT record. The census spellings and approximate birth years are retained; individual birth records have not been reviewed. No verified portrait is available for either person, so each profile uses one placeholder.

Life Story

Frank Grzybowski was born in Warsaw, Poland, on 27 February 1883, the son of Frank Grzybowski . He came to the United States in 1905 and settled in Detroit, where he and his wife Josephine raised a large family; the 1920 census shows them in Ward 16 with seven children, among them Eleanore, born in 1915 . The family remained in Detroit through 1940 . Frank died in Detroit on 25 April 1942, a widower, at age 59 .
