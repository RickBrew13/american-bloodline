Life Story

Frank Grzybowski was born in Warsaw, Poland, on 27 February 1883, the son of Frank Grzybowski DOCUMENTED. He came to the United States in 1905 and settled in Detroit, where he and his wife Josephine raised a large family; the 1920 census shows them in Ward 16 with seven children, among them Eleanore, born in 1915 SUPPORTED. The family remained in Detroit through 1940 SUPPORTED. Frank died in Detroit on 25 April 1942, a widower, at age 59 DOCUMENTED.
