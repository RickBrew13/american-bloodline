Life Story

Terri’s 29 April 2002 Cincinnati Enquirer obituary identifies her as “Terri Ann (née Weis)” and names Damaris Brewster, Tammy Rush, and Nick Patten as her children. It also names her Weis siblings. The obituary documents the children as Terri’s; it does not name their father; a 1985 marriage index points to Richard Lee Jordan. The 1950 census lists her as “Terry Ann Weis,” 4, the daughter of Abe and Margaret Weis in McVeigh, Pike, Kentucky.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Terri’s obituary calls her “Terri Ann (née Weis),” establishing Weis as her maiden surname; Patten is used at death. The 1985 Tammy marriage index lists Terri Weis and Richard Lee Jordan as Terri’s husband. This supports a Jordan relationship/name form by 1985, but no Jordan–Weis marriage record has been located.
  • Find a Grave gives her birth date and parents, but original birth/death records have not been inspected.
  • The family identifies Daniel “Gus” Patten as Terri’s former spouse FAMILY ACCOUNT; Gus’s 2006 obituary names Barbara Patten (née Helton) as his wife at death. The obituary does not establish when their relationship ended; the FamilySearch tree gives their marriage as 16 April 1977, Hamilton, Ohio (UNPROVED — tree only, no record seen). This distinction does not establish Gus as Damaris’s or Tammy’s biological father.
  • Richard Lee Jordan is a possible biological-father lead for Damaris and Tammy, not an established father: the 1985 marriage index only lists him as Terri’s husband; it does not name him as Tammy’s father. Damaris’s public-record index gives “Damaris Jordan” as an alias but lists no parents. Those clues warrant investigation but do not document or independently prove paternity. The sisters’ birth certificates or other parent-naming records are needed.
  • Daniel “Gus” Patten is Damaris and Tammy’s stepfather per a family member, not their biological father. His obituary calls Damaris and Tammy stepdaughters and names Barbara Patten (née Helton) as his wife; it does not name Terri.
  • Found 17 November 2026: her parentage is now corroborated beyond the memorials — her NUMIDENT names Margaret G Alexander and Abraham B Weis as her parents, her Ohio Death Index entry names father “Weis” and mother “Alexander,” and the 1950 census lists her as the daughter of Abe and Margaret Weis. Her parents’ pages are now linked above.
  • Her siblings are Ronald Weis (born 1937), Donald Weis (born 1940), Albert Bernard Weis (1941–1995), Jack Merril Weis (1943–2015), and Sharon Weis (born 1944) — the 1950 census and the FamilySearch tree agree exactly.
  • Her parents’ marriage record spells her mother’s surname “Alexandria,” while the tree, the censuses, and Find a Grave give “Alexander.”
  • Her mother was 21 on the 1935 marriage record (which would mean born about 1914), but the tree and Find a Grave give her birth as 10 March 1916 — she would have been 19 — and the 1920 census age of 3 years 10 months supports 1916; she likely overstated her age to reach 21.
  • Her mother’s birthplace is written “Latham, Va.” on the marriage certificate (the handwriting is not perfectly clear); the tree and the 1920 census give West Virginia.
  • Her father’s birth year is 1910 on his Social Security record, Find a Grave index, marriage record, and the tree, but 1911 in the Ohio Death Index and the 1950 census, and 1912 by the 1920 census age of 8.
