Family Relationship Notes

FamilySearch public profile KN3V-FN2 spells his given name “Nathanial”; the existing Lucy profile uses “Nathaniel,” and Find a Grave also uses Nathaniel. Retain both forms as source variants.

FamilySearch and Find a Grave both associate Nathaniel with Martha Ellen Stephens and Lucy Jane Price. FamilySearch reports an 20 March 1867 marriage in Scott County and eight children including Lucy; the original marriage record was not inspected.

FamilySearch reports birth in Scott County while Find a Grave reports Harrison County; the shared family-marker photo is displayed on both spouses’ memorials, but its inscription is not legible enough to identify a plot or prove individual burial on its own.
