# Source register — Nathaniel Price

Date reviewed: 28 November 2026
- [FamilySearch public Nathanial Price profile (KN3V-FN2)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/KN3V-FN2/nathanial-price-1846-1935) — Compiled profile gives 6 February 1846–12 December 1935, birth in Scott County, parents Franklin Price and Sarah Margaret Lynn, spouse Martha Ellen Stephens, 20 March 1867 marriage, eight children including Lucy, and seven siblings. It cites an 1860 census and county-marriage record; original images were not reviewed.
- [FamilySearch public Lucy Jane Price profile (KN3Y-MRJ)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/KN3Y-MRJ/lucy-jane-price-1882-1959) — Compiled child profile identifies Nathanial Price and Martha Ellen Stephens as Lucy’s parents and reports nine children for Lucy and Roscoe.
- [FamilySearch public Franklin Price profile (29SH-XGW)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/29SH-XGW/franklin-price-1807) — Compiled profile identifies Franklin Price and Sarah Margaret Lynn as Nathanial’s parents and lists seven children including Nathanial.
- [Find a Grave memorial 51886498, Nathaniel Price](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/51886498/nathaniel-price) — User-contributed memorial reports 6 February 1846–12 December 1935, birth in Harrison County and burial at Price Family Cemetery, Sadieville, Scott County; lists two photos. One is marked Person and another Grave.
- [Find a Grave person photo, Nathaniel Price](https://images.findagrave.com/photos/2010/122/51886498_127291042064.jpg) — Photo attached to memorial 51886498; Find a Grave identifies it as a Person image, credited to Janet Cochran, added 3 May 2010. Public profile attribution, not a newly made portrait.
- [Find a Grave grave photo, Price Family Cemetery marker](https://images.findagrave.com/photos/2010/120/51886498_127277590766.jpg) — Photo attached to both Nathaniel Price memorial 51886498 and Martha Price memorial 51886730, credited to Janet Cochran, added 1 May 2010. The visible stone inscription is faint and not individually legible.
