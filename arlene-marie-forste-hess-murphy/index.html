Life Story

Arlene Marie Forste was born 11 November 1936 in Kenton County, Kentucky, to Marie Barnhorst. She married John Richard Hess Sr.; they had three children — Holly Brewster, John Hess Jr., and Heidi Marie (Hess) Smith — and divorced in 1967. She later married Carl Gordon Murphy; her daughter Colleen Murphy was probably Carl’s. Her daughter Heidi’s 2018 obituary names her as “Arlene Murphy” and names John Hess Sr. as Heidi’s late father. No death date or place has yet been verified for Arlene.
