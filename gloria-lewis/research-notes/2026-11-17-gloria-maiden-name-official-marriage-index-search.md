# 17 November 2026 — Focused marriage-index search for Gloria Lewis's maiden name

## Question
Identify Gloria Lewis's name before marriage to Edward A. Lewis Sr., then use the verified birth name to trace parents and siblings.

## Search performed

1. Located Hamilton County Probate Court's official record-search portal. The court states its electronic marriage-license list is an alphabetical bride/groom index for 1984 forward; records from June 1973 through 1983 must be checked in the Marriage License Index books.
2. Inspected the official 1974–1982 indexed volumes for surname LEWIS, including the entries organized under the Lewis surname. These are linked from the court's official Marriage License Index (1817–1983) archive page. The actual pages were fetched and searched as text for LEWIS and GLORIA, with relevant Lewis entries read in context.
3. Result: no entry for Edward A. Lewis or Edward Arnold Lewis as a Hamilton County license applicant under the Lewis surname in the 1974–1982 index; no Gloria entry appears in the reviewed Lewis section. The section does contain numerous unrelated Lewis marriages (e.g., Gary Wayne Lewis–Sheila Denise Walters, 1976, vol. 9, p. 528; Granada Marie Lewis–Eugene Frederick Lewis, 1979, vol. 18, p. 551; Norman Lee Lewis–Mary Frances Lewis, 1980, vol. 23, p. 38), which are not Rick's couple.
4. Search-engine-indexed snippets also did not reveal an Edward/Gloria Lewis license or a bride's prior surname.

## Interpretation / limits

This is a negative result only for the official Hamilton County 1974–1982 index entries checked. It does NOT establish that the couple never married, nor that they married under those names: the marriage may have been in another county or state, outside that time range, or Gloria may appear under her maiden surname in the bride index. The relevant prospective period before Chad's reported 1977 birth cannot be fixed more precisely from sources reviewed here.

The separate 1973 Hamilton County index is not yet fully searched by the couple's names. The online index pages opened in this research route do not expose a name-search form in text, and the browser was unavailable because of account quota. No negative statement is made for 1973.

## Current conclusion

Gloria's maiden name remains unidentified. Do not treat Lewis as her maiden name. The index search did not provide a verified parent/sibling family; do not create their profiles/folder based on unrelated same-name households. The single most decisive evidence remains Chad's original birth record or the actual marriage application/license, which may show Gloria's prior surname and parents.
