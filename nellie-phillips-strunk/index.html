Family Relationship Notes

Nellie’s own obituary transcription says she was the wife of S. L. Strunk, born 17 May 1892, died 16 January 1924, and left seven children plus two who had died. Seigal’s 1969 obituary names Shelby and Nellie Phillips Strunk as his parents; it lists Flora McCartt among his sisters. Flora’s Find a Grave memorial also links Nellie as her mother. This is enough to retain Nellie as the probable maternal parent in the project’s working pedigree, but not to claim a direct original parent-child record. A Genealogy.com discussion from 2001 gives a different William Leslie McCartt (born 1901) married to a Flora Helen Strunk (born about 1911); that spouse pairing conflicts with the target Bill’s census ages and draft registration, so the two William identities are kept distinct.

Life Story

Nellie Phillips was the wife of S. L. Strunk according to a 1924 obituary transcription and is identified in Seigal Strunk’s obituary as his mother with Shelby. A Find a Grave memorial reports Nellie’s dates as 17 May 1892–16 January 1924 and links Flora G. Strunk McCartt as a daughter. Shelby’s 1965 obituary names Flora as his daughter, and Seigal’s obituary identifies Flora as his sister. Together these support Nellie as Flora’s probable mother, but no original birth record directly naming Nellie as Flora’s mother has been reviewed. The 1924 obituary says Nellie left seven children and two children who had died, without naming them.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Nellie’s 1924 obituary says seven children survived her and two had died, but names no children. The profile lists Seigal and probable daughter Flora; other names remain unknown.
  • Flora is a probable daughter based on Find a Grave links, Shelby’s obituary naming her as a daughter, Seigal’s obituary naming Flora as his sister, and the timing of Nellie’s marriage/death. No direct original record proves Nellie was Flora’s mother.
  • Find a Grave links Ewel Phillips and Texas “Texie” Thompson Phillips as Nellie’s parents and lists six siblings plus three half-siblings. These are user-contributed relationships and have not been checked against original records.
  • The 2001 forum reply’s William Leslie McCartt (1901–1976) does not fit the target Bill (census age about 1908 and 1940 draft record). Keep the William identities separate; Flora’s identity across the two pedigrees remains a research question.
  • Nellie died in 1924 according to the memorial/obituary transcription; Shelby’s 1965 obituary identifies Ada Pearl as his widow. The date and circumstances of Shelby’s later marriage remain unresolved.
