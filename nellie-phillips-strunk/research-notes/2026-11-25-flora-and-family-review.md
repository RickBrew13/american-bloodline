# Research note — Nellie Phillips Strunk (25 November 2026)

Nellie’s transcribed 1924 obituary states she left seven living children and two children who had died, but it names none. Current identifiable children include Seigal (his obituary identifies Shelby and Nellie as parents) and Flora G. Strunk McCartt (probable, through derivative memorial links, Shelby’s obituary and Seigal’s sister statement). Do not assume the seven-child list is exhausted by those two.

The user-contributed Find a Grave sibling group lists James Iowa Phillips (1886–1957), Fedman “Fed” Phillips (1888–1980), Della Phillips Jeffers (1888–1934), James Henry Phillips (1890–1967), Collie Victoria Phillips Duncan (1891–1922), and A.Y. Phillips Sr. (1893–1979), plus half-siblings Mattie Phillips West (1879–1956), John T. Phillips (1882–1916), and Ike Phillips (1892–1961). These are leads pending original records.

Rick confirms the 2001 forum’s William Leslie McCartt is the same person as Willie “Bill” McCartt, Flora’s husband. The forum’s 1901 birth and 1976 death dates are wrong for him; census ages support birth about 1908, and the shared marker/user-contributed memorial report 1972. The reported marriage date and Flora Helen/1911 details remain unverified.
