A separate 1936 SS-5 transcription gives the same father and date, mother “Rosalia Mule,” and birthplace transcribed “BLERMO, Italy.” These agreeing entries strongly support Angelo as another child and likely sibling of Ignazia; the original form and “Mule”/“Moule” spelling remain unchecked.

This is a name-only lead, not an assigned birth date or ancestry. Broad FamilySearch searches for Rosalia Mule return unrelated people; they are not merged.
