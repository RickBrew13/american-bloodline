Family Relationship Notes

Rosalia Mule was born in Zisa, Palermo, the daughter of Vincenzo Mule and Anna Pisciotto, and married Pasquale Carbonaro on 27 February 1886 in Brancaccio, Palermo. The marriage act was read from the image and re-read letter by letter; it names both sets of parents. The family-tree spelling of her name, “Rosalie Moule,” is not the record spelling.

She and Pasquale lived in Acqua dei Corsari, Brancaccio, where their children Angelo, Ignazia, and Vincenzo were born. She died 22 October 1893 in Palermo; the Vallelunga church letter of 26 November 1895 states the date twice. Pasquale remarried Giuseppa Noto in December 1895.

Her birth year is not settled: she is 23 on the February 1886 marriage act and 23 again on Ignazia’s August 1889 birth act. Her birth record has not been found. The 1872 France census lead was ruled out — a FamilySearch search of French records found only native French women with French parents, and her marriage act proves a Palermo birth.

Life Story

Rosalia Mule was born in Zisa, Palermo, the daughter of Vincenzo Mule and Anna Pisciotto, and married Pasquale Carbonaro on 27 February 1886 in Brancaccio, Palermo. Their children Angelo, Ignazia, and Vincenzo were born in Brancaccio in 1887, 1889, and 1892. She died 22 October 1893 in Palermo, and Pasquale remarried Giuseppa Noto in December 1895. Her birth year is not settled — she is 23 on both the 1886 marriage act and Ignazia’s 1889 birth act — and her birth record has not been found.
