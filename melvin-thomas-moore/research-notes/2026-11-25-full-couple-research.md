# Melvin Thomas Moore × Grace Irene Huffman — full-couple research update

**Date:** 25 November 2026

## Melvin Thomas Moore

### Census and burial evidence

The original 1950 schedule, Linville Township, Burke County, North Carolina, ED 12-13, sheet 26 (FamilySearch image 27, DGS 108931941), was retrieved as an image and reviewed. It records **Melvin T. Moore**, age 21, born in North Carolina, married, occupation written as cabinet-room work in a furniture factory, and wife **Irene Moore**, age 17, also born in North Carolina. This independently supports a birth about 1928–1929, local residence, marriage, and furniture work. It does not give Melvin's parents, full birth date, or his wife's maiden name.

On the same sheet, the next enumerated dwelling/household is headed by **Wiley J. Moore**, age 41, North Carolina-born, and **Edna Moore**, age 46. Both are recorded in the furniture trade/furniture factory. Their household includes **Magret Moore**, age 26, marked daughter, and **Eugene Moore**, age 3, marked grandson. Melvin's separate household immediately precedes theirs; he shares the occupation and local setting. Proximity and work are suggestive, not a stated parent-child relationship. The source does **not** label Melvin as Wiley's son.

The 1950 census image/index for Magret gives her as Wiley and Edna's daughter; this conflicts with the Find a Grave memorial/quoted Sossoman funeral-home obituary for **Margaret Moore Dellinger Whisenant** (20 June 1923–18 November 2016). That obituary calls James Wiley Moore and Daisy Causby Moore her parents and lists Wiley John Thomas Moore among her siblings. Margaret's birth year and name resemble 1950 Magret, but the different reported relationship and parents prevent assuming the census Magret is the same woman. The FamilySearch collaborative profile for Edna also lists Margaret Marie Moore (1923–2006) and Melvin Thomas Moore Sr. (1929–1986) as children, but this is tree data and may conflate the Margaret identities. **Do not list Margaret as Melvin's sibling or daughter until the identity and relationship conflict is resolved.**

FamilySearch's collaborative Edna Mae Garner profile describes Edna and Wiley John Thomas Moore as parents of Melvin and Margaret, and reports their 7 April 1928 marriage. Find a Grave memorials for Wiley (119984350) and Edna (119984378) identify the couple, dates, and Glen Alpine Cemetery burial; Wiley's memorial says he married Edna in 1928 and was son of James Wiley Moore and Daisy L. Eury. Considered with the 1950 Linville sequence and the Geni parent assignment already on Melvin's page, Wiley and Edna are a **probable working parent couple (about 65% confidence), not proven by a direct parent-child record**. The discrepancy around Magret/Margaret makes stronger confidence unwarranted. The Find a Grave memorial for Margaret instead lists Wiley as her sibling, a useful warning against copying the FamilySearch tree's unexamined child list.

A Find a Grave memorial, ID 8421142, identifies Melvin Thomas Moore (1929–1986) and burial at River Valley Baptist Church cemetery, Burke County. The memorial has one image, a small marker reading “M. MOORE,” credited to Ralph (BUCK) Buchanan (uploaded 5 July 2008). This is a memorial/grave-marker image, **not a portrait**. It supports a cemetery lead, but the memorial has no cited original vital source. Geni's exact birth (11 January 1929) and death (6 September 1986, Morganton) remain tree-reported; the census independently supports only circa-1929 birth, and the memorial only the years.

### Marriage and children

The 1950 original schedule independently proves that Melvin was married and names his wife as Irene, age 17, North Carolina-born. The Geni profile identifies his spouse as Grace Irene Huffman. The age and birthplace fit the reported Grace (born 1933), and a derivative compiled-index snippet expands aliases including Grace Irene Huffman / Irene Ben Moore / Grace Irene King. This makes the 1950 Irene **a probable identification of Grace Irene (about 75%)**, but no marriage certificate or primary record directly bridges the names. Retain the name variation and uncertainty.

Geni alone lists daughter **Jeanette Lorene Moore**, born 11 September 1962 in North Carolina. This is not independently corroborated. A separate 2024 obituary for a California-born Jeanette Lorene Moore reports birth 23 September 1962 and names Jeremiah and Marie (Quatrale) Moore as parents. The same full name and near-matching year, but conflicting date, birthplace, and parents, create an unresolved identity collision. Rick's family account independently connects a Jeanette Lorene Moore with Mitch Waulk as Nioka's mother; that family account should not be transferred to Melvin and Grace without a documentary identity bridge. Keep Geni's Jeanette assignment marked unverified; do not merge these Jeanettes.

No other direct child of Melvin and Grace has been established. Eugene, age three, is explicitly indexed as a **grandson of Wiley J. Moore**, not as Melvin's son; the page doesn't name Eugene's parent, and he must not be added as Melvin and Grace's child. The probable/possible Magret-Margaret conflict likewise prevents adding that person as their child.

### Siblings

No sibling of Melvin is independently established. A family-tree profile lists Margaret Marie Moore as a child of Wiley and Edna, but the Find a Grave/funeral-home evidence identifies a similarly aged Margaret as Wiley's sister, while the 1950 index calls Magret Wiley's daughter. This is unresolved; no sibling name is promoted in the profile. Wiley's own siblings are not Melvin's siblings and are not transferred to Melvin.

## Grace Irene Huffman

### Identity, parents, and 1950 household

The only independently located record placing a young wife in Melvin's household is the 1950 census: **Irene Moore**, age 17, born North Carolina, wife of Melvin T. Moore in Linville. It matches the age/place reported for Grace, but does not give her maiden name or parents. Geni calls Melvin's spouse Grace Irene Huffman and reports her birth on 17 May 1933; a derivative compiled index reports a Grace Irene Huffman also known as **Irene Ben Moore** and **Grace Irene King**, born in Morganton on 17 March 1933, and names **Richard Benfield** as father and **Nannie M. Huffman** as mother. It also reports a 27 April 1997 death. This derivative index appears to combine Social Security/NUMIDENT and older SS-5 sources, but the underlying records were not retrieved, and its exact date conflicts with Geni's May date.

The 1950 census indexes **Nannie M. Huffman**, 37, widowed, as daughter in a Valdese household, with Benny L. Huffman, 18, and Lewis Huffman Jr., 16, each called a grandson of the head. This is a plausible route into Grace's maternal family if Nannie is her mother, but the record does not name Grace and does not explicitly state that Benny or Lewis are her siblings. The Geni profile separately names Grace's mother as **Nannie M. Coleman** and lists no father. A SortedByName search result gives an additional proposed identity for Nannie M. Coleman / Nannie Mae Coleman / Nannie Mae Huffman and parents Lizzie A. and Thomas J. Huffman, but this is another derivative compilation and has not been joined to the Valdese census woman or Grace through original records.

**Working parent assessment:** Nannie M. Huffman / Nannie M. Coleman is a probable mother candidate (about 65% confidence) because both Geni and the derivative index connect a Nannie M. Huffman/Coleman to Grace; Richard Benfield is a plausible father candidate (about 60%), found only in the derivative compilation and no source image was reviewed. These are provisional assignments only. Birth-surname order, the Coleman/Huffman variants, and exact birth date remain unresolved. Do not change Grace's name to Benfield or Coleman as a fact.

### Siblings and children

No sibling of Grace is confirmed. Benny and Lewis Huffman (the two Huffman grandsons in Nannie's 1950 household) are possible maternal siblings only if Nannie is indeed Grace's mother and they are Nannie's sons. The census relationship is to the household head, not explicitly to Grace. Keep as a lead, not a sibling roster.

Geni lists Jeanette Lorene Moore as daughter, but the Jeanette identity collision described above applies. No other child of Melvin and Grace has documentary support. Eugene Moore in Wiley/Edna's 1950 household is only a grandson of Wiley; his parent is unstated.
