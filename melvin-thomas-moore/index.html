Family Relationship Notes

The 1950 census independently places Melvin T. Moore, 21, married, in Linville Township, Burke County, with wife Irene Moore, 17. The original sheet lists his occupation as cabinet-room work at a furniture factory. On the next household, the same sheet lists Wiley J. Moore, 41, Edna, 46, daughter Magret, 26, and grandson Eugene, 3; Wiley is also recorded in the furniture factory trade. The adjacency, local setting, and occupation make Wiley and Edna plausible parents for Melvin, but the census does not call Melvin their son.

FamilySearch's collaborative Edna Mae Garner profile lists Wiley and Edna as Melvin's parents and lists Margaret Marie Moore and Melvin as their children. This supports the working parent assignment, but it is tree evidence. The 1950 census index calls Magret a daughter of Wiley; a Find a Grave memorial quoting a funeral-home obituary identifies a similar Margaret (1923–2016) as the daughter of James Wiley Moore and Daisy Causby Moore and sister of Wiley John Thomas Moore. That conflict may mean the 1950 Magret is Wiley's sister, or a different person. Do not add Margaret as Melvin's sibling or child without resolving the identity.

Geni names Grace Irene Huffman as Melvin's spouse. The 1950 census names his wife only as Irene Moore, age 17, born North Carolina. The age and a derivative alias index make Grace a probable match, but no marriage or name-change record connects them directly.

Geni names Jeanette Lorene Moore, born 11 September 1962 in North Carolina, as their daughter. This is unverified. A 2024 obituary names a same-named Jeanette born in California on 23 September 1962 to Jeremiah and Marie (Quatrale) Moore. No identity bridge reconciles the conflicting profiles; do not merge them. No other child of Melvin and Grace has been established.

Life Story

Melvin T. Moore appears in the 1950 Linville Township census as a 21-year-old North Carolina-born furniture-factory worker with wife Irene Moore, age 17. A separate Wiley J. Moore/Edna household immediately follows on the same page, and both men are listed in the furniture trade. Wiley and Edna are probable parents based on that context plus FamilySearch tree evidence, but the schedule does not state the relationship. Geni identifies Irene as Grace Irene Huffman and Jeanette Lorene Moore as their daughter; both links remain under review, especially because a same-named Jeanette obituary names other parents. A Find a Grave memorial identifies Melvin's 1929–1986 lifespan and River Valley Baptist Church cemetery; its only photo is a small marker bearing “M. MOORE,” not a portrait.

All of these details come from his Geni profile; no record has been seen for any of them. He died 6 September 1986 in Morganton, Burke County, North Carolina, at the age of 57.
