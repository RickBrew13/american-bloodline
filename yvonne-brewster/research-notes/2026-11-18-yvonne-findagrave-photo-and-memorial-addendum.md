# Yvonne Suzanne Farrell Brewster — memorial photo addendum

18 November 2026. Reviewed Find a Grave memorial 154652437 and its photo tab: https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/154652437/yvonne-susan-farrell-brewster/photo

The memorial contains two distinct images credited to DancinSue:
- The marker at Arlington Memorial Gardens, Mount Healthy: “Yvonne S. Brewster, 1936–1994.” This marker was already stored and used on Yvonne's profile.
- A separate image of the Whispering Winds scattering garden sign. The memorial's Gravesite Details say Whispering Winds is a memorial garden for scattering cremains; this image is garden context, not a second portrait or a grave/headstone.

The page transcribes Yvonne Brewster Dukes's Cincinnati Post death notice (16 Feb 1994): age 57, Ocean Springs, Mississippi, “died Monday,” Mass at St. Elizabeth Seton Church, and Bradford-O'Keefe Funeral Home. Monday was 14 Feb; SSDI/NUMIDENT gives 15 Feb. Keep this one-day discrepancy visible. Find a Grave gives 26 May 1936–15 Feb 1994, Cincinnati-born, Ocean Springs death and the scattering garden. The husband section only links Charles Clay Goff, married 1970; it does not independently prove her relationship to Eugene.

A visible photo and updated citation have been added to her public profile using Find a Grave's public image URL. The image host prevents direct binary download in this research session, so no duplicate local image file was created. Existing marker and earlier source files remain in the unified Eugene–Yvonne–Carol shared family archive.
