## Mother identity conflict and possible later surname
The Wuelzer compiled family tree's Yvonne entry (ref. 182) names her parents as **Marie Suzanna Nicolai** and **Joseph Elmer Farrell**, gives Marie's later spouse as Joseph R. Courtois, and lists Yvonne as their child. It gives Joseph Elmer Farrell's dates as 21 December 1909–5 July 1976 and Marie's dates as 20 September 1911–22 April 1978. The same tree's source block references a 1950 census, Joseph's WWII draft card, and Find a Grave memorials. This is a compiled-tree lead, not proof of the parent-child link or spouse sequence.

A separately published Arlington Memorial Gardens cemetery transcription lists **Maria S. Courtois**, 20 September 1911–22 April 1978, with the alternate name Marie Suzanna Nicolai. This supports that a woman under the Courtois surname and the exact date pair was memorialized at Arlington; it does not itself prove that she was Yvonne's mother or establish the timing of a marriage to Courtois. The exact-date/name correspondence makes the record worth pursuing against the cemetery's original interment register and Marie's death certificate.

The 1955 Indiana marriage index for Yvonne and George Ernest Zurline reportedly names her mother as **Lorraine Walker**, while the Social Security NUMIDENT transcription names **Marie S. Nicolai**. Possibilities such as a stepmother, informant error or record error remain untested; do not resolve this by assumption. Needed: Yvonne's original birth certificate, 1940 and 1950 census images, Marie's marriage record(s), and the 1955 marriage application itself.

## Relationship and children evidence guardrail
The family tree attributes three children—Robert, Melanie Ann and Martin David—to Yvonne and Eugene E. Brewster and gives their marriage as 1959. The current indexed evidence is not equally strong for all three parent-child links:
- Robert's 22 March 1955 Kentucky birth index names mother Yvonne Farrell but does not identify his father. His birth predates Yvonne's 21 November 1955 marriage to George Ernest Zurline, but chronology alone does not prove Eugene's paternity or a Eugene marriage before the birth.
- Melanie (14 April 1960) and Martin (4 November 1961) have public-record index entries with Brewster surname, but those entries do not independently name both parents.
- No original Eugene–Yvonne marriage certificate or divorce decree has been located. The 1970 marriage to Charles Clay Goff, under the Brewster surname, documents that Yvonne used that name then, but does not supply the legal end date of the Eugene relationship.

Accordingly, retain the relationship and children as the established family-tree / converging-evidence working family, but do not present the exact 1959 marriage date, paternity of each child, or legal divorce date as proven. Original birth certificates and Hamilton County marriage/divorce files are needed. Do not add living descendants' private details.
