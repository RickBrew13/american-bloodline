Life Story

Lois Geraldine Shamblen was born 17 March 1938 in Cincinnati, Ohio. She married Clifford Earl Shamblen on 26 July 1958 in Cincinnati, Ohio. Their children were Sandra Sue Metzgar, born 30 June 1959; Sharon Lee Feltner, born 29 August 1960; and Stephen Richard Shamblen, born 6 February 1972. She died 9 March 2011 in Cincinnati, Ohio.
