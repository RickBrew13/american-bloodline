Life Story

George Washington Shamblen was born about August 1836 in Jackson County, Virginia — now West Virginia — the son of Jesse B. Shamblin and Margaret "Peggy" Parsons. The settled family line carries his middle name as Washington, but the records show only the initial "W." — his stone's initial was first read as "N." — so the full middle name is unverified, and the records themselves spell the surname Shamblin. In 1850, aged 14, he was living in his father's Jackson County household, and he grew up to farm at Grass Lick: a 1923 biography of his son-in-law Reuben Perry Shinn describes George's large farm there.

On 4 November 1858, in Jackson County, he married Sydna Ann Miller, the daughter of George S. Miller and Emily Lane. Together they had ten children: Josephine, Artemisia, David Clayton — the direct line — Lafayette, Martin Luther, Lucy Jane, Thomas Early, DeMarquis, Clora, and Frederick. The farm at Grass Lick held the family through the Civil War years; no record of service by George, Union or Confederate, has ever been found, and an old compiled account's story of service in an "11th West Virginia Cavalry" is refuted — no such regiment existed.

September 1883 broke the household. Sydna died on 11 September, aged 42, and seven days later, on 18 September, their infant son Frederick died, aged 2 years 3 months. No cause or shared-illness explanation survives for either death, and none is invented here. About 1885 George married again, a woman named Emma F. whose maiden name is unknown; she is known only from the 1900 census and an 1885 deed. George died on 6 January 1905 in Jackson County, aged 68, of angina pectoris, and lies in Shamblin Cemetery near Kenna, beside Sydna.

No photograph of George himself has been found. The couple folder holds four pictures — a Grass Lick cemetery overview, a Kenna historical marker, and two unidentified images — and none is a portrait of George, so none is shown here.
