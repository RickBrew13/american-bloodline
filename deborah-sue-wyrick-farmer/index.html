Life Story

Deborah Sue “Debby” Wyrick Farmer was born 24 December 1882 and died 7 August 1968 in Jackson County, Kentucky, according to her Find a Grave memorial; she is buried at Farmer Cemetery, Tyner. The local Farmer family genealogy identifies her as Charley Tipton Farmer’s wife and names seven children, including Clarence C. Farmer. The book gives their marriage date as 6 March 1901. The parent and child links are SUPPORTED by the compiled family book and cemetery memorials; civil records remain to be checked.
