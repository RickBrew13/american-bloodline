# Lewis parentage research — 17 November 2026

## Current conclusion

Edward Arnold Lewis Sr. (8 October 1947–20 February 2024) is a **SUPPORTED** identity match to the Edward A. Lewis Sr. in Rick Brewster's An American Bloodline profile. The 2024 Howard City Funeral and Cremation Services obituary names Edward Arnold Lewis Sr., gives those dates, says he was born to Arnold and Martha (Sullivan) Lewis of Lesterville, Missouri, and names sons Ed and Chad. A Legacy reproduction includes a condolence from a coworker who says he worked with Ed for many years at Mike Albert Fleet Leasing in Cincinnati. Public-directory search results associate an Edward Arnold Lewis with birth October 1947, an old Cincinnati address, and relatives Chad and Gloria; those directories are leads, not primary proof.

**Working assignment:** Edward A. Lewis Sr. in the public profile is probably the obituary subject Edward Arnold Lewis Sr.; therefore Arnold Lewis and Martha (Sullivan) Lewis are his parents. Keep the relationship graded **SUPPORTED**, not DOCUMENTED for the match between the two Edwards. The obituary itself explicitly states the obituary subject's parents; that statement is documented as the content of the obituary.

## Important discrepancy

Rick's profile identifies Gloria Lewis as Edward's spouse and Chad's mother [FAMILY ACCOUNT]. The 2024 obituary names Margie, married to Edward for over 50 years, as his wife, and names sons Ed and Chad. Do not silently replace Gloria with Margie. The available evidence does not resolve whether Gloria is another spouse, a different Edward, or another family-reported relationship; seek marriage/divorce and child birth records to bridge it.

## Similar-name census candidate excluded

A FamilySearch index for the 1950 Stark County, Ohio census lists a 3-year-old Edward A. Lewis, born Indiana, as son of Edward Lewis and Ruth M. Lewis. The same household identifies Edward Sr. as a Pennsylvania-born Church of the Brethren minister. This does not fit the available identity evidence for Edward Arnold Lewis Sr. and is retained as a distinct same-name candidate, not assigned to Rick's Edward.
