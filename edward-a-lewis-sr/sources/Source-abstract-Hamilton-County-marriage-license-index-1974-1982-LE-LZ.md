# Source abstract — Hamilton County Probate Court marriage-license indexes, 1974–1982

**Record group:** Hamilton County, Ohio, Probate Court, Marriage License Index (1817–1983), online index volumes.

**Search target:** Edward A. / Edward Arnold Lewis and Gloria, in connection with Rick Brewster's family-identified Edward A. Lewis Sr. × Gloria Lewis couple.

**Index scope statement:** Hamilton County Probate Court's public portal says the searchable alphabetical Marriage License index covers applications since 1 January 1984. The archived license index books cover the preceding period, through 1983. The official archive page explains that June 1973 through current licenses are available through its Marriage License Search route; the HCGS guide says pre-1984 licenses require the books/index.

**Checked volume section:** Official 1974–1982 scans for surnames LE–LZ, including the Lewis entries, represented by official scan URLs below. The index is OCR/text-extracted and entries were reviewed in context.

**Finding:** No entry for Edward A. Lewis or Edward Arnold Lewis was found as an applicant under surname LEWIS in the checked Hamilton County 1974–1982 volumes; no Gloria entry appeared in the Lewis section. This is not a complete marriage search. If Gloria married under her birth surname, her entry may be under that unknown surname, and the marriage could be outside Hamilton County or the indexed interval.

**Research use:** Negative result limited to this surname/time/county index section only. It neither disproves the marriage nor establishes a maiden name. Continue with 1973 index/other-county possibilities and obtain Chad's original birth record or marriage application/license.
