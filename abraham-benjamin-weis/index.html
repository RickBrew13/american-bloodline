Life Story

Abraham Benjamin Weis was born on 14 November 1910 in Cincinnati, Ohio, the son of Morris Weis and Bessie Sadler. On 6 July 1935 he married Margaret Alexandria in Newport, Campbell County, Kentucky. They raised six children — Ronald, Donald, Albert, Jack, Sharon, and Terri Ann — and lived in Cincinnati. Abraham died on 16 June 1977 in Cincinnati and is buried at Spring Grove Cemetery.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • His birth year is 1910 on his Social Security record, Find a Grave index, marriage record (age 25 in July 1935), and the FamilySearch tree, but 1911 in the Ohio Death Index and the 1950 census (age 39), and 1912 by the 1920 census age of 8. 1910 is carried as best-supported.
  • A different Abe Weis (born 1906/1907, son of Charles Weis × Bessie Weis) appears in the 1910 and 1920 censuses — that is NOT this man. Do not mix them.
  • His parents’ full names and dates are from the FamilySearch tree only: the 1935 marriage certificate names them only as “Morris” and “Bessie.” Morris Weis (1886–1963, FS 9W38-5D8) × Bessie Sadler (1890–1918, FS 9W38-5DR).
  • His siblings Samuel Weis (born 1909, FS LYBS-HR4) and Faye Weis (1914–1996, FS LYB3-31P) are tree-only; no pages have been built for them.
  • The other five children (Ronald, Donald, Albert, Jack, Sharon) come from the 1950 census and the FamilySearch tree; no pages have been built for them. Terri’s obituary names her Weis siblings, but its full text was not reachable (GenealogyBank, paid, not opened).
