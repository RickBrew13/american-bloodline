# St Francis of Assisi Catholic Church, Dayton, Kentucky — baptism transcription extracts

Transcribed by Carol Sanman in public Campbell County genealogy pages; derivative transcription, not the register image. The pages define b = born, bp = baptized, d/o = daughter of, s/o = son of.

- 1893–1896: “Mader, Maria Katharine b-4 July 1894; bp-15 July; d/o Robert and Frances (Hoh) ...”
- 1897–1901: “Mader, Michael Franz b-28 Sep 1897; bp-3 Oct; s/o Robert and Frances (Hoh) ...”
- 1897–1901: “Mader, Cyrus Johan b-10 June 1900; bp-16 June; s/o Robert and Frances (Hoh) ...”
- 1902–1906: “Mader, Anna Elisabeth b-22 July 1903 bp-29 July; d/o Robert and Frances (Hoh) ...”
- 1902–1906: “Mader, Lawrence Joseph b-18 Sep 1906; bp-23 Sep; s/o Robert and Frances (Hoh) ...”
- 1902–1906: “Mader, Frederic Leo b-20 May 1905; bp-4 June; s/o Frederic and Maria (Hauser) ...” This is explicitly not Robert and Frances’s child.
