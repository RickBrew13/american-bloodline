# Anne Elizabeth (Mader) Hess — research notes

## Identity and marriage
Anne Elizabeth Mader, born 21 July 1903 in Dayton, Campbell County, Kentucky; died 7 July 1990 in Columbus, Ohio; buried Saint Joseph New Cemetery, Cincinnati (all compiled memorial facts). Find a Grave says she married Howard Bernard Hess in 1928. A memorial photo caption explicitly identifies their wedding party.

## Parents and siblings
Find a Grave links parents Robert Joseph Mader (1861–1944) and Frances Hoh (1864–1943), spouse Howard, and two siblings: Marie Mader (1894–1984) and Michael Franz Mader (1897–1972). St Francis parish baptism transcriptions independently list children of Robert and Frances (Hoh): Maria Katharine/Maria Katherine (born 4 Jul 1894), Michael Franz (28 Sep 1897), Cyrus Johan (10 Jun 1900), Anna Elisabeth (22 Jul 1903; consistent with Anne), and Lawrence Joseph (18 Sep 1906). Thus Cyrus and Lawrence are additional probable siblings based on the derivative baptism transcription. Frederic Leo Mader (born 1905) in the same set has parents Frederic and Maria Hauser, so is not placed in this sibling group.

## Children
Howard and Anne’s 1953 obituary lists seven children; contributor memorial pages identify Katherine Hess Brady, Howard B. Hess Jr., Nancy Jane Hess Herbert, Robert Charles/Herbert Robert Charles Hess (name order varies), John Richard Hess, Mary Helen Hess Pratt, and Charles H. Hess. The original 1953 obituary transcript’s individual first names are not a perfect match to all later memorial names.

## Photos
The Hess wedding group is captioned with names left-to-right: Charles H. Hess Jr., Marie Mader, Howard B. Hess, Ann E. Mader-Hess, Lawrence Mader, Cyril Mader. The photo caption itself supplies a valuable independent family-group lead. A second couple image is present on Anne’s Find a Grave memorial, but its subjects are not identified; retain as unverified, not as Anne’s portrait.
