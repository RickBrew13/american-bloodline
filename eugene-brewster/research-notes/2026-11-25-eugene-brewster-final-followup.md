## Cross-checks and chronology
- The existing archive's Kentucky birth-index record for Robert A. Brewster (22 March 1955, mother Yvonne Farrell) is consistent with Yvonne using her birth surname when Robert was born. The 1955 Indiana marriage to George Ernest Zurline Jr. falls eight months later. This means the order of Yvonne's relationships and the legal parentage/marital status surrounding Robert's birth need original records; do not assume Robert was born during a legal marriage to Eugene.
- The 1959 Eugene marriage entry is chronologically compatible with Melanie's 1960 and Martin's 1961 births, but compatibility is not proof.
- The May 1970 marriage to Charles Clay Goff is supported by the original register image already linked in the earlier Drive-exported research notes. It establishes that Yvonne was using Brewster at that later marriage; it does not by itself establish a divorce decree or exact end date for her relationship with Eugene.
- Yvonne's 1994 memorial and death-notice evidence remains contradictory on 14 vs. 15 February. No new evidence resolved it.
- Yvonne's family-tree entry names parents Marie Suzanna Nicolai and Joseph Elmer Farrell. The older Indiana marriage index instead gives a bride's mother as Lorraine Walker. This conflict remains open; the profile should not promote either name without a birth record or original marriage application.

## Eugene: evidence status after this pass
- **Parents:** Homer William Brewster Sr. and Sarah Hazel Kelly are directly named by their obituaries and multiple sibling/family notices already in the archive.
- **Siblings:** Eunice, Aileene/Aileen, Elmer, Earl and Homer Jr. are supported by the mother's and father's obituaries, Eunice's death notice, Elmer's obituary, and Aileene's obituary. These sources identify the sibling group, although not every notice enumerates every sibling.
- **Spouses:** Yvonne Suzanne Farrell is a strong working spouse identification supported by the compiled family tree, children/birth-index links, her later name use and Geni; the exact Eugene marriage date remains unproved. Carol Ann Craig/Hillman is supported by Kentucky marriage indexes for 19 Nov 1977 and 9 Nov 1979, but the original certificates are still needed to explain why two index entries appear.
- **Children:** Robert, Melanie and Martin are identified in the compiled Wuelzer tree; the existing profile has Kentucky birth-index support for Robert and indexed public-record entries for Melanie and Martin. Do not publish current addresses or other living-person details. The original records needed to prove each parent-child link are not in this archive.
- **Birth:** exact day remains unknown. 1940 census index says Eugene T Brewster, age 6, Tennessee; 1950 census index says Eugene E Brewster, age 16, Tennessee; Ohio death index says born 1933 Tennessee, died 16 Oct 1983 age 50. The 1940 middle initial T is not harmonized with E without reading the original census image.
- **Death:** Ohio death index identifies Eugene E. Brewster, certificate 071732, volume 25392, died 16 Oct 1983 at Fairfield, Butler County, residence Hamilton. The record says married but does not name spouse or occupation. The original certificate remains unavailable in the public material reviewed.
- **Burial:** Greenlawn Cemetery, Milford, Section 21, lot 88, supported by Find a Grave and matching headstone image. The cemetery interment card/register has not been inspected.
- **Occupation, military, property:** no reliable source located in this pass to establish his adult occupation, military service, or real-property ownership. The 1950 census index transcription's occupation/industry is too unclear ("Paye…"/"Libaray") to interpret; inspect the original image before using it.
- **Photographs:** his folder contains the original headstone and separate surname-only Brewster family stone images. No authenticated portrait or family photograph identifying Eugene was found. Carol's portrait and Yvonne's memorial marker remain correctly stored under their own person folders; Charles Clay Goff's headstone was correctly routed to his own folder.
