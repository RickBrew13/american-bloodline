# **Eugene E. Brewster (1933–1983) and family: research report, second pass**

Research notes, 9 November 2026\. Living children are left out of this file. Grades: DOCUMENTED \= an original record, official index or a contemporary newspaper notice states it · SUPPORTED \= consistent indirect evidence · UNPROVED \= tree, family note, data broker, or a conflict.

---

## **Headline finds**

1. **Carol is fully identified:** Carol Ann **Craig** (14 Feb 1937 Cincinnati – 21 Sep 2019 Warren Co., OH), later Hillman, Henderson, Brewster and Rechel. Her Find a Grave page names her four husbands, including “Gene Brewster, no children, 8 years.”

2. **Yvonne’s 1994 death notice was found** (Cincinnati Post, 16 Feb 1994), along with her Find a Grave page. She was cremated, and her ashes were scattered at Arlington Memorial Gardens.

3. **Yvonne’s Zurline marriage record was found** (21 Nov 1955, Indiana). The bride’s birth date matches Yvonne’s exactly.

4. **Eugene’s grave** has a plot number, 2 photos on Find a Grave, and a BillionGraves entry.

**Conflict to flag:** the 1977 KY index says the bride had **one** previous marriage and the 1979 index says **two**. The family names two husbands before Eugene (Hillman and Henderson). By 1979 the true count would be three, because Eugene himself was a prior husband. The KY indexes under-count, or the Henderson marriage was very short or informal. The settling record is still KY certificate 24676 (1977) or 28705 (1979). Ruled out: Carole Jean Hillman × Don Martin, 19 Oct 1963, Clermont Co. (ark Q214-YSDT). The parents differ, so this is not her.

## **Records that would settle what’s left**

1. **Ohio death certificate 071732 / vol. 25392** (Eugene, 1983): occupation, military, informant (probably Carol), funeral home, and an obituary date.

2. **Gilligan Funeral Homes or Carol’s family** (Mary Beth Hillman-Greco is the family source on Find a Grave): may hold photos of Gene Brewster from 1975–83.

4. **Mississippi death certificate** (Yvonne, Jackson Co., Feb 1994): settles 14 vs 15 Feb and her husband at death. **Bradford-O’Keefe** may have records, and the Sun Herald / Mississippi Press of 15–17 Feb 1994 may carry a fuller obituary with survivors (her children).
