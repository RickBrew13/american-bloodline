## 3. Parents and same-name candidates
A 1950 U.S. census index cited on the previous profile lists a **Charles A. Asher**, born about 1940 in Kentucky, in the Hamilton, Ohio household of Russell and Eliza Asher, alongside children Marie and Wanda. This is a plausible lead for the father of Julie, but:
- The original 1950 population schedule has not been inspected.
- The index's Charles A. is not directly linked to Aileene.
- Russell and Eliza's own public profiles have conflicting family-marker and compiled-tree details.
- KYGenWeb's “Lineage of John Deaton and Isabella Spencer” gives a 1975 marriage between Eliza Combs and Russell Asher and lists Charles, Wanda and Marie as their children. That marriage date is impossible: Eliza's profile records her death as 9 December 1971, and the 1950 census index already places Russell and Eliza together. Do not use the compiled 1975 date or its implied child assignment as proof. Source: https://kygenweb.net/breathitt/databases/johndeaton_isabellaspencer/d6.htm .
- A derivative index surfaced a Charles Asher born 14 March 1943 and died October 1969 in Hamilton County, Ohio (Cheviot); another derivative page repeats those dates. No parent, spouse, child or other identifying evidence ties that person to Aileene. It is **not** attached to this profile.
- Other same-name candidates, including Charles Asher Jr. (1931–2023), Charles Thomas Asher (1947–2022), and Charles Asher (born 11 October 1953, died 3 September 2009 in Hamilton), do not have an identity bridge to Aileene and are not attached. The 1953-born candidate is too young to be Julie's father at her 1964 birth.
