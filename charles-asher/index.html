Life Story

Charles Asher was the husband of Aileene Willie Brewster, married by 1964. He was the father of her three younger children: Julie Marlene (Asher) Korman, Charlene (Asher) Eubanks, and Greg Asher. No marriage record has been found for Charles and Aileene. The marriage rests on daughter Julie’s Social Security record, which names Charles Asher and Aileene W. Brewster as her parents, and on Aileene’s 2015 obituary, which names Greg Asher as her son. No birth date, death date, birthplace, or other biographical details for Charles have yet been verified.

Research Notes and Open Questions
