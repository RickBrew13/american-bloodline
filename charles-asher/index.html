Life Story

Charles Asher is named as a parent alongside Aileene W. Brewster on daughter Julie Marlene Asher Korman’s Social Security Numident. That is the strongest direct identity evidence currently linking this Charles to Aileene’s family, but it does not establish that the two were legally married. Aileene’s 2015 obituary names Greg Asher as her son but does not identify Greg’s father. Her daughter Charlene’s obituary index names Aileene Gilpin as mother and Charles Asher among the named family, but the full obituary has not been read; it does not yet establish Charles’s paternity. No marriage certificate, birth certificate, obituary, death certificate, or other record establishing Charles’s own dates, birthplace, parents, occupation, residence or burial has been found.

  5. KYGenWeb, “Lineage of John Deaton and Isabella Spencer” — compiled lineage identifies Eliza Combs, later Russell Asher’s wife, and children Charles, Wanda, and Marie. Compiled secondary source, not an original record. KYGenWeb page.
  6. This is a possible alternative Asher sibling cluster, not yet connected to Aileene's Charles. Obituary.
  7. PeopleLegacy burial index — lists a Charles Asher born March 1943, died 27 October 1969, buried Manchester Memorial Gardens. This may be the Charles named as Taft's brother, but the aggregator is secondary and no original death or cemetery record has been inspected. Burial index.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Charles Asher’s fatherhood of Julie rests on her Social Security record; of Greg, on Aileene’s obituary naming him as her son and on the marriage; no record yet names Charles as Charlene’s father.
  • A 1950 census index places a Charles A. Asher, born about 1940 in Kentucky, in the Hamilton household of Russell and Eliza Asher with Marie and Wanda. This is a possible identity lead only; the original census image has not been inspected and no record connects that child to Aileene’s Charles. The KYGenWeb compilation’s claimed 1975 Eliza–Russell marriage is chronologically impossible because Eliza died in 1971 and the couple appears in the 1950 household; do not use that compiled marriage date or child assignment as proof.
  • The KYGenWeb compilation gives an apparent 1975 marriage date for Eliza and Russell, which conflicts with its own list of their children and the 1950 census household. Do not use 1975 as an established marriage date.
  • Charles’s exact dates, birthplace, parents, siblings, other spouses/children, residences, occupation, military service, property, death and burial remain unverified. A second, potentially related cluster has surfaced: Taft Asher's 2019 obituary names a deceased brother Charles among the children of Mack and Clarissa Helton Asher; a secondary burial index lists a Charles Asher born March 1943 and buried in Manchester Memorial Gardens in 1969. This could be the same man, but the identity is not proven and the burial index conflicts with the derivative Ohio death-index residence/date details. Do not merge him with Aileene's Charles or the separate 1950 Russell/Eliza Asher household without an original record.
