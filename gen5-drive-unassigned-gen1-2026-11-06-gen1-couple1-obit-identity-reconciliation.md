# 2026-11-06 — Homer Brewster death notice: identity reconciliation

**Couple:** Homer William Brewster Jr. × Judith Mae “Judy” Palmer  
**Question:** Does the Dayton Daily News death notice supplied by Dayton Metro Library refer to Homer William Brewster Jr.?

## Directly inspected notice
The Dayton Metro Library emailed a scan described as a death notice, identified as *Dayton Daily News*, 29 Sep 2000, page 12, under Montgomery County. Text: “BREWSTER, Homer, 67, formerly of Dayton, of Cincinnati, Wednesday. Memorial services 4 p.m. Saturday. (Newcomer-Barclay).” This is a contemporary notice, but it gives no middle name or relatives. The family request had cited page B6; the supplied scan says p. 12. Preserve this pagination discrepancy.

The age 67 points to birth circa 1932–33 and does not fit Homer Jr.'s family-reported 18 Mar 1939 birth (age 61 on 27 Sep 2000). Wednesday, given the Friday 29 Sep issue, is consistent with 27 Sep but the notice itself does not state a numeric death date.

## Competing Ancestry person-result lead (tree/profile claim, not record evidence)
Ancestry's public name-results page has two distinct Homer William Brewster person profiles with conspicuously conflicting birth years:
- `homer-william-brewster-24-23t7vj1`: b. 18 Mar 1912, Brewstertown, Morgan Co., TN; d. 27 Sep 2000, Hamilton, Ohio; parents William Alexander Brewster and Mary Ellen McKinney; spouse Sarah Hazel Kelly; children include Homer b. 1939.
- `homer-william-brewster-24-1bqs5gm`: b. 18 Mar 1939, Morgan County, TN; d. 27 Sep 2000, Preble, Ohio; parents listed as Homer William Brewster (1912–2000) and Sarah Hazel Kelly.

These are Ancestry public person-profile pages and can reflect submitted tree data or merges; they are not independent vital records. The 1912 profile is chronologically consistent with the project's Homer Sr. (b. 1911) but its death date 27 Sep 2000 appears incompatible with the parent profile's stated death date of 1991 and may be a conflation/duplicate. Do not adopt either profile's Hamilton-vs-Preble place or 2000 death data as proof.

## Assessment
At present, the notice's age 67 is a material contradiction with the target man's 1939 birth. The notice could concern another Homer Brewster in the Dayton/Cincinnati area; the Ancestry 1912 profile may itself be a conflation, and it is not enough to identify the notice's subject. The overlap of name, metro area, weekday and approximate date is a lead only. **Do not add this notice as a source for Homer Jr.'s death or use it to document his death place/date** unless a bridge appears (middle name, relatives, funeral home file, address, spouse/children, matching vital record).
