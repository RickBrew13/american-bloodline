Family Relationship Notes

Amanda is named as “Mandy Baker” in Merida’s NUMIDENT and as Amanda/Mandy Baker Wagers in the 2006, 2017, and 2025 family obituaries. These sources support her role as mother with Shirley of the ten-child Wagers family.

Amanda’s mother is Polly Baker, named on Amanda’s 7 August 1919 marriage record (Laurel County, Kentucky), which lists no father for her. She married Shirley as Amanda Helton; whether Helton was a prior married name or her birth surname is not established by the record, and no Helton spouse is named in any record reviewed. No record reviewed names Amanda’s siblings. The Jefferson Baker household in Clay County (Jefferson × China Baker) does not include Amanda — her mother is Polly Baker, not China — so Amanda is not Jefferson’s daughter and no record makes her his granddaughter. Baker alone is not enough to attach a same-name family.

The couple’s shared Drive Pictures folder has no files. Only son Merida’s 2000 reunion photo and VA marker were found in his own archive; neither is a portrait or burial record of Amanda.

Find a Grave reports Amanda 1901–1984; the 1930, 1940 and 1950 census images give ages that do not fully reconcile with that birth estimate. Preserve the conflict.

Life Story

Amanda “Mandy” Baker Wagers is named as mother of Merida in his NUMIDENT, and as Amanda/Mandy Baker Wagers in Merida’s, David’s and Kathleen’s obituaries. This supports Amanda as mother in the ten-child family group. Her mother is Polly Baker, named on her 1919 marriage record; her father is not named on any record reviewed, and no siblings have been found. No Baker relatives are attached by surname alone.

The Find a Grave family listing gives Amanda Baker Wagers as 1901–1984. The 1930, 1940 and 1950 census images list her as 28, 32 and 47, ages that do not reconcile with each other or with the listed birth year; dates remain qualified pending original records.
