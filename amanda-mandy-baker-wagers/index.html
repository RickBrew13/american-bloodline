Family Relationship Notes

Amanda is named as “Mandy Baker” in Merida’s NUMIDENT and as Amanda/Mandy Baker Wagers in the 2006, 2017, and 2025 family obituaries. These sources support her role as mother with Shirley of the ten-child Wagers family.

No reviewed record identifies Amanda’s parents, siblings, or other Baker relatives. Baker is not enough to attach a same-name family.

The couple’s shared Drive Pictures folder has no files. Only son Merida’s 2000 reunion photo and VA marker were found in his own archive; neither is a portrait or burial record of Amanda.

Find a Grave reports Amanda 1901–1984; 1930 and 1940 census indexes give ages that do not fully reconcile with that birth estimate. Preserve the conflict.
