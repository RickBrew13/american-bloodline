Life Story

Amanda “Mandy” Baker Wagers is named as Merida Henry Wagers’s mother in his Social Security NUMIDENT and obituary. Her identity as Merida’s mother is supported by two census indexes: in 1930 she appears as Mandy Wagers with infant Merida, and in 1940 Amanda Wagers appears with ten-year-old Merida in Clay County, Kentucky.

The Find a Grave family listing gives Amanda Baker Wagers as 1901–1984. The census indexes list her as 28 in 1930 and 32 in 1940, ages that do not reconcile with each other or with the listed birth year; dates remain qualified pending original records.
