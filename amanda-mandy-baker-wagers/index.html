Family Relationship Notes

Merida Henry Wagers’s Social Security NUMIDENT names Shirley Wagers and Mandy Baker as his parents. The 1930 census index lists Mandy Wagers as Merida’s mother in the Clay County household, and the 1940 index lists Amanda Wagers as his mother in the same family. Merida’s obituary names Shirley and Amanda Baker Wagers as his parents.

Additional names—Ida, Pearlie, Dudley, Linea/Lina, Aline/Alian, David, Price, and Freddie—appear across census indexes and obituaries as members of the Wagers family. Their exact identities, spellings, and individual relationships to Amanda need to be checked against original records; the names are retained as family research leads.

The Find a Grave family listing gives Amanda’s years as 1901–1984. Census indexes give her age as 28 in 1930 and 32 in 1940, which do not reconcile with each other or the reported birth year. No birth or death certificate, burial record, or record identifying Amanda’s parents has been located.
