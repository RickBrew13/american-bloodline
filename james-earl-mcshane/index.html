Life Story

James Earl McShane and his wife Doris raised their daughters Susan and Trish in Norwood, Ohio, where both girls graduated from Norwood High School. He was born in Hopkinsville, Kentucky, on 31 August 1919, the son of James Anthony McShane and Lillian Gardner, and served in the Army through World War II. He died on 15 January 1996, still living in Norwood.
