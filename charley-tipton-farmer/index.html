Family Relationship Notes

The local Genealogy of the Farmer family identifies Charley as a son of Stephen Farmer and Mary Frances Cook Farmer, gives his marriage to Debby Wyrick as 6 March 1901, and names seven children. The book is a compiled family source, not an original civil record. FamilySearch’s compiled Stephen Farmer profile also places Charley in the family and supplies the sibling group.

The seven children and sibling names are retained from the family genealogy and compiled profiles. The family book reports Stephen and Mary Frances had 16 children, while the material reviewed here names eleven siblings; the sibling list is therefore partial, not a complete list. Original birth, marriage, census, and death records have not been reviewed for every relationship.

Charley’s death is reported only as 1972 in the accessible memorial material. His exact death date, place, last residence, occupation, religion, burial, and parents’ original-record proof remain outstanding. The Find a Grave media could not be identified from the accessible captions as a portrait or grave marker, so it is not used as his hero image.

Life Story

The cataloged Genealogy of the Farmer family identifies Charley Tipton Farmer (born 20 August 1880) and wife Debby Wyrick (born 24 December 1882) as parents of Clarence C. Farmer and six other named children. It gives their marriage date as 6 March 1901. Find a Grave’s Debby memorial gives her death as 7 August 1968 and links her to spouse Charlie Tipton Farmer (1880–1972). The parent-child set is reported by the family book and derivative memorial data, but original civil records have not yet been checked.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Marriage date discrepancy: the family book says 6 March 1901; Clarence’s shared gravestone says Clarence and Mae married 29 November 1928, which is their later-generation marriage and not Charley/Debby’s. No Charley/Debby civil marriage record has been inspected.
  • Dates for children and the listed children derive primarily from the family book. The book is valuable family-source testimony but is compiled and OCR can err.
  • Charley’s exact death date/place and his portrait remain unverified. The accessible Find a Grave text gives only a spouse-linked 1880–1972 lifespan for him.
  • No portrait of Charley or Debby was verified in the accessible results. Debby’s memorial has one photo, but the extracted page does not identify whether it is a portrait or grave image; it is linked as a source rather than represented as a portrait.
