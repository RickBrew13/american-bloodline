Life Story

Andreas Lorch was born about 1818–1819 at Varjas, in the Banat — his birth is inferred from his age of 20 in his marriage entry; no baptism has been found. The 1818–1820 window of the Varjas baptism book was checked image by image and holds no baptism for a son of Petrus Lorch. His father Petrus is named in the marriage register; his mother Anna Maria Ochsenfeld is named in the 1809 marriage index and the Warjasch family book. He is known in America as Andrew Lorch. No photograph of Andrew has been found.

On 5 February 1839, at Varjas, Andreas married Catharina Metsang, 19, of Varjas — the marriage register entry, #13 of 1839, names him as the son of Petrus Lorch and her as the daughter of Jacobus Metsang, and names fathers only. The register writes her name Metsang; Dreyer writes Metschang; their son Johann’s Ohio death certificate writes Matchow. They had two documented children: Péter, baptized at Varjas on 31 August 1844, and Johann, born in 1862, who emigrated to Cincinnati in 1906 and married Theresa Novak. More children were likely born across the long gap between Péter and Johann; none is documented.

Andrew and Catherine stayed in the old country — no record shows either ever leaving Hungary, and no death or burial record has been found for either. Their son Johann sailed from Bremen on the Brandenburg and reached Baltimore on 8 April 1906, a carpenter going to join his brother-in-law Ludwig Hruschka in Cincinnati; he died there on 28 June 1911 and is buried at St. Joseph Old Cemetery. His daughter Margaret married Peter Palmer; she is the direct line. No occupation or military service is recorded for Andrew.
