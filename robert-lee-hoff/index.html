Life Story

Robert Lee Hoff was born 2 November 1930 in Clendenin, West Virginia. His 2007 funeral-home obituary identifies Betty Shircliff as his ex-wife and names Gregory Paul and Douglas Earl Hoff as his sons. The obituary supports the Hoff parentage link; a Geni entry separately lists James Wayne Shircliff as a father to Douglas, but no source found explains that entry, so Douglas’s paternity conflict remains unresolved. Robert died 25 October 2007 in Charleston, West Virginia, and was buried in Osborne Cemetery, Clendenin.
