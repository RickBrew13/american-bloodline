Life Story

Stanley Peter Cossman was born 18 July 1920 and died 15 November 2018, according to his Mihovk-Rosenacker Funeral Home obituary, which gives his age as 98, identifies him as a World War II veteran, and names Beulah M. Cossman (nee Cornett) as his late wife. His son Stanley William’s obituary names Stanley Peter as the father. Stanley’s Find a Grave memorial identifies his parents as John Adam Cossman and Henrietta Stella Bost; an Ancestry profile for Carl J. Cossman, a reported brother with the same parents, independently lists John Adam Cossman and Henrietta Bost. This parentage is supported by compiled family records, not established by an original birth record; the spelling Cossmann also appears for the parents.

All four photos from Stanley Peter’s Find a Grave gallery are included, plus the official funeral-home obituary image. Two gallery items are visibly other Cossman grave markers and are labeled as such rather than assigned to Stanley.
