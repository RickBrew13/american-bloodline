Life Story

The available compiled sources identify Henrietta Stella Bost (also written Henrietta Stella Cossmann) as Stanley Peter’s mother and Henrietta Bost as Carl J. Cossman’s mother. Stanley’s memorial lists Carl among Stanley’s siblings, which supports the same family grouping. Her exact birth name and parentage are not established by an original record in this review.
