Life Story

Felice Peluso is known only from his daughter Angelina’s Manhattan marriage certificate of 27 May 1903. The certificate names Felice as the bride’s father and Marianna Napolitano as her mother, and nothing more about either of them.

No dates are known for Felice himself — no birth, marriage, or death record has been found, and no other children have been found. Where he was born, when he lived, and where he died are all still open. His daughter Angelina was born in Italy, but her own birth date and place are not settled: the compiled profile gives 19 August 1885 at Roccapipirozzi, and Dominick’s 1934 naturalization declaration gives 17 September 1885 at Sperone.
