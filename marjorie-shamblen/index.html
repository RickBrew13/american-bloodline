Life Story

Marjorie Pilman Shamblen was born 23 August 1932 in McKee, Kentucky. She was also the wife of Thomas Charles Pilman. She married Clifford Earl Shamblen in 2011 in Cincinnati, Ohio. Her children were Michael Pilman and Kimberly Burrous, who died in 2020. She died 23 February 2020 in Cincinnati, Ohio.
