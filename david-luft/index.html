Family Relationship Notes

  • David is named as Alexander Luft’s father on the 1 July 1912 Hamilton County marriage application; the same application names Mina Edel as Alexander’s mother. Alexander’s 1944 Ohio death certificate instead gives his parents as “unknown,” so the application remains the only located direct record naming David as father.
  • A public Geneanet contributor tree lists David Michel Luft (25 Nov 1824–26 May 1905), born Hammerstein and died Flatow, as son of Michael Jacob Luft and Rahel Rahle Borchardt; spouse Minna Mirdel Edel. It lists siblings Nume, Atalie, Henriette Jette and half-sister Bertha Beile. Because the application also reports David Luft, Mina Edel and Alexander’s birth in Germany, this is a plausible identity lead, not an established match.
  • The Geneanet tree lists five children born 1858–1871 but does not list Alexander. Alexander’s reported age on the 1912 application implies birth about 1872; an omitted child is possible, but the gap and similar names do not prove identity. David’s parents and siblings remain unassigned on this profile pending a record bridge.
  • A search of the Jewish Cemeteries of Greater Cincinnati public register for Luft found Luftchitz entries but no matching David or Alexander burial. This is a bounded database search, not proof of burial elsewhere.
