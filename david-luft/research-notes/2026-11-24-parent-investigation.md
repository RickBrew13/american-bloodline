# David Luft — Generation VII parent investigation

**Reviewed:** 24 November 2026

## What is documented

Alexander Luft's 1 July 1912 Hamilton County, Ohio, marriage application reports Alex, age 40, born Germany, as son of David Luft and Mina Edel. Alexander's 25 May 1944 death certificate reportedly says his parents were “unknown.” Thus the application is the only located source naming David as Alexander's father. It does not give David's age, birthplace, home town, parents, siblings, or a fuller name. Alexander's Germany birthplace makes a German residence for David plausible only as an inference.

## Unlinked German Luft tree lead — do not attach

A public Geneanet tree maintained by David Mark Dror lists a **David Michel Luft** (25 Nov 1824–26 May 1905), born Hammerstein and died Flatow in the Prussian/Posen region, as son of Michael Jacob Luft and Rahel Rahle Borchardt. It gives siblings Nume Luft, Atalie Luft and Henriette Jette Luft, plus half-sister Bertha Beile Luft; it lists spouse Minna Mirdel Edel and five children: Machol Jacob (1858), Lina (1860), Eva (1862), Oscher Oskar (about 1868) and Nathalie (1871). Source is a contributor tree with a “Geni World Family Tree” reference, not primary records: https://gw.geneanet.org/daviddror?n=luft&oc=&p=david+michel&type=fiche.

This is not established as Alexander's father David. The five-child list does **not** include Alexander, the dates/identity differ from the currently documented Cincinnati family, and there is no record bridge from this Flatow family to Alex Luft's 1912 Cincinnati application. The similar given/surnames and German origin are insufficient. Do not add Michael Jacob, Rahel Borchardt, Nume, Atalie, Henriette, or Bertha Beile to this profile or Generation VIII without a bridge.

## Research outcome

- David's own parents: unknown; the Geneanet David Michel tree is an unlinked lead only.
- David's siblings: unknown; the candidate tree's siblings belong to its David Michel and are not shown to be this David.
- Spouse: Mina Edel is named on the same marriage application as Alexander's mother; no independent marriage record located.
- Child: Alexander, based on the 1912 application only; Alexander's death certificate conflicts by recording his parents as unknown.
- No portrait, headstone, exact death, or primary record for David has been located.
