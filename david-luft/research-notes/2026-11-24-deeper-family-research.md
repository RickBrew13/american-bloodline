# David Luft — deeper parent/family review

**Reviewed:** 24 November 2026

## Confirmed record evidence

Alexander Luft's 1 July 1912 Hamilton County marriage application reports David Luft as Alexander's father and Mina Edel as his mother. Alexander's 1944 Ohio death record reports the parents as “unknown.” The marriage application therefore remains the only located record naming David as Alexander's father. The original application is linked at FamilySearch, but its image redirected to sign-in during this review.

Alexander was reported born in Germany on the application; no town is supplied in the citation currently available. Any German residence attributed to David is inference, not a fact in the application.

## A plausible David Michel Luft candidate — not yet identified as this David

A public Geneanet contributor tree by David Mark Dror lists David Michel Luft (25 November 1824–26 May 1905), born Hammerstein and deceased at Flatow in the Prussian/Posen region, as son of Michael Jacob Luft and Rahel Rahle Borchardt. It lists spouse Minna Mirdel Edel and siblings Nume Luft (1828–1884), Atalie Luft, Henriette Jette Luft, plus half-sister Bertha Beile Luft. It lists five children born 1858, 1860, 1862, about 1868, and 1871. The cited tree points to a contributor family tree and Geni World Family Tree; it is not an inspected original record.

This is a plausible candidate for Alexander's father because the marriage application gives David Luft, Mina Edel and a German-born Alexander; the candidate tree has David Michel and Minna Mirdel Edel in the German/Posen region. Alexander's reported 1912 age implies birth about 1872, close to but after the candidate tree's youngest listed child in 1871. However, the tree does not list Alexander and no record bridges the Cincinnati man to this family. That absence could reflect an incomplete tree, but cannot be treated as proof. The candidate's parents and siblings are **not** attached to this David's profile.

## Research outcome and scope control

- David's own parents and siblings remain unidentified for the current profile. The Geneanet David Michel family is the leading specific candidate found, not an established assignment.
- JCGC's public cemetery database search for “Luft” returned Luftchitz entries but no matching David or Alexander. This only excludes a match in that database's returned entries; it does not establish where either man was buried.
- No new people were added to parent/sibling cards, no generation links changed, and no new profiles/folders were created.

## Next evidence needed

Inspect the original 1912 application and Alexander's full 1944 death certificate; identify Alexander's exact German birthplace; then seek an original German/Polish civil, Jewish-community, migration, or family record that places Alexander as child of David Michel Luft and Minna Mirdel Edel. Until then preserve the candidate as unresolved.
