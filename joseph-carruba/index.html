Life Story

Joseph Carruba was born 31 August 1891 in Italy. He married Mary Schillaci on 17 June 1922 at Thomas, in Jefferson County, Alabama. Their first children were born in Alabama — Sam about 1924, Rosa about 1926, Carmella about 1929 — and the family moved to Harlan County, Kentucky about 1930. Josephine was born at Totz in 1931, Susie about 1933, and Cora on 22 September 1934. By the 1940 census the household at Cumberland held Joseph and Mary with eight of their children.

Joseph died 10 December 1970 in Harlan County and is buried at Cumberland. His own parents are not named in any record found; a christening record for a Joseph Carruba at San Cataldo, Sicily, in 1892 has not been proved to be his. His daughter Cora's 1953 marriage certificate names him as her father, and the 1940 census lists her as his daughter — the two records that identify him as her father.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The family name is spelled Carruba and Carubba across the records; the profile uses Carruba, the spelling on Cora's marriage certificate and the censuses. His wife's name is spelled Schillaci, Schillace, Schillari and Shillaci; the profile uses Schillaci, the spelling on their 1922 marriage record.
  • Joseph's own parents are not named in any record found. A christening record for Joseph Carruba, 14 October 1892, San Cataldo, Caltanissetta, Sicily, naming parents Liboria Mistretta and Salvatore Carruba, has not been proved to be his — the Social Security Death Index gives his birth as 31 August 1891.
  • The family tree lists only seven of his children (Sam, Carmella, Josephine, Susie, Tina Maria, John and Thomas) and omits Cora, Rosa, Katherine, Margaret and Helen; a relationship was deleted on the tree in April 2026. The list here follows the censuses, the marriage certificates and the 2007 obituary.
  • The tree also lists a second spouse, Mary Tudisco, with no marriage events and no children; that attachment is unproved and is not carried here.
  • The tree copies the marriage place as "Thomas, Prairie, Arkansas" from an index; the original marriage record says Thomas, Jefferson County, Alabama.
  • The 1920 census shows a Joe Carruba, 27, single, at Millport, Alabama; he is probably Joseph before his marriage, but the record is attached to a different tree person and gives a different immigration year, so it is a lead only.
  • No 1950 census household has been found for Joseph, Mary or Cora in Harlan County under Carruba or Carubba; the family may have been in Alabama in 1950 or enumerated under another spelling.
