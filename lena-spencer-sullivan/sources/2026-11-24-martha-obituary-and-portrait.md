# Martha J. (Sullivan) Lewis — 2014 obituary and portrait

Source: Belleville News-Democrat obituary, published 27 March 2014 and reproduced by Legacy: https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/belleville/name/martha-lewis-obituary?id=20576088

The family-submitted obituary identifies Martha J. Lewis, née Sullivan, born 16 July 1928 in St. Louis, died 26 March 2014 in Maryville, Illinois, and buried at St. John Cemetery, Collinsville. It says her husband Arnold Lewis preceded her in death and that they married 16 May 1947. It names parents Matthew and Lena née Spencer Sullivan and siblings Mary Jane Schuab, Dorothy Sullivan, Matthew Edward Sullivan Jr., James S. Sullivan, Robert J. Sullivan, Dan J. Sullivan, Jo Ann Graham, and Elizabeth Wright. It lists children Patricia Dominick, Pamela Embick, Edward A. Lewis, Jeffery Lewis, and Thomas Lewis. The obituary page displays a small portrait of Martha; the local cropped copy is in `img/family-photos/`.
