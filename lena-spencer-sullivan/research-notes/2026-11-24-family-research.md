# Lena (Spencer) Sullivan — Gen VII source and family review

**Reviewed:** 24 November 2026.

## Evidence

Martha J. Lewis's 2014 obituary identifies her mother as Lena, née Spencer, wife of Matthew Sullivan. The original 1950 census image for Reynolds County, Missouri, ED 90-5, sheet 4, enumerated 4 April 1950, lists Lena O. Sullivan, age 58, born in Missouri, as wife of Matthew E. Sullivan, with son Daniel G., 18, and daughter Joann, 16. The image was inspected directly and saved in Matthew E. Sullivan's archive; this profile cites the shared source rather than duplicating the scan.

This supports Lena Spencer Sullivan as the Gen VII mother, while the “O.” middle initial in the census and the obituary's “Spencer” maiden name are not reconciled by an original vital record.

## Children / sibling reconciliation

Martha's obituary names her as a daughter and lists eight siblings. Census household members Daniel G. and Joann may match obituary sibling Dan J. Sullivan and Jo Ann Graham, but those variants remain unmerged pending a bridge. Other siblings named by the obituary are recorded on Martha's profile and in the parents' family notes; no separate sibling profiles were created.

## Boundaries and source URLs

Lena's parents, siblings, exact vital dates and burial are not established. No Gen VIII profiles were created.
