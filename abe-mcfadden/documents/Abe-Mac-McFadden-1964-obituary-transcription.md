# Abe “Mac” McFadden — obituary clipping transcription

**Source:** Hamilton Daily News Journal, 9 December 1964; clipping contributed to Find a Grave memorial 196169819’s photo tab by Jetta McQuiston on 17 April 2024: https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/196169819/abe-mcfadden/photo

> **Abe McFadden Succumbs At 59**
>
> Abe (Mac) McFadden, 59, R.R. 3, Brookville, Ind., died at 11:10 a.m. Tuesday in Mercy Hospital. He had been ill since June and hospitalized three weeks.
>
> Mr. McFadden was born in London, Ky., April 17, 1905, a son of James and Delia Ann Gregory McFadden. He had resided on a farm near Brookville since May and prior to that time had lived in Oxford.
>
> Mr. McFadden was a truck driver for the Burger Brewing Co. for 14 years, retiring June 12, 1964. He was a member of the Teamsters Union. He was educated in the schools of London, Ky., and on June 1, 1948 he married Rose Bolser in Covington. He enjoyed hunting and fishing.
>
> **Relatives Listed**
>
> Mr. McFadden leaves his wife, Mrs. Rose McFadden; one son, Abe McFadden Jr., Brookville; two daughters, Mrs. Laura Lee Lawson, Irving, Tex.; Mrs. Etta Marcum, Cincinnati; one sister, Mrs. John Overby, London; 14 grandchildren; two great-grandchildren; half brothers and half sisters; other relatives and friends.
>
> He was preceded in death by his parents and his first wife, Maggie Mooney McFadden, in 1943.
