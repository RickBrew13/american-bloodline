# McFadden family group and Etta link

Abe’s 9 Dec. 1964 newspaper obituary names his children as Laura Lee Lawson, Abe McFadden Jr., and Etta Marcum. It states that his first wife, Maggie Mooney McFadden, died in 1943; he later married Rose Bolser on 1 June 1948. The clipping is archived locally with transcription.

Laura Lee (McFadden) Lawson Johnson’s obituary says she was the oldest of three children born to Abraham “Abe” and Maggie Marie Mooney McFadden, and names brother Abe Jr. and sister Etta Fay Marcum. This supports the three children and the Etta–Maggie link. Find a Grave memorials provide the same child links and Maggie’s dated marker; these remain derivative sources.

Find a Grave gives Abe’s parents as James Garfield “Bud” McFadden and Cordilia Ann Gregory; the 1964 obituary spells them James and Delia Ann Gregory McFadden. Preserve this name-form conflict; no original birth record was inspected. Maggie’s Find a Grave memorial links father Lee B. Mooney but does not identify her mother.
