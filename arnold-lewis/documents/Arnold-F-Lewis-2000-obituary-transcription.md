**Arnold Lewis**

Arnold F. Lewis, 77, of Collinsville, Ill., born Thursday, Aug. 23, 1923, in Lesterville, Mo., died Friday, Nov. 24, 2000, at Maryville Manor, Maryville, Ill.

Mr. Lewis was a member of American Legion Post, Collinsville, Ill.; Purple Pride; and Operating Engineers Local 148 in Maryville, Ill. He was instrumental in starting Khoury League in Collinsville, Ill. He was a veteran of World War II and served in the Army. He was retired from Union Electric as a boiler operator.

He was preceded in death by his parents, James S. and Emma, nee Lee, Lewis; a brother, Elmo Lewis; and two sisters, Audrey Pinkley and Wilma Meyers.

Surviving are his wife, Martha, nee Sullivan, Lewis; his children, Edward Lewis of Cincinnati, Ohio, Patricia Dominick of Collinsville, Ill., Pamela Embick of Collinsville, Ill., Jeffrey Lewis of Granite City, Ill., and Thomas Lewis of Freeburg, Ill.; a brother, Winford Lewis of Essex, Mo.; three sisters, Osa Mazo of Collinsville, Ill., Mildred Sherrill of Arnold, Mo., and Bernice Durham of Dexter, Mo.; 15 grandchildren; and one great-grandchild.

Visitation was listed for 27 November 2000 at Herr Funeral Home; funeral services were listed for 28 November 2000 with burial at St. John Cemetery, Collinsville.
