# Arnold Lewis × Martha Sullivan — Arnold identity, parentage, and sibling review

**Reviewed:** 24 November 2026. **Generation VI target confirmed on the live index:** Arnold Lewis × Martha Sullivan.

## Arnold's likely identity

A derivative transcription of Arnold F. / Arnold Floyd Lewis's SS-5 (number 66353334323, May 1941) reports birth on 23 August 1923 at Lesterville, Missouri, and names father James S. Lewis and mother Emma L. Lee. The transcription reports his death as 24 November 2000. SortedByName also transcribes military enlistment data placing Arnold F. Lewis in the U.S. Army from 28 January 1943 to 29 December 1945. The SS-5 and military source images have not been directly inspected.

Martha J. Lewis's 2014 obituary says she married Arnold Lewis on 16 May 1947 and lists their five children. A FamilySearch 1950 census index result lists an Arnold Lewis, born about 1924 in Missouri, in St. Louis with wife indexed “Mertha Lewis” and children Edward A. and Patricia A. The year 1924 is one year later than the SS-5 transcription, and “Mertha” may be an indexing error; the original 1950 household image has not yet been matched/inspected. Still, the matching wife/children group supports the identity.

Edward Arnold Lewis Sr.'s 2024 funeral-home obituary names his parents as Arnold and Martha (Sullivan) Lewis of Lesterville, and Martha's obituary names a son Edward A. Lewis and the same children Patty, Pam, Jeff, and Tommy. Together these sources make Arnold Floyd Lewis a strong working identification for Martha's husband and the father in the 2024 notice; a direct marriage or child birth record is still desirable.
