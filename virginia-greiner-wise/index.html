Life Story

Virginia G. Wise (née Griener; her daughter’s 1976 marriage license spells the maiden name Greiner) was born 17 February 1920 and died 4 October 2006 at age 86 in Cincinnati, Ohio. Her obituary identifies her as the wife of the late Samuel P. Wise, mother of Sam “Skip” Wise and Monica Kelly, and sister of the late Gladys Adams. Monica’s 1976 marriage license names Virginia Greiner as her mother and Samuel P. Wise as her father, confirming that the Monica Kelly in Virginia’s obituary is the same daughter later known as Monica Lynn Wise.

Virginia’s obituary states that her graveside service was held at Wise Cemetery in Irvine, Kentucky, on 10 October 2006. Estill County cemetery listings identify a Wise Cemetery in the Irvine area, but the individual grave marker and cemetery register have not been located. Her parents, occupation and other family relationships remain unknown.
