Family Relationship Notes

Monica Lynn Wise’s 1976 Hamilton County marriage abstract names Virginia Greiner as her mother and Samuel P. Wise as her father, and gives Monica’s birth date as 17 July 1956 in Ohio. Virginia’s 2006 obituary independently identifies Samuel P. Wise as her late husband and Monica Kelly as their daughter. Find a Grave family memorials report Virginia’s parents as Stuart R. Greiner and Corrine J. Lanier Greiner. They also report a full brother, Stuart Richard Greiner (1921–1976), and her father’s daughter with his first wife, Gladys Lacy Greiner Adams (1908–1998), as a paternal half-sister. Virginia’s obituary simply calls Gladys her sister; the half-sibling distinction comes from the memorial family groups. Virginia’s obituary names children Sam “Skip” and Monica Kelly; Samuel’s Find a Grave memorial says the couple adopted Monica and “Sam Jr.” The two records suggest Sam Jr. may be Sam “Skip,” but that identity is not independently established. No original birth certificate, census entry naming Virginia’s parents, or adoption record was reviewed.

Life Story

Virginia G. Wise (née Griener; her daughter’s 1976 marriage license spells the maiden name Greiner) was born 17 February 1920 and died 4 October 2006 at age 86 in Cincinnati, Ohio. Her obituary names her late husband Samuel P. Wise, children Sam “Skip” Wise and Monica Kelly, and sister Gladys Adams. Find a Grave family memorials report her parents as Stuart R. Greiner and Corrine J. Lanier Greiner; they identify Stuart Richard Greiner as a full brother and Gladys Lacy Greiner Adams, her father’s daughter from his earlier marriage, as a paternal half-sister. These parent and sibling links are memorial-reported; original birth/family records have not been reviewed. Samuel’s memorial says he and Virginia adopted two children, Monica and “Sam Jr.” “Sam Jr.” may be the son called Sam “Skip” in Virginia’s obituary, but the identity link is unverified. Her obituary reports a graveside service at Wise Cemetery in Irvine, Kentucky; an individual marker or register has not been located. Estill County cemetery listings identify a Wise Cemetery in the Irvine area, consistent with the obituary, but Virginia’s individual interment has not been confirmed from a marker or register.
