# Virginia Greiner Wise — parent, sibling and child audit

**Reviewed:** 26 November 2026

## Parents

The new Gen VII pages for Stuart Raiford Greiner (1883–1926) and Corrine J. Lanier Greiner (1903–1974) link to Virginia's profile, and her Parents card now links back to both. Virginia's own Find a Grave memorial lists Stuart and Corrine as parents. Stuart's memorial narrative lists Virginia among his three children; Corrine's memorial lists Virginia as her daughter. These are multiple derivative memorial sources; no original birth certificate or census household directly naming Virginia's parents was found in this pass.

## Siblings and half-sibling

- **Stuart Richard Greiner (reported 1921–1976):** Corrine's Find a Grave memorial identifies him as her son; Stuart R. Greiner's memorial says the children of his second marriage to Corrine were Virginia and Stuart. This supports a reported full-brother relationship, but no original record or standalone memorial for Stuart Richard was inspected.
- **Gladys Lacy Greiner Adams (1908–1998):** Virginia's obituary calls Gladys her sister. Stuart R. Greiner's memorial says Gladys was his child with first wife Mary Leola Tucker; Virginia was a child of his second family with Corrine. This supports Gladys as a paternal half-sister. Find a Grave's Virginia page explicitly places Gladys in its half-sibling panel.

## Children

- **Monica Lynn Wise / Monica Kelly:** Monica's 1976 Hamilton County marriage abstract names Samuel P. Wise and Virginia Greiner as her parents. Virginia's 2006 obituary names daughter Monica Kelly; the shared parents and first name support that this is Monica Lynn Wise. Samuel Preston Wise's Find a Grave memorial reports Monica as one of the two children he and Virginia adopted.
- **Sam “Skip” Wise:** Virginia's 2006 obituary explicitly names Sam “Skip” as her son. Samuel Preston Wise's Find a Grave memorial reports the couple adopted a son “Sam Jr.” It is plausible that Sam Jr. is Sam “Skip,” but no record connecting the names was found. Leave the identification unresolved and attribute each name to its source.
