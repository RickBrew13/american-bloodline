**Date:** 25 November 2026  
**Folder-first review:** Virginia's existing profile and the related Monica and Samuel P. Wise profiles/research notes were inspected before research. No verified personal portrait or headstone image was present in her own archive.

- Husband: the late **Samuel P. Wise**.
- Children: **Sam “Skip” Wise** and **Monica Kelly**.

The obituary text is repeated on Tribute Archive:
https://www.tributearchive.com/obituaries/4113531/virginia-g.-wise

This is a funeral-home obituary, not a death certificate or cemetery register. It resolves prior open questions:

- Names Monica Kelly as their daughter.
- Names Sam “Skip” Wise as their son.
- Gives Virginia's life dates and reports Wise Cemetery as the burial place.

## Monica identity connection
Monica's original 1976 Hamilton County marriage license names her father as Samuel P. Wise and her mother as Virginia Greiner, and gives her birth as 17 July 1956 in Ohio. Virginia's obituary names her daughter Monica Kelly and identifies Virginia's husband as Samuel P. Wise. The matching two parents and daughter first name make Monica Kelly a strong match to Monica Lynn Wise/Cossman. The obituary documents the Kelly surname by 2006 but does not give the date or reason for the name change, nor identify a later spouse.

## Cemetery location
The obituary says “Wise Cemetery in Irvine, KY.” Estill County KYGenWeb lists a Wise Cemetery in the county, and RoadsideThoughts gives a Wise Cemetery location in Estill County, northeast of Irvine:
- https://kygenweb.net/estill/site/cemetaries/
- https://roadsidethoughts.com/ky/cemeteries/wise-cemetery-xx-estill-profile.htm

This is geographically consistent with the obituary, but no individual memorial, marker photograph or cemetery register has been found for Virginia. Her burial is reported by the obituary; the exact grave/plot is not independently confirmed.

The obituary page contains a profile image, but the image itself is a religious angel graphic, not a photograph of Virginia. It was not copied or added as a portrait.
