Family Relationship Notes

Wilbur’s parents are identified in the 1900 and 1910 census households, and the 1940 census places his widowed father, James Hope Taylor, in Wilbur’s household. James Hope Taylor’s death certificate names Wilbur as the informant.

Wilbur and Alice Costigan are named as Shirley Ann’s parents in her 1933 Kentucky birth index and in contemporary 1956 newspaper engagement notices. The exact marriage date and place for Wilbur and Alice have not been located.

Find a Grave and a Butler Cemetery survey report Wilbur’s burial at Butler Cemetery, with Alice nearby. A memorial photo is linked in the sources, but the image and inscription could not be independently inspected during this profile update. The burial is retained as reported, not treated as independently verified.

World War I and World War II draft registrations show that Wilbur registered. The World War II registration lists the Louisville and Nashville Railroad as his employer; draft registration does not establish military service.

Life Story

Wilbur Kyle Taylor was born in Pendleton County, Kentucky, on 16 June 1899, the son of James Hope Taylor and Ora (Mullins) Taylor . He and his wife Alice had a daughter, Shirley Ann, born in Boone County in 1933 . In August 1956 Mr. and Mrs. Wilbur K. Taylor of Butler announced Shirley's engagement to Fairest Richard Green . He died in Butler on 26 March 1976 and is buried in Butler Cemetery .
