Life Story

Eliza Combs was born in Breathitt County, Kentucky, around 1904–1905. Her memorial names her as the daughter of Richard and “Rosie” Combs; the larger Breathitt family compilation identifies them as Richard Craig Combs and Rosa Deaton. Rosa’s recorded death in 1918 and the 1920 census transcription listing Eliza, about 15, in Richard’s household are consistent with that placement, but neither original schedule nor Eliza’s birth record has been reviewed.

In 1921, the compiled family lineage reports Eliza married William J. Bryan Turner. Their children were later known as the Turner siblings in her memorial and in a 2005 obituary for Eliza’s sister Lacie. The lineage reports that Eliza later married Russell Asher; Find a Grave gives the Asher marriage as 1942, and the 1950 index shows the couple with Charles A., Wanda, and Marie in Hamilton, Ohio. Wanda’s obituary confirms Russell and Eliza as her parents.

The exact link between the 1950 child Charles A. Asher and the older Charles A. Turner entry remains unresolved. The previously cited “married in 1975” is not the couple’s marriage year: it appears to be a corrupted/shifted entry because the source separately reports Bryan Turner died in 1975, while Russell and Eliza are indexed together in 1950. Keep 1942 as the memorial-reported year, not as a primary-record-proved date.

Eliza died in Hamilton on 9 December 1971 and is recorded as buried at Greenwood Cemetery. No independent original birth, marriage, or death record has yet been reviewed for her.
