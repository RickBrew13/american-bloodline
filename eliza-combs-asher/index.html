Life Story

The 1950 census index lists Eliza Asher in Hamilton County, Ohio, with Russell Asher and children Charles A., Wanda, and Marie. A compiled Breathitt County genealogy identifies this Eliza as Eliza Combs, daughter of Richard Craig Combs and Rosa Deaton, and lists her with Russell and their children. The compiled source’s stated 1975 marriage date conflicts with the child chronology and the 1950 household, so it is not treated as established.
