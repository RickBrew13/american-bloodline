Family Relationship Notes

William’s parents are Henry B. Cornett and Mary Helen Randolph. The original 1910 census lists William M. (age 12) as their son; the 1925 marriage index independently gives “Hen Cornett” as the groom’s father. FamilySearch’s compiled profiles supply the full parent names. This parent pair is high confidence (about 95%).

FamilySearch’s compiled Henry–Mary family group lists seven children: James Franklin, “Nannie” Nancy Ethel, Mattie Frances, Luther Blaine, William, Theoplis, and Elyza. The 1910 schedule directly shows Luther B., William M., Theopolis, and Elsie A. The last may correspond to compiled Elyza, but spelling and identity remain unresolved. The other six are William’s reported siblings, with names and dates attributed to the compiled profile.

FamilySearch’s William–Roberta group lists five children: Beulah Mae, William McKinley Jr., Ralph Edwin, Sharon Lee, and Sandra Sue. The original 1950 census confirms William, Roberta, Ralph, Sharon, and Sandra in one household. It does not list Beulah or William Jr.; Beulah’s obituary and compiled family profiles support the remaining two child links.

The 1939 Hamilton County directory lists “Wm McK (Roberta)” at 629 3d Road and separately lists Norbert and Rebecca at the same address. Their relationship is not stated; they are not added as children or siblings. The 1941 county deed index lists William M. and Roberta Cornett as grantees from Amity Village Co. (book 1902, p. 625); the actual deed has not been examined. A different 1939 entry names Lora, widow of McKinley, at another address; she and her late husband are not merged with William.

The existing memorial galleries include a photograph captioned “William & Roberta Cornett.” It is a shared couple image, not an individual portrait. Other attached individual images remain described as unverified; no identities were inferred from the gallery alone.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Name forms and dates: William M. Cornett, William McKinley Cornett, and the 1925 marriage-index form “Bill Cornett.” His WWI draft card gives 6 August 1897 and Somerset, Kentucky; his compiled profile gives Pulaski County; 1950 census estimates 1898. Keep source-specific variants.
  • Parentage: Henry B. Cornett and Mary Helen Randolph are high-confidence parents (about 95%). The 1910 original census lists William M. (12) as their son, and the 1925 marriage index names “Hen Cornett” as father. Henry–Mary’s compiled family group lists six additional siblings; the 1910 census supports four entries and preserves Elsie/Elyza discrepancy.
  • Children: compiled sources list Beulah Mae, William Jr., Ralph, Sharon, and Sandra. The 1950 original census confirms William, Roberta, Ralph, Sharon, and Sandra together. Beulah’s obituary names her Cornett siblings, including late William Jr.; no original birth record for Beulah was reviewed.
  • 1939 directory at 629 3d Road lists Norbert and Rebecca beside William and Roberta, but gives no relationship. They remain unnamed household leads, not children or siblings. The 1941 deed index lists William M. and Roberta as grantees (book 1902, p. 625); the deed instrument was not reviewed.
  • Do not merge William with Lora Adams’s late husband McKinley Cornett: the directory lists Lora at a different address, and her obituary’s children do not connect to William M. Cornett Sr.
  • Photo review: the pre-existing galleries include a memorial-captioned “William & Roberta Cornett” couple photograph, plus additional photos with uncertain sitter identifications. Keep the couple label attributed; do not assign portraits where sitter identity is unverified. Added 1950 census scan is documentary evidence, not a portrait.
