Family Relationship Notes

William’s Find a Grave memorial lists Roberta as his wife and Beulah among his children. Roberta’s memorial also lists William as her spouse and Beulah as their child. Her public FamilySearch profile reports their 23 December 1925 marriage and five children, naming Beulah, William Jr., Ralph, Sharon, and Sandra. These are compiled memorial/tree claims; original vital records have not been reviewed here.

The 1939 Hamilton County directory independently lists “Wm McK (Roberta)” as a factory worker at 629 3d Rdg, Reading. The 1941 Hamilton County deed index lists “William M. & Roberta Cornett” as grantees from Amity Village Co. (book 1902, page 625), suggesting a property acquisition; the deed itself has not been examined, so the parcel and exact transaction terms remain unknown. William’s parents, siblings, exact birth and death dates, and burial details are still not established by the material reviewed for this profile. The displayed portrait is retained because it was selected for this memorial, but the memorial gallery itself notes that its identity/context is not independently verified. The couple photograph and grave-marker image remain in the gallery.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • An original Beulah birth record has not been inspected; parent-child links are reported, not DOCUMENTED.
  • William is listed as William M. Cornett Sr. and William McKinley Cornett in different compiled sources. Preserve the name variants.
  • William’s exact birth and death dates, parents, and full sibling set need further source review.
  • The 1939 directory also lists Norbert (student) and Rebecca at 629 3d Rdg; their relationships to William and Roberta are unknown and they are not added as children or siblings.
  • The same 1939 directory separately lists Lora Cornett, widow of McKinley, at 506 Harrison Avenue in Hamilton. Her 2004 obituary names her late husband McKinley and four children (Lillian, Ilene, Harold and Willis), but does not connect her to William M. Cornett Sr. Do not assume she was William's mother or that her late husband was this William.
