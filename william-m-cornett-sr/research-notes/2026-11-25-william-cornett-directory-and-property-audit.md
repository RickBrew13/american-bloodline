# William M. Cornett Sr. — 1939 directory and 1941 deed-index audit
**Date:** 25 November 2026

## New directly relevant evidence

### 1939 Hamilton County directory
The Williams' Hamilton County Directory for 1939 lists:
- **“Cornett Wm McK (Roberta) fctywkr h629 3d Rdg”** — William McK. Cornett, wife/household partner Roberta, factory worker, home at 629 3d Road, Reading.
- At the same address the directory also lists **Norbert Cornett**, described as a student, and **Rebecca Cornett**. The directory does not state their relationship to William and Roberta. They may be relatives or members of the same household, but they are not added as children or siblings without a birth/census record.
- The directory separately lists **“Cornett Lora (wid McKinley) h506 Harrison av Ha”**, a widow named Lora whose late husband was McKinley Cornett. This is a different entry at a different address; it is not evidence that Lora was William's mother.

Source: Williams' Hamilton County Directory, 1939, alphabetical Cornett entries:
https://apps.cincinnatilibrary.org/citydirectory/WilliamsHamiltonCountyDirectory_1939.pdf

### 1941 Hamilton County deed index
The official Hamilton County Recorder grantor–grantee index lists **Amity Village Co.** as grantor and **William M. & Roberta Cornett** as grantees, deed book **1902**, page **625**. The index row has no transaction date displayed. This is strong evidence that the couple were named in a real-estate transaction, likely as purchasers, but the deed instrument has not been inspected. The parcel, legal description, price, exact date, and whether it was their 629 3d Road home remain unknown.

Source: 1941 Hamilton County Recorder index, page 4, lines 208–210:
https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1941trtea.pdf

## Important McKinley Cornett name collision

A separate FamilySearch profile identifies **McKinley Cornett (1899–1936)**, married to **Lora Adams** on 28 November 1925 in Blackey, Letcher County, Kentucky. Their children are listed as Lillian, Ilene, Harold Remus and Willis. Lora's 2004 obituary independently names her first husband McKinley and these four deceased/surviving children; it does not name William M. Cornett Sr. The 1939 directory lists Lora as widow of McKinley at 506 Harrison Avenue, Hamilton, while the separate William McK (Roberta) entry is at 629 3d Road, Reading.

Therefore, do **not** attach Lora Adams or her late husband McKinley Cornett as William M. Cornett Sr.'s mother/father or spouse. The same-surname/middle-name similarity is a namesake trap.

## William's identity and family — evidence status
The existing profile reports William M. Cornett Sr. as born 1897 and died 1980, and reports his marriage to Roberta Cromer on 23 December 1925. The Find a Grave memorial, Roberta's memorial, and her FamilySearch tree support the couple and children, but original birth, marriage and death records have not yet been inspected. The 1939 directory independently supports a William McK. Cornett living with/associated with Roberta and working as a factory worker in Reading, Ohio. The 1941 deed index independently supports a William M. and Roberta Cornett property transaction in Hamilton County.

The exact birth/death dates, birthplace, parents, siblings, and burial details remain unproven. The 1939 directory's Norbert and Rebecca residents at the same address are open family leads only. No direct record has yet identified William's parents.
