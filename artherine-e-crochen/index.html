Life Story

The 1920 census records “Artha Rene Crackeson,” age 9, as a daughter in Albert and Callie’s household in Hayneville, Alabama. The 1973 Alabama death index for Artherine Niles separately names father Albert Crocker, mother Callie Martin and spouse Leon. Her age, Alabama birthplace, later Leon household and matching parent names support the identification of the census child as this Artherine, although her surname is spelled differently across records and the death-certificate image is restricted. The 1950 census and 1959 marriage index independently place Artherine Niles/Crochen with Leon and identify her as Ronald’s mother. Find a Grave reports 9 October 1909–28 July 1973; those exact dates and burial remain unverified from an original vital or cemetery record.
