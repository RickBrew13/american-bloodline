Life Story

The 1959 Alabama marriage index names “Artherine E Crochen” as Ronald C. Niles’s mother. The 1950 Birmingham census records Artherine Niles as Leon Niles’s wife and lists Ronald among their children. A public Ancestry result hints at a 1940 “Arthrene Niles” with spouse Leon, but the original schedule has not been checked. Her parents remain unidentified. Find a Grave reports dates of 9 October 1909–28 July 1973; those dates have not yet been verified from civil or cemetery records.
