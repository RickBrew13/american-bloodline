# Artherine E. Crochen — parentage follow-up
Date: 17 November 2026

## Research question
Identify parents of Artherine E. Crochen, identified as Ronald Coleman Niles's mother in the 1959 Alabama marriage index.

## New lead from this pass
Ancestry's public 1940 U.S. census search page for the query "Arthrene Niles" displays a result as "Arthrene Niles | Leon | abt 1913" (spouse column / estimate as exposed in the public result table). This is a plausible earlier record for the Birmingham woman later indexed as Artherine Niles with Leon in 1950, but the search-result snippet does not expose the household's residence, her parents' birthplaces, or original image. The spelling and birth estimate are not exact to 1950's age-based estimate (1910), but within a plausible census-age discrepancy. It is only a tentative identity lead until original schedule/household is inspected.
Source/search page: https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/2442/?name=Arthrene_Niles

## Evidence checked
- Artherine E. Crochen appears in FamilySearch's 1959 Alabama marriage index for Ronald C. Niles and Leslie M. Collins. The record links her as Ronald's mother; the same site's extracted relatives table also labels her Leon's wife, but the underlying marriage record concerns Ronald and does not prove an Artherine–Leon marriage. https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:Q21F-3YS3
- The 1950 census index (and schedule inspected previously) places Artherine Niles, age 40, Alabama-born, in Birmingham with Leon and children Jessie L., Ronald, and Altheria. It does not name her parents. https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:6F7J-PLSD
- Find a Grave's Artherine E. Niles memorial reports 9 Oct 1909–28 Jul 1973, Elmwood Cemetery, and links spouse Leon and child Ronald. This is a user-managed/compiled cemetery memorial, useful as an identity and vital-date lead but not a parent source; no parents are listed. https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/240731443/artherine_e-niles
- FamilySearch's "Similar Records" panel attached to the 1959 entry suggests an Arthrene Niles 1940 census item and a separate Artherine Niles Alabama death index item, but does not expose the details/record link in the page text.
- Exact-name collection queries for Artherine/Arthrene + Crochen in 1910/1920 census indexes did not return a relevant hit. Broader indexed searches were dominated by unrelated names; do not treat these as exhaustive negative evidence.
- Crochen cluster checked: 1950 original census page 5 lists Albert Crochen Sr, Callie, and Albert III (indexed as grandson) in a Birmingham-area household; a different 1950 Birmingham census household lists Albert L. Crochen (b. about 1913) with wife Thelma and children. Carl Crochen's 2016 obituary names parents Albert Lewis Crochen and Thelma Pugh Crochen and says Carl was the fifth child; he names siblings Albert Jr., Thelma Boston, Sharon Pugh, and deceased brothers Arthur Gene, George Alvin, and Wilbert. This establishes a distinct Crochen nuclear family, but none of these records names Artherine as a daughter/sister or bridges her to this family.
- Ancestry's public Albert Crochen results give a compiled profile for Albert L. Crochen with parents “Albert Cracheron” and “Mattie (Callie) Martin.” This is not an original parentage record and cannot establish a connection from Artherine to them.

## Assessment
No reliable record found naming Artherine's own parents or birth siblings. The 1940 Arthrene + Leon search hit makes the Artherine–Leon identity stronger to investigate, but provides no parent names. The Albert Sr/Callie and Albert Jr Crochen family is an UNPROVED lead only; do not assign either as Artherine's parents. No Gen VI couple/profile additions warranted.
