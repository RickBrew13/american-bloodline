Family Relationship Notes

Henrietta Green is Minnie M. Lyon Powell’s mother. Her daughter Minnie’s 1952 death record names Henrietta Green as her mother, and the 1880 census lists 9-year-old Minnie in the household of Andrew and Henrietta Lyon in Coal District, Harrison County. Minnie’s birth record, for an unnamed daughter born 11 September 1870 at Lamberts Run, names Henrietta Lyon as the mother.

Henrietta married Andrew Lyon in Harrison County. The family tree gives 10 March 1865, and the 1900 census says Andrew had been married 34 years, which points to about 1866; no marriage record has been found.

Life Story

Henrietta Green was born in Harrison County, West Virginia, and married Andrew Lyon. The 1880 census lists her in Coal District with Andrew and three of their children, including their daughter Minnie, 9, and the 1900 census lists her in Clarksburg with Andrew and three grown children. She died on 8 April 1908 and is buried in Odd Fellows Cemetery in Clarksburg. Her daughter Minnie married Dexter Powell in 1903 and was the mother of Rowland Edmund Powell.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • 25 November 2026: Her exact birth and death dates come from her tombstone; no West Virginia death certificate for her has been found. The 1900 census age 55 and her tombstone both point to a birth year of 1845; the 1880 census gives her age as 30, which points to about 1850.
  • The family tree names her parents as Richard Henry Green (1815–1892) and Susan Waldo Bartlett (1824–1901), married 30 June 1843 in Harrison County. The 1850 census lists her as “Harriet Green” in her father Richard Green’s household.
