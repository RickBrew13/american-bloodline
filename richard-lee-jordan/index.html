Life Story

Richard Lee Jordan is known so far from one Ohio county marriage index entry. When his daughter Tammy Lynn Jordan married Timothy W. Campbell in Clermont County, Ohio, on 11 May 1985, the index listed Terri Weis as mother of the bride and Richard Lee Jordan as Terri’s husband.

Tammy’s sister Damaris was also born a Jordan, on 1 September 1962, and both sisters are named as Terri’s daughters in Terri’s 2002 obituary. That makes Richard the probable father of both. Terri later married Daniel “Gus” Patten, who raised Damaris and Tammy as his stepdaughters. Richard’s birth, death, parents, and later life are not yet known.
