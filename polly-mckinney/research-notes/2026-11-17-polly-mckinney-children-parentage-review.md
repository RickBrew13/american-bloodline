# Polly McKinney — children and parentage review

**Research date:** 17 November 2026  
**Question:** Identify Polly McKinney’s children and parents, and establish whether the Polly listed on the profile is the same woman as Jackson Ahmeek McKinney’s wife.

## Summary

The existing Polly McKinney profile says she was the wife of Jackson Ahmeek McKinney, but labels that relationship FAMILY ACCOUNT and has no dates, maiden name, children, or parent names. Existing research notes say Polly was not found in the two reviewed Ancestry tree pulls; a Geni roster lists Jackson as husband of “Polly McKinney,” and the Dallas × Laverne couple profile says Jackson married Polly.

A contemporary 2014 obituary for Jackson names his wife as **Pauline M. McKinney (Northcott)** and identifies her as the love of his life. It names three children: **Michael McKinney**, **Joan Lindland** (husband Richard II), and **Patrick**, who predeceased Jackson. It also names grandchildren Patrick, Tracey, Richard III, Lori, and Thomas Jackson, five great-grandchildren, and one great-great-granddaughter. This is strong evidence for Jackson’s children and spouse, but the obituary does not say that Pauline was known as “Polly.” The “Polly” profile / Geni label therefore remains a likely but unproven identity match to Pauline M. (Northcott) McKinney. Do not silently merge them or attach these children to Polly until the name/identity bridge is verified.

No reliable source found in this pass identifies Pauline/Polly’s parents. A broad FamilySearch search for Pauline Northcott and Jackson McKinney returned 1950 census candidates including Pauline K. Northcott (born 1942, daughter of Bruce E. and Christine M. Northcott in Onaway, Michigan), plainly too young to be Jackson’s wife married in 1943; it is not a match. No parent names should be added to Polly’s profile from that result.

## Assessment

- Pauline = the “Polly McKinney” on the public profile / Geni: **UNPROVED**, plausible nickname/identity match but no source explicitly says Pauline was called Polly.
- Polly / Pauline’s parents: **UNPROVED / not found** in this pass.
