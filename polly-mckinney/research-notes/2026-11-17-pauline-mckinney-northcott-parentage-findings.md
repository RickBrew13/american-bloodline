# Pauline M. (Northcott) McKinney — parentage findings

**Research date:** 17 November 2026

## Finding
Pauline, whom Rick identifies as “Polly,” was the wife of Jackson Ahmeek “Jack” McKinney. Jackson’s 2014 obituary calls her Pauline M. McKinney (Northcott), says they married in 1943, and identifies her as being from Bay City, Michigan. Eugene D. Northcott’s 2009 obituary independently names his parents as Stanley and Pauline (Timrick) Northcott and lists “Pauline (Jackson) McKinney of Auburn” among his three sisters. These two obituaries together document Pauline M. (Northcott) McKinney as a daughter of **Stanley F. Northcott Sr. and Pauline (Timrick) Northcott**, and as Jackson’s wife. Rick confirms Pauline is the Polly on the family profile. The identity is therefore **DOCUMENTED**, with the nickname clarification from Rick’s direct family account.

The parents are not conjectural: Eugene’s obituary directly identifies them and their daughter Pauline. A separate 1950 index household in Bay City lists Pauline Northcott with husband Stanley and children Eugene D. and Patricia; a later obituary for Eugene is the stronger source linking Pauline (Jackson) McKinney as his sister. The 1950 entry may reflect a distinct/earlier household status, and should not be used to infer extra children of Jackson and Pauline.

## Pauline’s parents and siblings
- Father: Stanley F. Northcott Sr. (surname suffix “Sr.” appears in sister Virginia’s Find a Grave search result; Eugene’s obituary says “Stanley,” without suffix).
- Mother: Pauline Timrick Northcott (Eugene obituary gives “Pauline (Timrick) Northcott”).
- Siblings named across Eugene’s obituary: Eugene D. Northcott, Dorothy Mosher, Virginia (Otto) Post, and Pauline (Jackson) McKinney. The obituary says Pauline had three sisters; Eugene was her brother. The wording/source identifies siblings, not a complete birth order.
