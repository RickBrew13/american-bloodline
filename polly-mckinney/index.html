Life Story

Pauline M. (Northcott) McKinney was Jackson Ahmeek “Jack” McKinney’s wife; the Bay City Times obituary says they married in 1943 and names Michael, Joan Lindland and Patrick (who predeceased Jackson) as their children. Eugene D. Northcott’s obituary identifies Pauline (Jackson) McKinney as his sister and names their parents as Stanley and Pauline (Timrick) Northcott. Rick’s family identifies Pauline as “Polly.”
