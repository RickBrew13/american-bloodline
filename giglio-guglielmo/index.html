Life Story

Giglio Guglielmo is known from two New York City certificates for his son Domenico. The marriage certificate of 27 May 1903 names Giglio as the groom’s father, and the death certificate of 12 July 1942 names him again, both times with his wife Angelica Russo as the mother. Both certificates give his birthplace as Italy and nothing more.

No dates are known for Giglio himself — no birth, marriage, or death record has been found, and no other children have been found. Where in Italy he was born, when he lived, and where he died are all still open. His son Domenico was born at Trivio, in Formia, on 18 April 1875, and reached New York on 9 April 1899.

The record that would say more about Giglio is Domenico’s birth act at Formia. The Formia birth registers for 1875 are not on FamilySearch; Formia was in Caserta province before 1927, so the register may sit in the Caserta archive. That route has not yet been run.
