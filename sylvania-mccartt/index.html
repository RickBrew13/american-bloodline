Life Story

Sylvania McCartt was born in Scott County, Tennessee, about July 1886, the daughter of Plez and Sallie McCartt DOCUMENTED. She married Mike Stephens in 1910, and in that year's census she was working as a nurse DOCUMENTED. She raised five children in Scott County: Beatrice, Willie "Bill," Homer, Christine and Mary Jane DOCUMENTED.

In 1926 Sylvania married Tim Sexton, and in 1930 she was keeping house for him with her two youngest daughters DOCUMENTED. She died at the Scott County Nursing Home on 6 May 1970, aged 79, survived by all five children, and was buried in Keeton Cemetery at Huntsville DOCUMENTED.
