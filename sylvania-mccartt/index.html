Family Relationship Notes

The 1900 Scott County census places Sylvania in the household of Plez and Sallie McCartt; her 1970 obituary names Plez and Sally as her parents. The 1910 and 1920 census records place Willie/Bill in her household, and the 1910 schedule calls Willie Mike Stephens’s stepson. Mike is therefore a stepfather, not Bill’s biological father. Sylvania’s 1970 obituary names William McCartt as a surviving son and gives his residence as Browns Mills, New Jersey. A user-contributed Find a Grave family group lists Addie, John Halk, Claude and Nannie as Sylvania’s siblings; retain that sibling set as derivative until corroborated independently. Sallie’s surname/maiden-name variation remains unresolved.

Life Story

Sylvania McCartt was born in Scott County, Tennessee; the 1900 census gives July 1886, while her headstone says 4 July 1890. She married Mike Stephens in Scott County on 4 February 1910. Census records show her older children Beatrice and Willie in Mike’s household as stepchildren, along with children Homer and Christine; the 1930 census shows daughters Christine and Mary with her later husband T. S. “Tim” Sexton. Her 1970 obituary names all five surviving children, including William McCartt of Browns Mills, New Jersey. The 1910/1920 census and 1940 draft registration support identifying William as the Bill McCartt in this project, but his biological father is still unknown.

In 1926 Sylvania married Tim Sexton, and in 1930 she was keeping house for him with her two youngest daughters . She died at the Scott County Nursing Home on 6 May 1970, aged 79, survived by all five children, and was buried in Keeton Cemetery at Huntsville .

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Birth year conflict remains: 1900 census gives July 1886; headstone and Find a Grave give 4 July 1890. The obituary gives age 79 in May 1970, closer to 1890 than 1886. Do not silently normalize.
  • Sallie’s maiden name is unresolved. Find a Grave links “Sallie ‘Sarah’ Lowe Dixon,” while this profile and obituary use McCartt; no primary marriage/birth record was reviewed to settle it.
  • The four siblings listed here come from Sylvania’s user-contributed Find a Grave family links; sibling relationships and parentage need independent corroboration.
  • The 1940 draft record identifies Bill McCart’s wife as Flora and birthplace as Huntsville; together with census ages and the 1970 obituary, this supports but does not name Bill’s father.
  • Mike Stephens is a stepfather to Willie/Bill; keep him off the Gen VII index as Bill’s father.
