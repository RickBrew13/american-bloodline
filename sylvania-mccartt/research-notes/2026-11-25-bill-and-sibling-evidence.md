# Sylvania McCartt — Bill and sibling evidence (25 November 2026)

The 1910/1920 census and Sylvania’s 1970 obituary support Willie/William “Bill” McCartt as her son. Mike Stephens is expressly his stepfather. The 1940 draft registration index gives Bill’s birthplace as Huntsville and wife as Flora McCart.

Find a Grave’s user-contributed sibling group names Addie Monroe McCartt Davis, John Halk McCartt, Claude McCart and Nannie Lulu Young. Listed on Sylvania’s profile as derivative sibling leads; they are not individually profiled.
