Life Story

Edna McKinney was the older sister of Ruth Laverne McKinney, a daughter of Dallas Cecil McKinney and Laverne Hurt. She married Otto Graff, and they had Raymond Otto Graff and Linda Ellen Graff. Raymond was Edna's oldest son.

Family account from Lisa Graff

In a Facebook Messenger conversation dated 5 October 2026, Lisa Graff (Rick's cousin-in-law, married to Edna's oldest son Ray) said: “I was also so close to Edna and it upset me that she and Ruth didn't have a closer relationship. It had to do with the fact that when Nana died what Ruth went in and took out of the house.” Lisa attributed the sisters' rift to Ruth taking things from their mother's home after Nana died.

Lisa also said: “And it wasn't until Sona died and I was trying to put Ed in a nursing home that he shared stories about how abusive his father was and I had also heard stories that Nanna was too. But I'm sure you don't want that in your book.” She added: “Most of the abuse were centered on Jack, the oldest”. Lisa's report is preserved here in her words and as her account.
