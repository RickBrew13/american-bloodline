Life Story

Monica Lynn Wise was born on 17 July 1956 in Ohio, the daughter of Samuel P. Wise and Virginia Greiner, according to her original 1976 Hamilton County marriage license. She married Stanley William Cossman on 30 June 1976. A 2006 Cincinnati obituary for Virginia G. Wise (née Griener) names her daughter Monica Kelly and son Sam “Skip” Wise. Because Virginia’s obituary names her daughter Monica Kelly and the 1976 license names Virginia Greiner and Samuel P. Wise as Monica Lynn Wise’s parents, Monica Kelly is a strong identity match. The source establishes the later surname, but not when or how she acquired it. The obituary also confirms that Virginia was the wife of the late Samuel P. Wise. A compiled Ancestry entry identifies Monica Lynn Cossman as Sam Cossman’s mother, but that link remains unproved by a birth record.

  • Her 30 June 1976 Hamilton County marriage license names Samuel P. Wise as her father and Virginia Greiner as her mother, and gives her birth as 17 July 1956 in Ohio. The original license image was read in the prior profile audit.
  • Virginia G. Wise’s 4 October 2006 Gilligan Funeral Homes obituary calls Virginia the wife of the late Samuel P. Wise and names daughter Monica Kelly. The matching parents named on the 1976 license make Monica Kelly a strong identity match to Monica Lynn Wise. The obituary documents the later surname Kelly, but no record identifies when or how she acquired it or names a later spouse.
  • The same obituary names Monica’s brother as Sam “Skip” Wise. No independent birth/death record for him was located in this pass.
  • A derivative death-index entry for Samuel P. Wise reports 24 December 1913–7 July 1989 and last residence Cincinnati, Ohio. This is a probable identity match for Monica’s father, but the original death certificate or obituary naming Virginia/Monica has not been found. Two patents list inventors named Samuel P. Wise in the Cincinnati region; neither is yet directly tied to this family.
