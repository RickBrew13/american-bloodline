Life Story

A prior transcription of Monica's 1976 Hamilton County marriage abstract reports her birth as 17 July 1956 in Ohio and names Samuel P. Wise and Virginia Greiner as her parents. The official online index independently confirms her marriage to Stanley William Cossman on 30 June 1976 (vol. 9, p. 2454), but the underlying abstract image is not in the GitHub archive and was not rechecked in this pass. She married Stanley William Cossman on 30 June 1976. A 2006 Cincinnati obituary for Virginia G. Wise (née Griener) names her daughter Monica Kelly and son Sam “Skip” Wise. Because Virginia’s obituary names her daughter Monica Kelly and the 1976 license names Virginia Greiner and Samuel P. Wise as Monica Lynn Wise’s parents, Monica Kelly is a strong identity match. The source establishes the later surname, but not when or how she acquired it. The obituary also confirms that Virginia was the wife of the late Samuel P. Wise. A compiled Ancestry entry identifies Monica Lynn Cossman as Sam Cossman’s mother, but that link remains unproved by a birth record.
