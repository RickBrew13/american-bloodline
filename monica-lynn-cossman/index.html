Life Story

Monica Lynn Wise was born on 17 July 1956 in Ohio, the daughter of Samuel P. Wise and Virginia Greiner, according to her original 1976 Hamilton County marriage license. She married Stanley William Cossman on 30 June 1976. A 2006 Cincinnati obituary for Virginia G. Wise (née Griener) names her daughter Monica Kelly and son Sam “Skip” Wise. The matching mother’s name/maiden-name variant, Cincinnati connection and family context make Monica Kelly a likely later identity for Monica Lynn Wise, but no marriage or other record yet bridges the Kelly surname to this Monica. The obituary also confirms that Virginia was the wife of the late Samuel P. Wise. A compiled Ancestry entry identifies Monica Lynn Cossman as Sam Cossman’s mother, but that link remains unproved by a birth record.
