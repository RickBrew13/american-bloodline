Life Story

Monica Lynn Wise was born on 17 July 1956 in Ohio, the daughter of Samuel P. Wise and Virginia Greiner. She married Stanley William Cossman on 30 June 1976 in Hamilton County, Ohio. A compiled Ancestry entry identifies Monica Lynn Cossman as Sam Cossman’s mother, but that link has not been independently verified — no birth record for Sam has been found.
