# Monica Lynn Wise / Cossman / Kelly — court-case identity lead
**Research date:** 25 November 2026

## New public court record located
A published Ohio appellate opinion, *Merchants Bank & Trust Co. v. Kelly*, 2004-Ohio-3913, Butler County Court of Appeals, docket CA200309229, identifies the defendant as **Monica Kelly (Courtney)**. The opinion states that she purchased a 1989 Mercury Sable from Queen City Motors in Cincinnati on 25 September 1993, financed it through Merchants Bank, and had a 17-year-old son in August 1996. The opinion was issued 26 July 2004.

Source: https://law.justia.com/cases/ohio/twelfth-district-court-of-appeals/2004/2004-ohio-3913.html

## Why this may matter to Monica Lynn Wise Cossman
Monica Lynn Wise married Stanley William Cossman in Hamilton County on 30 June 1976. Her mother's 4 October 2006 obituary identifies a daughter named **Monica Kelly**. The court opinion's Monica Kelly was involved in a Cincinnati vehicle purchase and Butler County proceedings, which are geographically compatible with the known Cincinnati/Hamilton County family context. The court record also documents a teenage son in 1996, a potentially useful clue when investigating Monica's possible children.

## Identity remains unproved — do not merge
The appellate opinion does **not** provide Monica Kelly's date of birth, middle name, parents, former surname Wise/Cossman, address, spouse, or child's name. Its caption says “Monica Kelly (Courtney),” apparently reflecting a later name, but does not explain the name change. No record yet bridges this defendant to Monica Lynn Wise/Cossman, and no evidence connects the 17-year-old son to Sam Cossman or any other known family member.

Accordingly:
- Do not add the court case, Courtney surname, vehicle purchase, or son as established facts in Monica's public profile.
- Keep this as a high-priority identity lead for a future check against the underlying Butler County case file and any name-change/marriage records.
- If identity is confirmed, the case may establish another surname and support a child lead; until then, it is a namesake possibility only.

## Stanley–Monica divorce window and official record route

Kathryn “Kate” Cossman's 2016 obituary says she and Stanley William Cossman married in **1984** (reported date 12 October 1984 in the family archive). Since Stanley's 1976 marriage license documents his earlier marriage to Monica Lynn Wise, the Stanley–Monica marriage must have ended before the 1984 marriage. The exact divorce/dissolution date and case number have not been found. The 1979 deed sequence (Stanley to Monica, then Monica to Terrance M. Nagel et al) falls within the likely separation/divorce window, but no deed image or court decree proves that it was a divorce settlement or connected to the same parcel.

Hamilton County Clerk of Courts' Domestic Relations Division states that divorce decrees and dissolutions from **1885 to the present** are maintained in the Document Center, 1000 Main Street, Room 315, Cincinnati. The Hamilton County Genealogical Society says mid-20th-century to current divorce cases are indexed through the Clerk's website Name Search, with older case numbers often beginning “A” and later cases “DR.” I opened the official records-search page, but the Name Search endpoint timed out in this session; no online name-index search was completed. The published sources point to the exact repository and time range, but no contact or copy request was made.
