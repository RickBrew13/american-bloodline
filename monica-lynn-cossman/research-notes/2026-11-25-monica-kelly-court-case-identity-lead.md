# Monica Lynn Wise / Cossman / Kelly — court-case identity lead

## New public court record located
A published Ohio appellate opinion, *Merchants Bank & Trust Co. v. Kelly*, 2004-Ohio-3913, Butler County Court of Appeals, docket CA200309229, identifies the defendant as **Monica Kelly (Courtney)**. The opinion states that she purchased a 1989 Mercury Sable from Queen City Motors in Cincinnati on 25 September 1993, financed it through Merchants Bank, and had a 17-year-old son in August 1996. The opinion was issued 26 July 2004.

Source: https://law.justia.com/cases/ohio/twelfth-district-court-of-appeals/2004/2004-ohio-3913.html

Its caption says “Monica Kelly (Courtney),” apparently reflecting a later name, but does not explain the name change. No record yet bridges this defendant to Monica Lynn Wise/Cossman, and no evidence connects the 17-year-old son to Sam Cossman or any other known family member.

Accordingly:
