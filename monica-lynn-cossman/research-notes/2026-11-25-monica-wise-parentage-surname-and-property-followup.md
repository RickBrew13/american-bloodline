## Parentage now independently confirmed
The 1976 Hamilton County marriage license (Vol. 9, p. 2454, license 009-2454), already read directly in the earlier audit, names Monica Lynn Wise's father as **Samuel P. Wise** and her mother's maiden name as **Virginia Greiner**.

A new obituary for **Virginia G. Wise (née Griener)**, who died 4 October 2006 at age 86, states:
- Virginia was the beloved wife of the late **Samuel P. Wise**.
- Her children were **Sam “Skip” Wise** and **Monica Kelly**.
- Her sister was the late Gladys Adams.
- Graveside service was held 10 October 2006 at **Wise Cemetery, Irvine, Kentucky**.

Source: https://www.tributearchive.com/obituaries/4113531/virginia-g.-wise

This obituary resolves two open questions:
1. Samuel P. Wise and Virginia Greiner were spouses, not merely two people named on Monica's license.
2. Monica Lynn Wise is the daughter later identified as **Monica Kelly** in her mother's obituary. The obituary does not name Monica's later husband or give the date of any later marriage. “Kelly” is therefore recorded as a later surname, not a proven marriage event.

The obituary also names Monica's brother as **Sam “Skip” Wise**. No further birth/death details for him were located in this pass.

## Possible identity and life dates for Samuel P. Wise
A derivative SSDI-style index at LocateAncestors lists **Samuel P. Wise**, born 24 December 1913, died 7 July 1989, last residence Cincinnati, Hamilton County, Ohio 45243:
https://www.locateancestors.com/samuel-wise/

This is a strong candidate for Monica's father because:
- Monica's 1976 marriage license names her father Samuel P. Wise.
- Virginia's 2006 obituary calls her late husband Samuel P. Wise.
- The indexed Samuel P. Wise died before Virginia and has a Cincinnati last-residence ZIP, consistent with the Cincinnati family context.

However, the LocateAncestors listing is a derivative index, not the original Social Security application or death certificate. No original death certificate, obituary or cemetery record naming Virginia or Monica was located for this Samuel. The profile should treat 24 December 1913–7 July 1989 as a **probable but not yet fully proven** identity match, not an established birth/death pair.
