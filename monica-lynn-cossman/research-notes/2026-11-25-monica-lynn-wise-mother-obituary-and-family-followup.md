## Important new family source: Virginia G. Wise obituary
Located a 2006 Cincinnati obituary for **Virginia G. Wise**:
https://www.tributearchive.com/obituaries/4113531/virginia-g.-wise

The obituary text states:
- Virginia G. Wise, née **Griener** (variant of **Greiner** as written on Monica's 1976 marriage license).
- Born 17 February 1920; died 4 October 2006, age 86.
- Beloved wife of the late **Samuel P. Wise**.
- Devoted mother of **Sam “Skip” Wise** and **Monica Kelly**.
- Sister of the late **Gladys Adams**; aunt of Helen Strickland.
- Graveside service at **Wise Cemetery, Irvine, Kentucky**, on 10 October 2006.

This is a strong match to Monica's mother because her 1976 original Hamilton County marriage license names Virginia Greiner as Monica Lynn Wise's mother and Samuel P. Wise as her father. The obituary independently confirms a Cincinnati-based Virginia G. Wise née Griener, married to Samuel P. Wise, with a daughter named Monica. The obituary calls that daughter Monica Kelly, not Monica Wise or Cossman. It is highly plausible that this is the same Monica after a later surname change, but no marriage record or other direct document has yet bridged the Kelly surname to Monica Lynn Wise. The profile now labels the identification as likely, not certain.

### Possible sibling
If Monica Kelly is Monica Lynn Wise, then **Sam “Skip” Wise** is her brother. The obituary explicitly names both as Virginia's children. No independent birth, marriage or obituary record for Sam “Skip” was located in this pass; the profile lists him as a possible sibling only. **Gladys Adams is Virginia's sister**, not Monica's sibling; do not confuse the generations.

## New record routes
- Cincinnati & Hamilton County Public Library Newsdex indexes most 20th-century Cincinnati obituaries and death notices; a library card is not required. The local genealogy society explains how to obtain an obituary copy using an exact death/publication date: https://www.hcgsohio.org/cpage.php?pt=62 .

- The Ohio Obituary Index is another large state index for newspaper obituaries and notices: https://ohiohistory.libguides.com/newspapers/obituaries .
