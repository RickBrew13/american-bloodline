Life Story

Margaret Lorch was born on 29 June 1893 in Hungary, the daughter of Johann Lorch and Theresa Lorch née Novak. Her birth village is unknown. She crossed to America as a girl with her mother, but no passenger manifest for either of them has been found, and the census immigration years conflict — 1910 in one census, 1913 in another. The long-carried claim that mother and daughter arrived on the S.S. Main at Baltimore in August 1907 was checked against the full manifest and refuted: they are not on it.

Margaret and Peter (Petrus) Palmer married on 31 May 1913 at St. Henry's Catholic Church in Cincinnati; their Hamilton County marriage banns return survives. Their son William Peter Palmer was born on 1 June 1914 in Cincinnati, and their daughter Anna Violet Palmer on 19 July 1917. In 1920 the household at 1051 St. Gregory included Margaret's widowed mother Theresa Lorch.

Margaret outlived Peter by nearly nineteen years. She died on 25 October 1982 in Cincinnati, with a funeral at St. William Church; her burial place has not been proved. Her obituary names thirteen great-grandchildren and six great-great-grandchildren.
