# Etta Marcum obituary and household group

The 8 July 1970 Cincinnati newspaper clipping (FamilySearch Memories) identifies “Etta” as Harold Marcum’s wife and mother of Deborah, Beverly, Alton, Douglas, William, Ronald and Michael. It gives death Monday 6 July, a Blue Ash residence, and Strawser Funeral Home arrangements. Transcription and scan are archived in `documents/`.

Beverly Jane Marcum’s 21 Aug. 1982 Hamilton County marriage license names Harold C. Marcum as father and Etta F. McFadden as mother. Find a Grave identifies the same-generation couple as Harold Clayton Marcum and Etta Fay McFadden Marcum and shows matching military/cemetery markers. The full-name bridge is strong; the original license’s image is cited in Beverly’s live profile and the Gen VI couple’s prior research, but not duplicated here.

FamilySearch’s compiled profile for Michael Lee Marcum (1962–1989) names Harold Clayton and Etta Fay as parents. It lists a sibling “Deb Marcum”; this could correspond to obituary “Deborah,” but no identity bridge was found, so keep those forms separate.

Find a Grave’s Etta memorial displays “married 1940” to Harold, which is impossible for both given their 1927 birth years. That date is rejected as a memorial error; the 1970 obituary proves Etta called Harold her husband but supplies no marriage date.
