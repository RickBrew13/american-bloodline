# Research note — 25 November 2026

Etta’s 1970 obituary calls her wife of Harold Marcum and names seven children including Beverly. The 1982 Hamilton County marriage license cited on Beverly’s profile directly names her father Harold C. Marcum and mother Etta F. McFadden. Find a Grave gives the full form Harold Clayton Marcum, a 16 Dec 1927–24 Jul 1999 span and Hopewell Cemetery burial; the memorial photo shows his Navy WWII/Korea marker. FamilySearch’s compiled Michael profile (1962–1989) names Harold Clayton and Etta Fay as Michael’s parents. The parent assignment to Hiram Marcum and Bertha Owens Marcum is strongly supported by multiple FindaGrave family links; one memorial narrative contradicts its own linked father field by saying Harold Marcum rather than Hiram. The impossible 1940 marriage year on Etta’s memorial is rejected. No marriage date found.
