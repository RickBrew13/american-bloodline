# “Etta” Marcum — obituary clipping transcription

**Source page:** FamilySearch Memories, “Obituary for Etta Marcum,” Cincinnati, Hamilton County, Ohio, dated 8 July 1970: https://www.familysearch.org/en/memories/memory/181780481

The visible newspaper clipping is headed “MARCUM” and reads:

> Etta, beloved wife of Harold Marcum, devoted mother of Deborah, Beverly, Alton, Douglas, William, Ronald and Michael Marcum; Monday, July 6, 1970; residence, 10486 Plainview Rd., Blue Ash. Friends may call Wednesday after 4 P.M. at the Strawser Funeral Home, 9503 Kenwood Rd., Blue Ash. Services Thursday, 10:30 A.M.

The clipping identifies Etta as Harold’s wife and names seven children. The Hamilton County marriage license for daughter Beverly later spells the parents Harold C. Marcum and Etta F. McFadden; Find a Grave gives Etta’s expanded name as Etta Fay McFadden Marcum and her marker reads Etta F. Marcum. The evidence strongly connects these records, although the newspaper clipping itself gives only “Etta” and “Harold Marcum.” The obituary scan is archived as `documents/Etta-Fay-Marcum-1970-obituary-scan.jpg`, reassembled from 216 FamilySearch deep-zoom tiles so the full clipping and address remain legible; the public Memories record is linked above.
