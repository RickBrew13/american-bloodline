Family Relationship Notes

Ninfa Noto is named as the mother of Salvadore Ciancimino on his son’s baptism index of 30 December 1842, Sciacca: father Salvadore Ciancimino, aged 25, mother Ninfa Noto, aged 22. She is named again as the mother on his son’s Milan death act of 1892: “figlio di Salvatore e di Noto Ninfa.”

Her ten children named on the family tree are Vincenzo, Maria Antonia, Salvadore, Antonina, Antonia, Giuseppe, Giuseppa, Alfonzo, Accursio, and Giovanni. Only Salvadore is confirmed by a record; the other nine are named on the family tree and no records were found for them in this pass.

Her son Salvadore was born 30 December 1842 in Sciacca, worked as a day laborer, married Antonina Marotta, and died 21 January 1892 in Milan. Her grandson Salvatore Ciancimino, born about 1881, married Rosa Graffeo on 15 September 1907 in Manhattan.

Life Story

Ninfa Noto lived in Sciacca, Agrigento, Sicily. She is named as the mother of Salvadore Ciancimino on his son’s baptism index of 30 December 1842, aged 22, and again on his son’s Milan death act of 1892. Her husband was Salvadore Ciancimino, and the family tree names ten children. Her son Salvadore was born 30 December 1842 in Sciacca, worked as a day laborer, married Antonina Marotta, and died in Milan on 21 January 1892. Her grandson Salvatore, born about 1881, married Rosa Graffeo in Manhattan in 1907. No record of Ninfa’s own birth, marriage, or death has been found.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Her birth year comes from the family tree (born 1820); her son’s baptism index gives her age as 22 on 30 December 1842, which points to about 1820.
  • Her nine children other than Salvadore are named only on the family tree; no birth, marriage, or death records were found for any of them in this pass. They are listed as named, not as confirmed.
  • Her marriage to Salvadore Ciancimino has not been found. The record that would settle it is the Sciacca marriage film 1864–1894, or the marriage index.
  • No death record for her has been found. Next useful records: the Sciacca civil records for the 1810s–1820s for her birth; the Sciacca death records for her death.
