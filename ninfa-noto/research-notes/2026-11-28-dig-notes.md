# Ninfa Noto — dig notes
Date: 28 November 2026. FamilySearch signed-in pass (RickBrewster1), images read and re-read letter by letter.

## What was found
- Ninfa Noto is named as the mother of Salvadore Ciancimino on his son's baptism index of 30 December 1842, Sciacca (ARK 1:1:XVY2-SYN): father Salvadore Ciancimino, aged 25, mother Ninfa Noto, aged 22. The image is only available at a FamilySearch center or affiliate library.
- She is named again as the mother on his son's Milan death act of 1892 (image ARK 3:1:3Q9M-CSQJ-F9BW-3; index ARK 1:1:X32V-5BK1): "figlio di Salvatore e di Noto Ninfa." Read from the image and re-read letter by letter in a second pass.
- Her ten children named on the family tree are Vincenzo, Maria Antonia, Salvadore, Antonina, Antonia, Giuseppe, Giuseppa, Alfonzo, Accursio, and Giovanni. Only Salvadore is confirmed by a record.

## Not found / not assigned
- Her birth year comes from the family tree (born 1820); her son's baptism index gives her age as 22 on 30 December 1842, which points to about 1820.
- Her nine children other than Salvadore are named only on the family tree; no records were found for any of them in this pass.
- Her marriage to Salvadore Ciancimino has not been found. The record that would settle it is the Sciacca marriage film 1864-1894.
- No death record for her has been found.
