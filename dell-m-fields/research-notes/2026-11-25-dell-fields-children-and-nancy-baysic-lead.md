## New lead: Nancy Jean (Bowman) Baysic obituary
Nancy's obituary, published 10 January 2024 by Murfreesboro Funeral Home and indexed on Legacy, says she died 9 January 2024, aged 78, and names:
- Her daughters Malia (Larry) Armand, Zina (Daniel) Wagener and Cindy Baysic.
- Predeceased grandson **Dell Michael Fields**.
- Grandchildren **Richard and Tara Marcum, Toni and Robert Fields**, among others.
- Predeceased grandsons Michael Joseph Armand and Dell Michael Fields, and great-grandson Beckham Garrison Hughes.

Source: https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/name/nancy-baysic-obituary?id=54070298 .

The names **Richard, Tara Marcum, Toni, Robert Fields and Dell Michael Fields** closely match the five children named in Dell Michael Fields I's 2012 obituary (Richard, Tara, Toni, Dell, Robert). This may be the same family group. The exact connection is not yet established:
- Nancy's obituary does not name the parent of each grandchild.
- It does not state whether Dell Michael Fields was the son of Dell Michael Fields I or another same-name relative.
- It does not identify which of Nancy's daughters is the parent of the Marcum/Fields grandchildren.
- The family account in Beverly's profile identifies Beverly Jane Marcum as Tara's mother, while Beverly's 1982 marriage license names her parents as Harold C. Marcum and Etta F. McFadden. Nancy is not identified in those records as Beverly's mother.
- It is possible the obituary names a different Tara Marcum or another Fields family with the same names.

Therefore, **do not add Nancy as a grandmother, do not reassign Tara's mother, and do not merge the grandchildren solely on first/last names**. This is a high-priority lead for the next research pass because it may reveal another relationship or correct an incomplete family picture.

This could be the “Dell” named as one of Dell Michael Fields I's five children and the “Dell Michael Fields” listed as Nancy Baysic's predeceased grandson. But none of these sources gives his parents. No birth certificate, obituary or direct family source ties him to Dell M. Fields I. The candidate is **not** added as a child or descendant in the profile. Only the unresolved lead and official source links are recorded here.
