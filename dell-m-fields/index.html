Life Story

Dell Michael Fields was born 9 March 1954 in Kentucky. On 21 August 1982 he married Beverly Jane Marcum in Hamilton County, Ohio; the license gives his occupation as janitor and his residence as 4931 Laurel Avenue, Blue Ash, and names his parents as Dell Fields and “Corr. Carruba” as written. He is the father of Tara Michelle Marcum, named as such on her 2011 Hamilton County marriage license, when she married Henrico Lamon Moore on 23 September 2011. He died 8 April 2012 and is buried at Reading, Hamilton County, Ohio.
