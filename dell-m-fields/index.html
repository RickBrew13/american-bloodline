Life Story

The 1982 license records the groom’s parents as Dell Fields and “Corr. Carruba.” His 2012 obituary identifies his mother as Cora Fields; Clyde Mullins’s 2022 obituary gives “Cora Fields Mullins (nee Carubba)” and identifies the late Michael as a stepchild. These independent notices strongly support that the “Corr.” on the license is Cora (Carubba) Fields Mullins. The spelling is retained as written in the original license.

Additional Quick-Fact Details

  • Married: 21 August 1982, Hamilton County, Ohio, to Beverly Jane Marcum
  • Parent-name discrepancy: the 1982 marriage license records “Dell Fields” and “Corr. Carruba”; the 2012 obituary says his mother was “Cora Fields,” his father (also Dell Fields) was deceased, and Clyde Mullins was stepfather. A plausible link is that Cora’s earlier surname was Carruba, but no record proves this; keep the uncertainty and do not silently substitute.
  • The obituary adds five children by first name, but gives no mothers or birth details for Richard, Toni, Dell and Robert; only Tara has separate marriage-license paternity evidence. Do not add other parent-child links without records. A 2024 obituary for Nancy Jean Baysic names grandchildren Richard and Tara Marcum, Toni and Robert Fields, and a predeceased grandson Dell Michael Fields; this closely matches the five children listed in Dell's 2012 obituary, but no source yet establishes Nancy's relationship to them. Do not add her as grandmother or link the 2021 Dell Fields Jr. record without a direct identity bridge.
  • Death date/place now has a direct obituary: 8 April 2012, at home in Lockland. The index-only burial place Reading remains to be verified by cemetery/death certificate. Funeral service took place at Schmidt-Dhonau-Kucner Funeral Home in Reading, 11–12 April 2012.
  • The obituary photo is family-submitted by Tara Marcum and is archived as a family-associated photo; the companion’s identity is unverified.
