Life Story

Sonia Marie McKinney (née Cain) was a native of Dawes, West Virginia. Her funeral-home obituary gives her dates as 3 September 1926–21 August 2017 and says she died at her residence in Johnson City, Tennessee. It identifies her parents only as the late “Mr. & Mrs. C.R. Cain” of St. Albans and names her surviving husband as “Ed McKinney.” The family identifies that husband as Edgar A. McKinney; the full identity and parent names remain carefully graded in the evidence below.
