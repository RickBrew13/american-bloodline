Life Story

Callie Martin Crochen was the mother of Artherine E. Crochen. The 1920 census lists her in Hayneville, Lowndes County, Alabama, age 30, as the wife of Albert Crochen, with four children: Fannie, 12; Albert Jr., 7; “Artha Rene,” 9; and Anna May, 4. The enumerator wrote the family name “Crackeson.” Artherine’s 1973 Alabama death record names her mother as “Callie Martin” and her father as “Albert Crocker,” with her spouse Leon Niles. Her maiden name, Martin, is as given in that record. Albert’s tree profile spells her name “Collie” Crochen. She died in January 1985 in Birmingham; her exact death day is not proved by a death certificate. In 1900 she was enumerated as Callie Martin, 9, a niece in Lewis Calwell’s household at Prairie Hill, Lowndes County; by 1930 the family had moved to Birmingham, where the 1930, 1940 and 1950 censuses all place them.
