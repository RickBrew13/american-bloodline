Life Story

Callie Martin Crochen was the mother of Artherine E. Crochen. The 1920 census lists her in Hayneville, Lowndes County, Alabama, age 30, as the wife of Albert Crochen, with four children: Fannie, 12; Albert Jr., 7; “Artha Rene,” 9; and Anna May, 4. The enumerator wrote the family name “Crackeson.” Artherine’s 1973 Alabama death record names her mother as “Callie Martin” and her father as “Albert Crocker,” with her spouse Leon Niles. Her maiden name, Martin, is as given in that record. Albert’s tree profile spells her name “Collie” Crochen. No death date is recorded for Callie.
