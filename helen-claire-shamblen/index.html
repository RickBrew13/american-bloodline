Life Story

Helen Claire Shamblen was born 6 August 1935 in Sanford, Florida. She married Lowell Harold Shamblen on 18 December 1953 in Lake Mary, Florida. Their children were David Lowell Shamblen, born 3 October 1956 in Charleston, West Virginia; Donald Gene Shamblen, born 5 March 1959 in Charleston, West Virginia; and Lisa Karen Mountcastle (Shamblen), born 9 June 1962 in Sanford, Florida. She died 15 October 2005 in Lake Mary, Florida.
