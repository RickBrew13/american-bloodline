Life Story

Edgar A. McKinney was a son of Dallas Cecil McKinney and Laverne Hurt, and a brother of Ruth Laverne McKinney. He married Sonia McKinney, whose maiden name was Cain. His middle name is carried as "A" only — it is not expanded here. An unsourced Ancestry tree harvest lists possible children Robert L., Paul E., Carol Ann, and Mark E. McKinney; these remain tree-only leads and are not entered as confirmed children. No independent obituary or vital record was found to corroborate them in this pass.

from Lisa Graff
