# Kati (Kate) Tahmali — deeper parent/family review

**Reviewed:** 24 November 2026

## Parent-child evidence and Bella/Bertha identity

The 1 July 1912 Hamilton County, Ohio, marriage application names Kati (Kate) Tahmali as mother of Bella Mahle, age 25, born in Russia, and names Aaron Mahle as Bella's father. It is the only located direct source naming Kati as Bella's mother; its image redirected to sign-in during this review.

A derivative SortedByName compilation transcribes Social Security applications filed by Bella's children. Eva's records identify her mother as Bella and also as Bertha Tarmin/Tumarn; Minnie’s card gives mother Belle; David’s gives mother Berthia. The listed forms include Eva SS-5 65332608996 (Oct. 1962), 65332608997 (May 1938) and later numbers; Minnie SS-5 65337767611 (May 1938); David SS-5 66150905894 (Feb. 1937). This supports the working identification Bella Mahle = later Bertha Tarmin Luft, but does not establish Kati's own ancestry. The original applications were not inspected.

## William/Bill Tomarin: possible Bella sibling, not proved

FamilySearch's 1950 census index returns William Tamarin, born in Russia in 1880, in Cincinnati on 1 April 1950, without relationships. The JCGC Jewish cemetery register lists Bill Tomarin (15 April 1880–6 July 1957), buried 9 July at Love Brothers Cemetery, Section 003, Row 02, Grave 81, and identifies Violet Tomarin as his wife. A derivative transcription of William's SS-5 65327807598 (April 1938) gives the same birth date, Russia, and parents Adron Tomarin and Kessia T. Zeltzman. Matching dates, place and surname variant make William/Bill very likely one person.

No located record connects him to Kati or Bella. In particular, the SS-5 parent names Adron Tomarin and Kessia T. Zeltzman are not the names Kati Tahmali and Aaron Mahle on the 1912 application. This could reflect variants or a different family; evidence does not decide. The JCGC database also lists Marcus/Sima Tomarin and Harry/Toby Tomarin, but provides no kinship link to Bill or Bella.

## Research outcome and scope control

- Kati's own parents, siblings, birth/death, exact origin, and burial remain unidentified.
- William/Bill remains a possible but unproved sibling of Bella; he is not listed in Kati's Children card. No other Tomarin relationships were added.
- No parent/sibling cards, index entries, profile links, existing folders, or generation assignments were changed. No new profiles or folders were created.
