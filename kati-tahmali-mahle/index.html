Life Story

Kati (Kate) Tahmali is named as the mother of Bella Mahle on Bella's 1912 marriage application to Alex Luft in Hamilton County, Ohio, with Aaron Mahle as Bella's father DOCUMENTED. Bella's children later gave their mother's maiden name as Tomarin or Tarmin, which looks like the same name as Tahmali UNPROVED.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The bride's surname is written "Mahle" on the application, while her children's records give Tomarin, Tarmin, or Tamine. "Mahle" reads as her mother's name "Tahmali" without its first sound; both bride and groom signed by mark, so the clerk wrote the names by ear.
