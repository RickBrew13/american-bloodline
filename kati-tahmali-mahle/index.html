Family Relationship Notes

  • Kati (also written Kate) Tahmali is named as Bella Mahle’s mother on Bella’s 1912 Hamilton County, Ohio, marriage application. The same application names Aaron Mahle as Bella’s father.
  • Bella’s birthplace is recorded as Russia. Kati’s residence in Russia is inferred from her daughter’s birthplace, not documented directly.
  • The 1912 application gives Kati’s name as Tahmali. Later records of Bella’s children use forms including Tomarin, Tarmin, or Tamine. Whether these are variant spellings of the same family name remains unresolved.
  • No record has yet established Kati’s birth, death, parents, occupation, or burial.

Life Story

Kati (Kate) Tahmali is named as the mother of Bella Mahle on Bella's 1912 marriage application to Alex Luft in Hamilton County, Ohio, with Aaron Mahle as Bella's father . Bella's children later gave their mother's maiden name as Tomarin or Tarmin, which looks like the same name as Tahmali .
