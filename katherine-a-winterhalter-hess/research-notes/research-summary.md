# Katherine A. Hess — name/parentage conflict

The woman linked as spouse of Charles H. Hess and mother of Howard Bernard Hess lived 18 Oct 1877–14 Nov 1967, with memorial records giving Cincinnati birth, Phoenix death, and burial at Saint Joseph New Cemetery. Her surname identity is unsettled. Find a Grave memorial title/family links call her Katherine A. Winterhalter Hess; a 1967 funeral notice image says Katherine A. (née Wiedmer), and the 1923 Charles H. Hess notices say Katherine Hess (née Winterholter). Those may be variants or a distinct-person conflation. Do not treat Winterhalter/Wiedmer as reconciled.

The same memorial includes inconsistent parent text: one passage calls her daughter of John B. Winterhalter and Catherine Schlitz, another says parents John Wiedmer and Mary Stanger. No independent original birth/marriage record reviewed; parent couple left unresolved in profile.

A 1967 notice names her daughters Mrs Clarence A. Dorger and Mrs Edward T. Ryan, and sons Howard B. and Charles H.; it says the former Dorger daughter and Howard were deceased by 1967. Together with Charles’s 1923 notice, the likely children are Marguerite A. Hess/Dorger, Howard Bernard, Charles H. Jr., and Catherine Hess/Ryan; the relationships are derivative obituary/memorial evidence.

Find a Grave links siblings Marguerite (1898–1951), Howard (1899–1953), Charles Jr. (1902–1938), and Catherine Ryan (1908–1967). A source memorial photo shows a family inscription wall with additional Hess names, but identities and relationships must not be inferred from proximity alone. The main “c. 1921” image remains a memorial image, not a verified personal portrait.
