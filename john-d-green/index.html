Life Story

John D. Green was born on 16 September 1822, most likely in Georgia . He married Mahaley Goin in Claiborne County, Tennessee, on 4 April 1845, and they raised four children there and in Union County: Hewlet, Nancy, Winfield Scott and Clementine .

By 1880 John had moved across the line to Poplar Creek in Knox County, Kentucky, where he worked as a cabinet maker, with his son Hewlet next door . Mahaley died in 1889, and John in March 1896. Both are buried in Dowis-Lee Cemetery .
