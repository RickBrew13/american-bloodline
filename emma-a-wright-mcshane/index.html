Life Story

Emma A. Wright was born in Philadelphia on 16 February 1873 SUPPORTED. She married Hugh McShane on 15 November 1892, and in 1898 they settled in Hopkinsville, Kentucky SUPPORTED. She raised eleven children, buried her eldest son James in 1954, and died at home on 3 February 1960, survived by six daughters and four sons SUPPORTED.
