Life Story

Hugh Ignatius McShane was born in 1869, a son of James McShane and Margaret Greaves . He married Emma A. Wright on 15 November 1892, and their eldest son, James Anthony, was born eleven months later and named for Hugh's father . In 1898 the family moved to Hopkinsville, Kentucky, where they raised eleven children . Hugh outlived his eldest son and his wife and died in 1963 .
