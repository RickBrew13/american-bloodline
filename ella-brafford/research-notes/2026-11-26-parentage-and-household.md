# Research note — Ella Brafford

Reviewed 26 November 2026.

FamilySearch shared-tree profile GX61-6FN reports Ella Brafford (4 Mar 1856–1955), wife of William Runyon and mother of Frances and Robert. Its sources list an 1880 census entry titled “Ella Runyon in household of Wm. Runyon” and a 1870 census entry titled “Frances E Brafford in household of Samuel B Brafford.” The tree connects these names as one woman. This is plausible but the original schedules and the Frances-to-Ella identity bridge still need direct review. The tree lists her parents as Samuel B Brofford and Elisabeth Brofford; preserve the Brofford/Brafford spelling discrepancy and tree-only status.
