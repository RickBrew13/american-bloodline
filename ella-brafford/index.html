Life Story

The public FamilySearch shared-tree profile for Ella Brafford (GX61-6FN) reports a 4 March 1856 Kentucky birth, a 1955 death, and a marriage to William Runyon. Its listed sources include an 1880 census entry titled “Ella Runyon in household of Wm. Runyon” and an 1870 entry titled “Frances E Brafford in household of Samuel B Brafford.” The tree interprets those two record entries as the same woman. That is a plausible identity bridge, but the original census schedules and the identity substitution Frances-to-Ella were not independently reviewed; preserve the variants and uncertainty.
