Life Story

Sylvia Etta Pottorf was born in Oregonia, Warren County, Ohio, on 16 May 1886, and died in Cincinnati on 24 August 1969, according to her FamilySearch public-tree page. She married Charles Clarence Stephenson in Warren County on 12 October 1904. Charles’s reproduced contemporary funeral notice independently names his widow, Silvie E. (Pottorf) Stephenson, and gives the same marriage date.

The FamilySearch tree lists five child entries, including Florence Eskalene and an unnamed Stephenson infant in 1915. Charles’s funeral notice names four surviving children but does not mention the infant. Rose Hill’s cemetery transcription records “Silvie E. Stephenson” in the couple’s Lot 79. These compiled-tree and cemetery entries are useful leads; original civil records remain to be reviewed.
