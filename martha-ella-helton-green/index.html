Life Story

Martha Ella Helton was born in Knox County, Kentucky, on 18 March 1881, a daughter of Asberry "A. B." Helton and Eliza Tankersley DOCUMENTED. At 18 she married James O. Green on 19 August 1899 DOCUMENTED. They farmed in Knox County and raised ten or more children, her youngest son Fairest born near Barbourville about 1927 or 1928 SUPPORTED. After James died in 1954 she lived in Independence, Kentucky DOCUMENTED. She died on 23 May 1960 and is buried beside him at Forest Lawn Memorial Park in Erlanger SUPPORTED.
