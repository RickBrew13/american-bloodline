Family Relationship Notes

The 1899 Knox County marriage certificate for James O. Green and Ella Helton names Ella’s parents as A. B. Helton and Eliza Helton. The Helton marriage index and cemetery transcriptions identify them as Asberry Helton and Eliza Tankersley.

The family’s children are listed above from census, vital-index, and family memorial sources. James and Martha’s youngest son, Fairest Richard Green, was born 27 November 1928 in Barbourville according to his Social Security application index.

Two additional possible siblings appear in the research material: Mary Ann Helton (1869–1934), Ellen Helton (born 1872), and Michel Vesta Helton (1878–1958). Their relationship to Martha has not been established well enough to include them in the Siblings card. Kizzie and Annie are the two sibling candidates retained there from the available family records.

James’s death date remains conflicted: the Kentucky Death Index reports 8 February 1954, while the likely family marker reads 6 February 1956. The spouse’s date range is left open until the original certificate and cemetery record are checked.

Life Story

Martha Ella Helton was born in Knox County, Kentucky, on 18 March 1881, a daughter of Asberry "A. B." Helton and Eliza Tankersley . At 18 she married James O. Green on 19 August 1899 . They farmed in Knox County and raised ten or more children, her youngest son Fairest born near Barbourville about 1927 or 1928 . In August 1956 she was reported living in Independence, Kentucky, in her son Fairest’s engagement announcement . The Kentucky Death Index reports James’s death in 1954, but his likely family marker is inscribed 6 February 1956; the date conflict is unresolved. She died on 23 May 1960 and is buried beside him at Forest Lawn Memorial Park in Erlanger .
