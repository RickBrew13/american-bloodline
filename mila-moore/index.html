Family Relationship Notes

The 1940 U.S. census index lists Mila Moore, age 23, born in Indiana, as the wife of Leroy Moore in Detroit Ward 6, Wayne County, Michigan. It lists Patrick, age four, and Michael, age two, as their sons. The source implies a birth around 1916–1917; it does not provide a maiden name, exact birth date, or parents. The index reports her race as White, an enumerator’s category rather than proof for or against ancestry or tribal affiliation.

The original 1950 census sheets for Leroy’s Dearborn household were reviewed. They identify a wife named Elizabeth, not Mila, and list Patrick L. (14) and Michael J. (12) as sons in the household. Mila is absent; the census does not explain her status. No marriage record connecting Mila and Leroy, maiden name, later-life record, or death record is yet established.

Family oral history says Leroy met his first wife, Mila, during World War I and describes her as “full-blood Native American”; her tribe is unknown. This is family testimony, not documentary proof. The census age estimate for Mila makes the World War I meeting chronologically inconsistent, so the identity/date conflict remains open.
