Family Relationship Notes

Mila Christina Jones was born in Indiana on 28 November 1916, the daughter of Elmer S. Jones and Nancy Hall. In January 1920 she was living as Christina Jones, age 3 years 2 months, with her parents and her siblings Beulah and Ernest in Terre Haute, Vigo County, Indiana. In 1930 she was living as Christina Jones, age 13, with her widowed father and her siblings Beulah, Ernest, William, and George in Washington, Knox County, Indiana.

She married Leroy C. Moore in the mid-1930s, before the birth of their son Patrick on 28 February 1936. No marriage record has been found. The 1940 census lists her as Mila Moore, age 23, wife of Leroy in Detroit Ward 6, with sons Patrick, age four, and Michael, age two. She died in Dearborn, Wayne County, Michigan, on 20 December 1947, at age 31; her death certificate names her father Elmer S. Jones and her mother Nancy Hall, and gives her name as "Myrtle Or Mila Christina Moore". Her death, not a divorce, ended the marriage.

Family oral history, attributed to Susan Ruth Moore and Shawn Kevin Moore, describes Mila as “full-blood Native American”; her tribe is not known. This is family testimony, not documentary proof. A search of the Indian Census Rolls, 1885–1940, found nothing for Mila, and the 1940 census records her race as White, an enumerator’s category rather than proof for or against ancestry or tribal affiliation. The account that Leroy met and married her during World War I does not stand: born in 1916, she was an infant during the war.

Life Story

Mila Christina Jones was born in Indiana on 28 November 1916, the daughter of Elmer S. Jones and Nancy Hall. She grew up in Indiana: in January 1920 she was living with her parents in Terre Haute, Vigo County, and in 1930 she was living with her widowed father and her four siblings in Washington, Knox County. She married Leroy C. Moore in the mid-1930s, before the birth of their son Patrick on 28 February 1936, and their second son Michael followed about 1938. In April 1940 the family was living in Detroit Ward 6, Wayne County, Michigan. Mila died in Dearborn, Wayne County, Michigan, on 20 December 1947, at age 31. Her death certificate names her parents and gives her name as "Myrtle Or Mila Christina Moore". Family oral history, attributed to Susan Ruth Moore and Shawn Kevin Moore, describes Mila as Native American; no tribe is named and no enrollment record has been found. The account that Leroy met and married her during World War I does not stand, since she was born in 1916.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Maiden name, parents, and siblings are now established: Mila Christina Jones, daughter of Elmer S. Jones and Nancy Hall; siblings Beulah M., Ernest E., William J., and George F. Jones. Spellings vary between the censuses (Beulah/Beaulah, Earnest/Ernest).
  • No marriage record for Mila and Leroy has been found. They were married by the mid-1930s, before Patrick’s birth on 28 February 1936.
  • The death certificate gives her name as "Myrtle Or Mila Christina Moore"; the image is unavailable, so the record is index only.
  • The 1950 census stepchildren Raymond J. Kroft, Isabell Kroft, Florence M. Kroft, and Marvin E. Kroft are Leroy’s stepchildren by his second wife Elizabeth, not Mila’s children or relatives.
  • Family oral history, attributed to Susan Ruth Moore and Shawn Kevin Moore, describes Mila as Native American, tribe unknown. A search of the Indian Census Rolls, 1885–1940, found nothing for her, and the 1940 census records her race as White. Neither proves nor disproves the family account.
  • The GitHub family-photo and headstone folders contain no image beyond placeholders; no verified portrait or headstone has been located. A photograph of Mila is believed to exist in a family album held by Shawn’s sister Kelly Lynn Pires; it has not been obtained.
