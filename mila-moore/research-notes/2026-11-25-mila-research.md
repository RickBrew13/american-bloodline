# Mila Moore — research update

**Date:** 25 November 2026

The 1940 census index identifies Mila Moore, age 23, born in Indiana, as Leroy Moore’s wife and Patrick and Michael’s mother in Detroit Ward 6. This suggests birth about 1916–1917; maiden name, parents, siblings, and exact birth date remain unknown. The indexed race field says White, an enumerator’s category, not proof for or against Native ancestry.

The original 1950 census sheets 27A–28A for Leroy’s Dearborn household were visually reviewed. A wife named Elizabeth appears; Mila does not. Patrick and Michael are listed as sons; Gary W. is another son, not attributed to Mila. The source does not establish whether Mila died, divorced, separated, or used another name.

Family oral history describes Mila as “full-blood Native American,” tribe unknown, and says she met Leroy during World War I. This remains attributed family testimony, not documented ancestry or enrollment. Its timing conflicts with the age-based 1940 estimate, which places her as an infant/toddler in 1917–1918. FamilySearch’s “Similar Records” panel surfaces a possible “Myrtle Or Mila Christina Moore” Michigan death-certificate lead; it was not identified or linked to Mila. No death, marriage, parent, sibling, or photo has been verified. See `leroy-c-moore/research-notes/2026-11-25-leroy-mila-research.md` for full citations and next steps.
