## What the census evidence says

The accessible FamilySearch 1940 census index is the only record located that directly names Mila in the Leroy Moore household. It records her as age 23, born in Indiana, wife of Leroy, and mother of Patrick (4) and Michael (2). The age implies about 1916–1917 birth, but the index provides no maiden name, parents, siblings, or exact birth date. Original 1940 schedule image not reviewed here.

The 1950 Dearborn census sheets 27A–28A show Leroy with wife Elizabeth and sons Patrick, Michael, and Gary; Mila is not present. Raymond J. Kroft, Isabell Kroft, Florence M., and Marvin E. are explicitly classified on the original schedule as stepchildren of Leroy. The census does not identify those children as Mila’s, and it does not explain Mila’s absence. Do not transfer their names to Mila’s child or sibling lists.
