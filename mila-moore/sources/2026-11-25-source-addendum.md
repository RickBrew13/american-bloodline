# Mila Moore — sources added 25 November 2026

- [FamilySearch, 1940 U.S. Census index, Mila Moore](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:KHMT-J99?lang=en), Detroit Ward 6, Wayne County, Michigan, ED 84-246, sheet 13B, line 63, digital folder 005461733, image 352. Index identifies Mila, 23, born Indiana, as Leroy Moore’s wife and Patrick and Michael’s mother. Original 1940 sheet not inspected in this pass.
- [FamilySearch, 1950 U.S. Census index for Leroy C. Moore](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:6FSF-7TQV?lang=en) and [Patrick L. Moore](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:6FSF-7TQN?lang=en), Dearborn, Wayne County, ED 84-103, sheets 27A–28A, digital folder 108985260, images 28–29. Original pages were reviewed: wife Elizabeth is present; Mila is absent; Patrick and Michael are listed as sons. This does not establish Mila’s fate. Archival copies are linked from Leroy’s profile.
- Family oral history recorded for the project: Mila was described as “full-blood Native American,” tribe unknown; family history says she met Leroy during World War I. This is family testimony, not documentary proof; the chronology conflicts with the 1940 age estimate.
- [National Archives, Indian Census Rolls, 1885–1940](https://www.archives.gov/research/census/native-americans/1885-1940.html), collection scope description. No specific roll has yet been identified for Mila.
