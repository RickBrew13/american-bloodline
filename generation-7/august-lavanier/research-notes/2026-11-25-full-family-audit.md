# August Lavanier — Gen VII family and photo audit

**Research date:** 25 November 2026 (America/New_York)

## Working assignment

August Lavanier is named as Marie Louise Lavanier’s father in the public FamilySearch tree, and as the father of a ten-child Lavanier family with Mari Karch. The relationship is carried as a working assignment with source limits documented.

## Family group and evidence limits

- Marie’s public FamilySearch profile names August Lavanier and Mari Karch as her parents. August’s public profile lists her in a ten-child family group and cites census, county-marriage and WWI draft-registration sources. Secondary Social Security/NUMIDENT-style transcriptions indexed on SortedByName also identify August F. Lavanier as Marie’s father. The parent relationship is a working assignment (~75% confidence); original birth application was not reviewed.
- August’s public FamilySearch profile names Martin Lavanier and Flora Lawanier as his parents. No sibling list is shown on the reviewed public profile.
- No verified photograph or grave image of August was located. FamilySearch source thumbnails are archived under sources/record-thumbnails/.
