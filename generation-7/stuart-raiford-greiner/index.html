Life Story

Stuart Raiford Greiner is named by Virginia Corrine Greiner Wise’s Find a Grave memorial as her father. His own Find a Grave narrative lists three children: Gladys Lacy Greiner Adams, Virginia Greiner Wise and Stuart R. Greiner. Corrine J. Lanier Greiner’s memorial identifies Virginia and Stuart Richard Greiner (1921–1976) as her children, making Stuart Richard Virginia’s reported full brother and Gladys her paternal half-sister. The latter distinction is consistent with the obituary naming Gladys as Virginia’s sister and the memorials showing Gladys’s mother as Mary Leola Tucker.

Find a Grave reports Stuart was born 18 July 1883 in Wakulla County, Florida; died 5 October 1926 in Orlando after an automobile accident; and was buried at Rose Hill Cemetery in Kissimmee. Its narrative describes him as a railroad agent who opened depots and a land speculator; those occupational details are memorial-reported, not independently verified here.

His Find a Grave page lists parents Thomas M. “Tom” Griener and Lacy Elizabeth Porter Griner. It lists first spouse Mary Leola Tucker and second spouse Corrine Lanier. Keep the different Greiner/Griener/Griner spellings as shown by their respective memorials.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The father/child relationship is supported by Virginia’s and Stuart’s Find a Grave family panels and by the linked Corrine memorial. These are derivative memorials; a birth record or contemporary family document naming Virginia’s parents was not located in this pass.
  • Stuart’s first marriage to Mary Leola Tucker and child Gladys Lacy Greiner Adams are described in the memorial narrative; Mary’s memorial links Stuart as spouse and Gladys as their child. Virginia is consequently Gladys’s paternal half-sister, not full sister.
  • Corrine’s memorial reports a 1918 marriage to Stuart and lists Virginia and Stuart Richard Greiner (1921–1976) as children. The 1921 sibling identity is memorial-reported; no independent record for Stuart Richard was reviewed.
  • The three-item child set from Stuart’s memorial is Gladys, Virginia, and Stuart R. His grave narrative says he was survived by all three; Corrine’s memorial lists only the two children in her own family group. This fits Gladys’s different mother.
  • The shared marker image is on Stuart’s Find a Grave photo page and reads Stuart R. and Corrine J. with dates. It is a family gravestone, not an individual portrait.
