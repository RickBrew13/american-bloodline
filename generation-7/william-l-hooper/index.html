Family Relationship Notes

Rick and Tom’s saved Geni work identifies Marye’s father as William Luther Hooper. The Find a Grave memorial for Marye links her to Will L. Hooper (18 Apr 1880–19 Sep 1957); Geni search results show a William Luther Hooper c.1881–deceased, married to Ethel Minerva Hooper. The initial/name expansion and the identity match are plausible but not proven by an original document.

Marye’s obituary names sisters Grace, Hinda, Merle, and brother J. T. Hooper; the cemetery memorial links that same sibling group to Will and Ethel. This supports the family grouping but remains derivative. Working parent assignment as Marye’s father: about 70% confidence with Ethel; not documented.

A Find a Grave memorial for Virgil Prentice Hooper links him as Will’s child with first wife Edna A. Paschall. If the link is correct, Virgil was Marye’s paternal half-brother. The attached stone photo on Will’s memorial appears to name “Edith,” not Ethel, and gives conflicting dates; it is not used to confirm Will’s own grave or either marriage.

Life Story

Will L. Hooper is the name shown on his cemetery memorial; a public Geni tree expands the initial to William Luther. The memorial and the saved Geni notes connect him to Marye Hooper McGregor and her siblings, but no original record confirming his full name or paternity of Marye was found in this review. He is presented as the probable working father, with the source limitations kept visible.
