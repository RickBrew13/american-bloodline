# Will L. Hooper — research note

**Research date:** 25 November 2026

Rick and Tom’s saved 2021 Geni research calls Marye’s father William Luther Hooper. Find a Grave’s user-contributed memorial for Marye links her father as Will L. Hooper (18 Apr 1880–19 Sep 1957), spouse Ethel Minerva Howard, with the same Hooper sibling group named in Marye’s obituary. Geni public search results show a William Luther Hooper c.1881–deceased and Ethel as wife; direct profiles were blocked. A memorial for Virgil Prentice Hooper links him as child of Will and first wife Edna A. Paschall, supporting a possible paternal half-brother for Marye. All links are derivative. **Working confidence as Marye’s father with Ethel: ~70%, not documented.**

A grave photo attached to Will’s memorial appears to name “Edith” and dates inconsistent with the listed family; do not use it as Will’s confirmed grave or as Ethel’s marker.
