Family Relationship Notes

The 1962 McGregor genealogy places William Clarence as a son of William Nicholas McGregor and Mary Jane Reeves and identifies him as the husband of Cora Johnson and father of James William. Roy Logan, born 1885, is listed as William Clarence’s brother, not his child. It reports he died in Benton on 2 May 1908, when James was an infant. This is a detailed compiled family history, not a civil certificate.

Rick’s 2021 Geni work and Geni’s public search index independently repeat William’s 1883–1908 dates and marriage/children. No birth, marriage, or death original record was located in this pass. Working link to James: about 80% confidence, not documented by a direct parent-child record.

Locker’s list indicates William’s siblings Ada Demonia, Thomas Burnet, and Roy Logan. The book’s family grouping should be checked against primary records before using it to make later-generation claims.

Life Story

William Clarence McGregor was born in Benton, Kentucky, in 1883 and married Cora Johnson in 1907, according to a 1962 compiled McGregor genealogy. The book reports his death in 1908, while his son James was still an infant. The source names his parents and siblings, but no original vital records were located in this pass.
