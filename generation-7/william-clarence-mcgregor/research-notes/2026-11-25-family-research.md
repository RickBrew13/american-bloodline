# William Clarence McGregor — research note

**Research date:** 25 November 2026

**Identity:** William Clarence McGregor, reported born 12 Mar 1883 and died 2 May 1908 in Benton, Kentucky. **Source quality:** the full dates, spouse, parents, siblings, and children come from Ora Butler Locker’s compiled *McGregor Genealogy* (1962), printed p. 48 / scan image 57. Geni tree search index independently repeats 1883–1908 and wife Cora B. McGregor. No original vital record, photograph, or confirmed grave found.

**Working family:** parents William Nicholas McGregor and Mary Jane Reeves; spouse Cora Johnson; child James William (22 Jan 1908). Roy Logan (26 May 1885) is William Clarence’s younger brother, hence James’s paternal uncle. The couple’s first son James is Rick’s active Gen VI person. This remains derivative evidence, so keep relationship as probable (~80%) rather than documented.

**Open:** verify the family group against Benton/Marshall County marriage, death, and census records if access becomes available; do not use the conflicting 1998 marker photo attached to James/Marye memorials as William Clarence’s grave.
