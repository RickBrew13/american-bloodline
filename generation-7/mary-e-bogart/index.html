Life Story

Mary E. Bogart was born in Kentucky about 1880. She married William J. Jordan and lived in Cincinnati, Ohio, where the 1920 census lists her with William and their children Helen, Edward, Walter, and Leroy, and the 1930 census lists her with William and their son Leroy. She was the informant on William’s death certificate when he died in Cincinnati on 31 January 1936. Her son Leroy is Roy W. Jordan.
