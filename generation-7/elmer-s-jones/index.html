Life Story

Elmer S. Jones was born in Indiana about 1892. In January 1920 he was living in Terre Haute, Vigo County, Indiana, with his wife Nancy and their three children Beulah, Christina, and Ernest. By 1930 he was living in Washington, Knox County, Indiana, a widower with five children: Beulah, Christina, Ernest, William, and George. His daughter Christina is Mila Christina Jones Moore, who married Leroy C. Moore. No marriage record for Elmer and Nancy has been found, and no record naming Elmer’s parents or giving his death has been found.
