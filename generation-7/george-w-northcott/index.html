Family Relationship Notes

FamilySearch’s public collaborative profile for George W. Northcott links him with Mary Margaret Hamilton and lists four children: Golda, Theresa “Tressie,” George Sigel, and Stanley. Stanley’s public profile separately cites a 1900 census source titled “Stanley F Northcott in household of Mary M Northcott,” plus a Michigan county birth record. Those source titles support the proposed family group, but the original census and birth record images have not been independently transcribed here.

The FamilySearch profile reports George’s birth on 27 November 1861 in Tippecanoe, Indiana, and death on 27 January 1898 in Bay City, Michigan; these are collaborative-tree facts, not independently verified vital-record findings in this pass. One structured FamilySearch view also surfaced an additional spouse association with Edna Adelle Shurter; its basis and relationship to this family are unresolved, so it is not attached as a confirmed marriage or child group.
