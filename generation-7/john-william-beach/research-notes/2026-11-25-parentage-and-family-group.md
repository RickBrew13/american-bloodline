**Research date:** 25 November 2026 (America/New_York)  
**Status:** Working Gen VII profile; proposed child relationship remains unproved.

Working assignment, not proof: John is named as Lola G. Beach’s father in a public Ancestry family-history profile; the connected profile for Bertha Caroline Swing names the same couple as Lola’s parents. This is about 70% working confidence, but the cited childhood census images are subscription-gated and were not inspected, and no birth record naming the parents has been reviewed.John’s Find a Grave memorial gives the dates and places above, links spouse Bertha, and lists children Ruth Marie Beach and John Harold Beach. It does not list Lola. A public Ancestry profile for the Beach couple reports four children—Lola, John Harold, Ruth M., and Bertha Eileen Beach—but these connected tree claims have not been checked against original records. Lola’s own Find a Grave memorial (as Lola G. Patten) gives a 1914 birth and links spouses Cleve Sparks and Harry “Shine” Patten, but does not name parents. The proposed Lola parent link therefore rests primarily on the compiled public family-history profile, with age and Ohio/Brown County context as supporting fit.The 1950 census names Lola G. Sparks is recorded as Harry’s stepson; the derivative Social Security-application compilation names Lola G. Beach as Ronald’s mother, an identity lead rather than proof of Lola’s parentage.
