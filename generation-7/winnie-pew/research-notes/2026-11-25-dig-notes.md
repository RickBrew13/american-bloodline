# Winnie Pew — dig notes, 25 November 2026

Winnie Pew, born July 1864 in Bullock County, Alabama, is the mother of Cornelius Pew (Pugh). She is named as his mother in the 1910 and 1920 censuses of Bullock County, Alabama. She died in 1930 at Three Notch, Bullock County, and was buried at Enon Church.

## Her parents

Ben Pough and Louisa Pough, named on Winnie's 1930 Alabama death record (FamilySearch ARK 1:1:JDGC-3RQ). Her maiden name was Pough: the 1900 census lists her as Minnie Pough, 36, single, daughter of Ben Pough, in her father's household at Enon, Bullock County (FamilySearch ARK 1:1:M9ZR-3RG). Ben was born in South Carolina in November 1848; Louisa was born in Georgia about 1852.

## Her husband

Will Brent, named as the father of Winnie's son Will on Will's Social Security record (FamilySearch ARK 1:1:6K73-W6X7), which names Will's parents Will Brent and Winnie Pugh. No marriage record for Winnie and Brent has been found, and Winnie was listed as Single in the 1900 census with no husband in the household. No record names Brent as Cornelius's father directly; he is listed as Cornelius's father on Rick's word, as Winnie's husband and the probable father of her children.

## The records

- 1910 census, Midway, Bullock County, ED 28, sheet 5B, household 82 (FamilySearch ARK 1:1:MK3G-7XZ): Winnie Pew, 45, widowed, head; children Willie 25, Esaw 21, Lucinda 18, Mary 15, Martha 12, Cornelius 8. Recorded as mother of 10 children, 7 living.
- 1920 census, Beat 2 (Enon), Bullock County, ED 29, household 100, sheet 6A (FamilySearch ARK 1:1:MXCR-SHW): Winnie Pew, 54, widowed, head; Lucindy 28, Martha 21, Cornelius 17, and younger children Davison 8, Luella 6, Rosie 3 years 11 months, Ocie 2 years 7 months, Lawson 1 month.

## The Pew / Pugh spelling

The family name is spelled Pew in both censuses. Cornelius appears as Pugh in all his adult records — his 1924 marriage in Birmingham, the 1930 and 1950 censuses, his World War II draft card, and his 1961 death record. No record yet links the two spellings directly. The identification rests on the same name, birth year, county, and race, and on Cornelius Pew disappearing from the records after 1920 just as Cornelius Pugh appears in Birmingham in 1924. Rick ruled on 25 November 2026 that Winnie is listed as his mother.

## Her husband

Will Brent, named as the father of Winnie's son Will on Will's Social Security record (see above). No marriage record for Winnie and Brent has been found; it may be in Russell County, unindexed, or they may never have formally married.

## Her own parents

Ben Pough and Louisa Pough, named on Winnie's 1930 Alabama death record (see above). The 1910 census says her mother was born in Georgia, which matches Louisa (born Georgia about 1852).

## The younger 1920 children

Davison, Luella, Rosie, Ocie, and Lawson Pew, in her 1920 household, are entered as her sons and daughters, but at her age they are more likely her grandchildren, recorded as children. Their later records may name their parents.
