## 1. Will Brent — the record that names him

- Will Pugh, Social Security NUMIDENT, ARK 1:1:6K73-W6X7: born 26 March 1887, Hurtsboro, Russell, Alabama; Black, male; Social Security application September 1940; Parents: **Will Brent and Winnie Pugh**. FamilySearch's own linkage of that record shows Will Brent as spouse of Winnie Pugh and father of Will Pugh (tree person GW13-GXL). Parent ordering checked against a control NUMIDENT (George William Pugh, ARK 1:1:6KSY-KB8Q, parents George Pugh then Elizebeth Wilkerson — father first, mother second), which supports reading Will Brent as the father and Winnie Pugh as the mother.
- Corroboration that this Will Pugh is Winnie Pew's son Willie/Willis: 1940 census, Will Pugh, age 52 (birth est. 1888), head, married; wife Minnie Lee Pugh, 49; children Will Adam 20, Ozie B 18, Esau 16, Emma Mae 14, Willie Mae 9; Election Precinct 2 Enon, Bullock, ED 6-4, sheet 6B, line 71 (ARK 1:1:V1LH-7HQ) — the wife Minnie Lee and the Enon, Bullock location match the 1920 census Willis Pew (age 35, wife Minnie Lee, Beat 2 Enon) exactly, and the tree lists his siblings as Esaw, Mary and Martha, matching Winnie's 1910 children.
- Caveats: ages for Willie/Willis drift across records (1882, 1884, 1885, 1887), and a separate Willie Pugh NUMIDENT (b. 5 Apr 1884, Macon, Alabama, claim 23 Dec 1952, ARK 1:1:6K79-Z8Q3) names NO parents at all, so there may be two confusable Willie Pughs. The 1940 census + Minnie Lee link is the strongest tie.
- Will Brent himself: no census, marriage or death record for Willie/Will Brent in Bullock/Russell was found except as father/spouse in Will Pugh's NUMIDENT (the Fayette County Will Brents in search results are a different family). He was presumably dead by 1900 (Winnie single, no husband in household) — but if he is Cornelius's father he must have lived until about 1901–02, which conflicts with Winnie being "Single" in 1900; that tension is unresolved.
