Family Relationship Notes

Winnie Pew is named as the mother of Cornelius Pew in the 1910 and 1920 censuses of Bullock County, Alabama. In 1910 she is 45, widowed, head of household at Midway, with six children at home; in 1920 she is 54, widowed, head of household at Beat 2 (Enon), with Cornelius, 17, still at home.

Her parents were Ben Pough and Louisa Pough, named on her 1930 Alabama death record. She was born in Bullock County in July 1864 and died at Three Notch in 1930, age 66, and was buried at Enon Church. Her maiden name was Pough: the 1900 census lists her as Minnie Pough, 36, single, daughter of Ben Pough, in her father’s household at Enon.

The family name is spelled Pew in both censuses. Her son appears as Cornelius Pugh in his adult records — his 1924 marriage in Birmingham, the 1930 and 1950 censuses, his World War II draft card, and his 1961 death record. No record yet links the Pew and Pugh spellings directly; the identification rests on the same name, birth year, county, and race, and on Cornelius Pew disappearing from the records after 1920 just as Cornelius Pugh appears in Birmingham in 1924.

Her husband was Will Brent, named as the father of her son Will on Will’s Social Security record, which names his parents Will Brent and Winnie Pugh. No marriage record for Winnie and Brent has been found, and Winnie was listed as Single in the 1900 census with no husband in the household. No record names Brent as Cornelius’s father directly; he is listed as Cornelius’s father on Rick’s word, as Winnie’s husband and the probable father of her children.

The 1910 census records Winnie as the mother of 10 children, 7 living, so one living child was not in that household and three had died. The younger children in her 1920 household — Davison, Luella, Rosie, Ocie, and Lawson — are entered as her sons and daughters, but at her age they are more likely her grandchildren, recorded as children.

Her brother Edmond Pough is confirmed by his death record, which names his father Ben Pough and his mother Louisa James. A Willie Pough, 17, is listed as Ben’s son in the 1900 census; Winnie’s son Willie has birth years drifting across records (1882–1887), so it is not settled whether the 1900 Willie is Ben’s son or Winnie’s son.

Life Story

Winnie Pew was born in Bullock County, Alabama, in July 1864, the daughter of Ben Pough and Louisa Pough. She married Will Brent — he is named as the father of her son Will on Will’s Social Security record, though no marriage record for them has been found. By 1900 she was single again and living in her father’s household at Enon with her young children. By 1910 she was widowed and heading her own household at Midway, with six children at home, the youngest Cornelius, eight years old. Ten years later she was still heading the household at Beat 2 (Enon), with Cornelius, seventeen, still at home. Her son left Bullock County for Birmingham, where he married in 1924 and lived the rest of his life as Cornelius Pugh. Winnie died at Three Notch in 1930, age 66, and was buried at Enon Church.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Find Winnie and Brent’s marriage — Russell County (Hurtsboro) records first, where Will was born; it may be unindexed or they may never have formally married.
  • Find Will Pugh and Minnie Lee’s marriage, Bullock County, about 1910–1920; it would confirm Will is Winnie’s son Willis.
  • Find James Pugh, born January 1885 in Bullock County and listed as Ben’s grandson in 1900 — he may be Winnie’s eldest son under a middle name.
  • Read the Alabama death certificate images at a FamilySearch center for Winnie Pough (1930, certificate 24758) and Cornelius Pugh (1961, file 22328) for exact dates and informants.
  • Lucinda, Mary, and Martha: find their marriages first (Alabama County Marriages, Bullock, 1910–1930) to get married surnames, then search Alabama Deaths under those names.
  • No birth record has been found for Cornelius Pew or Cornelius Pugh, born 6 October 1902 in Bullock County.
