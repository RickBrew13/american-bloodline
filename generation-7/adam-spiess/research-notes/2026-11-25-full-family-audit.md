# Adam Spiess — Gen VII family and photo audit

**Research date:** 25 November 2026 (America/New_York)

## Working assignment

Adam Spiess is named as the father of Philip Adam Spiess in the public FamilySearch tree and in the linked Adam family group. The project carries that as a strong working relationship while distinguishing the public tree from the original records it cites.

## Family group and evidence limits

- Philip’s existing profile had parents unfilled. The public FamilySearch profile for Philip Adam Spiess (LJLJ-B4C) names Adam Spiess and Henrietta Wittner as his parents, and lists Ohio birth, census and marriage sources. Adam’s public profile independently repeats Philip among five children. This is a working parent assignment (~80% confidence) supported by a specific family group and relevant index/source references; original certificates/images were not fully reviewed here.
- The public FamilySearch page for Adam gives parents Philip Spiess and Jacobine Schulz, three siblings, spouse Henrietta and five children. The page is a compiled tree, not the underlying civil record.
- No independently verified portrait or marker photo for Adam was found. FamilySearch-attached census/marriage thumbnails are archived under sources/record-thumbnails/.

Small FamilySearch-attached record thumbnails relevant to the individual/family group are preserved in `sources/record-thumbnails/README.md`. They are low-resolution source pointers, not full-size or complete records.
