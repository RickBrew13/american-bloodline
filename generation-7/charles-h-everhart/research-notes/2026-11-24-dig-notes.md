# Charles H. Everhart — research notes, 24 November 2026

## What was found
- Born about 1891 in Ohio (28 in January 1920, 38 in April 1930).
- Wife Hazel M. Henderson. Children Helen M., Raymond R., Irene A., Virgil, Harold, Dorothy, and Geneva.
- Occupations: trucker (1920, Akron), ironworker in buildings (1930, Deer Park Village).
- His daughter Irene A. Everhart married Roy W. Jordan. Her Social Security NUMIDENT and her Ohio death record name her parents as Charles H. Everhart and Hazel M. Henderson.
- The 1920 census spells the surname "Everhardt"; the 1930 census spells it "Everhart".

## Records
- 1920 U.S. Census, Akron Ward 4, ED 170, sheet 7B (index ARK 1:1:M6MW-XBG; image ARK 3:1:33S7-9RNF-WQL).
- 1930 U.S. Census, Deer Park Village, Hamilton County, ED 91-376, sheet 24A (index ARK 1:1:X4WC-YZ5; image ARK 3:1:33S7-9RH1-PYT).
- Social Security NUMIDENT, Irene Jordan (ARK 1:1:6K39-36GN).
- Ohio Death Index, Irene Jordan (ARK 1:1:VKGJ-YYJ, certificate 018385).

## Open questions
- No death record for Charles H. Everhart has been found.
- No marriage record for Charles H. Everhart and Hazel M. Henderson has been found.
- His daughter Irene's middle initial is "A" in the 1920 census and "T" in the 1940 census; the records do not explain the "T".
