Life Story

Charles H. Everhart was born in Ohio about 1891. He married Hazel M. Henderson. In the 1920 census the family was living in Akron, Ohio, where Charles worked as a trucker, with children Helen, Raymond, Irene, and Virgil. By 1930 they were living in Deer Park Village, Hamilton County, Ohio, where Charles worked as an ironworker in buildings, with seven children: Helen, Raymond, Irene, Virgil, Harold, Dorothy, and Geneva. His daughter Irene married Roy W. Jordan.
