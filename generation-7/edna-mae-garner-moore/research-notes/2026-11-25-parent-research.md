# Edna Mae Garner Moore — research note

**Date:** 25 November 2026

## Summary

The FamilySearch collaborative profile for Edna Mae Garner lists Wiley John Thomas Moore as her husband and Melvin Thomas Moore among their children, and gives a 7 April 1928 marriage date. The profile’s source links were not audited. In the 1950 census, Edna is listed in Wiley’s Linville Township household; Melvin appears in the separate adjacent household, but the schedule does not identify him as their son. Keep this relationship as the project’s working assignment, not a direct documentary proof. The census index’s Magret/Margaret identity conflict remains unresolved and is not used here to add a child or sibling.
