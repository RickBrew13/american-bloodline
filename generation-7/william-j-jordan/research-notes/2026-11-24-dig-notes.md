# William J. Jordan — research notes, 24 November 2026

## What was found
- Born 17 June 1878 in Kentucky (death certificate; the family tree and the censuses give 1879). Died 31 January 1936 in Cincinnati; buried Evergreen Cemetery, 3 February 1936.
- Cement contractor, living at 4483 Eastern Avenue, Cincinnati. Earlier: butler in a private family (1920), finisher in cement (1930).
- Wife Mary E. Bogart. Children Irene M., Helen M., Edward L., Walter G., and Leroy — Leroy is Roy W. Jordan (Rick ruled, 24 November 2026).
- His death certificate names his father as William Jordan, born in Germany; his mother's name is not given.

## Records

- 1930 U.S. Census, Cincinnati Ward 1, ED 91-22, sheet 12A (index ARK 1:1:X47N-8P3; image ARK 3:1:33SQ-GR8H-92B2).

## Open questions
- No marriage record for William Jordan and Mary E. Bogart has been found.
- Irene M. Jordan, named as a daughter on the family tree, was not in the household in the 1920 or 1930 census.
- Birth year: 1878 (death certificate) vs 1879 (tree and censuses).
- Helen's and Edward's birth years differ by about a year between the tree (1903, 1906) and the 1920 census ages (about 1904, 1907).
