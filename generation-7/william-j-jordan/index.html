Life Story

William J. Jordan was born in Kentucky on 17 June 1878. He lived in Cincinnati, Ohio, where the 1920 census lists him as a butler in a private family, living with his wife Mary and their children Helen, Edward, Walter, and Leroy. By 1930 he was working as a finisher in cement, living with Mary and their son Leroy. He was a cement contractor by the time of his death in Cincinnati on 31 January 1936, and he was buried at Evergreen Cemetery on 3 February 1936. His son Leroy is Roy W. Jordan.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Irene M. Jordan, named as a daughter on the family tree, was not in William and Mary Jordan’s household in the 1920 or 1930 census.
  • William’s birth year is 1878 on his death certificate (age 57 years 7 months 14 days at death); the family tree and the censuses give 1879.
  • The family tree gives Helen’s birth about 1903 and Edward’s about 1906; the 1920 census ages suggest about 1904 and 1907.
  • No marriage record for William Jordan and Mary E. Bogart has been found.
