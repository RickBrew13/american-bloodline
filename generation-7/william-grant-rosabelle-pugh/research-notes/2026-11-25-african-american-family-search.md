# African American family-search addendum — Rosabelle Pugh parent assignment

**Research date:** 25 November 2026 (America/New_York)  
**Person:** William Grant

Rick identifies the Pugh family as African American. This is the historical context for further research, not evidence by itself that any same-name person belongs to this family.

The prior Rosabelle Pugh project profile already named William Grant as her reported father, citing compiled family trees. The source tree ID and cited records were not preserved. The name is linked in Generation VII as a working family-history assignment at Rick’s direction; no original parent-child document has yet been found. This page does not establish a marriage to the other named parent.

A 1930 census index reports Rosabelle Pugh age 22 in the same Birmingham household as Ruby age 7. Ruby’s marriage indexes later report mother-name variants Rosabelle Grant and Rosa Bell White. These records help identify Rosabelle as the daughter’s mother; they do not identify Rosabelle’s parents. The original images remain to be inspected.

Searches focused on African American genealogy resources did not locate an identity-linked record for William Grant. The [Alabama African American genealogy guide](https://www.theipsnow.org/alabama.html) points researchers toward the 1866 Alabama State Census, 1870 census, and Freedmen’s Bureau records. These are next sources to examine; no Pugh entry from them has been verified for this parent assignment in this pass. No life dates, spouse, siblings, or additional children are established.
