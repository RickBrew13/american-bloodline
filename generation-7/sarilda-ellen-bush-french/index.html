Family Relationship Notes

Rita’s 1997 obituary transcription says “Bulla (Bush) French” was her mother; her existing public FamilySearch tree gives Surilda Ellen Bush, while Find a Grave identifies Sarilda Ellen “Shillie” Bush French. These are treated as a likely identity match in the working parent pair, but “Bulla” remains an unresolved name difference and the original birth record has not been reviewed.

The linked Find a Grave memorial names eight siblings and seven named children; the linked FamilySearch profile for Rita counts four additional unnamed children beyond its four named siblings plus Rita. Do not infer the hidden names.

The photographed stone reads “Shillie E. French,” with dates 18 Jan. 1884–21 Aug. 1968. The public memorial gives the fuller name and parentage; the marker itself does not establish “Bush” or Rita’s parentage.
