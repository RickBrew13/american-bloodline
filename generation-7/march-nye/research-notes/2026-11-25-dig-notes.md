# March Nye — dig notes
Date: 25 November 2026. FamilySearch signed-in pass (RickBrewster1, read-only).

## What was found
- March Nye is named only as the father of Major Nye Sr. on Major's 1921 Alabama death record (Alabama Deaths, 1908–1974; certificate 15640; FamilySearch ARK 1:1:JKQZ-3M2). The record names Major's mother as Sarah Vaughn and his spouse as Lorena. Major was 61, est. birth 1860, died 1921 in Stockton, Baldwin County, Alabama.
- No census, marriage, death, or other record for March himself has been found.
- His son Major was born in Alabama about 1862–1863 (March 1862 per the 1900 census; age 47 in 1910) and died in Stockton in 1921.

## Next records
