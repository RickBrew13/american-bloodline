# Dexter Powell — candidate identification for Rowland Edmund Powell’s father

**Research date:** 25 November 2026 (America/New_York)  
**Working identification confidence: about 75%; not proven.** The marriage record supplies the given name Dexter and a spouse match to Minnie, but the middle initials conflict between the indexed record and scanned license, and no census/child record explicitly links Rowland to them.

## Evidence

- West Virginia birth index WVID 134534 identifies Rowland E. Powell, born 19 May 1911, birthplace Baltimore, Maryland; record filed in Harrison County. The original register names Minnie Powell as mother. Father handwriting was first read D. L.; reinspection may read D. G. The index has no parent-name fields.
- Harrison County marriage index WVID 5021 identifies a 1 July 1903 marriage of Dexter G. Powell and Minnie M. Lyon.
- The original license scan/OCR is ambiguous and appears to spell the groom Dexter J. Powell (possibly S.), not G. It reports groom age 52 and bride age 32; groom birthplace Doddridge County and both residents in Harrison County. OCR/text recognition can misread handwriting, so this remains unresolved pending human reading of the original.
- The matching groom given name/surname and Minnie/Minnie Lyon pairing make Dexter a plausible candidate for the father named only by initials on Rowland’s birth entry. Yet the middle-initial conflict and absence of an explicit Rowland child link prevent certainty.

## Conclusion and next steps

Dexter Powell is the strongest full-given-name candidate so far, about 75% working confidence. Do not report the middle initial as settled: the index gives G; the scanned license transcription appears J or possibly S; Rowland’s birth-register father initial is itself unclear (L or possibly G). Search 1910/1920 census households and other children’s vital records for an explicit link. Rowland’s birthplace remains Baltimore, while Harrison is the birth-record filing county.
