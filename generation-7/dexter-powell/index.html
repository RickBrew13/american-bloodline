Family Relationship Notes

Dexter Powell is Rowland Edmund Powell’s father. The 1911 birth register for Rowland E. Powell reads the father as “D. G. Powell” and the mother as “Minnie Powell,” and the 1920 census lists 8-year-old Rowland as “Son” in Dexter and Minnie’s household in Salem Township, Harrison County. Dexter’s middle initial is most likely G: the birth register reads D. G., the marriage index gives Dexter G. Powell, and his 1931 death record names him “Dexter Galen Powell.” The 1903 marriage license image writes the groom’s name “Dexter J. Powell” three times, so the J reading remains a genuine conflict on that one record.

Rowland’s birthplace is recorded as Baltimore, Maryland; Harrison County is the filing county for the West Virginia register.

Life Story

A Harrison County marriage index identifies Dexter G. Powell as the groom in a 1903 marriage to Minnie M. Lyon. The license image writes the groom “Dexter J. Powell” three times, so the middle initial is unresolved between G and J, with G the stronger reading. The 1911 birth register for Rowland E. Powell names Minnie Powell as mother and “D. G. Powell” as father, and the 1920 census lists Rowland as Dexter and Minnie’s son. Dexter Powell is Rowland’s father.

His middle initial is most likely G (birth register; indexes; 1931 death record “Dexter Galen Powell”), but the 1903 marriage license image writes “Dexter J. Powell” three times — the conflict is kept visible, not settled by guess. His parents, named on the 1903 license as Ralph M. Powell and Roxaline Powell, are listed on his Parents card; their profiles are now built and linked from his Parents card. Her first name is spelled many ways in the records; her profile is filed under Rocksalina Williams Powell, the spelling on her tombstone.

Dates: born about 1850 (death record; the marriage record says born Doddridge County, the death record says Salem — the two birthplaces conflict); died 8 May 1931 in Cabell County. Dexter was widowed when he married Minnie in 1903; his first-marriage children Fred Dallas Powell (born 1881), Fredrick Powell (born 1881), and Charles Ray Powell (born 1883) are Rowland’s half-siblings, per the tree — their records have not been checked in this pass.

25 November 2026: Dexter’s nine brothers and sisters are now listed on his Siblings card, from the family tree and the children’s birth and death records. The 1850 census does not line up with the tree on two of them — it lists a Martha, age 4, where the tree has Martha Elizabeth born in 1852, and a James L., age 0, where the tree has James Lloyd born in 1847 — and Dexter himself, born in February 1850 by the tree, is not listed in the 1850 household.
