Family Relationship Notes

The project’s James Edward profile already names this couple as his parents. James Edward’s Ohio Death Index identifies his father by the surname Shircliff and his mother’s maiden name Yates; it does not give these parents’ forenames. Find a Grave memorials for James Edward, Ida’s husband and son Marvin repeat the James Absalom–Ida Belle pair. Treat the couple as the working, strongly supported assignment, not as a direct birth-certificate proof.

A Find a Grave memorial for James Absalom lists ten children, including James Edward, and five siblings. The memorial is a compiled memorial, so names/dates are attributed to it; no original birth entries for the full group were inspected.

Marvin Andrew’s reproduced 4 July 2001 Courier-Journal obituary names siblings Ray, Marie Whelan, Rose Widmer, Theresa Shields, and “Selma Mills.” The memorial/tree names the latter as Thelma Cecilia Mills. Keep Selma/Thelma as an unresolved variant or obituary error; do not create a separate sibling on that basis.
