# Sources — Edith May Kinner

Relationship evidence is provisional. Different source types are kept distinct; the user-contributed memorial entries and connected public family-history profiles do not independently prove parentage.

- <a href="https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/138194998/edith_may-patten">Find a Grave memorial 138194998, Edith May Kinner Patten</a> — gives dates, burial, parents and sibling links, and lists Harry, Nellie, and Mildred as children. User-contributed memorial; source documentation for each family link is not supplied on the page.
- <a href="https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/249800508/ora_clifford-patten">Find a Grave memorial 249800508, Ora Clifford Patten</a> — contributor narrative reports marriage to “Edythe Mabel Kinner Patten” on 31 October 1909 and cites a 1993 Brown County history book; the book and marriage record were not independently reviewed.
