Family Relationship Notes

Working assignment, not proof: Edith is listed as Harry O. Patten’s mother in a public Ancestry family-history profile and as Harry’s mother on his Find a Grave memorial. The working parent assignment to Harry is about 70% confidence; the memorial relationship is user-contributed and the public Ancestry profiles are connected compiled-tree content. No reviewed birth record or childhood census image establishes the parent-child link.

Ora Clifford Patten’s Find a Grave contributor narrative calls Ora’s wife “Edythe Mabel Kinner Patten” and gives a 1909 marriage. Edith’s memorial name is “Edith May Kinner Patten.” This likely refers to the same woman, but the difference in given-name form has not been resolved by an original marriage record. Keep “Patten” distinct from the “Patton” spelling used on Harry’s 1950 census schedule.

Edith’s Find a Grave memorial links her to children Harry, Nellie, and Mildred, and lists parents Noah Otto Kinner and Elizabeth Belle Stevens plus nine Kinner sibling profiles. These are memorial relationships, not independently reviewed vital or census records. Harry’s father is not named on the 1950 schedule.
