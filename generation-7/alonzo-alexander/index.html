Life Story

The 1920 U.S. census index identifies “Maggie Alexander,” age 3 years 10 months, as daughter of Lon Alexander and Francis Alexander in Mingo County, West Virginia. Margaret’s existing profile and records identify her as Margaret G. Alexander Weis, born in 1916; the age and family setting are consistent. Her 1935 marriage record further names “Lonzo” as the bride’s father-in-law. Together these sources strongly support this Alonzo/Lon Alexander as Margaret’s father.

Find a Grave reports Alonzo “Lon” Alexander was born 20 December 1875 in West Virginia, died in Pike County, Kentucky, on 11 September 1933, and was buried at Johns Run Cemetery in Carter County, Kentucky. The 1920 census index gives his birthplace as Kentucky and age 44, so retain both birth-place/year discrepancies.

Find a Grave identifies his wife as Francis E. Runion and reports they married in Wayne County, West Virginia, in 1893. Margaret’s 1935 marriage record spells her mother-in-law’s first name “Frances”; the census uses “Francis.” The wife’s own memorial and 1967 obituary clipping identify her later as Francis Moon.

The one image attached to Alonzo’s Find a Grave page was read as an unrelated “John A. Baldwin” birth-certificate document and is excluded from this profile; I did not find a verified portrait or marker image for Alonzo.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The 1920 census schedule is the most direct relationship evidence: Maggie Alexander is explicitly indexed as Lon Alexander’s daughter. The 1935 Weis marriage record independently names her father-in-law as “Lonzo”; this supports the identity of “Lon” and Alonzo.
  • The date 20 December 1875 and West Virginia birthplace come from Find a Grave; the 1920 census index gives age 44 (about 1876) and Kentucky birthplace. Keep the conflict explicit rather than choosing one without a civil record. The existing Margaret profile uses 1876–1933 based on the linked FamilySearch tree.
  • The Find a Grave page names Lewis Hensley and Sarah “Sallie” Robinson as Alonzo’s parents. They are reported in the memorial text; no birth record or original household record for Alonzo was examined to independently establish them.
  • The names of additional children are drawn from Find a Grave family links and indexed 1920/1930 census transcriptions. The exact complete sibling set and each birth/death date need separate review; only Margaret’s link is the target of this task.
  • The attached Find a Grave image reads as a document for John A. Baldwin, not Alonzo Alexander. It is excluded as misattributed/uncorroborated; no verified portrait or personal marker image was found.
