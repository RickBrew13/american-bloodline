# Parent identity cross-check — Alonzo “Lon” Alexander

Reviewed 26 November 2026.

- The Find a Grave biography for Lewis Hensley (memorial 221832036) states that Lewis was born with surname Alexander and later used his mother’s maiden name Hensley; it names Alonzo (20 Dec 1875–1933) as his son.
- The Alonzo memorial (221840673) names Lewis Hensley and Sarah “Sallie” Robinson as his parents, and includes a transcribed 1880 household headed by Lewis Alexander, wife Sallie, and a five-year-old L. Alexander.
- James Pharoah Hensley’s memorial (182736232) independently repeats Lewis Alexander Hensley and Sarah/Sally Robinson as parents and Alonzo as a sibling. These are derivative memorial narratives, not original civil records.
- Lewis’s memorial’s title dates him 1847–1927, while its census-age transcriptions suggest a different birth year, about 1840–1842. The profile keeps the discrepancy explicit.
- The available evidence supports the parent links but does not establish them from an inspected original census image or birth record.

Sources: [Lewis Hensley](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/221832036/lewis-hensley); [Alonzo Alexander](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/221840673/alonzo-alexander); [James Pharoah Hensley](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/182736232/james-pharoah-hensley).
