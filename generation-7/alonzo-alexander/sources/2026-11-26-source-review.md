# Source review — Alonzo “Lon” Alexander

Accessed 26 November 2026.

- [Maggie Alexander, 1920 U.S. Census, FamilySearch MN2J-DZL](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MN2J-DZL) — Names Lon Alexander as father and Francis Alexander as mother; Maggie is age 3 years 10 months, born West Virginia, in Mingo County, West Virginia.
- [Abe Weis and Margaret Alexandria, Kentucky County Marriage record, 6 July 1935](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:Q2D4-5QZ3) — Names “Lonzo” as Margaret’s father-in-law and “Frances” as her mother-in-law; the original application is not re-inspected in this pass, but the current child profile identifies image 567 as the original certificate.
- [Alonzo “Lon” Alexander, Find a Grave memorial 221840673](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/221840673/alonzo-alexander) — Reports vital dates, burial, parents, spouse and family group; includes a transcription of census details. Derivative memorial, not original civil registration.
- [Alonzo Alexander, Find a Grave photo page](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/221840673/alonzo-alexander/photo) — The only attached image was read as a document for John A. Baldwin and is excluded as unrelated/uncorroborated.
- [Francis E. Runion Alexander Moon, Find a Grave memorial 217476260](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/217476260/francis-e-runion-alexander-moon) — Lists Alonzo as spouse and Margaret G. Alexander Weis among children; reports a 3 May 1877 birth and 10 March 1967 death.
- [Margaret G. Alexander Weis, An American Bloodline profile](https://rickbrew13.github.io/american-bloodline/margaret-g-alexander-weis/) — Existing profile links Alonzo and Frances as Margaret’s parents and records the census/marriage source distinctions.

## Limits

- No original birth, death or marriage certificates were retrieved for these four parent profiles; the existing project has a directly read image of Abraham and Margaret’s 1935 marriage application, while this audit reviewed its FamilySearch index entry.
- The FamilySearch shared-tree person pages for full names/dates required sign-in and were not independently rechecked. Use the original cited census/marriage records for relationship claims.
- Find a Grave family panels and user-attached photos are secondary/derivative and are identified as such.
