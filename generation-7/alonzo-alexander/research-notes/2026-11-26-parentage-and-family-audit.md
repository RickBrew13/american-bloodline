## Working relationship

## Caveats and open questions

- The 1920 census schedule is the most direct relationship evidence: Maggie Alexander is explicitly indexed as Lon Alexander’s daughter. The 1935 Weis marriage record independently names her father-in-law as “Lonzo”; this supports the identity of “Lon” and Alonzo.
- The date 20 December 1875 and West Virginia birthplace come from Find a Grave; the 1920 census index gives age 44 (about 1876) and Kentucky birthplace. Keep the conflict explicit rather than choosing one without a civil record. The existing Margaret profile uses 1876–1933 based on the linked FamilySearch tree.
- The Find a Grave page names Lewis Hensley and Sarah “Sallie” Robinson as Alonzo’s parents. They are reported in the memorial text; no birth record or original household record for Alonzo was examined to independently establish them.
- The names of additional children are drawn from Find a Grave family links and indexed 1920/1930 census transcriptions. The exact complete sibling set and each birth/death date need separate review; only Margaret’s link is the target of this task.
- The attached Find a Grave image reads as a document for John A. Baldwin, not Alonzo Alexander. It is excluded as misattributed/uncorroborated; no verified portrait or personal marker image was found.
