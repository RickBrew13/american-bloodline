# Rose Jane Clements Wethington — family and source audit
**Date:** 26 November 2026

## Parentage and Anna connection
Find a Grave memorial 20962441 reports Rose’s parents as Joseph Green Clements (1857–1930) and Lucinda Jane Goode Clements (1854–1929). Its attached obituary transcription says Rose was born in 1886, daughter of Lucinda Goode and Joe Green Clements, and names “Mrs. Edith Woodrum” among her children. The Anna Edith profile and memorial family panels identify that daughter as Anna Edith Wethington Woodrum. These sources support the connection, but no original birth record or marriage register was reviewed.

## Spouse and children
Find a Grave links Rose to William Bradford Wethington; her obituary transcription says she married William B. Wethington on 12 November 1907. The obituary lists eight children: Harvey Wethington; Edith Woodrum; Urial Clements; Curtis Wethington; Bernice Gouty; Vatus Green; Protus Wethington; and Verlin Wethington. The family-panel list supplies fuller names and dates: Harvey Glen, Anna Edith, Mary Urial, Bernice Arline, Protus E., Vatus M., William Herbert and James Verlin. Preserve “Curtis” vs. “William Herbert” and “Verlin” vs. “James Verlin” as unresolved discrepancies; the obituary may list surviving children only, but that is not established.

## Siblings
Find a Grave’s family panel lists William Edward “Willie,” John Warren, James Sherman, George Thomas, Rhoda Ann, Joseph Alfred “Joe,” Henry Sidney, Lafayette Elmer “Lafe,” and Clement Clements. The obituary transcription says she survived by three brothers, “Thomas, Sidney, and Elmer,” all of Clementsville. These likely refer to some listed brothers using middle/short names, but not all can be matched confidently; do not call either set exhaustive.

## Dates and residence
Find a Grave and obituary transcription report Rose born 21 November 1886 and died 4 May 1958; obituary says she died at St. Francis Hospital in Indianapolis and had lived in Lebanon, Indiana for 21 years. Find a Grave reports burial at Saint Bernard Cemetery, Clementsville. Attached marker transcription reads “Roni Jane” with birth 24 November 1896, death 4 May 1958, and William B. with birth 25 June 1897, death 23 June 1960. Death dates match Rose/William reports, but the names and birth years differ; this is a plausible shared grave marker with transcription uncertainty, not a reason to overwrite dates.

## Photo provenance
No verified portrait found. The main memorial image seems to be a shared marker candidate; its text transcription conflicts on names/birth years but aligns on death dates. The other image is an obituary clipping, not a portrait. Neither used as a profile portrait.
