Life Story

Rose Jane Clements Wethington’s Find a Grave memorial reports birth 21 November 1886 and death 4 May 1958. The obituary transcription attached to that memorial independently gives her birth year as 1886, names her parents as Lucinda Goode and Joe Green Clements, and says she married William B. Wethington on 12 November 1907.

The obituary names daughter “Mrs. Edith Woodrum” among eight children, supporting the identity of Edith as Anna Edith Wethington Woodrum. Find a Grave’s family panels list Anna Edith among Rose and William’s children. Anna’s new reciprocal links point to both parent profiles.

The 1958 obituary lists three brothers as Thomas, Sidney and Elmer Clements. Find a Grave’s sibling panel reports a larger set, including Willie, John, James, George, Rhoda, Joseph, Henry, Lafayette and Clement. Preserve this difference; the obituary may name only survivors or use shortened names.
