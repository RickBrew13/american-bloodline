# Will Brent — dig notes, 25 November 2026

Will Brent is the husband of Winnie Pew and the father of her son Will Pugh, named only on Will's Social Security record (FamilySearch ARK 1:1:6K73-W6X7): Will Pugh, born 26 March 1887, Hurtsboro, Russell, Alabama; parents Will Brent and Winnie Pugh.

No census, marriage, or death record for Brent himself has been found. No record names him as the father of Cornelius Pew (Pugh) directly; he is listed as Cornelius's father on Rick's word, as Winnie's husband and the probable father of her children. Winnie was listed as Single in the 1900 census with no husband in the household.

Will Brents in Fayette County, Alabama, are a different family and are not this man.

Next records: Winnie and Brent's marriage in Russell County (Hurtsboro) records; Brent in the 1880 and 1900 censuses of Russell and Bullock Counties; his death record; Will Pugh's SS-5 (Social Security application form), which would confirm the parents as written on the NUMIDENT.
