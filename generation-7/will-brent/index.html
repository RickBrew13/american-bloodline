Family Relationship Notes

Will Brent is named only as the father of Will Pugh on Will’s Social Security record, which names his parents Will Brent and Winnie Pugh. No census, marriage, or death record for Brent himself has been found.

His son Will was born 26 March 1887 in Hurtsboro, Russell County, Alabama, and married Minnie Lee; the 1940 census has them at Enon, Bullock County, with five children. That household matches the Willis Pew household of the 1920 census (wife Minnie Lee, Beat 2 Enon), which ties Will Pugh to Winnie’s son Willie.

No record names Brent as the father of Cornelius Pew (Pugh) directly. He is listed as Cornelius’s father on Rick’s word, as Winnie’s husband and the probable father of her children. Winnie was listed as Single in the 1900 census with no husband in the household, and no marriage record for Winnie and Brent has been found — the marriage may be in Russell County, unindexed, or they may never have formally married.

Will Brents in Fayette County, Alabama, are a different family and are not this man.

Life Story

Will Brent is known only from his son’s records. His son Will was born in Hurtsboro, Russell County, in 1887, and Will’s Social Security record names Will Brent and Winnie Pugh as his parents. No census, marriage, or death record has been found for Brent himself, and no record names him as the father of Winnie’s youngest son Cornelius directly — he is listed as Cornelius’s father on Rick’s word, as Winnie’s husband and the probable father of her children.
