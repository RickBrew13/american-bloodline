# Research note — James Thomas Grooms

**Updated:** 26 November 2026

## Working conclusion

James Thomas Grooms is linked as a Gen VII parent of Lenora Lovene (Grooms) Waulk in the existing Gen VI profile. The link is corroborated by Lenora’s 2013 obituary, which names both parents, plus the parents’ linked memorials and Grace’s funeral-home obituary. The profiles use source-attributed wording and do not claim original certificates were reviewed.

## Biographical facts and relationships

- **Birth:** 3 March 1878 · place not established by the reviewed primary records (Find a Grave reports USA)
- **Death:** 18 May 1951 · Elmville, Highland County, Ohio (memorial report)
- **Burial:** Dunkard Ridge Cemetery, Elmville, Highland County, Ohio
- **Parents:** John Franklin Grooms (1852–1914); Caroline L. (Bruce) Grooms (1846–1911)
- **Spouse(s):** Grace Evelyn (Smith) Grooms Coleman (14 April 1905–28 December 2004) — Find a Grave memorial reports marriage in 1924
- **Children:** Lenora Lovene (Grooms) Waulk (4 February 1936–18 May 2013); Vernon Charles Grooms (1930–1945)
- **Siblings:** Find a Grave lists Charles Henry Grooms (1872–1959), Nancy Jane Grooms Hilderbrand (1874–1933), Anna Louella Grooms Hilderbrand (1876–1968), and Eli Preston Grooms (1882–1940) as siblings; it lists Verna “Vernie” Grooms Davis (1890–1966) as a half-sibling.

## Findings, caveats and open questions

- The Find a Grave memorial family panel lists Verna “Vernie” Grooms Davis (born 1890) in both a half-sibling category and a child list. The categories conflict and she is not linked as a child in this profile; Lenora and Vernon are the child relationships corroborated by Lenora’s and Grace’s obituaries.
- The 1924 marriage to Grace is reported by Find a Grave, but the marriage certificate was not reviewed. Lenora’s obituary provides the direct parent names J. T. Grooms and Grace Evelyn (Smith) Grooms Coleman.
- The memorial gives James’s birth as 3 March 1878 but does not specify a state in the displayed vital facts; another derivative site result suggested Adams County, but the underlying source was blocked and was not reviewed. Therefore no county has been added.
- No portrait of James was located in the memorial images reviewed. The profile image is the Find a Grave headstone photo, credited to Roger Waulk, and labeled as a headstone.
