# Source review — James Thomas Grooms

**Accessed:** 26 November 2026

## Reviewed sources

- [Lenora Lovene Waulk, Journal-News obituary, 22 May 2013](https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/hamilton/name/lenora-waulk-obituary?id=22676822) — Names her late father J. T. Grooms and mother Grace Evelyn (Smith) Grooms Coleman; names her late brother Vernon Grooms.
- [James Thomas Grooms, Find a Grave memorial 112958349](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/112958349/james_thomas-grooms) — Reports dates/burial; links John Franklin Grooms and Caroline L. Bruce as parents, Grace as spouse (1924), and Lenora and Vernon among children.
- [Grace Evelyn Coleman, Thompson Funeral Homes obituary](https://www.thompsonfuneralhomes.com/obituaries/grace-coleman-4) — Reports first husband James Thomas Grooms, who died in 1951; identifies daughter Lovene and son-in-law Paul Waulk, and son Vernon Grooms.
- [Lenora Lovene Grooms Waulk, Find a Grave memorial 61634468](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/61634468/lenora_lovene-waulk) — Links James Thomas Grooms and Grace Evelyn Coleman as Lenora’s parents.

## Limits

- No original birth certificates, marriage certificates, death certificates, or original census images were retrieved in this pass.
- Preserve exact names and date discrepancies as cited; do not treat Find a Grave family panels or compiled family trees as primary evidence.
