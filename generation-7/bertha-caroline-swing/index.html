Family Relationship Notes

Working assignment, not proof: Bertha is named as a parent of Lola G. Beach in a public Ancestry family-history profile, with John William Beach as the other parent. This is about 70% working confidence, not an established parent-child relationship. The underlying childhood census images cited by the compiled profile were not inspected, and no birth record naming Bertha has been reviewed.

Find a Grave’s memorial for Bertha gives her birth as 28 June 1890; the linked public Ancestry profile gives 26 June 1890. This two-day discrepancy is retained rather than silently resolved. Her Find a Grave memorial links spouse John and children Ruth Marie Beach and John Harold Beach, but it does not list Lola. The public Ancestry profile for the Beach couple reports Lola, John Harold, Ruth M., and Bertha Eileen Beach as children; these are connected tree claims, not independently verified. The compiled Ancestry profile is currently the source for the proposed Lola parentage.

Lola’s own Find a Grave memorial gives a 1914 birth and lists her marriages to Cleve Sparks and Harry “Shine” Patten; it does not name her parents. The 1950 census records Lola G. Patton, age 36 and Ohio-born, as Harry’s wife, but has no named parents.
