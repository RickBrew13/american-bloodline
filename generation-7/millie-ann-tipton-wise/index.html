Life Story

Millie Ann Tipton Wise is identified as the mother of Samuel Preston Wise in Find a Grave and in her compiled FamilySearch family profile. The profile reports her marriage to Richard Hood Wise in Estill County on 15 February 1909 and six children; Find a Grave’s memorial panels list her husband and children. The project’s link from Samuel Preston Wise to Samuel P. Wise remains probable, not proven by a direct original identity record.

FamilySearch’s compiled profile reports Millie was born 28 January 1888 and died 15 June 1969 in Estill County, Kentucky. Find a Grave gives the same dates and Wise Cemetery #2 as her burial place. No original birth or death record was inspected.

The same marker image is attached to the Richard and Millie Find a Grave memorials. The extracted lettering appears to say 1832 and 1838 for the births, which is incompatible with the memorial and family-group dates; do not treat that reading as reliable.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The 15 February 1909 marriage date comes from the compiled FamilySearch profile. The original Estill County marriage register has not been reviewed; the earlier Ole.net family compilation gives 1 February 1909. Preserve only the year as settled until the original register is checked.
  • The compiled FamilySearch profile and memorial panels agree that Samuel Preston Wise was one of Millie’s children; the project-level link to Monica’s father is probable, conditional on the death-index identity match.
  • The family group’s six children are Elijah (1910), Marjorie (1911), Samuel Preston (1913), Wallace Ray (1918), Otha Elmer (1921), and Warren Calvin (1925). Elijah and Marjorie died as infants and are absent from surviving-child memorial panels.
  • The sibling list from Millie’s Find a Grave memorial differs from FamilySearch’s compiled Tipton profile. Find a Grave lists Ellen Liza, John Sherman, Clay, Betty, Curt Vernon, Margaret Emerine, William, Clyde and Ora Lee; FamilySearch’s compiled timelines add/replace Laura May and Nettie Lanald. Do not collapse the conflict into one certain complete sibling list.
  • The shared marker image’s OCR birth-year readings (1832 and 1838) conflict with the dates reported by the memorials and compiled family profile. The marker is retained as a credited image reference, not date proof.
