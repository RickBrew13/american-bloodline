## Family group and evidence limits

- Birthplace also conflicts: FamilySearch gives Czechia while SortedByName snippets identify Hungary or Cincinnati in various entries; retain both source-attributed forms until original application and birth/civil records are reviewed.
- No sibling list for Mari appears on her public FamilySearch profile. No photo or grave image was found.
