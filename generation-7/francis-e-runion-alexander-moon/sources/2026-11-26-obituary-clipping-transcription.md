> Services Held Monday For Francis Moon
>
> Mrs. Francis Moon, 88, of Midland, died at 2 a.m. last Friday at Clinton Memorial Hospital. She had been hospitalized for five days with pneumonia and a heart condition. She made her residence with her step-son, Roy Estep.
>
> Mrs. Moon was born May 3, 1877 in Pike County, Kentucky. Her husband, Blaine Moon, preceded her in death.
>
> Other survivors include three sons, Fred Alexander, Charleston, W. Va.; George Alexander and Roy Alexander, both of Kentucky; three daughters, Mrs. Pearl Spurlock, Mrs. Margaret Weiss and Mrs. Shirley Apking, all of Cincinnati; 51 grandchildren and several great and great-great grandchildren.
>
> Services were conducted at the Smith Funeral Home here Monday afternoon with the Rev. Arthur Helsley officiating.
>
> Burial was in Westboro IOOF Cemetery.
