# Source review — Francis E. (Runion) Alexander Moon

Accessed 26 November 2026.

- [Maggie Alexander, 1920 U.S. Census, FamilySearch MN2J-DZL](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MN2J-DZL) — Names Francis Alexander as Maggie’s mother and Lon Alexander as father; census age and household connect to the Margaret profile.
- [Abe Weis and Margaret Alexandria, Kentucky County Marriage record, 6 July 1935](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:Q2D4-5QZ3) — Names “Frances” as Margaret’s mother-in-law.
- [Francis E. Runion Alexander Moon, Find a Grave memorial 217476260](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/217476260/francis-e-runion-alexander-moon) — Reports vital dates/place/burial, links Alonzo as spouse and Margaret as child.
- [Francis Moon obituary clipping attached to Find a Grave memorial 217476260](https://images.findagrave.com/photos/2022/271/217476260_d4493f62-8344-49d5-b183-3ad4c72d099a.jpeg?size=photos250) — Clipping names Mrs. Margaret Weiss as a daughter, records birth 3 May 1877 in Pike County, Kentucky, and says husband Blaine Moon preceded her. Newspaper title/publication date are not visible in the reviewed image.
- [Margaret G. Alexander Weis, An American Bloodline profile](https://rickbrew13.github.io/american-bloodline/margaret-g-alexander-weis/) — Existing profile records Frances Armstrong Runion as Margaret’s mother, but notes that the expanded middle/maiden name came from a compiled tree.

## Limits

- No original birth, death or marriage certificates were retrieved for these four parent profiles; the existing project has a directly read image of Abraham and Margaret’s 1935 marriage application, while this audit reviewed its FamilySearch index entry.
- The FamilySearch shared-tree person pages for full names/dates required sign-in and were not independently rechecked. Use the original cited census/marriage records for relationship claims.
- Find a Grave family panels and user-attached photos are secondary/derivative and are identified as such.
