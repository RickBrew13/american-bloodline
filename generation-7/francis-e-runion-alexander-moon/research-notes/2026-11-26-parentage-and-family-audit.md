# Research note — Francis E. (Runion) Alexander Moon

**Reviewed:** 26 November 2026

## Working relationship

This Gen VII profile is linked as a parent of Margaret G. Alexander Weis based on the sources below. The existing child profile and new profile now link to one another.

## Caveats and open questions

- The 1920 census index directly identifies “Maggie Alexander” as daughter of “Lon Alexander” and “Francis Alexander.” The 1935 marriage record says “Frances” is Margaret’s mother; Francis Moon’s obituary clipping names “Margaret Weiss” among her daughters. This evidence supports the person link despite spelling and later-married-surname differences.
- The obituary clipping is from a public Find a Grave media attachment and names a daughter Margaret Weiss, but its newspaper name and publication date are not visible. Do not assign a newspaper or issue date.
- The obituary reports husband Blaine Moon had died earlier; Find a Grave links Alonzo Alexander as a spouse and gives a 1893 marriage. The chronology/order and any original marriage records for both spouses were not reviewed.
- The current project profile lists “Frances Armstrong Runion” and a death year of 1969 from a FamilySearch tree. The Find a Grave memorial gives “Francis E. Runion” and 1967; the obituary clipping supports 1877 birth and 1967-era death but no exact publication date. Keep the source and date discrepancy explicit; do not silently replace the current family-tree statement.
- The Find a Grave portrait/marker attachment for this memorial is an obituary clipping, not a portrait. It can be shown as a sourced clipping in the gallery but should not be used as a personal portrait.
