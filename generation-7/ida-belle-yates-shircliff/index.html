Family Relationship Notes

The existing James Edward profile identifies Ida Belle Yates as his mother. James Edward’s Ohio Death Index provides the Yates maiden name; linked Find a Grave memorials name Ida Belle as the spouse/mother and identify the same ten-child Shircliff family. The shared marker independently corroborates Ida Belle’s name and dates as inscribed, while the child relationships remain memorial/tree evidence rather than a reviewed birth record.

The Find a Grave memorial for Ida lists eight siblings and names her parents as Robert Edward Yates and Elizabeth Jane “Jennie” Bickett Yates. These are memorial-linked family claims; no original birth or probate record for the group was inspected here.

The 2001 Marvin obituary calls one sister “Selma Mills,” but the linked Shircliff memorial and public FamilySearch profile say Thelma Cecilia Mills. Preserve the discrepancy rather than creating a tenth sibling.
