Family Relationship Notes

Rita’s existing project profile had no parents entered. Her 1997 obituary transcription says she was born in Sunfish, Kentucky, daughter of “Jim and Bulla (Bush) French.” The public FamilySearch tree names James Joshua French and Surilda Ellen Bush, and the linked Find a Grave memorial for Rita names James Joshua and Sarilda Ellen “Shillie” Bush French. The geography, surname, age and recurring family group align. **Working parent assignment confidence: approximately 75%; not proven by a reviewed birth record.** The mother’s given name differs between “Bulla” in the obituary transcription and Sarilda/Surilda/Shillie in other compiled sources; keep that discrepancy explicit and do not silently normalize it.

The Find a Grave memorial for James Joshua names his own parents and eight siblings and lists five children. The FamilySearch public tree for Rita expands the sibling group with Oscar and Raymond, and counts four further unnamed children. The parent-child set is compiled evidence, not a full review of vital records.

His memorial has an individual marker photo credited to Christie Scott-Swentko. It is a stone image, not a portrait.

James Joshua French is the likely father of Rita Pauline French Shircliff. The 1997 obituary calls her a daughter of Jim and Bulla (Bush) French; FamilySearch and linked memorials identify a James Joshua French–Sarilda Ellen Bush household in the same Sunfish/Edmonson County community. This is a working attribution with a real given-name discrepancy for the mother, not a direct birth-record proof.
