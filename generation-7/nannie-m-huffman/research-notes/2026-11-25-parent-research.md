# Nannie M. Huffman — research note

**Date:** 25 November 2026

## Summary

A derivative SortedByName entry for Grace Irene Huffman / Irene Ben Moore gives Nannie M. Huffman as mother and Richard Benfield as father; the underlying application or source image has not been retrieved. Geni’s profile for Grace instead gives her mother as Nannie M. Coleman and no father. The 1950 Valdese census index lists a Nannie M. Huffman, age 37, widowed, North Carolina-born, as the daughter of the household head; Benny L. Huffman and Lewis Huffman Jr. are indexed as the head’s grandsons. That is compatible with a Huffman family connection but does not name Grace or establish the identity of this Nannie. The name variant, identity, dates, relationship to Richard, and parentage of Grace remain unresolved. Their placement together in Gen VII is provisional and is not independent proof that Richard and Nannie were spouses or Grace’s parents.
