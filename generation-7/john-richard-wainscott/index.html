Family Relationship Notes

Ray’s live profile identifies John Richard Wainscott (1862–1952) as his father using family records/memorial links. Ray’s Find a Grave memorial also lists John as parent, and the FamilySearch/Ancestry-derived public tree gives dates. This supports the father-child link; Ray’s death certificate has not been inspected.

Ray’s Find a Grave memorial links Nellie Tucker Wainscott Cox as his mother, but the existing profile notes a possible stepmother relationship based on her age relative to older children. Ray’s own death certificate is needed to settle his mother. Do not state Nellie as proven biological mother.

A separate public family site reports John’s parents as George W. Wainscott and Mary Susan Jackson. These are generation-above leads, not profiled here.

Ray and his brother Fred’s public FamilySearch profiles name Melvina Nellie Tucker as their mother; Ray’s Find a Grave panel also lists Nellie Tucker Wainscott Cox. This makes her Ray’s best-supported mother candidate (~75%), but the sources are compiled family panels, Nellie’s birth year differs (1886/1887/1890), and Ray’s 1965 death certificate is still needed for direct confirmation.
