# John Richard Wainscott — Generation VII parent profile audit

**Date:** 26 November 2026

Ray Henry Wainscott’s live project profile identifies John Richard Wainscott (1862–1952) as Ray’s father, based on family records and memorial links. Ray’s Find a Grave memorial 137165284 lists John and Nellie Tucker Wainscott Cox as parents. The live Ray profile explicitly leaves his mother unknown because the memorial-linked Nellie’s age relative to older children raises the possibility of a stepmother. This page therefore records John as father and does not claim Nellie as Ray’s biological mother.

A public family-site record reports John born 4 April 1862 in Owen County and died 17 May 1952 in Cincinnati; it names parents George W. Wainscott and Mary Susan Jackson, but is a derivative web tree. The profile does not create a Gen VIII couple or present those names as verified. The 1965 death certificate of Ray (Ohio certificate 03458) would settle his parent identities; it has not been inspected.
