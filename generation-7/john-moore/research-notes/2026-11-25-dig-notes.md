# John Moore — dig notes, 25 November 2026

## What was found
- John Moore is the father of Leroy Moore, born 27 September 1898 in Butte, South Dakota, son of John Moore and Mary Murphy. South Dakota Department of Health, Index to Births 1843–1914. ARK: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:QKD8-HDRH
- Leroy Moore's school records: age 7, enrollment 1905, Day Township, Clark, South Dakota; also Webster, Day, South Dakota in 1906, 1907 and 1910. ARK: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:QK79-1LF3
- No John Moore × Mary Murphy household was found in the Butte County, South Dakota, censuses of 1900, 1910, 1920, or 1930, and none was found in the 1925 South Dakota state census.
- Leroy Moore, Nevada World War II draft registration, 16 February 1942, Yerington, Lyon, Nevada: born 27 September 1898, Belle Fourche, South Dakota; WPA worker; person who would always know his address, Mrs Rosa Wilhems, Belle Fourche, South Dakota; marked "Lame". ARK: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:Q2B8-4BV4
- Oregon Death Index: Leroy Moore, born 27 September 1898, died 5 May 1982, Portland, Oregon, certificate 82-08359. ARK: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VZ4B-NBG
- The Oregonian obituary, 8 May 1982: Mr Leroy Moore, age 83, born 27 September 1898, Belle Fourche, South Dakota, died 5 May 1982, Portland, Oregon; occupation farm laborer; sister Edith Williams. ARK: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:Q5MQ-61M6
- Polk's Portland City Directory, 1962 (archive.org item polksportlandcity1962rich, printed page 933): "Moore, Leroy (Audrey N) leadermn Consolidated Frtway h7121 SE Carlton."
- Rosa Wilhems: no record found in any source checked — not in the 1940 Belle Fourche census, no marriage, obituary, or cemetery record.
- The one Edith Williams candidate found, of Benton County, Oregon, wife of Harry Williams, was proved to be Edith R. Wagner, daughter of Richard Wagner (born 1863, Iowa; died 1938) and Clara Bell Grubb (born 1869, Indiana; died 1955), married 20 September 1893, Iowa. Evidence: 1910 census, St Charles, Gregory, South Dakota (Edith R. Wagner, age 3, daughter of Richard and Clara); 1930 census, Gerlach, Washoe, Nevada (Edith R. Williams, parents' birthplaces Iowa and Indiana); her son Austin Richard Williams's Social Security application, June 1944, naming his mother Edith R. Wagner. She died 30 April 1973, Harney, Oregon.
