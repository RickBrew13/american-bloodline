# Research note — Grace Evelyn (Smith) Grooms Coleman

**Updated:** 26 November 2026

## Working conclusion

Grace Evelyn (Smith) Grooms Coleman is linked as a Gen VII parent of Lenora Lovene (Grooms) Waulk in the existing Gen VI profile. The link is corroborated by Lenora’s 2013 obituary, which names both parents, plus the parents’ linked memorials and Grace’s funeral-home obituary. The profiles use source-attributed wording and do not claim original certificates were reviewed.

## Biographical facts and relationships

- **Birth:** 14 April 1905 · Adams County, Ohio, USA
- **Death:** 28 December 2004 · Hamilton, Butler County, Ohio, USA
- **Burial:** Dunkard Ridge Cemetery, Elmville, Highland County, Ohio
- **Parents:** Charles Benton Smith (1869–1940); Ora Ellen (Powell) Smith (1876–1973)
- **Spouse(s):** James Thomas Grooms (3 March 1878–18 May 1951) — first husband; memorial reports 1924 marriage; Frank Coleman — second husband, named in obituary; marriage date not stated
- **Children:** Lenora Lovene (Grooms) Waulk (4 February 1936–18 May 2013); Vernon Charles Grooms (1930–1945)
- **Siblings:** Find a Grave lists Tanna Smith Osborne (1897–1976), Jesse Benton Smith (1899–1982), Addie Bell Smith Hoover (1903–1986), Dora Olive Smith Hawes (1908–1982), Ollie Otis Smith (1910–1992), Okie Faye Shoemaker (1913–1984), and Ora Edison Smith (1916–1990) as siblings. These memorial-linked sibling identities have not been independently verified in this pass.

## Findings, caveats and open questions

- The funeral-home obituary directly names Charles and Ora Powell Smith as Grace’s parents. Lenora’s obituary names her as “Grace Evelyn (Smith) Grooms Coleman”; keep that family name sequence distinct from the Find a Grave page styling “Smith/Grooms Coleman.”
- The official obituary identifies James Thomas Grooms as Grace’s first husband and reports that he died in 1951; it identifies Frank Coleman as her second husband and says he died in 1970. The obituary does not give either marriage date. A Find a Grave family panel reports the James marriage as 1924; do not treat it as a reviewed marriage certificate.
- The obituary names one daughter (Lovene, wife of Paul Waulk) and son Vernon Grooms. Lenora’s obituary confirms Lenora is the daughter and names the late brother Vernon; the memorial family panel links both children.
- Find a Grave’s portrait photo is credited to Roger Waulk; the separate grave image reads “Grace Grooms Coleman 1905–2004.” Both are archived and captioned separately.
