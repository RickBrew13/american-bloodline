# Source review — Grace Evelyn (Smith) Grooms Coleman

**Accessed:** 26 November 2026

## Reviewed sources

- [Grace Evelyn Coleman obituary, Thompson Funeral Homes](https://www.thompsonfuneralhomes.com/obituaries/grace-coleman-4) — Official funeral-home obituary gives birth/death, Adams County birthplace, parents Charles and Ora Powell Smith, first husband James Grooms (d. 1951), second husband Frank Coleman (d. 1970), daughter Lovene and son-in-law Paul Waulk, son Vernon, church and burial.
- [Lenora Lovene Waulk, Journal-News obituary, 22 May 2013](https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/hamilton/name/lenora-waulk-obituary?id=22676822) — Names Lenora’s parents as J. T. Grooms and Grace Evelyn (Smith) Grooms Coleman; says she married David Paul Waulk and had children.
- [Grace Evelyn Smith/Grooms Coleman, Find a Grave memorial 110805610](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/110805610/grace_evelyn-coleman) — Reports dates/burial and reproduces the obituary; links James as spouse and Lenora and Vernon as children; family members are memorial-reported.
- [James Thomas Grooms, Find a Grave memorial 112958349](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/112958349/james_thomas-grooms) — Links Grace as spouse (married 1924) and Lenora and Vernon among children; reports James’s dates/burial.
- [Charles Benton Smith, Find a Grave memorial 109164609](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/109164609/charles_benton-smith) — Family panel links Grace as a daughter and Ora Ellen Powell Smith as spouse; memorial describes their family.

## Limits

- No original birth certificates, marriage certificates, death certificates, or original census images were retrieved in this pass.
- Preserve exact names and date discrepancies as cited; do not treat Find a Grave family panels or compiled family trees as primary evidence.
- Photo files in this folder are credited in the companion photo-provenance note.
