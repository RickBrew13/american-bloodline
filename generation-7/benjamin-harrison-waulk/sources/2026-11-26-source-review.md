# Source review — Benjamin Harrison Waulk

**Accessed:** 26 November 2026

## Reviewed sources

- [Benjamin Harrison Waulk, Find a Grave memorial 14627457](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/14627457/benjamin_harrison-waulk) — Reports dates, Cynthiana Cemetery, family group; maintained by grandson Roger Waulk.
- [Benjamin Waulk, obituary clipping in Greenfield Daily Times (image posted to Find a Grave)](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/14627457/benjamin_harrison-waulk/photo) — Visible clipping names parents Martin and Mary Hale Waulk, birth date 7 Jan 1897, marriage to Mamie Parrish in 1921, some surviving children and church. Full date/header not visible in scan.
- [Mamie Sedalia Parrish Waulk, Find a Grave memorial 14629127](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/14629127/mamie_sedalia-waulk) — Widow of Harrison Waulk; names David Paul (“Paul”), Floyd and other children; obituary text gives her parents.
- [David Paul “Paul” Waulk, Find a Grave memorial 35374642](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/35374642/david_paul-waulk) — Paul’s obituary explicitly names Benjamin Harrison and Mamie (Parrish) Waulk as his parents and names wife/children.
- [Floyd Russell Waulk, Find a Grave memorial 61592320](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/61592320/floyd_russell-waulk) — Floyd’s obituary names parents Harrison and Mamie Waulk and Paul as a brother; memorial family panel also identifies Benjamin and Mamie.

## Limits

- No original birth certificates, marriage certificates, death certificates, or original census images were retrieved in this pass.
- Preserve exact names and date discrepancies as cited; do not treat Find a Grave family panels or compiled family trees as primary evidence.
- Photo files in this folder are credited in the companion photo-provenance note.
