# Pete White — dig notes, 25 November 2026

Pete White is the father of Rosa Belle White, named only on Rosa's 1951 Alabama death record (FamilySearch ARK 1:1:J6QM-K56), which also names her mother Sallie Parker. No census, birth, marriage, or death record for Pete himself has been found.

- Pete White married Sallie on 2 December 1903 in Autauga County, Alabama. The marriage index spells the bride "Sallie Smith," which conflicts with "Sallie Parker" on their daughter's death record.
- Children named on death and marriage records: Rosa Belle (born about 1905, died 1951 Birmingham), Ell White, Buster White, Gladys Bell White.
- A daughter Annie, born July 1904, would fit as Pete and Sallie's first child, but no record names her as theirs.
- Daughter Rosa married Cornelius Pugh on 7 September 1924 in Jefferson County, Alabama, and lived with him in Birmingham until her death in 1951.

Next records: the 1910 and 1920 censuses for a Pete White household with Sallie and daughters Annie and Rosa in Autauga or Jefferson County; the original 1903 marriage license image (to reconcile Smith vs Parker); Pete's own birth and death records.
