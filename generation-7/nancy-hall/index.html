Life Story

Nancy Hall was born about 1897. In January 1920 she was living in Terre Haute, Vigo County, Indiana, with her husband Elmer S. Jones and their children Beulah, Christina, and Ernest. She died before 1930: in that year’s census Elmer is listed as a widower in Washington, Knox County, Indiana, with their five children Beulah, Christina, Ernest, William, and George. Her daughter Christina is Mila Christina Jones Moore, who married Leroy C. Moore. Her birthplace is given as Illinois in the 1920 census and as Indiana in the 1930 census. No record naming her parents, no marriage record, and no death record has been found.
