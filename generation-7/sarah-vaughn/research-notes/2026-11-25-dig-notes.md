# Sarah Vaughn — dig notes
Date: 25 November 2026. FamilySearch signed-in pass (RickBrewster1, read-only).

## What was found
- Sarah Vaughn is named only as the mother of Major Nye Sr. on Major's 1921 Alabama death record (Alabama Deaths, 1908–1974; certificate 15640; FamilySearch ARK 1:1:JKQZ-3M2). Vaughn is her maiden name as given on that record. The record also names Major's father as March Nye and his spouse as Lorena.
- No census, marriage, death, or other record for Sarah herself has been found.
- Her son Major was born in Alabama about 1862–1863 and died in Stockton in 1921.

## Next records
- The 1870 and 1880 censuses for a March Nye household in Baldwin County, Alabama.
