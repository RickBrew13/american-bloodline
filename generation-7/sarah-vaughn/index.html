Family Relationship Notes

Sarah Vaughn is named only as the mother of Major Nye Sr. on Major’s 1921 Alabama death record, which also names his father March Nye. Vaughn is her maiden name as given on that record. No census, marriage, death, or other record for Sarah herself has been found.

Her son Major was born in Alabama about 1862–1863 and died in Stockton, Baldwin County, Alabama, in 1921. Major married Eliza Lang in 1884, Missouri Smith in 1887, and Mary Rigby in 1901.

Life Story

Sarah Vaughn is known only from her son’s death record. Major Nye Sr. died in Stockton, Baldwin County, Alabama, in 1921, and the record names his father March Nye and his mother Sarah Vaughn. Vaughn is her maiden name as given on that record. No record of Sarah’s own life — no census entry, marriage record, or death record — has been found. Her son Major was born in Alabama about 1862–1863, married three times, and died in Stockton in 1921.
