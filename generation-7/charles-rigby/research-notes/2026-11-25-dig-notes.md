# Charles Rigby — dig notes
Date: 25 November 2026. FamilySearch signed-in pass (RickBrewster1, read-only).

## What was found
- Charles Rigby was born in Alabama about 1850 (March 1850 per the 1900 census; about 1848 per his age in the 1880 census).
- He married Silla Kimball on 17 July 1871 in Baldwin County, Alabama, page 139 (Alabama County Marriages, ARK 1:1:XTWT-GQR). The license image was read in full: celebrated by E. Robinson. No parents or ages are given. This is the only Charles-as-groom marriage in Alabama County Marriages in 1870–1880, and no second marriage for him as groom was found in Alabama in 1880–1900.
- 1880 census, Stockton, Baldwin County (ARK 1:1:M4N1-4VR): Charley Rigby, 32, married, head, works on flatboat, with wife Sillah, 32, and daughters Ella, 7, and Ida, 3. Sheet 167D, ED 2, household 11725556, enumerated 17 June 1880.
- 1900 census, Perdido, Baldwin County (ARK 1:1:M9ZL-Z38): Charles Rigby, 50, born March 1850, widowed head, with Ida, 24, Mary, 19 (born April 1881), Charley, 17, Lillian, 6, and Francis, 3. ED 2, sheet 14B, household 241.
- Daughter Ella Petts died 1945 in Stockton, age 69, father Chas. Rigby (ARK 1:1:J6MK-SBX; certificate image restricted).
- Daughter Ida Carter died 1917 in Latham, Baldwin County, age 37, father Chas. Rigby, mother Pricilla Rigby, of Stockton (ARK 1:1:JDX5-ZXP; certificate 526).

## Not found / not assigned
- No 1870 census record for Charles; no 1910 or 1920 census record for him or his son Charley in Baldwin County.
- A Charles Rigby married Mary Thomas on 15 April 1906 in Baldwin County, page 281 (ARK 1:1:XTW8-S21), but the license gives no ages, so the groom could be Charles Sr., about 56, or his son Charley, about 23. Either way she is not Mary's mother.
- A July 1925 Stockton death record for a Charles Rigby, age 85, born about 1840, certificate 15220 (ARK 1:1:JDKS-S76), is not assigned to him: the birth year is ten years off, no spouse or parents are named, and the certificate image is restricted. The deciding record is that certificate image, viewable only at a FamilySearch center.
- Lillie Mae Stevens, born about 1910, died 1932 in Stockton, father Charles Rigby, mother Cilla Kimble (ARK 1:1:JDGY-9C3), belongs to a later generation and is not his daughter.
