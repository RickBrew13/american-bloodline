Life Story

Charles Rigby was born in Alabama about 1850. He married Silla Kimball on 17 July 1871 in Baldwin County, Alabama. In June 1880 he was living in Stockton with his wife Sillah and their daughters Ella and Ida, working on a flatboat. By 1900 he was widowed and living in Perdido with five children: Ida, Mary, Charley, Lillian, and Francis. His daughter Mary married Major Nye in 1901 and is the mother of Leon Niles. No death record for Charles has been found.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Charles’s birth year varies by record: the 1900 census gives March 1850, while his age in the 1880 census implies about 1848.
  • No 1870 census record for Charles Rigby has been found, and no 1910 or 1920 census record for him or his son Charley has been found in Baldwin County.
  • A Charles Rigby married Mary Thomas on 15 April 1906 in Baldwin County, but the license gives no ages, so the groom could be Charles Sr., about 56, or his son Charley, about 23. Either way, she is not the mother of Mary Rigby, who was born in 1881.
  • A July 1925 Stockton death record for a Charles Rigby, age 85, born about 1840, certificate 15220, is not assigned to him: the birth year is ten years off the March 1850 of the 1900 census, no spouse or parents are named, and the certificate image is restricted.
  • Lillie Mae Stevens, born about 1910 and died in 1932 in Stockton, whose death record names her father Charles Rigby and her mother Cilla Kimble, belongs to a later generation and is not Charles’s daughter.
  • The surname appears as Rigby in the censuses and Rigsby on the 1871 marriage license.
  • No photograph or headstone photo for Charles Rigby has been found; Find a Grave, BillionGraves, FamilySearch tree photos, and free newspapers were searched on 25 November 2026.
