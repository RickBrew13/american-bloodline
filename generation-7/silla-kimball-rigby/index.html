Family Relationship Notes

Silla Kimball married Charles Rigsby on 17 July 1871 in Baldwin County, Alabama. In June 1880 she was living in Stockton with her husband and their daughters Ella and Ida. Her daughter Mary was born in April 1881, ten months after that census, while Sillah was Charles’s wife, and no second marriage for Charles has been found between 1880 and 1900.

Her name appears as Silla Kimball on the marriage license and as Sillah on the 1880 census image. Her daughter Ida’s 1917 death record names Ida’s mother Pricilla Rigby, so her full name was probably Priscilla, but no record writes both forms for the same woman, so this page uses Silla.

Life Story

Silla Kimball married Charles Rigsby on 17 July 1871 in Baldwin County, Alabama. In June 1880 she was living in Stockton with her husband and their daughters Ella and Ida. Her daughter Mary was born in April 1881, ten months after that census, while Sillah was Charles’s wife, and no second marriage for Charles has been found between 1880 and 1900. She died before 1900, when Charles was recorded as widowed. Her daughter Mary married Major Nye in 1901 and is the mother of Leon Niles.
