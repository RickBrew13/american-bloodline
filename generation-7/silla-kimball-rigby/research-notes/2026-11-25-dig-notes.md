# Silla Kimball Rigby — dig notes
Date: 25 November 2026. FamilySearch signed-in pass (RickBrewster1, read-only).

## What was found
- Silla Kimball married Charles Rigsby on 17 July 1871 in Baldwin County, Alabama, page 139 (Alabama County Marriages, ARK 1:1:XTWT-GQR). The license image was read in full: celebrated by E. Robinson. No parents or ages are given.
- 1880 census, Stockton, Baldwin County (ARK 1:1:M4N1-4VT): Sillah Rigby, 32, born about 1848 in Alabama, wife, keeping house, with husband Charley Rigby, 32, and daughters Ella, 7, and Ida, 3. Sheet 167D, ED 2, household 11725556, enumerated 17 June 1880.
- Mary was born in April 1881, ten months after that census, while Sillah was Charles's wife, and no second marriage for Charles was found in Alabama in 1880–1900. The records point to Silla as the mother of all six children: Ella, Ida, Mary, Charley, Lillian, and Francis.
- Charles was widowed by the 1900 census, so Silla died before 1900. No death record for her has been found.
- Her name appears as Silla Kimball on the marriage license and as Sillah on the 1880 census image. Her daughter Ida's 1917 death record names Ida's mother Pricilla Rigby (ARK 1:1:JDX5-ZXP), so her full name was probably Priscilla, but no record writes both forms for the same woman.

## Tree notes (leads only, not proof)
- The tree has her as Sillah Rigby (G5L6-7JM) and separately as Silla Kimball (GZ2M-7L6), two tree persons for the same woman; they are not merged.
- The tree groups Mary, Charley, Lillian, and Francis under a second, mother-unknown spouse; that is a contributor's arrangement, not a record.
