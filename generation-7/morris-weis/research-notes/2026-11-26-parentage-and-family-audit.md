# Research note — Morris Weis

**Reviewed:** 26 November 2026

## Working relationship

This Gen VII profile is linked as a parent of Abraham Benjamin Weis based on the sources below. The existing child profile and new profile now link to one another.

## Profile findings

- **Birth:** 4 July 1886 · Romania (Find a Grave memorial report)
- **Death:** 10 December 1963 · Los Angeles County, California, USA (Find a Grave memorial report)
- **Burial:** Eden Memorial Park, Mission Hills, Los Angeles County, California (Find a Grave report)
- **Parents:** Julius Weis (1840–1939); Sarah Malka (Schmielowitz) Weis (1865–1929)
- **Spouses:** Bessie Sadler Weis (1890–16 October 1918); Find a Grave family panel reports marriage in 1907; Pearl Gittelman Lerner Weis (1894–1949); also listed as a spouse on Morris’s Find a Grave memorial
- **Children:** Samuel Weis (1908–1976); Abraham Benjamin Weis (1910–1977); Faye (Weis) Eve (1914–1996)
- **Siblings:** Find a Grave lists Carl Weis (1884–1950) and Frances Pearl Weis Holtzman (1899–1998) as siblings; these memorial-linked identities are not independently verified here.

## Caveats and open questions

- The marriage record names the groom’s parents only as “Morris” and “Bessie”; the surname Weis and full name Bessie Sadler come from the established child profile and memorial-linked family group.
- The 1920 census index places Morris with Julius Weis and lists Abe, age 8, as Julius’s grandson. It supports the family grouping but does not directly state that Abe is Morris’s son. The 1935 marriage record is the direct parent-name source.
- The memorial gives Morris a birth date of 4 July 1886 and the 1920 census gives him age 34, consistent with 1885/1886. However, the photo attached to his Find a Grave page is automatically read as a marker reading 1906–1963. Because that year conflicts, the image is not treated as proof of Morris’s birth year and is not used as his portrait.
- Find a Grave lists both Bessie Sadler Weis (1890–1918, married 1907) and Pearl Gittelman Lerner Weis (1894–1949) as Morris’s spouses. The sequence and underlying marriage records for Pearl were not examined; do not infer more than the memorial reports.
- The Find a Grave profile associates the displayed parents Julius Weis and Sarah Malka Schmielowitz Weis with Morris. No original birth record was reviewed to verify this generation link.

FamilySearch lists image 567; the existing Abraham profile records that the original certificate image was read.

The index does not state Abe’s relationship to Morris explicitly.

- [Abraham Benjamin Weis, Find a Grave memorial 79067667](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/79067667/abraham_benjamin-weis) — Links Morris Weis and Bessie Sadler Weis as Abraham’s parents; family-panel relationships are derivative.
