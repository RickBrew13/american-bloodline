# Source review — Morris Weis

Accessed 26 November 2026.

- [Abe Weis and Margaret Alexandria, Kentucky County Marriage record, 6 July 1935](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:Q2D4-5QZ3) — Indexed record from Campbell County, Kentucky; explicitly names Morris as Abe’s father and Bessie as his mother. FamilySearch lists image 567; the existing Abraham profile records that the original certificate image was read.
- [Abe Weis, 1920 U.S. Census, FamilySearch MDT8-1LT](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MDT8-1LT) — Lists Morris Weis, age 34, born Romania, in Julius Weis’s Cincinnati household; Abe, age 8, is recorded as Julius’s grandson. The index does not state Abe’s relationship to Morris explicitly.
- [Morris Weis, Find a Grave memorial 193782971](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/193782971/morris-weis) — Reports dates, birthplace and burial; links parents, two spouses and three children, including Abraham. Memorial/photo details are derivative.
- [Morris Weis, Find a Grave photo page](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/193782971/morris-weis/photo) — Only attached image is a marker; its OCR-transcribed 1906–1963 dates conflict with the memorial birth date 1886 and the 1920 census age. Not used as a verified portrait.
- [Abraham Benjamin Weis, Find a Grave memorial 79067667](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/79067667/abraham_benjamin-weis) — Links Morris Weis and Bessie Sadler Weis as Abraham’s parents; family-panel relationships are derivative.
- [Bessie Sadler Weis, Find a Grave memorial 144146108](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/144146108/bessie-weis) — Links Bessie as Morris’s spouse and lists Samuel, Abraham and Faye as their children; memorial reports a 1907 marriage.

## Limits

- No original birth, death or marriage certificates were retrieved for these four parent profiles; the existing project has a directly read image of Abraham and Margaret’s 1935 marriage application, while this audit reviewed its FamilySearch index entry.
- The FamilySearch shared-tree person pages for full names/dates required sign-in and were not independently rechecked. Use the original cited census/marriage records for relationship claims.
- Find a Grave family panels and user-attached photos are secondary/derivative and are identified as such.
