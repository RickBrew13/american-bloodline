Life Story

Morris Weis is identified as Abraham Benjamin Weis’s father by the 6 July 1935 Kentucky marriage record, which names the groom’s parents as Morris and Bessie. In the 1920 census index, Morris is listed in the Cincinnati household of Julius Weis, while Abe appears in that household as Julius’s grandson. Together these support the father–son identification, although the census index itself does not label Abe as Morris’s son.

Find a Grave reports Morris was born 4 July 1886 in Romania, died 10 December 1963 in Los Angeles County, California, and was buried at Eden Memorial Park. The same memorial links him to Bessie Sadler Weis and Pearl Gittelman Lerner Weis as spouses and names children Samuel, Abraham and Faye. These memorial particulars are not a substitute for vital records.

The single photo attached to Morris’s Find a Grave memorial is described by the image extractor as a marker reading “Morris Weis … 1906–1963.” That apparent 1906 year conflicts with the memorial’s 1886 birth date and the 1920 census age of 34. I have not treated the marker’s year as a correction or used it as an identified portrait.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The marriage record names the groom’s parents only as “Morris” and “Bessie”; the surname Weis and full name Bessie Sadler come from the established child profile and memorial-linked family group.
  • The 1920 census index places Morris with Julius Weis and lists Abe, age 8, as Julius’s grandson. It supports the family grouping but does not directly state that Abe is Morris’s son. The 1935 marriage record is the direct parent-name source.
  • The memorial gives Morris a birth date of 4 July 1886 and the 1920 census gives him age 34, consistent with 1885/1886. However, the photo attached to his Find a Grave page is automatically read as a marker reading 1906–1963. Because that year conflicts, the image is not treated as proof of Morris’s birth year and is not used as his portrait.
  • Find a Grave lists both Bessie Sadler Weis (1890–1918, married 1907) and Pearl Gittelman Lerner Weis (1894–1949) as Morris’s spouses. The sequence and underlying marriage records for Pearl were not examined; do not infer more than the memorial reports.
  • The Find a Grave profile associates the displayed parents Julius Weis and Sarah Malka Schmielowitz Weis with Morris. No original birth record was reviewed to verify this generation link.
