Family Relationship Notes

Locker’s 1962 McGregor genealogy identifies Cora Johnson as William Clarence’s wife and mother of James William. It reports birth 16 August 1880 and death 13 January 1959, and gives her parents as James and Augusta Heath Johnson.

The public Geni index identifies the same woman as “Cora B McGregor” (1880–1959), wife of William Clarence, and names mother “Cornelia Augusta Johnson,” sibling Gillard Bane Johnson Sr., and an unnamed half-sibling. The likely relationship is strong but those name differences and sibling links are tree assertions, not original records. Working confidence that this Cora is James’s mother: about 80%; the exact full identity of her parents is unresolved.

Life Story

Cora Johnson, later identified in Geni as Cora B. McGregor, is reported in the 1962 McGregor genealogy as William Clarence McGregor’s wife and the mother of James William. Roy Logan is William Clarence’s brother, not Cora’s child. The book says her parents were James and Augusta Heath Johnson. A Geni index instead names her mother as Cornelia Augusta Johnson. Without an original record bridging the names, both descriptions are preserved as attributed evidence.
