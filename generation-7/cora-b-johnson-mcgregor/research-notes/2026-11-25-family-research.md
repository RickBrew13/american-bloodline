# Cora B. (Johnson) McGregor — research note

**Research date:** 25 November 2026

Locker’s 1962 *McGregor Genealogy*, printed p. 48 / scan image 57, says Cora Johnson was born 16 Aug 1880, died 13 Jan 1959, married William Clarence McGregor 2 Feb 1907, and was daughter of James and Augusta Heath Johnson. Geni’s public search result calls her Cora B. McGregor (1880–1959), names William Clarence as husband and two children, and gives mother as Cornelia Augusta Johnson; it also reports sibling Gillard Bane Johnson Sr. The mother’s differing form may refer to one person but is unbridged. No original vital record or image located. Working confidence as James William’s mother ~80%; the names of Cora’s parents and siblings remain attributed/uncertain.
