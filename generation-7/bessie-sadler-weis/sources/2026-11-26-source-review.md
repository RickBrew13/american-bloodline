# Source review — Bessie Sadler Weis

Accessed 26 November 2026.

- [Abe Weis and Margaret Alexandria, Kentucky County Marriage record, 6 July 1935](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:Q2D4-5QZ3) — Names “Bessie” as Abe’s mother; the indexed record derives from Campbell County marriage records.
- [Bessie Sadler Weis, Find a Grave memorial 144146108](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/144146108/bessie-weis) — Reports 1890 birth year, Russia birthplace, death date/place, burial; memorial links Morris as spouse and Samuel, Abraham and Faye as children.
- [Bessie Sadler Weis, Find a Grave photo page](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/144146108/bessie-weis/photo) — Shows the attached marker image and source credit to contributor Syndi; the marker is not a portrait.
- [Morris Weis, Find a Grave memorial 193782971](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/193782971/morris-weis) — Lists Bessie as spouse (reported 1907 marriage) and Abraham among their children.
- [Abraham Benjamin Weis, An American Bloodline profile](https://rickbrew13.github.io/american-bloodline/abraham-benjamin-weis/) — Existing profile cites the 1935 marriage record and lists Bessie Sadler (1890–1918) as Abraham’s mother; full-name/dates were previously tree-derived.

## Limits

- No original birth, death or marriage certificates were retrieved for these four parent profiles; the existing project has a directly read image of Abraham and Margaret’s 1935 marriage application, while this audit reviewed its FamilySearch index entry.
- The FamilySearch shared-tree person pages for full names/dates required sign-in and were not independently rechecked. Use the original cited census/marriage records for relationship claims.
- Find a Grave family panels and user-attached photos are secondary/derivative and are identified as such.
