Life Story

Bessie Sadler Weis is named as Abraham Benjamin Weis’s mother in his 6 July 1935 Kentucky marriage record. The record gives only “Bessie” as the mother’s given name; the full name Bessie Sadler and the 1890–1918 lifespan come from the Find a Grave family memorials.

Find a Grave reports Bessie was born in Russia in 1890 and died in Cincinnati on 16 October 1918, with burial at Golf Manor No. 3 Cemetery. The image attached to her memorial is described as a grave marker bearing “Bessie Weis,” the death date and age 28; it is a marker, not a portrait.

Morris and Bessie’s memorial-linked family group names Samuel Weis, Abraham and Faye Weis Eve as their children. The 1935 marriage record independently names Bessie as Abraham’s mother; the other two child links remain memorial-reported in the material reviewed.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The original/indexed 1935 marriage record names Abe’s mother only as “Bessie.” The surname Sadler, birth year/place and death/burial details are from the Find a Grave memorial and its family group; no birth or death certificate has been reviewed.
  • The Find a Grave marker image reads “Bessie Weis,” “Died Oct. 16, 1918,” and “Age 28 years.” The image is a headstone/marker rather than a portrait. The memorial reports a 1890 birth year but no exact date.
  • Find a Grave reports that Bessie and Morris married in 1907. No marriage register or certificate for them was reviewed.
  • Abraham’s Find a Grave memorial and Morris’s Find a Grave memorial both list Samuel, Abraham, and Faye as Bessie/Morris’s children; the 1935 marriage record directly establishes only Abraham’s maternal name in the reviewed records.
  • No parent identities or siblings for Bessie were established in the sources reviewed.
