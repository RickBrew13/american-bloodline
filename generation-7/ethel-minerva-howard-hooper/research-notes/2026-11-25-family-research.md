# Ethel Minerva (Howard) Hooper — research note

**Research date:** 25 November 2026

The saved Geni read from Rick and Tom (2021) identifies Ethel Minerva Hooper, née Howard, as Marye’s mother. The Find a Grave memorial for Marye links Ethel as her mother and lists the same siblings named in Marye’s 1994 obituary (Grace, Hinda, Merle, J. T.). Ethel’s own user-contributed memorial reports 6 Apr 1881–21 Mar 1974, parents James Thomas Howard and Sarah Elizabeth “Sallie” Dennis, and spouse Will L. Hooper. Geni search results report wife of William Luther Hooper, c.1881–1974. No original census, birth or death record directly identifying Marye as Ethel’s daughter was located. **Working confidence as Marye’s mother with Will: ~70%, not documented.**

The photo attached to the memorials appears to name “Edith” and dates inconsistent with Ethel, so it is not used as a portrait or verified grave marker.
