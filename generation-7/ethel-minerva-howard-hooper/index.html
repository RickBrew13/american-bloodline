Family Relationship Notes

Rick and Tom’s saved Geni notes identify Marye’s mother as Ethel Minerva Hooper, née Howard. Marye’s Find a Grave memorial links Ethel as her mother and names the same daughters and siblings reported in Marye’s obituary. This consistency supports a probable working parent assignment for Marye (about 70%), but no primary record directly naming Ethel as Marye’s mother was located.

Ethel’s own memorial gives 6 April 1881–21 March 1974, parents James Thomas Howard and Sarah Elizabeth “Sallie” Dennis Howard, and spouse Will L. Hooper. The Geni search result instead gives birth c.1881 and the name Ethel Minerva Hooper. All those family links are user-contributed derivative evidence.

The image attached to both Ethel’s and Will’s memorials appears to read “Edith” and dates inconsistent with Ethel’s 1974 death. It is not used as her grave marker or portrait.
