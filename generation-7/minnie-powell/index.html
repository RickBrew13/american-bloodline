Family Relationship Notes

Minnie M. Lyon is Rowland Edmund Powell’s mother. The 1911 birth register names Rowland E. Powell’s mother as “Minnie Powell,” a Harrison County marriage license records Minnie M. Lyon marrying Dexter Powell on 1 July 1903, and the 1920 census lists 8-year-old Rowland as “Son” in Dexter and Minnie’s household in Salem Township, Harrison County — the direct record tying the couple to Rowland. The license names her parent Andrew Lyon; her mother Henrietta Green is named on her 1952 death record. Dexter’s middle initial is most likely G, though the license image writes “Dexter J. Powell.”

Life Story

Minnie M. Lyon married Dexter Powell in Harrison County in 1903, according to the original marriage license. In 1911, a birth register for Rowland E. Powell names a mother “Minnie Powell” and a father “D. G. Powell,” and the 1920 census lists Rowland as Dexter and Minnie’s son. Minnie M. Lyon is Rowland’s mother.
