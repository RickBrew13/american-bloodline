Life Story

The Find a Grave memorial family panel names Susan Henrietta Wethington Woodrum as the mother of Clement Herman and James A. Woodrum. Clement’s 1972 obituary calls his deceased mother “Suskennek Wethington.” This may be the same woman, but the full-name link is not explicit in that obituary.

The memorial metadata reports Susan born 3 February 1887 and died 11 December 1916. Its attached grave photo appears to read “Susan Woodrum Feb 2, 1887 Dec 11, 1905,” which is inconsistent with Clement’s 1908 birth. A transcribed index displayed on the memorial gives death 13 December 1916. I have not treated the memorial photo as belonging to this Susan or resolved the date conflict.

The attached indexed death entry names John Wethington and Roma Ellen Wethington as parents, but the identity match is uncertain because the same memorial has conflicting death materials. They are not assigned as established parents.

Clement’s obituary calls his father “Eddie Woodrum” and his mother “Suskennek Wethington”; the reported names in the memorial are probable matches, not fully proven identities.
