# Susan Henrietta Wethington Woodrum — family and source audit
**Date:** 26 November 2026

Find a Grave memorial family panels (Susan 22562151, Edward 22561883, Clement 22561887) report Susan as spouse of Edward and mother of Clement and James A. Woodrum. Clement’s 1972 obituary clipping says his deceased mother was “Suskennek Wethington.” The link to Susan Henrietta is plausible but the obituary does not spell out her full name.

The memorial metadata reports Susan as born 3 February 1887 and died 11 December 1916. Its attached marker image appears to read “Susan Woodrum Feb 2,1887 Dec 11,1905,” and a transcribed death-index entry shown on the memorial gives 13 December 1916. The conflicting 1905 inscription cannot be used to identify this person, because Clement was reportedly born in 1908. Exact date and image identity remain unresolved. The index transcription names John Wethington and Roma Ellen Wethington, but the identity match is unproved; they are not promoted to established parents.

No original birth/death/marriage/census record, cemetery register, or verified portrait was found. Direct Find a Grave image downloads were blocked; no image was copied.
