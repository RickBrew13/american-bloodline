Family Relationship Notes

Dudley and Ellen are listed as Shirley’s parents in reciprocal Find a Grave family panels. The Dudley page also names Shirley among Dudley and Ellen’s children. This supports a working assignment (~75%), but the underlying birth record was not reviewed.

The Dudley memorial transcribes details attributed to Kentucky death certificate #687: parents “Dut Wagers” and Lucy, wife Jane at death, burial at Eastern State Hospital. The memorial narrative separately identifies his parents as Dutton and Lucinda Holland and first wife Ellen as Jefferson and China Smith Baker’s daughter. These are memorial-reported, not a full certificate review; nativity and spouse sequence vary across the page.
