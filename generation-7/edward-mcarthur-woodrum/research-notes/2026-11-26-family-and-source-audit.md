# Clement Herman Woodrum — family and source audit
**Date:** 26 November 2026

## Scope
Reviewed the existing Clement and Anna profiles, existing Janice research note, live Gen VII style/index, FamilySearch’s compiled profile for Anna, the Virts family group, Find a Grave memorial family panels, and obituary transcriptions attached to memorial photos. No Google Docs or Drive originals were changed.

## Clement’s reported parents
The Find a Grave family panels for Clement (memorial 22561887), Edward McArthur “Hicks” Woodrum (22561883) and Susan Henrietta Wethington Woodrum (22562151) link Clement to Edward and Susan. The 1972 obituary clipping attached to Clement’s memorial says “son of Suskennek Wethington, deceased, and Eddie Woodrum, who survives.” This is consistent with the memorial names but does not state the parents’ full names. The identification is plausible, not proven by a reviewed original birth record. The two new Gen VII profiles preserve that distinction and link back to Clement.

## Siblings
Clement’s memorial family panel reports full brother James A. Woodrum (1913–1981), and the clipping calls Clement’s brother “Jim Woodrum.” The obituary calls him a half-brother to Mrs. Eileen Hamm, Mrs. Juanita Harrison and Mrs. Fay Sanders. Find a Grave separately links Dorothy Eileen Hamm and Anna Juanita Harrison as Edward’s later children; those are plausible matches to the first two obituary references. Fay Sanders is unidentified. Edward’s memorial panel lists siblings Mariah Jane “Mary” Woodrum Wethington (1874–1914), Mary Elizabeth Woodrum Wethington (1879–1958), Mathew Thomas Woodrum (1886–1968), James Logan Woodrum (1888–1965), and Fanny Mae Woodrum Zachary (1895–1969); these are memorial-reported, not independently proven.

## Clement and Anna’s children
The 1972 clipping names eight: Milburn J.; Bobby C.; Alta Roe Gabehart; Janice E. Campbell; Velma M. Wethington; Wilda Jean Warfield; William Clement Woodrum; Anna Charlene Warner. Anna’s 2002 obituary transcription names seven surviving her: Milburn, Bobby, Clement, Alta Gabehart, Janice Campbell, Velma Wethington and Charlene Warner. The Virts family compilation gives Milburn J., Bobby C., William Clement, Alta Roe, Janice E., Velma M., Anna Charlene and Wilda Jean. The common eight-child group is strongly supported by multiple derivative sources and the obituary transcriptions, but the expanded names and identity matches remain source-qualified.

Name cautions: the 1972 clipping uses “Wilda Jean Warfield,” while Find a Grave/Virts use Woodrum/Hogue; the relationship and married-name sequence need a bridge. Do not expand Clement’s son Clement Woodrum as William Clement without a direct record. Anna Charlene/Charlene Warner is listed as “Sharleen” in one 1950 census index. A tempting Bobby Joe Woodrum (1941–2017) is excluded because his obituary names Everett and Rena Grant Woodrum as parents. A possible Milburn Jr. match from a veterans’ burial index is not independently established.

## Anna’s siblings
The 2002 obituary directly names Anna’s sisters Urial Clements and Vatus Green. Rose Jane’s 1958 obituary transcription names children Harvey, Edith (Anna), Urial, Curtis, Bernice, Vatus, Protus and Verlin. Find a Grave family panels list Harvey Glen, Mary Urial, Bernice Arline, Protus E., Vatus M., William Herbert and James Verlin; the compiled FamilySearch sibling list also includes Ira and Curtis. Keep all source-specific forms; do not claim the lists are complete or resolve discrepancies without original records.

## Dates, residence and burial
Find a Grave and compiled profiles report Clement’s dates 22 Nov 1908–21 Jul 1972; the obituary clipping reports death in the Liberty area at 11 a.m. and burial after service at St. Bernard’s/Clementsville cemetery. Anna’s compiled profile and obituary report 1 Jun 1911–29 Jul 2002, and the obituary says funeral at St. Bernard’s Church with burial in its cemetery. The cemetery details are consistent with the memorials, but no cemetery register or original certificates were inspected. Marriage on 22 Nov 1927 in Casey County is FamilySearch/Virts reported; original marriage register not reviewed.

## Photos reviewed / excluded
- Clement memorial’s main image OCR reads a marker for “Celene Hickman” and “Anna Edward Fay” with dates unlike Clement’s; it is not his marker/portrait. The other memorial image is an obituary clipping, not a photo of Clement.
- Anna’s memorial images show a marker reading “Roni Jane” and “William B.” with dates 1896–1958 and 1897–1960; they do not match Anna and were not used.
- Edward’s memorial has an image yielding only “OCT. 11, 1884” and a two-person photo without a verifiable caption. Susan’s attached marker appears to say 1887–1905, inconsistent with her reported 1916 death and the 1908 birth of Clement. No image was used as a portrait.
- Direct image downloads from Find a Grave were blocked by Cloudflare; linked images remain hosted by their source. No picture was copied locally or misattributed.
