Life Story

Find a Grave memorial family panels list Edward as Clement Herman Woodrum’s father and link his parents as Edmond McCarthy Woodrum and Martha Ann Clements Woodrum. No original birth, marriage or death record for Edward was examined.

Clement’s 1972 obituary clipping says he was the son of “Suskennek Wethington, deceased, and Eddie Woodrum, who survives.” “Eddie” is consistent with Edward, but the obituary does not give Edward’s full name. Preserve that distinction.

The memorial reports five siblings and lists Clement and James A. as children with Susan. The 1972 obituary independently calls Clement’s brother “Jim” and names three half-siblings; “Mrs. Fay Sanders” is not identified in the memorial family panel.

The Find a Grave memorial’s spouse panels include multiple Pearl Woodrum entries, but their relationships and sequence are not sufficiently clear for attribution here. No undocumented additional spouse or child has been added.
