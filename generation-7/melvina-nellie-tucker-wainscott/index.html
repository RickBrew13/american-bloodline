Family Relationship Notes

Ray’s public FamilySearch profile (LD22-H34) names John Richard Wainscott and Melvina Nellie Tucker as his parents and lists seven siblings. Fred Lee Wainscott’s public FamilySearch profile (L6JF-JHD) names the same parents and places Ray and Fred in the same sibling group. This is consistent family-tree evidence, but both profiles are from the shared FamilySearch tree, not independent original birth records.

Ray’s Find a Grave memorial (137165284) separately lists John Richard Wainscott and Nellie Tucker Wainscott Cox as parents, but reports Nellie’s birth as 1890. Other public family profiles report her birth as 1886 or 1887. If she was born in 1886/1887, she would have been about 19–21 when Ray was born; the 1890 memorial date would make her about 16. The date discrepancy needs an original record.

Conclusion: Nellie Tucker is the best-supported candidate and a probable biological mother of Ray (working confidence ~75%), but no direct record naming her on Ray’s birth certificate or death certificate has been inspected. Do not present biological maternity as proven.

The Wainscott family has multiple same-name and multi-spouse tree entries. Ancestry lists other spouses for John; do not automatically assign every child in those tree groupings to Nellie.

Life Story

Melvina Nellie Tucker is the probable mother of Ray Henry Wainscott. Ray’s and his brother Fred’s public FamilySearch profiles name her as their mother; Ray’s Find a Grave memorial also links Nellie Tucker Wainscott Cox as a parent. Because these are compiled family panels and her reported birth year varies (1886, 1887, or 1890), maternity remains a working conclusion pending an original birth or death record.
