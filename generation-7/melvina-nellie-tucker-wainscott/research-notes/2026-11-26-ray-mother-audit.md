# Ray Henry Wainscott — mother identification audit

**Date:** 26 November 2026

## Conclusion
Melvina Nellie Tucker (also listed as Nellie Tucker Wainscott Cox) is the best-supported candidate for Ray’s mother, a **probable but unproven working assignment (~75% confidence)**. Ray’s public FamilySearch profile LD22-H34 and brother Fred’s profile L6JF-JHD both name John Richard Wainscott and Melvina Nellie Tucker as parents; each places Ray and Fred in the same sibling group. Ray’s Find a Grave memorial 137165284 independently lists John and Nellie Tucker Wainscott Cox in its parents panel. The FamilySearch profiles are from a shared compiled tree, so they are not independent primary-record proof.

## Date/identity discrepancies
Nellie’s birth year varies: 1886 on Ray and Fred’s FamilySearch public profiles, 1887 on the public Ancestry John profile, and 1890 on Ray’s Find a Grave panel. The 1886/1887 dates make her about 19–21 at Ray’s reported 1906/1907 birth, while the 1890 date makes her 16. Ray’s date also varies between 1906 (project profile/headstone) and 1907 (FamilySearch tree). Keep both conflicts explicit.

## Evidence still needed
Ray’s Ohio death certificate, certificate 03458, is the most direct available target and should identify his parents; it has not been inspected. The 1910 census is reported as Shelby, Kentucky, and the 1920 census as Gratz, Owen County, Kentucky, in the FamilySearch profile, but original schedules were not viewed. Do not call Nellie’s maternity proven until those records are reviewed.
