# Research and family audit — Richard Hood Wise

**Reviewed:** 26 November 2026

This profile was added as a Generation VII parent of Samuel P. Wise. The child profile links back to this parent. These relationship assignments retain the identity/source caveats stated below.

## Source-specific caveats

- The connection from Richard and Millie to Samuel Preston Wise is reported by multiple compiled/memorial sources, but the project’s Samuel P. Wise remains a probable match to that named son, not a proved identity. Keep that distinction on both profiles.
- Find a Grave reports Richard’s parents as William Preston Wise and Sarah Elizabeth Chaney; FamilySearch’s compiled Wise family also reports these names. No original birth record for Richard was reviewed.
- The family-group memorial lists four sons (Samuel, Wallace, Otha and Warren) and FamilySearch lists six children, adding infant Elijah and Marjorie. Record both the compilation and memorial panels as evidence, rather than inferring the memorial is an exhaustive list.
- Find a Grave lists one full sibling and seven half-siblings for Richard. Those collateral links come from family panels and were not independently investigated in this pass.
- The joint marker image appears to show both Richard and Millie. Text extraction reads the birth years as 1832 and 1838, unlike their reported 1882 and 1888 births. Dates on the marker have not been resolved; rely on the source-specific date discussion, not the noisy extraction.
