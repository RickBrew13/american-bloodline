Life Story

Corrine J. Lanier Greiner is identified as Virginia Greiner Wise’s mother in Virginia’s Find a Grave memorial. Corrine’s own memorial names Virginia and Stuart Richard Greiner (1921–1976) as her children. Virginia’s 2006 obituary independently confirms the family context by identifying Virginia as wife of Samuel P. Wise, mother of Monica and Sam “Skip,” and sister of Gladys Adams; it does not itself name Corrine.

Find a Grave reports Corrine was born 12 August 1903 in Duplin County, North Carolina, and died 21 January 1974 in Bradenton, Florida. It reports burial at Rose Hill Cemetery in Kissimmee. Her memorial narrative names her parents as Claud and Josephine Lanier and siblings Viva and Philip, and reports a 1918 marriage to Stuart R. Greiner. No original birth, marriage, death or cemetery record was inspected.

The Find a Grave narrative also describes later marriages in 1928, 1939 and 1940, but the 1928 spouse is unnamed and the sequence has not been checked against original records. Those claims are recorded as leads, not confirmed spouse assignments.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The mother–daughter relationship is supported by the Corrine and Virginia Find a Grave family panels. This is derivative evidence; no original birth record for Virginia naming her mother was located in this pass.
  • Corrine’s memorial narrative identifies her parents as Claud Lanier and Josephine Lanier, with no full surname for Josephine, and names siblings Viva and Philip. Treat the relationship and sibling names as memorial-reported until checked against records.
  • Find a Grave reports Stuart R. Greiner married Corrine in 1918, and separately mentions later marriages to an unnamed person in 1928, W. D. Magill in 1939, and Jack F. Starkey in 1940. These later relationships were not verified or attached as profile links.
  • Corrine’s own memorial lists Virginia and Stuart Richard Greiner (1921–1976) as children. Her former spouse’s memorial lists three children overall, adding Gladys Lacy Greiner Adams, whose mother was Mary Leola Tucker. Gladys is Virginia’s paternal half-sister.
  • The shared stone image is linked from both Stuart’s and Corrine’s Find a Grave pages; it is a couple marker, not an individual portrait.
