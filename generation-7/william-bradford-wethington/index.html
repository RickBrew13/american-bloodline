Life Story

Find a Grave memorial family panels report William as husband of Rose Jane Clements and father of Anna Edith and seven other children. Rose’s obituary transcription gives her marriage date as 12 November 1907 and names Anna as “Edith Woodrum” among their children.

The Find a Grave memorial reports William born 25 June 1887. The attached shared marker’s text transcription instead appears to give 25 June 1897, while its death date matches 23 June 1960. Preserve this conflict; the marker image is not treated as proof of the birth year until inspected directly.

The linked children and siblings are memorial-panel reported. Rose’s 1958 obituary independently names eight children, including Edith Woodrum, and names three brothers of her own; these source distinctions are documented in the parent folder note.

Photographs & Family Gallery

The main image attached to William’s memorial appears in text transcription to show a shared Wethington marker for “Roni Jane” and “William B.” The death dates align with Rose and William memorial reports, but the reported birth years and name transcription conflict. It may be their shared marker; it is recorded as a candidate, not an individually verified headstone or portrait.

Other memorial images include an unidentified two-person photograph and another image with conflicting stone text. No individual portrait was verified or used.
