# William Bradford Wethington — family and source audit
**Date:** 26 November 2026

## Family group
Find a Grave memorial 20962463 lists parents George Oliver Wethington (1858–1929) and Marget E. Murphy Wethington (1859–1928); spouse Rose Jane Clements Wethington (1886–1958); and siblings Lottie Wethington Hiestand (1877–1956), Noah Clement Wethington (1879–1908), Mark (1882–1938), Luke (1882–1944), M. Alice Wethington Belton (1890–1970), John Brady (1892–1954) and Sarah Jane Wethington Cox (1894–1970). These are memorial family-panel assignments, not checked against original records.

The same memorial lists children Harvey Glen (1908–1962), Anna Edith (1911–2002), Mary Urial (1913–2009), Bernice Arline (1918–1995), Protus E. (1920–1986), Vatus M. (1923–2005), William Herbert (1926–1954) and James Verlin (1928–1970). Rose’s 1958 obituary transcription independently names Harvey, Edith Woodrum, Urial Clements, Curtis, Bernice Gouty, Vatus Green, Protus and Verlin. The lists mostly correspond but preserve the obituary’s “Curtis”/“Verlin” forms without assuming exact identity matches. Anna’s existing profile links her to both parents; William and Rose’s new profiles link reciprocally to Anna and one another.

## Dates and marriage
Find a Grave reports William born 25 June 1887, died 23 June 1960, buried Saint Bernard Cemetery. The attached marker text transcription appears to read “WILLIAM B. JUNE 25, 1897 JUNE 23, 1960”; the death date agrees, but the birth year conflicts. Do not silently resolve it. Rose’s obituary transcription says she married William B. Wethington on 12 November 1907. No original register or certificate reviewed.

## Photo provenance
No individual portrait verified. Find a Grave images include a probable shared marker with names transcribed as “Roni Jane” and “William B.” and birth years that differ by ten years from the memorial reports; the matching death dates suggest it could be the couple’s stone, but the image itself is not independently inspected. Another image is an unidentified two-person photograph. Both are excluded as profile portraits.
