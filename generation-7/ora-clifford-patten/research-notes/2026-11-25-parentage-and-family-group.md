# Ora Clifford Patten — parentage and family-group research

**Research date:** 25 November 2026 (America/New_York)  
**Status:** Working Gen VII profile; proposed child relationship remains unproved.

Working assignment, not proof: Ora is listed as Harry O. Patten’s father in a public Ancestry family-history profile and is the proposed father in Harry’s Gen VI profile. This is about 70% confidence for the working pedigree, based on the connected profile’s family group and the strong Ohio/Brown County fit; no inspected birth record or childhood census image names Ora as Harry’s father. The 1950 census confirms Harry’s age, Ohio birthplace, residence, wife, and household relationships, but does not name his parents.Ora’s Find a Grave memorial reports the dates and burial shown above. That is a derivative memorial transcription of a secondary book, not a reviewed copy of the book or a civil marriage record.
