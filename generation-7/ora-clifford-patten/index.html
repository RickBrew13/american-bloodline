Family Relationship Notes

Working assignment, not proof: Ora is listed as Harry O. Patten’s father in a public Ancestry family-history profile and is the proposed father in Harry’s Gen VI profile. This is about 70% confidence for the working pedigree, based on the connected profile’s family group and the strong Ohio/Brown County fit; no inspected birth record or childhood census image names Ora as Harry’s father. The 1950 census confirms Harry’s age, Ohio birthplace, residence, wife, and household relationships, but does not name his parents.

Ora’s Find a Grave memorial reports the dates and burial shown above. Its contributor narrative cites the 1993 History and Families of Brown County, Ohio, gives a marriage date of 31 October 1909 to “Edythe Mabel Kinner Patten,” and says the couple had 11 children. That is a derivative memorial transcription of a secondary book, not a reviewed copy of the book or a civil marriage record. Edith’s separate memorial lists Harry, Nellie, and Mildred as her children; do not infer the full eleven-child list or assign every child to Ora without checking the underlying evidence.

Ora’s memorial narrative gives his parents as “Charles Plan Patten” and Sarah Ann Hancock; its Family Members section links Charles O. Patten. Treat the spelling and parent link as an unverified lead. The memorial lists Olen Ellsworth Patten as Ora’s sibling; that relationship is likewise derivative.
