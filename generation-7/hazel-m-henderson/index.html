Life Story

Hazel M. Henderson was born in Ohio about 1894. She married Charles H. Everhart. In the 1920 census the family was living in Akron, Ohio, with children Helen, Raymond, Irene, and Virgil. By 1930 they were living in Deer Park Village, Hamilton County, Ohio, with seven children: Helen, Raymond, Irene, Virgil, Harold, Dorothy, and Geneva. Her daughter Irene married Roy W. Jordan.
