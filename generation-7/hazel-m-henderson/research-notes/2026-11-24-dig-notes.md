# Hazel M. Henderson Everhart — research notes, 24 November 2026

## What was found
- Born about 1894 in Ohio (25 in January 1920, 35 in April 1930).
- Wife of Charles H. Everhart. Children Helen M., Raymond R., Irene A., Virgil, Harold, Dorothy, and Geneva.
- Her maiden name, Henderson, comes from her daughter Irene's Social Security NUMIDENT and Ohio death record, which name Irene's parents as Charles H. Everhart and Hazel M. Henderson.
- The 1920 census spells the family surname "Everhardt"; the 1930 census spells it "Everhart".

## Records
- 1920 U.S. Census, Akron Ward 4, ED 170, sheet 7B (index ARK 1:1:M6MW-XBG; image ARK 3:1:33S7-9RNF-WQL).
- 1930 U.S. Census, Deer Park Village, Hamilton County, ED 91-376, sheet 24A (index ARK 1:1:X4WC-YZ5; image ARK 3:1:33S7-9RH1-PYT).
