# Sallie Parker — dig notes, 25 November 2026

Sallie Parker is the mother of Rosa Belle White, named only on Rosa's 1951 Alabama death record (FamilySearch ARK 1:1:J6QM-K56), which also names her father Pete White. No census, birth, marriage, or death record for Sallie herself has been found.

- Sallie married Pete White on 2 December 1903 in Autauga County, Alabama. The marriage index spells the bride "Sallie Smith," which conflicts with "Sallie Parker" on their daughter's death record. Parker may be her maiden name and Smith a previous married name, or the other way around — no record settles it.
- Children named on death and marriage records: Rosa Belle (born about 1905, died 1951 Birmingham), Ell White, Buster White, Gladys Bell White.
- A daughter Annie, born July 1904, would fit as her first child, but no record names her as Pete and Sallie's daughter.
- Daughter Rosa married Cornelius Pugh on 7 September 1924 in Jefferson County, Alabama, and lived with him in Birmingham until her death in 1951.

Next records: the 1910 and 1920 censuses for a Pete White household with Sallie and daughters Annie and Rosa in Autauga or Jefferson County; the original 1903 marriage license image (to reconcile Smith vs Parker); Sallie's own birth and death records and her parents.
