Family Relationship Notes

Sallie Parker is named only as the mother of Rosa Belle White on Rosa’s 1951 Alabama death record, which also names her father Pete White. No census, birth, marriage, or death record for Sallie herself has been found.

Sallie married Pete White on 2 December 1903 in Autauga County, Alabama. The marriage index spells the bride “Sallie Smith,” which conflicts with “Sallie Parker” on their daughter’s death record; the two names have not been reconciled. Parker may be her maiden name and Smith a previous married name, or the other way around — no record settles it.

Her children Rosa Belle, Ell, Buster, and Gladys Bell are named on death and marriage records. A daughter Annie, born July 1904, would fit as her first child, but no record names her as Pete and Sallie’s daughter, so she is not listed as their child.

Her daughter Rosa married Cornelius Pugh on 7 September 1924 in Jefferson County, Alabama, and lived with him in Birmingham until her death in 1951.

Life Story

Sallie Parker is known only from her daughter’s records. She married Pete White in Autauga County in 1903, and their daughter Rosa Belle was born about 1905. Rosa married Cornelius Pugh in Birmingham in 1924, and when she died there in 1951 her death record named Sallie Parker as her mother and Pete White as her father. Whether her maiden name was Parker or Smith is unresolved — the marriage index says Smith. Nothing else has been found for Sallie herself.
