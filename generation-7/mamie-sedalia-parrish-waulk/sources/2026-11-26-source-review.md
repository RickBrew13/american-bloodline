# Source review — Mamie Sedalia (Parrish) Waulk

**Accessed:** 26 November 2026

## Reviewed sources

- [Mamie Sedalia Parrish Waulk, Find a Grave memorial 14629127](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/14629127/mamie_sedalia-waulk) — Reports birth/death/burial and reproduces an obituary naming her parents, husband, children, siblings and church.
- [Mamie Waulk obituary clipping preserved on Find a Grave memorial 14629127](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/14629127/mamie_sedalia-waulk/photo) — The clipping identifies her parents as George Parrish and Julia Humphries Parrish; lists her husband and surviving children, siblings, four predeceased children, church and burial. The newspaper/date heading is not visible in the saved clipping.
- [Benjamin Harrison Waulk, Find a Grave memorial 14627457](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/14627457/benjamin_harrison-waulk) — Family panel links Mamie as spouse (1921); Benjamin’s obituary clipping confirms marriage to Mamie Parrish.
- [David Paul “Paul” Waulk, Find a Grave memorial 35374642](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/35374642/david_paul-waulk) — Paul’s obituary identifies Mamie (Parrish) Waulk and Benjamin as his parents.
- [Mary Francis Waulk Davis, Find a Grave memorial 18648963](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/18648963/mary_francis-davis) — Family panel lists Benjamin and Mamie as parents and David Paul as a sibling.

## Limits

- No original birth certificates, marriage certificates, death certificates, or original census images were retrieved in this pass.
- Preserve exact names and date discrepancies as cited; do not treat Find a Grave family panels or compiled family trees as primary evidence.
- Photo files in this folder are credited in the companion photo-provenance note.
