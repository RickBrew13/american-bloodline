# Research note — Mamie Sedalia (Parrish) Waulk

**Updated:** 26 November 2026

## Working conclusion

Mamie Sedalia (Parrish) Waulk is linked as a Gen VII parent of David Paul “Paul” Waulk in the existing Gen VI profile. The link is corroborated by the child’s Find a Grave family panel and obituary text, plus the parents’ linked memorials and a newspaper obituary clipping. The profiles use source-attributed wording and do not claim original certificates were reviewed.

## Biographical facts and relationships

- **Birth:** 6 September 1904 · Kentucky, USA
- **Death:** 19 August 1973 · Hamilton, Butler County, Ohio (obituary/memorial report)
- **Burial:** Cynthiana Cemetery, Cynthiana, Pike County, Ohio
- **Parents:** George Parrish; Julia Humphries Parrish (also memorial-listed as Julia Frances Humphries Ingles)
- **Spouse(s):** Benjamin Harrison Waulk (7 January 1897–23 October 1967) — married 1921
- **Children:** Mildred Louise Waulk Rhodes (1922–2002); Mary Francis Waulk Davis (1923–1994); Gabriel Harold “Harold” Waulk (1925–2002); David Paul “Paul” Waulk (1927–2009); Floyd Russell Waulk (1928–2010); Michael E. Waulk (1945–1945)
- **Siblings:** Iva Parrish Evans; Phyllis Ingles Masters; Boone L. Parrish; James M. Ingles; and Edward “Eddie” Ingles are named as siblings in Mamie’s obituary. Their separate identities are not further researched in this pass.

## Findings, caveats and open questions

- Mamie’s 1973 obituary text calls her 69, but the memorial gives 6 September 1904–19 August 1973, making her 68 on that date. Keep the discrepancy explicit; dates are not changed to fit the age statement.
- The obituary names George Parrish and Julia Humphries Parrish as her parents. The memorial parent panel lists Julia Frances Humphries Ingles but not George. Preserve the obituary’s exact wording and note the memorial name variation; no record connecting Julia’s later Ingles surname to the “Julia Humphries Parrish” form was reviewed.
- The obituary identifies five siblings by married/name forms as transcribed above, and four predeceased children. It does not name those four in the text. Do not assume that the infant Michael E. Waulk is the only one or identify the other three without records.
- The archived portrait is an identified Find a Grave memorial photo uploaded by Roger Waulk; the marker and clipping are separate images and captioned separately.
