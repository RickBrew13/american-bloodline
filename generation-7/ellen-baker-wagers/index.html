Family Relationship Notes

Shirley’s 7 August 1919 marriage record names Ellen Wagers as his mother, confirming the memorial panels. Ellen’s memorial identifies Dudley as spouse and Mary Belle, Conrad, Shirley and Anderson as children.

The 1880 census lists Ellen, 5 months old, as Jefferson and China Baker’s daughter (Pigeonroost, Clay County, Kentucky), confirming the memorial’s claim about her parents. The “Smith” maiden name for China appears in no record found; it rests only on the memorial narrative.

Burial place conflicts within the memorial notes (East Colony/Mount Moriah versus death-certificate Pine Hill); do not treat the burial site as resolved.

Life Timeline

About 1879–1880 · 1880 census lists Ellen as 5 months old in Jefferson and China Baker’s household, Pigeonroost, Clay County, Kentucky. Find a Grave reports birth 27 January 1868; the census year is used here.

27 May 1897 · Married D. W. Wagers, Clay County, Kentucky (licence 24 May 1897), at J. W. Baker’s house; the record names no parents for Ellen.

1898–1908 · Ellen and Dudley’s memorial lists four children, including Shirley.

24 May 1916 · Find a Grave reports death in Laurel County, Kentucky; burial discrepancy unresolved.

Life Story

Ellen Baker Wagers is named as Shirley A. Wagers’s mother on his 7 August 1919 marriage record. Her parents Jefferson and China Baker are confirmed by the 1880 census, which lists Ellen as their 5-month-old daughter.

Research Notes and Open Questions

Ellen’s parents Jefferson and China Baker are confirmed by the 1880 census, which lists Ellen as their daughter. Gen VIII profiles for Jefferson and China have not been built.

Ellen’s siblings from the 1880 and 1900 censuses are John, Elizabeth, Cirmentha, Sarah, Martha, Andrew, Lizzie, Ida and Wilson Baker; the 1900 five are listed when Jefferson’s wife is recorded as Corina, not China, so whether they are full siblings is not settled.

Her memorial lists one photo; image not directly available for visual checking and not used on profile.
