Life Story

Jackson A. McKinney was born in Beckley, West Virginia, in 1924, the eldest son of Dallas and La Verne McKinney. His obituary reports U.S. Coast Guard service from 1943 to 1945, including overseas duty. He married Pauline M. McKinney (Northcott) in 1943; the family identifies Pauline as “Polly.” The Bay City Times obituary names their children Michael, Joan Lindland and Patrick, who died before his father, and records Jackson’s death on 6 April 2014 at age 90 after surgical complications at Cleveland Clinic. His World War II draft card separately records his exact birth date as 21 January 1924 and Beckley birthplace; registration is not proof of service.
