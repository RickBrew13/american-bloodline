Life Story

Elizabeth Engle was born in Kentucky on 26 November 1817 SUPPORTED. She married William Helton in Knox County in May 1839; Jacob Engle, most likely her father, signed the bond DOCUMENTED.

Left a widow in 1863 with a houseful of children, Elizabeth kept the farm on Indian Creek and lived beside her son Asberry for the rest of her life DOCUMENTED. She died on 18 August 1894 and is buried in the family cemetery there SUPPORTED.
