# Sources for Thomas C. Combs

- [Find a Grave, Thomas C Combs, memorial 18113050](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/18113050/thomas_c-combs) — Contributor-maintained memorial gives 26 December 1843–11 March 1922, Raney Cemetery; lists parents James H. “Joe” Combs and Lucy Huddleston, spouse Elizabeth Edward Edwards Combs, and daughter Maud Allen Combs Campbell. Grave photo by Rochelle Riordan, added 24 June 2007.
- [FamilySearch, Thomas C Combs, profile LVYV-RN5](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/LVYV-RN5/thomas-c-combs-1843-1922) — Published profile reports 26 December 1843–11 March 1922, Casey County, parents James Henry Combs and Lucy A. Huddleston, and marriage to Elizabeth Williams on 13 December 1885. Compiled profile; visible source cards include 1910 census and a Kentucky county marriage index.
- [FamilySearch, Maude Allen Combs, profile LJJX-TQM](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/LJJX-TQM/) — Names Thomas C. Combs as Maude’s father and Elizabeth Williams as her mother; compiled-profile evidence.
- [Combs &c. Families of Casey County, Kentucky](https://combs-families.org/combs/records/ky/casey/) — Page transcribes the 1885 Thomas C. Combs–Elizabeth Edwards marriage and excerpted family narrative on Thomas and children. Narrative gives an 1846 birth and 13 March 1922 death, conflicting with marker/Find a Grave dates; secondary compiled family history.
- [FamilySearch Indiana marriage index, Thomas Combs and Elizabeth Williams, record VZQT-B1Z](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VZQT-B1Z) — Index reports marriage license dated 24 May 1870, Jackson Township, Jackson County, Indiana, page 326. Identity link to the Casey County couple is unresolved.
- [WikiTree profile Combs-5889, citing 1870 census record MX75-4BV and 1885 Kentucky marriage record FWTW-3YW](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Combs-5889) — Contributor-maintained page reproduces a 1870 census index for Thomas Combs, age 23, born Tennessee, Lee County, Kentucky, and a Kentucky marriage-index citation. It is a derivative reference; the underlying images were not examined.
