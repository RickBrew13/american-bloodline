Family Relationship Notes

The 5 April 1950 census schedule for Pike Township, Brown County, Ohio (ED 8-26, pages 6–7) lists Lola G. Patton, age 36, Ohio-born, as the wife of Harry O. Patton. Page 6 lists David N. Patton, 8, and Carolyn J. Patton, 5, as children; page 7 continues the household and lists Daniel G. Patton, 3, as a son. Ronald C. Sparks, 14, is explicitly recorded as Harry’s stepson; the schedule does not establish whether Lola was his mother. The original sheets were checked directly.

Daniel G. Patton is a strong, but not conclusive, match to Daniel “Gus” Patten (died 15 June 2006, age 59). The obituary names a late brother Dave Patten and brother Ronnie Sparks, names that correspond to David N. Patton and Ronald C. Sparks in the 1950 household. It also names sister Janey Stormer, not identified in the 1950 household; her relationship to Harry or Lola is unresolved. The Patton/Patten difference has no direct identity-document bridge.

The 1950 record reports Lola’s birth year as 1914, birthplace Ohio, and her parents’ birthplaces as USA, but does not name her parents. FamilySearch’s “similar records” suggestion lists Lola G. Beach in NUMIDENT and “Lola Genieve Beach” in Ohio county-marriage and stillbirth indexes. These are unverified same-person leads only; no marriage, birth, or death record has been inspected to establish Beach as Lola’s maiden name. No evidence yet identifies Lola’s parents or siblings, and her death/burial remain unknown.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Lola G. Patton is documented in the 1950 census as age 36, born in Ohio; the record gives parental birthplaces as USA but no names. No reliable record yet identifies her parents or siblings. Targeted 1930/1940 FamilySearch searches under Patton in the estimated birth-year range returned no results; a likely prior surname has not been proven.
  • FamilySearch’s similar-record suggestions list Lola G. Beach in two NUMIDENT records and “Lola Genieve Beach” in Ohio county-marriage and stillbirth indexes. These are unverified leads, not proof that Lola’s maiden name was Beach. The cited records and identity bridge still need review.
  • The 1950 census lists David N., Carolyn J., and Daniel G. Patton as children in Harry and Lola’s household. Daniel G. Patton is likely Daniel “Gus” Patten based on age and obituary sibling names; the surname spelling/identity bridge remains unproved.
  • Ronald C. Sparks is recorded as Harry’s stepson, not a child of Harry and Lola together; whether Lola was his mother is unresolved. Daniel’s obituary also names Janey Stormer, whose relationship to this couple is unknown.
  • No verified portrait, family photograph, or gravestone has been located in the current profile folder. The census sheets are records, not portraits.
  • A separate 1950 FamilySearch census record shows a New York-born Harry O. Patton, age 42, with wife Katherine and daughter Pamela. He is a distinct same-name man from the Ohio Harry (age 37, Ohio-born); do not assign Pamela to Lola and Harry.
