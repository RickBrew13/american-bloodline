Family Relationship Notes

The 5 April 1950 census schedule for Pike Township, Brown County, Ohio (ED 8-26, pages 6–7) lists Lola G. Patton, age 36, Ohio-born, as the wife of Harry O. Patton. Page 6 lists David N. Patton, 8, and Carolyn J. Patton, 5, as children; page 7 continues the household and lists Daniel G. Patton, 3, as a son. Ronald C. Sparks, 14, is explicitly recorded as Harry’s stepson; the schedule does not establish whether Lola was his mother. The original sheets were checked directly.

Daniel G. Patton is a strong, but not conclusive, match to Daniel “Gus” Patten (died 15 June 2006, age 59). The obituary names a late brother Dave Patten and brother Ronnie Sparks, names that correspond to David N. Patton and Ronald C. Sparks in the 1950 household. It also names sister Janey Stormer, not identified in the 1950 household; her relationship to Harry or Lola is unresolved. The Patton/Patten difference has no direct identity-document bridge.

The 1950 record reports Lola’s birth year as 1914, birthplace Ohio, and her parents’ birthplaces as USA, but does not name her parents. A secondary compiled entry for Ronald C. Sparks (also “Ronald Cleve Sparks”), attributed to his Social Security application, identifies his mother as Lola G. Beach and father as Cleves Sparks; it gives his birth as 6 March 1936 in Williamsburg, Clermont County, Ohio. The shared name and the 1950 census relationship of Ronald as Harry’s stepson strongly support Lola G. Beach as this Lola’s earlier name and Ronald as her son. This is a working identity, not yet documented by an inspected original SS-5, birth record, or marriage record. The marriage relationship, if any, between Lola and Cleves is not established. No evidence yet identifies Lola’s own parents or siblings, and her death/burial remain unknown.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The strongest current parent candidate for Lola G. Patton is the Ohio-born **Lola G. Beach** in a public Ancestry family-history profile. It gives her birth as 5 February 1914 in Ohio and death as 18 December 1986 in Cincinnati, and names her parents as **John William Beach and Bertha Caroline Swing**. Separate linked profiles describe John as born 6 February 1886 in Tate Township, Clermont County, and Bertha as born 26 June 1890 in Tate Township. Their profiles place their later deaths in Brown County, where Harry and Lola’s 1950 household was enumerated. The dates, geography, Ronald Sparks clue, and family links make this Lola a strong match to the 1950 wife; the cited 1920/1930/1940 census images were not inspected because the underlying Ancestry record pages are subscription-gated. The connected public profiles are not independent sources. **Working assignment: John William Beach and Bertha Caroline Swing, about 70% confidence; not yet established by an inspected birth record or childhood census image.**
  • The 1950 census step-son relationship and the Social Security-application derivative for Ronald make Lola G. Beach a strongly plausible earlier name for this Lola, but the original application has not been inspected. The public Ancestry profiles then name John William Beach and Bertha Caroline Swing as her parents; record links are listed in Sources and Evidence. Treat both as strong working candidates (~70%), not proven until a birth record or childhood census image is examined.
  • The 1950 census lists David N., Carolyn J., and Daniel G. Patton as children in Harry and Lola’s household. Daniel G. Patton is likely Daniel “Gus” Patten based on age and obituary sibling names; the surname spelling/identity bridge remains unproved.
  • Ronald C. Sparks is recorded as Harry’s stepson, not a child of Harry and Lola together; whether Lola was his mother is unresolved. Daniel’s obituary also names Janey Stormer, whose relationship to this couple is unknown.
  • No verified portrait, family photograph, or gravestone has been located in the current profile folder. The census sheets are records, not portraits.
  • A separate 1950 FamilySearch census record shows a New York-born Harry O. Patton, age 42, with wife Katherine and daughter Pamela. He is a distinct same-name man from the Ohio Harry (age 37, Ohio-born); do not assign Pamela to Lola and Harry.
