Life Story

Roland Hurt was born about 1801 in Patrick County, Virginia — the year rests on the 1850 census, which lists him at 49, while the 1860 census lists him at 55, pointing to about 1805 instead; no birth record exists, since Virginia recorded no civil births before 1853. He married Elizabeth Lawson on 3 March 1825 in Patrick County, and the censuses name eight children born to them between about 1829 and 1847. He was a laborer in 1850 and, after moving to Beckley about 1855, a tailor on North Kanawha Street. No portrait of him has been found anywhere, and no stone.

The household is the clearest record of the family. In 1850 in Patrick County the house held Roland, 49, Elizabeth, 43, eight children from Anna, 21, down to Surinda, 3, and Dicy Lawson, 68, an older Lawson woman whose place in the family is stated in no record. Elizabeth died in December 1857, and in June 1860 Roland was still head of the house at Beckley, a widower of 55, with four children at home — Nancy, 21, Ira, 19, Elizabeth, 15, and Dicy, 13. No record after that June has been found, and the year 1860 is carried as his death year on the trees alone.

His parentage is the open question of this family, and the page carries it honestly. No record read names Roland as the son of Moses Hurt outright. What points that way is a web, not a single paper: a deed of 1835 in which James Lawson Jr. and others sold land to Rowland Hurt, calling him James’s brother-in-law; a marriage register of 1868 naming a John Hurt’s parents “Moses & Mary Hurt” — John Mann Hurt, of Roland’s own generation in Raleigh County; and a chronology with no competing father. Against it stands one instrument: the November 1830 power of attorney, in which Moses Hurt’s named heirs appointed Rowland Hurt their attorney and did not list him among the heirs. A son acting as his siblings’ attorney is an ordinary arrangement, and the deed does not say he is not a son — but the wording is carried here, and a WikiTree manager’s note grades the son link “refuted at 90–95%”. That strength is not sustained by the records read, and the link stands at probable. A second Moses Hurt, born 1793 in Virginia and settled in Todd County, Kentucky, is a different man — his will, proved in February 1863, names only John J. Hurt, Nancy Pace, and Franklin A. Hurt, and no child born in Patrick County.

One more record touches the family without settling it. A Confederate service record names “Ira D. Hurt,” Private, 36th Virginia Infantry, enlisted 3 June 1861 at Raleigh County, born 1839 — the birth year of Roland’s son Ira, but the middle initial of John Mann Hurt’s son Ira, born 1834. Two Iras lived in Raleigh County, the service record names no father, and the 16-page file behind it has not been read, so the service is stated here with the father link at unproved. His daughter Nancy Estelle carries the line.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Grades: DOCUMENTED means an original record read directly says it; SUPPORTED means several records point to it; FAMILY ACCOUNT means the family’s own telling; UNPROVED means a lead, not proved. Every fact on this page carries its grade.
  • Birth year, open: the 1850 census age 49 points to about 1801; the 1860 census age 55 points to about 1805. No birth record exists. Bedford County, given on one tree, is unproved and is not used here. A compiled 1803 is unproved. WikiTree’s duplicate profile Hurt-1728 gives “about 1815” with no source — contradicted by the 1850 census age and by a marriage in 1825, and discarded.
  • Marriage date, open: 3 March 1825 is the marriage index and the trees; 15 February 1825 (from the research notes) is most likely the bond date. The Patrick County marriage register has not been read; its first section records bond date, security, and witnesses, and its second records marriage date and minister.
  • Death, open: about 1860 rests on the trees alone; no death record has been found in the West Virginia death register, all years. He was alive 7 June 1860 at Beckley. The 1860 census narrows the death to after 7 June 1860, and no further.
  • Burial, unknown: Find a Grave memorial 199934602 names Blue Ridge Memorial Gardens, Prosperity, with no stone photo, no bio text, and no source. Blue Ridge Memorial Gardens is a modern cemetery; a stone for a man who died in 1860 would be a later marker at best. The burial is not stated here.
  • Parents, PROBABLE — the dispute, carried in full: the dig grades Moses Hurt × Mary Parmer PROBABLE, and this page follows the dig. For it: the 1835 deed calling Rowland Hurt the brother-in-law of James Lawson Jr.; the 1868 marriage register naming a John Hurt’s parents “Moses & Mary Hurt”; a coherent chronology; no competing father. Against it: the November 1830 power of attorney names Rowland Hurt as ATTORNEY for Moses’s named heirs and does not list him among the heirs — absence of naming in one instrument, not a denial. WikiTree’s duplicate profile Hurt-1728 carries a 2 October 2026 manager note grading the son link “refuted at 90–95%”; that strength is not sustained by the records read. The link reopens only if a record names another father. Tricia’s Ancestry link is refuted as stated: her Moses (born 1793, Todd County, Kentucky) × Seluda Mason Shelton (married 1817) would have been seven and two years old at Roland’s birth, and married seventeen years after he was born; the Todd Moses’s will names only John J. Hurt, Nancy Pace, and Franklin A. Hurt. The two Moseses are two different men and are never merged here.
  • The Moses duplicates: FamilySearch carries three Moses Hurts who are never merged here — LDHQ-Q6M (1768–before 1815, Patrick, the folder’s Moses); PFMP-7WD (1768–9 February 1863, Stuart, Patrick), whose only attached child is John R Hurt 1800–1886; and the Todd County Moses, LHKR-9PH (born 1793, married Elizabeth Allen September 1856).   • Moses’s father, UNPROVED: the 1830 power of attorney calls Moses “one of the children and heirs” of Joseph Hurt of Wayne County, Kentucky. WikiTree Hurt-3016 makes Moses the son of Joseph Hurt Sr. × Mary Eliza (Wilson) Hurt, but its own biography says no evidence has been found yet for that link.
