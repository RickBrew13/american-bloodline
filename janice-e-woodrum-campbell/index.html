Life Story

Janice E. Woodrum is named as the mother of Rhonda Sue Campbell on Rhonda’s October 1980 marriage license in Hamilton County, Ohio. Because Rhonda was a minor, Janice, signing as Janice E. Campbell, and her husband Samuel G. Campbell gave written consent on 17 October 1980. The family then lived in Cincinnati.

Research on her Kentucky family points to Clement Herman Woodrum and Anna Edith Wethington as her parents. FamilySearch lists a Janice Woodrum among their children, and the 1950 census index lists Janice in Casey County as daughter of Herman and Edith Woodrum. The original census image and Janice’s birth record have not been reviewed, so the link is SUPPORTED rather than DOCUMENTED.
