Life Story

Janice E. Woodrum married Samuel Eugene Campbell on 1 December 1956 at Clementsville, Casey County, Kentucky. Their original marriage record names her parents as Herman and Edith Woodrum. The 1980 Hamilton County marriage abstract and parental-consent form directly identify Janice as Rhonda Sue Campbell’s mother and Samuel as Rhonda’s father; both signed consent on 17 October 1980. Anna “Edith” Woodrum’s 2002 obituary names Janice Campbell of Cincinnati as one of her daughters, strongly supporting the identification of Edith as Janice’s mother.

The 1 December 1956 Kentucky marriage record for Janice Elaine Woodrum and Samuel Campbell names her parents as Herman and Edith Woodrum. Anna “Edith” Woodrum’s 2002 obituary independently names Janice Campbell among her daughters. Compiled family profiles identify Anna as the wife of Clement Herman Woodrum, making the parental identification strong; the original census and Janice’s birth certificate have not been inspected. The 1950 census index reports Janice born about 1938, while a compiled Kentucky birth index gives 26 November 1937. The marriage record calls her 19, consistent with 1937, but the exact birth date remains unproved.
