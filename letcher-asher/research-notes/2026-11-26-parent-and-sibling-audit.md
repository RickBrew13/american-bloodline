# Letcher Asher — parent and sibling evidence audit

Reviewed 26 November 2026. The existing project parent assignment is preserved: Letcher is listed as child of Blevins Asher and Ruth (Muncy) Asher. This update adds reciprocal profile links and sibling evidence; it does not reopen the assignment.

- 1850 index: Blevens Asher (26), Rutha Asher (20), Anersteid (3), Tanner (2), Robert (5 months), Clay County. The index does not define each relationship.
- 1860 index: Bleve Asher (38), Ruth (28), Josiah (7), Hetta (6), Nancy (4), Dill (2), plus other co-residents not identified as children by the index.
- 1870 index: Blivius/Blevins Asher (47), Ruthy (also indexed as Kathy, 40), Josiah (18), Hilley (16), Nancy (14), Dilian (10), Hilsey (5), and Litcher (3). This is the clearest indexed sibling cluster.
- 1880 index: Blevins (59), wife “Brittny” (53), Dillion (21), Kelts (16), Andrew (13), Mary Jane (11), Ivanna (7). Ruth is reported deceased by the Muncy database by 1870; don't identify Brittny as Ruth without evidence. Andrew is not assumed to be Letcher.
- Muncy Family Database and WikiTree include additional children/variants; ages and names do not align perfectly across 1850/1860/1870. Candidate siblings are listed with uncertainty in the public profile.
