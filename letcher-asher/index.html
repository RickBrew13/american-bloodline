Family Relationship Notes

Russell W. Asher’s Find a Grave memorial names “Letcher & Elizone Asher” as his parents, and the family account identifies this same couple. This supports recording Elizone as Russell’s mother, but does not independently establish a marriage record between Letcher and Elizone.

A separate FamilySearch public-tree group reports Letcher’s 1892 marriage to Agatha Hubbard and lists children. The available material does not establish whether Agatha and Elizone were the same person. Dillion Asher’s reported November 1898 birth may overlap Russell’s reported 1898–1900 dates, so they should not be treated as definitely separate people.

Compiled sources disagree about Letcher’s parents: one WikiTree profile gives Blevins Asher and Ruth Muncy, while a FamilySearch public-tree profile gives David Asher and Nancy Sizemore. Census indexes for “Litcher” and “Andrew” in Blevins Asher’s household are leads, but the original images have not yet been reviewed.

Life Story

Current evidence points to Letcher Asher as the father named on Russell W. Asher’s Find a Grave memorial. Rick confirms Letcher and Elizone Asher as Russell’s parents in the family account. A separate FamilySearch public-tree profile gives Letcher a 1868 Kentucky birth, a 1910 death, and an 1892 marriage to Agatha Hubbard. It names six children with Agatha; however, Russell is not named in that group, and the tree does not show whether Agatha and Elizone are the same person. The evidence supports a paternal Asher cluster but does not yet resolve the women’s identities or whether Dillion (b. Nov. 1898) and Russell (marker 1898; memorial 1899; 1950 index about 1900) are distinct men.

FamilySearch’s tree further cites the 1880 census for a “Litcher Asher” in the household of Blevins Asher in Leslie County; its same profile identifies parents as David Asher and Nancy Sizemore, while WikiTree gives Blevins Asher and Ruth Muncy. This parentage conflict remains open. This page records the family confirmation and leads without overstating the underlying records.
