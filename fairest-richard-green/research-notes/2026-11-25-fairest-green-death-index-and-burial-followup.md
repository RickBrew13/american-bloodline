## Death-index lead found
A derivative SSDI-style index at LocateAncestors lists:
- Name: Fairest R. Green
- Birth: **27 November 1928**
- Death: **15 February 2000**
- Last residence: **Cherry Grove, Hamilton County, Ohio 45255**

Source: https://www.locateancestors.com/fairest-green/

This is a strong identity match, not an unrelated namesake:
- The 1946 draft registration card gives 27 November 1928, Barbourville, Kentucky, and names father James O. Green as contact.
- The Social Security application index also gives 27 November 1928, Barbourville, and parents James O. Green and Martha E. Helton.
- The 1930 census transcription lists “Pharis,” age 2, in James and Ella Green's Knox County household.
- Shirley's 2015 obituary identifies the late Fairest Green as her husband.
- The Find a Grave memorial 171317730 is already cited in the profile and reports birth 27 November 1928, death **24 February 2000**, burial Rest Haven Memorial Park, Evendale, Ohio.

The exact full name and matching 1928 birth date make the LocateAncestors death-index entry likely to refer to this Fairest. However, it is a derivative index, not the original death certificate. The 15 February 2000 date conflicts with Find a Grave's 24 February 2000. The Cincinnati Enquirer notice was published 27 February 2000 (p. 38), but its full text was not retrieved and the publication date does not establish the death date. Preserve both dates until the Ohio death certificate or full notice is inspected.
