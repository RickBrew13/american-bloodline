Life Story

Fairest grew up in Kenton County and was a senior at Simon Kenton High School in Independence, Kentucky, in the class of 1948. In November 1948 he married his classmate Carrie Easton; that marriage had ended before his marriage to Shirley Ann Taylor.

Fairest Richard Green is named as Maggie Suzanne Green's father on her 1995 Hamilton County marriage license. His wife Shirley's 2015 obituary calls him "the late Fairest Green" and names their seven children.
