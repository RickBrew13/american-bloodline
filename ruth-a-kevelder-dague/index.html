Life Story

Ruth A. Kevelder was born on 15 March 1914, the daughter of Anthony and Elizabeth (Schipper) Kevelder DOCUMENTED. Her father, a St. Henry restaurant keeper, died when she was four, and her widowed mother later moved the family to Columbus DOCUMENTED. She married Woodrow W. Dague in Franklin County, Ohio, on 20 June 1936, and in August 1937 she gave birth to their sons William and Robert on the same day SUPPORTED. She died on 8 October 1988 SUPPORTED.
