Life Story

Ruth A. Kevelder was born on 15 March 1914, the daughter of Anthony and Elizabeth (Schipper) Kevelder . Her father, a St. Henry restaurant keeper, died when she was four, and her widowed mother later moved the family to Columbus . She married Woodrow W. Dague in Franklin County, Ohio, on 20 June 1936, and in August 1937 she gave birth to their sons William and Robert on the same day . She died on 8 October 1988 .
