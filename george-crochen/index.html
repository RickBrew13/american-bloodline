Family Relationship Notes

The 1870, 1880, 1900, and 1910 census households identify George with Hannah and children. Albert is listed as George’s son in the 1900 census, and Albert’s 1962 death index names George as his father. Emanuel’s 1937 death record names George Crochon and Hannah Canion as his parents. The census and index evidence supports the eight children shown; Rawil, age 13 in the 1900 household, is not included because the record does not state his relationship.

Lucinda in the 1870 census and Anda in the 1880 census may be one daughter under different name forms and are not counted separately. Daisy Cheatham appears in no census and is not assigned as a child. The family surname appears as Crocheron, Cracheron, Crochen, Croshion, and Crochon across records.

George’s own parents, siblings, exact death date, and burial are unknown. He was last recorded alive in 1910; Hannah’s 1932 death record describes her as widowed. The 1910 census labels Albert’s relationship inconsistently in the index and original image; the 1900 census and Albert’s death index support George’s parentage.

Life Story

George Crochen lived in Hayneville, Lowndes County, Alabama, and married Hannah about 1865. The censuses place the couple in Hayneville in 1870, in Gordonville in 1880, and back in Hayneville in 1900 and 1910, with a large family: at least eight children are named across the four censuses, including their son Albert, born about 1888.

George's parentage of Albert rests on two records that agree: the 1900 census lists Albert, 12, as his son, and Albert's 1962 death index names George Crochen as his father. His son Emanuel's 1937 death record names George and Hannah as parents. No record names George's own parents, and no death record for George has been found; he was last recorded alive in the 1910 census and was gone by the time Hannah died a widow in 1932. The family name is spelled Crocheron, Cracheron, Crochen, Croshion and Crochon across the records.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The family name is spelled Crocheron, Cracheron, Crochen, Croshion and Crochon across the records; the profile uses Crochen, the spelling on their son Albert's records.
  • The 1910 census: the FamilySearch index labels Albert "Son" and Callie "Wife," while a reading of the original image gives Albert as son-in-law and Callie as George and Hannah's daughter. The 1900 census and Albert's 1962 death index both name Albert as George's son, so the parentage stands; the 1910 labels remain an unresolved conflict — see Albert Crochen's profile.
  • Rawil, 13, appears in George's 1900 household; the record does not state his relationship and he appears in no other census, so he is not listed as a child here.
  • Lucinda (1870) and Anda (1880) may be one daughter under two name forms — not listed as separate children. Daisy Cheatham appears in no census — not listed as a child.
  • Birth year: April 1840 in the 1900 census; ages 25 in 1870, 40 in 1880 and 66 in 1910 give 1840 to 1845. About 1840 is the working year.
  • Death: no death record has been found. Last recorded alive in the 1910 census; his wife was widowed when she died in 1932.
  • George's own parents are not named in any record found.
