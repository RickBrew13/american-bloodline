Family Relationship Notes

The Cincinnati Health Department birth-register image attached to John’s Find a Grave memorial is transcribed as naming John Adam and Sophia Bischoff as his parents and lists cabinet maker as an occupation. The HCGS abbreviated church-marriage index lists “John A. Kohsmann” and Sophia Bischoff at St. Louis Catholic Church in 1879. This is a strong candidate for his parents’ marriage, but the surname spelling differs from Cossmann and the original church register has not been inspected. The image caption’s archival credit is truncated, and the birth-register image has not been independently checked against the original record. A public Ancestry profile also gives his vital dates and family relationships.

Stanley Peter’s Find a Grave memorial names John Adam Cossmann as his father and Henrietta Stella Bost/Cossmann as his mother. It lists Carl J. Cossman among Stanley’s siblings; Carl’s public Ancestry profile names John Adam and Henrietta as his parents. These compiled sources support the two children listed, but original birth records or census images establishing their parentage have not been reviewed.

The sources use Cossman and Cossmann spellings. Both are retained where they appear in source descriptions; no claim is made that every same-name Cossman belongs to this family.

Unresolved household and child leads

Cincinnati directories list “Cossmann John, coffee” at 2123 Loth in 1922–1928. The 1924 directory also lists Theresa, a saleslady, at the same address. In 1929–1933, directories list John at 2223 Loth with Harold G.; Carl J. is also listed in the same directory sequence, but at a separate address by 1930–1931. The 1942 voter register lists John Cossman at 2223 Loth. These records establish a recurring household/address cluster, not the exact relationships.

A compiled Geneanet tree identifies Theresa M. Cossman (born 1907) and Carl J. Cossman (born 1908) as children of John Adam and Henrietta Stella Bost. The 1924 directory supports Theresa's presence in John's household, and the 1929–1933 directories are consistent with Carl as an adult son living separately. Harold G. Cossmann appears at John's address in 1929–1933 and later in city directories and 1943–1944 deed indexes with wife Mary Helen; he may be another son, but no birth or census record has yet proved the relationship. Theresa and Harold are therefore retained as unresolved leads and are not added to the confirmed children card.

Full directory sequence and deed-index citations are in the research note 2026-11-25-john-cossmann-household-and-children-directory-audit.md.
