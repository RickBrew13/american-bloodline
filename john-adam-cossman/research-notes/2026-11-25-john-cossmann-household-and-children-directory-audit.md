# John Adam Cossman/Cossmann — Cincinnati directory and collateral-family audit
**Date:** 25 November 2026

## Purpose
Investigate the reported children and household of John Adam Cossman (7 January 1884–25 August 1958), father of Stanley Peter Cossman. The existing John profile names Stanley Peter and Carl J. as children, but the existing profile's own notes acknowledge that original birth/census images have not been reviewed. The directories below provide additional household leads, not proof of parentage.

## Cincinnati directory sequence

1. **1922, 1923, 1924, 1925, 1926–27 and 1927–28:** Cincinnati directories repeatedly list **“Cossmann John, coffee, h 2123 Loth.”** The 1924 directory also lists **“Theresa, saleslady 1110 Main, h 2123 Loth”** directly under John's entry. The shared residence makes Theresa a plausible daughter/household member, but the directory does not state the relationship.
   - 1922: https://apps.cincinnatilibrary.org/citydirectory/WilliamsCincinnatiDirectory_1922_Pt01.pdf
   - 1923: https://apps.cincinnatilibrary.org/citydirectory/WilliamsCincinnatiDirectory_1923_Pt01.pdf
   - 1924: https://apps.cincinnatilibrary.org/citydirectory/WilliamsCincinnatiDirectory_1924_Pt01.pdf
   - 1925: https://apps.cincinnatilibrary.org/citydirectory/WilliamsCincinnatiDirectory_1925_Pt04.pdf
   - 1926–27: https://apps.cincinnatilibrary.org/citydirectory/WilliamsCincinnatiDirectory_1926-27_Pt01.pdf
   - 1927–28: https://apps.cincinnatilibrary.org/citydirectory/WilliamsCincinnatiDirectory_1927-28_Pt01.pdf
2. **1929–30 and 1930–31:** **“Cossmann Carl J”** and **“Harold G”** are listed at 2223 Loth, with **“John”** also at 2223 Loth. In 1930–31 Carl is listed at flat 6, 311 Seitz, while Harold and John remain at 2223 Loth. This is a household cluster consistent with a family, but it does not prove the relationship of the three men.
   - 1929–30: https://apps.cincinnatilibrary.org/citydirectory/WilliamsCincinnatiDirectory_1929-30_Pt01.pdf
   - 1930–31: https://apps.cincinnatilibrary.org/citydirectory/WilliamsCincinnatiDirectory_1930-31_Pt01.pdf
3. **1931–32 and 1932–33:** the directory again lists Carl J. at flat 6, 311 Seitz, and Harold G. and John at 2223 Loth. In 1932–33 the John entry is in the street directory at 2223 Loth.
   - 1931–32: https://apps.cincinnatilibrary.org/citydirectory/WilliamsCincinnatiDirectory_1931-32_Pt01.pdf
   - 1932–33: https://apps.cincinnatilibrary.org/citydirectory/WilliamsCincinnatiDirectory_1932-33_Pt01.pdf
4. **1936–37 and 1944:** Harold G. Cossmann is later listed as a clerk at 115 E. Court, residing at 2223 Loth in the 1936–37 directory, and at 2409 Vine in the 1944 directory. This is consistent with the same Harold G. in the earlier household, but still does not prove parentage.
   - 1936–37: https://apps.cincinnatilibrary.org/citydirectory/WilliamsCincinnatiDirectory_1936-37_Pt01.pdf
   - 1944: https://apps.cincinnatilibrary.org/citydirectory/WilliamsCincinnatiCityDirectory_1944_Part4.pdf
5. **1942 voter register:** Cincinnati's Ward 11, Precinct T register lists **John Cossman at 2223 Loth**. This independently supports John's continued residence at the address after the earlier directory listings; it does not name family relationships.
   - https://digital.cincinnatilibrary.org/digital/api/collection/p16998coll15/id/401312/download

## Additional collateral records for Harold G.

- Hamilton County's 1943 deed index lists **Harold G. Cossmann and Mary Helen** in a transaction with Pearl and Ernest G. Friedrich (book 1988; the OCR snippet is partially garbled, so the exact page/date must be read from the index PDF). This shows a named spouse/partner and property transaction, but no parcel/address is established.
  - https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1943tetrc.pdf
- The 1944 deed index lists **Harold G. and Mary Helen Cossmann** as grantors to Catherine and Louis Allevato, book 2059, page 625, dated 28 August 1944.
  - https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1944tetra.pdf
- These records support a Harold G. Cossmann married to Mary Helen, plausibly the Harold at 2223 Loth. They do not prove he is John's son or Stanley Peter's brother.

## Compiled child and sibling leads

Geneanet search results from a user-maintained family tree report:
- **Theresa M. Cossman**, born 1907 Ohio, as daughter of John Adam Cossmann and Henrietta Stella Bost. The 1924 city directory's Theresa entry at John's 2123 Loth address is consistent with this claim, but no original birth record has been inspected.
- **Carl J. Cossman**, born 26 March 1908, died 3 August 1971; marriage to Emma D. Reinhart on 27 March 1929; three children. Geneanet lists John Adam Cossmann and Henrietta Stella Bost as his parents. The 1929–33 directory entries show a Carl J. Cossman at a separate address from John, consistent with an adult married son, but the connection remains compiled-tree evidence plus directory correlation.
- **Harold G. Cossmann**, possibly born about 1915, appears in a WikiTree suggestion report and in the Cincinnati directory/deed records above. No original birth or census record has linked him to John and Henrietta.
- **Stanley Peter Cossman**, born 18 July 1920, is the reported son of John Adam and Henrietta; his Find a Grave memorial names them as parents. The current parent profile lists Stanley Peter and Carl J. only.

## Earlier generation — possible siblings of John Adam (not proved)

A Geneanet user tree reports **Kathryn Anna Cossmann** (born 1881, died 1974) as daughter of John Adam Cossmann and Sophia Bischoff, married to **George William Holtmann** on 7 February 1901, with a reported daughter Kathryn (born 1905). The same tree reports Johann Carl Cossman (1880–1881), Caroline Cossman (1882–1883), and an unnamed infant born 1888 as children of John Adam and Sophia. These are compiled-tree claims only. No original birth, death, marriage, or census record has been reviewed for this sibling group; do not publish them as proven siblings yet.
