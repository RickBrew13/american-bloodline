## HEADLINE FINDINGS — TWO REFUTATIONS OF THE ANCESTRY-BASED PREMISE
1. The Salvatore who married Ignazia Carbonaro is NOT the Sciacca couple's son. His 1906 New York marriage record (index ARK https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:2498-F2D; retired duplicate ARK https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:F64S-V4R) names his parents as Pasquale Cincimino and Francesca Perone, and his own 1933 Declaration of Intention — image read in full (image 44 of 187, EDNY Declarations Apr 3–10, 1933; image ARK https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QHV-F3CP-DPNY; index ARK https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:684C-65WJ) — states in his own words that he was born in Corleone, Sicily, on 30 April 1881, entered New York 21 June 1904 on the vessel North America, and was married in New York on 11 November 1906 (the marriage record says 15 September 1906 — a conflict to note). His wife Ignazia was born in Palermo on 29 July 1889 (verification re-read; the first read's '6 July 1884' was a misread of fainter figures in a retyped date field) and entered the U.S. 8 September 1903 — matching the tree's Ignazia birth of 29 July 1889, Palermo. Their four children, all born in New York City, per the declaration: Frances 18 February 1910, Josephine 3 February 1912, Rosalie 29 September 1919, Pasquale 25 March 1922 (verification re-read corrected Josephine and Pasquale). The Ancestry chain Salvadore x Antonina → Salvatore (b. 19 Mar 1879, Sciacca) → Ignazia → Frances is therefore refuted at the Salvatore–Ignazia link. The tree person is Salvatore Cincimino 9ZMF-PTH (b. 30 Apr 1881 Corleone; parents Pasquale 9ZMF-PTZ and Francesca Perone 9ZMF-PT8; d. 18 Apr 1948 Brooklyn, 1 source, not verified in this pass).
2. Salvadore did NOT die in 1905. An original Milan civil death act, read on the image (Milano Death Records 1892, image 938 of 2,038; image ARK https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSQJ-F9BW-3; index ARK https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X32V-5BK1), records act 123: Salvatore Ciancimino, age 49, bracciante (day laborer), born in Sciacca, son of Salvatore and Ninfa Noto, died 21 January 1892 in Milan, in the Regia Casa institution (the act does not name San Pier Giuliano Eymard — that is the index's designation; verification re-read). Age 49 + Sciacca birthplace + parents Salvatore and Ninfa Noto match Salvadore K8K1-8BP exactly (FamilySearch also proposes him as the tree match). The reported 1905 death is unsourced Ancestry material and should be replaced by 21 January 1892, Milan, subject to the marriage act confirming the same man.
