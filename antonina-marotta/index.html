Family Relationship Notes

Antonina Marotta married Salvadore Ciancimino (30 December 1842 – 21 January 1892). Their son Salvatore, born about 1881, married Rosa Graffeo on 15 September 1907 in Manhattan; the marriage record names his parents as Salvatore Ciancimino and Antonina Marotta.

The compiled tree gives her birth as 1848 and her death as 1904. The 1848 birth year is refuted: the only Antonina Marotta born in Sciacca in 1848 died 19 December 1849, aged 1, daughter of Salvatore Marotta and Maria Falcone. Her parents, birthplace, and death are unknown.

Ninfa Ciancimino, born 2 November 1884 in Sciacca, is listed on the compiled tree as her daughter, but no record found names Ninfa as Antonina’s daughter. She is not listed as her daughter here.
