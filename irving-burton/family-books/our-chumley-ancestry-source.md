## Relevant sections for the conditional Irvin ancestry branch
- Pages 39–54: Robert Chumley and Elizabeth “Betsy” (surname unproved).
- Pages 55–71: Andrew Chumbley, wife Elizabeth “Betsy” Cadle, their children and family migration.
- Pages 76–79: Civil War service of Elias Briton Chumbley and brother Samuel.
- Page 71: 1912 photograph captioned as descendants of Andrew Chumbley and Elizabeth Cadle.
- Endnotes on pages 53–54 and 85–88 cite census, court, land, marriage, military and pension records.

## Evidence caution
This is a detailed compiled family history, not a substitute for the original records it cites. It explicitly says Robert Chumley's wife Betsy's maiden surname is unknown and that the proposed Ford connection is not proven. The book's proposed link to Irvin remains conditional until Elihu Jones Burton and Mary Ellen Roy are proved as parents of Grady Elvis Burton Sr.

The book is linked rather than copied into GitHub; preserve the source URL and page references here.
