# Rose Hill Cemetery (Hamilton, Butler County) — historic tombstone-photo archive lead

The Butler County OHGenWeb cemetery directory lists **“ROSE HILL Cemetery, Hamilton”** and describes a collection of **select tombstone photographs by C. A. Miller**. This is a separate county-specific source from the Hamilton County Recorder's WPA veteran cards for **Rose Hill (Mt. Carmel)** in Anderson Township; those two cemeteries must not be confused.

## Exact legacy photo-collection link
- Butler County OHGenWeb directory entry, line 67: https://www.butler.ohgenweb.org/butler-cem.html
- Legacy C. A. Miller photo page linked from that entry: https://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.com/~charlotteamiller/rose_hill_cemetery.htm
