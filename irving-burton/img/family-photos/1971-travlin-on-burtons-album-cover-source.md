# Burton Family — 1971 *Travlin' On With The Burtons* album-cover image source

**Image located:** A black-and-white group photograph used on the 1971 *Travlin' On With The Burtons* gospel LP cover. The cover shows four people in a railroad-track setting. This is a family-group image associated with the Burton Family gospel act; **it is not identified as a portrait of Irvin David Burton, and no individual in the photograph is identified here**.
