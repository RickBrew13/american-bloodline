# 1912 Chumbley descendants group photograph — source record

**Photograph:** A group photograph printed on page 71 of *Our Chumley Ancestry* and captioned as descendants of **Andrew Chumbley and Elizabeth Cadle**. The book says it was probably taken in the Jabez section of Russell County, Kentucky, and that some people are not identified; proposed identifications are expressly uncertain.

**Source:** Kathy Chumley, *Our Chumley Ancestry*, p.71, PDF:
https://roryvantuyl.com/PDFs/Chumley.pdf

## Relevance to Irvin
This is a possible photograph of the wider Chumbley family line that may connect to Irvin through the conditional chain:
Irvin → Grady Elvis Burton Sr. (parentage link still under investigation) → possible parents Elihu Jones Burton and Mary Ellen Roy → Mary Ellen's reported mother Martha Frances Chumbley → Elias Brittain Chumbley → Andrew Chumbley and Elizabeth Cadle.

The photograph is **not a portrait of Irvin**, and no individual in it is identified here. It should be treated as a collateral/ancestor-family photograph lead until the Burton-to-Roy link is proved.
