# The Burtons — 1972 *Comin' On Strong* album-cover image source

**Image located:** The 1972 gospel LP *The Burtons — Comin' On Strong* is reproduced in the record-collector article below. The article includes a family comment by Dennis Burton, who describes the Burton family group and identifies the members of the act in the source discussion. This is a group/album-cover image associated with Irvin's immediate family's gospel-music history; **it is not identified here as a portrait of Irvin, and no individual in the image is identified here**.

## Sources
- *The Good, Bad & Ugly Gospel Record Barn*, “The Burtons — Comin' On Strong,” 22 March 2011, with family comment by Dennis Burton dated 1 June 2011: https://gbugrb.blogspot.com/2011/03/burtons-comin-on-strong.html
- Praguefrank's Country Discography, Burton Family / Burtons releases: https://countrydiscoghraphy2.blogspot.com/2023/01/burton-family-burtons.html

## Relevance to Irvin
The album and family comment document the musical activity of his father and brother. They do not establish that Irvin performed with the group or appears in the cover photograph.

## Preservation and rights
The album-cover image is reproduced by a third-party record collector. No local copy has been saved to GitHub because image reproduction rights have not been established.
