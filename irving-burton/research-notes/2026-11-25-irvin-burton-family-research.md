# Irvin David Burton — 25 November 2026 research continuation

## Identity and profile spelling
The Generation V index labels this person "Irving Burton," but the live profile and the records already collected identify him as **Irvin David Burton** (also Irvin D.). His headstone, 1940/1950 census entries, Ohio death index, Social Security records and Eunice's death notice use Irvin/Irvin D. No record reviewed establishes Irving as his legal given name. Continue using Irvin David Burton as the profile heading while preserving Irving as the Generation V display-name variant until the index can be reconciled.

## New family detail carried forward from Ora's 1991 obituary
The obituary clipping for Irvin's mother Ora Alice (Allen) Burton, preserved at [Find a Grave memorial 23174855](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/23174855/ora_alice-burton/photo), says she was a daughter of "the late Hank and Betty Burton Allen" and names a brother, Ed Allen, of Edinburg, Indiana. The clipping does not give Hank's full given name or further details about Ed. These are reported obituary statements; the newspaper title/date of publication are not visible on the clipping.

## Sibling-family detail confirmed
[Grady E. Burton Jr.'s obituary](https://www.browndawsonflick.com/obituaries/grady-burtonjr) at Brown Dawson Flick Funeral Home confirms he was the son of Grady Elvis Sr. and Ora Allen, identifies Irvin as his deceased brother, and gives Grady Jr.'s marriage to Betty Wyatt on 17 September 1955 in Carthage, Ohio. It names his children Danita, Donald, Mark and Dennis, grandchildren and great-grandchildren, and identifies brother Danny Ray Burton and wife Charlene. These details help document Irvin's sibling network; they do not establish Irvin's own children by themselves.

## Additional lead, not accepted as proof
A compiled five-generation Chumley family PDF places Mary Ellen Roy (4 February 1887–25 January 1956) as a daughter of Lewis Francis Roy and Martha Frances Chumbley, and spouse of Elihu Jones Burton (5 April 1883–22 December 1951). This is a compiled family-tree source, not a primary record, and the connection of Elihu and Mary to Irvin's father Grady Sr. remains unproved. The 1950 census shows E. Jones Burton, age 67, and Mary, age 62, living next door to Ora's household; their ages/names make them a plausible grandparent couple, but proximity alone does not establish the relationship. Do not add them as Irvin's grandparents until Grady Sr. is linked to them by a census, vital record, obituary, or other direct evidence.
