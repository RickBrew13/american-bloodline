## Further continuation — Butler County records and the 1994 Journal-News index (25 November 2026)

### Correct custodian for Irvin's 1994 death certificate
Irvin's death occurred in Hamilton, Butler County, Ohio, on 4 March 1994. The Ohio History Connection confirms it holds Ohio death certificates only through 1970; records from 1971 onward are held by the Ohio Department of Health or the local public health department. The Butler County local-record directory identifies the **City of Hamilton Health Department** as holding birth and death records from 1913 to the present for events within Hamilton city limits:

- City of Hamilton Health Department, 1 Renaissance Center, 345 High Street, Third Floor, Hamilton, Ohio; phone **513-785-7080**.
- If the death location turns out to have been outside Hamilton city limits, the Butler County Health Department is listed as holding township records from 1909 onward (513-863-1770).

The death-index location is Hamilton, so the City of Hamilton office is the first place to ask for the 1994 death certificate. Ask whether a genealogical/non-certified copy can be issued and cite certificate **017200**, volume **29741**, death date 4 March 1994. Do not assume the certificate will be released or that the index's marital-status field is correct until the original is inspected. Sources: [Ohio History Connection death-record FAQ](https://resources.ohiohistory.org/death/faq.php); [Butler County OHGenWeb — important addresses and record custodians](https://www.butler.ohgenweb.org/addresses.html), lines 44–58; [Ohio Department of Health — ordering certificates](https://odh.ohio.gov/know-our-programs/vital-statistics/how-to-order-certificates).
