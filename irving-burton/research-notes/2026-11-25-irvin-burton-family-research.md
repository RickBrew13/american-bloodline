# Irvin David Burton — 25 November 2026 research continuation

## Identity and profile spelling
The Generation V index labels this person "Irving Burton," but the live profile and the records already collected identify him as **Irvin David Burton** (also Irvin D.). His headstone, 1940/1950 census entries, Ohio death index, Social Security records and Eunice's death notice use Irvin/Irvin D. No record reviewed establishes Irving as his legal given name. Continue using Irvin David Burton as the profile heading while preserving Irving as the Generation V display-name variant until the index can be reconciled.

## Additional research pass — cemetery and newspaper-record leads (25 November 2026)

### Whited Cemetery family entries
The Casey County KYGenWeb transcription for [Whited Cemetery, B surnames](https://mail.kygenweb.net/casey/cemetery/WhitedB.htm) lists:
- **Grady Burton Sr.** — 7 September 1908 to 1 August 1990.
- **Ora Burton** — 25 December 1908 to 1 June 1991; explicitly described as wife of Grady Burton Sr.
- **Maxine Burton** — 14 July 1930 to 30 March 1943, age 12; explicitly described as daughter of Grady Sr. and Ora.

This independent local cemetery transcription supports the Grady–Ora relationship and their burial at Whited Cemetery. It also sharpens an existing conflict: Ora's 1991 obituary clipping says she was buried at Glenwood Cemetery, while Find a Grave and the Whited Cemetery list place her at Whited. Do not silently erase either source. A readable marker photograph or cemetery register would help settle it.

**Important Maxine discrepancy:** Irvin's existing profile and Generation 5 JSON currently give Maxine as 1929–1942. The Whited Cemetery transcription instead gives 14 July 1930–30 March 1943, age 12, and ties her directly to Grady Sr. and Ora. The 1940 census age of 10 is broadly compatible with a 1930 birth but does not settle the death year. Do not change the structured tree dates yet; verify the marker or original death record first. The Irvin profile now displays the discrepancy instead of presenting the old dates as settled.

### Newspaper obituary access route
The Hamilton County Genealogical Society explains that 20th-century Cincinnati-area death notices are indexed in the Cincinnati & Hamilton County Public Library's **Newsdex** database; the index can be searched without a library card. The Newsdex site did not load in this research session, so Irvin's March 1994 notice has **not** been located or read. The Society says that once the exact publication date and newspaper are known, the library can provide a copy by email or phone; its listed contact is research@chpl.org / 513-369-6900. Source: [HCGS, Death Notices and Obituaries](https://www.hcgsohio.org/cpage.php?pt=62), especially its Newsdex and copy-request instructions.

The Ohio History Connection's obituary research guide independently points researchers to Newsdex for Cincinnati-area obituaries: [Ohio History Connection, Obituaries](https://ohiohistory.libguides.com/newspapers/obituaries).

### Probate and marriage-record collections
