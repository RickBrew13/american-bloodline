# Irvin David Burton — Elias Brittain Chumbley's Civil War company discrepancy
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Cross-checked the Civil War unit information in *Our Chumley Ancestry* against the published roster of the 1st Kentucky Cavalry.

## Conflicting company assignments
The 2020 family history *Our Chumley Ancestry* says **Elias Briton “Britt” Chumbley** and his brother Samuel enlisted together on **10 August 1863** and mustered on **15 August 1863** at Somerset in the **1st Kentucky Cavalry, Company F**. It then says they returned to the regiment on **20 March 1864** and were transferred to **Company A** on **31 December 1864**.

The published roster of the 1st Kentucky Cavalry, however, lists **“Chumbley, Elias B.” as a Private in Company E**. The roster entry itself does not supply an enlistment date or identify a transfer. A modern transcription of the official roster repeats the Company E listing.

Sources:
- Kathy Chumley, *Our Chumley Ancestry*, “Chumbleys from Russell and Pulaski Counties, Kentucky Who Fought in the Civil War,” pp. 76–79: https://roryvantuyl.com/PDFs/Chumley.pdf
- *The Union Regiments of Kentucky*, Vol. II, 1st Kentucky Cavalry, Company E roster (digitized transcription): https://ldsgenealogy.com/KY/books/The-Union-regiments-of-Kentucky-Vol-2-part-32.htm
- Company E roster transcription: https://www.oocities.org/heartland/ridge/7616/39the.htm

## Identity and unit assessment
The surname, rare given-name combination, regiment, Kentucky family location and desertion/return narrative make it plausible that these sources refer to the same Elias Brittain Chumbley. However, the company designation conflicts:
- Family-history narrative: Company F, then Company A.
- Published regiment roster: Company E.

Possible explanations include a company reassignment not fully represented in the roster, an error in the compiled family narrative, or a same-name soldier. The available sources do not establish which explanation is correct. Do not silently harmonize the company assignments.

## Best original records to obtain
1. Elias B. Chumbley's compiled military service record (CMSR), National Archives, for the 1st Kentucky Cavalry.
2. His pension application/file, including the later 1912 application by **Anna J. Copley** for daughter **Queen Victoria Chumbley**, which the family history says was based on Elias's Civil War service.
3. Muster-in, muster-out, desertion, return-to-duty and transfer cards, including the alleged 31 December 1864 transfer to Company A.
4. Samuel Chumbley's CMSR, to compare the brothers' enlistment and company assignments.

## Tree and profile treatment
The conditional Chumbley ancestry note may describe Elias as a Civil War veteran of the **1st Kentucky Cavalry**, but the company must remain **unresolved (E/F/A discrepancy)** until the CMSR or pension file is checked. No company has been selected as proven.
