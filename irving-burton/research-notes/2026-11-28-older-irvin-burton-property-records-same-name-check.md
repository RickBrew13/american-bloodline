# Irvin David Burton — older Irvin Burton and Hamilton County property-record same-name check
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Checked Hamilton County deed-index hits for “Irvin Burton” and tested whether the 1951–1961 Irvin & Marie Burton property transactions could belong to Irvin David Burton (1932–1994).

## Deed-index entries located
Hamilton County Recorder's historical grantor/grantee indexes contain these entries for **Irvin & Marie Burton**:
- **11 January 1951:** grantor Irvin & Marie Burton; grantee Dove & Sam Funk; Deed Book 2517, page 340.
- **19 August 1953:** grantor Irvin & Marie Burton; grantee S. C. & Ola M. Homes; Deed Book 2632, page 304.
- **2 August 1961:** grantee Joseph & Maggie Scruggs; grantor Irvin & Marie Burton; Deed Book 3168, page 499.

The indexes show recorded transactions, not the full deed text, legal description or the couple's ages/addresses.

Sources:
- Hamilton County Recorder, 1951 grantor index: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1951tetrb.pdf
- Hamilton County Recorder, 1953 grantor index: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1953trteb.pdf
- Hamilton County Recorder, 1961 grantee index: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1961tetrs.pdf

## Why these are not Irvin David's deeds
A contemporaneous **26 September 1951 Hamilton County voter register** lists an **Irvin Burton at 843 Barr**, Cincinnati, Ward 16, Precinct H. Ohio's voting age in 1951 was 21. Irvin David Burton was recorded as age 17 in the 1950 census, so he would have been only about 18 or 19 in September 1951 and could not be the eligible voter in this entry. The 1951 deed transactions therefore should not be attributed to Irvin David without contrary direct proof.

The voter register and deeds appear to describe an older same-name Irvin Burton, consistent with a separate secondary cemetery index for **Irvin Burton (1904–1987), Spring Grove Cemetery, Cincinnati**. Whether that older Irvin is related to Grady Elvis Burton Sr. or to Irvin David remains unknown.

Sources:
- Cincinnati & Hamilton County Public Library, *Register of Voters*, Cincinnati, 26 September 1951, Ward 16 Precinct H: https://cdm16998.contentdm.oclc.org/digital/api/collection/p16998coll15/id/518545/download
- PeopleLegacy, Irvin Burton burial index, entry 1904–1987, Spring Grove Cemetery: https://peoplelegacy.com/burial-records/irvin/burton/
- Irvin David's original 1950 census sheet is archived in this folder's documents and transcribed in the existing research notes.
