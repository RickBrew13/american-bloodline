# Irvin David Burton — 1994 Hamilton Journal-News obituary index route
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Located the specific 1994 Journal-News obituary-index resource and the free CHPL Newsdex route for retrieving Irvin's original death notice/obituary.

## Specific annual index volume
A catalog record confirms the annual volume **_Obituaries & death notices as appeared in the Journal News, Hamilton, Ohio, 1994_**, compiled by **Helen F. Moore and Karen A. DeMonge** and published by the Southwest Butler County Genealogical Society. The annual series consists of newspaper obituary/death-notice citations and is listed as a library-use resource, with no online preview.

Sources:
- Open Library author bibliography for Karen A. DeMonge, listing the 1994 volume: https://openlibrary.org/authors/OL12483933A/Karen_A._DeMonge
- Columbus Metropolitan Library catalog record for the ongoing Journal-News obituary/death-notice series: https://cml.bibliocommons.com/v2/record/S105C1008332
- LDSGenealogy, Hamilton in Butler County death-record guide, listing the 1989–1999 Journal-News obituary index: https://ldsgenealogy.com/OH/Hamilton-in-Butler-County.htm

## Free online index
The Cincinnati & Hamilton County Public Library confirms that **Newsdex** indexes Cincinnati-area articles, death notices and obituaries. A library card is not required. It supplies exact newspaper date, page and column and sometimes notes whether a photograph accompanied the item.

- CHPL Newsdex overview: https://chpl.org/resources-types/newspaper/
- CHPL's 2023 Newsdex how-to article: https://chpl.org/blogs/post/newsdex-upgrade/
- Historical Newsdex URL: http://newsdex.cincinnatilibrary.org/uhtbin/cgisirsi/x/0/0/49 (the database requires a JavaScript-enabled browser and was not accessible to the web retrieval session).
