# Irvin David Burton — conditional Chumbley ancestry line and Civil War service
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed the possible Elihu Jones Burton–Mary Ellen Roy parental couple back through Mary Ellen's reported mother, Martha Frances Chumbley, into the Chumbley family history.

## The first link remains conditional
The 1950 census notes identify an adjacent household in Elmwood Place headed by **E. Jones Burton**, age 67, with **Mary Burton**, age 62. A compiled family profile identifies **Elihu Jones Burton (5 April 1883–22 December 1951)** as husband of **Mary Ellen Roy (4 February 1887–25 January 1956)**. The age and household context fit, but the census page for E. Jones and Mary has not yet been read from the original image, and no direct record has yet named Grady Elvis Burton Sr. as their son. Therefore the line below is a **conditional ancestor branch**, not a proven parentage chain for Irvin.

## Mary Ellen Roy's maternal line — detailed family-history source
A 2020 family-history book, *Our Chumley Ancestry* by Kathy Chumley, includes a detailed chapter on Andrew Chumbley and a Civil War chapter by Alice Chumbley Ray Lora. It identifies the following lineage:

1. **Martha Frances Chumbley** (18 June 1868–21 November 1923), reported daughter of Elias Brittain Chumbley and Elizabeth Ellen “Nelly” Dunsmore; married **Lewis Frances Roy** on 12 February 1886 in Russell County, Kentucky.
2. **Elias Brittain “Britt” Chumbley** (March 1837–31 October 1900), also recorded as Brit, Britton or Britain Chumbley; son of Andrew Chumbley and Elizabeth “Betsy” Cadle. Married Elizabeth Ellen “Nelly” Dunsmore on 11 November 1858 in Pulaski County, Kentucky.
3. **Andrew Chumbley** (18 February 1803–19 October 1892), son of **Robert Chumley** and **Elizabeth “Betsy”** (maiden surname not established). Andrew married **Elizabeth “Betsy” Cadle**, daughter of Mark Jackson Cadle and Mary Polly Covey, in August 1826 in Claiborne County, Tennessee.
4. **Robert Chumley** (born 1786 in Virginia; died 18 January 1853 in Bolivar, Polk County, Missouri) and wife **Elizabeth “Betsy”**, reportedly born about 1775 in Virginia. The book explicitly states that Betsy's maiden surname is unknown and that no record proves it was Ford. A possible relationship to John Chumlea is suggested by DNA and geography, but the authors say the parentage remains unproved.

## Independent census-transcription cross-check
The Hebroots Chumney/Chumley/Chumbley research page publishes additional census transcriptions that connect the name variants:
- **1850 Pulaski County:** Andrew Chumley, 46, wife Elizabeth, 46, and child **Britain**, 12, all reported born in Tennessee except Elizabeth (North Carolina) and later-born children. This is the candidate childhood household for Elias Brittain.
- **1860 Russell County:** **Britton Chumbley**, 21, born Tennessee, wife **Ellen**, 19, born Kentucky, and son **Andrew J.**, 10 months, born Kentucky.
- **1880 Russell County:** **Elias B. Chumley**, 43, born Tennessee, wife **Ellen**, 44, born Kentucky, daughter Elizabeth E. Turpen, 19, and children Martha F., 12; William T., 10; Elias B., 7; John W., 5; and George E., 2.

The progression Britain (1850) → Britton (1860) → Elias B. (1880), combined with the same wife name Ellen and the marriage entry for Elias Britton and Elizabeth Ellen Dunsmore in 1858, strongly supports that these are the same man. The original census images still need to be checked; these are transcriptions, not images.

**Birthplace/age conflicts:** The 1850 census says Andrew Chumley was born in Tennessee, while the 1860, 1870 and 1880 transcriptions and the family book give Virginia. The 1850 census says Elizabeth was born in North Carolina, while the 1860 transcription says Virginia; the family book identifies her as Elizabeth Cadle, born in North Carolina. Andrew's ages are 46 (1850), 56 (1860), 65 (1870) and 76 (1880), which are broadly consistent with an 1803/04 birth. Preserve the birthplace conflict rather than silently choosing one.

Source:
- Hebroots, Chumney/Chumley/Chumbley marriage and U.S. census transcriptions, Kentucky 1850, 1860, 1870 and 1880: https://www.hebroots.org/marrcens.html

**DNA-line caution:** The same Hebroots page's DNA-group summary states that the Robert Chumley and Betsy Ford line is not related through the male line to several other tested Chumley groups. This does not disprove the local Robert → Andrew → Elias family chain, but it warns against merging this branch with unrelated Chumley/Chumney lines based only on surname. The surname Ford for Betsy remains unproved in *Our Chumley Ancestry* and must not be added as fact.

## Census and record anchors supplied by the book
- **1850 U.S. census:** Pulaski County, Kentucky, Division 2, page 253. The book transcribes Andrew Chumbley, 46, farmer, born Virginia; wife Elizabeth, 46, born North Carolina; children Martha A. 19, James 16, **Britain 12**, Martin 10, John 8 and Samuel 4. A nearby household contains Andrew's brother Britain Chumbley, 35, and family. This provides a strong candidate identification for Elias Brittain, whose name variants include Britain.
- **1860 U.S. census:** the book's cited family material identifies Britton Chumbley with wife Ellen and infant son Andrew J. (about 10 months); this is consistent with the 1858 marriage and the reported first child, but the original census image has not been re-read here.
- **1880 U.S. census:** Russell County, Kentucky, page 513A, Enumeration District 101, Family History Film 1254441, image 84. The Chumley book's endnote cites this census for Andrew Chumbley (the older generation); the separate FamilySearch profile for Martha cites her as a child in the household of **Elias B. Chumley** and Ellen, with siblings. The original 1880 schedule should be inspected directly to verify the exact household and all children.
- **1858 marriage:** Elias Britton Chumbley and Elizabeth Ellen Dunsmore, 11 November 1858, Pulaski County, Kentucky; Marriage Book II, p.18 / Book 1, p.391. The family book gives the residence of Joseph Dunsmore, minister William S. Weddle, and witnesses John Campton, Perry Dunsmore and John Chaney.
- **Civil War service:** Elias Briton and brother Samuel Chumbley enlisted together on 10 August 1863 and mustered 15 August 1863 at Somerset in Kentucky 1st Cavalry, Company F. The book reports a desertion entry on 25 December 1863, return on 20 March 1864, and transfer to Company A on 31 December 1864. A War Department pension description gives Elias's complexion, eyes, hair and occupation as farmer.
- **Second marriage:** after Nelly's death (reported 20 December 1886), Elias married Anna J. Lyons on 6 April 1887 in Pulaski County. The book says they had four daughters; Anna later applied for a pension for daughter Queen Victoria Chumbley in 1912. Elias died 31 October 1900 at Teddy or Evona, Casey County, Kentucky.

## Photo lead
*Our Chumley Ancestry* includes a **1912 group photograph captioned as descendants of Andrew Chumbley and Elizabeth Cadle**, printed on page 71. The source says the photograph probably came from the Jabez section of Russell County, Kentucky; some people in it are not identified, and the author expressly treats some proposed identifications as uncertain. It may be relevant to this conditional ancestor branch, but it is not a photograph of Irvin and should not be captioned as such.

## Evidence boundary and next work

It does not resolve the first link between Elihu/Mary and Grady Sr. The most important next record is the original 1950 census sheet 18 for ED 31-69, followed by Grady Sr.'s birth/baptism or death record. Once the Burton parentage is proved, inspect the cited 1880 census, 1858 marriage book, Civil War service/pension file, Andrew's will and deeds, and the 1830/1840 Tennessee census records.
