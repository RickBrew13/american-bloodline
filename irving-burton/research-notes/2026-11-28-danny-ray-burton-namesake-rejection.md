# Irvin David Burton — Danny Ray Burton namesake rejection
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Checked a web-search hit for a deceased Danny Ray Burton to avoid incorrectly attaching a same-name person to Irvin's younger brother.

## Candidate found, but rejected
A search for Danny Ray Burton returned a funeral-home obituary for **Danny Ray Burton (23 January 1950–2 January 2024)** of Stewart, Ohio. That obituary names his parents as Claude and Pauline Yeater Burton, his wife as Wanda Bowers Burton, and three sons. It is not Irvin's brother:
- The 1950 census already places Irvin's brother **Danny Ray Burton** in Ora's household at age **3**, implying a birth around 1946–1947, not 23 January 1950.
- The 2010 obituary for Grady Burton Jr. names his surviving brother **Danny Ray (Charlene) Burton of Loveland**, while the 2024 obituary's Danny Ray was the son of Claude and Pauline and lived in Stewart.
- The 2024 obituary's parents and family group do not match Grady Sr. and Ora's known children.

Therefore, do not merge the 1950–2024 Danny Ray Burton with Irvin's brother. The brother's spouse is reported as **Charlene** in Grady Jr.'s 2010 obituary; the full identity of Charlene and the brother's children remain unverified.
