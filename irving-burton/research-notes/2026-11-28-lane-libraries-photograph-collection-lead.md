**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Located the strongest regional archival photograph route for a possible image of Irvin or his immediate family in Hamilton, Butler County, Ohio.

## Collections with relevant date/place coverage
Lane Libraries' Cummins Local History Room in Hamilton preserves:
- The **George C. Cummins “Remember When” Photograph Collection**, approximately 5,500 photographs documenting Butler County, especially Hamilton.
- The **Joseph A. Cella Photograph Collection**, approximately 100 photographs from 1950s Butler County. Joseph Cella was a Hamilton Journal-News professional photographer and personal photographer.
- Local newspapers, yearbooks, city directories, maps and other local history material.

The Cella collection's 1950s date range is particularly relevant to Irvin's young-adult years in Hamilton/Elmwood Place. The Cummins collection may also contain a family, workplace, church, neighborhood or event photograph, though its described coverage is largely historic.
