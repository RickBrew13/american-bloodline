## New finding: the Burton family’s gospel-music history
- [Grady Burton Jr.’s Journal-News obituary (Legacy)](https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/hamilton/name/grady-burton-obituary?id=26097818), published 19 February 2010, says he began playing and singing gospel music with his family in 1944, identifies his parents Grady Elvis Sr. and Ora (Allen) Burton, and names Irvin as a deceased brother.
- [GJ Artist Discography’s Gospel Singing Burton Family entry](https://gj.rcs-discography.com/search.php?type=artpk&key=805) identifies a Cincinnati group and indexes 16 records, including a 1963 EP and a 1964 album with C. E. Dozier, plus 1965 releases. [Discogs’ The Burton Family page](https://www.discogs.com/artist/6439437-The-Burton-Family) lists a 1960 single and releases in the 1960s/1970. [The Good, Bad & Ugly Gospel Record Barn’s “Homecoming Day” note](https://gbugrb.blogspot.com/2015/12/homecoming-day-gospel-singing-burton.html) describes the group’s Cincinnati/Fairfield connection but hedges on which family members participated.
- These establish family musical activity, not Irvin’s participation. The sources reviewed do not name Irvin in the group lineup; the profile expressly leaves his own performance UNPROVED.
