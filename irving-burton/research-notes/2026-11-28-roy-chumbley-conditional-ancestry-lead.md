**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed the compiled identification of Elihu Jones Burton and Mary Ellen Roy as a possible parental couple for Grady Elvis Burton Sr., and traced Mary Ellen's reported parents and siblings.

## Evidence chain — still conditional at the first link
The existing 1950 census research notes identify **E. Jones Burton (about 1883)** and **Mary Burton** as living next door to Ora and the children in Elmwood Place. A compiled five-generation family PDF identifies **Elihu Jones Burton (5 April 1883–22 December 1951)** as the husband of **Mary Ellen Roy (4 February 1887–25 January 1956)**. ## Mary Ellen Roy's reported parents
FamilySearch's compiled profile for **Martha Frances Chumbley (18 June 1868–21 November 1923)** identifies her as the daughter of:
- **Elias Britton Chumbley** (born about 1837)
- **Elizabeth Ellen Dunsmore** (born about 1836)

The profile cites an 1880 census entry for Martha in the household of Elias B. Chumley, a Kentucky county marriage record for Martha F. Chumbley, and a Find a Grave index entry. It reports Martha's marriage to **Lewis Frances Roy** on **12 February 1886** in Russell County, Kentucky, and identifies Mary Ellen Roy (1887–1956) among their seven children. This is a useful record-backed family-tree lead, but the actual 1880 census image and marriage instrument have not yet been independently re-read in this project.

Sources:
