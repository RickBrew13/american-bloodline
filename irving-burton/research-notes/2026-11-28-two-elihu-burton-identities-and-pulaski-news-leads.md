# Irvin David Burton — distinguish two Elihu Burton identities
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed Pulaski County, Kentucky newspaper and vital-record references to “Elihu Burton” and compared them with the conditional Elihu Jones Burton (1883–1951) profile.

## Two men must be kept separate

### A. Elihu Burton, 1873–1933 — son of John Wesley Burton
A FamilySearch compiled profile for **John Wesley Burton (October 1849–1910)** identifies his children as:
- Ida Frances Burton (1870–1936)
- **Elihu Burton (1873–1933)**
- James Thomas Burton (1878–1938)

John Wesley lived in Nancy, Pulaski County, Kentucky, in 1900 and is buried at Fairview Cemetery, Ferguson. A secondary cemetery aggregation also lists Elihu Burton (1873–1933) at Fairview Cemetery.

Sources:
- FamilySearch, John Wesley Burton (L6YM-5MW): https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/L6YM-5MW/john-wesley-burton-1849-1910
- PeopleLegacy, Fairview Cemetery, Pulaski County (lists Elihu Burton 1873–1933): https://peoplelegacy.com/william_allen_burdine-1n4v411

### B. Elihu Jones Burton, 1883–1951 — possible husband of Mary Ellen Roy
The Chumley five-generation family report identifies **Elihu Jones Burton (5 April 1883–22 December 1951)** as husband of **Mary Ellen Roy (4 February 1887–25 January 1956)**. The 1950 census index/transcription identifies an **E. Jones Burton, age 67**, with Mary, age 62, in a household adjacent to Ora and the children in Elmwood Place, Ohio. The ages and names fit the 1883/1887 couple, but the original sheet 18 has not yet been retrieved.

Source:
- *Robert and Betsy Chumley — 5 generations*, lines 505–506: https://chumleyfamilytree.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/robert-and-betsy-chumley-5-generations.pdf
- NARA 1950 census ED 31-69 retrieval note: `2026-11-28-nara-1950-ed31-69-household-retrieval.md`

The 1873 Elihu cannot be the man on the 1950 sheet if the age 67 is correct: he died in 1933 and would have been about 77 in 1950. The original sheet is still needed to check whether the age was read correctly and whether the name is indeed E. Jones.

## Pulaski County newspaper and vital-record leads — identity unresolved
A 1920 *Somerset Journal* community column for Nancy reports:
- 7 November 1919: “Elihu Burton and family” visited at Walter Hudson's; W. L. Burton and wife were also present.
- 25 June 1920: “Mrs. Evie Minsie and baby of Shafter” visited her father **Elihu Burton** at Nancy; the same item says a little daughter of Elihu Burton broke her arm.
- 22 October 1920: “Elihu Burton and family” visited at Elmore Burton's.
- 20 February 1920: a separate Nancy note mentions “Vivie Burton” visiting at Shafter.

Sources:
- 7 November 1919: https://kygenweb.net/pulaski/news/11_7_1919.htm
- 25 June 1920: https://mail.kygenweb.net/pulaski/news/6_25_1920.htm
- 22 October 1920: https://kygenweb.net/pulaski/news/10_22_1920.htm
- 20 February 1920: https://mail.kygenweb.net/pulaski/news/2_20_1920.htm

A Pulaski County birth-index transcription lists **Arnold F. Mincey**, born **6 March 1919**, with mother **Evia Burton**. This may be the baby mentioned with Mrs. Evie Minsie, but the spelling and identity are not confirmed.

A Pulaski County stillbirth certificate transcription for **Leslie Eugene McGahan**, born/died 28 May 1928 in Nancy, gives:
- Father: Leslie R. McGahan
- Mother: Blonnie Burton
- Informant: **Elihu Burton**, address Nancy, Kentucky
- Burial: Fair View, 1 June 1928

Source:
- Pulaski County death certificate transcription, Leslie Eugene McGahan, certificate 13801: https://mail.kygenweb.net/pulaski/death/mcgahanleslie.html

## Why these records are not attached to Elihu Jones Burton
The Nancy newspaper and 1928 vital record fit the residence and life dates of **Elihu Burton (1873–1933)** particularly well. They could refer to him rather than the younger Elihu Jones Burton (1883–1951). The 1919 Mincey birth record and 1920 Evie Minsie news item may reveal a daughter of the older Elihu, but the parentage is not yet proven. No Evie Mincey, Blonnie Burton or McGahan descendant has been attached to the younger Elihu Jones Burton or to Irvin's line.
