# Irvin David Burton — Grady and Ora Burton marriage-date verification
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Tested the 1927 marriage date currently reported for Irvin's parents, Grady Elvis Burton Sr. and Ora Alice Allen.

## What supports the couple
- The 1940 U.S. census identifies Grady Burton as head and Ora Burton as his wife in Batavia Township, Clermont County, Ohio. It gives no marriage date in the current transcription.
- The 1950 census identifies Ora as Grady's wife and lists their children in the Elmwood Place household.
- Ora's 1991 obituary calls her the widow of Grady Burton Sr.
- Find a Grave memorials for both Grady and Ora report that they married in **1927**. This is a derivative memorial claim; no original marriage record or county index citation is supplied there.

## Searches and results
- Casey County KYGenWeb's additional marriage transcription, which includes some entries through the twentieth century, contains **no Burton marriage entry**. It is not a complete index and its absence is not proof that no Casey County marriage occurred.
  - https://kygenweb.net/casey/GT-Marriages.htm
- Casey County's main marriage resource page lists county marriage records from 1807 and identifies published marriage volumes, including *Casey County Marriages 1916–1935* and other transcripts. The 1916–1935 index was not accessible as a searchable online list in this pass.
  - https://kygenweb.net/casey/marriages.htm
  - https://kygenweb.net/casey/resources.htm
- The Kentucky County Marriages 1786–1965 collection contains original county marriage licenses, certificates, bonds and returns, but the name search interface was not accessible to this session.
  - https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/1804888

## Current conclusion
The marriage itself is established by the 1940 and 1950 census household relationships and Ora's 1991 obituary. **The year 1927 is not yet verified**; it currently rests on Find a Grave memorials. Do not publish 1927 as a proven date until the original marriage record, license or certificate is located.
