# Irvin David Burton — St. Bernard–Elmwood Place yearbook photo lead
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed a school-photo lead based on Irvin's 1950 residence and school-age timeline.

## Candidate school and yearbooks
The 1950 census places Irvin, age 17, in Elmwood Place, Hamilton County, Ohio, at 5902, looking for work. The 1950 Hamilton County school-record guide lists yearbooks for **St. Bernard–Elmwood Place High School** for 1946, 1948, 1949, 1950 and later years. A separate alumni/yearbook listing also confirms a 1950 volume and identifies the school in St. Bernard, adjacent to Elmwood Place.

The 1940 census says Irvin was in first grade at age 7. His 1994 Ohio death-index entry reports 12 years of schooling. These facts make the 1948–1950 school-yearbook range worth checking, but **do not prove that he attended St. Bernard–Elmwood Place High School**. He was recorded as looking for work rather than attending school in April 1950, so the relevant image could be in a 1948 or 1949 volume, or he may not appear in these yearbooks at all.

## Access and image status
The school-record guide identifies the following access routes:
- St. Bernard–Elmwood Place High School yearbook, 1950, through the US Yearbooks collections on Ancestry, MyHeritage and Classmates.
- A 1950 volume is listed on the St. Bernard–Elmwood Place alumni/yearbook page.

No yearbook page or portrait was retrieved in this pass; the sites list the volumes but do not expose a free, searchable page-level scan here. No image has been added to GitHub and no portrait has been attributed to Irvin.
