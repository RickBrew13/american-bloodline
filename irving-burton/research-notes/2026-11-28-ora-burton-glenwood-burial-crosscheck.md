**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Person under review:** Irvin David Burton  
**Folder-first review:** Completed before this research pass. The existing profile, all seven research/transfer notes, both census originals, the Ora obituary clipping, headstone image, and the empty family-photo/headstone subfolders were inventoried. Existing originals were left untouched.

## New result: the Glenwood cemetery transcription matches the obituary
Ora's original 1991 obituary clipping, already archived in Irvin's folder, says she was buried in **Glenwood Cemetery** after her 4 June 1991 funeral in Liberty, Casey County, Kentucky.

The Casey County KYGenWeb **Glenwood Cemetery** transcription now provides a direct cemetery-listing match:
- **Burton, Ora Allen**
- Born **25 December 1908**
- Died **1 June 1991**

Those dates exactly match the archived obituary clipping and the separate Whited Cemetery transcription's Ora entry. This is a stronger corroboration of the obituary's stated burial location than was available in the prior research pass.

However, the Whited Cemetery transcription also lists **Ora (25 December 1908–1 June 1991), wife of Grady Burton Sr.** The same exact woman cannot ordinarily have a grave in both cemeteries. The Glenwood listing names her full name as Ora Allen; the Whited listing names her relationship to Grady. Neither online transcription supplies a grave number or a readable marker photograph. The evidence now leans toward **Glenwood Cemetery** because the contemporary obituary explicitly says Glenwood and the Glenwood list independently names Ora Allen with matching exact dates. The Whited entry may be a duplicate/transcription-placement error, but that explanation is not yet proven.
