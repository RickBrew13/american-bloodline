# Irvin David Burton — Grady and Ora Burton's 1966 Hamilton County deed verified
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Rechecked the official Hamilton County Recorder's 1966 grantor/grantee index for the property transaction previously carried as a lead.

## Official index entry
The Hamilton County Recorder's 1966 index, printed page 30 (PDF page 15), lists:
- **Date:** 16 May 1966
- **Grantees/buyers:** **Grady & Ora Burton**
- **Grantors/sellers:** **Allen C. & B. J. Berbar** (OCR spelling in the index; the second surname should be checked against the deed image)
- **Deed book:** **3480**
- **Page:** **120**

This confirms the named couple and deed-book/page reference. It does not provide the street address, legal description, purchase price, parcel number, or whether the property was their principal residence.

Official source:
- Hamilton County Recorder, *1966 Grantor–Grantee Index*, printed page 30 / PDF page 15: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1966tetrb.pdf
- The OCR-searchable entry appears on the official PDF around the Grady & Ora Burton line and gives book 3480, page 120, date 16 May 1966.

## Genealogical significance and evidence boundary
This is a property transaction by **Irvin's parents**, not by Irvin. It may help locate the family home and trace the household's moves, but it is not evidence that Irvin owned or inherited the parcel. No ownership claim has been added to Irvin's biography.
