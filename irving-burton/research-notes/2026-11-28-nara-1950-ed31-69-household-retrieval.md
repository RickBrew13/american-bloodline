## Official NARA identification of the correct schedule
The National Archives' 1950 Census Ohio metadata summary identifies:
- **State/county:** Ohio, Hamilton County
- **Enumeration district:** **31-69**
- **Place:** Elmwood Place village
- **Official ED description:** the part bounded by village limits, McGregor Avenue, Walnut, Vine, Locust, McGregor Avenue, Township Avenue and village limits
- **Population schedule folder:** `1950census/43290879-Ohio/43290879-Ohio-025452`
- **ED description image:** `1950census-descriptions/Ohio/Ohio-0031Ohio-0031_0027.jpg`
- **ED map:** `1950census-maps/Ohio/Hamilton/16-a2-077-04726.jpg`

This confirms that the relevant E. Jones Burton/Mary index hit and Ora's household belong to the same Elmwood Place ED. The census sheet for Ora is already archived in this folder; the sheet containing E. Jones Burton and Mary is not.

Official source:
- National Archives, *1950 Census Dataset*, Ohio metadata summary, ED 31-69: https://nara-1950-census.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/metadata/html/oh.html
- NARA's 1950 Census dataset documentation explains that the metadata links each ED to its population-schedule image folder, ED map and written description: https://www.archives.gov/developer/1950-census

## What this does and does not establish
The ED-level source verifies the correct original census series and geographic district. It does **not** prove that E. Jones Burton and Mary were Grady Sr.'s parents; that depends on reading the household's relationship column and confirming the couple's identities against the 1950 census and other records.

The previous research transcription reports E. Jones Burton, age 67, and Mary, age 62, on sheet 18, while Ora and the children are on sheet 28. The image for sheet 18 has not yet been obtained and transcribed in this project. No relationship has been added to the tree.
