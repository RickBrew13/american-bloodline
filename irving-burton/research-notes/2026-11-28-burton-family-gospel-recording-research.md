# Irvin David Burton — Burton Family gospel recordings and family-group photo research
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed the documented gospel-music activity of Irvin's father and brother, checked whether his brother Danny Ray may be the Cincinnati session musician credited as Danny Burton, and located family-group album-cover photographs.

## Confirmed family musical history
The 2010 obituary of **Grady Burton Jr.** says he began playing and singing gospel music with his family in **1944** and continued throughout his life. The obituary identifies his parents as Grady Elvis Sr. and Ora (Allen) Burton, names his brother **Danny Ray (Charlene) Burton of Loveland**, and gives Grady Jr.'s own family and descendants.

The vintage gospel record collector's blog *The Good, Bad & Ugly Gospel Record Barn* reproduces information from Burton Family LPs and includes a family comment posted by **Dennis Burton**, identified as Grady Jr.'s son. The comment states that the Burton group included Grady Jr., his brother Danny and their father Grady Burton Sr.; it also says Grady Jr. and Danny were known for instrumentals as well as singing. The comment is direct family testimony, though it is a public blog comment rather than a formal record.

## Recording and musician credits
The record history shows a long Cincinnati-area recording activity:
- 1965: *Homecoming Day*, The Burton Family.
- 1969: *Gospel Singing... Burton Family Style*.
- 1970: *In Harmony*.
- 1971: *Travlin' On With The Burtons*, recorded at Rite Records in Cincinnati; the discography credits Dan Burton on piano and Grady Burton Jr. on electric guitar, with session musicians.
- 1972: *Comin' On Strong*, The Burtons.
- 1975: *Stepping On The Clouds*.
- 1979: Danny Burton and The City Limits, Gateway Records, Cincinnati.
- 1984: *Merry Christmas From Al Morgan*, Jewel Records, Cincinnati; the credits list Danny Burton and Charlene Burton as background singers and Danny Burton as a pianist.

The 1984 Danny/Charlene credits are a **probable lead** for the Danny Ray and Charlene Burton named in Grady Jr.'s obituary: the names, Cincinnati-area recording scene, and family gospel background fit. No record explicitly states that the studio musician Danny Burton is Danny Ray Burton, so keep the identity unconfirmed until a family source, interview, recording contract or other direct record connects them.

The group sources do not name Irvin as a member, singer or instrumentalist. **Do not identify him in either image or imply that he performed with the group.**

## Effect on Irvin's biography
This research adds a documented family context: Irvin's father and brother were part of a Cincinnati-area gospel family with recordings from the 1960s through the 1980s. It does not establish that Irvin himself performed, recorded, toured, or appeared on any album. His own musical participation remains unproved.

No copyrighted album-cover image has been copied into GitHub. The image-source note preserves the viewable link and copyright/permission boundary. No claims about Irvin's musical participation were added to the family tree.
