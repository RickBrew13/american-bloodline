# Irvin David Burton — Andrew Chumbley's second marriage and possible half-sibling branches
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Scope:** Followed Andrew Chumbley's second marriage and the children in his 1870/1880 household, relevant to the conditional Chumbley ancestor line.

## Second marriage
The Chumley Chumbley Research Project identifies Andrew Chumbley's second wife as **Mary Elizabeth Jane “Jane” Goodin**, daughter of John Goodin and Sarah Gums Montgomery, married **1 April 1866** in Pulaski County, Kentucky. It says this was a second marriage for both and that Jane had previously been married to **William McClendon**.

Sources:
- “Children of Andrew Chumbley,” Chumley Chumbley Research Project: https://chumleyfamilytree.com/2020/08/27/andrew-chumbley/
- Kathy Chumley, *Our Chumley Ancestry*, Andrew Chumbley chapter, pp. 55–71: https://roryvantuyl.com/PDFs/Chumley.pdf

## 1870 and 1880 census household comparison
A census transcription for 1870 Pulaski County, Kentucky, gives Andrew Chumley, 65, wife Jane, 43, and children:
- Lurinda J., 20
- Mary S., 15
- Leander, 8
- Lewis W., 4
- Wiley W., 3

The 1880 Russell County transcription gives Andrew Chumley, 76, wife Mary E. J., 51, and sons:
- Leander, 18
- Lewis W., 14
- Wiley W., 13

The ages of Andrew, Jane/Mary E. J., and the three boys are broadly consistent across the two censuses. The girls are no longer in the 1880 household, presumably having married, moved or died, but their later lives are not yet identified.

Source:
- Hebroots, Chumley/Chumney census transcriptions, 1870 and 1880 Kentucky sections: https://www.hebroots.org/marrcens.html

## 1872 will — primary-record lead
The Chumley family-history page transcribes **Andrew Chumbley's will**, dated **19 July 1872**, Russell County Will Book 4, page 55. It states that:
- His wife **Jane Chumbly** is to receive support/livelihood from the land.
- His son **Lewis Chumbley** is to receive the home farm after Jane's death, with control to cultivate it and retain proceeds above Jane's support.
- The remainder of his estate is to be divided equally among all his legal heirs.
- Andrew signed by mark; Dr. J. B. Scholl attested.

This confirms Jane as his wife in 1872 and names Lewis as his son. It does not list all legal heirs by name. The original will image should be retrieved from Russell County Will Book 4, p.55.

## Which children belong to which marriage?
The current sources do not fully resolve the parentage of all five children in the 1870 household:
- **Lurinda J. (about 1850)** and **Mary S. (about 1855)** were born before Andrew's 1866 marriage to Jane; they may be children of his first wife Elizabeth Cadle, or they may be Jane's children from her previous marriage who were enumerated under the Chumbley surname. Do not assign them to either parentage without birth/marriage records.
- **Leander (about 1861/62)** was also born before the 1866 marriage. The 2020 family page groups him with Andrew and Jane, but the dates conflict with the marriage. He may be a child of Andrew's first marriage, or a child of Jane's previous marriage. Keep parentage unresolved.
- **Lewis W. (about 1866)** and **Wiley W. (about 1867)** were born around/after the 1866 marriage. Lewis is explicitly called Andrew's son in the 1872 will. They are likely children of Andrew and Jane, but original birth/census records should be checked before formal attachment.

## Relationship to Elias Brittain Chumbley
If Elias Brittain Chumbley is confirmed as Andrew's son by the 1850 census and family-history evidence, then children from Andrew's second marriage would be Elias's half-siblings. The exact list is not yet secure; the first-marriage and stepchild possibilities above must be resolved first.
