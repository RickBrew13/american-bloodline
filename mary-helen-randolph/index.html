Family Relationship Notes

The 1880 census indexes Mary Helen Randolph, age 17, as a daughter in Jeremiah Randolph’s household; it names Jeremiah’s wife as Elzira F. Randolph. Her 1921 death certificate, recorded as Mary H. Cornett, names father G. M. Randolph and the mother’s surname “Rash.” The FamilySearch compiled profile expands this family as Jeremiah M. Randolph and Nancy Jane Rash. These sources may refer to the same woman, but no record reviewed here bridges Elzira F. to Nancy Jane; the mother’s given name remains unresolved.

The 1882 marriage index records Mary H. Randolph marrying Henry B. Cornet in Pulaski County. The 1910 census later shows Mary H. Cornett with Henry and children including William M. The compiled family profile lists seven children; the census directly corroborates several but does not establish the full list.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Identity: Mary Helen Randolph is the 17-year-old daughter in the 1880 Jeremiah Randolph household and the Mary H. Randolph who married Henry B. Cornet in 1882; the 1910 census and 1921 death certificate support her later name Mary H. Cornett.
  • Maternal identity remains unresolved: the 1880 census indexes Jeremiah’s wife as Elzira F. Randolph; the death certificate gives father G. M. Randolph and mother surname “Rash”; the compiled profile names Nancy Jane Rash. Do not conflate Elzira and Nancy Jane without a documentary bridge.
  • Children: the seven-child family group is compiled in FamilySearch; the 1910 original census shows William M. and several siblings. “Elsie A.” in that census may correspond to compiled Elyza, but keep the variant unresolved.
  • No verified portrait located in the sources reviewed. The profile uses the original census, marriage, and death-record scans as documentary images, not portraits.
