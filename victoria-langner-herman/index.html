Family Relationship Notes

Peter and Victoria appear together in the 1900 census in Holding Township, Stearns County, Minnesota. Their son Lawrence’s 1943 Michigan death certificate names Peter Herman and Victoria Langner as his parents. The 1910 census lists Victoria as head of household with five sons; the Minnesota death record for infant Stanislas names Peter and Victoria as his parents.

Victoria’s 1921 Detroit marriage record to John K. Urbaniec names her parents as Jos. Langner and Christina. The 1900 and 1910 census entries, later Detroit census records, and the 1921 marriage record trace her move from Minnesota to Michigan. Peter is absent from Victoria’s 1910 household, and Frank’s 1942 death certificate is unrelated to Peter’s fate; Peter’s death date remains unknown. A 1937 Hennepin County death-index entry is only a candidate and is not assigned to him.

The children listed are those appearing in the census and infant death record. Some dates are estimates from census ages. No verified portraits have been located for Peter or Victoria, so each profile uses one placeholder.

Life Story

Victoria Langner was born in Minnesota in May 1879, the daughter of Jos. Langner and Christina . She married Peter Herman about 1899 and raised their sons in Holding Township, Stearns County, where her son Lawrence was born in 1900 . By 1910 she was heading the household herself with five boys . She moved to Detroit, where she married John K. Urbaniec on 25 January 1921, and she appears in Detroit in the 1930, 1940 and 1950 censuses . Online family trees give her death as 1970, but no record has yet confirmed it .
