Life Story

Victoria Langner was born in Minnesota in May 1879, the daughter of Jos. Langner and Christina SUPPORTED. She married Peter Herman about 1899 and raised their sons in Holding Township, Stearns County, where her son Lawrence was born in 1900 SUPPORTED. By 1910 she was heading the household herself with five boys SUPPORTED. She moved to Detroit, where she married John K. Urbaniec on 25 January 1921, and she appears in Detroit in the 1930, 1940 and 1950 censuses SUPPORTED. Online family trees give her death as 1970, but no record has yet confirmed it UNPROVED.
