## 2. Important correction — FamilySearch does have a Franklin County marriage collection covering 1966

The existing profile previously stated that FamilySearch's Franklin County catalog records ended in 1953 and that no 1966 Franklin County marriage record was available there. That statement is too broad and is corrected by the current collection documentation.

FamilySearch has a dedicated collection titled **Indiana, Franklin County, Marriage Records, 1853–1997**. Its collection description says the records were obtained from the Franklin County Court and include marriage licenses and marriage information; birth and parents' information may be included. It currently describes 15,138 records and 25,292 images. Therefore 1966 is within the stated date range and this collection is a direct research route for the reported 18 June 1966 Edna Bailey–Homer Brewster claim. A search result for the couple has **not** yet been obtained; do not claim either a match or a negative result.
- Collection page: https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/5000717
- Official collection description and scope: https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/5000717
- Citation/collection details: FamilySearch, “Indiana, Franklin County, Marriage Records, 1853–1997,” database (accessed 25 November 2026).

Indiana Archives and Records Administration separately explains that state marriage data begins in 1958; 1958–2005 digitized marriage records are available through Ancestry for genealogical use, and original county records remain with the county clerk unless transferred to the State Archives. The Indiana State Library's 1958–2018 marriage index is another route. These sources establish where to search, not whether Edna's alleged marriage occurred.
- IARA official vital-record guidance: https://www.in.gov/iara/services-for-public/search-archives-holdings/vital-records/

## 9. Marriage-order evidence makes the Edna–Homer tree claim substantially less credible

A closer comparison with Homer's own indexed divorce/marriage sequence adds an important contradiction beyond the overlap with Judith:

- The Ohio Department of Health/Henschen index entry for Homer's 2 May 1968 divorce from Judith Mae Palmer reports **“Number of this marriage” = 2** for Homer.
- The U.S. National Center for Health Statistics' *Handbook on Divorce Registration* explains that item 24 records the number of the marriage being dissolved for each spouse: “First” if never previously married, “Second,” “Third,” etc. if previously married. This is a count of the person's legal marriages, not simply a number of ceremonies. See pages 12–13, especially the instructions at lines 428–434 in the online PDF: https://ftp.cdc.gov/pub/Health_statistics/NCHS/Publications/DVD/DVD_3/Misc/hb_div.pdf
- Homer's later 12 September 1977 marriage to Flora L. is indexed as his third marriage, with two previous marriages. That is consistent with the 1968 Judith divorce being marriage number two and implies an earlier, still-unidentified first marriage before Judith.
- The tree's alleged Edna marriage is dated 18 June 1966, ten years after Homer married Judith in 1956 and while that marriage was still legally in effect until May 1968. It therefore cannot sensibly fill the earlier-first-marriage slot. If it were a separate legal marriage in 1966, it would be later than Judith's marriage and would make the 1968 “second marriage” count difficult to reconcile; it also does not fit the later third-marriage count in 1977.
- This is not yet a final disproof because the Ohio index transcription and the underlying divorce abstract have not been checked against the original, and the Indiana marriage claim has not been searched in the underlying county register. But the independent marriage-order evidence strongly suggests the Edna–Homer link in the online trees is misattached, misdated, or otherwise erroneous. Do not place Edna in Homer's spouse sequence as fact.

**Next decisive checks:** obtain the original 1968 Ohio divorce abstract/case file to verify item 24 and its marriage date; search the actual Franklin County, Indiana, 1966 register/license applications for Edna Bailey and Homer Brewster (including name variants); and identify Homer's earlier first marriage before Judith through the Tennessee and Ohio record routes already documented in Homer's own research file. Keep Homer-specific research and records in Homer's folder; this Edna log records only the contradiction relevant to evaluating Edna's tree claim.

**Current conclusion for Edna:** the 18 June 1966 Homer Brewster marriage remains an unsupported online-tree claim and is now in direct conflict with the recorded marriage-order sequence. It should be treated as probably a tree error unless an original Indiana record proves otherwise.
