# Homer Brewster–Edna S. Bailey relationship reference

Homer William Brewster Jr.'s archive contains the canonical copy of the Ohio marriage/divorce index extract: [Homer's Ohio marriage/divorce index extract](https://github.com/RickBrew13/american-bloodline/blob/main/homer-william-brewster-jr/sources/Homer-Ohio-marriage-divorce-index.pdf).

That extract records Homer and Judith's Hamilton County divorce on 2 May 1968, noting Homer had been married twice and Judith once. The Gen V profile for Edna Sharon Bailey reports a FamilySearch-tree claim of a 18 June 1966 marriage to Homer in Franklin, Indiana, but says the marriage is unsourced and unresolved; that date overlaps the documented 1956–1968 Homer–Judith marriage period. The Ohio index extract does not establish an Edna–Homer marriage. Keep the family-tree entry explicitly uncertain; do not treat it as documented or resolved by the Ohio index.

Reference note dated 2026-11-24 during Generation V archive transfer. The source index extract is in Homer's archive; the Drive couple folder had no separate Edna folder or identified Edna-specific record in its listing.
