Life Story

Edna Sharon Bailey was born on 6 December 1939 in Jackson, Kentucky. The Kentucky birth index names her as Edna S. Bailey, daughter of Cleo Hunter; no father is named in that record. The family tree names her parents as James Boyd Bailey and Cleo Marian Hunter, married 15 April 1939 at McKee, Jackson County, Kentucky — a marriage the county records confirm. Her middle name, Sharon, is spelled out only in the tree; the birth index gives only the initial S.

A FamilySearch tree claims that Edna married Homer William Brewster Jr. on 18 June 1966 in Franklin, Indiana. No independent marriage record has been located; the date and place rest on the tree alone. The claim also conflicts with the indexed record timeline: Homer and Judith Mae Palmer's marriage was not dissolved until 2 May 1968, and Edna's tree-reported death date of 29 December 1967 falls before that divorce. The Edna–Homer marriage therefore remains an unverified and chronologically conflicting family-tree claim, not an established relationship.

Nothing else is known of Edna's life. No death record for her has been found in the Ohio death index under either Bailey or Brewster, and no burial place is recorded. No photograph of her has been found yet, and no children of hers are recorded. The records that would settle the open questions include the Franklin County, Indiana, marriage record for 1966 and an Ohio death certificate. A dedicated FamilySearch collection, Indiana, Franklin County, Marriage Records, 1853–1997, includes the year 1966 and must be searched directly; no couple-specific result has yet been obtained. The death certificate has not been located.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Marriage: no independent marriage record for Homer and Edna has yet been verified. The date and place — 18 June 1966, Franklin, Indiana — come from the family tree alone, with no source attached. A dedicated FamilySearch collection covers Franklin County marriages from 1853 through 1997, including 1966; it is a direct search route and no couple-specific result has yet been retrieved. (FamilySearch collection)
  • Overlap: the tree's marriage date, 18 June 1966, falls inside Homer's first marriage to Judith Mae Palmer (18 April 1956 – 2 May 1968). The tree's death date for Edna, 29 December 1967, also falls before that divorce, so the whole marriage as dated in the tree overlaps the first marriage. This conflict is not resolved.
  • Death: no death record for Edna has been found. The Ohio death index has no entry for Edna Brewster or Edna Bailey in 1967. The tree's date and place — 29 December 1967, Cincinnati — are unsourced.
  • Middle name: "Sharon" is spelled out only in the family tree. The birth index calls her "Edna S Bailey."
  • Father: the birth index names only her mother, Cleo Hunter. James Boyd Bailey as her father rests on the family tree.
  • Cleo's birth year: the tree gives 31 March 1920, but the 1939 marriage record gives her age as 21, which points to about 1917–1918.
  • What would settle it: the original Franklin County, Indiana, marriage record or license for 1966, and an Ohio death certificate or other independent record supporting Edna's reported 1967 death. The Franklin County FamilySearch collection covers 1853–1997 and needs a direct search; the existence of that collection does not establish that the claimed marriage occurred.
