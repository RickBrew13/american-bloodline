      Life Story

      Bertha M. Kissel is identified in Tricia’s Shamblen/McKinney Ancestry tree as the wife of Otto Graff Sr. and mother of Otto Graff, reportedly born in Cincinnati on 5 May 1925. The tree reports Bertha about 1902–1989, but its underlying records have not been inspected. Her parents, siblings, exact vital dates and place, and any other children remain unknown.

      The MyHeritage 1930 Silverton snippet is not a census record transcription and does not identify Bertha. The directory snippets for 3933 Kirkup are contradicted by checked Cincinnati directories: George D. Bercher is listed there in 1935, and Alex Stesch (Grace E.) in 1940. No verified portrait or image of Bertha has been identified.

      Research Notes and Open Questions

      23 November 2026 — a four-leg search of free records looked for Bertha’s parents and siblings in the 1910–1950 censuses, Ohio births and deaths, Cincinnati city directories, obituaries, and marriage indexes. No parents and no siblings were found, and no 1930 or 1940 census household for Otto Sr., Bertha, and their son turned up. The strongest candidate household is Gottfried Kissel and Elizabeth Guenthner (or Guenther), whose sons were Fred Gottfried, born 24 April 1886 in Winton Place, and Robert, born May 1900; a daughter born about 1902 would fit, but no record names a Bertha as their daughter, and the 1910 and 1920 censuses still need to be checked. A Bertha Graff born 27 March 1897 and died 1 January 1975 is buried at Spring Grove Cemetery, Section 114, Lot 53, Space 2, but her dates do not match the tree’s dates for Bertha, and no link to Otto Sr. or to the Kissel name has been found; who else lies in that lot is not known. No marriage record for Otto Sr. and Bertha has been found; a marriage about 1924 in Hamilton County is only an inference from their son’s 1925 birth. The son Otto’s dates are in conflict: the tree says he was born 5 May 1925 and died 25 August 2004 in Cincinnati, while the Social Security Death Index, NUMIDENT, and Find a Grave Index agree on 11 November 1925 and 5 January 2004, with NUMIDENT adding a birthplace of North Bergen, New Jersey and burial at Wildwood Cemetery, Beckley, West Virginia; the conflict is unresolved and no date on this page has been changed. Still open: a signed-in search of the 1910 and 1920 censuses for a Bertha Kissel born about 1902, the son’s 1925 birth record, and the Ohio death index for 1989.
