## Findings

Powell, born 19 May 1911, with place of birth Baltimore, Maryland; the record is filed in Harrison County. I inspected the original Register of Births scan (film 847188, frame 503, book page 163). The child entry appears as Rowland E. Powell; the father field appears to read **D. L. Powell**, and the mother field reads **Minnie Powell**. Rowland’s Find a Grave memorial reports the same full name, date, and birthplace. **Working identity/parent assignment: about 85% confidence; the record directly names parents but does not expand D. L.’s initials or establish a marriage between the adults.**
- **Margaret’s parentage:** the Margaret memorial names Adolphe Monad Glover and Alice Louise Sellers Glover; each parent memorial also lists Margaret as a child. This is a consistent but derivative memorial family group, likely not independent corroboration. Working assignment about 70%, not established by reviewed primary records.
- **Children:** Helen Claire Powell/Shamblen’s SS-5 transcriptions identify Rowland E. Powell as father and Irene Glover / Margaret I. Glover as mother. Martha “Marcie” Powell Atha’s 2020 obituary names Rowland and Irene Glover Powell as parents and Helen Claire Powell Shamblin as her sister. This makes Martha a supported daughter/sibling connection, not merely a memorial guess. Helen’s birth data conflict: the current site/Geni-derived profile gives 6 Aug 1935 in Sanford, Florida; the SS-5 transcription gives 13 Mar 1935 in Huntington, West Virginia (the transcribed place string is truncated). Do not collapse the conflict without the original application/birth record.
- **Marriage:** MyHeritage search-result text reports a 1933 Maryland marriage between Rowland and Margaret. The page is inaccessible in this pass and the original record was not inspected. West Virginia Culture’s online marriage query for the exact names and 1933 returned no result; that does not disprove a marriage in Maryland. Keep the year/location as a lead, not a fact.
- **Deaths/burial:** Find a Grave reports Rowland’s death 25 Oct 1977 and Margaret’s death 23 Mar 1987, both buried at Marmet Memorial Gardens. West Virginia Culture’s online death collection ends in 1973, so it cannot verify either death year.
