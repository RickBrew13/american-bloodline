# Rowland Edmund Powell × Margaret Irene Glover — couple research continuation

**Research date:** 25 November 2026 (America/New_York)

## Findings

- **Rowland’s birth and father:** West Virginia birth-index entry WVID 134534 identifies Rowland E. Powell, born 19 May 1911, place of birth Baltimore, Maryland; record filed Harrison County. The original register (film 847188, frame 503, book page 163) was inspected. The father’s handwriting now appears to read **D. G. Powell** (not D. L.); the mother is **Minnie Powell**. The register does not spell out the father’s full name.
- **Full-name identification:** Original Harrison County marriage-license image and index WVID 5021 identify **Dexter G. Powell** and **Minnie M. Lyon**, married 1 July 1903. The license names Dexter’s parents Ralph M. Powell and Caroline Powell, and Minnie’s parent Andrew Lyon. The shared D. G. initials, Powell surname and Minnie / Minnie M. Lyon pairing strongly identify Dexter as Rowland’s father and Minnie as his mother: **about 90% working confidence**, not an explicit full-name parent entry. No census or separate child record yet supplies a direct bridge from Rowland to the couple.
- **Birth location distinction:** Rowland’s indexed birth place is Baltimore, Maryland; Harrison is the county where the West Virginia record is filed. Preserve both.
- **Margaret’s parentage:** Find a Grave memorial 97191604 links Adolphe Monad Glover and Alice Louise Sellers Glover as Margaret’s parents; their memorials also list Margaret. Working assignment remains about 70%, based on user-contributed derivative memorials, not reviewed primary proof.
- **Children:** Helen’s SS-5 transcription names Rowland E. Powell and Irene Glover / Margaret I. Glover. Martha “Marcie” Powell Atha’s 2020 obituary names Rowland and Irene Glover Powell as parents and says Helen Claire Powell Shamblin was her sister. Helen’s birth conflict (6 Aug 1935, Sanford, Florida, versus 13 Mar 1935, Huntington, West Virginia in the truncated SS-5 transcription) remains unresolved.
- **Rowland and Margaret’s reported marriage:** MyHeritage snippet reports a 1933 Maryland marriage; original record not examined. Keep it as an unverified lead.
- Find a Grave reports Rowland’s death 25 Oct 1977 and Margaret’s 23 Mar 1987; original death records not inspected.
