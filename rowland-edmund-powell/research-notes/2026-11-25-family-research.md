# Rowland Edmund Powell × Margaret Irene Glover — couple research continuation

**Research date:** 25 November 2026 (America/New_York)

## Findings

- **Rowland’s birth:** West Virginia birth-index entry WVID 134534 identifies Rowland E. Powell, born 19 May 1911, birthplace Baltimore, Maryland; record filed in Harrison County. The index does not show parent names. In the original register (film 847188, frame 503, page 163), the mother appears as Minnie Powell. Father handwriting is ambiguous: an earlier reading was D. L.; reinspection may be D. G. Do not treat either middle initial as settled.
- **Full-name candidate for father:** Harrison County marriage index WVID 5021 records Dexter G. Powell and Minnie M. Lyon marrying on 1 July 1903. OCR/transcription of the original license appears instead to read Dexter J. Powell, possibly S. The original license is difficult to read. Dexter Powell is a plausible full-given-name candidate for Rowland’s father because of the shared surname, matching D. initial and Minnie/Minnie M. Lyon pairing. **Working confidence about 75%; not proven**, as no census or other child record explicitly links Rowland to this couple and the father’s middle initial conflicts.
- The license OCR appears to give groom age 52, bride age 32, groom birthplace Doddridge County, and Harrison County residence; treat these as scan/OCR readings, not verified biographical facts until the handwriting is examined directly.
- **Margaret’s parentage:** Find a Grave memorial links Adolphe Monad Glover and Alice Louise Sellers Glover as parents. This remains a roughly 70% memorial-based working assignment, not established by reviewed primary records.
- **Children:** Helen’s SS-5 transcription names Rowland and Irene Glover / Margaret I. Glover; Martha “Marcie” Powell Atha’s 2020 obituary names Rowland and Irene Glover Powell as parents and Helen as sister. Helen’s date/place conflict remains unresolved (6 Aug 1935, Sanford, Florida, versus 13 Mar 1935, Huntington, West Virginia in the truncated transcription).
- Rowland and Margaret’s reported 1933 Maryland marriage is still only a MyHeritage snippet; original record not reviewed.
- Find a Grave reports Rowland’s death 25 Oct 1977 and Margaret’s 23 Mar 1987; original death records not reviewed.
