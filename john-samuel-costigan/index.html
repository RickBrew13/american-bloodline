Life Story

John Samuel Costigan was born in Kentucky on 30 June 1868 . He and his wife Amelia Friend raised a family that included William Swope, Charles, and Alice . He died in Scott County, Indiana, on 18 September 1955, eight months after Amelia, and is buried in Scottsburg Cemetery .
