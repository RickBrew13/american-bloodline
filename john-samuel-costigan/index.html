Life Story

John Samuel Costigan was born in Kentucky on 30 June 1868 SUPPORTED. He and his wife Amelia Friend raised a family that included William Swope, Charles, and Alice SUPPORTED. He died in Scott County, Indiana, on 18 September 1955, eight months after Amelia, and is buried in Scottsburg Cemetery SUPPORTED.
