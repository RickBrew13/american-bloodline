Life Story

Herman Washington was born in Tennessee about 1905, the son of William and Cecelia Washington SUPPORTED. On 13 February 1930 he married Pansy Robertson across the river from Cincinnati in Newport, Kentucky DOCUMENTED, and the couple settled in Cincinnati, where their children were born through the 1930s DOCUMENTED. In 1940 he headed a household of seven in Ward 7 DOCUMENTED. By 1950 he and Pansy were separated DOCUMENTED, and his later life and death have not yet been found.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Death and burial not yet found. FamilySearch death indexes for Ohio show no Herman or Heman Washington born about 1905 in Tennessee (checked 15 November 2026).
  • Find his birth record or a Tennessee census household with parents William and Cecelia Washington.
  • His son’s Social Security record spells the name Heman; the censuses and marriage record use Herman.
  • Harold Russell Washington is proved to be Herman and Pansy’s son by the 1940 and 1950 censuses and his Social Security record. That he is the same Harold who fathered Brandon Emil Niles, Sr. rests on the family account and his birth month; no record yet names Brandon or Leslie Mae Niles with this Harold.
  • Pansy is recorded as separated in 1950 and Herman is not in her household. Whether Herman is the father of Yvonne (about 1945) and Barbara (about 1947) is not established.
