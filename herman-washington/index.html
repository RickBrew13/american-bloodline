Life Story

Herman Washington was born in Memphis, Tennessee, about 1905, the son of William and Cecelia Washington. On 13 February 1930 he married Pansy Robertson across the river from Cincinnati in Newport, Kentucky, and the couple settled in Cincinnati, where their children were born through the 1930s. In 1940 he headed a household of seven in Ward 7. By 1950 he and Pansy were separated, and his later life and death have not yet been found.

Additional Quick-Fact Details

  • Also known as: Heman Washington (son Harold’s Social Security record)
  • Married: 13 February 1930, Newport, Campbell County, Kentucky, to Pansy Robertson
  • Lived: Cincinnati, Hamilton County, Ohio, 1930–1940 (Ward 7 in 1940)
  • Death and burial not yet found. FamilySearch death indexes for Ohio, Kentucky and Tennessee show no Herman or Heman Washington born about 1905–1906 in Tennessee (re-checked 26 November 2026); the Ohio Death Index has coverage gaps 1933–1937 and 1945–1957, so a death in a gap year would not appear. No death certificate image exists on FamilySearch for his sons Herbert (d. 30 Dec 2000, cert. 107059) or Harold (d. 15 Jul 2006, cert. 002555) — index entries only. Harold is buried at Vine Street Hill Cemetery, Cincinnati.
  • Three checks on his parents, 26 November 2026: the Newport marriage register images 235 and 237 are other couples only, and no parents’ ages exist anywhere — the license form has no such field and the consent forms are blank. His son Herbert’s 1953 marriage abstract names mother Pansy Robertson but the father’s field repeats the groom’s own name, “Herbert Washington” — Herman is not named there; Herbert’s Social Security record does name Herman as father. Full log: Washington three-checks pass (26 November 2026).
  • Find his birth record or a Tennessee census household with parents William and Cecelia Washington.
  • His son’s Social Security record spells the name Heman; the censuses and marriage record use Herman.
  • Harold Russell Washington is proved to be Herman and Pansy’s son by the 1940 and 1950 censuses and his Social Security record. That he is the same Harold who fathered Brandon Emil Niles, Sr. rests on the family account and his birth month; no record yet names Brandon or Leslie Mae Niles with this Harold.
  • Pansy is recorded as separated in 1950 and Herman is not in her household. Whether Herman is the father of Yvonne (about 1945) and Barbara (about 1947) is not established.
