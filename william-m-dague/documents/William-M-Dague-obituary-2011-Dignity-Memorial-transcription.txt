Full text (as published):

"William M. Dague, age 73, of Columbus died Thursday, March 31, 2011 at Riverside
Methodist Hospital. He retired from National Electric Coil and attended St. Matthias
Catholic Church. He is preceded in death by his parents Woodrow and Ruth Dague; son
Dwayne Dague; and brother, Robert A. Dague. He is survived by his grandchildren,
Brian and Amber; cousin, Emilie Dague Crawford and various other cousins. Funeral
service will be held at 11:30 am, Wednesday April 6, 2011 at Schoedinger North
Chapel, 5554 Karl Rd., where friends will be received from 10:00 am until the time
of service. Fr. James Smith officiating. Interment at St. Joseph's Cemetery. In
lieu of flowers, contributions in William's name may be made to the Central Ohio
Diabetes Association, 1100 Dennison Ave., Columbus, Oh. 43201. Please visit
www.schoedinger.com to share a favorite memory of William."
