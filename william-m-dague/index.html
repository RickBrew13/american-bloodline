Life Story

William M. Dague is named as the father of Dewayne Miles Dague in Dewayne's Social Security record, with Dorothy Ann McCartt as his mother. Their son was born in Columbus, Ohio, on 17 December 1964. He was born on 21 August 1937 to Woodrow W. and Ruth A. (Kevelder) Dague, the same day as his brother Robert, and died on 31 March 2011; he is buried at St. Joseph Cemetery in Lockbourne. His father's 2001 obituary names his son William and grandson Dwayne.

He retired from National Electric Coil and attended St. Matthias Catholic Church in Columbus. His funeral service was held at 11:30 am on Wednesday, 6 April 2011, at Schoedinger North Chapel, 5554 Karl Rd., Columbus, with visitation from 10:00 am and Fr. James Smith officiating; in lieu of flowers, contributions in his name were asked for the Central Ohio Diabetes Association. His obituary names his surviving grandchildren Brian and Amber, and his cousin Emilie Dague Crawford.

Dewayne's daughter remembers that William disowned his son Dewayne, who was born with red hair. When she and her brother were little, Dewayne's family came to William's house in Columbus for Christmas and was turned away. About a year before Dewayne died in 2002, he found William's phone number and they talked a few times, until William blocked his number. She says it was the only time she ever saw her father cry, and that day he promised he would never do that to his own children.
