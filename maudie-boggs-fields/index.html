Life Story

Maudie (Boggs) Fields is identified as Dell Fields’s mother on Dell’s Find a Grave memorial. The separate memorial for Cora Lee (Fields) Smith also names Maudie and Arlie as her parents and Dell as her brother. A FamilySearch 1950 census index links a Dell Fields and Cora Lee Fields to Arlie and Maudie in Letcher County, Kentucky, and names four other children in the household. The convergence supports these relationships, but the original census schedule and civil vital records have not yet been inspected.

Find a Grave reports Maudie’s birth as 25 September 1908 in Leslie County, Kentucky, death on 22 October 1983 in Cumberland, Kentucky, and burial at Maggard Cemetery. Her memorial identifies parents Henry Boggs and Cora Lee Elkins, spouse Arlie Blair Fields (married 1927), nine siblings, and two linked children. These remain compiled memorial claims unless independently verified in original records.
