Life Story

David Clayton Shamblen was born in Jackson County, West Virginia, the son of George W. Shamblen and Sydna Ann Miller. His birth year is not settled: his age of 21 on his 1888 marriage license points to about 1867, family lists give 26 May 1869, and his death index age of 76 years, 9 months, 20 days points to about June 1870. No birth record has been found.

He married Sarah Louise Staats on 31 May 1888 at her father William F. Staats's residence, by A. S. Blagg, a United Brethren minister. The license, issued 26 May 1888, records the bride as "Louisa Stoots," the clerk's phonetic rendering of Staats. David farmed at Fairplain, at the corner of Charleston Road and Given Road, and sold the farm to his son Arvel on installments in the late 1930s and 1940s.

David and Sarah had nine children. After Sarah's death in 1933, David married a second time; Geni links his second wife as Eva Shamblen (Martin). David died on 16 March 1947 and is buried at Fairview Cemetery, Fairplain, where a shared headstone names him and Sarah. No portrait of him has been found.
