Family Relationship Notes

The 1940 census index lists Leroy Moore, age 42 and born in Iowa, in Detroit Ward 6, Wayne County, with wife Mila Moore, age 23 and born in Indiana; sons Patrick, age four, and Michael, age two, are listed in the household. The index gives the household on ED 84-246, sheet 13B, line 63. It reports both Leroy and Mila as White; that is the enumerator’s category, not a determination of ancestry or tribal affiliation.

The 1950 census original pages were reviewed. On sheet 27A, the FamilySearch index transcribes the handwritten head as “Leray C. Moore,” age 52, married, living at 3337 in Dearborn, Wayne County, with wife Elizabeth, age 39. Leroy is listed as a grinder in the auto-manufacturing industry. The household continues onto sheet 28A: Patrick L. (14), Michael J. (12), and Gary W. (infant; the original schedule records “Dec 1949” in the age/birth entry) are recorded as sons. Raymond J. Kroft and Isabell Kroft on sheet 27A, and Florence M. and Marvin E. on sheet 28A, are explicitly labeled stepson/stepdaughter. The schedule does not name the four stepchildren’s biological father; they must not be counted as Leroy’s biological children or assigned to Mila. The 1950 index records both Leroy’s father’s and mother’s birthplaces as USA, but gives neither parent’s name. It does not identify Mila’s fate, establish whether Elizabeth was a later wife, or name the Moore boys’ mother.

Family oral history reports that Leroy met his first wife, Mila, during World War I and describes Mila as “full-blood Native American”; her tribe is not known. This remains attributed family testimony, not a documented ancestry, enrollment, or blood degree. The 1940 census estimate places Mila’s birth about 1916–1917, which conflicts chronologically with a meeting during World War I. Do not resolve the contradiction by changing her age, identity, or the family account without new evidence.

Parents and siblings remain unidentified. Compiled-tree suggestions for Orrin C. Moore and Mary C. Randall Moore are unconfirmed and are not assigned as parents. A MyHeritage search-result snippet describes a Leroy William Moore born in Burlington, Iowa, in 1898 to E. L. Moore and “Elizabrth,” and reports a 1965 Boise death; the site blocked access to the underlying record, and no identity bridge to this Leroy was found. It is a separate unverified candidate, not a profile fact.

Life Story

Leroy C. Moore, indexed as Leroy in 1940 and “Leray C. Moore” in 1950, was born in Iowa about 1897–1898 according to the census ages. The 1940 Detroit census index lists wife Mila and sons Patrick and Michael. The 1950 Dearborn census images show a wife named Elizabeth and continue the household across sheets 27A and 28A, listing Patrick L., Michael J., and Gary W. as sons, as well as several stepchildren. The records establish two different wife names in the household snapshots but do not explain what happened to Mila or identify the boys’ mother in 1950. Family oral history says Leroy met first wife Mila during World War I and describes her as Native American; this is unverified family testimony and conflicts with the 1940 age-based birth estimate for Mila. Her tribal affiliation is unknown. Leroy’s parents, siblings, marriage records, death, and burial remain unidentified.
