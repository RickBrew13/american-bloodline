Family Relationship Notes

The 1940 census lists Leroy Moore, age 42 and born in Iowa, in Detroit Ward 6, Wayne County, with wife Mila Moore, age 23 and born in Indiana; sons Patrick, age four, and Michael, age two, are listed in the household. The household is on ED 84-246, sheet 13B. The 1950 census gives his birthplace as South Dakota, which conflicts with the 1940 census. Both censuses report Leroy and Mila as White; that is the enumerator’s category, not a determination of ancestry or tribal affiliation.

Mila Christina Jones Moore died in Dearborn, Wayne County, Michigan, on 20 December 1947, at age 31. Her death, not a divorce, ended the marriage. The 1950 census original pages show Leroy in Dearborn with his second wife Elizabeth, age 39, born in North Dakota. Their son Gary W. Moore, born in December 1949, is Elizabeth’s son, not Mila’s. The household continues onto sheet 28A: Patrick L. (14), Michael J. (12), and Gary W. (infant) are recorded as sons. Raymond J. Kroft, Isabell Kroft, Florence M. Kroft, and Marvin E. Kroft are explicitly labeled stepson/stepdaughter; all four are Kroft, and they must not be counted as Leroy’s biological children or assigned to Mila.

Family oral history reports that Leroy met his first wife, Mila, during World War I and describes Mila as “full-blood Native American”; her tribe is not known. Mila was born in 1916, so she was an infant during World War I, and the meeting account as told does not stand. The 1950 census index lists Leroy as a World War I veteran, but no draft card or service record has been found.

Parents and siblings remain unidentified. The compiled-tree suggestion that Orrin C. Moore and Mary C. Randall Moore were his parents does not hold: the 1900 census household in Adair County, Iowa, is Orrin C. Moore with his wife Ida Alice Havens and sons Leslie and Lyle, with no Leroy; and Mary C. Randall belongs to a different Orrin, Orestus Clement Moore of Plymouth County, Iowa, whose son was Leroy Seaborn Moore, a different man. The death candidates found in the search are also ruled out: the Kalamazoo man was Leroy Henry Moore, born in Michigan, and the Idaho man was Leroy William Moore, born in Burlington, Iowa, while this Leroy was living in Detroit and Dearborn in 1940 and 1950. A possible lead, not proven, is a Leroy Moore born 27 September 1898 in Butte, South Dakota, son of John Moore and Mary Murphy; another is Leroy G. Moore in Raritan, Day County, South Dakota, in 1910.

Life Story

Leroy C. Moore, indexed as Leroy in 1940 and “Leray C. Moore” in 1950, was born about 1897–1898; the 1940 census gives his birthplace as Iowa and the 1950 census gives it as South Dakota, and no birth record has been found. The 1940 Detroit census lists his wife Mila and sons Patrick and Michael. Mila, born Mila Christina Jones, died in Dearborn on 20 December 1947, at age 31, and her death ended the marriage. The 1950 Dearborn census images show Leroy with his second wife Elizabeth and sons Patrick, Michael, and Gary, the youngest born in December 1949 to Elizabeth, as well as four stepchildren, all Kroft. Family oral history says Leroy met his first wife Mila during World War I and describes her as Native American; Mila was born in 1916, so that account as told does not stand, and no draft card or service record has been found for Leroy, though the 1950 census index lists him as a World War I veteran. Leroy’s parents, siblings, marriage records, death, and burial remain unidentified.
