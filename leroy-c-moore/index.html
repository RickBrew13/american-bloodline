Family Relationship Notes

The 1940 census lists Leray C. Moore, age 42, in Detroit Ward 6, Wayne County, Michigan, with wife Mila Moore, age 23, and sons Patrick, age four, and Michael, age two. The census is the basis for the estimated birth year and the two child links.

The 1950 census lists Leroy C. Moore in Dearborn, Michigan, with a wife named Elizabeth and son Patrick L. Moore. Mila is not in the household. It is not yet known whether Elizabeth is Mila under another name, a later wife, or another household relationship. The spouse names are kept separate pending further records.

Some compiled trees suggest Orrin C. Moore and Mary C. Randall Moore as Leroy’s parents, but no record reviewed connects them to this Leroy. They remain unconfirmed candidates and are not listed in the Parents card.

No death, burial, or additional family records have been located for Leroy. His death date, burial place, parents, and siblings remain unknown.
