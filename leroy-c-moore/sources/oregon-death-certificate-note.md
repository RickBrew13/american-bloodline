# Paid source note — Oregon death certificate for Leroy Moore

**Record:** Death certificate, Leroy Moore, died 5 May 1982, Multnomah County
(Portland), Oregon. Certificate 82-08359. Born 27 Sep 1898.

**What it would settle:** Whether the Portland Leroy — born 27 Sep 1898 in Butte,
South Dakota, parents John Moore × Mary Murphy (South Dakota birth index) — is our
Leroy C. Moore of Detroit/Dearborn, Michigan (wives Mila Christina Jones, d. 1947,
and Elizabeth; sons Patrick, Michael, Gary). The certificate names the man's parents,
spouse, and informant. If it names John Moore × Mary Murphy as parents, or Mila /
Elizabeth as spouse, the Butte lead is PROVEN and Leroy's parents are found. If it
names a different family, the lead is KILLED.

Available" and names no spouse, parents, or informant. Free in-person alternative:
