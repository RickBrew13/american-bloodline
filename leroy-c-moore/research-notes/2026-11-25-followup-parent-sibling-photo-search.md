The sheet does not name the Kroft children’s biological father or explicitly name their mother beyond Elizabeth being the wife in the household. Treat those relationship labels as the enumerator’s classifications, not proof of Kroft parentage. Do not count any of the four stepchildren as Leroy’s biological children, nor as Mila’s children.

The public FamilySearch detail pages for Raymond and Isabell organize household members into “parents/siblings” tables that show Leroy as father and Elizabeth as mother, but the same records’ relationship-to-head fields explicitly call them stepson/stepdaughter. The primary schedule’s step-relationship is the stronger and more specific evidence. This is a data-linkage/household-clustering discrepancy, not evidence to silently assign Leroy as their biological father.

The 1950 FamilySearch index for Leroy records both his father’s and mother’s birthplace as USA. It names neither parent. This is a useful boundary clue only; it cannot identify parents or siblings.
