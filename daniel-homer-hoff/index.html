Life Story

Daniel Homer Hoff and Elmanza Clementine Reed Hoff raised a large family in Roane County, West Virginia. Harry Earl Hoff’s 1892 birth register names Daniel and Elmanza; the 1910 household transcription lists their children Grover, Earl, Laura, Atlee, Grace, and Darrell. A separate captioned family-group photo names Daniel H. Hoff and Elmanza (Reed) Hoff, alongside Harold and Paul Hoff; those younger names are not assigned a relationship here.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The 1910 household transcript reports 12 births and 10 surviving children, but its named list is incomplete relative to the shared FamilySearch graph. The sibling list above therefore reflects a compiled graph, not 13 independently proven children.
  • The shared tree gives simultaneous 1887 birth years for Grover Cleveland and Flora B.; it lists both Jasper B. and Harry Earl with the identical 25 February 1892 date. These could reflect a twin, a date error, or merged records; no conclusion is made.
  • Family memorial parent/sibling links remain derivative. The 1910 transcription and Harry’s birth register are more direct for Daniel’s household/parentage.
  • The family photo caption names Harold and Paul Hoff; no evidence reviewed ties either name to Daniel as son, grandson, or another relation.
