Life Story

John Samuel Hill was born on 3 October 1861 in Bracken County, Kentucky, the son of George Hill and Catharine Kiskaden DOCUMENTED. In 1884 he married Sarah Alice Hill, a neighbor's daughter, and they had their first children in Kentucky DOCUMENTED.

By 1900 the family had crossed into Ohio and was farming in Jefferson Township, Clinton County, where their daughter Maggie was born in 1902 DOCUMENTED. John farmed in Clinton and Warren Counties and died at Blanchester on 14 February 1940. He is buried at Westboro DOCUMENTED.
