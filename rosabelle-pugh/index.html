Family Relationship Notes

Ruby Mae Pugh’s 15 November 1939 Alabama marriage index names Cornelius Pugh and Rosabelle Grant as her parents. Ruby’s 5 February 1945 marriage index is cited as naming Cornelius Pugh and Rosa Bell White. Ruby’s 1930 census index is reported to show Ruby M. Pugh, age 7, with Rosabelle Pugh in the Shelton and Mattie Willis household in Birmingham. These records support investigating the same woman as Ruby’s mother, but the 1930 index image and the two marriage images have not been inspected in this pass; surname forms remain unresolved.

The compiled-profile birth date 11 July 1922 is incompatible with the reported 1930 age-7 daughter relationship: a woman born in 1922 would herself have been about seven or eight in 1930. Treat the 1922 date as an unverified, conflicting claim, not an established fact. The reported 10 May 2001 death is likewise Geni-derived and unverified.

The 1939 and 1945 records are Ruby’s marriages, not Rosabelle’s. They do not document Rosabelle’s marriage to Lawrence “Larry” Pugh or James White. The reported 1950 Birmingham household with James White was not corroborated by the bounded NARA name/OCR search for Rosabelle/Rosabell in Jefferson County. That index is imperfect, so the non-result is not proof that no such household existed.

The NARA images returned other women, not a clear match: a 15-year-old Rosabelle listed as a daughter on sheet 22 of ED 37-16, a 9-year-old Rosabell daughter on sheet 9 of ED 37-20, and a 43-year-old Rosabell Berry listed as head on sheet 4 of ED 37-112. Do not attach any of them to Ruby’s mother.

Compiled family trees naming William Grant and Mattie Lee as Rosabelle’s parents remain unverified. No birth, death, marriage, or census record directly identifying those parents has been reviewed.

A public FamilySearch Memories entry tagged “Rosabelle Grant” is dated 5 October 1985; search-index text describes a 75th-birthday photo with a dog named Fred. This community-contributed memory suggests a possible birth around 1910, but the page date and search description are not a vital record and do not prove the tagged woman is Ruby’s mother. It is a lead only; the reported 1922 birth date remains unsupported and conflicts with the alleged 1930 mother–daughter household.

The public FamilySearch Memories entry tagged “Rosabelle Grant” (item 220372990) is dated 5 October 1985. Its associated listing describes a 75th-birthday photo with a dog named Fred. The image is preserved in this profile’s Family Photographs folder as a candidate lead. It is not yet verified that the woman pictured is Ruby’s mother, and it is not used as the profile portrait.
