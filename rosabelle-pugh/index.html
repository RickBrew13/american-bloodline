Family Relationship Notes

Ruby’s 1930 U.S. census index reports Rosabelle Pugh, age 22, and Ruby M. Pugh, age 7, in the same Birmingham household. The prior research transcription gives household 208, sheet 13B, line 55. The original schedule has not been inspected. The index’s Willis parent inference conflicts with the ages and is not accepted as Ruby’s parentage.

Ruby’s 15 November 1939 marriage index reports Cornelius Pugh and Rosabelle Grant as her parents. Ruby’s 5 February 1945 marriage index is reported to name Cornelius and Rosa Bell White. These support investigating one mother across Pugh / Grant / White forms; the two original marriage images were not inspected here.

The existing Rosabelle profile already named William Grant and Mattie Lee as her parents, attributed to compiled family trees. Those exact names are now carried as linked Gen VII working assignments. Their source tree identifiers were not preserved, and no original parent-child record has been located in this review. This does not establish that William and Mattie married.

No sibling, additional child, or spouse of Rosabelle is established in reviewed identity-linked records. Ruby’s marriage records are Ruby’s, not Rosabelle’s; they do not establish Cornelius, Lawrence “Larry” Pugh, or James White as her spouse. A reported 1950 Rosabelle White–James White household remains unverified.

Her parents are Pete White and Sallie Parker, named on her 1951 Alabama death record (certificate 16719). Pete and Sallie married on 2 December 1903 in Autauga County; the marriage index spells the bride “Sallie Smith,” which conflicts with “Sallie Parker” on the death record. The earlier William Grant and Mattie Lee parent assignment was a compiled-tree claim with no recovered citations and is superseded by the death record; it is carried in Research Notes, not on the card.

The FamilySearch Memories photo tagged Rosabelle Grant remains a candidate image only. It does not establish a birth year or prove that the woman pictured is Ruby’s mother.
