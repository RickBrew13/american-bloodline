# Cornelius and Rosabelle Pugh — couple research continuation

**Research date:** 25 November 2026 (America/New_York)
**Generation:** VI (live Generation VI order places Cornelius Pugh immediately with Rosabelle (Pugh / Grant / White)).

## Evidence for Ruby Mae Pugh’s parents

- Ruby’s 15 November 1939 Alabama County Marriages index for Joseph Collins identifies Cornelius Pugh and Rosabelle Grant as her parents (FamilySearch VZVK-B1Q; image identifier cited on Ruby’s profile as 939V-4BS4-6P). This is an index citation from the existing profile; original page was not inspected in this pass.
- Ruby’s 5 February 1945 Alabama marriage index for George Moore is cited as identifying Cornelius Pugh and Rosa Bell White as her parents (FamilySearch 29NV-B74). The item page/original image was not accessible in this pass.
- Ruby’s profile cites a 1930 census index (FamilySearch 32XF-JPZ) reporting Ruby M. Pugh, age 7, with Rosabelle Pugh in the Shelton and Mattie Willis household in Birmingham. Original image not reviewed in this pass.
- The two daughter-marriage indexes provide repeated support for Cornelius as Ruby’s father and preserve two variants of her mother’s name. They do not establish that Cornelius and Rosabelle were married to one another, and they do not establish either parent’s full identity or life dates.

## Rosabelle identity and chronology conflict

The profile’s prior compiled birth claim, 11 July 1922 in Birmingham, is incompatible with the reported 1930 census entry if Ruby age 7 is her daughter. A woman born in 1922 would herself have been about 7–8 in 1930. Treat the 1922 date as erroneous or misattributed until an original record resolves it; do not retain it as a fact. The reported 10 May 2001 death comes from a Geni profile and lacks an inspected original death record. A public FamilySearch Memories page tagged “Rosabelle Grant” is dated 5 October 1985; a search-index snippet describes it as a “75th Birthday with Fred (dog)” memory. The page itself is a contributed family memory, not a vital record; its displayed date may be the memory/photo date, and the 75th-birthday description has not been verified from an original caption. It is a useful lead toward a birth around 1910, which would fit the reported 1930 daughter relationship much better than 1922, but it does not establish Rosabelle’s birth year or prove the tagged woman is Ruby’s mother.

The surname variants—Rosabelle Pugh (reported in the 1930 index), Rosabelle Grant (Ruby’s 1939 marriage index), and Rosa Bell White (Ruby’s 1945 marriage index)—may describe one woman, but an explicit identity bridge is still needed. Family trees naming William Grant and Mattie Lee as her parents have not been supported by an original record review.

The 1939 and 1945 records are Ruby’s marriages, not Rosabelle’s. Therefore they do not prove Rosabelle married Lawrence “Larry” Pugh in 1939 or James White in 1945. The reported 1950 Birmingham Rosabelle White–James White household remains unverified.

## 1950 census exclusion check

The National Archives 1950 Census name/OCR search for Rosabelle/Rosabell and Rosa Belle in Jefferson County, Alabama, returned same-name sheets but no clear adult Rosabelle Pugh/White household matching Ruby’s mother. Original sheets inspected include:

- ED 37-16, sheet 22, schedule 48809: Rosabelle recorded as a daughter, age 15; not the adult mother in the reported Ruby household. - ED 37-112, sheet 4, schedule 51403: Rosabell Berry, age 43, listed as head; no Pugh/White or Ruby link. [Original image](https://1950census.archives.gov/iiif/2/1950census%2F43290879-Alabama%2F43290879-Alabama-119767%2F43290879-Alabama-119767-0005.jpg/full/2000,/0/default.jpg)
- Another result, ED 37-233, sheet 4, lists Rosa Bell E. as the wife of Edward Barlow, age 56, born Michigan; not a plausible match. [Original image](https://1950census.archives.gov/iiif/2/1950census%2F43290879-Alabama%2F43290879-Alabama-121735%2F43290879-Alabama-121735-0005.jpg/full/2000,/0/default.jpg)

The NARA index is OCR-based and acknowledged to be imperfect. This bounded search neither proves that Rosabelle was absent from the 1950 census nor disproves the reported James White household; it simply fails to corroborate that identity. Do not attach the same-name entries above.

## Same-name candidates and weak leads

An Ancestry public search result surfaced several Cornelius Pugh profiles associated with Alabama, including men reported born about 1900 with parents David H. Pugh and Sarah Elizabeth Bush, and another about 1899 with a different parent claim. The result page is not an original record and was blocked from direct review; none of these men has an identity bridge to Ruby. Do not assign them as Ruby’s father.

A FamilyTreeNow search-result snippet describes a Rosabelle Pugh born about 1908 in Birmingham and lists Cornelius Pugh and Ruby M. Pugh among family associations. The underlying page was blocked by a human-verification challenge, so its data source and relationship labels could not be checked. This is a useful lead because the approximate birth timing is more compatible with Ruby’s reported 1930 age than 1922, but it is not evidence and must not replace the unresolved birth date.
