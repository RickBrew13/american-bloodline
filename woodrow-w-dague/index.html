Life Story

Woodrow W. Dague was born on 29 November 1915, the son of Miles and Rose (Ritzer) Dague . He married Ruth A. Kevelder in Franklin County, Ohio, on 20 June 1936, and their sons William and Robert were born on the same day in August 1937 . He outlived Ruth by more than twelve years and died in Columbus on 6 March 2001 .
