# Margaret Irene Glover — Powell-side family research

**Research date:** 25 November 2026 (America/New_York)

## Findings

- Find a Grave memorial 97191604 reports Margaret Irene Glover Powell as born 31 March 1913 in Marion, West Virginia; died 23 March 1987 in Charleston, Kanawha County; and buried in Marmet Memorial Gardens. These exact dates/places remain memorial-reported, not verified by an original vital record.
- The memorial links **Adolphe Monad Glover and Alice Louise Sellers Glover** as parents. Adolphe’s own memorial and Alice’s memorial list Margaret among their children. The reciprocal family group is consistent but is user-contributed and may share a contributor/source; it is not independent primary evidence. Working relationship confidence about 70%, not proven.
- The memorial lists ten siblings: Ora May Glover Edgell (1891–1971); Roy Holden Glover (1892–1965); Howard Willis Glover (1896–1972); John Sellers Glover (1897–1979); Harry Nelson Glover (1899–1905); George Edgar Glover (1900–1976); Walter Monad Glover (1903–1998); Mildred Glover (1905–1906); Fred J. Glover (1906–1971); Dorothy Ruth Glover (1917–1920). These are memorial family links and remain unverified in original records.
- Helen Claire Powell/Shamblen’s SS-5 transcription identifies her mother as Irene Glover / Margaret I. Glover. Martha “Marcie” Powell Atha’s 2020 obituary names Rowland and Irene Glover Powell as parents and says Helen Claire Powell Shamblin was her sister.
- A MyHeritage search-result snippet reports a 1933 Maryland marriage to Rowland Edmund Powell. The underlying record was not reviewed; WV’s online marriage index returned no 1933 match for the exact names. Treat this as a lead only.
- A targeted West Virginia birth-index search for Margaret Glover, 1908–1918, returned Margaret R. Glover (1914, Marshall County) and Margaret Wanda Glover (1918, Wetzel County), but no exact Margaret Irene entry. Neither similar-name result is identified with this woman; this bounded index search is not proof of absence.
