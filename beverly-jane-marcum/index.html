Life Story

Beverly Jane Marcum was born 5 March 1956 in Ohio, the daughter of Harold C. Marcum and Etta F. McFadden, named as her parents on her marriage license. On 21 August 1982 she married Dell Michael Fields in Hamilton County, Ohio; the license gives the couple’s residence as 4931 Laurel Avenue, Blue Ash. The family identifies Beverly as the mother of Tara Michelle Marcum; that link rests on his word, not on a record — Tara’s 2011 marriage license redacts the mother’s maiden name, and her birth certificate would settle it.
