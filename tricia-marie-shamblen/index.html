Life Story

Tricia Marie Shamblen, born 22 April 1965 in Cincinnati, is the younger daughter of Lawrence Richard Shamblen and Ruth Laverne McKinney {SUP}. She grew up in Roselawn, Cincinnati, with her sister Susan {FAM}.

She became an international flight attendant for Delta {FAM}, and later lived in Seattle, where the family account says she trained dogs in prisons, sang in a band, and owned a wine shop {FAM}.

She married John David Woodhouse on 15 September 1990 at Kenwood Baptist Church, Cincinnati, and divorced in 1992 in Kentucky {SUP}; she married Michael William Calhoon on 2 October 1999 at Verrazano's restaurant, Federal Way, Washington {SUP}. She died on 13 May 2016 in Gig Harbor, Washington, and her ashes are held by the family {SUP}.
