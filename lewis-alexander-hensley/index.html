Life Story

The sources identify this man under both Alexander and Hensley. Find a Grave describes him as born Lewis Alexander and later using his mother’s surname Hensley. Its memorial connects him to Alonzo “Lon” Alexander, and the same memorial transcribes census households with Lewis and Sarah “Sallie” Robinson. The precise birth year is unresolved: the memorial reports 1847, while its own 1850, 1860, 1870, 1880, 1900, and 1910 census summaries give ages that do not consistently support that year. The father link to Alonzo is supported by converging derivative family links and census transcriptions, but the original schedules and a direct birth record have not been reviewed.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The main memorial reports 1847, but its census-age transcriptions imply different birth years. Preserve the discrepancy rather than choosing one.
  • Parent names for Lewis (James Alexander and Mahala Hensley) are memorial-reported; no primary document linking Lewis to them was reviewed.
  • The Lewis/Sarah-to-Alonzo relationship is supported by a memorial family link and census transcription, not yet verified from the original 1880 image or a birth record.
  • A separate later marriage to Mary Grant is reported for Lewis. It is not evidence that Mary was Alonzo’s mother.
