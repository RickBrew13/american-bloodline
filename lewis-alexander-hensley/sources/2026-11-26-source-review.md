# Source review — Lewis Alexander (later Hensley)

Accessed 26 November 2026.

- [Lewis Hensley, Find a Grave memorial 221832036](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/221832036/lewis-hensley) — reports vital data, parentage, marriages and census transcriptions. Derivative, not original civil or census records.
- [Alonzo “Lon” Alexander, Find a Grave memorial 221840673](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/221840673/alonzo-alexander) — names Lewis Hensley and Sarah “Sallie” Robinson as parents; includes a transcription of 1880 and other censuses. Its attached image is an unrelated John A. Baldwin document and is excluded.
- [James Pharoah Hensley, Find a Grave memorial 182736232](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/182736232/james-pharoah-hensley) — gives Lewis Alexander Hensley and Sarah/Sally Robinson as parents in a memorial biography.
- [1920 U.S. Census index, Lon Alexander](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MN2J-DZF) — reports age 44, estimated birth 1876, Kentucky birthplace; this is for Alonzo, not his father.

No original census image for Lewis or original birth/marriage/death certificate was reviewed in this pass.
