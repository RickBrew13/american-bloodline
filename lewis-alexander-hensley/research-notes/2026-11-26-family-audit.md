# Research note — Lewis Alexander (later Hensley)

Reviewed 26 November 2026.

- The Find a Grave memorial reports Lewis Hensley (1847–1927), but its own transcribed census ages conflict with 1847 and instead cluster around about 1840–1842. Preserve the contradiction.
- The memorial says Lewis was born Alexander and later used his mother’s maiden surname Hensley; it names James Alexander and Mahala Hensley as his parents. Those parent names are derivative memorial claims; original records were not inspected.
- The Find a Grave memorial for Alonzo “Lon” Alexander names Lewis Hensley and Sarah “Sallie” Robinson as his parents. The 1880 census transcription lists a five-year-old L. Alexander in Lewis and Sallie’s household. This supports the parent link but does not replace an original census image or birth record.
- James Pharoah Hensley’s memorial likewise identifies Lewis Alexander Hensley and Sarah/Sally Robinson as parents and Alonzo as sibling. It is another derivative account, not an independent original record.
- Lewis’s later reported marriage to Mary Grant must remain separate from Alonzo’s mother Sarah.
- No verified portrait or accurately identified headstone image was found. The only Lewis memorial image is a document read as an unrelated Metro de Alicante form, so it is excluded.
