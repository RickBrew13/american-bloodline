# Edith Elizabeth Samples Hoff: source notes and transcription limits

Edith’s state birth index gives 18 Nov 1903. FamilySearch 1910 and 1920 census indexes place Edith E. Samples as daughter in Philander Samples’s household. In 1910 her age implies about 1905; in 1920 age 17 supports 1903. The 1940 census reports age 36. Parent pair Philander and Mary Ida is supported by the birth index family panel, successive census households, and compiled genealogies. Census mother initials vary (Ida M., Mary T.); preserve this.
