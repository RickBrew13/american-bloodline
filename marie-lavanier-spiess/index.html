Life Story

Marie L. (Lavanier) Spiess was the mother of David Michael Spiess and Ronald P. Spiess. Her maiden name Lavanier is given in her son Ronald’s obituary. In 1950 she was living in Williamsburg Township, Clermont County, Ohio, with her husband Philip A. Spiess and their sons Ronald, 15, and David, 7. DOCUMENTED

No birth date or place, marriage record, death date or place, or grave has been found for Marie. She is known only from the 1950 census household and her son Ronald’s obituary.
