Family Relationship Notes

Marie Louise Lavanier’s public FamilySearch profile (GQQ8-L5H) reports 4 June 1908–29 December 1993 and names August Lavanier and Mari Karch as her parents. A derivative SS-5/NUMIDENT transcription also reports August F. Lavanier and Mary/Mari Karch. This supports a working parent assignment (~75%), but neither the original birth certificate nor SS-5 application was reviewed.

Marie’s public profile names Philip Adam Spiess as spouse and lists an Ohio County Marriages source. The underlying marriage item was not available for inspection; the tree spouse link and shared 1950 household support the couple assignment, but the exact marriage date remains unknown here.

The 1950 census transcription lists Marie L. Spiess in a household with Philip and sons Ronald P. and David M. Ronald’s obituary names Marie as his mother and David as his brother. His obituary does not establish the paternity of every sibling named there.

FamilySearch counts seven children by Philip and Marie. Its visible family group names five and the timeline displays Mary Spiess and Betty J. O’Neal. Do not collapse the timeline Mary into listed Mary C. Spiess without an identity bridge; obituary names may be married forms, not proven aliases.
