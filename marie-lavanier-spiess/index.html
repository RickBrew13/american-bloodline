Family Relationship Notes

The 1950 census lists Marie L. Spiess in the same Williamsburg Township, Clermont County, Ohio, household as Philip A. Spiess and sons Ronald P. and David M. Ronald’s 2024 obituary names his mother as Marie Lavanier Spiess and identifies David as a brother. These records support the family relationships shown here.

Ronald’s obituary also names Debbie Duncan, Jackie Wells, and Phyllis Stump as siblings who died before him, and Mary Montgomery and Betty O’Neal as younger sisters. They do not appear in the 1950 household. Their relationship to Marie and Philip has not been independently established, so they are retained as research leads rather than listed as confirmed children.

No marriage record has been located for Marie and Philip. The census records them in one household as parents of Ronald and David, but does not itself prove a marriage. Marie’s parents, siblings, life dates, occupation, and burial remain unknown.

Life Story

Marie Louise Lavanier Spiess (4 June 1908–29 December 1993) is named as a daughter of August Lavanier and Mari Karch in her public FamilySearch tree and as the mother of seven children by Philip Adam Spiess. Her parent names are corroborated by multiple public family-group profiles and derivative SS-5/NUMIDENT transcriptions, but the original birth/SS-5 applications have not been inspected. One derivative index conflicts on the Karch parents and her birth location; the discrepancy is documented rather than reconciled by assumption.

No birth date or place, marriage record, death date or place, or grave has been found for Marie. She is known only from the 1950 census household and her son Ronald’s obituary.
