# Philip A. Spiess × Marie L. Lavanier Spiess — couple research audit

**Research date:** 25 November 2026 (America/New_York)  
**Generation VI couple:** Marie L. (Lavanier) Spiess × Philip A. Spiess

## Existing evidence and marriage status

The existing profiles cite the 1950 census household in Williamsburg Township, Clermont County, Ohio: Philip A. and Marie L. Spiess with sons Ronald P. and David M. The transcription supports their shared household and parental family group, but the census alone is not a marriage record.

Marie’s public FamilySearch profile (GQQ8-L5H) lists an Ohio County Marriages source. That citation is a lead to a marriage record, but I could not inspect the underlying entry/image in this pass, so I am not asserting a marriage date or license details from that source. The live profiles say exactly that: the public-tree spouse claim and shared household support the couple assignment; the original marriage entry still needs verification.

Ronald’s 2024 B.J. Meyer Sons obituary directly identifies Marie Lavanier Spiess as his mother and David Spiess as his brother. It does not name Philip as Ronald’s father, so the father–son attribution remains based on the public family tree and 1950 household, not on Ronald’s obituary alone.

## Philip’s parents and siblings

Philip Adam Spiess’s public FamilySearch profile (LJLJ-B4C) gives parents **Adam Spiess (1866–1932)** and **Henrietta Wittner (1867–1931)**. The same profile lists an Ohio birth source, census and county-marriage sources. Adam’s profile independently includes Philip in a five-child family group with Henrietta.

**Working parent assignment: approximately 80% confidence; not directly proved here by a reviewed birth certificate.** Both parents have Gen VII profiles, reciprocal links to Philip, and source audits.

The public Philip tree lists four siblings: Charles Spiess (1892–1932), Louise Spiess (1894–1900), Fred A. Spiess (1896–; later life not shown in the public profile), and Catherine E. Spiess (1898–1957). The names/dates are attributed to the public tree; original civil images for each were not fully reviewed here.

## Spiess children and obituary variants

The public FamilySearch family group counts seven children. It names Ronald P (tree birth year 1935), Jacqueline Spiess (1936–1969), Mary C Spiess (born 1939), Phyllis A Spiess (born 1940), and David M Spiess (tree birth year 1943); the FamilySearch timeline also displays “Mary Spiess” and “Betty J. O’Neal.” The updated parent profiles preserve these two timeline-only name forms as unconfirmed child leads.

Ronald’s funeral-home obituary lists deceased siblings David Spiess, Debbie Duncan, Jackie Wells and Phyllis Stump, plus younger sisters Mary Montgomery and Betty O’Neal. The married-name/name-form correspondences are plausible but not directly bridged. “Jackie Wells” may be Jacqueline Spiess; “Phyllis Stump,” “Mary Montgomery” and “Betty O’Neal” may correspond to Spiess daughters. “Debbie Duncan” is an obituary-only sibling name not matched to a tree child. No duplicate public child profiles were created from these name-only leads.

The existing project profile for David Michael Spiess gives 1 August 1942–11 January 2020 based on public records and a matching funeral-home obituary; the FamilySearch family tree says birth year 1943. The 1950 census reports age seven, which is consistent with 1942 if enumerated in April. The conflict remains recorded.
