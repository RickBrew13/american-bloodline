Life Story

Kathryn “Kate” Coffey Cossman (5 September 1952–10 August 2016) was born in Oneida, Tennessee, to Clyde Estill and Dora (Dobbs) Coffey. Her obituary identifies her husband as Stanley Cossman, her biological children as Daniel and Ashley, and her stepsons as Samuel and Matthew. It also names four grandchildren — Kayla Cossman, Tessa Cossman, Carter Cossman and Audrey Long — and nine step-grandchildren: Casey, Alex, Samuel, Cody, Michael, Brie, Jordyn, Keira and Cooper. The obituary does not assign the grandchildren to individual parents, so those links remain unasserted. Kate was a member of Cherokee United Baptist Church, enjoyed cooking, caring for grandchildren and family gatherings, and was described as a caretaker for family. She died at Bethesda North Hospital.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Her obituary names parents Clyde Estill and Dora (Dobbs) Coffey, her biological children Daniel and Ashley, and explicitly identifies Samuel and Matthew as stepsons. It names grandchildren Kayla Cossman, Tessa Cossman, Carter Cossman and Audrey Long, plus step-grandchildren Casey, Alex, Samuel, Cody, Michael, Brie, Jordyn, Keira and Cooper. The source does not specify the parent of each grandchild; no individual parentage is inferred. Do not list Kathryn as Sam’s biological mother.
  • Family-line follow-up, 26 November 2026: later Edward Coffey Project entries clarify that Thomas Dobbs and Julia Jones had distinct daughters Ester (born 4 September 1918) and Dora (born about 1920); the earlier 2010 post had questioned whether these were one or two women. Dora is the daughter who married Clyde Estill Coffey and is Kathryn’s mother. This resolves the compiled-source name confusion, but the birth dates and parentage still need original certificates or census images.
  • The same compiled Coffey Project identifies Clyde Estill Coffey (born 26 December 1918) as a son of Reuben Coffey (born 13 October 1875, died 17 March 1943) and Mary Alice Vaughn (born 29 November 1877, died 17 December 1954), and lists his siblings as Emma, Perk, Oda, John Herbert, Lonia, Nelson, George, Sterling E., Fordie and Carl Homer. These are provisional family-history leads, not yet independently verified in primary records. The compiler reports Reuben and Mary Alice married 25 December 1895 in Tennessee and were buried at Cedar Grove Church Cemetery.
  • The same source reports Dora’s parents as Thomas Dobbs and Julia Jones, married 28 January 1900; Julia was born 19 February 1879 and died 21 February 1940. It lists Dora’s siblings as Lillie (1910), Parker (1913), Ester (1918) and Edith (1929). The source itself says Julia’s parents may have been Charlie and Lizzie Jones; that ancestry remains unproven. No birth/death certificates, marriage license image or cemetery register for these parents have been inspected.
  • The Edward Coffey Project reports that Clyde and Dora married on 11 June 1941 in Stearns, Kentucky. This is a compiled-source date/place lead; the original marriage record has not been located.
  • A 2008 McCreary County Death Records index lists Harold Coffey, born 20 November 1949 and died 17 December 2002 in Whitley City. This is a plausible match to Kathryn’s deceased brother Harold named in her obituary, but the index contains no parents and no direct identity bridge; keep the match provisional. The 2005 Helen Panko obituary previously reviewed is a different woman (Helen Chavan Panko, New Jersey) and remains excluded.
  • No primary record has yet confirmed the parentage of Clyde or Dora, the sibling identities, or the 1941 marriage. No additional portrait or headstone image was found beyond the existing contributor-credited Find a Grave portrait. The original death certificates, marriage register, cemetery register and family census images remain outstanding.
