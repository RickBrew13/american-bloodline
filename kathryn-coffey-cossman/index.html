Life Story

Kathryn (Kate) Coffey Cossman (5 September 1952–10 August 2016) was born in Oneida, Tennessee, to Clyde Estill and Dora (Dobbs) Coffey. Her obituary says she was a member of Cherokee United Baptist Church, enjoyed cooking, caring for grandchildren and family gatherings, and was a family caretaker. She died at Bethesda North Hospital. Her husband was Stanley; her stepsons Samuel and Matthew are explicitly identified as stepchildren, not sons by birth. Her children Daniel and Ashley are named in the obituary.
