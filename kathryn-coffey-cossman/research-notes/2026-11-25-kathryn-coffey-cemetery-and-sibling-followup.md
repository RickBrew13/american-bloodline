## Sibling leads
The obituary directly names siblings:
- Harold Coffey (predeceased Kathryn).
- Helen Panko (predeceased Kathryn).
- Norman Coffey.
- Opal Stewart.

A derivative index lists a Harold Coffey born 20 November 1949, died 17 December 2002, last residence Whitley City, McCreary County, Kentucky:
https://www.locateancestors.com/harold-coffey/

The name, county and dates make this a plausible match for Kathryn's brother Harold, but the index does not name his parents. It remains a lead, not an established identity link.

A 2005 Star-Ledger obituary for Helen (Chavan) Panko, 88, of Rahway, lists siblings William Chavan, Mary Tywoniw, Irene Forstenhausler, Edward, John and Joan Chavan:
https://obits.nj.com/us/obituaries/starledger/name/helen-panko-obituary?id=14811676

No Coffey or Dobbs parent connection is stated. The New Jersey Helen Panko is therefore not attached to Kathryn's sister Helen Panko. Kathryn's obituary directly confirms she had a sister named Helen Panko, but that sister's own identity, birth details and obituary remain unresolved.

## Duplicate cemetery-index entry checked
PeopleLegacy's Sherd Dobbs Cemetery list contains two entries:
- Kathryn Coffey Cossman, 1952–2016, with parents Clyde Estill and Dora Dobbs Coffey and spouse Stanley Cossman.
- Kathryn Coffey Crossman, 1953–2016, with no family details.

The second entry gives the same death date and age but a different surname spelling and a one-year birth discrepancy. It is likely a duplicate or indexing variant of the same Kathryn, not evidence of a second person. The contemporary obituary and the first PeopleLegacy record support 5 September 1952 and the Cossman spelling. Do not create a second profile or change the established birth date based on the duplicate.
