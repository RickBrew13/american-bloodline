## What is established directly

## Maternal line: Dora (Dobbs) Coffey
The Edward Coffey Project's 2010 article reports that Thomas Dobbs married Julia Jones on 28 January 1900 and that they had a daughter Dora/Dora Ester who married Clyde Estill Coffey on 11 June 1941 in Stearns, Kentucky. The 2010 author noted uncertainty over whether Dora Ester and another daughter Ester were one or two people:
https://coffeycousins.blogspot.com/2010/11/sheridan-l-sherd-dobbs-sarah-sally.html

A later Dobbs-family update on the same project clarifies that Thomas and Julia had **separate daughters**:
- Lillie, born 1910
- Parker, born 1913
- Ester, born 1918
- Dora, born about 1920
- Edith, born 1929

The update also says Thomas and Julia were buried at the James C. Coffey family cemetery in Stearns. It describes Julia as born 19 February 1879 in Slavens, McCreary County, and died 21 February 1940 in Cooperative. It suggests Julia may have been a daughter of Charlie and Lizzie Jones, but this ancestry is not established:
https://coffeycousins.blogspot.com/search/label/Dobbs

This later account resolves the compiler's earlier name-confusion: Dora is distinct from her sister Ester, and Dora is the mother named in Kathryn's obituary. The exact birth year and the parents remain compiled-source claims until confirmed by original birth, marriage or census records.

## Paternal line: Clyde Estill Coffey
The Edward Coffey Project's July 2008 Reuben Coffey entry identifies Clyde Estill Coffey (born 26 December 1918) as a son of **Reuben Coffey** and **Mary Alice Vaughn**. It reports:
- Reuben: born 13 October 1875 in Wayne County, Kentucky; died 17 March 1943 in Slavans, McCreary County, reportedly homicide.
- Mary Alice Vaughn: born 29 November 1877; died 17 December 1954 in McCreary County.
- Marriage: 25 December 1895 in Tennessee.
- Burial: Cedar Grove Church Cemetery, McCreary County.
- Their listed children include Emma, Perk, Oda, John Herbert, Lonia, Nelson, George, Sterling E., Clyde Estill, Fordie and Carl Homer.

Source: https://coffeycousins.blogspot.com/2008/07/

This is a useful compiled family reconstruction, but no original marriage record, birth certificates, death certificates or census images were located in this pass. The reported siblings and exact dates are therefore research leads, not proven relationships. The compiler's claim that Reuben died by homicide should not be expanded into a cause or circumstances without a death certificate or contemporary newspaper report.

## Kathryn's siblings
The 2016 obituary names:
- Harold Coffey (predeceased Kathryn)
- Helen Panko (predeceased Kathryn)
- Norman Coffey
- Opal (Coffey) Stewart

The McCreary County Death Records index lists Harold Coffey, born 20 November 1949 and died 17 December 2002, residence Whitley City:
https://kygenweb.net/mccreary/deaths/death-ssdi-ca-fu.htm

This is a plausible identity match to Kathryn's brother Harold, but the index contains no parents and no source directly connects this Harold to Clyde and Dora. Keep it provisional. A 2005 New Jersey obituary for Helen Panko identifies its subject as Helen (Chavan) Panko, wife of Michael Panko and sister to Chavan relatives; it does not connect her to Kathryn's family and is excluded:
https://obits.nj.com/us/obituaries/starledger/name/helen-panko-obituary?id=14811676

A 2012 Hickman-Strunk obituary for Joshua Keith Jones names Mrs. Opal Stewart and a great-grandmother Dora Coffey. This suggests the Opal Stewart in Kathryn's obituary may be in the same extended family as Dora, but the obituary does not identify Opal's maiden name or parents; it is not sufficient to prove that she is Kathryn's sister. Do not merge the Opal without an identity bridge:
https://www.strunkfuneralhome.net/obituaries/Joshua-Jones?obId=11817692

## Research results and limits
- The maternal “two Esters” ambiguity is resolved in the later compiled Dobbs family list: Ester (1918) and Dora (about 1920) are treated as two separate sisters.
- Dora's likely parents are Thomas Dobbs and Julia Jones; her likely siblings are Lillie, Parker, Ester and Edith.
- Clyde's likely parents are Reuben Coffey and Mary Alice Vaughn; a compiled list names ten siblings.
- The reported Dora–Clyde marriage date/place is 11 June 1941, Stearns, Kentucky, but the original marriage record has not been located.
- Harold's 1949–2002 death-index entry may match Kathryn's deceased brother; parentage remains unverified.
- No verified additional photo, personal headstone image, cemetery register, original birth/death certificate, or primary marriage record was found.
