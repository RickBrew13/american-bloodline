## Clyde Estill Coffey and the maternal Dobbs line — working leads only
The Edward Coffey Project's November 2010 post gives a secondary compiled family account:
https://coffeycousins.blogspot.com/2010/11/
It states:
- Clyde Estill Coffey was born 26 December 1918 in Kentucky and died 22 December 1968 in McCreary County.
- Clyde married Dora Dobbs on 11 June 1941 in Stearns.
- Clyde was a son of Reuben Coffey and Mary Alice Vaughn Coffey.
- Dora was likely one of the daughters of Thomas Dobbs and Julia Jones Dobbs, whose listed children include Lillie, Parker, Ester, another possible Ester/Dora Ester, and Edith.
The author explicitly says there may be confusion between two daughters named Ester or Dora Ester and plans to investigate. Thus the proposed Dobbs grandparent link is not secure. The Coffey parental link is a compiled genealogy lead, not an original birth or marriage record. Neither set of grandparents has been added to Kathryn's verified immediate-family fields.

The same project describes an extended Coffey kin network and gives birth/death dates for Clyde, but its own source/correction context is user-submitted genealogy. Treat it as a useful roadmap for primary-record research, not final proof.
