Life Story

Emily Lane was born on 20 December 1817 — the date is on the shared headstone she shares with her husband in Miller Cemetery at Goldtown — and married George Miller on 8 September 1834 in Kanawha County, Virginia, when she was sixteen and he was seventeen. They settled in Jackson County, where the censuses find her from 1850, and raised thirteen children, eleven of whom lived to adulthood and ten of whom married. She joined the Baptist Church about 1864, by her obituary’s reckoning. She died on 7 March 1899, aged 81, and is buried beside George under the shared stone.

Her obituary is the fullest account of her life — and it survives only as a transcription, reproduced in the compiled pedigree. The original paper, date, and page have not been located. It says she gave birth to thirteen children, raised eleven, saw ten married, and counted 165 descendants; that she had been sick since last December; and that she “lived as a Christian mother and companion up to her death.” The 165-descendant count is the obituary’s arithmetic, not a verified count. A poem follows the obituary, signed “J. E. Horn, Son-in-law” — but no Horn married any of George and Emily’s six daughters, and no J. E. Horn has been found in Jackson County. Whether “Son-in-law” is the paper’s label or the pedigree’s, and who Horn was, is unknown; it is not asserted here.

Her parents are carried as Lawrence Conklin Lane × Eleanor “Nell” Smith, the position in the research folder, at UNPROVED — no primary record makes Emily their daughter, and the parentage wall stands. Four traditions exist, all unproved: the Lawrence × Eleanor tradition of an old compiled account (still attached on Geni, with a detach-pending flag — not detached); the pedigree’s Laurens Lane × Elizabeth Barnhart, which collapses as stated, since the 1849 Barnhart marriage was the son Lawrence, born 1822, not the elder Lawrence; Lawrence × Unknown Fleming (a Find a Grave bio); and Lawrence × Nancy Elizabeth Fleming (attached on FamilySearch). The chronology problem stands against the Lawrence × Eleanor pair: Eleanor remarried Lawrence only after her first husband Samuel Jackson died in November 1821, but Emily was born in 1817–1819. The Rootsweb “Descendants of William Lane” page, once tried as a source for Emily, is a different Tennessee family and is contradicted here. Her maiden name “Williams,” which appears only on her daughter Sydna’s page, is likewise unproved; the settled form is Emily Lane. No portrait of Emily is placed on this page.
