Life Story

Hannah Crochen lived in Hayneville, Lowndes County, Alabama, and married George about 1865. The censuses place the couple in Hayneville in 1870, in Gordonville in 1880, and back in Hayneville in 1900 and 1910, with a large family: at least eight children are named across the four censuses, including their son Albert, born about 1888.

Her 1932 death record names her father as Jesse Konnon and her husband as George; it gives her age as 95. Her birth surname is not established — the records spell her name Croshion, Canion, Kingdom and Keenan in different places. Her death certificate image is restricted to a FamilySearch center or affiliate library, so the exact death date, birthplace and informant were not obtained. In 1910 her son Albert and his wife Callie were living in the household, and after her death Albert's family moved to Birmingham.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The family name is spelled Crocheron, Cracheron, Crochen, Croshion and Crochon across the records; the profile uses Crochen, the spelling on their son Albert's records.
  • The 1910 census: the FamilySearch index labels Albert "Son" and Callie "Wife," while a reading of the original image gives Albert as son-in-law and Callie as George and Hannah's daughter. The 1900 census and Albert's 1962 death index both name Albert as George's son, so the parentage stands; the 1910 labels remain an unresolved conflict — see Albert Crochen's profile.
  • Rawil, 13, appears in George's 1900 household; the record does not state his relationship and he appears in no other census, so he is not listed as a child here.
  • Lucinda (1870) and Anda (1880) may be one daughter under two name forms — not listed as separate children. Daisy Cheatham appears in no census — not listed as a child.
  • Birth year: age 53 in the 1900 census (about 1847); age 24 in 1870 (about 1846); age 30 in 1880 (about 1850); age 70 in 1910 (about 1840); age 95 at death in 1932 (about 1837). About 1847 is the working year; the range is not settled.
  • Maiden name: her father is Jesse Konnon, named in her 1932 death record. The records spell her name Croshion (her own death record), Canion (her son Emanuel's death record), Kingdom (her son Albert's death record) and Keenan (a competing death attachment on her tree profile) — none is established as her birth surname.
  • Competing death attachments on her FamilySearch profile (1936 Hannah Keenan, 1937 Hannah Canion, 1942 and 1962 Hannah Kingdom) are not her; the 1932 Hannah Croshion record is the match.
  • Her death certificate image is restricted to a FamilySearch center or affiliate library — the exact death date, birthplace and informant were not obtained.
