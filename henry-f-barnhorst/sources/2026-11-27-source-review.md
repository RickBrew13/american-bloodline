# Source review

[Find a Grave memorial 103882650](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/103882650/henry-f-barnhorst); grave marker photo credited there to James (Jim) F. Kelly, added 11 Aug. 2016. [FamilySearch profile LH15-GHD](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/LH15-GHD/henry-f.-barnhorst-1850-1927), a compiled profile reporting a 1850 birth, marriage to Mary Gertrude Strefelt in 1875, parents Bernard Barnhorst and Mary Steuri, and eight children. George’s [1922 death-notice clipping](https://rickbrew13.github.io/american-bloodline/assets/barnhorst/George-A-Barnhorst-death-notice-photo-2020.jpg) names his parents Henry and Mary (née Strefelt); the newspaper masthead and issue date are cropped.
