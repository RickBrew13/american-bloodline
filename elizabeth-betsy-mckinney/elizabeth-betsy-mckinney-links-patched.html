Life Story

Elizabeth "Betsy" McKinney was born in October 1837 in Morgan County, Tennessee, the daughter of Lumpkin McKinney and his wife Catherine — a link carried as supported, not proved. No primary record names them as her parents; the case rests on the 1850 and 1860 census households, where she appears at Crooked Fork in her father's household, and on her signature as an heir beside her brothers on the 1900 Lampkin-heirs quitclaim deed. Lumpkin's wife is recorded as "Elizabeth" in 1850 and "Catherine" in 1860, and the research treats them as one woman. Catherine's maiden name is carried as Staples on the trees only — no document names it. Lumpkin's own parentage is unproved: an old compiled account's link to Vincent McKinney has no documentary support, and the Vincent-to-Joseph Floyd II link is chronologically impossible and refuted. The best lead, by naming pattern only, is the older Lampkin McKinney and Sarah "Sally" Copley, married 7 August 1787 in Montgomery County, Virginia.

Her son James Alexander was born in April 1857 and carried his mother's surname. No record names his father — no paternity, guardianship, or marriage record — and a Tennessee State Library and Archives search for any pre-1867 marriage of Betsy's, in Morgan and Roane Counties, came back negative on 23 October 2026. An old compiled account's framing — that she never married and deliberately chose to give James her name — is unsourced dramatization, not carried as fact. On 25 October 1867 she married Ezekiel "Zeke" Cochran in Roane County; Ezekiel was James's stepfather only — the 1880 census records Alexander as his "Stepson," with Betsy's mother Catherine in the household as Ezekiel's mother-in-law. Ezekiel's first wife, Serena Gallimore, had died in November 1863, and his older children were in the Scott County household with Betsy and Alexander in 1870. Ezekiel died in 1893.

In 1900 Betsy was widowed and living in her son James's Scott County household, where she reported one child born and one living. She is last seen on 25 June 1900; when and where she died, and where she is buried, are not recorded. No photograph of Betsy exists. Her occupation in the 1860 census is given as "Domestic," in her father's household. No church affiliation is recorded for her, and no military service.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Grades on this page: DOCUMENTED means an original record was read (the 1860 and 1880 census images, the TSLA negative); SUPPORTED means a claim attested by filed research — censuses, the marriage index, the Gough citations, the signed-in tree checks — but not by a register read here; UNPROVED means a claim carried from the research folder with no corroboration found.
  • Parentage: Lumpkin McKinney × Catherine is supported, not proved — no parent-child document names them. The link rests on the 1850 and 1860 census households and on Betsy's signature as an heir beside her brothers on the 1900 Lampkin-heirs quitclaim deed. Catherine's maiden name "Staples" is unproved — tree entries only, no document names it. Lumpkin's wife is recorded as "Elizabeth" in 1850 and "Catherine" in 1860; the research treats them as one woman, and if they are two wives the parentage changes.
  • Lumpkin's parentage is unproved. An old compiled account's Vincent McKinney × Jane Wilda Edwards link has no documentary support, and the Vincent-to-Joseph Floyd II link is chronologically impossible (Jane born 1780 cannot be grandmother of a man born 1773) and is refuted. Best lead, not proof: the older Lampkin McKinney × Sarah "Sally" Copley (married 7 August 1787, Montgomery County, Virginia), naming pattern only. The documented intersection of the two McKinney lines is the Copley sisters: Sally married the older Lampkin, and Elizabeth "Betsy" Copley married Joseph Floyd McKinney I — the two patriarchs were brothers-in-law; shared McKinney blood unproved.
  • James's father is unknown — a genuine brick wall. No paternity, bastardy, maintenance, guardianship, or marriage record names him, and the Tennessee State Library and Archives searched Morgan and Roane Counties for any pre-1867 marriage of Betsy's (LibAnswers ticket #19393477) — negative. Geni's labeled "Unknown Unknown" profile is a placeholder, not a person; it is not linked here and is not presented as a spouse.
