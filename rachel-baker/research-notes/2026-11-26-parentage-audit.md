# Rachel Baker — parentage and family audit

Reviewed 26 November 2026. FamilySearch public-tree profile L85K-XVC names Rachel Baker (1845–1928) as wife of William M Wooton (married 28 March 1860) and mother of their nine listed children, including Elizabeth Ann Wooton. Elizabeth’s attached 1870 census source is titled “Lizaney Wooton in household of William Wooton,” supporting the family placement but not independently naming Rachel in that record.

This profile does not promote the public-tree parent names attached to Rachel; they have not been verified against original records in this pass.
