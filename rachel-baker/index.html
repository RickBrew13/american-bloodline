Life Story

The available FamilySearch public-tree family group names Rachel Baker as the wife of William M Wooton and mother of nine children, including Elizabeth Ann Wooton. On Elizabeth’s profile, the attached census source is titled “Lizaney Wooton in household of William Wooton” for 1870. The naming and household context make Rachel a plausible parent of the woman later indexed as Elizana/Elizane Asher and called Elizone in Russell’s memorial, but the original record images and a direct parent-child statement were not independently transcribed here.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Rachel Baker is named as Elizabeth Ann Wooton’s mother by the FamilySearch shared-tree profile. A 1870 census source title on Elizabeth’s profile places “Lizaney Wooton” in William Wooton’s household; this supports the family grouping but the original schedule image has not been independently read.
  • Rachel’s reported life dates and nine-child list come from a compiled public-tree family group; no original marriage record or direct birth record for Elizabeth was reviewed in this pass.
  • The identity bridge between Elizabeth Ann Wooton and the “Elizone” named on Russell’s memorial is probable, not proven. A separate Letcher Kilas Asher family is in the shared-tree profile; the exact relation to the project’s Letcher (1868–1910) remains unresolved.
