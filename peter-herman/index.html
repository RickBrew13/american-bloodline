Life Story

Peter Herman was born in July 1872 in Poland, then under German or Russian rule, and by 1895 was living in Holding Township, Stearns County, Minnesota SUPPORTED. He married Victoria Langner about 1899, and their son Lawrence was born in Holdingford on 5 September 1900 DOCUMENTED. The family was still in Holding Township in 1905, and an infant son, Stanislas, died there in December 1908 SUPPORTED. By the 1910 census Peter was no longer in the household, and in 1921 Victoria remarried in Detroit SUPPORTED. When and where Peter died is not yet proved UNPROVED.
