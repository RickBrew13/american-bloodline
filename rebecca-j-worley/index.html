Life Story

Rebecca Worley was born in Pulaski County, Kentucky, in 1876 according to her compiled profile and memorial. Find a Grave links her as spouse to Green Berry Gilreath and their children, including Glenneth. The labeled family photograph caption identifies Rebecca and Green with Carl, Glenneth, and granddaughter Glenna Lee.

Glenneth’s 1948 Kentucky death certificate image names his mother “Rebecca Worley” and father “Green B. Gilreath,” providing direct evidence for her relationship to Glenneth. Other dates and family relationships remain supported by compiled profile and memorial evidence; the underlying records have not all been individually reviewed.
