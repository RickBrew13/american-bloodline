Life Story

Charles Clay Goff was born on August 28, 1941, in Perry County, in the coalfields of eastern Kentucky, a son of Ollie Clay Goff and Violet Naomi Yeary. SUPPORTED The portrait on his headstone shows him as a young man in uniform. SUPPORTED

By 1970 he was living at Goshen, Ohio, east of Cincinnati. On May 18 of that year he and Yvonne Suzanne Brewster, Eugene Brewster's former wife, crossed into Tennessee and were married by a justice of the peace at Jellico. DOCUMENTED The marriage did not last; Yvonne later married Raymond Dean Dukes. SUPPORTED Charles died at Livonia, Michigan, on July 18, 2005, aged 63, and was brought home to be buried in Jackson Cemetery in Breathitt County, Kentucky. SUPPORTED
