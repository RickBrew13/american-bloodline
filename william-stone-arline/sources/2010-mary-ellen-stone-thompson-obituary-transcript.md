# Mary Ellen Stone Thompson obituary transcription

Source: Find a Grave memorial 62267367, https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/62267367/mary-ellen-thompson. The memorial reproduces the following obituary; newspaper masthead/date are not shown in the accessible text, so this is a derivative transcription rather than an original clipping image.

> Mary Ellen Stone Thompson, 78 years of age, of Faubush, Kentucky, departed this life on Wednesday, November 24th, 2010 at the Sunrise Manor Nursing Home in Somerset, Kentucky.
>
> She was born on May 26th, 1932 in Cincinnati, Ohio to the late William Stone and Helen Margaret Abel Stone. She was a homemaker and of the Catholic faith. She loved to play bingo and spend time with family and friends.
>
> Mary was survived by four sons, Henry W. (and Stella) Thompson Jr. of Nancy, Kentucky, Rodney Gumm, Billy Gumm, and Ricky Gumm; four daughters, Mary (and Michael) Parido of Frost Proof, Florida, Karen Gumm, Kathy Gumm, and Betty Jean Gumm; one sister, Arlene Stone; five grandchildren, Sherri Reese, Christopher Thompson, Anthony Deal, Shannon Deal, and Steven Thompson; and four great grandchildren, DaKayla Richardson, Katie Jo Dodson, Ryan Thompson, and Jasmine Thompson.
>
> She was preceded in death by her parents, William and Helen Margaret Stone; her husband, Henry William Thompson, whom she married on April 13th, 1966 in Richmond, Indiana and who departed this life on May 16th, 2002.
>
> Visitations were held on Monday, November 29th, 2010 after 10:00 a.m. at the Lake Cumberland Funeral Home in Somerset, Kentucky. Funeral services were held on Monday, November 29th, 2010 at 12:00 noon in the Chapel of the Lake Cumberland Funeral Home with Dr. Greg Ryan officiating. Interment followed in the Lakeside Memorial Gardens in Somerset, Kentucky.
