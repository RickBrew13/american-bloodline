- Ruth's maiden name remains unknown. The Ruth Justine Corry lead (round 1) is still unproven and is not adopted.

## Limits

FamilySearch's search is fuzzy — spouse, parent and date filters return records where the names appear in other roles — so "no match" above means no record with the named person in the matching role among the returned, fully loaded results, not proof that no unindexed or mis-indexed record exists. The 1950 census conclusion rests on the index; the Pendleton sheets themselves were not browsed, because no 1950 ED covering 1307 Pendleton Street was established.
