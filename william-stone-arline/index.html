Family Relationship Notes

The existing project transcription of the 1940 census lists William Stone, age 30, in a household at 1307 (rear) Pendleton Street, Cincinnati, with Ruth (29), May Ellen (8), and Arline (3). The FamilySearch record page was unavailable in this pass, and the original schedule/relationship column was not inspected. The household supports William as Arline’s father; it does not alone prove the mother-child link to Ruth.

Mary Ellen Stone Thompson’s reproduced obituary directly names William Stone and Helen Margaret Abel Stone as her parents and says she had one sister, “Arlene Stone.” This supports Mary Ellen as William’s daughter by Helen and supports a paternal sibling relationship to Arline. It does not independently identify Ruth as Arline’s mother.

Ruth is the probable mother of Arline because she is the adult woman listed with William and young Arline in the 1940 household. No birth certificate for Arline or William–Ruth marriage record has been reviewed. The 1940 age suggests Arline born about 1936–1937, conflicting with GENI’s unsourced 22 February 1938 claim.

No names for William’s parents or siblings were found. No Gen VII ancestor profiles have been created for unidentified people; same-name Stones are not attached without an identity bridge.
