## 1. Hamilton County, Ohio property records — new findings

Two deed-index entries connect Homer W. Brewster and Flora L. Brewster to a Hamilton County property transaction:

- **13 August 1975:** Grantor Wilbur C. Rapp & Ruth; grantees Homer W. Brewster & Flora L.; deed book **4025**, page **55**. Official index: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1975tetrb.pdf
- **5 March 1976:** Grantor Homer W. Brewster; grantee Flora L. Brewster; deed book **4040**, page **738**. Official index: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1976trteb.pdf

The 5 March 1976 date is read from the March 5 chronological sequence in the official index page image. These entries establish indexed property transactions, but not the property address, deed type, consideration, or reason for the transfer. Obtain the original deeds through the Hamilton County Recorder's book/page search before describing the 1976 transfer as a quitclaim, divorce settlement, or other specific legal action.

## 2. Edna Sharon Bailey — spouse claim is unverified and conflicts with the timeline

The live Homer profile currently lists Edna Sharon Bailey as a wife, allegedly married **18 June 1966** in Franklin County, Indiana. Edna's profile says this date comes from a FamilySearch family-tree relationship and that no marriage certificate or independent marriage record has been located.

This alleged marriage overlaps the documented Homer William Brewster Jr. × Judith Mae Palmer marriage (18 April 1956) and the indexed divorce from Judith (2 May 1968). The 1968 divorce index's “times married” field for Homer is not a spouse identification and does not prove that Edna was the other marriage.

**Do not list Edna as a confirmed spouse unless the Indiana marriage record is found and the identity is established.** Treat her as an unresolved lead, with a real possibility of a wrong-person or tree-link error.

A better verification route has now been identified:

- Indiana State Library, **Indiana Marriages 1958–2022** search: https://digital.statelib.lib.in.us/legacy/search.aspx?index=2
  - Search Applicant 1 surname Brewster / first name Homer and Applicant 2 surname Bailey / first name Edna.
  - Search the reverse applicant order as well.
  - Try date 18 June 1966 and county Franklin; also search Homer Brewster without Edna to find any other Indiana marriages.
  - The State Library says the index derives from Indiana State Department of Health public records and warns that transcription errors may exist.
- FamilySearch, **Indiana, Franklin County, Marriage Records, 1853–1997**: https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/5000717
  - The collection description states it contains Franklin County marriage licenses and records; it has 15,138 indexed records and 25,292 images. The relevant year may need image browsing if no name-index result appears.

## 3. Possible later marriage — death-index marital status needs explaining

The archived Ohio death-index research for Homer reports:
- death date: **27 September 2000**;
- place of death: **Preble County, Ohio**;
- birth date: **18 March 1939**;
- indexed age: **64**;
- marital status: **married**.

A birth on 18 March 1939 makes him **61** on 27 September 2000, not 64. The Dayton Daily News notice dated 29 September 2000 reportedly gives “Homer, 67,” also inconsistent. The notice's name, Cincinnati connection, Wednesday death timing, and Newcomer-Barclay service details make it a possible match, but its age conflict means it should not be treated as conclusive identity evidence without the death certificate or funeral-home record.

The “married” field is a separate unresolved clue: the currently identified divorce records include the 1978 Flora L. Brewster divorce. Homer may have married again after 1978, or the death-index marital-status field may be wrong. No later spouse has yet been identified.

A 1998 public-record entry for a Homer W. Brewster in Osprey, Florida, with a matching birth date has been noted in the existing research, but identity is unconfirmed. It may be relevant to the later-marriage question; do not assume it is the same man.

## 4. Census, occupation, race/ethnicity, religion, and military

- The 1940 census places one-year-old Homer in his parents' household in Morgan County, Tennessee.
- The original 1950 census sheet places eleven-year-old Homer in Cincinnati, Ohio. It lists his father as a press operator in motor manufacturing and his mother as handling bottles in the bottling industry. **Those are his parents' occupations, not Homer's.** No occupation is recorded for Homer at age 11.
- The 1950 census race field is recorded as “W.” This is a census race classification, not proof of a more specific ethnic identity.
- No reliable evidence of Homer's religion has been found.
- The prior military-record sweep found no military service record for Homer. His age made him draft-eligible, but eligibility is not proof of service. Do not describe him as a veteran without a record.
- No criminal record for Homer himself has surfaced in the searches to date. Family testimony alleging abuse remains attributed oral history, not a verified court finding. Do not confuse the separate 2012 criminal case involving his son Michael with Homer.
