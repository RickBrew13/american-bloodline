# Homer William Brewster Jr. — Hamilton County deed retrieval follow-up

**Research date:** 25 November 2026  
**Subject:** Homer W. Brewster and Flora L. Brewster  
**Status:** Official index entries verified; original deed images still not inspected.

## Verified index entries

1. **13 August 1975 — Deed Book 4025, page 55.** The official Hamilton County Recorder chronological “TO / FROM” index identifies **Homer W. Brewster and Flora L. Brewster as grantees** and **Wilbur C. Rapp and Ruth Rapp as grantors**. This is a Rapp-to-Brewster transfer, not a Brewster-to-Rapp transfer. The entry is in the Aug. 13 sequence.
   - Official index: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1975tetrb.pdf
   - Search result transcription: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1975tetrb.pdf

2. **5 March 1976 — Deed Book 4040, page 738.** The official 1976 grantor-to-grantee index lists **Homer W. Brewster as grantor** and **Flora L. Brewster as grantee**, Book 4040, page 738. The date is read from the chronological date sequence on the same index page; the date marks repeat until the next explicit date.
   - Official index: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1976trteb.pdf

## How to obtain the original deed images

Hamilton County Recorder's current help page says deed books **3225 through 4061 (1962–1976)** are available through its document search system. Both target books (4025 and 4040) fall within that range.

The Hamilton County Genealogical Society's updated instructions (7/21/2025) say to:
1. Open the Recorder's Acclaim system: https://acclaim-web.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/AcclaimWebLive
2. Choose the **BOOK/PAGE** search.
3. Select book type **DEED - Deed Books**.
4. Enter book **4025**, page **55**; then repeat for book **4040**, page **738**.
5. Open the matching brown result to view the document image. If the viewer presents paired microfilm pages, use the Recorder's page-number guidance to navigate to the correct numbered page.

Official instructions:
- Recorder's deed-book availability and date ranges: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/helpful_information/help_deeds_21-4414.html
- Genealogical Society Acclaim retrieval walkthrough: https://hcgsohio.org/cpage.php?pt=286

## What remains unknown

Until the original deed pages are inspected, do not state the property's street address, parcel, legal description, consideration, deed instrument type, or why the 1976 transfer was made. The index alone does not establish that the transfer was connected to a divorce, separation, or any other family event.
