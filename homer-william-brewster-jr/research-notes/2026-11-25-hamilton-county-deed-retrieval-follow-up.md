# Homer William Brewster Jr. — Hamilton County deed retrieval follow-up

**Research date:** 25 November 2026  
**Subject:** Homer W. Brewster and Flora L. Brewster  
**Status:** Official index entries verified; original deed images still not inspected.

## Verified index entries

1. **13 August 1975 — Deed Book 4025, page 55.** The official Hamilton County Recorder chronological “TO / FROM” index identifies **Homer W. Brewster and Flora L. Brewster as grantees** and **Wilbur C. Rapp and Ruth Rapp as grantors**. This is a Rapp-to-Brewster transfer, not a Brewster-to-Rapp transfer. The entry is in the Aug. 13 sequence.
   - Official index: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1975tetrb.pdf
   - The index entry appears in the official PDF at the Aug. 13 sequence: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1975tetrb.pdf

2. **8 January 1976 — Deed Book 4040, page 738.** The official 1976 grantor-to-grantee index lists **Homer W. Brewster as grantor** and **Flora L. Brewster as grantee**, Book 4040, page 738. The prior research note incorrectly dated this entry to March 1976. Re-reading the chronological index shows that the entry falls in the **January 8** sequence, following the January 8 date marker and before the January 9 section. The earlier March date was an erroneous inference and must not be reused.
   - Official index: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1976trteb.pdf
   - The entry is visible in the official index around lines 2706–2712 of the web-readable transcription: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1976trteb.pdf

3. **26 November 1969 — Deed Book 3708, page 344.** The official 1969 grantor-to-grantee index lists **“Brewster, Homer et al”** as grantor and **James A. Katsanis** as grantee. This is a new same-surname deed lead. The index does not provide Homer's middle name, age, spouse, or the other grantor(s), so it is **not yet attributed to Homer Jr.** It may relate to Homer Jr., Homer Sr., or another Brewster. Do not add it as a confirmed event to either public profile until the original deed identifies the parties.
   - Official index: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1969trteb.pdf
   - Web-readable entry appears in the November 26 sequence at Book 3708, page 344.

## How to obtain the original deed images

The Hamilton County Genealogical Society's updated instructions (7/21/2025) describe the Recorder's Acclaim system for historical documents. The relevant books—**3463 (1966), 3708 (1969), 4025 (1975), and 4040 (1976)**—are within the Recorder's stated historical Book/Page retrieval range for this research period.

1. Open the Recorder's Acclaim system: https://acclaim-web.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/AcclaimWebLive
2. Choose the **BOOK/PAGE** search.
3. Select book type **DEED - Deed Books**.
4. Enter the relevant book and page:
   - 3708 / 344 — unidentified “Homer et al” to James A. Katsanis, 26 Nov 1969
   - 4025 / 55 — Rapp to Homer W. and Flora L. Brewster, 13 Aug 1975
   - 4040 / 738 — Homer W. Brewster to Flora L. Brewster, 8 Jan 1976
5. Open the matching brown result to view the document image. If the viewer presents paired microfilm pages, use the Recorder's page-number guidance to navigate to the correct numbered page.

The 1966 deed, Book 3463, page 428, is a separate lead for **Homer Sr. and Sarah** and belongs in Homer Sr.'s archive, not Homer Jr.'s.

Official instructions:
- Recorder's deed-book availability and date ranges: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/helpful_information/help_deeds_21-4414.html
- Genealogical Society Acclaim retrieval walkthrough: https://hcgsohio.org/cpage.php?pt=286
- Recorder's index portal: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/

## What remains unknown

Until each original deed page is inspected, do not state the property's street address, parcel, legal description, consideration, deed instrument type, or why a transfer was made. The 1976 transfer is not evidence of a divorce settlement or other family event by itself. The 1969 “Homer et al” entry is not attributable to either Homer until the instrument identifies the parties.

## Research priority

Retrieve and inspect the original deed pages for Books 3708/344, 4025/55, and 4040/738. For each, transcribe the full legal description, instrument type, parties, execution/recording dates, consideration, witnesses/notary, and parcel/address exactly. Then use any identified parcel/address to follow ownership history through the Hamilton County Auditor. Keep each transfer separate and file each person's material in that person's own archive.
