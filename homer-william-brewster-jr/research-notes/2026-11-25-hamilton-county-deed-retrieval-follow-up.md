# Homer William Brewster Jr. — Hamilton County deed retrieval follow-up

**Research date:** 25 November 2026  
**Subject:** Homer W. Brewster and Flora L. Brewster  
**Status:** Official index entries verified; original deed images still not inspected.

## Verified index entries

1. **13 August 1975 — Deed Book 4025, page 55.** The official Hamilton County Recorder chronological “TO / FROM” index identifies **Homer W. Brewster and Flora L. Brewster as grantees** and **Wilbur C. Rapp and Ruth Rapp as grantors**. This is a Rapp-to-Brewster transfer, not a Brewster-to-Rapp transfer. The entry is in the Aug. 13 sequence.
   - Official index: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1975tetrb.pdf
   - The index entry appears in the official PDF at the Aug. 13 sequence: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1975tetrb.pdf

2. **8 January 1976 — Deed Book 4040, page 738.** The official 1976 grantor-to-grantee index lists **Homer W. Brewster as grantor** and **Flora L. Brewster as grantee**, Book 4040, page 738. The prior research note incorrectly dated this entry to March 1976. Re-reading the chronological index shows that the entry falls in the **January 8** sequence, following the January 8 date marker and before the January 9 section. The earlier March date was an erroneous inference and must not be reused.
   - Official index: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1976trteb.pdf
   - The entry is visible in the official index around lines 2706–2712 of the web-readable transcription: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1976trteb.pdf

3. **26 November 1969 — Deed Book 3708, page 343/344 (cross-index discrepancy).** The grantor-to-grantee index records “Brewster, Homer et al” → James A. Katsanis at page 344; the reverse grantee-to-grantor index records the same parties and date at page 343. This is no longer treated as a Homer Jr. lead. It is tracked in Homer Sr.'s research folder because a 1966 deed names “Homer & Sarah” as grantees and the 1969 deed may be their later conveyance. That connection is unproven until the original deeds' grantor names and legal descriptions are compared. See Homer Sr.'s note: https://github.com/RickBrew13/american-bloodline/blob/main/homer-william-brewster-sr/research-notes/2026-11-25-1966-apollo-land-company-deed-lead.md

## How to obtain the original deed images

The Recorder's own deed-book guide states that Books 852–4061 (1901–1971) are on microfilm at the Recorder's Office. Books 4025 and 4040 are in that range, so they should **not** be described as confirmed downloadable online Acclaim images. The Acclaim Book/Page page provides an electronic search interface, but the original images for these particular books were not retrieved here. The 1966 and 1969 deed books are also in the 1901–1971 microfilm range and are tracked separately in Homer Sr.'s folder.

1. The Acclaim BOOK/PAGE search interface can be tried first: https://acclaim-web.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/AcclaimWebLive
2. Choose **BOOK/PAGE**, select **DEED - Deed Books**, and enter:
   - 4025 / 55 — Rapp to Homer W. and Flora L. Brewster, 13 Aug 1975
   - 4040 / 738 — Homer W. Brewster to Flora L. Brewster, 8 Jan 1976
3. Remote access to the original images for these 1901–1971 microfilm books is **not confirmed**. If the search only identifies the book/page or no image is displayed, use the Recorder's microfilm/copy service rather than assuming the image is downloadable.
4. The Recorder's Office is at **138 E. Court St., Room 205, Cincinnati, OH 45202**; public hours are Monday–Friday, 7:30 a.m.–4:00 p.m. General information: **(513) 946-4596**; office supervisor: **(513) 946-4574**; email **recordersoffice@hamiltoncountyohio.gov**. The office says public self-service copies are **$0.10/page** and staff-produced copies are **$2.00/page**; certified copies are $2.00/page plus a $1.00 certification seal. Mail requests require a self-addressed stamped envelope. Confirm any mailing arrangement and current fee with staff before sending payment.
5. Recorder guidance and fee details:
   - Deed-book date ranges and microfilm: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/search_help/search_deed_books.htm
   - Office contact and hours: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/contact_us_feedback/
   - Copy services and fees: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/additional_services/
   - Fee schedule: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/fees/main_office.html

## What remains unknown

Until each original deed page is inspected, do not state the property's street address, parcel, legal description, consideration, deed instrument type, or why a transfer was made. The 1976 transfer is not evidence of a divorce settlement or other family event by itself. The 1969 “Homer et al” entry is not attributable to either Homer until the instrument identifies the parties.

## Research priority

Retrieve and inspect the original deed pages for Books 4025/55 and 4040/738. For each, transcribe the full legal description, instrument type, parties, execution/recording dates, consideration, witnesses/notary, and parcel/address exactly. Then use any identified parcel/address to follow ownership history through the Hamilton County Auditor. Keep each transfer separate and file each person's material in that person's own archive.


## Additional route: Hamilton County Auditor ownership cards and historical sales

The Recorder's official deed-search guide says to use the Auditor's Property Search for the parcel history and, for older transfers, open **“See Ownership Card”**. The Auditor's historical references page states that permanent duplicate cards, owner/address indexes, and plat maps exist from about 1940 onward. This gives a second route to recover the address and parcel behind the 1975/1976 deed entries even if the Acclaim image cannot be opened.

- Hamilton County Auditor owner search: https://hammap.hcauditor.org/
- Official Recorder search guide: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/helpful_information/how_to_search_pdi.pdf
- Auditor historical records overview: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/helpful_information/hamilton_county_history.html
- Auditor public-records request page and custodian: https://hcauditor.org/downloads.asp
- Public-records custodian: Connor Moreton, County.Auditor@auditor.hamilton-co.org, (513) 946-4000, 138 E Court Street, Room 304, Cincinnati, Ohio 45202.
