## Transcription and details
Under “MONTGOMERY COUNTY,” the brief notice reads: “BREWSTER, Homer, 67, formerly of Dayton, of Cincinnati, Wednesday. Memorial services 4 p.m. Saturday. (Newcomer-Barclay)” (line breaks normalized). It gives no middle name, spouse, relatives, exact death date, or burial place. The Wednesday reference in the Friday issue is consistent with Wednesday, 27 Sep 2000; this date is an inference from the issue date and wording, not an explicit date in the notice. The service is listed for Saturday at 4 p.m.; Newcomer-Barclay is the named provider/location reference.

## Identity assessment
The item is a directly inspected contemporary newspaper death notice and supports that a Homer Brewster, age 67, formerly of Dayton and of Cincinnati, died Wednesday before the 29 Sep 2000 issue. It does **not** identify the person by middle name or independently establish that this is Homer William Brewster Jr. The reported age 67 is a material conflict with the project’s working birth year 1939 (which would make him about 60–61 in Sep 2000). Keep this as an unresolved same-name identity question; do not attach the age or residence details to Homer William Brewster Jr. without an independent identity bridge. The date may align with the family-reported 27 Sep 2000 death date but does not resolve the age/identity discrepancy.
