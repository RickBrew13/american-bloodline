# Correction to Homer Brewster property-index transcription — 25 November 2026

A reinspection of the official Hamilton County 1976 grantor-to-grantee index corrects the direction of the March 1976 transfer.

- 13 August 1975: Wilbur C. Rapp and Ruth are grantors; Homer W. Brewster and Flora L. are grantees. Deed book 4025, page 55. Official index: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1975trter.pdf
- March 1976: Homer W. Brewster is the grantor; Flora L. Brewster is the grantee. Deed book 4040, page 738. Official index: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1976trteb.pdf

The earlier 25 November research update incorrectly described the 1976 transfer in the reverse direction (Flora to Homer). That statement is superseded by this correction. The deed index shows Homer as grantor and Flora as grantee.

The exact day in March 1976 has not been established from the text extraction, and the original deed has not yet been inspected. Do not infer the property address, deed type, consideration, or reason for transfer. The transfer could be relevant to their household/property history, but it should not be characterized as a divorce settlement without the deed itself.
