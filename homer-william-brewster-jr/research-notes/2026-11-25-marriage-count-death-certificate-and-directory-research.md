## 1. The 1968 divorce count points to an earlier marriage before Judith

The Ohio divorce index entry for Homer Brewster and Judith, granted in Hamilton County on 2 May 1968, reports Homer as having been married twice and Judith once. This is more significant than a simple note that Homer had “two weddings.”

Ohio-specific evidence clarifies the field. The Ohio Department of Health's certified abstract of divorce uses the field **“Number of this marriage”** separately for the husband and wife. A 2008 federal/state systems study reproduces the Ohio form and confirms that ODH receives certified paper divorce abstracts from the county courts and enters the fields into its statewide index:
- Ohio Department of Health, *Certified Abstract* form, HEA 2744: https://odh.ohio.gov/wps/wcm/connect/gov/debe66dd-da23-4dcf-a75d-092b5e115c6b/combined%2Bpdf%2B632019.pdf
- The Lewin Group, *Electronic Collection of Marriage and Divorce Statistics: Findings from Seven States* (2008), Ohio site visit, pp. 49–52: https://acf.gov/sites/default/files/documents/opre/electronic_coll.pdf
- Henschen's search help explains that divorce results include each spouse's personal details and information about the marriage and decree: https://hacourts.com/VitStats/Help/index.shtml

**Working inference:** If the 1968 index value was transcribed correctly, the “2” for Homer means his marriage to Judith was his second marriage; Judith's “1” means her marriage to Homer was her first. This indicates at least one earlier marriage for Homer before Judith. It is not explained by the family story of a second ceremony after their Tennessee elopement; do not treat that ceremony as the missing prior marriage. No earlier spouse, date, county, or certificate has yet been identified. Verify the field value against the actual 1968 abstract before treating the inference as final.

This count needs to be checked against the actual 1968 divorce abstract and complete court file before the earlier marriage is treated as fully established. Keep the unknown prior spouse as an open research question.

### Best-supported marriage sequence and the unresolved 1977 entry

- 18 April 1956: Homer and Judith married in Fentress County, Tennessee. Their recorded birth dates make them 17, although the application appears to give both ages as 18.
- 2 May 1968: The Ohio divorce index records “Number of this marriage” as 2 for Homer and 1 for Judith. If the index transcription is correct, the marriage to Judith was Homer's second marriage and her first. This indicates an earlier Homer marriage before Judith, but the earlier spouse, date, and place are unknown.
- 12 September 1977: The Ohio marriage index names Homer W. Brewster Jr., age 38, and Flora L. Brewster, age 41, and records two past marriages for each. This is consistent with Homer's earlier marriage plus his marriage to Judith being his two prior marriages; the marriage to Flora would then be his third.
- 27 November 1978: The Ohio divorce index names Homer W. Brewster (birth year 1939) and Flora L. (birth year 1935) and records “Number of this marriage” as 3. This is consistent with the September 1977 marriage being Homer's third marriage.
- 18 March 1977: A separate Ohio divorce-index entry names Homer W. Brewster (birth year 1939) and Flora L. (birth year 1935), reports a seven-year marriage, and records “Number of this marriage” as 2 for each. Its marriage-order value conflicts with the 1968, September 1977, and November 1978 entries if all refer to the same couple. It may be a same-name couple or an index error. **Do not yet attach this March 1977 divorce to the confirmed September 1977/November 1978 Homer–Flora sequence.**

The strongest working reconstruction is therefore: an unidentified first marriage before 1956; marriage two to Judith in 1956, ending in the 1968 divorce; marriage three to Flora in 1977, ending in the 1978 divorce. This reconstruction depends on the accuracy and identity of the Ohio index entries; it does not identify the first wife. Edna Sharon Bailey's alleged 1966 marriage remains an unverified and chronologically unresolved lead, not the identified first wife. The original 1968 and 1977–1978 abstracts, marriage license, and case files are needed to settle the sequence. The family account of repeated Homer/Judith marriages also remains separate from what the indexes prove.

## 4. Selective Service and military record request

Homer was born 18 March 1939 and turned 18 on 18 March 1957. He was therefore within the Selective Service registration period; his birth year is not among the later years exempted when registration was suspended. No military service has been established from the online research completed so far.

The Selective Service System confirms that records for men born before 1 January 1960 are held by the National Archives. NARA's St. Louis facility holds the WWII-through-Vietnam-era records for men born 28 April 1877 through 28 March 1957. The available individual records are:
- **Draft Registration Card (SSS Form 1): $7** — may provide name, registration number, date/place of birth, residence at registration, and physical description.
- **Classification History (SSS Form 102): $27**, including the registration card — may show classification, examination, induction or entry into active duty, branch, and separation information.

Request route:
- National Archives at St. Louis, ATTN: RRPOR, P.O. Box 38757, St. Louis, MO 63138-0757
- Email: **stl.archives@nara.gov**
- Official NARA instructions and request form: https://www.archives.gov/st-louis/selective-service
- Selective Service confirmation that pre-1960 registrant records are at NARA: https://www.sss.gov/history-and-records/

The most useful request is the **Classification History (Form 102)** because it includes the card and may establish whether Homer entered active duty, the branch and separation date, or another classification. Use the exact full name, date of birth, and likely 1957 residence in Cincinnati/Hamilton County, Ohio. No request has been submitted and no fee has been paid.
