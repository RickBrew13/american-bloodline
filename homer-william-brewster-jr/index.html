Life Story

Homer William Brewster Jr. was born March 18, 1939, in Morgan County, Tennessee, on the Cumberland Plateau, the son of Homer William Brewster Sr. and Sarah Hazel Kelly. The family called him "Bruce."

In the postwar years he followed the Hillbilly Highway north to Cincinnati and southwest Ohio, part of the great migration out of the coalfields. There he met Judith Mae Palmer. They were seventeen when they ran off to Tennessee to marry; they married in Fentress County on April 18, 1956, with Homer giving his age as eighteen. They married and divorced more than once.

The family gives his death as September 27, 2000, in Preble County, Ohio, at sixty-one. A 1991 date in some family records matches his father's death in the Social Security Death Index, so it is likely a mix-up. No death record for Homer Jr. has been found yet.

Vicki tells the elopement story as her mother told it: Judy took a ride with Homer down to Tennessee, where Homer said he was eighteen though he was seventeen, and when her family found out, they paid an old man ten dollars to stand up and say he was her father so the underage couple could marry; her grandpa and the woman who raised her then made them marry again, properly. They settled in the Hamilton and Preble County areas and raised four children: Victoria, Michael, Garry, and Ronald. After Homer left for good, Judy held the family together from a house in Hartwell with a pool in the backyard where the grandchildren swam.
