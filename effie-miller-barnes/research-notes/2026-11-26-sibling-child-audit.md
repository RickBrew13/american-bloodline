# Effie M. Miller Barnes — sibling and child audit

Reviewed 26 November 2026. Effie’s existing parents, William Jackson Miller and Sophia J. Kimbler Miller, were left unchanged as requested.

## Children
The Cincinnati Enquirer death notice for George, 19 Apr. 1973, p. 68, names seven children; Nina Stretch’s 2010 Enquirer obituary independently calls herself the eldest of seven and names siblings. Lillian Fisher’s 1988 and Ruby Christine Steinmann’s 2001 obituaries also identify members of the group. No additional child was located in these sources.

## Siblings
Effie’s Find a Grave memorial lists Orla Lee Miller (1896–1950) as her sibling; Orla’s separate memorial lists Effie and names William Jackson Miller and Sophia J. Kimbler Miller as his parents. These are reciprocal contributor-entered memorial links. The Effie memorial also labels Lacy Eugene Miller (1930) and Roy C. Miller (1931–2010) as half-siblings; they appear as later children in William Jackson Miller’s family memorial group, likely by his later wife Daisy Pearl Sullivan. Preserve this as memorial-derived evidence until checked against original records.
