Life Story

Effie M. Miller Barnes was born in Kentucky on 19 July 1891 and died in Cincinnati on 17 April 1933, according to her Find a Grave memorial. George’s and Effie’s memorial pages list the same seven children, including Garvice Euel Barnes. A later obituary clipping about Garvice names Effie Miller among his deceased parents. The relationship is supported, but the original birth/census records and Effie’s death certificate have not been reviewed.

Effie’s memorial links her parents as William Jackson Miller and Sophia J. Kimbler Miller. Treat these as unverified memorial-family links.
