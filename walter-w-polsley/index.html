Life Story

Walter Weston Polsley was born on 10 December 1862 in Mason County, West Virginia, the son of Weston Polsley and Harriet Sophia Rine. His father died before about 1880, and his mother remarried Nelson Green Ogden in 1879. Walter was, in his obituary's words, "for many years a resident of Kenna" in Jackson County, West Virginia.

He married Nancy Caroline Ridenour, daughter of Rev. Benjamin Franklin Ridenour and Emaline Mazella Smith. No marriage record for the pair has been found: the family genealogy gives 3 January 1884, FamilySearch gives 1883, and a Yates transcription gives 21 May 1886 — the last sitting impossibly against Rhoda's birth on 15 May 1886, six days earlier. They had four children: Ollie, who died in infancy; Rhoda Alice, the direct line; Edna Vella; and Verna Valeria. Nancy died on 28 July 1894, aged 26; no cause of death is recorded, and none is guessed here.

On Christmas Day 1895, Walter married Esther Donie Skeen in Jackson County, at the residence of Ro Skeen, Rev. John W. Skeen officiating — a license-image marriage, the best-documented event of his life after his own obituary. The second family numbered eight children by the genealogy's roster. Walter died on 11 February 1948 in Ohio, "at his home", aged 85; his funeral was at Ewington, Gallia County, and he was buried "in the cemetery there". His obituary's survivors were his widow Donie, his children, his sister Rose Mason of Mt. Alto, 31 grandchildren and 16 great-grandchildren — counts reported as obituary reporting, not verified as fact.

No portrait of Walter himself has been found; his gravestone photograph, shown here, comes from his Find a Grave memorial. The couple folder holds five record images (Nancy's birth register page, the 1895 marriage license, Rose Mason's death record, Verna's death certificate and a zoom of it) and four contextual place photographs (Ewington, Kenna, Mt. Alto, Vinton); documents are cited below, place photographs are not shown as family pictures.
