# Elmanza Clementine Reed Hoff: research summary

Updated 24 November 2026.

1934 West Virginia death certificate image on the contributor memorial identifies Elmanza Hoff, widow of D. H. Hoff, died 13 Jan 1934; birth 30 Mar 1858, Barbour County; informant R. D. Hoff; burial Hoff Cemetery on 14 Jan. The certificate gives father “Nason Reed” and mother “Anise Engle”; marriage transcription says Nathan and A. Reed/Engle, and Find a Grave uses alternate spellings. Keep those discrepancies visible.
