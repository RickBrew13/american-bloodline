Life Story

Rita Pauline French Shircliff was born in 1916. On 18 April 1936 she married James Edward Shircliff in Meade County, Kentucky. Their children were James Wayne, Joseph H., Raymond E., Ronald E., Mary R., Jerome C., Howard A., and Charles K. Shircliff, and Joyce Ann Shircliffe, who died in infancy.

In the censuses she appears as Pauline Shircliff — in 1940, age 23, with her husband Edward, 29, and their three young sons in Meade County, Kentucky; in 1950, age 33, as Pauline P. Shircliff with her husband James E., 39, and eight children in Hamilton Township, Franklin County, Ohio. Her son James Wayne’s Kentucky birth index entry names her as Rita French, and her husband’s family tree profile gives her full name as Rita Pauline French — one woman, not two.

She died in 1997. Both her birth and death years come from the family tree alone; no birth or death record has been located yet.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Her birth year, 1916, and death year, 1997, come from the family tree alone; no record has been seen for either year.
  • Her marriage date, 18 April 1936, comes from the family tree alone, but the marriage itself is shown by the 1940 and 1950 censuses, which list her and James Edward as married wife and head of household.
  • She appears as Pauline Shircliff in the censuses, while her son’s Kentucky birth index names her as Rita French and her husband’s family tree profile gives her full name as Rita Pauline French — one woman, not two.
  • Her own parents are not yet identified.
  • The 1950 census lists her son Raymond E. as 13, the same age as James, although the 1940 census had Raymond 0 and James 3 — that looks like a census or indexing error; every other household identifier aligns.
  • Her husband’s family tree profile lists only one child, Joyce Ann Shircliffe (1941–1941); James Wayne and the other children are not attached there.
