Life Story

Rita Pauline French Shircliff (27 April 1916–23 May 1997) was born in Sunfish, Kentucky, and appears as Pauline in the 1940 and 1950 censuses. Her obituary says she was a daughter of “Jim and Bulla (Bush) French,” supporting a working link to James Joshua French and Sarilda Ellen Bush; the mother’s given-name discrepancy remains open. The obituary names nine surviving children plus Joyce Ann, while the census initials and family-tree records do not fully reconcile. The couple’s headstone says they married 21 April 1936; the public tree gives 18 April.

In the censuses she appears as Pauline Shircliff — in 1940, age 23, with her husband Edward, 29, and their three young sons in Meade County, Kentucky; in 1950, age 33, as Pauline P. Shircliff with her husband James E., 39, and eight children in Hamilton Township, Franklin County, Ohio. Her son James Wayne’s Kentucky birth index entry names her as Rita French, and her husband’s family tree profile gives her full name as Rita Pauline French — one woman, not two.

She died in 1997. Both her birth and death years come from the family tree alone; no birth or death record has been located yet.
