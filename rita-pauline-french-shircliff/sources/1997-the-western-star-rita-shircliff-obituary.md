# Pauline (Rita Pauline French) Shircliff — obituary transcription

**Source cited by Find a Grave memorial 53662524:** *The Western Star* (Lebanon, Ohio), Wednesday, 28 May 1997, page 4A. This is transcribed from the clipping text reproduced on the memorial; the original newspaper image was not located in this pass.

> Pauline Shircliff, 81, of South Lebanon and formerly of Evendale, died Friday, May 23 at the Garden Manor Nursing Home in Middletown.
>
> She was born in Sunfish, Ky., the daughter of Jim and Bulla (Bush) French. She was a homemaker and a member of the St. Francis DeSales Catholic Church in Lebanon.
>
> She was preceded in death by her parents; and daughter Joyce Ann.
>
> She is survived by her husband of 61 years, James Edward Shircliff of South Lebanon: daughter Rita Gallagher of Middletown; sons Wayne and Raymond of Cincinnati, Joseph, Ronald, Jerry and Lawrence all of Mason, and Kenneth and Robert both of West Chester; 37 grandchildren: and 17 great-grandchildren.
>
> Funeral service was held Tuesday, May 27 at St. Francis DeSales Catholic Church with burial at Lebanon Cemetery. Arrangements were handled by Stine Funeral Home, Lebanon.

**Research use:** This obituary directly reports that Pauline was a daughter of “Jim and Bulla (Bush) French,” supporting the French/Bush parent family for Rita. It does not provide their full names; “Bulla” conflicts with the Sarilda/Surilda/Shillie name forms from FamilySearch/Find a Grave. It also gives the names of nine surviving children, Joyce Ann predeceased, with some names not reconciled to the census initials. Preserve the original wording and unresolved identity issues.
