# Rita Pauline French Shircliff — parentage, siblings, children, obituary and photos

**Research date:** 25 November 2026 (America/New_York)  
**Couple:** Rita Pauline French Shircliff × James Edward Shircliff

## Parents (working assignment; discrepancy preserved)

The 1997 obituary transcription calls Rita a daughter of “Jim and Bulla (Bush) French,” and says she was born in Sunfish, Kentucky. Rita’s public FamilySearch tree (GKWH-G1S) names James Joshua French (1882–1962) and Surilda Ellen Bush (1884–1968); Find a Grave memorials for Rita and Sarilda name the couple as James Joshua French and Sarilda Ellen “Shillie” Bush French. Geography, age, surname and repeated household group align. I’ve added them to Gen VII as **working parents (about 75% confidence, not proven)** and linked both ways. The mother's name is not glossed over: “Bulla” in the obituary transcription conflicts with Sarilda/Surilda/Shillie in the family-tree/memorial data. No original birth certificate naming Rita’s parents was reviewed here.

## Siblings

Rita’s public FamilySearch tree says “Siblings (9)”—including Rita—and shows Oscar Francis French (1905–1978), Ruth Ann French Hayes (1907–1971), Raymond French (1909–; death not shown), Mary Laura French Ray (1914–1988), and Rita (1916–1997), followed by four unnamed children hidden from the public text. Sarilda’s Find a Grave memorial also names Teresa Mildred French Whelan (1918–2003) and Lula Gertrude French Sipes (1919–1938) as children. The profile now lists these **six named siblings**, with a note that the public tree count implies two other children whose identities are not visible. Those two are not guessed or made into profiles.

## Children and the “more kids” question

The 1940 and 1950 census transcriptions on the project identify nine child entries across the two household snapshots: James Wayne, Joseph H., Raymond E., Ronald E., Mary R., Jerome C., Howard A., Charles K., and Joyce Ann (1941–1941; the latter is currently from tree/memorial material, not original death evidence). James/Rita’s memorial page also links Robert Anthony Shircliff (1949–2020).

The text of a 1997 newspaper obituary reproduced on Rita’s memorial names her surviving daughter **Rita Gallagher** and sons **Wayne, Raymond, Joseph, Ronald, Jerry, Lawrence, Kenneth, and Robert**, plus deceased daughter Joyce Ann. This is a substantial additional child-name list, but it conflicts with the earlier census initials and the FamilySearch tree’s short child list. Wayne is supported as James Wayne; Jerome/Jerry and Robert/Robert Anthony are plausible matches but not yet bridged. Rita Gallagher, Lawrence and Kenneth are not reconciled to specific census children. Mary R., Howard A., and Charles K. appear in the 1950 transcription but not under those names in the obituary. I have not merged or suppressed any form. The exact total may be ten including Joyce, based on the obituary’s nine surviving children plus Joyce, but retain the differing-source lists until original birth records or more obituaries identify aliases.

## Photos archived and credited

The shared headstone photo on Find a Grave memorials 53662522 and 53662524 was copied to Rita’s `img/headstones/` archive and displayed in her gallery. The source page credits Find a Grave contributor **DebOhio**. The stone inscribes Pauline’s and Edward’s names/dates and the marriage date April 21, 1936; it is a marker image, not a portrait. No individual portrait of Rita was found.

## Date and burial notes

Find a Grave’s stone inscription gives 21 April 1936 for marriage; Rita’s public FamilySearch tree gives 18 April 1936. The original marriage entry is still needed to resolve the three-day discrepancy. The Lebanon Cemetery index separately lists Pauline Shircliff, interment 10532, Section I, lot 67-9.
