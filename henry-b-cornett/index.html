Family Relationship Notes

FamilySearch’s compiled profile assigns Henry B. Cornett to parents Arthur Cornett and Amanda Cornett. No original birth, census, or parent record for Henry was reviewed here; keep that parent couple as a compiled-tree assignment, not an independently established fact.

FamilySearch’s compiled family group identifies Henry’s spouse as Mary Helen Randolph and lists seven children. The 1882 marriage index records Henry B. Cornet and Mary H. Randolph in Pulaski County; an original image is saved below. The 1910 census places Henry B. and Mary H. together and lists William M. (age 12) as their son. William’s 1925 marriage index also names “Hen Cornett” as father, making the Henry–Mary parent assignment for William high confidence.

The 1910 census index spells the son “Luthur B.”, while the compiled profile names “Luther Blaine”; it also lists daughter “Elsie A.” while the compiled child group lists “Elyza” (1902–1981). These may be corresponding people, but exact identity/spelling is not proven. The compiled Henry profile also contains a later family with Temperance Jane Nicely; those later children are not included in the Henry–Mary child list here.
