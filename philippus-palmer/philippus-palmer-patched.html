Life Story

Philippus Palmer was born on 29 September 1864 in Kleinjetscha, a Danube Swabian village in the Banat, today Iecea Mică in Romania. In Giel's Kleinjetscha family book he heads family P56 and is called Philipp; in his son's baptism register entry he is Philippus Palmer, tenant cottager — inquilinus — living at house 217 in the village. He was the third child of Philipp Palmer and Marianna Heinrich, who had married at Kleinjetscha in 1860.

On 9 January 1888, per the family book, he married Barbara Wagner of Hatzfeld, today Jimbolia. The wedding record itself has never been found, though the Kleinjetscha marriages 1852–1893, the Hatzfeld register for January 1888, and the Großjetscha marriages 1887–1889 were all read entry by entry — Giel's book's date may be a banns or betrothal date, or the wedding took place in a fourth parish. Four children followed: Anna in 1889, Heinrich, who lived twelve days in November 1890, Nikolaus, who lived two days in April 1892, and Peter, born and baptized on 20 September 1893 at Iecea Mică, the only one known to have survived childhood. Peter would emigrate to Cincinnati and marry Margaret Lorch.

Philippus died on 31 October 1910, per Giel's P56 entry citing the civil register — a date recovered in October 2026, after the couple profile had recorded his death date as not found. No photograph of Philippus has been found, and no burial record or Find a Grave memorial has been found.
