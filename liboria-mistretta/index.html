Family Relationship Notes

Liboria Mistretta is named as Joseph Carruba’s mother on the San Cataldo christening for Joseph Carruba, baptised 14 October 1892, born 13 October 1892, godmother Carmela Mistretta. Her husband Salvatore Carruba is named as Joseph’s father on the same record.

Her son Joseph was born 31 August 1891 per the Social Security Death Index and the Kentucky death index, died 10 December 1970 in Harlan County, Kentucky, married Mary Schillaci on 17 June 1922 in Thomas, Jefferson County, Alabama, and worked as a coal miner. Her son Norman, born about 1894–1895 in Italy, married Cora Romano and also worked as a coal miner; Norman is proved as Joseph’s brother by the 1920 census image, which lists Joseph as “Brother” in Norman’s household at Millport, Alabama.

The christening gives Joseph’s birth as 13 October 1892, against 31 August 1891 on the Social Security Death Index and the Kentucky death index. The birth-date conflict is carried in Research Notes, not on the family cards.

Life Story

Liboria Mistretta lived in San Cataldo, Caltanissetta, Sicily. She is named as Joseph Carruba’s mother, with her husband Salvatore Carruba as Joseph’s father, on the San Cataldo christening of 14 October 1892; the godmother was Carmela Mistretta. Her son Joseph was born 31 August 1891, married Mary Schillaci in Alabama in 1922, worked as a coal miner, and died in Harlan County, Kentucky, in 1970. Her son Norman, born about 1894, married Cora Romano and also worked as a coal miner. No record of Liboria’s own birth, marriage, or death has been found.
