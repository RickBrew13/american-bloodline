# Liboria Mistretta — dig notes
Date: 28 November 2026. FamilySearch signed-in pass (RickBrewster1), images read and re-read letter by letter.

## What was found
- Liboria Mistretta is named as Joseph Carruba's mother on the San Cataldo christening for Joseph Carruba, baptised 14 October 1892, born 13 October 1892, godmother Carmela Mistretta (ARK 1:1:X936-8HRH). Read from the image and re-read letter by letter in a second pass.
- Rick ruled 28 November 2026 that Salvatore Carruba and Liboria Mistretta ARE Joseph Carruba's parents. They are listed plain on Joseph's page and here.
- Her son Joseph was born 31 August 1891 per the Social Security Death Index and the Kentucky death index, died 10 December 1970 in Harlan County, Kentucky, married Mary Schillaci 17 June 1922, and worked as a coal miner. Her son Norman, born about 1894-1895, married Cora Romano and also worked as a coal miner.

## Not found / not assigned
- The christening gives Joseph's birth as 13 October 1892, against 31 August 1891 on the Social Security Death Index and the Kentucky death index. The birth-date conflict is carried in Research Notes on Joseph's page and here, not on the family cards.
- The godmother on the christening, Carmela Mistretta, carries Liboria's surname; no record ties her to Liboria beyond that.
- No record of Liboria herself has been found beyond her naming as Joseph's mother on the 1892 christening.
