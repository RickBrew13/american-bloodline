## Ohio marriage record search limitations
Ohio History Connection's marriage guide says there is **no statewide marriage index**; marriage records are kept by the county probate courts. FamilySearch's Ohio County Marriages collection includes indexes and images, but some records are not name-indexed and require browsing by county:
- Ohio History Connection guide: https://ohiohistory.libguides.com/vital/marriagerecords
- FamilySearch Ohio County Marriages, 1789–2016: https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/1614804
- Franklin County Probate Court says it holds marriage records from 1803 to present; the public online search covers 1995 onward, and historical records 1803–1994 are held by the court: https://probate.franklincountyohio.gov/Record-Search/Marriage-License-Search

The couple's son was born in Columbus in 1964, so Franklin County is a logical place to begin, but it is not proof that either marriage occurred there. Other counties and states remain possible, especially because Dorothy's family lived in Michigan and she later lived in Highland County. No court record search or paid request was submitted.
