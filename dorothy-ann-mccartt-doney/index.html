Life Story

Dorothy Ann McCartt was born on 14 March 1939 in Caretta, a coal town in McDowell County, West Virginia, the daughter of William "Bill" and Flora (Strunk) McCartt SUPPORTED. Her father was a coal miner; about 1950, when Dorothy was eleven, the family left Tennessee for Michigan so he could work in the automobile industry, settling first in Warren SUPPORTED. With William M. Dague she had a son, Dewayne, born in Columbus in 1964 DOCUMENTED.

For twenty-three years she ran Babington's Campground, 11993 Spruance Rd, Hillsboro, near Rocky Fork Lake. She loved cooking, cards and board games, and making floral arrangements SUPPORTED. Her grandchildren were Brian Dague and Amber Dague, and her great-grandchildren Miles and Kailen Brewster SUPPORTED. She outlived her husband Robert L. Doney, who died in 2004, and her son Dewayne, and died on 22 October 2020, at eighty-one. She rests in Hillsboro Cemetery, Section Q, lot 51A, grave 3, beside Robert DOCUMENTED.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Babington's first name and the dates of her marriages are not yet found. Emerson Lee Babington, who owned Babington's Campground for over 60 years, is not her Babington husband — his wives were Darlene (Conaway) Babington and Mary Margo West Babington.
  • Her sister Janice McCartt died before November 2018; no dates or married name found. A Janice McCartt Beatty of Baton Rouge, Louisiana (1932–2024) is a different woman — her parents were Henry McCartt and Ruth Mayhall McCartt.
  • Her brother Kenneth McCartt and sister Thelma Frazier have no separate obituaries found in free sources.
  • Robert L. Doney's birth year, 1947, is in the Hillsboro Cemetery record; an SSDI-derived index gives his birth date as 25 December 1947. His middle name is not found.
  • Mary Margo West Babington's death is given as June 2012 in her husband's obituary and June 5, 2013 in the Hillsboro Cemetery record — a one-year conflict, unresolved.
  • No portrait or headstone photograph has been found. Find a Grave memorial 217657427 carries no photos, and the Hillsboro Cemetery site posts stone photos only for Sections A and C, not Section Q where Dorothy lies.
