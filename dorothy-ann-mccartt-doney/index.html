Life Story

Dorothy Ann McCartt was born on 14 March 1939 in Caretta, a coal town in McDowell County, West Virginia, the daughter of William “Bill” and Flora (Strunk) McCartt. Her father was a coal miner; about 1950, when Dorothy was eleven, the family left Tennessee for Michigan so he could work in the automobile industry, settling first in Warren. Her parents' shared cemetery marker and user-contributed memorials report Bill as about 1908–1972 and Flora as 1910–1977; the exact civil birth/death records have not been reviewed. By 1970, Bill was listed in his mother's obituary as living in Browns Mills, New Jersey, and both parents are reported buried together at Odd Fellows Cemetery in Pemberton, New Jersey. With William M. Dague she had a son, Dewayne, born in Columbus in 1964.

For twenty-three years she ran Babington's Campground, 11993 Spruance Rd, Hillsboro, near Rocky Fork Lake. The campground was owned and operated for over 60 years by Emerson Lee Babington, whose obituary names his wives as Darlene and Mary Margo; no record links Emerson as Dorothy's husband. The Babington surname in Dorothy's obituary therefore remains an earlier, unidentified marital name, not a proven relationship to the campground owner. She loved cooking, cards and board games, and making floral arrangements. Her grandchildren were Brian Dague and Amber Dague, and her great-grandchildren Miles and Kailen Brewster. She outlived her husband Robert L. Doney, who died in 2004, and her son Dewayne, and died on 22 October 2020, at eighty-one. She rests in Hillsboro Cemetery, Section Q, lot 51A, grave 3, beside Robert.
