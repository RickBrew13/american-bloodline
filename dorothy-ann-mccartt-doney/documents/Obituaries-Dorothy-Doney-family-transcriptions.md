## 1. Dorothy Ann (McCartt) Babington Doney — Times Gazette (Hillsboro, OH), published Oct. 26–27, 2020

Source: https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/timesgazette/name/dorothy-doney-obituary?id=10199132
Funeral home: Turner & Son Funeral Home and Crematory, 602 N High Street, Hillsboro, OH 45133.

> Dorothy Ann (McCartt) Babington Doney, 81, of Loveland, formerly of Hillsboro, passed away Thursday, Oct. 22, 2020 at the Hospice of Cincinnati in Blue Ash.

>
> In addition to her parents, she was preceded in death by her husband, Robert Doney; son, DeWayne Dague; sister, Thelma Frazier; two brothers, Kenneth McCartt and Denny McCartt; and two brothers-in-law, Mont Frazier and Clifford Bridges.
>
> Graveside services will be held at 1 p.m. on Tuesday, Oct. 27 in the Hillsboro Cemetery.
>
> In lieu of flowers, memorial contributions may be made to the St. Jude Children's Research Hospital, 501 Jude Place, Memphis, Tennessee 38105.
>
> The Turner & Son Funeral Home is serving the family.

## 2. Denny McCartt (brother) — D.S. Temrowski & Sons Funeral Home, Warren, MI, Nov. 10, 2018

Source: https://www.dignitymemorial.com/obituaries/warren-mi/denny-mccartt-10351773
Also published on Legacy.com: https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/name/denny-mccartt-obituary?pid=190718985

> Denny McCartt, age 73, died Saturday, November 10, 2018 at his home in Warren surrounded by his loving family. Denny was born July 12, 1945 in Huntsville, Tennessee, son of the late William McCartt and the late Flora (nee: Strunk) McCartt. Denny's family moved from Tennessee to Michigan when Denny was 5 years old so that his father could leave coal mining for the automobile industry. The family first settled in Warren, Michigan and later moved to Detroit's eastside. Denny's love affair with his wife began early. At the age of 14, he and his family moved down the street from the Self family and soon thereafter young Denny found himself courting Jaclyn Self. The couple were married in October of 1963 and the romance continued for the next 55 years. Denny learned the trade of welding in his early 20's and worked for Punch Press as a welder, and later for Superior Welding, before starting his own welding business in the late 70's. Denny was a gifted welder and could weld just about any material. He also created artistic welded pieces, as well as fixing many different items he bought over the years at garage sales. During his life, Denny always looked at something broken and tried to figure out how he could fix and later use the item. When Denny was not working he enjoyed traveling to the northern Michigan town of Frankfurt where he kept a 5th wheel camper so he and his family could enjoy the summers up north. Denny enjoyed fishing, and trolling garage sales, along with target shooting. He loaded his own ammunition: a true outdoors man. He and his wife later traveled to Hawaii many times, along with spending Easter in Florida either in Daytona or Disney. When Denny was at home he could be found tinkering and trying to fix something or mowing his lawn to a meticulous standard. Whatever he did he did to the best of his ability and took great pride in doing so. Denny will forever be missed and remembered by his entire family, as well as everyone he has met over the years. Denny was the loving husband of Jaclyn for 55 years. Beloved Father of Denise (Ronald) Huren, Deborah (James) Lapere and Michele McCartt. Dear brother of Nellie Bridges, Jack (Alice) McCartt, Dorothy Doney, Melvin (Gloria) McCartt, the late Kenneth McCartt, the late Thelma Frazier, and the late Janice McCartt. Special grandfather of Daniel (Mandy) Lapere, Shauna (Kevin) Frankowski, Jennifer (Jonathon) Sitarski, Kristen (Derek) Cervi, Michael Helsper and Zachary Adams. Treasured great grandfather of Payton, Daniel, Jordyn, Eastyn, Kaitlynn, Laikyn and Phelan. The family will receive friends on Friday, November 16th from 9:30 am until the time of his memorial service at 11:30 am at the D.S. Temrowski & Sons Funeral Home, 30009 Hoover Road at Common (12 ½ Mile Rd) in Warren. Memorial donations may be made to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital, 501 St. Jude Place, Memphis, TN 38105.

Key points for Dorothy's family: father's full name William McCartt; family moved Tennessee → Michigan about 1950 when Denny was 5, so the father could leave coal mining for the auto industry; first settled in Warren, Michigan, later Detroit's east side; a sister Janice McCartt, deceased before November 2018, is named here and nowhere else found.

## 5. Emerson Lee Babington — Times Gazette (Hillsboro, OH), published Mar. 23–24, 2021

Source: https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/timesgazette/name/emerson-babington-obituary?id=7416577

> Emerson Lee Babington, 91, of Hillsboro, passed away Sunday, March 21, 2021 at the Laurels of Hillsboro.
>
> He was born on Sept. 27, 1929, in Montgomery County, Ohio, the son of the late Earl and Dorothy (Recher) Babington.
>
> Emerson attended the United Missionary Church on Harriett Road. He worked for General Electric for 16 years and then went on to own and operate Babington's Campground for over 60 years. He was a hard worker and never met a stranger.
>
> Emerson is survived by three children, Daniel Babington of Hillsboro, Genny (David) Glassburn of Leesburg and J.P. Recher of Palm Springs, California; and seven grandchildren, Celena, Chris, Brian, Emerson, Tracilynn, Matthew and Marcus.
>
> In addition to his parents, he was preceded in death by his wife, Mary Margo Babington, in June 2012; and two sisters, Ruth Cline and Evelyn Oney.
>
> Private gravesides services will be held in the Hillsboro Cemetery at the convenience of the family.
>
> In lieu of flowers, memorial contributions may be made to the American Cancer Society, 2808 Reading Rd., Cincinnati, Ohio 45206.

Why this is filed here: Emerson owned Babington's Campground, which Dorothy operated for 23 years. His obituary does NOT name Dorothy as a wife, and his son Daniel's obituary names Daniel's mother as Darlene (Conaway) Babington — so Emerson is NOT Dorothy's Babington husband. Do not merge them. Filed to keep the distinction on record.

Note: Emerson's obituary says his wife Mary Margo died "in June 2012"; the Hillsboro Cemetery person file gives her death as June 5, 2013. Unresolved one-year conflict; the cemetery record is the later, stone-verified source.
