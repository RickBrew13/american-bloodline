Family Relationship Notes

Anna’s compiled FamilySearch profile names William Bradford Wethington and Rose Jane Clements as parents, reports her marriage to Clement Herman Woodrum on 22 November 1927, and lists eight children. Virts family genealogy also lists eight children.

Rose Jane’s 1958 obituary transcription (attached to her Find a Grave memorial) names children Harvey, Edith Woodrum, Urial Clements, Curtis, Bernice, Vatus, Protus and Verlin. Anna’s 2002 obituary directly names her sisters Urial Clements and Vatus Green and seven children who survived her.

The various sibling lists differ. FamilySearch’s compiled sibling list includes Ira and Curtis; Find a Grave panels list William Herbert and James Verlin; the 1958 obituary gives “Verlin” but not the expanded James Verlin. Keep these as source-attributed names rather than asserting a complete sibling set.

Clement’s 1972 obituary clipping identifies eight children; Anna’s 2002 obituary names seven surviving her. Their lists are consistent with Wilda having died earlier but do not independently prove that identification.

Life Story

Anna Edith Wethington was born 1 June 1911 in Casey County, according to compiled FamilySearch and Find a Grave profiles. FamilySearch names William Bradford Wethington and Rose Jane Clements as her parents and reports her 1927 marriage to Clement Herman Woodrum. The original birth and marriage records have not been reviewed.

The Virts family genealogy lists eight children. A 1972 obituary clipping for Clement also names eight children, while Anna’s 2002 obituary transcription names seven who survived her: Milburn, Bobby, Clement, Alta Gabehart, Janice Campbell, Velma Wethington and Charlene Warner. The obituary identifies Anna’s sisters as Urial Clements and Vatus Green. Rose Jane’s 1958 obituary transcription names Anna as “Edith Woodrum” among her children and lists her siblings by names used at that time.

Anna’s Find a Grave entry reports burial at Saint Bernard Cemetery. Its attached image set contains a marker whose names and dates do not match Anna; it is not used as her headstone or portrait.
