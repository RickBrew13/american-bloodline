Life Story

Anna Edith Wethington was born in Casey County, Kentucky, on 1 June 1911, according to her FamilySearch profile. That profile names William Bradford Wethington and Rose Jane Clements as her parents, records her 1927 marriage to Clement Herman Woodrum, and lists Janice Woodrum among their children. The Virts family genealogy independently lists Janice E. Woodrum as a child of Anna Edith and Clement. Janice’s 1950 census index entry gives parents as Herman and Edith Woodrum, which fits this couple, though the original census image and Janice’s birth record have not been reviewed.
