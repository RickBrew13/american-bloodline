# Anna Edith (Wethington) Woodrum — family and source audit
**Date:** 26 November 2026

## Scope
Reviewed Anna’s existing profile and person folder, Clement’s profile, the existing Janice follow-up note, FamilySearch’s compiled Anna profile, the Virts family group F30293, Find a Grave memorial panels and obituary transcriptions. No Drive/Docs originals were touched.

## Parents and siblings
Anna’s parents are already listed as William Bradford Wethington and Rose Jane Clements. FamilySearch and Find a Grave report the same parents; Rose’s 1958 obituary transcription names daughter Edith Woodrum and children Harvey, Urial, Curtis, Bernice, Vatus, Protus and Verlin. Anna’s 2002 obituary names her sisters Urial Clements and Vatus Green. Other sibling names vary across FamilySearch and Find a Grave: Ira is on the compiled FS list; William Herbert is on a memorial panel; Verlin may be the same as James Verlin but that expansion is not proven. The updated page states these differences rather than claiming a complete sibling set.

## Marriage and children
FamilySearch and the Virts compilation report Anna married Clement Herman Woodrum 22 November 1927 in Casey County and had eight children. Clement’s 1972 obituary clipping lists eight children by names: Milburn J.; Bobby C.; Alta Roe Gabehart; Janice E. Campbell; Velma M. Wethington; Wilda Jean Warfield; William Clement Woodrum; Anna Charlene Warner. Anna’s 2002 obituary names seven children surviving her, and supports Janice Campbell, Alta Gabehart, Velma Wethington, Charlene Warner and the sons Milburn/Bobby/Clement. Virts family group uses some different expanded names; all variants remain attributed. Wilda’s absence from Anna’s 2002 survivor list may reflect an earlier death; no direct record was reviewed.

## Images
The images attached to Anna’s memorial were checked through image transcription. They show a marker for “Roni Jane” and “William B.” with dates 1896–1958 and 1897–1960, not Anna’s dates. They are misattached or otherwise unrelated and are excluded from the profile. The memorial image for Rose/W. Bradford Wethington also contains stone dates inconsistent with Find a Grave metadata; do not treat those image inscriptions as Anna’s parent dates. No identity-verified portrait or local family photo was found. Direct Find a Grave image downloads were blocked; no pictures were copied to the repository.

## Burial / dates
Compiled profile and memorial report Anna as born 1 June 1911 and died 29 July 2002. Her obituary transcription reports death at Summit Manor, Columbia, and funeral at St. Bernard’s Church in Clementsville with burial in its cemetery. This is consistent with the memorial’s Saint Bernard Cemetery entry, but no original certificate or cemetery register was inspected.

## Profile changes prepared
Updated Anna’s Gen VI page with parent names, eight reported children and sibling evidence, source-qualified facts, timeline, source links and photo exclusions. Added reciprocal spouse link to Clement, and dated research/source/photo notes in both Gen VI folders.
