Life Story

Nancy Caroline Ridenour was born on 18 February 1868 in Jackson County, West Virginia, the daughter of Benjamin Franklin Ridenour and Emaline Mazella Smith. Her birth is one of the best-documented in this generation: the county Register of Births, page 579, names her father "B. F. Ridenhour" and her mother Emaline, and her mother signed the entry herself. She was the second of the register's three recorded children of the couple, after Alpha Omega (6 April 1866) and before E. M. (25 October 1869).

She married Walter Weston Polsley. No marriage record for the pair has been found, and the claims in circulation do not agree: the family genealogy gives 3 January 1884, FamilySearch gives 1883, and a Yates transcription gives 21 May 1886 — six days after Rhoda's birth, so not adopted. What is certain is the family itself: four children — Ollie, who died in infancy; Rhoda Alice, the direct line, born 15 May 1886 at Letart; Edna Vella; and Verna Valeria, born 24 February 1893.

Caroline died on 28 July 1894, aged 26, and was buried at Hopewell Baptist Church Cemetery at Mt. Alto, where her marker spells her married name "Polsey". No obituary or family tale for Caroline herself has been found, and no cause of death is recorded; the page does not guess at one. Her husband married again the following Christmas, to Esther Donie Skeen.

No portrait of Caroline herself has been found; her gravestone photograph, shown here, comes from her Find a Grave memorial. The couple folder holds five record images (her birth register page, the 1895 marriage license, Rose Mason's death record, Verna's death certificate and a zoom of it) and four contextual place photographs; documents are cited below, place photographs are not shown as family pictures.

  • Naming: the list and profile title use "Caroline Ridenour"; the folder and the body use Nancy Caroline Ridenour.
