Life Story

Eliza Tankersley was born on 26 December 1845 SUPPORTED. She married Asberry Helton in Knox County, Kentucky, on 5 October 1868 DOCUMENTED. Her daughter Ella's 1899 marriage certificate names her as the bride's mother, Eliza Helton DOCUMENTED. She died on 25 January 1914 and is buried beside Asberry on Indian Creek SUPPORTED.
