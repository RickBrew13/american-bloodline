Life Story

Bertha, known as Bella, was born in Russia, the daughter of Aaron Mahle and Kati (Kate) Tahmali DOCUMENTED. A nurse in Cincinnati, she married Alex Luft, a German-born butcher, on 1 July 1912 DOCUMENTED, and they raised their children in Cincinnati SUPPORTED. Her daughter Minnie's death record gives her maiden name as Tomarin, a different spelling of the same name SUPPORTED. Her marriage application writes it "Mahle," close to her mother's name Tahmali. She outlived Alexander by thirty-two years and died in Cincinnati on 21 June 1976 DOCUMENTED.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Spelling of her maiden name (Mahle, Tahmali, Tarmin, Tomarin, Tamarin) is not settled. The bride's surname is written "Mahle" on the application, while her children's records give Tomarin, Tarmin, or Tamine. "Mahle" reads as her mother's name "Tahmali" without its first sound; both bride and groom signed by mark, so the clerk wrote the names by ear.
