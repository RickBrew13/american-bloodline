Family Relationship Notes

The 1 July 1912 marriage application names the bride as Bella Mahle, age 25, born in Russia, daughter of Aaron Mahle and Kati (Kate) Tahmali. The Social Security/death index identifies Bella Luft as born 7 April 1885 and dying 21 June 1976. Her children’s records use the names Bertha Tarmin, Tarmin, Tomarin, or similar forms. These records are the basis for treating Bella Luft and Bertha Tarmin as the same woman, but the exact maiden-name spelling remains unresolved.

The marriage application lists Bella’s occupation as nurse. Alexander’s death certificate records his death in 1944 and burial at Covedale; Bertha’s own burial place has not been identified.

Records identify Minnie Luft (1914–1999), David Luft (1916–1993), and Eva L. Luft (1918–2002) as children. Harry (Henry) Luft, born about 1913, appears in the existing family research. Anne Luft, later Grinter, remains an unconfirmed additional child and is not included in the Children card.

William Tamarin, born about 1880 in Russia, is a possible brother only. No record has yet established the relationship, so he is not listed in the Siblings card.

Life Story

Bertha, known as Bella, was born in Russia, the daughter of Aaron Mahle and Kati (Kate) Tahmali . A nurse in Cincinnati, she married Alex Luft, a German-born butcher, on 1 July 1912 , and they raised their children in Cincinnati . Her daughter Minnie's death record gives a similar maternal surname, transcribed as Tomarin; that variation does not by itself establish that it matches the bride's “Mahle” on the marriage application . Her marriage application writes it "Mahle," close to her mother's name Tahmali. She outlived Alexander by thirty-two years and died in Cincinnati on 21 June 1976 .
