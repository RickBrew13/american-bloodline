# Bertha Tarmin / Bella Mahle — surname and William/Bill lead

**Reviewed:** 24 November 2026

## Bella / Bertha identity evidence

The 1912 Hamilton County marriage application calls the bride Bella Mahle and names her parents as Aaron Mahle and Kati (Kate) Tahmali. A derivative SortedByName compilation transcribes several of Bella's children's Social Security applications: Eva's cards identify her mother as Bella and also use Bertha Tarmin/Tumarn; Minnie’s card gives mother Belle; David’s card gives mother Berthia. The listed cards include Eva SS-5 65332608996 (Oct. 1962) and 65332608997 (May 1938), Minnie SS-5 65337767611 (May 1938), and David SS-5 66150905894 (Feb. 1937). This provides converging support for the working identification Bella Mahle = Bertha Tarmin Luft, but the SS-5 cards themselves have not yet been inspected. The surname relationship Mahle / Tahmali / Tarmin / Tomarin / Tamarin remains unresolved; a prior phonetic explanation was speculation and is withdrawn.

## William Tamarin / Bill Tomarin — possible sibling lead, not proof

FamilySearch's 1950 census index returns William Tamarin, born in Russia in 1880, in Cincinnati on 1 April 1950; no family relationships are supplied. The Jewish Cemeteries of Greater Cincinnati register lists Bill Tomarin, born 15 April 1880, died 6 July 1957, service 9 July, Love Brothers Cemetery, Section 003, Row 02, Grave 81. The register identifies Violet Tomarin as his wife. A derivative transcription of William's SS-5 65327807598 (April 1938) gives the same exact birth date and Russia as birthplace, and names his parents Adron Tomarin and Kessia T. Zeltzman. William Tamarin and Bill Tomarin are very likely the same man across these records.

This does not prove he was Bella/Bertha's brother. The reported parents Adron Tomarin / Kessia T. Zeltzman do not directly match the 1912 application's Aaron Mahle / Kati Tahmali. The relationship could involve name variants or a separate family, but no record currently bridges them. Bill remains unlisted in the Siblings card.

Other Cincinnati cemetery entries include Marcus/Sima Tomarin and Harry/Toby Tomarin; the register does not connect them to Bill or Bella. No kinship was inferred from shared surname or cemetery.
