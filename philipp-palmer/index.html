Life Story

Philipp Palmer was born on 22 August 1837 in Kleinjetscha, in the Banat — a date carried from Dietmar Giel’s Familienbuch, entry P53, and not yet checked against a church register. He was the son of Peter Palmer and Elisabeth Reisz of Kleinjetscha, at house 125 in the village; his marriage register entry names them as his parents. He was the seventh child of Peter and Elisabeth. No photograph of Philipp has been found.

On 31 January 1860, at the Catholic parish of Kleinjetscha, Philipp married Marianna Heinrich, 23, the widow of Georgius Benz, of house 147. The marriage register entry — entry #1, page 14 — is the couple’s founding record. They had six children: two whose names have not yet been recovered, born about 1860–1863; Philippus, born 29 September 1864; Anna, born 9 December 1866, who died on 19 April 1868; Josef, born 28 April 1869, who died on 21 November 1872; and Gertrud, whose birth date is cut off in every recovered text window of the Familienbuch entry. Philippus married Barbara Wagner; he is the direct line.

Philipp died between 1860 and May 1883 — by then Marianna had remarried, to Nicolaus Konrad, and the record names her as the widow of Philipp Palmer. His exact death date has not been found: no Kleinjetscha volume later than FamilySearch film 858420 is listed in the FamilySearch catalog, so no 1860 marriage volume and no death register covering 1860–1883 exists there, and the Iecea Mică baptism register that would hold his two oldest children’s baptisms is held staff-only at the Romanian National Archives. Marianna lived on until 21 February 1916.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Grades on this page: DOCUMENTED means an original record was read (the marriage register entry; the Kleinjetscha registers on FamilySearch film 858420); SUPPORTED means a claim attested by filed research — Giel’s Familienbuch, the signed-in tree checks, the bracketed death window — but not by a register read here; UNPROVED means a claim carried from the research folder with no corroboration found.
  • Parentage: Philipp’s link to Peter Palmer × Elisabeth Reisz is documented — his own marriage register entry names them as his parents, at house 125. The open question is Peter’s wife: Giel and the marriage register give Elisabeth Reisz (born 11 November 1813, died 24 December 1887), while the Luxembourg-Banat extraction (luxracines family F8357, as given in the research folder) shows Peter Palmer (born about 1797, died 1 May 1865, married 7 January 1821) × Magdalena Mayer. The death dates are compatible, so the conflict is the wife, not the man. Possibilities: two different Peter Palmers, two marriages (which would require Magdalena to have died before about 1836), or a data error on one side. A full read of Giel’s Palmer entries (P49–P57) would settle it. The Each Generation page carries Peter × Elisabeth Reisz at the next generation back.
  • Marianna’s parents, Peter Heinrich × Anna Maria Moling, are documented from the original registers, read from the images on FamilySearch film 858420: Peter baptized 21 October 1814, son of Michael Heinrich × Elisabetha Junger; Anna Maria baptized 7 January 1818, daughter of Joannes Molling × Anna Maria Sander (the register spells the surname Molling; the family form is Moling); married 15 February 1835 at Kleinjetscha.
  • Birth and death dates: Philipp’s birth date (22 August 1837) and Marianna’s dates (3 November 1836 – 21 February 1916) come from Giel’s Familienbuch, entry P53, and have not been checked against the church registers. Philipp’s death is bracketed only: married 1860, his widow remarried in 1883. No Kleinjetscha volume later than film 858420 is listed in the FamilySearch catalog, so no 1860 marriage volume and no death register covering 1860–1883 exists there.
  • Children: six per Giel P53, recovered by snippet-mining on 23 October 2026 — the Familienbuch is search-only online and no full page of P53 has been displayed. The two oldest children’s names have not been recovered, and Gertrud’s birth date is cut off in every recovered text window. Their baptisms sit in the Iecea Mică baptism register 1852–1876 (Romanian National Archives, cota TM-F-00004-01194-182-6), held staff-only online; a request for it is on hold. Anna and Josef both died young, in 1868 and 1872. Geni and FamilySearch list only Philippus as a child.
  • Marianna’s first husband was Georgius Benz — the marriage register spells Benz. Familienbuch snippets reading “Pencz” or “Pentz” misread the register. The Benz × Heinrich marriage record itself has not been found; the Konrad marriage of 1883 has not been pulled and may name Philipp’s death date.
