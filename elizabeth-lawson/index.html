Life Story

Elizabeth Lawson was born in 1807 in Grayson County, Virginia, and died 4 December 1857 in Raleigh County, aged 50, of apoplexy. She married Roland Hurt on 3 March 1825 in Patrick County, and the censuses name eight children born to them between about 1829 and 1847. Her death register names her parents “Charles & Diana Lawson” and her birthplace Grayson County — the only record that names her parents at all. No portrait of her has been found anywhere, and no stone.

The death register is the strongest paper in her file, and it was read twice. The row reads “Hurt, Eliza” — white, female, died December 4, place Raleigh County, cause Apoplexy, age 50 — and on the same ruled line: parents “Charles & Diana Lawson,” born Grayson, consort Roland Hurt, informant Roland Hurt, designation “Consort.” Her husband gave the information, exactly as expected. An earlier transcription misread the designation; the misreading is not carried here.

Her mother’s name is the unresolved question. Her daughter Mary’s 1922 death certificate names Mary’s mother “Nancy Lawson,” against Elizabeth Lawson on the 1825 marriage record, the 1850 census, and the 1857 death register; her daughter Nancy’s own certificate leaves the mother blank; and Find a Grave gives her maiden name as “Dawson,” unsourced. Three answers compete for her father as well — Charles Lawson, on the death register and on Geni; James Lawson Sr., on FamilySearch; and “Charles James Lawson Sr.,” on WikiTree’s James Lawson Jr. — and Diana’s maiden name is open. The name Elizabeth Lawson stands on the marriage record and the census, and the rest is carried here as conflict, not as settled fact.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Grades: DOCUMENTED means an original record read directly says it; SUPPORTED means several records point to it; FAMILY ACCOUNT means the family’s own telling; UNPROVED means a lead, not proved. Every fact on this page carries its grade.
  • Her parents, three live answers: the Raleigh County death register names “Charles & Diana Lawson” and her birthplace Grayson County — the only record that names her parents at all, and the answer carried here. FamilySearch attaches her to James Lawson Sr. (1760–1835) × Lucinda Cox (1782–1830), married 1798, with eight siblings attached. WikiTree’s James Lawson Jr. (Lawson-2809) has a father named “Charles James Lawson Sr.” × “Lucinda Dicey (Cox) Lawson.” Find a Grave gives her maiden name as “Dawson,” unsourced. Diana’s maiden name is open and is never given as Cox here. The father question is settled only by the register’s naming of Charles; the identity of that Charles — whether he is the “Charles James Lawson Sr.” of WikiTree or another man — stays open.
  • Roland’s parents, PROBABLE: his page carries Moses Hurt × Mary Parmer at probable — no record names Roland as Moses’s son outright; the November 1830 power of attorney names him attorney, not heir; Tricia’s Ancestry link to a Moses born 1793 is refuted as stated (that Moses was seven years old when Roland was born). The full dispute is on his page.
  • Her mother’s name, open (C17): her daughter Mary’s 1922 death certificate names Mary’s mother “Nancy Lawson”, against Elizabeth Lawson on the 1825 marriage record, the 1850 census, and the 1857 death register. Three possibilities, none established: the informant (her daughter Adelia) gave the wrong name; Roland married a second time after Elizabeth’s 1857 death (a second marriage would fall in 1857–1860, and no record of one has been found); or Elizabeth’s full name was Nancy Elizabeth Lawson. Her daughter Nancy’s own certificate leaves the mother blank. WikiTree Hurt-3484 already records the “Nancy Lawson” mother and leaves Elizabeth unattached as Mary’s mother.
  • Dicy Lawson, 68 in 1850: probably Lucy “Dicy/Ticie” Cox, wife of James Lawson Sr. — not Elizabeth’s mother Diana, and the two women are never merged. Why she lived with Roland × Elizabeth in 1850 is unproved. The compiled tradition that Elizabeth’s mother was “Dicey Cox” is the confusion of these two women.
  • Children roster: eight census children, as listed in the Children card. FamilySearch attaches nine, adding Matilda (1826), absent from the 1850 household and not carried here as a verified daughter; Geni attaches three only. WikiTree’s “Ian, Ira, Samuel, possibly triplets born 1839” is unsupported. Delilah Mellen (Find a Grave 119953922) is John Mann Hurt × Lawsey West’s daughter, not Roland’s.
  • Nancy Estelle’s birth year: her death certificate says 19 October 1837 (age 91 years 8 months 22 days at 11 July 1929, arithmetic that fits 1837); her stone reads 1838, and Find a Grave follows the stone. The certificate governs here. Her four children on FamilySearch (Minnie May, Annice Irene, Ira Jay, James Emmette) against six in the 1880 census (adding William W. Hurt and Olga B. Hurt) stays open.
  • No portrait, no stone: no portrait of Elizabeth has been found anywhere; her Find a Grave memorial has PHOTOS 0. The register images in the couple folder are documents, cited in Sources, not gallery images.
  • Still open: the Patrick County marriage bond of 1825 (a bondsman or consent may name her father); Dicy Lawson in the Patrick County records; the 1850 census image for the Roland household; the children’s marriages (Anna, Delila, Drucilla, Elizabeth, Surinda); and the identity of Charles Lawson in the Grayson tax lists and censuses.
