Family Relationship Notes

Obituaries for Dorothy Ann Doney, John “Jack” McCartt, and Nellie J. Bridges identify Flora as the mother of the McCartt children and Bill McCartt as their father. The children named across these obituaries are Nellie, Kenneth, Denny, John “Jack,” twin sister Janice, Dorothy Ann, and Thelma. Family research also notes one additional child who is still living and whose name is intentionally not published here.

Flora’s father Shelby Lee Strunk’s 1965 obituary lists a daughter Flora McCartt living in Detroit, Michigan. The same family material names Seigal, Rev. Leslie Earl, Pearl, and Harold as Flora’s siblings. Three additional sisters are mentioned in family obituaries, but their names have not been reconciled here.

Flora’s mother is most likely Nellie (Phillips) Strunk, Shelby’s first wife, but no record located so far directly identifies Nellie as Flora’s mother. She is therefore not listed as a confirmed parent.

Find a Grave memorial 105862078 is user-contributed and gives Flora’s birth as 7 July 1910, death in December 1977, and burial at Odd Fellows Cemetery, Pemberton, New Jersey. The memorial includes one photo, but the image has not yet been verified as a headstone or checked for an inscription. Bill’s 1908–1972 dates on that memorial are not independently confirmed.

Life Story

Flora Strunk was born in 1910, a daughter of Shelby Lee Strunk of Scott County, Tennessee . She married Bill McCartt; their daughter Nellie was born in Huntsville, Tennessee, in 1929, and they later raised their children in Caretta, McDowell County, West Virginia . Two of her children's obituaries name her as Flora Strunk McCartt . By 1965 she lived in Detroit, and she died in 1977 .
