Family Relationship Notes

The 1940 draft registration for Bill McCart identifies Mrs. Flora McCart as his wife; their children’s obituaries independently identify Bill and Flora (Strunk) McCartt as parents. Shelby’s 1965 obituary lists Flora McCartt among his daughters. Flora’s memorial links Nellie Phillips Strunk as her mother, and the 2001 Strunk genealogy post names a Flora Helen Strunk as daughter of Shelby and Nellie. Rick confirms the post’s William Leslie McCartt is the same man as Willie “Bill” McCartt in this couple; the forum’s 1901 birth and 1976 death dates for him are incorrect. Census ages support Bill’s birth about 1908, and the shared marker/user-contributed memorial report 1972 as his death year (exact dates not independently verified). The forum’s 16 May 1928 marriage date and Flora Helen/1911 details remain unverified. Nellie remains a probable working mother, not proven by a direct birth record. Working confidence that Nellie was Flora’s mother is about 60%; this is a cautious assignment, not proof, because the links are derivative and no direct birth record naming Nellie was reviewed.

Life Story

Flora G. (Strunk) McCartt was a daughter of Shelby Lee Strunk, whose 1965 obituary listed “Flora McCartt, Detroit, Michigan.” Her Find a Grave memorial links Shelby and Nellie Phillips Strunk as parents; this is the working parent assignment, with Nellie supported as probable mother rather than proved by a direct parent-child record. Obituaries for Nellie Bridges, John “Jack” McCartt, Dorothy Doney, Denny McCartt and Thelma Frazier identify Flora and Bill as parents of their children. The children were born in Tennessee and West Virginia; Denny’s obituary says the family moved to Michigan about 1950. Flora’s Find a Grave memorial and the shared marker report 1910–1977 and burial in Pemberton, New Jersey. The marker is a verified shared grave image, not a portrait.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Nellie Phillips Strunk is a probable, not proven, mother. Flora’s Find a Grave family links name Nellie; the Strunk family material associates Flora with Shelby and Nellie, but no original birth or civil record naming Nellie as Flora’s mother was reviewed.
  • Flora’s exact birth date is unresolved: her memorial and shared marker give 7 July 1910 / 1910, while a separate indexed lead reports 19 September 1910. Do not treat either day as established without the underlying record.
  • The 1965 Shelby obituary names Ruby Jeffers, Truie Sharp, Flora McCartt, Pearl Clark, Virginia Mae Taylor, Harold Strunk, Earl Strunk, Gordan Strunk and Seigal Strunk. The linked memorial sibling set also lists Rev. Leslie Earl; whether he is the obituary’s Earl is not proven. Maternal assignments for most siblings remain unresolved.
  • Rick confirms that the 2001 Genealogy.com reply’s William Leslie McCartt is this Bill; do not split him into a different person. The reply’s 1901 birth and 1976 death dates are incorrect in light of the 1910/1920 census ages and the 1972 shared-marker/memorial year. Its reported 16 May 1928 marriage date and Flora Helen/1911 details remain unverified.
  • Find a Grave photo tab has one shared headstone image. It is suitable as a couple marker, not as an individual portrait; attribution retained to contributor Clara McIver.
  • No marriage date for Bill and Flora has been located. They were together by Nellie’s 1929 birth and the 1940 draft registration identifies Flora as Bill’s wife.
