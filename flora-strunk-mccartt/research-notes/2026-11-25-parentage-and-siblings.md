# Flora (Strunk) McCartt — parentage and siblings (25 November 2026)

Shelby Lee Strunk is supported as Flora’s father by his 1965 obituary naming daughter Flora McCartt of Detroit, by Seigal’s obituary calling Flora a sister, and by Find a Grave’s child link. Nellie Phillips Strunk is a **probable working mother at about 60% confidence**, based mainly on user-contributed memorial links and the Strunk family evidence; sources are derivative and may not be independent, and no direct birth record naming Nellie was reviewed.

The shared marker photo is a joint headstone, not a portrait. It reports 1910–1977 for Flora and 1908–1972 for Bill; the text extraction reads only “BILL” for Bill’s name. Rick confirms the 2001 forum’s William Leslie McCartt is the same man as Flora’s husband Bill. The reply’s 1901 birth and 1976 death dates are wrong; census ages support a birth about 1908, and the shared marker/user-contributed memorial report 1972. The forum’s 16 May 1928 marriage date and Flora Helen/1911 details remain unverified.

Shelby’s 1965 obituary lists eight siblings besides Flora by name/relationship: Harold, Earl, Gordan, Seigal, Ruby, Truie, Pearl and Virginia Mae. Find a Grave separately links Rev. Leslie Earl as a sibling and Harold as a half-sibling. “Earl” may be Leslie Earl, but do not merge without a record. Mothers of most siblings are unresolved.
