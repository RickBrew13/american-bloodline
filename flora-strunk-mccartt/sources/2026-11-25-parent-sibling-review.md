# Flora (Strunk) McCartt — parent and sibling review (25 November 2026)

## Parentage

- Shelby Lee Strunk’s 1965 obituary (The Scott County News, 12 Nov. 1965, p. 1) names Flora McCartt of Detroit among his five daughters; memorial/transcription: https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/60817877/shelby-lee-strunk. This supports Shelby as Flora’s father.
- Flora’s user-contributed memorial https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/105862078/flora_g-mccartt links Shelby and Nellie Phillips as parents.
- Nellie Phillips Strunk’s 1924 obituary transcription says she was wife of S.L. Strunk and left seven children, two deceased: https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/139743759/nellie-strunk. Seigal Strunk’s obituary/memorial names Shelby and Nellie as his parents and Flora among his sisters: https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/38216997/seigal_james-strunk.
- Nellie is retained as a **probable working mother, about 60% confidence**. This is a cautious working estimate from the linked memorial parent group, the same Shelby/Nellie family context in Seigal’s obituary and the 2001 family genealogy post; these sources are derivative/possibly related and there is no direct birth record naming Nellie as Flora’s mother.

## Siblings

- Find a Grave links Seigal James Strunk (1911–1969), Rev. Leslie Earl Strunk (1912–2005), Pearl Louise (Strunk) Clark (1919–1999), and Harold Strunk (1929–2017; marked as half-brother) as Flora’s siblings. These links are derivative.
- Shelby’s 1965 obituary names Harold, Earl, Gordan and Seigal among sons; daughters Ruby Jeffers, Truie Sharp, Flora McCartt, Pearl Clark and Virginia Mae Taylor. The obituary does not assign every child to a mother.
- “Earl Strunk” in the obituary may be Rev. Leslie Earl (Find a Grave lists that person as Flora’s sibling), but that identity match is not proven. “Zeke,” “Josie,” “Occie,” and “Bell” sibling forms in Shelby’s obituary are not merged with Find a Grave’s Ezekiel/General, Josephine, Osey Violet and Verde Belle without a bridge.

## Couple’s family

Children’s obituaries identify Bill and Flora as parents. Rick confirms the Genealogy.com forum’s William Leslie McCartt is the same Bill; its 1901/1976 birth/death dates are wrong, while the census supports birth about 1908 and the shared marker/memorial report 1972. Its marriage date and Flora Helen/1911 details remain unverified. Denny’s obituary names Melvin and other siblings and reports the family move to Michigan about 1950: https://www.dignitymemorial.com/obituaries/warren-mi/denny-mccartt-10351773. Flora’s shared headstone photo is https://images.findagrave.com/photos/2013/56/105862078_136191400449.jpg?size=photos250, credited to Clara McIver; it is a grave marker, not an individual portrait.

## Unresolved

- Flora’s exact birth day differs between derivative sources (7 July vs. 19 September 1910); neither day is confirmed here.
- Marriage date/place not found.
- Maternal identities for most Strunk siblings remain unsettled.
