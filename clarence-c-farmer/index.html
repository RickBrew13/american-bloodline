Family Relationship Notes

The local Farmer family book names Charley Tipton Farmer and Deborah Sue “Debby” Wyrick Farmer as Clarence’s parents and lists the six siblings above. Debby’s memorial independently lists Charley as spouse and Earsie as their child. These compiled sources support the working family group; no Clarence birth certificate has been inspected.

The shared grave marker records Clarence and Mae (Hamilton) Farmer as married on 29 November 1928. The marker supports the date and couple identification but is not a civil marriage record.

Mona’s 2013 obituary, the Farmer family book, and cemetery memorials identify three daughters. Mabel married Paul Victor “Vic” Marcum; their son was Bruce Wayne Marcum (4 May 1950–29 March 1952). Marjorie married Thomas Charles Pilman and later Clifford Earl Shamblen; her children were Michael Thomas Pilman and Kimberly Ann (Walter) Burrous, and her obituary names grandchildren Grant Silas Pilman and Page Sanders Pilman. Mona married Walter Jackson; their daughter was Sharon (Dan) Colley, and Mona’s obituary names granddaughters Erin Colley and Sarah Bauer.

The family book lists Earsie, Lizzie B., Robert, Lillian Pearl, Arlie Jane, and Ruford Daniel as children of Charley and Debby. The book is a compiled family source, not an original vital record.

Life Story

The inscribed Tyner West Cemetery marker records Clarence C. Farmer (29 April 1902–28 May 1974) and Mae (Hamilton) Farmer (12 October 1912–28 September 1999) as married on 29 November 1928. Find a Grave places Clarence’s death in Fayette County and burial at Tyner West Cemetery, Jackson County. Their daughter Mona’s 2013 obituary names Clarence and Mae as her parents and names Mabel Marcum and Marjorie Shamblen as Mona’s sisters. The same obituary independently establishes the three daughters as a sibling set. Parentage of Clarence himself, his siblings, and siblings/parents of Mae have not yet been identified in the records reviewed. A local family genealogy identifies his parents as Charley Tipton Farmer and Debby Wyrick and names six siblings; those compiled family claims are now graded SUPPORTED, pending original vital records.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Parents and siblings of Clarence are not identified in the records reviewed. Do not transfer parents from same-name Farmer families.
  • The cemetery marker gives 1902; the 1950 census index estimates birth year 1903. Preserve both readings until an original birth/death record is found.
  • The 1950 index calls a child “Magoie.” The original census image has not been inspected; this could be an indexing/transcription issue and is not treated as an additional child beyond the three daughters named by Mona’s obituary.
  • Rick Brewster identifies the photo attached to Mabel’s Find a Grave memorial as their daughter Mabel. The shared inscribed marker is their profile image; no portrait of the parents is currently available.
  • Parents and six siblings are identified by the local Farmer family genealogy and linked family memorials; these are secondary/compiled sources, not original proof. Marriage date for Charley and Debby is stated in the book as 6 March 1901.
  • Full catalog OCR and original scan catalog link have been added to the Gen VI couple Books & Sources folder; a copy is also stored with Charley and Debby’s Gen VII family archive.
