Clarence C. Farmer × Anna Mae (Hamilton) Farmer — final research pass and source register
Date: 21 November 2026
Scope: Generation VI couple #10 only, with the evidence-supported Generation VII parent couple and records about the couple’s children.

EXECUTIVE FINDINGS
- Clarence’s parents are strongly supported as Charley Tipton Farmer (born 20 Aug 1880; died 1972 per spouse memorial) and Deborah Sue “Debby” Wyrick Farmer (24 Dec 1882–7 Aug 1968). The local family genealogy explicitly names Charley and Debby’s children, including Clarence; Debby’s Find a Grave memorial independently links spouse Charlie Tipton Farmer and daughter Earsie. The working parent link is SUPPORTED, not DOCUMENTED, because Clarence’s birth certificate/original census entry tying both parents has not yet been inspected.
- The family book lists six siblings of Clarence: Earsie, Lizzie B., Robert, Lillian Pearl, Arlie Jane, and Ruford Daniel. The site now lists them with source and uncertainty. The book is a compiled family source, not original vital proof.
- Clarence and Anna Mae’s three daughters are Mabel, Marjorie, and Mona Pearl. Mona’s obituary independently names Clarence and Mae as her parents, Mabel and Marjorie as her sisters. The family book adds names, exact dates, and spouses, with its readings graded as secondary compiled evidence.
- Anna Mae’s parents and siblings remain unidentified. Surname/cemetery coincidence does not justify assigning her to one of the other Hamilton families. FamilySearch historical-record searches were broad/noisy; targeted searches did not return a matching Kentucky 1920/1930 record for her. The 1950 index has “Annamay” with Clarence and a child indexed “Magoie”; original image not viewed and no child identity assigned from that transcription.
- The family book says Clarence and Anna Mae married 28 Nov 1928; their shared headstone says 29 Nov 1928. Keep this one-day discrepancy explicit until the civil marriage record is checked.

Charley Tipton Farmer
- Born 20 Aug 1880 per the local family book; Find a Grave’s spouse entry gives death year 1972. Exact death date/place and a primary birth record remain unlocated.
- Parents: Stephen Farmer (4 May 1848–19 Feb 1936) and Mary Frances Cook Farmer (27 Nov 1857–23 Nov 1943), SUPPORTED by FamilySearch’s compiled profile and the local book’s descent narrative. Original Charley birth record not inspected.
- The FamilySearch profile reports 16 children for Stephen and Mary; the profile view lists only a partial set of siblings. Charley’s sibling list on his new profile is labeled partial, not complete.
- Spouse: Debby Wyrick; family book says married 6 Mar 1901. No original civil marriage record yet checked.
- Children listed by the family book (p. 28 / scan p. 32): Clarence, Earsie, Lizzie B., Robert, Lillian Pearl, Arlie Jane, Ruford Daniel.

Deborah Sue “Debby” Wyrick Farmer
- Find a Grave memorial 5350233 gives 24 Dec 1882–7 Aug 1968, Jackson County, Kentucky; burial Farmer Cemetery, Tyner.
- Parents per Find a Grave family links: William A. “Billy” Wyrick (1841–1905) and Martha “Patsy” Simpson Wyrick (1843–1921). George Washington Wyrick’s memorial repeats this couple.
- Find a Grave memorial links siblings George Washington Wyrick (1871–1944), Anna Jane “Annie” Wyrick Farmer (1872–1965), Silas Buren Wyrick (1880–1962), Mary Ellen Wyrick York (1885–1968), and Malinda “Lindy” Wyrick Davis (1886–1972). Secondary memorial evidence; no original birth record for Debby was inspected.
- Spouse Charley Tipton Farmer (1880–1972); family book says marriage 6 Mar 1901.

CLARENCE AND ANNA MAE’S CHILDREN
1. Mabel Farmer Marcum — book says born 1 Apr 1930; Find a Grave memorial 151866553 gives death 29 Nov 1991, spouse Paul Victor “Vic” Marcum, and son Bruce Wayne Marcum. Mona’s obituary independently calls Mabel her sister and says Mabel’s son Bruce predeceased Mona. Find a Grave memorial 151866531 gives Bruce’s dates 4 May 1950–29 Mar 1952 and parents Mabel and Paul. Book and memorials are secondary; the sibling relationship is directly stated in Mona’s obituary.
2. Marjorie Farmer, later Pilman/Shamblen — book says born 23 Aug 1932 and married Thomas Pilman 23 Dec 1953. Her obituary identifies her as née Farmer, names both husbands (Thomas Charles Pilman and Clifford Earl Shamblen) and children Michael Thomas Pilman and Kimberly Ann Burrous. Mona’s obituary identifies her as Mona’s sister. Her profile now adds the book as a source and raises Clarence/Mae parentage to SUPPORTED.
3. Mona Pearl Farmer Jackson — book says born 10 Mar 1936 and married Walter Jackson 12 Jun 1954. Her 2013 obituary independently says age 77, names parents Clarence and Mae Farmer, and sisters Mabel Marcum and Marjorie Shamblen. The middle name Pearl and exact marriage date are from the compiled book, not the obituary.

ANNA MAE PARENTAGE AND SIBLINGS — OPEN
The family book gives Anna Mae’s Hamilton maiden name and the marriage/daughters, but no parents or siblings. Find a Grave memorial 5869410 likewise identifies spouse and Mabel, not parents. Searches of Kentucky Hamiltons in and near Tyner show multiple families; none was linked to Anna by a record. Andrew Jackson Hamilton and Jane Bullock’s family is a tempting cemetery/name lead but their memorial/obituary names five children, not Anna. Do not assign Anna Mae to that family. Find a birth certificate, 1920/1930 original schedules, marriage record, death certificate/obituary, or a family record before creating her Gen VII parent couple.
