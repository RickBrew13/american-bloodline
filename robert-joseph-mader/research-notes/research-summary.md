# Robert Joseph Mader — research notes

Robert Joseph Mader (29 Apr 1861–1 Jan 1944), Campbell County-born and Dayton, Campbell County, Kentucky death; buried 4 Jan 1944 at Saint Stephen Cemetery, Fort Thomas (Find a Grave memorial 26986793, with funeral-home text). Memorial describes him as husband of Frances Hoh. Parents named Felix Bernard Mader (1834–1912) and Elizabeth Enzweiler Mader (1838–1907).

Contributor-linked siblings: Maria Anna Mader (1860–1885), Josephine Mader Blesch (1863–1895), Elizabeth “Lizzie” Mader Breitenstein (1865–1952), Katherine Mader (1868–1956), Frances Maria “Fanny” Mader (1869–1894), Rosa Matilda Mader (1871–1877), Regina Agatha “Aggie” Mader Kreidler (1874–1956), and Grace Fannie Mader Mack (1877–1938). These are derivative memorial family links.

Children in memorial and baptism evidence include Marie (born 1894), Michael Franz (1897), Anne/Anna Elisabeth (1903), Cyrus Johan (1900), and Lawrence Joseph (1906). The latter two are from the parish-baptism transcription; additional children not found in the accessible material. Do not include Frederic Leo, whose baptism entry gives different parents.
