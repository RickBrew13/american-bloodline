Life Story

Lucy Jane Price married Roscoe Carman and was mother of Lillie Mae Carman. Find a Grave memorial pages for Lucy, her father Nathaniel Price and brother John Thomas Price repeat her parents and sibling set, providing convergent memorial-tree evidence. Her 1920 daughter’s marriage register calls her “Lucy Carmen”; the full name Lucy Jane Price is supplied by compiled trees and memorials, not that register.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Find a Grave gives Lucy’s birth county as unspecified in the memorial; FamilySearch-linked trees vary on county. Do not assert a precise birth county without a primary record.
  • Find a Grave lists eight children and does not include Emily, while the FamilySearch Roscoe profile lists Emily (1920–deceased) as a ninth child. Treat Emily as a compiled-tree candidate, not resolved.
  • Find a Grave memorial family links are collaborative/derivative, not primary civil records.
  • 28 November 2026 review: parent entries remain Nathaniel Price and Martha Ellen Stephens Price; no parent names, dates, generation placements, or parent links were edited. FamilySearch public profile KN3Y-MRJ also identifies those parents and Roscoe Carman as spouse. A saved transcript of Lillie’s 1920 Lincoln County marriage entry names “Roscoe Carmen” and “Lucy Carmen” as her parents.
  • FamilySearch compiled profiles report Lucy and Roscoe married 23 December 1902 and list nine children. The Find a Grave memorial lists eight and omits Emily (1920–deceased), who appears only in the FamilySearch family compilation; retain her as unresolved rather than silently adding her as documented.
  • FamilySearch’s Nathanial Price / Martha Ellen Stephens profiles list eight children including Lucy, corresponding to seven siblings. They give Nathanial 1846–1935 and Martha about 1847–1925; the existing Lucy parent card uses the memorial-derived Nathaniel spelling and Martha dates 1848–1926. Preserve the displayed parent entries and note the source variants rather than replacing them.
  • A separate FamilySearch Lucy Jane Price profile (L2SB-7Q2) reports a 20 August 1882 birth in Webster, Kentucky and a 1952 death. It has no established connection to Roscoe or to Nathanial and Martha; do not merge it with this Lucy. The actual marriage, census and death records behind the compiled profiles were not visually reviewed in this update.
  • No photo was added. The Find a Grave photo page lists four images, but the prior photo-search log records that the image assets could not be retrieved for visual identification. Do not describe any gallery item as Lucy’s portrait or as a verified record image until it is inspected.
  • The four Find a Grave images are now visually reviewed and copied into this profile folder with contributor/date captions. Two clearly show Lucy’s marker; the group photos remain unidentified and are not credited to Lucy or relatives as sitters.
