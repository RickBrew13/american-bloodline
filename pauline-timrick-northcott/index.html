Family Relationship Notes

Obituaries for Eugene, Virginia, Stanley “Bud” Jr., and Dorothy identify Stanley F. Northcott Sr. and Pauline (Timrick) Northcott as parents and corroborate their sibling group. The FamilySearch collaborative profiles list seven children: Virginia, Geraldine, Stanley Jr., Pauline (“Polly”), Eugene, Dorothy, and Patricia. The 1950 census index also places Pauline with Stanley as his wife and Eugene and Patricia in the Bay City household.

FamilySearch’s public collaborative profile reports Pauline’s dates as 6 March 1899–26 June 1979 and identifies Julius Albert Timreck and Wilhelmina H. “Minnie” Dupke as her parents. Its source list includes “Palanena Z Timrick in household of Julius Timrick, United States Census, 1900,” and a Michigan marriage-index entry. The original records have not yet been transcribed. The parent assignment is a working relationship (about 65% pending review); the listed mother link currently depends on the collaborative family group.

The FamilySearch tree lists nine sibling entries, several with Limerick/Timrick variants and overlapping birth years. They are included as unresolved candidates in the Siblings box and are not all asserted as full siblings.
