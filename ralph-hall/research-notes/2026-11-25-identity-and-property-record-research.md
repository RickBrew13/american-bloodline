## 3. Property ownership — priority research target

No deed or assessor record has yet been located that establishes Ralph Hall as an owner of property jointly with Judy or separately in Rockaway Beach.

The [official Taney County Recorder of Deeds page](https://www.taneycounty.org/index.php?department=18&page=10544&section=departments) states that it maintains county land transactions, including deeds, deeds of trust, liens, plats, and related instruments. Its page says records dating from 1894 to the present are searchable through a Laredo subscription or Tapestry pay-per-search service. The page also explains that records are indexed by name.

The county's online property-record system was not searched to completion in this pass; its subscription/payment access is an external step. Search the exact working name Ralph Hall and Judy's known legal-name variants, then inspect the actual instruments for grantor/grantee names, legal description, dates, marital status, and any joint conveyance. A shared address in a data-broker result is not proof of joint ownership.

Official portal information: https://www.taneycounty.org/index.php?department=18&page=10544&section=departments
