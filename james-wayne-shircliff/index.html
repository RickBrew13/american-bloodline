Life Story

James Wayne Shircliff was born 2 April 1937 in Meade County, Kentucky. His parents are supported as James Edward Shircliff and Pauline “Rita Pauline” French: the 1940 and 1950 censuses each list James W. as their son (1940 image spells the parents Edward and Pauline; the 1950 image spells them James E. and Pauline P.), while the Kentucky birth index names mother Rita French but does not name his father. His father candidate is consistent with a 1999 Ohio death-index record for James Edward Shircliff, though that index alone does not explicitly identify James Wayne as his son. Exact name forms and source limits are retained below. He is the husband of Betty Carolyn Shircliff and is living in a nursing home (family-account status); no death date or burial is assigned.
