## Birth, parents and siblings
- Aileene's parents Homer William Brewster Sr. and Sarah Hazel Kelly are directly supported by her 2015 Britton obituary and by the parents' own obituaries; her siblings Elmer, Eugene, Earl, Homer Jr. and Eunice are named in her obituary and independently in the family notices.
- The 1940 census index lists Aleene W. Brewster, daughter, age 3, Tennessee-born, with Homer W. and Sarah H. in Civil District 12, Morgan County. The 1950 census index lists Aleene W. Brewster, daughter, age 12, Tennessee-born, with Homer and Sarah in Cincinnati. Original census images have not been transcribed in this pass.
- A public-records index reports 28 May 1937. That date is compatible with her age 78 at death on 2 September 2015 and her 1950 age of 12, but the 1940 census age of 3 would make her about a year older. The date remains indexed rather than certified. Tennessee is the reported birthplace; Morgan County/Brewstertown is only a household/geographic lead, not an established birth location.
- The 1950 index gives her father's middle initial as N rather than W; the original schedule should be read before silently normalizing the discrepancy.
