## 3. Family relationships and source strength
### Parents and siblings
The 2015 obituary names Homer and Sarah Brewster as parents and lists siblings Elmer, Eugene, Earl, Homer Jr. and Eunice. Earlier family obituary clippings independently identify Aileene/Aileen Gilpin as their daughter/sister:
- Sarah's 1987 notice calls Aileene Gilpin her daughter.
- Homer's 1991 notice calls Aileene Gilpin his daughter.
- Eunice's 1987 notice calls Aileen Gilpin her sister.
- Elmer's 1993 notice identifies Aileene Gilpin as his sister and places her in West Palm Beach at that time.

These notices are family-informant evidence and the original newspaper mastheads/publication dates are not visible in the archived clippings, but together they strongly support the Brewster family placement.

### Children
- Yvonne Diane Wagers: her Social Security Numident names Merida Wagers and Aileene Brewster as parents. Her 1955 birth and later Wuennemann name match the Aileene obituary. This is a direct indexed parentage record, not an original application image.
- Paul J. Wagers: his 1971 Ohio marriage index names Merida Wagers and Aileene Brewster as parents. This supports the parentage link, not a legal marriage between the parents.
- William Wagers: named as Aileene's surviving son in the 2015 obituary; no separate original parent-naming record was located in this pass.
- Julie Marlene Asher Korman: her Social Security Numident names Charles Asher and Aileene W. Brewster as parents. This supports parentage, not the date or legal status of the parents' relationship.
- Charlene: Aileene's obituary calls her Charlene Banks; the Find a Grave retyping says Charlene Asher; her own obituary index is Charlene Eubanks and names Aileene Gilpin as mother and Charles Asher among the named family. The full 6 January 2009 Corbin Times-Tribune text has not been read. The exact surname sequence and independent paternal proof remain open.
- Greg Asher: Aileene's obituary explicitly names him as her surviving son, but does not name his father. Do not assign Charles as father solely from the surname.

The obituary reports 16 grandchildren and uses the wording “21 great, great grandchildren.” The live profile had simplified this to 21 great-grandchildren; that was not faithful to the published wording and has been corrected to preserve the obituary's exact relationship level.

- **Birth:** 28 May 1937 Tennessee is from a public-records index, not a viewed birth certificate. The 1940 census index says age 3 and the 1950 census index says age 12. The 1940 enumeration district covers Brewstertown and Rugby, but neither census establishes exact birthplace. Keep birthplace unknown and retain the age discrepancy.
- **Death:** 2 September 2015, Manchester, Clay County, Kentucky, supported by the Britton Funeral Home obituary and ClayCoNews transcription.

A public-records index lists Cincinnati, Reno, Jacksonville and Corbin entries from 1996–2006; the dates overlap, so they do not prove a strict sequence of moves. No cemetery interment card, register, plot record or headstone image confirms Aileene at either location. Keep burial unverified.

## Additional cemetery-place distinction
A Morgan County gazetteer page lists a **Brewster Cemetery** in Morgan County, Tennessee, 17 miles north of Frankfort, and links it to Find a Grave cemetery ID 9679: https://roadsidethoughts.com/tn/frankfort-xx-morgan-cemeteries.htm . This is a plausible candidate for the obituary's “Brewster Town Cemetery in Tennessee,” given the nearby Brewstertown community. Aileene's individual Find a Grave memorial, however, points to cemetery ID 9680 in New Tazewell, Claiborne County—a separate cemetery. The gazetteer confirms the Morgan County cemetery exists, not that Aileene was buried there. The Find a Grave cemetery page was inaccessible in this session, and no interment list or headstone for Aileene was found. The profile now distinguishes these two locations rather than treating the Claiborne assignment as a confirmed burial.
