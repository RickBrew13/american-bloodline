Life Story

A public-records index reports Aileene Willie Brewster's birth as May 28, 1937, in Tennessee. The 1940 census places two-year-old Aleene in the household of Homer William and Sarah Hazel Brewster in Morgan County's Civil District 12, an enumeration district covering Brewstertown and Rugby; it does not establish the exact birthplace. The 1940 age of three is one year older than the age implied by the reported May 1937 date. By 1950 the family had moved to Cincinnati, where the census lists twelve-year-old Aleene with her parents and brothers.

Her children connect her to two family groups. Yvonne's Social Security application names Aileene Brewster and Merida Wagers as parents; Paul's 1971 marriage index also names them. Julie's Social Security application names Aileene W. Brewster and Charles Asher as parents. Aileene's 2015 obituary names sons Paul and William Wagers and Greg Asher, and daughters Yvonne Wuennemann, Julie Korman and Charlene Asher. These records establish the named parent-child links at different evidence levels, but no original marriage certificate has been found for any of Aileene's three reported relationships, and the sequence or legal end dates remain unproved. Her final surname, Gilpin, is documented in her obituary and family notices.

Her surviving address-index trail and family notices place her at different times in Cincinnati, West Palm Beach, Reno, Jacksonville, Corbin and finally Manchester, Kentucky; overlapping index dates do not establish a precise move sequence. She outlived three of her daughters. She died on September 2, 2015, at 78, in Manchester, Kentucky. Her obituary names three surviving sons and reports sixteen grandchildren and “21 great, great grandchildren.” It says burial would follow at Brewster Town Cemetery in Tennessee; the completed interment has not yet been independently confirmed. A 1988 Alaska Railroad Corporation annual report lists her among the railroad's employees, although it does not identify her role or residence. A later same-name property-index lead in Corbin, Kentucky, remains unverified. Her exact birthplace remains unknown.

Additional Quick-Fact Details

  • Funeral: Funeral Saturday 12 September 2015, Britton Funeral Home Chapel, Manchester, Kentucky
  • Reported burial plan: Brewster Town Cemetery, Tennessee
    The Morgan County Brewster Cemetery (Find a Grave cemetery 9679) is a plausible match near Brewstertown; her individual memorial instead points to a different Brewster Cemetery in Claiborne County. No interment register, plot record or headstone confirms where she was buried.
  • Name: the "W" is Willie, a birth middle name (it appears in the 1940 and 1950 censuses, before any marriage). Spellings vary: Aileene in the obituaries, Aleene in both censuses, Aileen and Ailene elsewhere. Wagers, Asher and Gilpin are surnames associated with her in later records, but the legal marriage sequence has not been established. This page keeps the aileene-wagers address.
  • Birth: 28 May 1937 rests on one public-records index, not an inspected birth certificate. It fits her obituary age and 1950 census age, but the 1940 census lists her as 3, one year older than the reported date implies. Exact birthplace is not established; the 1940 enumeration district includes Brewstertown and Rugby but does not prove she was born in either place. No birth record has been found.
  • Burial: the obituary’s wording is “Brewster Town Cemetery in Tennessee.” A gazetteer lists a Brewster Cemetery in Morgan County (Find a Grave cemetery 9679), 17 miles north of Frankfort and in the Brewstertown area; this is a plausible candidate for the obituary wording, but no individual memorial or interment record confirms Aileene there. Find a Grave memorial 151841686 instead files her in a different Brewster Cemetery, New Tazewell, Claiborne County (cemetery 9680), with no stone photo. Do not treat either location as confirmed burial until a register or marker is found.
  • Relationships and marriages: parentage records support Merida Wagers and Aileene as Yvonne’s parents, and Charles Asher and Aileene as Julie’s parents. Aileene’s obituary names Greg Asher as her son; the available notice does not identify his father. Her final surname Gilpin is documented by 1987 and at death, but the husband’s identity is unknown. No original marriage certificate or divorce decree has been found for any of these relationships. Do not present a legal marriage sequence or divorce chronology as established.
  • Charles Asher and Mr. Gilpin: neither has been identified. Two Ohio men named Charles Asher (1931–2023 and 1947–2022) are not shown to be her husband.
  • Charlene: Aileene’s obituary calls her Charlene Banks; the Find a Grave retyping gives Charlene Asher; her own obituary index is Charlene Eubanks. These may reflect a married name and variants, but the full 6 January 2009 Corbin Times-Tribune obituary has not been read and the exact name sequence is not established.
  • Family tree problems (leads only): Geni gives a birth range of 1916–1950, only one spouse, a wrong child list ("Merdy Junior Wagers" is Merida's son by a later marriage) and misspells Yvonne's name; a merge with the duplicate profile is approved but not finished. FamilySearch G6TW-2PC has no dates, and a probable duplicate, "Willie Eileen Brewster" GXCQ-XVM, holds the public-records match.
  • Still to find: her 2015 Kentucky death certificate and actual cemetery interment record; a verified portrait or clearly identified family photograph. The MyHeritage Wagers tree remains a lead only.
  • Photo: none of Aileene has been verified in Find a Grave, FamilySearch, Geni, the funeral home page or her own folder.
