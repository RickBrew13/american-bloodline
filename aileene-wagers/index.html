Life Story

Aileene Willie Brewster was born on May 28, 1937, in the Brewstertown hills of Morgan County, Tennessee, the younger of the two daughters of Homer William Brewster Sr. and Sarah Hazel Kelly, with four brothers around her. SUPPORTED By 1950 the family had come north to Cincinnati, where the census found twelve-year-old Aleene at home with her parents and brothers. DOCUMENTED

She married young. By 1955 she was the wife of Merida Henry Wagers, and their daughter Yvonne was born in Cincinnati that December, when Aileene was eighteen; sons Paul and William followed. SUPPORTED By 1961 that marriage was over, and by 1964 she had married Charles Asher, with whom she had Julie, Charlene and Greg. SUPPORTED Later still she took the name Gilpin, which she kept for the rest of her life. DOCUMENTED

Her later years took her from Cincinnati to West Palm Beach, Reno, Jacksonville and Corbin, Kentucky, and she outlived three of her daughters. SUPPORTED She died on September 2, 2015, at 78, at home in Manchester, Kentucky, survived by three sons, sixteen grandchildren and twenty-one great-grandchildren. DOCUMENTED She was brought home to be buried in Brewstertown, Tennessee, where her life had begun. SUPPORTED

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Name: the "W" is Willie, a birth middle name (it appears in the 1940 and 1950 censuses, before any marriage). Spellings vary: Aileene in the obituaries, Aleene in both censuses, Aileen and Ailene elsewhere. Wagers, Asher and Gilpin are her married names in turn, so "Gilpin vs Wagers" is not a conflict. This page keeps the aileene-wagers address.
  • Birth: 28 May 1937 rests on one public-records index, which fits her obituary age and the 1950 census. The 1940 census lists her as 3, though she would have been 2 on census day; a small enumerator variance. No birth record has been found; check the Tennessee delayed birth records for Morgan County.
  • Burial: her obituary says Brewster Town Cemetery, which is Brewstertown Cemetery, Morgan County, Tennessee (Find a Grave cemetery 9679). Find a Grave memorial 151841686 instead places her in Brewster Cemetery, New Tazewell, Claiborne County (cemetery 9680), probably the wrong cemetery of a similar name, and has no stone photo. No memorial for her was found in Brewstertown Cemetery.
  • Marriages: no record found for any of the three. Still to search: Wagers × Brewster, about 1954–55; Asher × Wagers or Brewster, about 1961–64 (Hamilton or Clermont County, Ohio, or Kentucky); a Gilpin marriage before 1987 (Ohio or Florida indexes); Florida and Ohio divorce indexes; Kentucky and Tennessee marriages.
  • Charles Asher and Mr. Gilpin: neither has been identified. Two Ohio men named Charles Asher (1931–2023 and 1947–2022) are not shown to be her husband.
  • Charlene: her surname is given as Banks (Aileene's obituary), Asher (Find a Grave's retyping) and Eubanks (her own obituary index). Her obituary in the Corbin Times-Tribune, 6 January 2009, has not been read.
  • Family tree problems (leads only): Geni gives a birth range of 1916–1950, only one spouse, a wrong child list ("Merdy Junior Wagers" is Merida's son by a later marriage) and misspells Yvonne's name; a merge with the duplicate profile is approved but not finished. FamilySearch G6TW-2PC has no dates, and a probable duplicate, "Willie Eileen Brewster" GXCQ-XVM, holds the public-records match.
  • Still to find: her 2015 Kentucky death certificate; a photo of her (her son Paul's "Wagers Web Site" tree on MyHeritage is the best lead).
  • Photo: none found on Find a Grave, FamilySearch, Geni, the funeral home's page or in the family folders. A Geni photo once thought to show her with her parents shows only Homer Sr. and Sarah.
  • Aileene's profile is filed in a folder of her own in the Grand Generation, with her siblings.
