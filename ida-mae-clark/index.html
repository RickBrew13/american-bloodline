Life Story

Ida Mae Clark was born on 23 December 1879 at Aurora, Indiana, the daughter of Morrison Clark and Elizabeth Nightengal. Her birth and death dates are contributor-entered, corroborated by her Geni profile and by her Find a Grave memorial, but no birth record has been read, and her death certificate — cited by a FamilySearch contributor note as Kentucky file no. 4589 — has not itself been read. She married James Bernard Rich, but no marriage record has been found, so no marriage date is carried here.

Ida and James had one established child, Helen, who married John Thomas Williams. Ida died on 15 March 1948 at Dayton, Campbell County, Kentucky, aged 68, and lies at Highland Cemetery, Fort Mitchell, Kentucky, Section 19, Lot 123, Grave 3, beside James in Grave 4. No photograph of Ida herself has been found. The four pictures in the couple folder are contextual views of Loveland and Aurora places, not family photographs, so none is shown here.
