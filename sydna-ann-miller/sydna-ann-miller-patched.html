Life Story

Sydna Ann Miller was born about July 1841, the daughter of George S. Miller and Emily Lane. Her stone reads "Sidney (Sudna Miller)" — two name variants on one stone — and the records also give Sudna and Sunda; the settled family line carries her as Sydna Ann Miller. No birth record has been found, and no birthplace is carried: an old compiled account gives 29 June 1841, at Goldtown, but the date conflicts with her stone's arithmetic and Goldtown is unbacked.

On 4 November 1858, in Jackson County, Virginia, she married George Washington Shamblen, a farmer at Grass Lick. Together they had ten children: Josephine, Artemisia, David Clayton — the direct line — Lafayette, Martin Luther, Lucy Jane, Thomas Early, DeMarquis, Clora, and Frederick. A 1923 biography of their son-in-law Reuben Perry Shinn describes George's large farm at Grass Lick, where the family lived and where the children married into the Fisher, Shinn, Casto, and Moore families of the same Jackson County ground.

Sydna died on 11 September 1883, aged 42, and lies in Shamblin Cemetery near Kenna, beside George. Seven days later, on 18 September, their infant son Frederick died, aged 2 years 3 months. No cause or shared-illness explanation survives for either death, and none is invented here. George married again about 1885, a woman named Emma F., and lived until 1905.

No photograph of Sydna herself has been found. The couple folder holds four pictures — a Grass Lick cemetery overview, a Kenna historical marker, and two unidentified images — and none is a portrait of Sydna, so none is shown here.
