Life Story

Willie "Bill" McCartt and his wife Flora Strunk started their family in Huntsville, Tennessee, where their daughter Nellie was born in 1929 SUPPORTED. They later raised a large family in the coal town of Caretta, in McDowell County, West Virginia, where their twins John and Janice were born in 1936 and their daughter Dorothy in 1939 SUPPORTED. He died before his son John in 2021 SUPPORTED.
