Life Story

Willie "Bill" McCartt was listed as age 2 in Scott County, Tennessee, in 1910; his 1940 draft registration gives Huntsville, Tennessee, as his birthplace, supporting a birth about 1908 SUPPORTED. He grew up in Scott County, Tennessee. In 1910 he was living with his mother, Sylvania McCartt, and her new husband, Mike Stephens SUPPORTED. Bill and his wife Flora Strunk started their family in Huntsville, Tennessee, where their daughter Nellie was born in 1929 SUPPORTED. They later raised a large family in the coal town of Caretta, in McDowell County, West Virginia, where their twins John and Janice were born in 1936 and their daughter Dorothy in 1939 SUPPORTED. A user-contributed memorial for Flora lists her spouse as Bill McCartt (1908–1972) and burial at Odd Fellows Cemetery in Pemberton, New Jersey; his death date and burial are not independently verified UNPROVED.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Exact birth date, death date, and burial are not confirmed. The 1910 census lists Willie McCartt, age 2; the 1940 draft registration gives Huntsville, Tennessee, as birthplace; together these support birth about 1908. Flora’s Find a Grave memorial lists spouse Bill McCartt (1908–1972), Odd Fellows Cemetery, Pemberton, New Jersey; seek an original death or burial record before treating those dates and cemetery as established.
  • Leads: the 1930 census of Scott County, Tennessee; the 1940 census of McDowell County, West Virginia; the McCartt families of neighboring Morgan County, Tennessee (unproved).
  • His father is not named in any record found. He has no Social Security application on file at FamilySearch, and his draft card names only his wife.
  • Sylvania's birth year varies from 1884 to 1890 across records. Her mother Sallie's maiden name is given as Dixon in one online tree and Low in another; neither is proved UNPROVED.
  • The Scott County courthouse burned in 1946, so local marriage and probate records may be lost.
  • He is not Bill McCartt of Jonesborough, Tennessee (1937–2025), son of Arthur Reed and Zella Hall McCartt.
