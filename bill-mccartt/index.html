Family Relationship Notes

The census evidence supports identifying this Bill as Sylvania’s son: Willie is age two in 1910 and twelve in 1920, while Mike is explicitly his stepfather. The 1940 draft registration names Bill McCart and wife Flora, linking the same-name records to the couple; children’s obituaries independently name Willie “Bill” and Flora as parents. A Find a Grave memorial and shared marker now provide a matching Bill record, correcting the earlier statement that no matching memorial had been located. A 2000–2001 Genealogy.com discussion about “Willie McCart/Stephens” includes a reply assigning a different William Leslie McCartt (born 1901, died 1976) to Flora Helen Strunk (born about 1911). That reply conflicts with this Bill’s 1910/1920 census ages and 1940 registration; it is preserved as an unresolved same-name lead and is not merged into this couple.

Life Story

The 1910 Scott County census lists Willie McCartt, age two, as Sylvania McCartt Stephens’s son and Mike Stephens’s stepson. The 1920 household again lists Willie, age twelve, with Sylvania and Mike. Bill’s 1940 draft registration gives Huntsville, Tennessee, as birthplace and Mrs. Flora McCart as his wife. Their children’s obituaries identify Willie “Bill” and Flora (Strunk) McCartt as parents and place the children in Tennessee and West Virginia before the family moved to Michigan about 1950. Sylvania’s 1970 obituary later reports William McCartt living in Browns Mills, New Jersey. A Find a Grave memorial and shared marker report Bill as 1908–1972 at Odd Fellows Cemetery, Pemberton; those dates and the cemetery are supported by the user-contributed marker photo, not by a civil death record.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Bill’s biological father is still unidentified. Mike Stephens was the stepfather named in the 1910 and 1920 census context and is not indexed as Bill’s Gen VII parent.
  • The shared Find a Grave marker and memorial support years 1908–1972 and burial at Odd Fellows Cemetery, Pemberton, but no civil death certificate or cemetery register has been examined; exact dates remain unverified.
  • Do not expand the marker form “Bill L.” to William Leslie. The 2001 forum reply gives a William Leslie born 1901 and died 1976, which conflicts with Willie’s census ages and 1940 draft registration.
  • Bill and Flora were together by Nellie’s 1929 birth; no civil marriage record or date has been located.
  • Sylvania’s obituary provides Browns Mills, New Jersey, as William’s residence in 1970; this is consistent with the reported later New Jersey burial but does not alone prove it.
  • Children’s sources now identify at least eight named children, including Melvin. One additional living child remains intentionally unnamed on the public profile.
