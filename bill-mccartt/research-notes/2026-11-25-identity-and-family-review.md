# Bill McCartt — identity and family review (25 November 2026)

Working identification: Bill McCartt is the Willie McCartt listed in Sylvania’s 1910 household as age two and Mike Stephens’s stepson, and as age twelve in the 1920 census. His 1940 draft registration index records Bill McCart, birthplace Huntsville, Tennessee, with Mrs. Flora McCart as wife. A matching Find a Grave memorial and shared marker report Bill 1908–1972 beside Flora G. McCartt 1910–1977. The marker is a user-contributed memorial image; exact date of birth/death, full legal name and civil burial record remain unverified.

Mike Stephens is a stepfather, not the biological father. The 2001 Genealogy.com reply’s William Leslie McCartt (1901–1976) does not fit the target Willie’s census ages and draft registration; do not merge them.

Children’s obituaries identify eight named children, including Melvin; a ninth child is referenced in the family notes as living and remains unnamed publicly. Maternal siblings Beatrice, Homer, Christine and Mary Jane are listed on Bill’s profile; no biological father is assigned.
