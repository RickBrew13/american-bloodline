# Bill McCartt × Flora (Strunk) McCartt — source review (25 November 2026)

## Direct/near-contemporary records

- 1910 U.S. Census, Scott County, Tennessee, Civil District 10, FamilySearch: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MGFD-5WH — Willie McCartt, age 2, is in Sylvania Stephens’s household and is identified as Mike Stephens’s stepson.
- 1920 U.S. Census, Scott County, Tennessee, Civil District 3, FamilySearch: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MNPW-6LQ — Willie McCartt age 12 with Mike and Sylvania Stephens.
- World War II draft registration, Bill McCart, 16 October 1940, FamilySearch: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:Q2CY-FMZH — index gives Huntsville, Tennessee, as birthplace and Mrs. Flora McCart as wife. Original image not reviewed in this work session.
- Sylvania’s 1970 obituary (Scott County News, 15 May 1970, p. 4) names William McCartt of Browns Mills, New Jersey, as her surviving son; transcription is on https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/65691783/sylvania-mccartt-sexton.

## Family records

- Nellie J. Bridges, obituary, Gilligan Funeral Homes: https://www.gilliganfuneralhomes.com/obituaries/nellie-bridges — names Willie “Bill” McCartt and Flora (Strunk) as parents.
- Dorothy Ann Doney, obituary, Turner & Son Funeral Home: https://www.turnerfuneralhomes.cc/obituaries/Dorothy-Ann-Doney?obId=42921177 — names Bill and Flora (Strunk) as parents; lists siblings including Melvin.
- John “Jack” McCartt, obituary, Crossville Chronicle: https://obituaries.crossville-chronicle.com/obituary/john-mccartt-1081722581 — names Bill and Flora and identifies twin Janice.
- Denny McCartt, obituary, D.S. Temrowski & Sons: https://www.dignitymemorial.com/obituaries/warren-mi/denny-mccartt-10351773 — names William and Flora as parents, all seven siblings, and reports the family’s move from Tennessee to Michigan about 1950.
- Thelma Mae McCartt Frazier, obituary, Tufts Schildmeyer: https://www.tuftsschildmeyer.com/obituaries/thelma-mae-nee-mccartt-frazier — names Bill and Flora and some siblings.

## Memorial and photo evidence

- Bill McCartt memorial 105861992: https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/105861992/bill-mccartt. Flora G. Strunk McCartt memorial 105862078: https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/105862078/flora_g-mccartt. Both link the same spouse and shared marker.
- Shared marker photo, credited on Find a Grave to Clara McIver: https://images.findagrave.com/photos/2013/56/105862078_136191400449.jpg?size=photos250. Web-extracted text gives Bill 1908–1972 and Flora G. 1910–1977, The marker text extraction reads only “BILL”; no middle initial is visible. Use it as a shared-marker image, not a portrait.
- The Find a Grave entries are user-contributed. The marker text extraction gives a useful cemetery/date clue, but no civil death record or cemetery register was checked.

## Same-name conflict

- A 25 Dec. 2000 Genealogy.com post asks about Sylvania’s son Willie McCartt/Stephens and Flora Strunk: https://www.genealogy.com/forum/surnames/topics/strunk/333/. A 10 Feb. 2001 reply assigns a William Leslie McCartt (born 9 Aug. 1901, died 19 May 1976) to Flora Helen Strunk (born 1911), married 16 May 1928: https://www.genealogy.com/forum/surnames/topics/strunk/347/. These details conflict with the target Willie’s 1910/1920 census ages and 1940 registration. Do not merge the William Leslie family into this couple without an identity bridge.
