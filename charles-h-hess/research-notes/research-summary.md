# Charles H. Hess — research notes

Charles H. Hess (22 Dec 1869–23 Apr 1923), born Aurora, Dearborn County, Indiana; died Cincinnati, Hamilton County, Ohio; buried Saint Joseph New Cemetery (Find a Grave derivative memorial 188780579). Memorial-linked wife Katherine A. Hess (1877–1967), but her maiden-name evidence conflicts (Winterhalter/Winterholter vs Wiedmer); retain variant and uncertainty.

A 1923 obituary photo on the memorial says he lived at 535 Elberon Avenue, Price Hill; president/treasurer of Charles H. Hess Company, president of Tri-State Butter Co. and Merchants Ice and Cold Storage Co.; died suddenly of heart disease at home, and was survived by widow, two sons and two daughters. Another notice lists widow Katherine (née Winterholter), sons Charles H. Jr. and Howard, daughters Catherine and Mrs Margaret Borger, and interment at St Joseph Catholic Mausoleum. These are newspaper-derived memorial image transcriptions; original papers not independently accessed.

Parents/siblings: Find a Grave links Margaret Ott Hess (1839–1926) as a parent and lists siblings Anna Hess Groneman (1866–1940), Katherine Hess (1869–1955), Erasmus Alexander Hess (1872–1959), Oscar Rudolf Hess (1874–1934). The father is not identified on the memorial. These are contributor-maintained family links, not original-record proof.

Children linked across parental memorials: Marguerite A. Hess (Dorger; 1898–1951), Howard Bernard Hess (1899–1953), Charles H. Hess Jr. (1902–1938), and Catherine Hess Ryan (1908–1967). The children’s sources have spelling and identity inconsistencies; do not merge with Howard and Anne’s later son Charles H. Hess (1941–2002).

A Charles-memorial gallery image is captioned “1921” but its content/provenance is not established. It is archived only as a memorial-image lead, not presented as a verified portrait.
