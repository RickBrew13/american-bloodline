Amelia’s Find a Grave memorial biography names Lucy Ethel, Alice, and Ralph as her children. John Samuel Costigan’s existing profile additionally lists William Swope and Charles F. as children, with memorial/obituary references. These names are retained as the reported family group, but the underlying original birth records have not been reviewed here.

The Kentucky Death Index gives her death as 21 January 1955 in Pendleton County at age 80, with Kenton County residence. Her parents and siblings are not identified in the material reviewed.

Alice’s birth year differs in the existing family sources (about 1904 versus about 1905). The date is left qualified rather than forcing a single year. The original profile’s cited evidence and open research questions remain available below.

Life Story

Amelia Friend was born on 26 January 1874 . She married John Samuel Costigan, and her Find a Grave biography names her as the mother of Lucy Ethel, Alice, and Ralph . A resident of Kenton County, she died in Pendleton County on 21 January 1955, at 80, and is buried in Highland Cemetery, Fort Mitchell .
