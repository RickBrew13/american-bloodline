**Research date:** 26 November 2026  
**Scope:** Eunice's own possible property interests and property records tied to her known husband. The existing Eunice folder was checked first. No property ownership is added without a deed that establishes the identity.

## Hamilton County, Ohio — 1980–1987 deed-name indexes
Reviewed the Hamilton County Recorder's published alphabetical grantor and grantee indexes for 1980 through 1987, searching the B surname section and checking exact text for “Eunice” and “Burton.” No entry matching Eunice E. Burton, Eunice Brewster, Eunice Whittle, or Irvin Burton was located in those name indexes. Some unrelated people named Burton appear. This is a limited name-index result, not proof that Eunice or Irvin owned no property: a property could have been recorded in another county, under a different legal name, or through a trust/estate/other party.

The Recorder's official guide explains that these annual PDFs are alphabetical grantor/grantee indexes, and that pre-1988 deed images require the indexed book/page to be entered into the Recorder's book/page search. The 1987 indexes were checked as well as the earlier years because Eunice died in December 1987.

## 1951–1955 Hamilton County deed entries — likely a different Irvin Burton
A separate older Irvin Burton appears in earlier Hamilton County indexes:
- 28 November 1951: grantor Funk, Dove and Sam; grantee **Irvin Burton & Marie**; deed book 2517, page 340.
- 25 September 1953: grantor William A. and Emma Del Favero; grantee **Irvin Burton et al.**; book 2639, page 124.
- 19 April 1955: grantor William R. Evans; grantee **Irvin Burton et al.**; book 2750, page 154.

The 26 September 1951 Hamilton County register of voters also lists **Irvin Burton** at 843 Barr, Ward 16, Precinct H. The same register separately lists Frances Burton at 848 Barr. It does not state an age or spouse.

These entries are **not attributed to Eunice's husband**. The cemetery-index identity for Eunice's husband is Irvin D. Burton, reported born in 1932 and died in 1994. He would have been only 19 in September 1951, whereas a person listed on the 1951 voter register was required to meet the voting-age threshold then in force. The 1951 deed's co-grantee “Marie” also does not match any spouse presently established for Eunice's husband. The voter and deed evidence therefore points to a likely older same-name Irvin, and must not be used as proof that Eunice or her husband owned the property. The actual deed images have not been read, so even the older Irvin's identity and property description remain open.

## Butler County, Ohio — correct county for Eunice's later Hamilton residence
Eunice's 1987 family death notice gives her last residence as Hamilton, Butler County, Ohio. Butler County Recorder's official search is available online, and the Recorder's imaging notice says records can be accessed by book/page; the Cloud Search manual describes name and full-text OCR search. This is the most relevant public index to check for any property in her final residence county, particularly 1987 records and earlier geographic/legacy indexes.

The current search page is an interactive portal. No name query was submitted through it in this session, so no negative result is claimed. The Recorder's historical resources include older geographic and alphabetical indexes, and the online portal may not expose every older record by a simple web-indexed search.
