## Evidence rechecked
1. **Birth and identity:** Existing record compilation cites Social Security NUMIDENT and SSDI for Eunice Edna Brewster, born 22 December 1930 in Cincinnati; aliases include Burton and Taylor. The state death index identifies Eunice E. Burton, died 16 December 1987 in Butler County, age 56. The age-derived 1931 year in the index is not a demonstrated conflict with the 1930 date.
2. **Parents:** The original 1947 Campbell County, Kentucky marriage certificate names her parents as Homer and Sarah Brewster. The 1940 census household transcription places Eunice with Homer, Sarah and siblings in Morgan County, Tennessee. Her parents are therefore supported by the original marriage certificate and census context.
3. **Marriage to Avery:** Original Campbell County marriage certificate, book 196, page 83, dated 23 July 1947, names Avery E. Whittle and Eunice Brewster and reports each as single. It lists her parents and their Cincinnati residence. It records her age as 21 and contains a sworn statement that both were 21, although her recorded birth date makes her 16. Preserve the discrepancy; do not infer the reason.
4. **1950 household and work:** Original 1950 census image, Cincinnati, Hamilton County, ED 91-588, sheet 4, lines 19–20, enumerated 10 April 1950, 1326 rear house: Avery is head, 23, gardener at a distillery; Eunice is wife, 19, candy packer for a wholesale grocer. This documents the shared household, not children.
5. **Occupation at death:** Ohio death-index entry VKTL-N6K lists Eunice's occupation as registered nurse in nursing/personal-care facilities, certificate 088337, volume 27087. This is a strong indexed occupation lead, not a reviewed original certificate or a named employer/license record.
6. **Marriage/relationship at death:** The family death notice identifies Eunice as née Brewster, daughter of Homer and the late Sarah, and beloved wife of Irvin Burton at her death; it names children Demetria Sexton, Elaine Bishop and Anthony Q. Burton. The clipping's newspaper title and publication date are not visible. Paternity by Irvin remains family account unless direct records are found.
7. **Burial:** Existing headstone photo and memorial listing place Eunice at Rose Hill Burial Park, Hamilton, Section 11, Lot 191, Grave 3, adjacent to Irvin's Grave 4. Cemetery register/interment card has not been inspected.

The public Newsdex site returned a fetch error in this session, so no Eunice result was obtained. Its Domestic Relations name-search page is publicly listed, but the page timed out when opened here. No divorce case has been found or ruled out.
