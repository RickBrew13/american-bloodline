## Search results and exclusions

### Irvin Burton marriage
Exact-name searches for Eunice Edna/E. Brewster, Whittle, Taylor and Burton paired with Irvin/Irvin David Burton did not surface an indexed marriage return or reliable newspaper announcement. The previously identified 1950 census proves Eunice and Avery were still recorded as married on 10 April 1950; Eunice's 1987 death notice identifies Irvin as her husband. The intervening legal marriage date and county remain unlocated. No date or county has been inferred.

A Hamilton County/Cincinnati 1951 voter register lists an **Irvin Burton** at 843 Barr, Ward 16, Precinct H. It does not give an age, spouse, or middle name. It is not attributable to Irvin David Burton: the known 1940 census places Irvin David as a child, and his recorded 1932 birth would make him about 19 in September 1951, below the voting age. This entry is therefore explicitly excluded from Eunice's spouse evidence unless a separate identity link is found.

### Original 1987 death notice / obituary
The family's original clipping remains the strongest source presently available for Eunice's death, husband, named children, named grandchildren, funeral home and sibling list. Public exact-name searches did not return a separately accessible copy of the 16 December 1987 notice. Hamilton County library and newspaper indexes identify the *Journal-News* as a long-running local newspaper and catalog historical obituary indexes, but no exact issue/page was retrieved in this session. The newspaper issue, full obituary, and any accompanying photograph remain open.
