# Eunice Edna Brewster Burton — parents' siblings and extended-family audit
**Research date:** 26 November 2026  
**Folder-first protocol:** Eunice's own folder was inventoried and reviewed first. This note records extended relatives relevant to Eunice; it does not edit another person's profile or folder.

## Paternal side — Homer William Brewster Sr.'s half-siblings
Homer Sr. was a son of William Alexander Brewster and his first wife, Mary Ellen McKinney. William later married Julia Bell Kelly, who was also Eunice's maternal aunt. Contemporary obituaries for children of William and Julia establish a paternal half-sibling group for Homer Sr., making them Eunice's paternal half-aunts and half-uncles and also connecting the Kelly and Brewster branches.

### Direct obituary evidence
**Sarah Elizabeth Brewster Galloway** died 7 February 2009 at age 65 in Brewstertown. The Morgan County obituary transcription identifies her parents as Julia K. Brewster and William Brewster; it names a deceased brother Edward Brewster and surviving siblings James and William Brewster, Cleo Boston and Mary Evans. It names former husband Everett “Buddy” Galloway; children Robin R. Galloway and Richard Galloway; and grandchildren Jessica N. Hadidsaz, Jeff and Mike Myers, and Paige Galloway. The obituary does not state Sarah's exact birth date or enumerate all of her siblings.

**Cleo R. Brewster** was born 18 October 1940 in Brewstertown, Tennessee, and died 13 November 2024. Her funeral-home obituary identifies her parents as William and Julia (née Kelly) Brewster. It names one surviving brother, James Brewster, and deceased siblings Frankie Brewster, Laura Belle, Sara Galloway, William “Junebug” Brewster, Marylee Evans and Edward Brewster. Cleo's husband was William “Bill” Lowe; her sons were Willie Boston and the late Thomas R. Boston. The obituary names grandchildren and great-grandchildren, but those descendants are not Eunice's direct descendants.

## Maternal side — Sarah Hazel Kelly's siblings
The 20 January 1920 census index for Aragon, Polk County, Georgia, places James P. Kelley and wife Belle in one household with four younger people: Bessie M. (21), Sallie (9), William E. (6) and Julia B. (4). The family tree identifies Sarah Hazel Kelly with the child Sallie. Sarah's mother's 1936 Tennessee death certificate names “Mrs. Sally Brewster” as her daughter, strongly supporting the Sallie/Sarah identification. The 1920 census index says Sallie was 9, while Sarah's reported birth date is 22 February 1911, which would make her 8—not 9—on 20 January 1920. The original census image must still be checked for handwriting, relationship column and age before the whole household is treated as a proved sibling group. The following are therefore potential maternal aunts/uncle, not all fully proved:
- **Bessie M. Kelly / Bessie M. Ramsey** — about 1899–18 April 1967; the 1920 census index places Bessie in the household, and the Tennessee death-record index associates Bessie M. Ramsey with James Patrick Kelly and Laura Bell Underwood. The age/birth chronology predates the estimated marriage of James and Laura, so the exact parentage and chronology remain unresolved.
- **William E. Kelly** — age 6 in the 1920 census index (born about 1913–1914); household relationship and later identity, spouse or descendants still require confirmation.
- **Julia B. Kelly / Julia Bell Kelly** — age 4 in the 1920 census index; later married William Alexander Brewster on 15 June 1929 in Newport, Campbell County, Kentucky. The marriage index lists her parents as P. Kelly and B. Kelly. Her identity as the Julia in the Kelly household is probable but the original census image and marriage record should be compared. If confirmed, she was Sarah's sister and Eunice's maternal aunt; by marrying William Alexander Brewster, she also became Eunice's step-grandmother. This is the Kelly–Brewster double connection.
- **Margaret Kelly** — present in family-tree/book material but not in the 1920 census household and no independent record was located in this pass. Keep as family-reported/unverified; do not add as a proved sibling.

## Name-collision exclusion: Lola Brewster Crabtree
A 2022 family-history article by Donna Rowland Gough, based on an email from Brewster descendant Wilma Crabtree Gibson, mentions **Lola Brewster Crabtree** and her twin sister Nola. The same article identifies Wilma's great-uncle Jesse Brewster as a son of James Thomas Brewster. This Lola belongs to the Jesse Brewster branch and is not evidence for Homer Sr.'s sister **Lola Edna Brewster Sears**, who belongs to the William Alexander Brewster × Mary Ellen McKinney branch in the existing archive. Do not merge these two women or treat the Crabtree account as proof about Eunice's aunt. The article may help trace collateral Brewster cousins later, but it does not change Eunice's direct pedigree.

Source: Donna Rowland Gough, “Tidbits about Brewstertown and the Brewsters,” 14 October 2022, including attribution to Wilma Crabtree Gibson's personal email: https://www.thegoughfamily.com/tidbits-about-brewstertown-and-the-brewsters/


## Paternal full aunt — Lola Edna Brewster Sears
The existing Homer William Brewster Sr. family profile identifies **Lola Edna Brewster Sears (1914–1968)** as his full sister, a daughter of William Alexander Brewster and Mary Ellen McKinney. The 1920 Brewster household and the existing family profile support the sibling placement, but a primary marriage, death or obituary record for Lola was not located in this Eunice-focused pass. Retain her as a family-archive working identification pending the underlying source records. She is distinct from **Lola Brewster Crabtree**, who belongs to the Jesse Brewster branch (see the name-collision exclusion above).
