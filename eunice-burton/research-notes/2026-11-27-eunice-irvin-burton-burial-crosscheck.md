## Interpretation and limits
This is useful corroboration that the Irvin D. Burton in the adjacent grave is the same Irvin named as Eunice's husband. It supplies a more precise birth and death date for the working spouse identity and makes the spouse match stronger than proximity of graves alone.

However, PeopleLegacy expressly warns that its data are derived from public sources and are not independently verified. It is a secondary index, not the original cemetery interment card, death certificate, obituary or marriage record. The birth date, death date and spouse relationship should remain labelled as secondary-source corroboration until checked against original records. No parents, siblings, children, prior spouses, military service or occupation for Irvin have been established by this result.
