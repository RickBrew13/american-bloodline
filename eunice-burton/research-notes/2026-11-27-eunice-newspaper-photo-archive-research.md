## New Cincinnati Enquirer Photo Archive lead
The Cincinnati & Hamilton County Public Library now documents a dedicated **Cincinnati Enquirer Photo Archive** containing more than one million staff photographs from **1945–1995**. That date span overlaps Eunice's adult life in Cincinnati/Hamilton and her death in 1987. The library provides:
1. A searchable inventory (hosted in SharePoint).
2. A photo-request form; staff may scan and send up to 20 matching images if found.
3. A stated minimum turnaround of four weeks and a limit of one request per four-week period.
4. In-person access by appointment.
5. A separate reproduction-rights process through the Cincinnati Enquirer for any image intended for publication.

The public library page and search inventory are accessible as references, but the inventory link redirected to a Microsoft sign-in page in this research environment. Therefore **no name-level inventory search and no photo match is claimed**. This is a new, targeted lead for a possible portrait or family/news photograph, not evidence that a photograph exists.

## Newsdex access follow-up
The library's current local-history page confirms that **Newsdex** is a public searchable index of local stories, death notices and obituaries. It can provide exact titles, publication dates, pages and column numbers, and sometimes notes whether an image accompanies an item. A library card is not required. The Newsdex homepage exposes a full-text search interface and a separate record-data explorer; its tutorial describes keyword search and filtering by publication year.

The web research interface exposed the search controls but did not return a usable query result for Eunice through this session. Accordingly, this note does **not** claim that Eunice has or does not have a Newsdex entry. The appropriate query set remains Eunice Brewster / Eunice Whittle / Eunice Burton / Eunice Taylor, initially covering 16–22 December 1987, with a broader search for family notices and related names.
