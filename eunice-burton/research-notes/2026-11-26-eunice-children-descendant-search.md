# Eunice Edna Brewster Burton — children and descendant search
**Research date:** 26 November 2026  
**Scope:** Eunice only. Her existing profile and folder were reviewed before this search. The 1987 family death notice remains the primary starting point.

## What the 1987 notice actually establishes
The original clipping in `documents/Eunice-Burton-1987-death-notice-Drive-original.jpg` calls Eunice the mother of:
- Demetria Sexton
- Elaine Bishop
- Anthony Q. Burton

It separately names four grandchildren:
- Michael Sexton
- Melissa Sexton
- Alicea Bishop
- Brandy Bishop

The clipping does not give birth dates, identify the father(s), state which spouse was the parent of which child, or map each grandchild to one of the three named children. No child is assigned to Avery Whittle or Irvin Burton from the notice alone.

## Search work and exclusions
Ran exact-name and combined-name searches for all three children and the four grandchildren, including Hamilton/Butler County, Cincinnati, Brewster, Whittle, Burton and known spouse variants.

- **Demetria Sexton:** No source found that reliably identifies the person named in Eunice's notice. A Demetria Kim Lawrence obituary from Lansing, Michigan (born 22 September 1965 to Jack E. and Delilah Lawrence) is not this Demetria on the evidence available and is excluded. It must not be attached to Eunice.
- **Elaine Bishop:** Search results returned multiple unrelated Elaine Bishops, including people with different parents and locations. No source connected any one of them to Eunice. None was attached.
- **Anthony Q. Burton:** A commercial people-directory result contains this exact name in Butler County, but it is a people-search record, not a birth, marriage, obituary or other genealogical record. It does not establish identity, parentage or descent and is excluded from the evidence. Personal contact/address data was not copied into the archive.
- **Michael and Melissa Sexton; Alicea and Brandy Bishop:** Exact-name combinations did not return a reliable record that connects any of these grandchildren to Eunice or maps them to a specific child. No same-name person was attached.
