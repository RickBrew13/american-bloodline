# Eunice Edna Brewster Burton — follow-up name disambiguation and record access

**Research date:** 25 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Continue the investigation after inspecting the original family death notice and related Brewster obituaries. This is a supplemental note; it does not replace the main research continuation.

## Searches and findings

### Sarah Brewster false match excluded
A broad search returned a derivative listing for a Sarah Brewster born 22 February 1910 and dying 1 February 1987 in Hamilton, Ohio. This is not Eunice's mother Sarah Hazel Kelly. The established Sarah archive gives Sarah Hazel's birth as 22 February 1911 in Calhoun, Georgia, and death as 28 February 1987 in Cincinnati, Ohio. The derivative listing differs in both birth year and death day and provides no spouse or parent link to Homer Brewster. It is excluded as a same-name false match.

Eunice's mother remains Sarah Hazel Kelly, wife of Homer William Brewster Sr. Eunice's 1987 death notice calls her “the late Sarah Brewster”; that notice alone does not state Sarah's maiden name. The Kelly identity is supported by the separate Sarah/Homer archive and their original obituary clippings.

### The children's and grandchildren's identities remain open
Exact-name web searches for Demetria Sexton, Elaine Bishop, Anthony Q. Burton, Michael Sexton, Melissa Sexton, Alicea Bishop and Brandy Bishop did not produce a reliable obituary or vital record that could be tied to Eunice. Common-name results and people-search pages were excluded. No candidate has been attached to the tree from a name-only match.

The direct source remains Eunice's original 1987 family death notice. It names three children—Demetria Sexton, Elaine Bishop and Anthony Q. Burton—and four grandchildren—Michael and Melissa Sexton, Alicea and Brandy Bishop. The surname groupings suggest possible maternal branches, but the notice does not explicitly state each grandchild's parent. It also does not distinguish biological, step-, or adopted grandchildren. Those links remain unproven.

A Demetria Kim Lawrence obituary from Lansing, Michigan, was checked as a possible same-name result and rejected: it identifies her parents as Jack E. and Delilah Lawrence and her 1983 J.W. Sexton High School graduation. She is not the Demetria Sexton in Eunice's notice. Source: https://www.rileyfuneralhome.com/obituaries/demetria-lawrence

### Morgan County obituary search is not yet exhaustive

This is not a countywide or surname-wide negative search: the site separately links annual newspaper clippings beginning in 1916 and surname-specific obituary pages. Individual year pages and other surname pages remain to be searched. Index entry point: https://tngenweb.org/morgan/obituaries-of-morgan-county/

Catalog: https://ldsgenealogy.com/OH/Hamilton-County-Newspapers-and-Obituaries.htm

### Nursing license lookup: actual searchable interface found
The Ohio eLicense system has a dedicated search form with fields for first name, last name, maiden name, city, county and license type/status. The Nursing Board is one of the selectable boards, and the Ohio Attorney General's guidance says to select the regulating board and enter all or part of the licensee's name. This is a more precise route than the general landing page. However, the form is interactive and did not return a readable search result in this session; no Eunice hit or no-hit is claimed.

- Official Ohio license lookup: https://elicense.ohio.gov/OH_VerifyLicense
- Search form: https://elicense2-secure.com.ohio.gov/Lookup/LicenseLookup.aspx
- Ohio Attorney General instructions: https://www.ohioattorneygeneral.gov/FAQ/Regulatory-board-unit-FAQs/How-do-I-verify-the-licensee-of-a-healthcare-provi
- Ohio Board of Nursing: https://nursing.ohio.gov/home/registered-nurse

Recommended searches in that form: Eunice Burton, Eunice Brewster, Eunice Whittle and Eunice Taylor; select Nursing Board and try the maiden-name field as well as the current/last-name field. Her Ohio death-index occupation is “registered nurse,” but the license, school and employer remain unverified.

## Cross-checking the original obituaries of Eunice's parents and brother

I reopened the actual JPEG scans in the existing Homer, Sarah and Elmer folders rather than relying on their transcriptions. They provide unusually strong independent corroboration of Eunice's immediate family.

### Homer W. Brewster's original obituary
The archived image at `homer-william-brewster-sr/img/homer-brewster-obituary.jpg` was visually inspected. It reads, in relevant part:

> Homer W., beloved husband of the late Sarah H. Brewster (nee Kelley), dear father of Aileene Gilpin, Elmer and Homer Brewster and the late Eunice Burton, Eugene and Earl Brewster, also survived by 24 grand and 38 great-grandchildren. Friday, March 8, 1991. Age 79 years. Formerly of College Hill. Friends may call at the Hodapp Funeral Home, 6041 Hamilton Ave., College Hill, Sunday 6–8 P.M. Graveside service Spring Grove Cemetery, Monday 10 A.M.

This independently identifies Eunice Burton as a daughter of Homer W. Brewster and Sarah H. (Kelley) Brewster and places her among the same six children. The age 79 on 8 March 1991 is consistent with a birth in 1911, but the clipping does not print his birth date. The obituary calls him “Homer W.”, not “Willie H.”; this supports the Homer identity but does not by itself resolve the separate Social Security Claims Index transcription.

### Sarah Hazel (nee Kelley) Brewster's original obituary
The archived image at `sarah-hazel-kelly/img/sarah-kelley-brewster-obituary.jpg` was visually inspected. It reads:

> Sarah Hazel (nee Kelley), beloved wife of Homer W. Brewster, dear mother of Eunice Burton, Aileene Gilpin, Elmer and Homer Brewster and the late Eugene and Earl Brewster. Also survived by 24 grand and 37 great-grandchildren. Saturday, February 28, 1987. Age 76 years. Resident of Hamilton, OH. Friends may call at the Hodapp Funeral Home, 6041 Hamilton Ave., College Hill, Monday from 6 to 8 P.M. Funeral services at the funeral home Tuesday, 10 A.M. Remembrances may be made to the American Cancer Society.

This is particularly valuable because it explicitly names **Eunice Burton as Sarah's daughter** and prints Sarah's maiden name as **Kelley**. It confirms the same six-child sibling group shortly before Eunice's own death notice. Sarah's obituary also states 24 grandchildren and 37 great-grandchildren; Homer’s 1991 obituary gives 24 grandchildren and 38 great-grandchildren. These are the respective obituary counts at those dates and should not be treated as a discrepancy or used to assign specific grandchildren.

### Elmer Brewster's original 1993 obituary
The canonical scan was opened from both `sarah-hazel-kelly/img/elmer-brewster-obituary.jpg` and `elmer-brewster/documents/Elmer-Brewster-1993-Palm-Beach-Post-obituary-Drive-original.jpg`. They have the same SHA-1 (`935c4df24a274455e473d4ff6cf9a98da177bdf4`) and are exact duplicates, so the existing shared image remains the single source; no copy was added.

The clipping is headed “ELMER BREWSTER.” It says he was 58, of West Palm Beach, died Saturday, 21 August 1993; had lived in the area four years after coming from “Perrysville, Missouri”; consulted for printing firms for over 35 years and supervised Lantana Toll Plaza for the last three years. It names his wife Mary Jane; children Cathy Bubolz, Diana Keaton, Michael Brewster, Cheryl Schaefer and Wanda Bilby; ten grandchildren; brother Homer Brewster Jr.; sister Aileene Gilpin; and says he was predeceased by twin brother Earl, brother Eugene, sister **Eunice Burton**, and parents **Homer and Sarah Brewster**.

This is a third independent contemporary obituary naming Eunice as a sibling of the same Brewster family. It also confirms the family death notice's spellings “Eunice Burton” and “Aileene Gilpin.” The 1993 clipping states the family had lived in Perrysville, Missouri before the move to Florida, an important possible lead for the parents' later-life residence and the children's upbringing/migration; it does not say when any family member lived there.

### Evidence conclusion after the three original obituary images
- **Parents:** Homer W. Brewster and Sarah Hazel (nee Kelley) Brewster — directly named in Sarah's own obituary as her husband and Eunice as their daughter; Homer’s own obituary and Elmer's obituary independently agree.
- **Siblings:** Aileene/Aileene Gilpin, Elmer Brewster, Homer Brewster Jr., Eugene Brewster and Earl Brewster — the six-child family is named across the parents' obituaries, Elmer's obituary and Eunice's own notice. The parents' clippings spell the sister's name “Aileene”; Eunice's own clipping spells it “Aileen”; her 2015 obituary is titled Aileene Gilpin.
- **This resolves the parent/sibling identity question to a strong documentary standard for the profile.** It does not resolve the “Willie H Brewster” field in the Social Security Applications and Claims Index, which remains a separate transcription/field discrepancy, nor does it establish each sibling's exact birth order or birth date.
- **Migration lead:** Elmer's 1993 obituary says he came to West Palm Beach from “Perrysville, Missouri.” Investigate whether Homer and Sarah or other siblings also lived there, using Missouri directories, census records, land/deed records and local newspapers. Do not assume the whole family moved together.

## Nursing-school image archives checked for similarly named Mercy schools (25 November 2026)

The latest search surfaced several digitized “Mercy Hospital School of Nursing” collections. I checked their institutional locations before treating them as leads:

- **Hamilton, Ohio Mercy Hospital School of Nursing**: the local history source says the diploma school trained nurses from 1906 until its final class graduated in June 1970. A Butler County nursing-honor-guard profile confirms a Hamilton Mercy graduate in 1957 who then worked at Mercy Hospital. This keeps Hamilton Mercy as a plausible local school for Eunice, but there is still no record naming Eunice as a student or graduate. Sources: https://nkhistory.org/content/97-memories-of-early-rural-life and https://www.ohioshospice.org/nursing-honor-guard-ohios-hospice-of-butler-warren-counties/
- **Portsmouth, Ohio Mercy Hospital School of Nursing**: Scioto County Public Library's digital collection has 75 items, including 1946–1960 capping/commencement programs, class lists and portraits. Its own item descriptions explicitly locate the collection in Portsmouth/Scioto County. This is not Hamilton's Mercy school; the Portsmouth collection cannot establish Eunice's training without a separate connection. It is excluded as a direct Eunice record. Collection: https://www.sciotolibrary.org/history/items/browse?collection=232
- **Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania Mercy Hospital School of Nursing**: the UPMC Mercy Archives digital collection contains 1950s graduation and student-life photographs. Its institutional identity is Pittsburgh, not Hamilton, Ohio. Excluded from Eunice's local school evidence. Sources: https://upmcmercydigitalcollection.omeka.net/collections/show/1 and https://upmcmercydigitalcollection.omeka.net/exhibits/show/mercy-hospital-school-nursing/graduations/1950s
- **Good Samaritan Hospital School of Nursing, Cincinnati**: Ohio Memory's digitized 1947–1970 two-volume history is a valid Cincinnati institution source, and the school was active in the relevant years. The digitized item is a historical scrapbook, not an indexed student roster; its public description does not identify Eunice. Source: https://ohiomemory.org/digital/collection/p16007coll72/id/2976/rec/3
- **University of Cincinnati College of Nursing**: the university's finding aid confirms College of Nursing records from 1940–2004 and student rosters/class records 1946–1987. This remains a valid archival route, but no named Eunice entry has been found in the online finding aid. Source: https://www.libraries.uc.edu/libraries/arb/collections/university-archives/ua-collections.html

The search result for a different Mercy school's yearbook/photograph collection was not used as evidence about Eunice. The distinction matters because “Mercy Hospital School of Nursing” is not a unique institutional name.

**No new photograph, class roster, license record or graduation notice for Eunice was identified.**
