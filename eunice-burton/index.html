Life Story

Eunice Edna Brewster was born on December 22, 1930, in Cincinnati, Ohio, to Homer William Brewster Sr. and Sarah Hazel Kelley. DOCUMENTED She was their first child, born four months after their wedding, and the elder of their two daughters. SUPPORTED In 1940 she was eight years old and living with her parents, brothers and little sister in Morgan County, Tennessee. SUPPORTED

The family remembers that she married Ello Whittle first, and the Social Security record confirms she used the name Whittle. FAMILY ACCOUNT The same record shows she also went by Taylor, which may point to another marriage. UNPROVED By the end of her life she was the wife of Irvin Burton and lived in Hamilton, Ohio, and her children were Demetria Sexton, Elaine Bishop and Anthony Q. Burton. DOCUMENTED

Eunice died suddenly on December 16, 1987, at 56, less than ten months after her mother. DOCUMENTED The Hodapp Funeral Home of Carthage handled her funeral, and services were held at the Rose Hill Burial Park Chapel in Hamilton. DOCUMENTED
