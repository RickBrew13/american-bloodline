Life Story

Dell Fields is documented as the father of Dell Michael Fields by the son’s 1982 Hamilton County marriage license (Vol. 28, p. 1742). The license gives Dell Michael’s date/place of birth and names his parents as “Dell Fields” and “Corr. Carruba.” Dell Michael’s 2012 obituary independently calls his parents Cora Fields and the late Dell Fields.

A 2022 obituary for Clyde Mullins names his wife as Cora Fields Mullins, née Carubba, and calls Gary, Joy, Tony, and the late Michael his stepchildren. Together with Dell Michael’s sibling list, this strongly supports that “Corr. Carruba” refers to Cora (Carubba) Fields Mullins. The marriage of Dell Fields and Cora is not established by these records.

The obituaries identify Garry/Gary, Dave Anthony/Tony, and Joy Dianne as Dell Michael’s siblings and Cora’s children, but do not name their biological fathers. They are not assigned as Dell Fields Sr.’s children here. No reliable birth, death, parent, sibling, or portrait record for Dell Fields Sr. has yet been located.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • “Corr. Carruba” is the spelling/form in the 1982 license. Later obituaries give Cora Fields and Cora Fields Mullins (née Carubba). The identity reconciliation is strong, but retain the license transcription and source spelling.
  • Do not state that Dell and Cora married: the 1982 record identifies them as parents, not as a married couple.
  • Dell Michael’s three siblings are reported by his obituary. The 2022 obituary for their mother’s later husband adds a stepchild list. Other siblings’ paternity by Dell Sr. remains unresolved.
  • Dell Fields Sr.’s parents, siblings, dates, and portrait remain unidentified. Targeted FamilySearch searches returned many unrelated same-name Fields records; no identity bridge was found.
  • The family-submitted photo on Dell Michael’s obituary shows Dell Michael with an unidentified companion. It is not evidence of a portrait of Dell Fields Sr. or Cora and is not used as either parent’s profile image.
