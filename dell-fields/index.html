Family Relationship Notes

The 1950 census index places Dell in a Letcher County household with Arlie and Maudie Fields and names Cora Lee, Edgar, Phyllis, Imagene, and Flora Lee as siblings. Two Find a Grave memorials also link Arlie and Maudie as Dell’s parents and Cora Lee as his sister. The original census schedule has not been inspected; the additional sibling names are therefore retained as index leads.

The 1982 Hamilton County marriage license names “Dell Fields” as the father of Dell Michael Fields and “Corr. Carruba” as his mother. Dell Michael’s 2012 obituary calls the late Dell Fields his father and identifies his mother as Cora Fields. These records support the parent-child link, but do not establish that Dell Fields and Cora (Carubba) were married. Cora Lee Fields Smith, Dell’s sister, is a different person from Cora (Carubba) Fields Mullins.

Compiled FamilySearch and Ancestry pages identify possible earlier children of Arlie and a woman named Rosa Mae or Rosa Maudie Fields: Madge Fields (1924), Felix Gillis Fields (1925–1991), and Mae Fields (1928–2009). Rosa Mae/Rosa Maudie may be Maudie (Boggs) Fields, but no original record directly connects those names. These remain possible members of the older family group, not established siblings in this profile.

Life Story

Dell Fields is strongly supported as the same man born 24 September 1931 in Whitesburg, Kentucky, and buried at Rose Hill Burial Park. His Find a Grave memorial reports his death on 11 May 1976 and links him as the father of Dell Michael Fields. The 1982 Hamilton County marriage license independently names “Dell Fields” as Dell Michael’s father, and Dell Michael’s 2012 obituary calls his father the late Dell Fields. The exact identity bridge relies on the linked memorial and remains graded corroborated rather than proved by an original birth or death record.

Two Find a Grave memorials and the FamilySearch 1950 census index identify Arlie Blair Fields and Maudie Boggs Fields as Dell’s parents. The same memorial set identifies Cora Lee (Fields) Smith as his sister. FamilySearch’s 1950 census index lists additional children in the household—Edgar, Phyllis, Imagene, and Flora Lee Fields—but the original schedule has not been inspected. The index assigns Dell an estimated birth year of 1932, while the memorial and marker report 1931; keep that one-year difference open.

This Dell is not his son Dell Michael Fields. Nor is Dell’s sister Cora Lee Fields Smith the same person as his son’s mother, Cora (Carubba) Fields Mullins. The 1982 license names Dell Fields and “Corr. Carruba” as Dell Michael’s parents, but does not establish that the parents married.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Identity match: the 1982 license and 2012 obituary establish a father named Dell Fields; Find a Grave supplies the 1931–1976 identity and family links. Keep the match corroborated until an original record connects them.
  • Birth year discrepancy: marker/memorial reports 1931; the 1950 census index estimates 1932. Do not normalize away the discrepancy before viewing the original census schedule.
  • Arlie and Maudie are supported as Dell’s parents by the 1950 index and two linked memorials. Their child list in the 1950 index is Edgar, Phyllis, Dell, Flora Lee, Imagene, and Cora Lee; not necessarily a complete lifetime list.
  • Cora Lee Fields Smith is Dell’s sister, not the same person as Cora (Carubba) Fields Mullins. Keep the two women and their family roles distinct.
  • The three siblings named in Dell Michael’s obituary and Clyde Mullins’s stepchild list are not assigned as Dell Sr.’s children; their biological fathers remain unresolved.
  • Additional earlier child leads: compiled FamilySearch identifies Arlie and Rosa Mae Fields as parents of Madge (1924) and Felix Gillis (1925–1991); Ancestry identifies Arlie and Rosa Maudie Fields as parents of Mae (1928–2009). Rosa Mae / Rosa Maudie may be Maudie (Boggs) Fields, but no original record explicitly bridges those names. Keep these as possible, not confirmed, parent-child links.
