Life Story

Orrin C. Moore, also recorded as Orie or Orrie, was born in Iowa about 1869 to an Irish-born father SUPPORTED. With his wife Mary he was living in Garfield Township, Plymouth County, Iowa, in 1910, with their daughter Viola and son Leroy S., born in August 1897 SUPPORTED. An Iowa birth index names Leroy S.’s parents as Orrin C. Moore and Mary C. Randall UNPROVED. Whether that Leroy is Leroy C. Moore of Detroit, Patrick Moore’s father, is not yet proved UNPROVED.
