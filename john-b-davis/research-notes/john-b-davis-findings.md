## Plain-words summary (for Rick — no research jargon)

Here's what the dig found on John B. Davis, plain and simple:

**John himself is still a ghost.** Not one record turned up that says who he was —
no birth date, no death date, no middle name, no parents, no brothers or sisters,
no address, no job, no army record. The only paper that proves he existed at all
is still the two marriage licenses where he's named as the father of Debbie and
Alex. We checked a long list of men named John B. Davis and none of them fit — the
closest-looking one, a John Bernard Davis from Massachusetts who died in Dayton,
has no tie to Eva or Cincinnati, so we didn't attach him. Don't let anyone tell you
the "B" stands for Bernard — that's that other man's name, not ours.

**Eva's identity got a lot firmer.** The woman Eva Leea Luft, later Vanhorn — born
9 February 1918 in Cincinnati, died 9 August 2002, daughter of Alexander and
Bertha Luft — is real, backed by her Social Security records. She had a brother
David (1915–1993) and a sister Minnie (1914–1999). But nothing we found proves she
is Debbie and Alex's mother. Her 2002 obituary, which would probably name her
children, couldn't be found anywhere free — that's the one paper that would settle
it, and it's still out there to be got through the library.

**The two Roberts are sorted out.** Robert Vanhorn is Debbie and Alex's brother —
that stands. Whether John was his father, nobody's paper says, and the likeliest
story is that he's Eva's son by a Vanhorn husband, making him their half-brother
on their mother's side. There's a real Robert R. Vanhorn living in Cincinnati at
2632 Barthas Pl, about 82 years old, born January 1944, and a woman named Cherie
at the same address who might be his wife — but that's just address listings, not
proof of who his parents were. Robert Luft is a different person: he's Debbie's
son, John's grandson, so he belongs a generation down from Debbie and Alex, not
alongside them. And the Robert Luft who runs a company in Cincinnati is a much
younger man, born 1982 — not ours.

**Alex's birthday is pinned down: 21 February 1956.** Debbie's still 26 January
1953. Nobody found Charles's last name, or Debbie's other children, or whether
Debbie and Alex had kids of their own. One thin lead: listings call Debbie "Debbie
Salley" — if Salley is a married name, it might be Charles's, but there's no paper
behind it yet.

**Maggie's mother is confirmed.** Shirley Ann Green's obituary was found and read:
11 August 1933 to 11 September 2015, wife of the late Fairest Green, mother of
Michael, Mardi, Michelle, Rusty, Mary Jo, Eddie, and Maggie. It doesn't name Alex
or any grandchildren, so it doesn't help with Alex and Maggie's children.

**No marriages found.** No paper shows John and Eva ever married, no paper shows
Eva marrying a Vanhorn, and no paper shows John marrying anyone else. If Eva did
marry a Vanhorn, it would most likely have been around 1942–43, before Debbie was
born — but that's a guess from Robert's birth year, not a record.

**Mary, Robert's guardian:** Mary is Debbie's adopted daughter — Rick's fiancee, Mary Ellen Megan Ort — and the legal guardian of her adoptive uncle Robert Vanhorn. No court filing turned up in a web search. The filing lives at the Hamilton County Probate Court in Cincinnati, and
