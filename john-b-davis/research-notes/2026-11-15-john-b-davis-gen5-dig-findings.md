## What this dig found

### F1 — CORRECTION: the "Robert R. Vanhorn, b. Jan 1944" lead is dead — CONTRADICTED
The Nov 11 dig's best Robert lead was a Cincinnati "Robert R. Vanhorn, 2632
Barthas Pl, age ~82, born Jan 1944" from people-search aggregators, graded
SUPPORTED (search summary only). That man is NOT our Robert.

**Robert Vanhorn was born 15 July 1962; his birth certificate names Eva "Luft"
as mother and William Allen VanHorn as father. DOCUMENTED** (how-known: read
directly — the fact is published on Eva L. Luft's live profile page and on
William Allen VanHorn's live profile page, both read by my own curl 15 Nov
2026; I did not see the certificate image itself, and its issuing jurisdiction
and certificate number have not yet been recorded — both live pages say so).
- Eva's live page: "Son Robert Vanhorn born; his birth certificate names Eva
  'Luft' as mother and William Allen VanHorn as father", timeline 15 July 1962.
- William Allen VanHorn's live page
  (https://rickbrew13.github.io/american-bloodline/william-allen-vanhorn/):
  "father of Robert Vanhorn", son Robert Vanhorn born 15 July 1962, birth
  certificate names William as father and Eva "Luft" as mother (DOCUMENTED).
- Consequence: Robert is Deborah and Alex's brother **through their mother
  Eva** (licenses name Eva as Deborah/Alex's mother; certificate names Eva as
  Robert's mother) — established by the two records, no family-account framing
  needed. John B. Davis is definitively NOT Robert's father. The Jan-1944 birth
  month, the 2632 Barthas Pl address, and "Cherie A. Vanhorn" as possible wife
  all belonged to a different, older Robert Vanhorn and are struck from this
  family. Do not re-attach them.
- Consequence for the marriage question: Eva was 44 when Robert was born
  (b. 9 Feb 1918). The old reasoning "a Vanhorn marriage would fall ~1942–43"
  (built on the 1944 birth year) is void. No Vanhorn marriage date can be
  inferred from Robert's birth at all now.
