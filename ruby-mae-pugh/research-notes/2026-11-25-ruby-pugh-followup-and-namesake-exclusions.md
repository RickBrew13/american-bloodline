## New namesake candidate found and excluded
A search for Ruby Mae Moore/Griffin surfaced an unusually close candidate:
- **Ruby Mae Griffin**, born 31 August 1923 in Phenix City, Alabama; died 29 October 2016; funeral home: Bass Funeral Home, Gordonsville Chapel. Obituary: https://www.bassfh.com/obituaries/Ruby-Griffin-46155/ .
- The 31 October 2016 Carthage Courier transcription states she was a native of Rome, Georgia, daughter of Walter Ornald Moore and Pearl Howard; married Robert Lawrence Griffin on 23 September 1939; preceded in death by brothers Jack and Earl Moore and sister Louise Smith; lived in Rome until moving to Tennessee in 2010; survived by son Robert L. Griffin Jr. and daughter Mona Joan Young; burial at Floyd Memory Gardens in Rome. Source: https://www.carthagecourier.com/2016/10/31/obit-mrs-ruby-mae-griffin-age-93-of-gordonsville-tn/ .
- This is **not** the project’s Ruby Mae Pugh. Her 1930 census identifies a child Ruby M. Pugh in Birmingham with Rosabelle; her 1939 marriage names Cornelius Pugh and Rosabelle Grant as parents; and her 1945 marriage index names Cornelius Pugh and Rosa Bell White. The 2016 Griffin obituary instead gives parents Walter Ornald Moore and Pearl Howard and a different 1939 husband, Robert Lawrence Griffin. The coincident 1923 birth year and Alabama origin are not enough to overcome the contradictory parentage and marriage. Do not attach the Griffin death date, spouse, children, or Floyd Memory Gardens burial to this Ruby.
- The funeral-home and newspaper obituary provide a clear exclusion; the PeopleLegacy aggregation's “Ruby Mae Moore Griffin 1923–2016” burial line is a secondary index, not a distinct record and is not evidence for our subject.

Other surfaced Ruby Moore/Griffin results also lacked a family bridge and were not attached.
