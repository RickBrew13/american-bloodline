Life Story

Ruby Mae Pugh was born in Alabama about 1923, the daughter of Cornelius Pugh and Rosa Belle White DOCUMENTED. In 1930 she was seven, living in Birmingham with her mother in the household of Shelton and Mattie Willis DOCUMENTED. At about sixteen she married Joseph Leslie Collins on 15 November 1939 DOCUMENTED, and their daughter Leslie Mae was born in Birmingham in July 1940 DOCUMENTED. On 5 February 1945 she married George Howard Moore DOCUMENTED, and their son George Howard Moore, Jr. was born in 1947 SUPPORTED.
