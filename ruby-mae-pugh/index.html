Family Relationship Notes

The 1939 Alabama marriage index names Cornelius Pugh and Rosabelle Grant as Ruby’s parents. The 1945 marriage index names Cornelius Pugh and Rosabelle White. The 1930 census lists Ruby, age seven, as the daughter of Cornelius and Rosabelle Pugh in their Birmingham household. These sources support Cornelius as her father and Rosabelle as her mother, while the mother’s surname forms remain unresolved.

Ruby married Joseph Leslie Collins on 15 November 1939 and George Moore on 5 February 1945 (the groom’s middle name is not confirmed by the index). The marriage record gives her age as 19 in 1939, which conflicts with the 1930 census age of seven. The 1930 census age of seven and 1945 marriage index age of twenty-two suggest about 1922–1923; the 1939 marriage and 1940 census ages of nineteen suggest about 1920–1921. The original marriage image should be checked before changing the birth estimate.

Her documented child is Leslie Mae (Collins) Niles, whose Social Security NUMIDENT and later marriage record connect Ruby to Joseph Leslie Collins. A compiled family source also reports a son, George Howard Moore Jr., but his birth, parentage and death dates have not been independently verified. The 1945 marriage index names the groom only as George Moore; the identification as George Howard Moore comes from family-tree material and remains provisional.

Life Story

Ruby Mae Pugh was born in Alabama about 1923. Her father is identified as Cornelius Pugh in both the 1939 and 1945 marriage indexes; her mother appears as Rosabelle Grant in 1939, Rosabelle Pugh in the 1930 census, and Rosa Bell White in 1945. These are likely surname forms for the same mother, but the name sequence is not established. In 1930 she was seven, living in Birmingham with parents Cornelius and Rosabelle Pugh in household 208. She married Joseph Leslie Collins on 15 November 1939. Her age on the 1930 census and 1945 marriage index points to a birth around 1922–1923, while the 1939 marriage and 1940 census both give age nineteen and point to about 1920–1921. The discrepancy remains unresolved. Their daughter Leslie Mae was born in Birmingham in July 1940. On 5 February 1945 an Alabama marriage index records her marriage to George Moore; family records identify the man as George Howard Moore, but the original image has not been inspected to confirm the middle name. A compiled family source reports a son, George Howard Moore Jr., born in 1947, but no original birth or parentage record has been located.
