# Sources for Elizabeth (Williams) Edwards Combs

- [FamilySearch, Elizabeth Williams, profile G8H2-RN4](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/G8H2-RN4/elizabeth-williams-1835-1918) — Published profile reports 2 November 1835–20 May 1918, parents William Quin Williams and Mary “Polly” McAnally, marriage to Jacob Edwards in 1856, later spouse Thomas C. Combs in 1885, and Maude among three children. Compiled-profile evidence.
- [Find a Grave, Elizabeth Edward Edwards Combs, memorial 18113047](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/18113047/elizabeth-edwards_combs) — Contributor-maintained memorial reports the same dates; links spouse Thomas C. Combs and children including Maud Allen Combs Campbell. One grave-marker photo was added by Rochelle Riordan 24 June 2007; a second image is a 1918 obituary clipping added by treesrmoi 2 July 2025.
- [Casey County News, 28 May 1918, page 3; clipping attached to Find a Grave memorial 18113047](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/18113047/elizabeth-edwards_combs/photo) — Contemporary obituary calls Elizabeth T.C. Combs’s “beloved wife,” reports prolonged blindness, and says she was buried near her old home at the Williams burying ground. The displayed clipping identifies the issue/date/page; image uploaded by treesrmoi 2 July 2025.
- [Combs &c. Families of Casey County, Kentucky](https://combs-families.org/combs/records/ky/casey/) — Transcribed Casey County marriage entry and family-history excerpt call Thomas C. Combs’s wife Elizabeth Edwards / Elizabeth Edwards Williams; secondary compilation, not original civil proof.
- [FamilySearch Indiana marriage index, Thomas Combs and Elizabeth Williams, record VZQT-B1Z](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:VZQT-B1Z) — Index reports a marriage license dated 24 May 1870 in Jackson Township, Jackson County, Indiana. The identity match to Elizabeth in the Casey County family is not yet established.
- [FamilySearch, Maude Allen Combs, profile LJJX-TQM](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/LJJX-TQM/) — Names Elizabeth Williams as Maude’s mother; compiled-profile evidence.
