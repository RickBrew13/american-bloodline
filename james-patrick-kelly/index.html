Life Story

James Patrick Kelly was born on 8 May 1842 in New York, by the family's telling; no birth record has been found. Nothing is documented about his life before 1920 — no parents, no marriage record, no military record, no earlier census household has been proved to be his.

On 20 January 1920 he was enumerated at Aragon, Polk County, Georgia, as James P. Kelley, 78, born in New York to Ireland-born parents, with his wife Belle, born in Mississippi, and four children in the household: Bessie M., 21; Sallie, 9; William E., 6; and Julia B., 4. That census sheet is the one record that certainly shows him.

He married Laura Bell Underwood about 1906–1910, though no marriage record has been located. He is reported to have died about 1928 in Tennessee; no death record or burial has been found. He appears as father on his daughter Bessie's death record index and as spouse on Laura's 1936 death certificate.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Parents are not proved. A Clinton County, New York family (John Kelly × Mary Kelly) is the current working lead at about 60%, not a proved parentage; it is not carried as his parents here.
  • No marriage record for James × Laura has been located; the marriage window of about 1906–1910 is an estimate.
  • No death record or burial has been found for James; the "about 1928, Tennessee" death is unsourced.
  • Civil War service is not proved. A Company M, 2nd New York Veteran Cavalry roster entry for a James Kelley (enlisted 1 December 1863, New York) is a 40–50% lead only; the full service record has not been ordered. A 1921 pension card naming a James Patrick Kelly in the Artillery/Coast Artillery has been examined and is NOT this man.
  • Bessie's birth about 1899 predates the assumed 1906–1910 marriage by years; how she fits the family chronology is unresolved, and her parent link rests on a death-record index, not the certificate image.
  • Julia's birth year conflicts (1920 census about September 1915, Georgia, vs 6 January 1914, Rome, Georgia, on Geni), and her death day conflicts (27 August vs 22 August 1997); years only are carried here.
  • A daughter Margaret appears in family telling only and in no record; she is not listed among the children.
  • The family has not been found in the 1930 census under Kelly or Kelley.
