Life Story

James Hope Taylor, known as Hope, was born on 11 November 1863 in Pendleton County, Kentucky, the son of James Norris Taylor and Sarah Jane Gedge DOCUMENTED. He farmed around Butler all his life DOCUMENTED.

Hope married Ora Mullins in 1897, and they raised six daughters and a son, Wilbur SUPPORTED. After Ora died in 1940 he lived with Wilbur's family, and he died at Butler on 1 November 1943 DOCUMENTED. They lie together in Butler Cemetery SUPPORTED.
