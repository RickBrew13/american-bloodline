Family Relationship Notes

  • James’s Kentucky death certificate gives his birth as 11 November 1863, names his parents as James N. Taylor and Sarah J. Gedge, and records his occupation as farmer and burial at Butler Cemetery.
  • He was known as “Hope.” The suggestion that this name came from his maternal grandfather Christian Hope Gedge remains unproved.
  • James and Ora married on 4 February 1897 according to the Pendleton County marriage index. The original marriage record has not yet been inspected.
  • The seven children listed follow the family research and census records. Wilbur Kyle is named as the informant on James’s death certificate.

Life Story

James Hope Taylor, known as Hope, was born on 11 November 1863 in Pendleton County, Kentucky, the son of James Norris Taylor and Sarah Jane Gedge . He farmed around Butler all his life .

Hope married Ora Mullins in 1897, and they raised six daughters and a son, Wilbur . After Ora died in 1940 he lived with Wilbur's family, and he died at Butler on 1 November 1943 . They lie together in Butler Cemetery .
