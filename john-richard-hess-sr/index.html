Life Story

John Richard Hess Sr. was the husband of Arlene Marie (Forste) Hess Murphy; they married and divorced in 1967. He was the father of Holly Brewster, John Hess Jr., and Heidi Marie (Hess) Smith. His daughter Heidi’s 2018 obituary names him as “the late John Hess Sr.” and names Arlene as Heidi’s mother. His birth and death dates come from his Find a Grave memorial; the marriage and divorce come from the FamilySearch tree. The best- parent identification is Howard Bernard Hess and Anne Elizabeth (Mader) Hess: a secondary transcription of John’s 1950 SS-5 names Howard B. Hess and Ann E. Mader, and John’s Find a Grave memorial links the same parents. The original SS-5 has not yet been inspected. His birthplace, siblings, residences, and occupation have not yet been independently established.
