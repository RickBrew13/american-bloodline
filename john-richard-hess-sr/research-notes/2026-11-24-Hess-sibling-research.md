# John Richard Hess Sr. — sibling research

## Findings
John Richard Hess Sr. (17 Feb 1935–9 Feb 1986) is one of seven children attributed to Howard Bernard Hess and Anne Elizabeth Mader. Their memorial-linked family lists and the 1953 Howard Hess newspaper notice identify the children as John Richard, Katherine (later Brady), Howard B. Jr., Nancy Jane (later Herbert), Robert Charles / Herbert Robert Charles (name order varies), Mary Helen (later Pratt), and Charles H. Hess.

The 1953 notice enumerates four sons and three daughters at the family home but uses only first names for some: Howard Jr., Richard, Charles, Robert; Catherine, Nancy, Mary Helen. Anne’s 1990 obituary transcription names Katherine Brady, Nancy Herbert, Mary Helen Pratt, Robert C., Charles H., late Howard B. Jr., and J. Richard Hess. The memorial-linked date/name forms are secondary and have discrepancies, so this page lists six siblings with variants noted, not as fully reconciled identities.
