# Hess parentage and profile-link update — 17 November 2026

## Research conclusion
John Richard Hess Sr. (17 February 1935–9 February 1986) is identified as the son of **Howard Bernard Hess (10 April 1899–23 October 1953)** and **Anne Elizabeth (Mader) Hess (21 July 1903–7 July 1990)**. Grade: **SUPPORTED**, not DOCUMENTED in this review. Sorted By Name transcribes John's 1950 SS-5 application as naming Howard B. Hess and Ann E. Mader as his parents. The original SS-5 image/application has not been examined. Find a Grave memorial 86978469 lists Howard and Anne as John's parents; their separate memorials list each other as spouses and John as a child. These are compiled memorial links, not substitutes for original records.
