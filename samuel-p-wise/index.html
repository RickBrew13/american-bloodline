Family Relationship Notes

Monica Lynn Wise’s 1976 Hamilton County marriage abstract names Samuel P. Wise as her father and Virginia Greiner as her mother; Virginia’s 2006 obituary independently identifies Samuel as her late husband and Monica Kelly as their daughter. The compiled FamilySearch profile and Find a Grave family group report parents Richard Hood Wise and Millie Ann Tipton Wise for Samuel Preston Wise (1913–1989), a probable match to this Samuel P. Wise if the Cincinnati death-index candidate is correct. The Find a Grave memorial says Samuel and Virginia adopted Monica and “Sam Jr.”; Virginia’s obituary calls their son Sam “Skip,” plausibly the same child but not independently equated. Find a Grave gives Samuel Preston’s birth as 25 December 1913; the derivative Cincinnati death index gives 24 December 1913. The original birth/death records have not been reviewed. The two patent inventors named Samuel P. Wise remain possible, unconfirmed matches; no occupation is assigned.

Life Story

Samuel P. Wise was the late husband of Virginia (Greiner) Wise and father of Monica Lynn Wise. Monica’s 1976 marriage abstract names him as her father; Virginia’s 2006 obituary names him as her late husband and Monica Kelly as their daughter. Virginia’s obituary also names son Sam “Skip.” Samuel Preston Wise’s Find a Grave memorial says he and Virginia adopted Monica and “Sam Jr.”; Sam Jr. may be Sam “Skip,” but that identity remains unverified. A Cincinnati death-index entry for Samuel P. Wise (24 December 1913–7 July 1989) is a probable identity match to Samuel Preston Wise (memorial birth 25 December 1913; 1913–1989), whose parents and sibling group are reported as Richard Hood Wise and Millie Ann Tipton Wise. Keep those parent/sibling assignments conditional on the identity match. Possible patent records do not establish occupation.
