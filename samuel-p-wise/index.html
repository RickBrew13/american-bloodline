Life Story

Samuel P. Wise is known only as the father of Monica Lynn Wise. When Monica married Stanley William Cossman on 30 June 1976 in Hamilton County, Ohio, her marriage license named Samuel P. Wise as her father and Virginia Greiner as her mother, and gave Monica’s birth as 17 July 1956 in Ohio. Nothing else is yet known about Samuel — no birth, death, marriage, or residence record has been found, and he has no profile on FamilySearch.
