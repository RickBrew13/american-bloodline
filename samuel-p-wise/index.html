Family Relationship Notes

Monica Lynn Wise’s 1976 Hamilton County, Ohio, marriage license names Samuel P. Wise as her father and Virginia Greiner as her mother. It gives Monica’s birth date as 17 July 1956 in Ohio. This is the only located record naming Samuel.

Virginia G. Wise’s 2006 obituary independently identifies her as the wife of the late Samuel P. Wise and names children Sam “Skip” Wise and Monica Kelly. Monica’s 1976 marriage license names Samuel P. Wise as her father and Virginia Greiner as her mother. Together these sources establish the couple’s marriage and Monica’s parentage. The 1989 death-index entry for a Samuel P. Wise born in 1913 and last resident in Cincinnati is a probable match, but the original death certificate or obituary has not been located.

Two patents list an inventor named Samuel P. Wise: a 1955 Sherwin-Williams patent naming Samuel P. Wise of Deer Park, Ohio, and a 1967 Drackett Co. patent naming Samuel P. Wise. The Cincinnati-area location and matching name make these possible professional records, but no document directly connects either inventor to this family. Do not assign an occupation from them without an identity bridge.

Do not merge Samuel with another person of the same name or add additional family relationships without an identity-linked record.

Life Story

Samuel P. Wise was the husband of Virginia (Greiner) Wise and father of Monica Lynn Wise. Monica’s 1976 Hamilton County marriage license names him as her father; Virginia’s 2006 obituary calls him her late husband and names Monica Kelly as their daughter. A derivative death index lists a Samuel P. Wise born 24 December 1913 and died 7 July 1989 in the Cincinnati area. This is a strong candidate for Monica’s father, but the original death certificate and a full obituary naming Virginia or Monica have not yet been located.

Two patent records list an inventor Samuel P. Wise in the Cincinnati region, one associated with Sherwin-Williams in 1955 and one with Drackett Co. in 1967. They may refer to this Samuel, but the identity bridge is not complete, so his occupation remains unknown.
