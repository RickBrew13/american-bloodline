# Samuel P. Wise — identity and family follow-up
**Date:** 25 November 2026  
**Folder-first review:** Samuel's existing profile and the related Monica and Virginia profiles and research notes were inspected before this search. No personal photograph, birth certificate, death certificate, cemetery marker or standalone Samuel obituary was present in his profile archive.

## Family identity now strengthened
Monica Lynn Wise's original 1976 Hamilton County marriage license names **Samuel P. Wise** as her father and **Virginia Greiner** as her mother. Virginia G. Wise's obituary, published by Gilligan Funeral Homes, says she was the beloved wife of the late Samuel P. Wise and mother of Sam “Skip” Wise and Monica Kelly:
https://www.gilliganfuneralhomes.com/obituaries/virginia-wise

The matching father and mother names on Monica's 1976 license and Virginia's 2006 obituary establish that Samuel P. Wise and Virginia (Greiner) Wise were spouses and that Monica Kelly is the daughter later known as Monica Lynn Wise/Cossman. The obituary also names a son, Sam “Skip” Wise. It does not independently identify Samuel as Sam Skip's father, so his paternal link remains less direct.

## Probable birth/death index match

Source: https://www.locateancestors.com/samuel-wise/

This is a strong candidate for Monica's father: exact full name, Ohio/Cincinnati context, and death before Virginia's 2006 obituary. The original SS-5, death certificate, full obituary and cemetery record have not been located. Treat these dates as **probable, not fully proven**. Do not include or copy the index's Social Security number into the family archive.
