# Samuel P. Wise — identity and family follow-up
**Date:** 25 November 2026  
**Folder-first review:** Samuel's existing profile and the related Monica and Virginia profiles and research notes were inspected before this search. No personal photograph, birth certificate, death certificate, cemetery marker or standalone Samuel obituary was present in his profile archive.

## Family identity now strengthened
Monica Lynn Wise's original 1976 Hamilton County marriage license names **Samuel P. Wise** as her father and **Virginia Greiner** as her mother. Virginia G. Wise's obituary, published by Gilligan Funeral Homes, says she was the beloved wife of the late Samuel P. Wise and mother of Sam “Skip” Wise and Monica Kelly:
https://www.gilliganfuneralhomes.com/obituaries/virginia-wise

The matching father and mother names on Monica's 1976 license and Virginia's 2006 obituary establish that Samuel P. Wise and Virginia (Greiner) Wise were spouses and that Monica Kelly is the daughter later known as Monica Lynn Wise/Cossman. The obituary also names a son, Sam “Skip” Wise. It does not independently identify Samuel as Sam Skip's father, so his paternal link remains less direct.

## Probable birth/death index match
LocateAncestors' derivative Social Security death-index listing includes one **Samuel P. Wise**:
- Birth: 24 December 1913
- Death: 7 July 1989
- Last residence: Cincinnati, Hamilton County, Ohio 45243

Source: https://www.locateancestors.com/samuel-wise/

This is a strong candidate for Monica's father: exact full name, Ohio/Cincinnati context, and death before Virginia's 2006 obituary. The original SS-5, death certificate, full obituary and cemetery record have not been located. Treat these dates as **probable, not fully proven**. Do not include or copy the index's Social Security number into the family archive.

## Possible professional records — not yet linked
Two patents list an inventor named Samuel P. Wise:
1. **US2956962A**, filed 24 May 1955, *Viscosity stabilization of latex emulsion paints containing proteins by inclusion therein of aldehydes*. The specification names Samuel P. Wise of Deer Park, Ohio, and Betty Oberdorfer Straus of Norwood, Ohio, assigning to Sherwin-Williams: https://patents.google.com/patent/US2956962A/en
2. **US3463735A**, filed 18 October 1967, *Glass cleaning composition*. Google Patents lists Samuel P. Wise and Melvin E. Stonebraker as inventors and Drackett Co. as assignee: https://patents.google.com/patent/US3463735A/en

Deer Park and Norwood are in the Cincinnati region, geographically consistent with the 1989 index's Cincinnati residence. However, no record directly ties either patent inventor to Monica's father. Do not state an occupation, employer or professional biography as fact until a direct identity bridge is found.

## New compiled Wise / Tipton family-group expansion — 25 November 2026

It names his parents as **Richard Hood Wise (1882–1960)** and **Millie Ann Tipton Wise (1888–1969)**, married in Estill County, Kentucky, in 1909. The marriage date conflicts: FamilySearch gives 15 February 1909; the older Ole.net Tipton genealogy gives 1 February 1909. ### Samuel Tipton parentage and spouse-history warning
The KYGenWeb / Ole.net Tipton genealogy claims Samuel Tipton (born 1863, died 1955) was a son of William H. Tipton, but the same tree's own child list for William H. and Malinda omits Samuel and lists only children born 1844–1850. A separate Hiatt compilation gives Samuel's birth as January 1865 and death as 18 September 1955, but does not expose his parents in the accessible entry. Samuel's parentage is therefore **unknown**; do not attach William H. Tipton and Malinda Crowe as his parents.

The same sources disagree about Samuel's spouses:
- Mary Emma McIntosh, married 21 March 1887, is consistently named as wife and mother of Millie Ann.
- Hiatt also lists a later marriage to Martha Ann Brinegar (1890–1963), but no date is provided in the accessible excerpt.
- Ole.net lists Samuel and Martha as parents of Ora Lee (1907) and Marcella (1909), while Mary Emma was still living (died 1925). This may reflect two same-name Samuel Tiptons or a conflated tree; no second marriage or parentage assignment is adopted.
- Hiatt family compilation: https://www.hiattfamily.net/gen/lda-2014-10-03/aqwg10828.htm
- KYGenWeb Estill County Tipton family page: https://mail.kygenweb.net/estill/site/family/families/tipton/
