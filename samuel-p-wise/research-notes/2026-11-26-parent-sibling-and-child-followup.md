# Samuel P. Wise — parent, sibling and child follow-up

**Reviewed:** 26 November 2026

## Parents and identity boundary

The Gen VII pages for Richard Hood Wise (1882–1960) and Millie Ann Tipton Wise (1888–1969) are linked from this profile and link back here. Their family group includes a son named Samuel Preston Wise (1913–1989). Find a Grave links Richard and Millie as Samuel Preston's parents; the compiled FamilySearch profile for Millie lists the same couple and six children.

The identity bridge to Monica's father, Samuel P. Wise, remains **probable, not proven**. The candidate Cincinnati death-index entry gives 24 December 1913; the Samuel Preston Wise Find a Grave memorial gives 25 December 1913 in Irvine, Estill County, Kentucky. The compiled FamilySearch profile supplies only the year 1913. This one-day discrepancy and the lack of an original birth/death certificate or census identity bridge remain unresolved.

## Reported siblings

FamilySearch's Millie Ann Tipton Wise profile lists these six children of Richard and Millie:

- Elijah Wise (1910–1910)
- Marjorie Wise (1911–1911)
- Samuel Preston Wise (1913–1989)
- Wallace Ray Wise (1918–1987)
- Otha Elmer Wise (1921–2003)
- Warren Calvin Wise (1925–1955)

This makes Elijah, Marjorie, Wallace, Otha and Warren the reported siblings of the candidate Samuel Preston Wise. Find a Grave memorial panels independently link the surviving siblings Wallace, Otha and Warren, but do not show the two infants in the surviving-child list. The sibling group remains conditional on the Samuel P./Samuel Preston identity match.

## Children of Samuel and Virginia

Monica Lynn Wise's original 1976 Hamilton County marriage abstract, as recorded in the current project profile, names Samuel P. Wise and Virginia Greiner as her parents. Virginia's 2006 Gilligan Funeral Homes obituary calls Samuel her late husband and names Monica Kelly as their daughter, establishing the couple and Monica's family link.

Samuel Preston Wise's Find a Grave memorial says that he and Virginia adopted two children, Monica and “Sam Jr.” Virginia's obituary names a son Sam “Skip” Wise. Sam Jr. and Sam “Skip” may be the same child, but no birth/adoption record or other identity bridge was found; retain both source wordings and do not silently merge the names. Monica's birth/adoption status is likewise reported by the memorial; her parents are named on the 1976 marriage abstract.
