Family Relationship Notes

The 19 January 1914 Davidson County, Tennessee, marriage index names the bride as Lillie Irene Gardner and the groom as James McShane. It gives their ages as 20 and 21 (estimated birth years 1894 and 1893). A 14 September 1926 Hamilton County, Ohio, marriage record identifies Lillian McShane, age 31, born 21 January 1895 in Tennessee, daughter of J. M. Gardner and Emma Guthrie; it explicitly records her marital status as divorced when she married Harley Gillispie, age 37, widowed. This establishes that her first marriage had ended by 1926, but not the divorce date or case. The “T.” in her current profile name is not explained by these records; keep the middle initial unresolved rather than equating it with Irene.

The 1930 census index lists Lillian Gillespie in Norwood, Ohio, with three McShane children: Ruth, Dorothy, and James. Their birth records and later Social Security records identify Lillian Gardner as their mother. Dorothy’s 1998 obituary calls her parents “the late James Anthony and Lillian Gardner McShane.” These sources support three children; no additional child is identified in the reviewed sources, although later or out-of-state children are not ruled out.

Lillian’s parents are identified as J. M. Gardner and Emma Guthrie by her 1926 marriage record. A separate 1893 Nashville marriage index for James M. Gardner and Emma Guthrie matches those names, but it is an index-only linkage; her siblings and the identity of a possible 1900 household remain unresolved. Her 1926 record says divorced, not widowed. Her later life, death, and burial after the 1930 census have not been established.

Life Story

Lillian Gardner was born in Tennessee, with a birth date of 21 January 1895 reported in her 1926 Ohio marriage record. A 1914 Tennessee marriage index calls her Lillie Irene Gardner and records her marriage to James McShane; the two records give slightly different implied birth years, which remain unresolved. Their three children known from the 1930 census are Ruth, Dorothy, and James. In 1926 Lillian’s record describes her as divorced when she married Harley Gillispie and names her parents as J. M. Gardner and Emma Guthrie. No siblings, later-life record, death date, or burial has been verified.

Lillian and James divorced, and on 14 September 1926 she married Harley Gillispie, a widower from Kentucky, in Hamilton County, Ohio . In 1930 she was living in Norwood, Ohio, as Lillian Gillespie, heading a household with her three McShane children; no husband was present .
