Family Relationship Notes

The 19 January 1914 Davidson County, Tennessee, marriage record names the bride as Lillie Irene Gardner and the groom as James McShane. A 14 September 1926 Hamilton County, Ohio, marriage record identifies Lillian McShane, age 31, born 21 January 1895 in Tennessee, daughter of J. M. Gardner and Emma Guthrie, marrying Harley Gillispie. The first marriage had ended before the 1926 marriage, but a divorce record has not been located.

The 1930 census lists Lillian Gillespie in Norwood, Ohio, with her three McShane children: Ruth, Dorothy, and James. Their birth records and later Social Security records identify Lillian Gardner as their mother. Dorothy’s 1998 obituary calls her parents James Anthony and Lillian Gardner McShane.

Her middle initial appears as T. in this profile, while the 1914 marriage record calls her Lillie Irene. The initial needs further checking. Her death date, burial, and later life after the 1930 census have not yet been established.

Life Story

Lillian Gardner was born in Tennessee on 21 January 1895, the daughter of James M. Gardner and Emma Guthrie . She married James Anthony McShane in Davidson County, Tennessee, on 19 January 1914 , and they lived in Hopkinsville, Kentucky . The Kentucky birth index names her as the mother of Dorothy Gardner McShane in 1917 and James McShane in 1919, and both children's Social Security records give her maiden name as Gardner . An older daughter, Ruth, was born in Nashville in 1915; her Social Security record names her parents as James McShane and Lillian Gardener . Dorothy's 1998 obituary calls her parents "the late James Anthony and Lillian Gardner McShane" .

Lillian and James divorced, and on 14 September 1926 she married Harley Gillispie, a widower from Kentucky, in Hamilton County, Ohio . In 1930 she was living in Norwood, Ohio, as Lillian Gillespie, heading a household with her three McShane children; no husband was present .
