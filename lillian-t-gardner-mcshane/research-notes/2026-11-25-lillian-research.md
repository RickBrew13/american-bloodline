# Lillian (Gardner) McShane — research update

**Research date:** 25 November 2026

The 1914 Tennessee marriage index names James McShane and Lillie Irene Gardner; ages 21 and 20, with estimated birth years 1893 and 1894. The 1926 Ohio marriage index names Lillian McShane, daughter of J. M. Gardner and Emma Guthrie, and records her status as divorced when she married Harley Gillispie. It gives birth date 21 January 1895, a discrepancy against the 1914 estimated year. The “T.” in the live profile name is not explained by these records.

The indexed 1930 Norwood household lists Lillian Gillespie with Ruth, Dorothy, and James McShane. These are three supported children; no siblings for Lillian were identified. A previously noted 1900 Gardner household lead (James and “Kate,” daughter Lillian reportedly born Feb 1896) is not tied to this Lillian and conflicts with the 1926 parent/birth information, so remains a lead only. No post-1930 life record or verified portrait was found. James’s profile now uses a Find a Grave contributor-added headstone image as a grave image—not a portrait. No Drive files, folders, or Google Docs were changed.
