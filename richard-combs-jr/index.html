Life Story

Richard Combs Jr. is identified as Eliza Combs’s father by the Find a Grave memorial for Eliza, which gives the parent note “D/O Richard & Rosie Combs” and links Richard as her father. The compiled Breathitt lineage names him as Richard Craig Combs Jr. and identifies Rosa Deaton as his wife. Lacie Combs Tucker’s obituary independently names her parents as Richard and Rose Combs and calls Eliza Asher her sister. Those sources support the family connection, but the original census and vital records have not yet been examined.

Find a Grave gives Richard’s dates as 1879–1936 and burial at Crockettsville Cemetery. These are memorial claims, not yet checked against certificates or cemetery records.
