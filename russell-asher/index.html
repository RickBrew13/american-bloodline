Life Story

The records reviewed so far place Russell W. Asher between Leslie County, Kentucky, and Hamilton, Butler County, Ohio. Find a Grave identifies him as born in Leslie County in 1899 and dying in Hamilton on 9 February 1966. The 1950 census index independently places a Russell Asher, born about 1900 in Kentucky, in Hamilton with Eliza and children Charles A., Wanda, and Marie. Wanda Back’s obituary later names Russell W. and Eliza (Combs) Asher as her parents and names Charles as her brother and Marie as her sister. Those details strongly support that the memorial and the 1950 household concern the same Russell.

Rick identifies Letcher Asher and Elizone Asher as Russell’s parents, matching the “S/O Letcher & Elizone Asher” note on Russell’s Find a Grave memorial. The live family assignment follows Rick’s confirmation. The separate Ancestry entry for a Russell born in 1893 with parents Nathanel Asher and Sarah E. Lester remains a possible namesake and is not assigned to this Russell. His birth year varies between the family marker (1898), Find a Grave (1899), and the 1950 index estimate (about 1900).

Find a Grave lists marriages to Orlena Combs in 1917 and Eliza Combs in 1942, plus children with both women. Russell’s own memorial links Clarence, Nettie, Edward, and Wanda; Wanda’s obituary independently names Russell and Eliza as her parents and identifies Charles and Marie as Wanda’s siblings. A separate Letcher–Agatha public-tree group offers possible paternal siblings, but Agatha is not yet reconciled with Elizone and Dillion’s 1898 birth may overlap Russell’s identity. These sibling links therefore remain qualified. The marker photo used as the profile image is a family marker naming “Father Russell 1898–1966,” “Mother Elva 1904–1921,” and “Widdy Jean 1923–1966”; its relationship to Russell’s documented Eliza household is not established.
