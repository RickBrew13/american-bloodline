Life Story

A 1950 census index places Russell Asher in Hamilton County, Ohio, in the same household as Eliza Asher and children Charles A., Wanda, and Marie. A compiled Breathitt County genealogy also identifies Charles, Wanda, and Marie as children of Russell and Eliza. These sources support the family relationship, but no original census image or direct linking record has yet been inspected.
