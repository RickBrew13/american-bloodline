Life Story

A 1950 census index places Russell Asher in Hamilton County, Ohio, with Eliza and the children Charles A., Wanda, and Marie. A compiled Breathitt County genealogy also lists those three children with Russell and Eliza. The census image has not been inspected, and the identity bridge from Charles A. to the later Charles connected to Aileene remains open.

Ancestry’s public Russell Asher page reports a September 1893 Kentucky birth and names Nathanel Asher and Sarah E. Lester as parents. A FamilySearch public-tree page for Tipton Monroe Asher likewise lists a Russell, born 1893, as Tipton’s brother, and names Hattie, William Franklin, and John Douglas as siblings. These are leads, not original-record proof. The project’s separate 1950 census-index birth estimate for Russell is about 1900; no record yet reconciles the difference or demonstrates that the 1893 tree person is the Ohio householder.
