# Rev. James William McGregor — parentage, family group, and image review

**Research date:** 25 November 2026

## Working conclusion

**Parents:** William Clarence McGregor (1883–1908) and Cora Johnson / Cora B. McGregor (1880–1959), **about 80% working confidence, not documented by a direct birth record**. The strongest evidence located is the detailed family group in Ora Butler Locker’s 1962 *McGregor Genealogy* (printed p. 48 / scan image 57), which names William and Cora as James’s parents and lists James with spouse Mary Elizabeth Hooper and sons. Rick’s saved account of the 2021 Geni work independently records the same couple. Geni and Ancestry are user-contributed trees, and the printed genealogy is compiled, not an original vital record.

## Records and facts checked

- 1950 U.S. Census: James W. McGregor, 42, Kentucky-born, minister/church, head in Boone County on 10 Apr 1950; wife Mary E., 40, Tennessee-born; sons William C. (14), James T. (12), and Wayne M. (10). FamilySearch record 6F7X-XF3C, image 27, ED 8-3, page 72, lines 14–17. Supports the household, occupation, and estimated birth years, but does not identify James’s parents.
- Locker, 1962, printed p. 48: William Clarence McGregor, born 12 Mar 1883 and died 2 May 1908 in Benton; married Cora Johnson on 2 Feb 1907; Cora born 16 Aug 1880 and died 13 Jan 1959; identifies James as their son, born 22 Jan 1908; reports James married Mary Elizabeth Hooper on 21 Feb 1935; lists their sons William Clarence, James Thomas, and Wayne Martin. It lists Roy Logan McGregor (born 26 May 1885) as William Clarence’s brother, hence James’s paternal uncle.
- Geni search index surfaced public profiles for William Clarence McGregor (1883–1908; profile ID 6000000152632346847) and Cora B. McGregor (1880–1959; profile ID 6000000150805755849). Their direct Geni profile pages are currently blocked by Incapsula. A saved Geni read from Rick’s account identifies William Clarence and Cora B. as James’s parents.
- Bill’s 2 Jun 2021 obituary names James W. and Marye (Hooper) McGregor as his parents and identifies brothers James Thomas and Wayne Martin.
- Tom’s Ohio death certificate, State File 2022013320, names James McGregor and Marye Hooper as his parents. Tom’s birth year conflicts: certificate says 1936; Geni/family-tree sources say 1937. Do not “resolve” that disagreement here.

## Conflicts and exclusions

- Locker gives Cora’s mother as “Augusta Heath Johnson”; a public Geni search result names “Cornelia Augusta Johnson.” This may be one woman described differently, but no record examined here establishes that identity bridge. Keep both wordings attributed.
- Rick’s 10 Oct 2026 Geni readout also called “William McGregor Jr.” a brother of Rev. James. The 1962 family genealogy lists no sibling in James’s household group; Roy Logan is William Clarence’s brother (James’s uncle). The Geni-only William Jr. report remains unreconciled.
- Find a Grave memorials report James’s birth/death dates and Glen Haven Cemetery, but the attached image appears to read a different set of dates (James W. Jan 2 1908–Jun 2 1998; Mary E. Apr 11 1908–Jan 2 1998). That image conflicts with Marye’s 1994 obituary and the family-group dates; it is not treated as a verified grave marker.
- The family-photo archive already contains `rev-james-william-mcgregor-papawl-and-tom.jpg`. The image shows the adult standing man behind seated Tom; its existing filename and family archive identify them as James and Tom. It was reused by URL; no source file moved or duplicated.

## Siblings and children

- The 1962 genealogy lists Roy Logan McGregor (26 May 1885; married Mary L. Thomas) as William Clarence’s younger brother—James’s paternal uncle. It names Roy’s children as Roy L. Jr., Horace, and Billy (born 1916, died 1918).
- Unresolved sibling report: “William McGregor Jr.” in the old Geni readout; no independent bridge or corroborating family-group entry found.
- Children with Marye, supported across Locker, 1950 census, Bill obituary, and Tom’s death certificate: William Clarence “Bill” (1936–2021), James Thomas “Tom” (born 1936 on his certificate vs 1937 in Geni/tree; died 2022), Wayne Martin (born 1939 in Locker; lived in Germany per family account).
