Life Story

Emma Lou Gregory was born in McCreary County, Kentucky, in 1900. Her headstone confirms her name and dates. A clipped obituary says she died at home in Cincinnati on 18 July 1961, had lived most of her life in the area, and belonged to Pleasant Rock Baptist Church in Smithtown; it also records her family and funeral.

FamilySearch’s compiled profile names Benjamin Gregory and Sarah Burk as her parents and Manuel Levi Foster as spouse, with six children. Beulah Rose Foster Anderson’s independent funeral-home obituary names her parents as the late Manuel L. and Emma Lou Gregory Foster, and names Trula Mae Gilreath as her sister. The couple and sibling group are supported, while exact marriage details and each child link still need original-record review.
