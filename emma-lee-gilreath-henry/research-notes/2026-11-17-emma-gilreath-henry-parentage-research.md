# Emma Lee (Gilreath) Henry — parentage lead
**Research date:** 17 November 2026

## Working conclusion
Likely parents: **Glenneth Gilreath and Trula Mae Foster** — evidence grade **SUPPORTED**, not DOCUMENTED from an original parent-child record. The evidence strongly identifies the FamilySearch Emma as Rick's Emma, but the underlying birth/census record explicitly naming both parents still needs direct inspection.

## Identity bridge and supporting evidence
- Rick's live An American Bloodline profile identifies Emma as Emma Lee (née Gilreath) Henry, wife of Elmer David Henry and mother of James David “Jimmy” Henry, born 30 Dec 1956 in Covington, Kentucky. It cites Jimmy's Social Security NUMIDENT, Kentucky birth entry, 1975 marriage license, and 2006 obituary.
- FamilySearch's public compiled profile for Emma Lee Gilreath (ID M2RR-Z4B) identifies her as born 15 Dec 1939 in McCreary County, Kentucky; gives parents Glenneth Gilreath and Trula Mae Foster; and links her spouse Elmer David Henry and son James David Henry (1956–2006). The same profile also lists sons Alan Lee Henry and Steven Manuel Henry. These family-tree assertions are not themselves original-record proof.
- Find a Grave memorial 165421057 is specifically titled Emma Lee Gilreath Henry, gives dates 15 Dec 1939–22 Jun 1998, and names Glenneth Gilreath as her father. Its stated birthplace is Cincinnati, Ohio, conflicting with FamilySearch's McCreary County birthplace. Find a Grave is secondary/compiled evidence; the memorial does not name her mother.
- Find a Grave memorial 24238018 for Glenneth reports his Kentucky birth record (31 Dec 1920, McCreary County) naming his mother Rebecca Worley and lists Emma Lee Gilreath Henry (1939–1998) as his child. The memorial's family listing is compiled; it does not independently prove Glenneth's paternity of Emma.
- Search results also surfaced an older Emma Lee Gilreath Bingham (1909–2004); she is a different person and was excluded.

## Assessment and limits
The matching full name, maiden surname, birth date, Henry spouse, and Jimmy as a child make the FamilySearch Emma a compelling identity match to Rick's Emma. This supports Glenneth Gilreath and Trula Mae Foster as the likely parents. However, no original birth certificate or legibly inspected 1940/1950 census image naming both parents was obtained in this pass. The Cincinnati vs McCreary birthplace discrepancy remains unresolved. Keep parentage SUPPORTED until a contemporary record directly bridges Emma to both parents; do not treat FamilySearch/Find a Grave relationship lists alone as DOCUMENTED.
