# Research note — Sarah “Sallie” Robinson

Reviewed 26 November 2026.

- Sarah is named “Sarah Sallie Robinson” or “Sarah/Sally Robinson” in the derivative Find a Grave biographies of Alonzo Alexander and James Pharoah Hensley.
- Those same memorials and their census transcriptions place a Sallie/Sally Alexander with Lewis Alexander in the 1870/1880/1910 household summaries. No original census image or marriage record was reviewed.
- Exact birth/death dates, burial, parents and sibling set remain unidentified. Census age summaries vary.
- An earlier marriage to Jules Thompson and two Thompson sons are memorial-reported, not established by a reviewed original record.
- No verified portrait, marker, obituary clipping, or other identifiable image was found.
