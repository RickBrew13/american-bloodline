Life Story

Flora L. was born in 1935. The place of her birth has not yet been established — the year comes from the Ohio marriage and divorce indexes, which give her age at each event. Her maiden name has not been found either: every record so far names her only as Flora L., and after her marriage to Homer she is listed as Flora L. Brewster. Her parents and any brothers or sisters are likewise not yet known.

The Ohio marriage index confirms that Flora L. Brewster married Homer William Brewster Jr. in Hamilton County on 12 September 1977. The index records two past marriages for each applicant; the November 1978 divorce index numbers the marriage as Homer's third. This establishes the 1977 marriage but does not identify Flora's two earlier marriages or their spouses.

A separate 18 March 1977 Ohio divorce index entry names Homer W. Brewster and Flora L. and reports a seven-year marriage, marriage number 2, and no minor children. If this is the same couple, it could indicate an earlier marriage around 1970 followed by a 1977 remarriage. However, that count conflicts with the 1977 marriage index's two past marriages and the 1978 divorce's marriage number 3. The original March 1977 divorce abstract has not been examined, so the entry may be a same-name couple or an indexing error. It is not presented as a proven divorce or a proven 1970 marriage.

A 1979 Hamilton County deed index supplies a possible fuller name: “Flora Lorene Brewster.” It lists her as the grantor in a transfer to David K. Hood and Cynthia A. on 8 January 1979 (Deed Book 4144, page 1074). This is a promising identity lead, not yet proof that the deed's Flora is the woman in the 1977 marriage index.

Two earlier land-index entries connect Flora L. Brewster and Homer W. Brewster before their 1977 indexed marriage: a joint acquisition from Wilbur C. and Ruth Rapp on 13 August 1975 (Deed Book 4025, page 55), followed by a transfer from Flora L. Brewster to Homer W. Brewster on 8 January 1976 (Deed Book 4040, page 738). These transactions establish a property connection but do not independently prove that they were married at the time. They make the unresolved seven-year marriage/divorce index entry especially worth investigating. The original deeds have not been inspected.

No independent record yet establishes Flora's parents, siblings, children, earlier spouses, death, or burial. The 1977 marriage index reports two prior marriages for her, but neither former spouse has been identified. No photograph has been verified. The original 1977 marriage application and the original deeds are the priority records for the next research stage.
