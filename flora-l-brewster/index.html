Life Story

Flora L. was born in 1935. The place of her birth has not yet been established — the year comes from the Ohio marriage and divorce indexes, which give her age at each event. Her maiden name has not been found either: every record so far names her only as Flora L., and after her marriage to Homer she is listed as Flora L. Brewster. Her parents and any brothers or sisters are likewise not yet known.

She married Homer William Brewster Jr. about 1970. The date and place of that first wedding have not been found — it is not in the Ohio marriage index, so it may have taken place outside Ohio. Homer had divorced his first wife, Judith Mae Palmer, in 1968. The marriage and both divorces are recorded in Hamilton County, Ohio. The marriage lasted about seven years: Homer and Flora divorced on 18 March 1977, and the divorce record lists no minor children. They married again six months later, on 12 September 1977, and divorced a second time on 27 November 1978. The 1977 marriage record counts two past marriages for Flora, which means she had been married once before Homer; that husband's name has not been found.

Nothing is known of Flora's later life. No death record for her has been found, and no burial place is recorded. She was born in 1935, so she may still be living — nothing here assumes otherwise. No photograph of her has been found yet, and no profile for her has been verified on any family-tree site. The one record that would likely settle her maiden name and her parents is the 1977 marriage license itself, held by the Hamilton County Probate Court (Marriage Licenses, volume 13, page 1432); it is not among the records FamilySearch holds.
