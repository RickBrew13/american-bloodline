# Flora L. Brewster — fuller-name and land-record research

**Research date:** 25 November 2026  
**Person:** Flora L. Brewster, indexed as Homer William Brewster Jr.'s spouse in Hamilton County, Ohio, on 12 September 1977.

## New finding: possible fuller name

The Hamilton County Recorder's 1979 grantor/grantee index records **“Brewster Flora Lorene”** as grantor in a deed to **David K. Hood & Cynthia A.**, Deed Book **4144**, page **1074**. The row is on page 2 of the index (PDF page 1) and is in the January 8 recording group. The date is **8 January 1979**, not 12 January. The original deed has not been inspected.

This is a strong candidate for the same Flora L. Brewster in the 1977 marriage index because the surname, first name, middle initial/name, and Hamilton County context align. Still, the index alone does not prove that the deed's Flora Lorene is the woman who married Homer. Keep “Lorene” as a likely but unconfirmed full-name expansion until the deed or another record connects them.

## Earlier joint property transaction with Homer

Two earlier Hamilton County deed-index entries show a property connection between Flora L. Brewster and Homer W. Brewster before the indexed 1977 marriage:

1. **13 August 1975**, Deed Book **4025**, page **55**: the grantor index names **Wilbur C. Rapp & Ruth** as grantors and **Homer W. Brewster & Flora L.** as grantees. This records a joint acquisition by the two named buyers. It does not by itself prove marriage or the nature of their relationship.
   - Official index: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1975trter.pdf
2. **5 March 1976**, Deed Book **4040**, page **738**: the FROM/TO index names **Homer W. Brewster** as grantor and **Flora L. Brewster** as grantee. The index does not state the legal nature of the instrument or why the transfer was made.
   - Official index: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/deed_indexes/di1976trteb.pdf

These transactions establish a property relationship before the September 1977 indexed marriage. Together, the 1975 joint acquisition, the 5 March 1976 transfer from Homer to Flora, and the 8 January 1979 transfer by Flora Lorene may form a chain of title. If the legal descriptions match, that would strongly support identifying the 1979 Flora Lorene as the same Flora L.; the original deeds have not yet been compared. The sequence also makes the separate Ohio divorce-index lead dated 18 March 1977 (Homer W. Brewster and Flora L.; reported seven-year marriage) more important to investigate. It does not resolve the conflict in the marriage-order fields or prove that the March divorce belongs to this Homer and Flora.

## 1977–1978 marriage/divorce context

- The Ohio marriage index records Homer W. Brewster Jr. and Flora L. Brewster marrying in Hamilton County on 12 September 1977 (certificate 066628, volume 09140). It reports two prior marriages for each applicant.
- The Ohio divorce index records their divorce on 27 November 1978 after one year; Homer's marriage count is indexed as three.
- A separate 18 March 1977 divorce-index entry names Homer W. Brewster and Flora L., reports seven years of marriage, no minor children, and marriage number 2. If this is the same couple, it may indicate an earlier marriage around 1970 and a 1977 remarriage. However, the “marriage number” conflicts with the later 1977/1978 counts and Homer's indexed 1968 divorce from Judith. Do not attach this March 1977 entry to this couple without the original divorce abstract/case file.

## Additional route for the 1977 marriage application

FamilySearch's statewide **Ohio, County Marriages, 1789–2016** collection includes licenses, certificates, declarations, affidavits, loose documents, and abstracts. Its description says individual images may include the applicants' ages, residences, birthplaces, occupations, parents (including the mother's maiden name), and witnesses. This makes it a potentially valuable route to the original 12 September 1977 application, not just the index.

- Collection: https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/1614804
- Search target: Flora L. Brewster and Homer William Brewster Jr., Hamilton County, Ohio, 1977.
- Known index locator: Hamilton County Marriage Licenses, volume 13, page 1432; FamilySearch index image 123 of 207.
- Hamilton County Recorder's FamilySearch instructions: https://recordersoffice.hamilton-co.org/helpful_information/family_search.html
- Hamilton County Genealogical Society marriage-record guide: https://www.hcgsohio.org/cpage.php?pt=60

Hamilton County's Probate Court public Marriage License Search begins 1 January 1984, while its online Marriage Record Archive Search covers 1808–1973 (with a limited portion of 1973 licenses). Thus the standard Probate Court web search does not directly expose the 1977 application. The Court's own filing in a 2018 Ohio Supreme Court case states that the completed original marriage license remains on file with Probate Court. The license can therefore be requested from the Court even if no FamilySearch image is available. The collection's image browser was opened to the Hamilton County waypoint, but the page returned a FamilySearch “Something went wrong” error in this research session. I did not retrieve or inspect the 1977 license image, and I am not claiming that this exact volume/page is present in the collection. A free FamilySearch account is required for many image views. The original completed license remains on file with the Hamilton County Probate Court; a copy can be requested from the court if the FamilySearch image is unavailable.
