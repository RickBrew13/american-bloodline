# Flora L. Brewster — record retrieval research

**Research date:** 25 November 2026  
**Scope:** Flora L. Brewster, the woman indexed as marrying Homer William Brewster Jr. in Hamilton County, Ohio, on 12 September 1977. This note is Flora-specific; Homer's broader life history remains in his own archive.

## Established starting point

- The Ohio marriage index identifies Homer W. Brewster Jr., age 38, and Flora L. Brewster, age 41, marrying in Hamilton County on 12 September 1977 (certificate 066628, volume 09140). The index reports two prior marriages for each applicant. This implies Flora had two prior marriages before the 1977 marriage if the index field is correctly transcribed; it does not identify either former spouse.
- A FamilySearch Hamilton County Marriage Record Index image (1974–1982, image 123 of 207; Image Group 100698656) lists both applicants and points to Hamilton County Marriage Licenses, volume 13, page 1432. This is an index reference, not the application or license itself.
- The Ohio divorce index records the 27 November 1978 divorce and labels Homer's marriage as his third. The 18 March 1977 divorce-index entry naming Homer W. Brewster and Flora L. (seven-year duration; marriage number 2) is not assigned to this Flora because it conflicts with the later marriage/divorce sequence and the original abstract has not been examined.
- No independent record found in this research pass establishes Flora's maiden name, parents, birthplace, prior spouses, children, death, or burial. Do not infer these details from the 1935 birth year alone.

## Official retrieval route

Hamilton County Probate Court's Court Records Search page says its Marriage License Search is an alphabetical index of brides and grooms who applied for a license in Hamilton County from 1 January 1974 onward, and that detail views may provide additional record data. It separately identifies the Archive Search as access to historical bound volumes. The court's Marriage Record Archive Search form covers 1808–1973, so it is not itself the obvious interface for the 1977 license.

- Court Records Search and Marriage License Search: https://www.probatect.org/court-records/court-record-search
- Hamilton County Probate Court archive search form (marriage records 1808–1973): https://www.probatect.org/ArchiveSearchForms/bukmarriages.aspx
- FamilySearch index image with the volume/page reference: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-89L2-QWZT
- Ohio History Connection guidance: county probate courts maintain marriage records; records may include residence, birth, age, and sometimes parents. The guidance also cautions that failure to find a name in an index does not prove the record is absent: https://ohiohistory.libguides.com/vital/marriagerecords

## Specific record to obtain

Request the complete 12 September 1977 marriage application/license and return for Homer W. Brewster Jr. and Flora L. Brewster, Hamilton County, certificate 066628, volume 09140; FamilySearch index reference: Marriage Licenses, volume 13, page 1432.

Ask for all pages and attachments, including any application, prior-marriage declarations, divorce information, affidavits, license, officiant's return, and amendments. Specifically seek Flora's full legal name at application, maiden name, age/date and place of birth, residence, parents, and the names/dates/places of her two prior marriages. Also ask whether the court can provide a non-certified genealogical copy and what fee and request method apply.

No request has been submitted and no original application has been viewed. Do not write Flora's maiden name, parents, or prior spouses into her public profile until a record supports them.
