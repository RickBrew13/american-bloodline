# Homer Brewster–Flora L. shared record reference

This record is kept in Homer William Brewster Jr.'s archive as the canonical copy: [Homer's Ohio marriage/divorce index extract](https://github.com/RickBrew13/american-bloodline/blob/main/homer-william-brewster-jr/sources/Homer-Ohio-marriage-divorce-index.pdf).

The extract transcribes Ohio Department of Health index entries: Homer W. Brewster and Flora L. (born 1935) divorced in Hamilton County on 18 March 1977 after seven years of marriage; they remarried on 12 September 1977; and divorced again on 27 November 1978 after one year. The 1977 marriage entry names Flora as “Flora L. Brewster,” age 41, with two prior marriages. This supports the 1977 events and approximate first-marriage timing, but does not establish Flora's maiden name, parents, or the exact place/date of her first marriage. The underlying license has not been read. The Drive subfolder “Flora L. Brewster” under the Homer–Judith couple folder was empty when inspected.
