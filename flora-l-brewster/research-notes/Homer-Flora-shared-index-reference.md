# Homer Brewster–Flora L. shared record reference

This record is kept in Homer William Brewster Jr.'s archive as the canonical copy: [Homer's Ohio marriage/divorce index extract](https://github.com/RickBrew13/american-bloodline/blob/main/homer-william-brewster-jr/sources/Homer-Ohio-marriage-divorce-index.pdf).

The Ohio Department of Health index confirms a Hamilton County marriage for Homer W. Brewster Jr. and Flora L. Brewster on **12 September 1977** and a divorce on **27 November 1978** after one year; the 1978 divorce index numbers the marriage as Homer's third. The 1977 marriage index reports **two prior marriages for each applicant**, which is consistent with the 1977 marriage being Homer's third and Flora's third, if the index field is correctly transcribed.

A separate **18 March 1977** divorce-index entry names Homer W. Brewster and Flora L. (born 1935), gives a seven-year marriage, and records marriage number 2 for each. This conflicts with the 1968 Homer–Judith marriage count, the September 1977 count of two prior marriages, and the November 1978 marriage number 3 if all entries refer to the same couple. **Do not attach the March 1977 divorce to this Flora or infer a 1970 marriage until the original abstract is inspected.** It may be a same-name couple or an index/transcription error.

The FamilySearch Hamilton County Marriage Record Index (1974–1982, image 123 of 207) gives the underlying 12 September 1977 license reference as **Marriage Licenses, volume 13, page 1432**. The original application/license, return, and any marriage-history attachment have not been read; request them from the Probate Court. The application may identify Flora's maiden name and previous spouses. The Drive subfolder “Flora L. Brewster” under the Homer–Judith couple folder was empty when inspected.
