# Ben Pough — dig notes, 25 November 2026

Ben Pough, born November 1848 in South Carolina, is the father of Winnie Pough (Pew), named on Winnie's 1930 Alabama death record (FamilySearch ARK 1:1:JDGC-3RQ), which also names her mother Louiser Pough.

- 1900 census, Precinct 2 Enon, Bullock County, ED 27, sheet 13B, household 259 (FamilySearch ARK 1:1:M9ZR-3RG): Ben Pough, head, 52, born November 1848 South Carolina, married 20 years, father born Virginia, mother born Virginia, farmer; wife Louisa J Pough, 48, born Georgia; daughter Minnie Pough, 36, single, mother of 9 children, 6 living; Willie Pough, 17, listed as Son of Ben; grandchildren James 15, Esau 10, Mary 6, Martha 3, Boston 2.
- Ben and Louisa are recorded as married 20 years in 1900 (about 1880), yet Winnie was born in 1864, so Louisa may be a stepmother or the marriage year is wrong. The tree lists a conflicting second mother for Winnie, Martha Ann Harvey — user-submitted tree data, not a record.
- Son Edmond Pough is confirmed by Edmond's death record, which names his father Ben Pough and his mother Louisa James.
- The Willie tangle: a Willie Pough, 17, is listed as Ben's son in the 1900 census; Winnie's son Willie has birth years drifting across records (1882–1887). It is not settled whether the 1900 Willie is Ben's son or Winnie's son.
