Family Relationship Notes

Ben Pough is named only as the father of Winnie Pough on her 1930 Alabama death record, which also names her mother Louiser Pough, and as head of household in the 1900 census at Precinct 2 Enon, Bullock County, with his wife Louisa and his daughter Minnie (Winnie), 36, single, living with him.

Ben was born in South Carolina in November 1848, according to the 1900 census, which also gives his father’s birthplace as Virginia and his mother’s as Virginia. He was a farmer.

Ben and Louisa are recorded as married 20 years in 1900, which would put the marriage about 1880 — yet Winnie was born in 1864, so Louisa may be a stepmother, or the marriage year is wrong. The tree also lists a conflicting second mother for Winnie, Martha Ann Harvey; that is user-submitted tree data, not a record.

His son Edmond Pough is confirmed by Edmond’s death record, which names his father Ben Pough and his mother Louisa James. A Willie Pough, 17, is listed as Ben’s son in the 1900 census; Winnie’s son Willie has birth years drifting across records (1882–1887), so it is not settled whether the 1900 Willie is Ben’s son or Winnie’s son.

Winnie’s children James, Esau, Mary, Martha, and Boston are listed as Ben’s grandchildren in the 1900 census.

Life Story

Ben Pough was born in South Carolina in November 1848 and farmed in Bullock County, Alabama. His daughter Winnie was born in Bullock County in July 1864. By 1900 he was heading a household at Enon with his wife Louisa and Winnie, then single with young children, living under his roof. His death record has not been found.
