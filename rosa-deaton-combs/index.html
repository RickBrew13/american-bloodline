Life Story

Rosa Deaton Combs appears in the compiled Breathitt County family and cemetery records as the daughter of Thomas Deaton and Eliza Little. The same compilation lists a birth date of 5 November 1883 and death date of 28 May 1918, based on Breathitt County death-certificate records; the certificate itself has not been examined.

Her relationship to Richard Combs Jr. is supported by the compiled lineage and their children’s records. Eliza’s Find a Grave memorial calls her mother “Rosie,” while Lacie Combs Tucker’s obituary identifies Lacie as a daughter of Richard and “Rose” Combs and names Eliza Asher as her sister. This corroborates the family grouping but does not replace an original marriage or birth record.
