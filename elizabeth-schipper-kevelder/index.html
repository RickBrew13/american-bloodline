Life Story

Elizabeth Schipper was born on 28 September 1885 in Dinsmore Township, Shelby County, Ohio, the daughter of Barney and Theresa (Steinke) Schipper DOCUMENTED. She married Anthony Kevelder of St. Henry about 1910 SUPPORTED, and they had five children DOCUMENTED.

Anthony died in November 1918, leaving her a widow at 33 with five small children DOCUMENTED. She never remarried SUPPORTED. By 1930 she was living at Botkins with her children and her widowed mother, and by 1936 she had moved the family to Franklinton in Columbus, where her daughter Ruth married Woodrow Dague DOCUMENTED. She died in Columbus on 29 July 1961 and is buried at St. Joseph Cemetery, Lockbourne SUPPORTED.
