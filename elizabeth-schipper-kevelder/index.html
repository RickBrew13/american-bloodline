Family Relationship Notes

  • Elizabeth’s 1885 birth record names Barney Schipper and Theresa Steinke as her parents. Census records also place her with their household and identify her siblings.
  • Elizabeth married Anthony Kevelder about 1910–1911. Both were single in the 1910 census; no marriage record has been located.
  • The five children listed are the children carried in the family research and supported by later records. An infant, Oliver Frederick Kevelder (7 January–27 February 1911), is a possible additional child, but that connection remains unproved and he is not added to the roster.
  • After Anthony’s death in 1918, Elizabeth appears as a widow with her children in later census records. No later spouse is identified in the current research.

Life Story

Elizabeth Schipper was born on 28 September 1885 in Dinsmore Township, Shelby County, Ohio, the daughter of Barney and Theresa (Steinke) Schipper . She married Anthony Kevelder of St. Henry about 1910 , and they had five children .

Anthony died in November 1918, leaving her a widow at 33 with five small children . She never remarried . By 1930 she was living at Botkins with her children and her widowed mother, and by 1936 she had moved the family to Franklinton in Columbus, where her daughter Ruth married Woodrow Dague . She died in Columbus on 29 July 1961 and is buried at St. Joseph Cemetery, Lockbourne .
