Life Story

William Helton was born in Kentucky on 25 February 1817 SUPPORTED. In May 1839 he married Elizabeth Engle in Knox County, with her father Jacob Engle as bondsman DOCUMENTED, and they raised at least eleven children on Indian Creek DOCUMENTED.

William died on 20 December 1863, in the middle of the Civil War, though no record shows that he served SUPPORTED. He is buried in the family cemetery on Indian Creek that now carries his son Asberry's name SUPPORTED.
