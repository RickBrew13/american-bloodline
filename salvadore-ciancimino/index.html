Life Story

Salvadore Ciancimino was born 30 December 1842 in Sciacca, Agrigento, Sicily, son of Salvadore Ciancimino and Ninfa Noto. He worked as a day laborer and married Antonina Marotta. His son Salvatore, born about 1881, married Rosa Graffeo in Manhattan on 15 September 1907; the marriage record names Salvadore and Antonina as his parents. Salvadore died 21 January 1892 in Milan, in the Regia Casa institution, aged 49, married with offspring. The compiled tree had given his death as 1905; the Milan death act, read from the image, gives 21 January 1892. The family tree names nine other children; no records were found for them in this pass.
