Research Notes and Open Questions

  • FamilySearch reports nine children; the Find a Grave memorial lists eight and omits Emily. Keep Emily as a tree-only candidate pending original/census corroboration.
  • Variant spellings and dates include Carman/Carmon, Serena/Cyrena, and Dallas birth year 1923/1924; do not collapse or “correct” without primary records.
  • Sibling names/dates are compiled; Find a Grave and FamilySearch differ on Quintella and several exact name forms.
  • 28 November 2026 review: parent entries remain Daniel W. Carman and Sarah Jane Bastin Carman (listed as Sarah Jane Bastin in compiled source profiles); no parent names, dates, generation placements, or parent links were edited. FamilySearch compiled profiles KJZ3-7VS and KGCQ-3BL independently show this couple as Roscoe’s parents; the saved 1920 Lincoln County marriage-register transcript for daughter Lillie names Roscoe Carmen and Lucy Carmen as her parents.
  • FamilySearch compiled profiles report Roscoe’s marriage to Lucy Jane Price on 23 December 1902 and nine children. The Find a Grave memorial’s family list has eight and omits Emily (1920–deceased), who remains a FamilySearch-only candidate. Keep Emily distinct and unresolved until supported by a census or original record.
  • FamilySearch’s Roscoe page labels seven siblings, but Daniel and Sarah’s family page reports seven children total including Roscoe. The named sibling roster in this profile has six other people; the displayed sibling count is internally inconsistent. FamilySearch gives Quintella 1871–1942 while Find a Grave-derived family data give 1872–1945. Keep the existing variants visible.
  • The 1900 and 1910 census schedule images and Sarah Jane Carman’s 1921 Kentucky death-certificate image were inspected in this update. No original Roscoe birth record or original 1902 marriage record was reviewed. The Find a Grave death-certificate image is a reproduced/posted copy, not a certified copy obtained directly from Kentucky.
  • Additional independent compiled support: Austins of America (August 1985, p. 167) calls Ruth Ellen Carman the daughter of Daniel and “Sarah Jane (Bastin) Carman.” It is secondary genealogy, not a primary record and not direct proof of Roscoe’s parentage; a clipping is saved in this profile folder.
  • Name resolution (28 November 2026): high confidence (about 90%) that Roscoe’s mother was Sarah Jane Bastin Carman. Her own 1921 Kentucky death certificate records “Sarah Jane Carman” and names William Bastin and Sarah Ann Salyers as her parents; the 1910 original schedule records “Sarah J. Carman,” widowed. A 1900 census sheet records a Sarah J. Carmon as wife of Daniel N. Carmon, but Daniel’s age/initial differ from the compiled Daniel W. entry, so that record is supportive with an identity caveat, not a standalone bridge. Roscoe’s 1939 death-certificate reproduction instead says “Mary Jane Bastin,” informant Roy Carman. Treat that as most likely an informant/recording error, not a second wife or established alias; retain the certificate’s exact wording.
