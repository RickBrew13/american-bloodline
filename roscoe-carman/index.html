Life Story

Roscoe Carman married Lucy Jane Price in 1902, according to the FamilySearch compiled profile. Find a Grave and FamilySearch both name their daughter Lillie and list a large Carman sibling group. The record-level parentage comes from compiled profiles and memorial family sections; no original birth act was inspected.
