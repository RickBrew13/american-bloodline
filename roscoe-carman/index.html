Research Notes and Open Questions

  • FamilySearch reports nine children; the Find a Grave memorial lists eight and omits Emily. Keep Emily as a tree-only candidate pending original/census corroboration.
  • Variant spellings and dates include Carman/Carmon, Serena/Cyrena, and Dallas birth year 1923/1924; do not collapse or “correct” without primary records.
  • Sibling names/dates are compiled; Find a Grave and FamilySearch differ on Quintella and several exact name forms.
  • 28 November 2026 review: parent entries remain Daniel W. Carman and Sarah Jane Bastin; no parent names, dates, generation placements, or parent links were edited. FamilySearch compiled profiles KJZ3-7VS and KGCQ-3BL independently show this couple as Roscoe’s parents; the saved 1920 Lincoln County marriage-register transcript for daughter Lillie names Roscoe Carmen and Lucy Carmen as her parents.
  • FamilySearch compiled profiles report Roscoe’s marriage to Lucy Jane Price on 23 December 1902 and nine children. The Find a Grave memorial’s family list has eight and omits Emily (1920–deceased), who remains a FamilySearch-only candidate. Keep Emily distinct and unresolved until supported by a census or original record.
  • FamilySearch’s Roscoe page labels seven siblings, but Daniel and Sarah’s family page reports seven children total including Roscoe. The named sibling roster in this profile has six other people; the displayed sibling count is internally inconsistent. FamilySearch gives Quintella 1871–1942 while Find a Grave-derived family data give 1872–1945. Keep the existing variants visible.
  • No original birth record, original census image, or original 1902 marriage record was inspected for this update. The 1920 daughter-marriage entry is a saved transcript/index, and the FamilySearch profiles and Find a Grave memorial remain compiled/derivative sources. The Find a Grave gallery has three listed images, including one described by its uploader as a death certificate; its actual image was not visually inspected and is not treated as reviewed evidence.
  • The memorial’s reproduced Kentucky death certificate names Roscoe’s father Daniel Carman and mother “Mary Jane Bastin” (both Casey County); informant Roy Carman. FamilySearch and memorial family groups name Sarah Jane Bastin. Do not assume Mary Jane and Sarah Jane are the same person; retain the discrepancy pending an identity bridge. The certificate reports death 7 July 1939, while other compiled sources give 8 July.
