# Source register — Roscoe Carman

Date reviewed: 28 November 2026
- [FamilySearch compiled profile KJZ3-7VS](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/KJZ3-7VS/roscoe-carman-1882-1939) — Reports Roscoe born 18 July 1882 in Casey County, died 8 July 1939 in Kings Mountain, married Lucy Jane Price 23 December 1902, parents Daniel W. Carman and Sarah Jane Bastin, and nine children. Its cited sources include census and Kentucky county-marriage records; not all underlying originals were reviewed.
- [Find a Grave memorial 17419340, Roscoe Carman](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/17419340/roscoe-carman) — User-contributed memorial gives dates and Salyers Cemetery; family group lists Daniel W. Carman and Sarah Jane Bastin and eight children (Emily omitted).
- [Find a Grave photo gallery for Roscoe memorial 17419340](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/17419340/roscoe-carman/photo) — Three images visually reviewed: unidentified man/woman photo; marker; and a reproduced Kentucky death certificate credited to Rochelle Riordan (24 May 2014). Certificate gives mother “Mary Jane Bastin,” informant Roy Carman. This conflicts with Sarah’s own death certificate and census name forms; Sarah Jane Bastin Carman is the best-supported identity, and Mary Jane is most likely an informant/recording error. Preserve the literal reading.
- [FamilySearch Daniel W. Carman profile (29SH-F11)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/29SH-F11/daniel-w-carman-1836-1905) — Compiled profile’s spouse-and-children section names Sarah Jane Bastin and Roscoe among the children; its narrative also reports a separate Mary Ashley marriage, unresolved.
- [FamilySearch Sarah Jane Bastin profile (KGCQ-3BL)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/KGCQ-3BL/sarah-jane-bastin-1847-1921) — Compiled profile identifies Sarah Jane as Daniel’s spouse and parent in the family of seven children including Roscoe; gives birth 5 August 1847 and death 21 April 1921.
- [FamilySearch, 1900 U.S. census, Daniel N Carmon household (M9WP-NR9)](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:M9WP-NR9) — Original sheet 19B reviewed, showing Daniel N. Carmon and wife Sarah J. Carmon; the indexed extended-family view associates Roscoe as son, but Roscoe is not visible on the scanned household lines 98–100.
- [FamilySearch, 1910 U.S. census, Sarah J Carman (M28L-WV7)](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:M28L-WV7) — Original image 547 reviewed; widowed Sarah J. Carman, age 65, in Walltown, Casey County.
- [FamilySearch, Kentucky Deaths, 1911–1967, Sarah Jane Carman (N9PL-WRK)](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:N9PL-WRK) — Original image 788 / file 7379 reviewed; full name Sarah Jane Carman, parents William Bastin and Sarah Ann Salyers (index spelling Salyer). A full copy is saved in Sarah’s profile folder.
- [FamilySearch 1920 Kentucky marriage index for Lillie May Carmen and William P. Henry (Q2Q6-KKCQ)](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:Q2Q6-KKCQ) — Saved project transcript for Lincoln County, 1 June 1920, record no. 102, names Roscoe Carmen and Lucy Carmen as Lillie’s parents; original register image not inspected.

- Austins of America, August 1985, p. 167, entry J3E6 on Ruth Ellen Carman Horton Austin, identifies Ruth as daughter of Daniel and “Sarah Jane (Bastin) Carman.” Compiled secondary genealogy supports Sarah Jane, but it is not a direct parent record for Roscoe.
