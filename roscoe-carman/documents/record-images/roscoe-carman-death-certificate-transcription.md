# Roscoe Carman — selected death-certificate fields

The image is attached as “Death Certificate” to Find a Grave memorial 17419340. Visible fields read: Roscoe Carman; born 18 July 1882 in Casey County, Kentucky; died 7 July 1939; father Daniel Carman, born Casey County; mother “Mary Jane Bastin,” born Casey County; informant Roy Carman; burial at Salyers Cemetery, 9 July 1939. The certificate gives probable heart failure as cause of death.

## Name conflict and assessment

This is the only located record naming Roscoe’s mother “Mary Jane Bastin.” Sarah’s own 1921 Kentucky death certificate records “Sarah Jane Carman,” names her parents William Bastin and Sarah Ann Salyers, and her 1910 census entry is “Sarah J. Carman.” A 1900 schedule records a “Sarah J. Carmon” as wife of Daniel N. Carmon, although that Daniel’s age/initial differs from the compiled Daniel W. profile. Compiled family sources identify Daniel’s wife and Roscoe’s mother as Sarah Jane Bastin. On balance, Sarah Jane Bastin Carman is the best-supported identity (high confidence, about 90%); “Mary Jane” on this later certificate is most likely a reporting or recording error. Preserve the certificate’s exact reading rather than silently changing it, and do not create a second wife or an alias without a new identity bridge.

## Provenance and limitations

This is a reproduction of a Kentucky death certificate posted by contributor Rochelle Riordan to Find a Grave on 24 May 2014, not a certified copy obtained directly from Kentucky. The informant’s statement is important evidence but is not assumed error-free. The certificate reports death 7 July 1939, while Roscoe’s marker and compiled sources give 8 July 1939.
