# Roscoe Carman — family review and name resolution

Date: 28 November 2026

## Sarah Jane / Mary Jane evidence

The 1939 death-certificate image attached to Find a Grave memorial 17419340 was visually reviewed. It names Roscoe’s mother as “Mary Jane Bastin,” both parents’ birthplaces as Casey County, and informant Roy Carman. This is a reproduced image uploaded by Rochelle Riordan on 24 May 2014, not a certified copy obtained directly from Kentucky.

The name is contradicted by Sarah’s own original 1921 Kentucky death certificate (FamilySearch N9PL-WRK, image 788/file 7379), which records her as “Sarah Jane Carman,” names William Bastin and Sarah Ann Salyers as parents, and by the 1900/1910 census schedules, which identify Daniel’s wife/widowed head as Sarah J. Carmon/Carman. The working conclusion is high confidence (about 90%) that Roscoe’s mother was Sarah Jane Bastin Carman; “Mary Jane” on the 1939 certificate is most likely a reporting/recording error. Preserve the certificate’s exact wording as a conflicting source, but do not create an additional wife or rename the mother Mary Jane absent new evidence.

The FamilySearch 1900 index’s extended-family panel associates Roscoe as son of Daniel and Sarah. The original schedule image retrieved for Daniel’s household is sheet 19B; visible lines 98–100 list Daniel, Sarah J. and Quintella, not Roscoe. It cannot independently establish Roscoe’s presence in that household.
