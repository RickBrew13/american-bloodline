# Roscoe Carman — media and record review

Date: 28 November 2026

## Memorial images reviewed and saved

- A photo attached to Roscoe memorial 17419340 depicts a man and woman, but the memorial provides no sitter identities; it is not credited as a verified portrait of Roscoe or Lucy. Contributor: Janet Cochran, 1 May 2010.
- Roscoe’s Salyers Cemetery marker is legible for his name and dates 18 July 1882–8 July 1939. Contributor: Rochelle Riordan, 6 August 2007.
- The image captioned “Death Certificate” is a Kentucky death certificate reproduction. Visible entries name Daniel Carman and Mary Jane Bastin as Roscoe’s parents, both born Casey County; informant Roy Carman. This conflicts with the compiled profile’s Sarah Jane Bastin. Keep both name forms distinct pending an identity bridge. The certificate gives death 7 July 1939, whereas the marker and compiled profiles report 8 July.

The three images and a transcription of selected visible fields are stored in `documents/record-images/` and the photo folders. The death certificate is a Find a Grave reproduction credited to Rochelle Riordan (24 May 2014), not a certified original obtained directly from Kentucky.
