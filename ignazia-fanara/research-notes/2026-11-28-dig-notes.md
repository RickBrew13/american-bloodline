# Ignazia Fanara — dig notes
Date: 28 November 2026. FamilySearch signed-in pass (RickBrewster1), images read and re-read letter by letter.

## What was found
- Ignazia Fanara is named as Pasquale's mother on his marriage act of 27 February 1886 (Palermo, Brancaccio, Act No. 15; ARK 3:1:3QS7-997B-VWVB): Fanara Ignazia, resident in Palermo, wife of Angelo Carbonaro.
- She is named again in the Vallelunga marriage papers of 26 November 1895 (letter No. 32; ARK 3:1:3Q9M-CS86-SQQ4-M), described as deceased ("della fu Ignazia Fanara"), as is her husband Angelo. Both were dead by that date.
- Her son Pasquale was born 15 February 1855 in Palermo, worked as a miller, married Rosalia Mule 27 February 1886, was widowed 22 October 1893, and remarried Giuseppa Noto 19 December 1895 in Vallelunga Pratameno.

## Not found
- No record of Ignazia herself beyond the two namings above. No birth, marriage, or death record for her has been found. Next: the Palermo marriage records of the 1850s for Angelo Carbonaro and Ignazia Fanara, and the Palermo death records before November 1895.

- Vallelunga Pratameno, Marriage Supplements 1894-1911, letter No. 32, 26 November 1895 (ARK 3:1:3Q9M-CS86-SQQ4-M).
