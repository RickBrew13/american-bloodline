# Francis Menden Barnes — source and family audit (26 Nov 2026)

Existing family memorial evidence identifies Ozias A. Barnes as his parent, lists Alice Young and Josephine Bloyd as spouses, and enumerates a group of children including George. Do not infer which spouse was mother of every child: the memorial links and two marriages require a more complete documentary timeline.

A contemporary obituary clipping reproduced on Francis’s Find a Grave memorial says he died near Denmark, Russell County, about midnight on 1 February 1928; was born on Fall Creek in Wayne County; would have turned 81 that April; served in the Confederate army, as the clipping claims; had been married twice; and was survived by his second wife, five sons, six daughters and four stepsons. It identifies Joe Barnes as a son and calls Francis an auctioneer and stock trader. The clipping does not give a newspaper name/date in the memorial caption. Preserve its claims as attributed, not independently verified.
