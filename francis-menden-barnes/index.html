Family Relationship Notes

Memorial family links identify Ozias A. Barnes as Francis’s parent and list Alice Young and Josephine Bloyd as spouses. The sources consulted do not establish the order of the marriages. Francis’s obituary clipping says he had been married twice, was survived by his second wife, five sons, six daughters and four stepsons; it does not name his second wife or enumerate those children.

Find a Grave’s family group links George Ozais Barnes with Francis and Alice; this is a contributor-entered memorial relationship. George’s own 1973 death notice identifies his sister Bessie Miles but does not name his parents. Keep all such parent links attributed to their source, not treated as a reviewed birth record.
