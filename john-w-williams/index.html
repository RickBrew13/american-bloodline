Life Story

John W. Williams was born in July 1860 in Cincinnati, Ohio, the son of Robert Williams and Margaret née Fogerty. He appears as a child in his father's Cincinnati household in the 1870 census, and in 1880, aged 20, he was already working as a drayman. The city directories trace him at the family home at 108 Butler Street from 1881/82 — laborer, then expressman, then drayman, then driver — until he left the household at his marriage. He worked as a drayman and teamster all his life, hauling in the Cincinnati streets his father had come to from Ireland.

On 29 July 1886, in a Catholic ceremony in Hamilton County, he married Mary Collins, an Ireland-born widow who brought two daughters, Julia and Mary Green, from her first marriage. Together they had five children: Robert Emmett, Margaret, Lenora, John Thomas — the direct line — and Edward. John died in Cincinnati between 1921 and January 1923; his own death certificate has never been found in Ohio or Kentucky, and his burial place is not known — he is not buried with Mary at Spring Grove. Two old errors once clung to him and are both settled here: the 1936 death that used to be attached to him belongs to his younger brother John, born in 1876, and the printer at 108 Butler Street in 1885–1886 was his brother Patrick — John himself was a teamster, not a printer.

No photograph of John himself has been found. The two pictures in the couple folder are contextual views of Lytle Park and Spring Grove Cemetery, not family photographs, so none is shown here.
