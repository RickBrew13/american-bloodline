Life Story

Victoria Moore, remembered in the family as “Grandma,” was Shawn Kevin Moore's mother. Patrick L. Moore's funeral-home obituary names his late wife as Victoria M. “Vicki” (Herman) Moore and names Shawn (Susan) Moore among their children. The matching relationships support identifying this Victoria with Shawn's mother. Her obituary reports that she was born 28 December 1941, died 6 October 2018 at Reid Health in Richmond, Indiana, and was survived by sisters Eleanor and Janet; it names the late Rose as a sister.

The family remembers visits with Victoria and Patrick in Massachusetts. The available notes do not establish when or how long they lived there.
