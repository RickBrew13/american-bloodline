Life Story

Rebecca Farr was born in 1826 in Wayne County, Kentucky, the daughter of William Farr and Elizabeth Holmes. Her parents had moved from North Carolina to Wayne County about 1822, and in 1848 the Farrs moved on to Morgan and Cumberland Counties, Tennessee. Her father grew flax in 1850, the same crop John and Rebecca's farm produced in 1870.

About 1848–49 Rebecca married John W. Brewster, though no marriage record survives. They raised nine children at Brewstertown, and buried six of them young: Phoebe, Sheron, Fairbee, Andrew, George, and Jesse. Only James Thomas, Jasper Newton, and Farsina reached adulthood. No occupation is recorded for Rebecca; the farm's flax and linen work is noted in the research, and no church affiliation is recorded for her.

Rebecca died in Morgan County in 1892 or 1894 — the year is contested, and no death record or located stone settles it. Family tradition says 1892; a modern newspaper cemetery column and Find a Grave give 1894. She is said to be buried in Carpenter Cemetery beside John. After her death John married twice more. No photograph of Rebecca has been found.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Birth year: 1826 by the 1860 and 1880 censuses, against about 1823 in Gough's narrative and an 1870 reading. The page uses 1826.
  • Death year: 1892 by family tradition against 1894 in the 2025 Independent Herald column and Find a Grave. The "1894" is not an 1894 source — the Independent Herald was founded in 1976 — and the column and the filed article are one source, not two.
  • Mother's maiden name: Holmes (the standing working name) against Webb (Find a Grave) against unknown (Gough's Farr page) — all unproved, and no record has been seen. The Farr pension files may name her mother; ordering them is a paid step, noted, not pursued.
  • Siblings: Gough's two pages disagree on Elijah's placement, and "Aley F. Farr" is book-only lore, unverified.
  • Burial: said to be Carpenter Cemetery beside John, but no memorial or transcription has been independently confirmed beyond Find a Grave memorial 28431234's header.
  • No photograph of Rebecca has been found; the hero image is the shared placeholder. Place photographs in the folder (bridges, cemetery entrances, the courthouse) are not shown on this page.
