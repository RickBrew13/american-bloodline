Life Story

Elmer David Henry was the father of James David “Jimmy” Henry, who was born in Covington, Kentucky, on 30 December 1956 to Elmer and Emma Lee (Gilreath) Henry DOCUMENTED. Elmer is named as Jimmy’s father on Jimmy’s Social Security record and on both of Jimmy’s marriage licenses DOCUMENTED. Emma died before 2006, and by then Elmer was married to Joy DOCUMENTED. Jimmy’s 2006 obituary names five other children in the family: Alan, Sandy, Jeff, Deron, and the late Steven DOCUMENTED.

Elmer was born on 6 August 1934 in Lincoln County, Kentucky, the son of William Pollard Henry and Lillie Mae Carman. The 1940 census lists the family in Cincinnati, Ohio, where Elmer, age 5, lived with his parents and seven siblings. He was still living in June 2006, when his son Jimmy died; his own death date has not been found.
