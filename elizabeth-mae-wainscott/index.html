Life Story

Elizabeth Mae Lawrence was born on 15 April 1913 in Nicholas County, Kentucky, a daughter of Robert Russell Lawrence and Gertrude Mers. SUPPORTED

She was very young when she married Ray Wainscott in Campbell County, Kentucky, on 15 October 1927; she gave her age as 19. DOCUMENTED They settled in Cincinnati, where their four daughters were born: Catherine in 1930, Wanda in 1933, Margaret in 1935 and Ruth in 1936. Elizabeth buried two of them, Margaret in 1938 and Wanda in 1942. DOCUMENTED

She died in Cincinnati on 5 April 1958, ten days before her 45th birthday. DOCUMENTED She is buried at Baltimore Pike Cemetery beside Ray, under a stone that reads "Our Darling Mother." SUPPORTED
