Life Story

Elizabeth Mae Lawrence was born on 15 April 1913 in Nicholas County, Kentucky, a daughter of Robert Russell Lawrence and Gertrude Mers.

She was very young when she married Ray Wainscott in Campbell County, Kentucky, on 15 October 1927; she gave her age as 19. They settled in Cincinnati, where their four daughters were born: Catherine in 1930, Wanda in 1933, Margaret in 1935 and Ruth in 1936. Elizabeth buried two of them, Margaret in 1938 and Wanda in 1942.

She died in Cincinnati on 5 April 1958, ten days before her 45th birthday. She is buried at Baltimore Pike Cemetery beside Ray, under a stone that reads "Our Darling Mother."
