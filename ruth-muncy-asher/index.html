Life Timeline

1850 · Rutha Asher, 20, appears with Blevens Asher, 26, and three young Asher children in Clay County; index does not define each relationship.

1860 · Ruth Asher, 28, appears with Bleve Asher and Josiah, Hetta, Nancy, and Dill in Perry County; additional people appear in the household without an indexed relationship.

1870 · Ruthy Asher (also indexed as Kathy), age 40, appears with Blivius Asher and children/young people including Litcher, age 3, in Perry County. This conflicts with the Muncy Family Database’s reported 1870 death year.

1880 · The Blevins Asher household includes a wife indexed as Brittny. Since Ruth is reported deceased by 1870, do not identify Brittny as Ruth without a bridge.

Life Story

The census indexes place Ruth/Rutha Asher in the same Clay/Perry County household as Blevens/Bleve/Blivius Asher in 1850, 1860, and 1870. The 1870 index gives Ruthy but also shows the alternate indexed form Kathy; the same household includes “Litcher” age 3. This supports her place in the family group without identifying every household member’s relationship. The Muncy Family Database reports Ruth born about 1830 and died 1870, and names William Sherman Muncy Sr. and Agnes Bowling as parents; however, the 1870 census index lists her in that year, so the database death year is not reliable as an exact date. It also describes a separate nonmarital relationship between Blevins and Ruth’s sister Mary (Polly) Muncy; her children are not assigned to Ruth.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Ruth’s birth-family assignment (William Sherman Muncy Sr. × Agnes Bowling) is compiled-tree/database evidence, not an original vital record. MuncyFamilyData and FamilySearch public tree agree on the couple; source methods differ and source wording/date conflicts remain.
  • The reported 1870 death conflicts with the 1870 census index that places Ruthy/Kathy Asher in Blevins/Blivius’s household. Treat 1870 as a reported year, not established death date.
  • A Muncy Family Database sibling list includes John, Agnes, William Sherman Jr., Levi, Susan, Mary/Polly, Elizabeth, Keziah, and James. A FamilySearch tree adds/varies names and dates (including Asa); do not present a complete sibling set as proven.
