# Ruth Muncy — family group and sibling audit

Reviewed 26 November 2026.

The Muncy Family Database and FamilySearch public tree attribute Ruth Muncy (about 1830–reported 1870) to William Sherman Muncy Sr. and Agnes Bowling. FamilySearch’s public profile for Agnes reports the pair’s marriage on 11 April 1827 and lists eleven children; Muncy Family Database lists ten. Both are compiled family sources, not original records proving Ruth’s parentage. The Muncy database’s list of Ruth’s siblings includes John (about 1828), Agnes (1829), William Sherman Jr. (1832), Levi (about 1834), Susan (1836), Mary/Polly (1838), Elizabeth (1841, infant), Keziah (1846), and James (1846). FamilySearch adds/varies Asa and dates; treat this as a compiled sibling set, not a definitive complete list.

Ruth appears in the 1850, 1860 and 1870 census indexes in the same Blevens/Bleve/Blivius household. The 1870 index lists her at age 40 (sometimes indexed as Kathy); therefore the database’s “died 1870” should not be treated as an exact fact.

The same Muncy database describes Ruth’s sister Mary (Polly) Muncy as a separate nonmarital partner of Blevins, with a separate group of children. Keep those two family branches distinct.

No verified Ruth portrait or individual marker located; the Blevins-labeled database thumbnail is not presented as Ruth.
