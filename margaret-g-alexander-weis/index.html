Family Relationship Notes

Margaret’s 6 July 1935 Kentucky marriage certificate names the bride as “Margaret Alexandria” and her parents as “Lonzo” and “Frances.” The 1920 census lists Maggie Alexander, age three years and ten months, as the daughter of Lon Alexander and Francis Alexander in Mingo, West Virginia. Her Social Security record and Terri Ann’s records use the name Margaret G. Alexander.

The full names Alonzo Alexander and Frances Armstrong Runion, their dates, and their 1893 marriage come from the FamilySearch tree. The same tree supplies the siblings listed above. These details have not all been independently confirmed in original records.

The 1950 census and Terri Ann’s records support Margaret and Abraham as parents of six children. The 1935 marriage record gives Margaret’s age as 21, while her 1916 birth date and the 1920 census age indicate she was 19; the age on the marriage record may have been misstated. Her birthplace is written “Latham, Va.” on the marriage certificate, while the 1920 census gives West Virginia.

Her reported death date and Spring Grove burial come from the Find a Grave index and FamilySearch tree. No Ohio death-index entry or original burial record for Margaret has been reviewed.

Life Story

Margaret G. Alexander was born on 10 March 1916 in West Virginia, the daughter of Alonzo Alexander and Frances Armstrong Runion. On 6 July 1935 she married Abe Weis in Newport, Campbell County, Kentucky — the certificate spells her surname “Alexandria.” They raised six children — Ronald, Donald, Albert, Jack, Sharon, and Terri Ann — and lived in Cincinnati. Margaret died on 27 June 1983 in Ohio and is buried at Spring Grove Cemetery. The Gen VII Alonzo “Lon” Alexander and Francis E. Runion Alexander Moon profiles now link back to Margaret and preserve the “Lon/Lonzo/Alonzo” and “Francis/Frances” spelling and date discrepancies.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Her surname is “Alexandria” on the 1935 marriage record, but “Alexander” on the FamilySearch tree, the censuses, and Find a Grave.
  • Her age on the marriage record is 21 (which would mean born about 1914), but the tree and Find a Grave give her birth as 10 March 1916 — she would have been 19 in July 1935 — and the 1920 census age of 3 years 10 months supports 1916. She likely overstated her age to reach 21.
  • Her birthplace is written “Latham, Va.” on the marriage certificate (the handwriting is not perfectly clear); the tree and the 1920 census give West Virginia.
  • No Ohio Death Index entry for Margaret herself has been seen — her death date rests on the Find a Grave Index and the FamilySearch tree.
  • Her mother’s full maiden name “Armstrong Runion” and her parents’ marriage (9 October 1893, West Virginia) are tree-only — Alonzo Alexander (1876–1933, FS GX6W-W3K) × Frances Armstrong Runion (1877–1969, FS GX67-TFX).
  • Her siblings are tree-only: Cordia (1895–1962), Isaac Sanford (1898), Fred H. (1900–1967), Pearl (1903–1971), Roy (1905–1972), Bonnie (1906), George Russell (1911–1985), Lon Francis (1918), and Shirley Juanita (1921–2004). No pages have been built for them.
  • The other five children (Ronald, Donald, Albert, Jack, Sharon) come from the 1950 census and the FamilySearch tree; no pages have been built for them. Terri’s obituary names her Weis siblings, but its full text was not reachable (GenealogyBank, paid, not opened).
