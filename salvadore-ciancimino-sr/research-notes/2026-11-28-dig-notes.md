# Salvadore Ciancimino — dig notes
Date: 28 November 2026. FamilySearch signed-in pass (RickBrewster1), images read and re-read letter by letter.

## What was found
- Salvadore Ciancimino is named as the father of Salvadore Ciancimino on his son's baptism index of 30 December 1842, Sciacca (ARK 1:1:XVY2-SYN): father Salvadore Ciancimino, aged 25, mother Ninfa Noto, aged 22. The image is only available at a FamilySearch center or affiliate library.
- He is named again as the father on his son's Milan death act of 1892 (image ARK 3:1:3Q9M-CSQJ-F9BW-3; index ARK 1:1:X32V-5BK1): "figlio di Salvatore e di Noto Ninfa." Read from the image and re-read letter by letter in a second pass.
- His ten children named on the family tree are Vincenzo, Maria Antonia, Salvadore, Antonina, Antonia, Giuseppe, Giuseppa, Alfonzo, Accursio, and Giovanni. Only Salvadore is confirmed by a record.
- His son Salvadore was born 30 December 1842 in Sciacca, worked as a day laborer, married Antonina Marotta, and died 21 January 1892 in Milan. His grandson Salvatore Ciancimino, born about 1881, married Rosa Graffeo 15 September 1907 in Manhattan (index ARK 1:1:24HG-N97).

## Not found / not assigned
- His birth year comes from the family tree (born 1818); his son's baptism index gives his age as 25 on 30 December 1842, which points to about 1817-1818.
- His nine children other than Salvadore are named only on the family tree; no records were found for any of them in this pass.
- His marriage to Ninfa Noto has not been found. The record that would settle it is the Sciacca marriage film 1864-1894.
- No death record for him has been found.
