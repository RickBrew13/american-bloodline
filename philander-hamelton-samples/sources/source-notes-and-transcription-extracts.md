# Philander Hamelton Samples: source notes and transcription limits

State birth index: 7 Mar 1868, Procious, Clay County. 1910/1920 census indexes place Edith as daughter in his household; 1900 household index identifies wife M. I. Samples and daughter Berta. The 1897 marriage record index pairs Philander Samples and Mary I. Hayes. The larger children/sibling sets are partly derivative memorial/tree data; preserve name variants rather than adding unverified dates.
