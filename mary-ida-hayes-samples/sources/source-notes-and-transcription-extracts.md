# Mary Ida Hayes Samples: source notes and transcription limits

State birth index gives Mary J. Hays, 14 Aug 1875. The 1910 census index calls the wife Ida M.; the 1920 index calls her Mary T.; other compiled records identify Mary Ida Hayes. A 1959 death-record transcription is reported to name her father incorrectly as Vincent Samples; WikiTree cites birth/1880 records for Vincent Hayes. Keep unresolved until originals are reviewed.
