Family Relationship Notes

John’s Kentucky birth and marriage records identify his parents as George Hill and Catharine Kiskaden and record his 26 October 1884 marriage to Sarah A. Hill. His 1940 Ohio death certificate records his vital details, occupation as farmer, and burial at Westboro IOOF Cemetery. The 1900–1930 census records place the couple and their children in Ohio.

Sarah’s 1952 Ohio death certificate records her birth and death details, widowhood, last address, and Westboro burial. The 1880 census places her with her mother Susan Hill and siblings in Bracken County. The proposed identification of her parents as Matthew Thomas Hill and Mary Susan Florence is supported by the marriage of Matthew and Mary and by the death record of Sarah’s brother Oather, but no single record yet names Sarah as their daughter. Her own death certificate gives incorrect parent names, apparently confusing her with her husband’s parents.

The six children shown are drawn from census and vital records; some dates are approximate. No verified portraits or headstone photographs have been located, so each profile uses one placeholder.

Life Story

Sarah Alice Hill was born on 16 November 1868 , most likely the daughter of Matthew T. Hill, a Union cavalryman, and Mary Susan Florence . She married John Samuel Hill in Bracken County in 1884, at fifteen, already carrying the Hill name .

Sarah raised six children in Kentucky and then in Clinton County, Ohio, where her daughter Maggie was born in 1902 . She outlived John by twelve years and died at Blanchester on 29 June 1952. She is buried beside him at Westboro .

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • Her death certificate names her parents as "George Hill and Mary Kiskaden," but those are her husband's parents; the informant was her daughter Elva. Kiskaden is not her maiden name.
  • No single record yet names her as Matthew and Susan Hill's daughter. Her 1868 birth entry, Matthew Hill's Civil War pension file, or her 1952 obituary could prove it.
  • The 1880 census gives her birthplace as Ohio; later records say Kentucky.
  • No headstone photo found.
