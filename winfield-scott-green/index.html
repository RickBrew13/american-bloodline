Family Relationship Notes

Hewlet’s 1925 death certificate names John Green and Mahala Goins as his parents, adding support to the proposed parent couple. Life Story

Winfield Scott Green, called Scott, was born in Claiborne County, Tennessee, on 11 July 1855 . He and his wife Sarah Jane Stiner raised a large family and moved from Tennessee to Knox County, Kentucky, by 1899 . That August their son James married Ella Helton "at Scot Green's," with Scott as a witness . He died in Whitley County on 13 September 1934 and is buried in Barbourville Cemetery .
