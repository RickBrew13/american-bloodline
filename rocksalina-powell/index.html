Family Relationship Notes

Rocksalina Williams Powell is Dexter Galen Powell’s mother. Her maiden name Williams comes from her 1838 marriage record to Ralph Powell. The records spell her first name many ways — Rosalina and Rasaline on the marriage record, Rocksalina in the 1850 census, Rockelina in 1870, Rocksaline in 1880, Roxaline on her son Dexter’s 1903 marriage license — and her tombstone reads Rocksalina. Her son Dexter’s 1931 death record names his mother Roxalina Williams. In 1870, widowed after Ralph’s death, she headed a household in Grant District, Doddridge County, with four of her children, including Dexter, 20.

Rocksalina married Ralph Powell in Harrison County, Virginia, in September 1838. The two copies of the marriage record give 17 and 20 September.

Life Story

Rocksalina Williams married Ralph Powell in Harrison County, Virginia, in September 1838. The 1850 census lists her in Doddridge County with Ralph and five of their ten children. After Ralph died in 1866, she headed her own household in 1870 with four children, including her son Dexter, and in 1880 she lived with her son James. She died on 21 February 1900 in West Union, Doddridge County, and is buried in Victory Cemetery. Her son Dexter Galen Powell married Minnie M. Lyon in 1903 and was the father of Rowland Edmund Powell.
