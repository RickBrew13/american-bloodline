# William and Ruth Stone — parentage and family-group audit

**Research date:** 26 November 2026 (America/New_York)  
**Family:** William Stone × Ruth Stone; likely daughter Arline (Stone) Brewster Gayhart.

## Records and relationship conclusions

### 1940 census household
The existing research transcription cites the 1940 U.S. census, Cincinnati Ward 6, 1307 (rear) Pendleton Street, as listing William Stone, age 30; Ruth Stone, age 29; May Ellen, age 8; and Arline, age 3. The index handle is FamilySearch **KWPD-SBP**. The FamilySearch page returned a 404 in this pass, and the original schedule/image and relationship column were not re-read. Treat the household information as an attributed prior transcription. It supports William as Arline’s father and Ruth as her probable mother, but is not an explicit parent-child document without the relationship field.

### Mary Ellen and William’s first marriage
Find a Grave memorial [62267367](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/62267367/mary-ellen-thompson) reproduces an obituary for Mary Ellen Stone Thompson (26 May 1932–24 Nov 2010) saying she was born in Cincinnati to **William Stone and Helen Margaret Abel Stone** and had “one sister, Arlene Stone.” The 1940 household’s May Ellen, age 8, may be Mary Ellen, but is about a year older than her reported birth and the census relationship column/image has not been inspected. Keep “May Ellen” as a census name form and Mary Ellen as a likely identity match rather than silently merging the entries. Mary Ellen is not assigned as Ruth’s biological child: evidence points to her being William’s daughter with first wife Helen.

Existing project citations report Helen Margaret Stone died 1 June 1932, age 22, wife of William (FamilySearch index [X692-KBP](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1/X692-KBP)); this record link was unavailable during direct access. No original William–Helen marriage return or William–Ruth marriage return has been reviewed. Ruth’s maiden name remains unknown.

### Arline’s birth date
GENI reports 22 February 1938, but it supplies no source. The 1940 transcription reports age 3 on 1 April, which suggests about 1936–1937. Preserve this discrepancy; no Ohio birth certificate/index image naming her parents has been obtained in this pass.

### Children and siblings
- William and Helen: Mary Ellen is named as their child in her obituary.
- William and Ruth: Arline is the probable daughter indicated by the 1940 household and later family/obituary identity chain.
- The census “May Ellen” may be Mary Ellen; original census fields needed to settle the household relationship.
- No additional biological child of William or Ruth was established from independent records.
- No siblings or parents for either William or Ruth were identified. Searches returned only unrelated same-name Stone families. No Gen VII ancestors were fabricated or linked.
