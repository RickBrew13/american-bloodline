## 3. Best route to the original 1984 Larry Clark divorce record

The earlier Ohio divorce-index lead remains: a 6 September 1984 divorce entry for Larry Clark and a Judith M. born in 1939, Hamilton County, with an eight-year marriage and one minor child. The wife’s surname is not shown in the abbreviated information currently available, so this remains a probable but unconfirmed match.

The following official/local repositories describe how to get the actual case and supporting documents:

- [Hamilton County Clerk of Courts — Records Search](https://www.courtclerk.org/records-search/) — use the Common Pleas Civil name search and identify any matching Domestic Relations case.
- [Hamilton County Clerk — Domestic Relations Division](https://www.courtclerk.org/our-office/common-pleas-court-clerk/domestic-relations-division/) — divorce decrees from 1885 onward are available through the Document Center; the page lists the records office and contact details.
- [Hamilton County Genealogical Society — Marriages and Divorces](https://www.hcgsohio.org/cpage.php?pt=60) — explains the local divorce index and record-retrieval process.
- [Ohio Vital Statistics marriage/divorce search help](https://www.hacourts.com/VitStats/Help/index.shtml) — describes the statewide marriage/divorce index and the fields captured, including spouse identities, marriage length, county, and decree information.

**Next record to obtain:** the full 1984 divorce decree/case file, not just the index. It may confirm Judy’s full legal name, Larry’s full name, marriage date, child’s identity/age, and whether this is the correct Judith.
