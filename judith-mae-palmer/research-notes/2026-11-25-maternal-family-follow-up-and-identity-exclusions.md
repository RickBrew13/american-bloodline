## New family context located

Judy’s mother, **Mary Jane Williams (23 February 1918–1990)**, has a separate American Bloodline profile. That profile identifies Mary Jane’s parents as **John Thomas Williams (28 March 1892–1961)** and **Helen E. Rich (about 1897–about 1967)**, and lists Mary Jane’s siblings as:

- Kathleen Rita Williams (born 1922)
- Margaret May “Margy May” Williams (born 21 August 1924)
- John Thomas Williams Jr. (born 10 March 1934, Cincinnati)

This makes John Thomas Williams Jr. Judy’s **maternal uncle**, not Judy’s brother. Judy’s own listed sisters remain Mary Ann Palmer and Penelope Jane “Penny” Spiess. Do not move Mary Jane’s siblings into Judy’s sibling list.

## Same-name Ancestry result excluded

A search result for **Richard Lewis Jacobs** (reported born 2 June 1943, died April 1986) includes an Ancestry member-tree association with a Judith Mae Palmer. The associated Judith is reported as born in 1942, conflicting with Judy’s well-supported 9 February 1939 birth date. No identity bridge connects this couple to Judy and Homer, and the page itself warns that public-tree entries require verification.

- [Ancestry public-tree result for Richard Lewis Jacobs](https://www.ancestry.de/genealogy/records/richard-lewis-jacobs-24-2gffzkq)

**Decision:** exclude Richard Lewis Jacobs as a spouse of this Judy unless an independent marriage or other identity-matching record is found. Do not add him to Judy’s profile or family tree.
