Life Story

Gobelle Moore was born on October 14, 1933, at Sandy Hook in the hills of Elliott County, Kentucky, the son of John and Lacie (Adkins) Moore, one of a large family. DOCUMENTED He married Alma Jean Noble, and they raised nine children while he drove a truck for B.G. Danis Construction, a Teamster for more than thirty-five years. DOCUMENTED He belonged to Faith Hill Church of God and Enterprise Baptist Church. DOCUMENTED

Alma died in 1998. DOCUMENTED Gobelle then married Ruth Lee Moore, once the wife of Elmer Brewster, but she died in July 2000. SUPPORTED He lived on at Fairborn, Ohio, and died there on February 5, 2011, aged 77, survived by his nine children, 28 grandchildren and 46 great-grandchildren. DOCUMENTED He was buried in Byron Cemetery at Fairborn, beside Alma. SUPPORTED
