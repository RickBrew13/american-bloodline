Family Relationship Notes

FamilySearch’s United States Social Security NUMIDENT entry for Leon Niles (record 6K7Q-ZC4F) explicitly lists Major Niles as a parent and Mary Riggby as a parent. The associated person-detail pages identify Leon as Major’s son and Mary as Major’s spouse. Those links all come from the same NUMIDENT correspondence entry; they are not independent records.

Major Nye Sr. was born in Alabama about 1862–1863 and died in Stockton, Baldwin County, Alabama, in 1921. His 1921 death record names his parents March Nye and Sarah Vaughn. The 1900 census gives his birth as March 1862; he was 47 in the 1910 census. In 1900 he was a widowed head of household in Perdido with three children, Lucious, Major Jr., and Phillis.

He married Eliza Lang on 22 April 1884 and Missouri Smith on 18 May 1887, both in Baldwin County. He married Mary Rigby on 25 November 1901. A wife named Lorena is named as his spouse on his 1921 death record; no marriage record for Lorena has been found, so Mary evidently died, or the marriage ended, before 1921.

The family is recorded under the surname Nye in the early records, not Niles.

Life Story

Major Nye Sr. was born in Alabama about 1862–1863 and died in Stockton, Baldwin County, Alabama, in 1921. His 1921 death record names his parents March Nye and Sarah Vaughn. He married Eliza Lang on 22 April 1884 and Missouri Smith on 18 May 1887, both in Baldwin County. He married Mary Rigby on 25 November 1901. In 1900 he was a widowed head of household in Perdido with three children from his earlier marriages, Lucious, Major Jr., and Phillis. In 1910 he was living in Stockton with Mary and their children Clinton, Jessie, Robin, and Learmon. A wife named Lorena is named as his spouse on his 1921 death record; no marriage record for Lorena has been found. His son Leon was born in Stockton in 1908.

Research Notes and Open Questions

  • The NUMIDENT is the only located record naming Major as Leon’s father. FamilySearch’s linked Major entry says “son,” and the linked Mary entry says “spouse”; those are related views of the same source, not independent corroboration.
  • Leon’s 1950 census entry supports his Birmingham household but names no parents. Its age-derived birth year (about 1910) differs from the NUMIDENT birth date (1908); preserve the discrepancy rather than treating the census age as exact.
  • 25 November 2026: Major’s family was found in the early records under the surname Nye, not Niles — that is why searches for Major Niles found nothing. His 1921 Alabama death record names his parents March Nye and Sarah Vaughn; the 1900 census (Perdido) and the 1910 census (Stockton) list him as Major Nye with his household; and his marriage to Mary Rigby on 25 November 1901 in Baldwin County is recorded on page 140 of the county marriages.
  • Find a Grave exact-name searches did not identify a plausible Alabama memorial for Major, under Niles or Nye. A same-name Major Niles memorial in Ohio (1891–1930) matches his son Major Jr., who was born in Stockton in 1891 and died in Toledo, Ohio, on 20 January 1930; it is likely his, but it is not assigned without reading the memorial.
  • The older children — Lucious, Lucinda, Major Jr., and Phillis — are from Major’s earlier marriages, not Mary’s. The tree attaches all eight children to Mary, but three of them were born before she could have been their mother.
  • Lucious and Lucinda may be one child: the 1900 census calls the child Lucius, son, 13, and the 1910 census calls the child Lucinda, daughter, 23, with the same birth year about 1887. Whether they are one child or twins is unresolved.
  • Learmon, age 1 in the 1910 census, may be Leon under an earlier name; see Leon’s page.
  • No photograph or headstone photo for Major has been found; Find a Grave, BillionGraves, FamilySearch tree photos, and free newspapers were searched on 25 November 2026.
  • Next useful records: the 1870 and 1880 censuses for a March Nye household in Baldwin County; a death record for Mary Nye; the deciding record for whether Learmon is Leon (see Leon’s page).
  • Follow-up dated 25 November 2026: a sample of the Alabama 1900 and 1910 Niles Soundex cards did not verify Major in a household or identify Major’s parents or siblings. The sample was not exhaustive. A reported Black William Niles household in Montgomery in 1910 (William, 54; wife Angie, 47; son Grady, 19) remains an unlinked lead; its card locator and full transcription were not retained, so it is not assigned to Major Niles or Leon. A separate Birmingham N-420 Soundex card coded White is a different, excluded household. See the dated research note in this profile folder.
