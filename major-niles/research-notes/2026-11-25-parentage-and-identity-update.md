# Major Niles — parentage and identity research update

**Date:** 25 November 2026

## Finding

FamilySearch’s [U.S. Social Security NUMIDENT entry for Leon Niles](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:6K7Q-ZC4F) gives Leon’s birth as 27 November 1908 in Stockton, Alabama, and names Major Niles and Mary Riggby under “Parents and Siblings.” Its linked [Major Niles detail](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:6K7Q-ZC42) explicitly describes Leon as Major’s son and lists Mary Riggby as Major’s spouse. The linked [Mary Riggby detail](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:6K7Q-ZC45) reciprocally lists Major as spouse and Leon as son.

These are related views of a single NUMIDENT correspondence entry, not three independent records. The entry reports a Social Program Application Date of 17 October 1990 and Claim Date of 19 July 1975; it does not provide an image or biographical dates for Major. It is the only located evidence for Major’s parentage of Leon. The source display associates Major and Mary as spouses, but no separate civil marriage record has been found.

- Public FamilySearch indexed searches for Major Niles in the 1900/1910/1920/1930 census collections and targeted Alabama marriage/death searches returned no matching record in the access path used. The results are bounded index searches, not proof that records do not exist.
- Exact-name Find a Grave searches did not identify a plausible Alabama memorial for Major. A same-name Major Niles memorial in Ohio (1891–1930) has no identity bridge and is excluded.

## Current assessment

Leon is the only child identified in the located NUMIDENT relationship display; this does not establish that Major had no other children. Major’s own parents, siblings, dates, residence, occupation, death, burial and photo remain unknown. No unsupported parent or sibling profiles are created. The existing Major–Mary couple remains in Generation VI; its index entry was checked and left unchanged.
