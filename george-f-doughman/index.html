Life Story

George F. Doughman was born on 20 January 1864 SUPPORTED. The Warren County birth register names him and his wife Etta May as the parents of a son born in 1898, Wm. Virgil Harley Dewey Doughman DOCUMENTED. The family most likely came to Warren County from Macon County, Illinois, where two older sons were born (inferred). George outlived his daughter Myrtle and his wife and died on 6 January 1943; the three share a family stone at West Woodville Cemetery SUPPORTED.
