Life Story

Clement Herman Woodrum’s Find a Grave memorial reports his dates as 22 November 1908–21 July 1972 and links his parents as Edward McArthur “Hicks” Woodrum and Susan Henrietta Wethington Woodrum. The 1972 obituary clipping attached to the memorial says his parents were “Eddie Woodrum” and “Suskennek Wethington.” Those are plausible name matches, but I have not found an original record that bridges the full names to the obituary wording.

The same obituary clipping names his brother Jim Woodrum, three half-sisters, and eight children: Milburn J., Bobby C., Alta Roe Gabehart, Janice E. Campbell, Velma M. Wethington, Wilda Jean Warfield, William Clement Woodrum and Anna Charlene Warner. It reports he died in the Liberty area and was buried in Clementsville. Anna’s 2002 obituary independently names the seven children who survived her: Milburn, Bobby, Clement, Alta, Janice, Velma and Charlene. The 1972/2002 child-name and married-surname variations remain source-specific.

Janice is linked to this household by her 1956 marriage record naming parents Herman and Edith Woodrum, Anna’s 2002 obituary naming daughter Janice Campbell, and compiled family sources that expand the parents’ names to Clement Herman and Anna Edith.
