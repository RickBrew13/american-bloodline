# Rick's Tom McGregor stories (2026-10-10)

Source: Rick Brewster, main chat 2026-10-10. Rick's own memories of Tom, as told.

## Tom's background

- Born and raised in Kentucky, per Tom himself.
- Tom and Rick did work on Tom's Geni tree together before Tom passed. Tom comes from the same Appalachian trails as Rick's bloodline, Rick believes.

## The nannie

- Tom told Rick he had a nannie when young — an African American woman who loved him and his siblings.
- She fed them her breast milk (Tom said this was common).
- She gave Tom a nickname that most people would call racist today. Tom said it wasn't racist to them back then, it was just the way things were. In Tom's words, she called him "N***a Tom" — because of his long red curly hair; after she gave him a bath his hair would poof up like an afro. (Tom's words, not Rick's.)
- She stayed with them when Tom was young. She'd be out yelling over the Kentucky hills for him by that nickname. All the siblings and neighbor kids called him the same nickname until high school, Tom said.

## Post Office

- Tom worked at the Post Office for as long as Rick remembers.
- He'd tell Rick stories of his walks and routes. Rick wishes he could remember them all.

## Kenwood Baptist — counting money

- Every Sunday they'd be at Kenwood Baptist church. Tom counted the money every Sunday after service. Rick helped him, along with whoever else was helping.
- One of Tom's favorite church friends: Jim and Pat Crabtree — "awesome people," always helping the family over the years. Rick later found out they're from Tennessee, Morgan County / Brewstertown area — and saw Crabtree/Brewster ties, "which was really cool."

## The big money at church

- Rick remembers seeing big money move through that church as a kid. Big business people from Cincinnati were all in that church.
- The Mills Family — Rick, his dad, his brother Mike, and some cousins all did fence work for them.
- The Lindner Family — owned Chiquita and UDF "and whatever else they own." Rick would see them drop big checks, especially around holidays. ("I know you're not supposed to look at those things but I couldn't help it at the time. I was young and amazed people could have that much money just to give away. Even for church.")
- Grandma Ruth was real good friends with Edith Lindner — they talked all the time. Edith would roll up in Grandma's driveway in "that shiny new Rolls Royce all the time." Grandma loved her "like a sister probably."
- Gma and Tom went to the Lindners' parties all the time. Tom told Rick about a dinner party at the Lindners' with President Bush at their house. Tom said it wasn't the only president or senators and congressmen they met through the Lindners and their parties.
