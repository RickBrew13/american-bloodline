Life Story

James M. Fisher was born in 1858 in Jackson County, Virginia, the son of William Fisher Jr. and his second wife Susanna Crites Koontz. William’s first wife Sarah Samuels had died by 1853, and an old compiled account that placed James among her children is wrong on chronology alone: two marriage records, his own and his brother Isaac’s, name Susanna as his mother, and his widowed mother was living in his household in 1880.

He married Nancy Jane Casto on 12 June 1878. The register transcription names both sets of parents, and it is the record that settles both sides of this couple at once. He farmed his whole recorded life in the Ripley district — farmer on the 1880 census, farmer and employer on his own farm in 1910, “Farmer” on his death certificate. He and Nancy had three children: Edward, Okey, who died at seven weeks, and Virdie Lee, who married Rhoda Alice Polsley and carried the line forward. James died a widower on 27 September 1919 at Fairplain and rests at Ephesus Baptist Church Cemetery beside Nancy.
