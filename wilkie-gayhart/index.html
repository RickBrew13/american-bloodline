Life Story

Wilkie Gayhart was born on August 4, 1935, one of the children of Kelly Gayhart and Bertha Centers. His father died when he was two, and by 1950 his widowed mother, now Bertha Ward, was raising Wilkie and his brothers and sisters at Greenup, in northeastern Kentucky. His headstone records that he served in the United States Navy during the Korean War era.

After Earl Brewster's death in 1959, Wilkie married Earl's widow, Arline, and their daughter Kristy was born in Cincinnati on Christmas Day 1961. He died in Kentucky on October 12, 1993, aged 58, and was buried at Spring Grove Cemetery in Cincinnati under a Navy marker that also reads "Loving father and papa · We love you."
