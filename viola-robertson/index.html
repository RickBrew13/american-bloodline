Family Relationship Notes

Pansy Robertson’s 13 February 1930 Kentucky marriage record names Viola Robertson as her mother and Ned Robertson as her father. The 1910 census lists Viola, 18, as the wife of Ned Robertson, 22, in Floyd, Newberry County, South Carolina, so the two were married by 1910, though no marriage record has been found. The 1910 census gives Viola’s birth year as about 1892 and both her parents’ birthplaces as South Carolina, but it does not name them, and her maiden name is not known — Robertson may be her married name or her maiden name.

No birth, death, or other record for Viola has been found beyond the 1910 census and her daughter’s marriage record. Her parents are not named on any record, and no other children besides Pansy have been found.

Life Story

Viola Robertson was born in South Carolina about 1892. By 1910 she was married to Ned Robertson and living in Floyd, Newberry County, South Carolina. Her daughter Pansy was born in South Carolina about 1910–1913 and married Herman Washington in Newport, Kentucky, on 13 February 1930; the marriage record names Viola as her mother. No birth, death, or other record for Viola has been found, her maiden name is not known, and her parents are not named on any record.
